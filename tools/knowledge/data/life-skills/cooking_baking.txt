# tag: life.cooking.baking
how do i make cookies from scratch | basic cookie method | steps to bake cookies => Cream softened butter and sugars, beat in eggs and vanilla, then mix in the dry ingredients until just combined. Fold in chips, scoop onto a lined pan and bake until the edges are golden.
why should i chill cookie dough | chilling dough benefits | how long to chill cookie dough => Chilling firms the butter so cookies spread less, and gives the flour time to soak up moisture, making them thicker with more flavor. 30 minutes helps, and up to a day or two is even better.
why do cookies need to cool on the pan | cookies falling apart | when to move cookies to a rack => Fresh cookies are soft and fragile. Let them set on the pan for about 2 to 5 minutes, then move them to a rack so the bottoms don't keep cooking or get soggy.
how do i make brownies fudgy | fudgy vs cakey brownies | how to make chewy brownies => More fat and chocolate and less flour make fudgy brownies, and more flour and leavening make cakey ones. Take them out when a toothpick has moist crumbs, not clean.
how do i make a cake from scratch | cake baking steps | basic cake method => Grease and line the pan and preheat the oven. Cream butter and sugar, add eggs one at a time, then add the dry ingredients and milk in turns, mixing just until smooth. Bake until a toothpick comes out clean.
why is my cake dry | moist cake tips | dry cake fix => Overbaking and too much flour are the usual reasons. Measure flour by spooning and leveling, check the cake early, and brush layers with a little sugar syrup if it's already dry.
why should you not overmix batter | overmixing muffins | tough muffins reason => Overmixing builds too much gluten, which makes cakes and muffins tough, chewy and full of tunnels. Mix until the flour just disappears, lumps are okay for muffins.
how do i make muffins rise high | domed muffins | tall muffin tops => Fill the cups nearly to the top, don't overmix, and start baking at a higher temperature, around 220 °C (425 °F), for the first 5 minutes before lowering it.
how do i frost a cake | cake frosting tips | crumb coat meaning => Let the cake cool completely first. Spread a thin crumb coat to trap crumbs, chill it 15 to 30 minutes, then add the final thicker layer and smooth it with a spatula.
how do i make buttercream frosting | basic buttercream | frosting from scratch => Beat softened butter until creamy, then slowly beat in powdered sugar, a splash of milk and vanilla until fluffy. A common start is about 1 cup butter to 3 or 4 cups powdered sugar.
why is my frosting runny | frosting too thin fix | thicken frosting => Add more powdered sugar a little at a time, or chill it for 15 to 30 minutes. Warm butter or too much liquid usually causes runny frosting.
how do i make icing for cookies | simple glaze | royal icing what is it => A simple glaze is powdered sugar mixed with a little milk or lemon juice. Royal icing is powdered sugar with egg whites or meringue powder and dries hard, great for decorating cookies.
how do i make food coloring brighter | gel vs liquid food coloring | how to make red frosting => Gel food coloring is much stronger than liquid drops and won't make frosting runny. Deep colors like red and black get darker if left to sit a few hours.
how do i make bread at home | basic bread steps | how is bread made => Mix flour, water, yeast and salt, knead until smooth, let it rise until doubled, shape it, let it rise again, then bake in a hot oven until golden and hollow sounding when tapped on the bottom.
how do i know if my yeast is still alive | proofing yeast | yeast not foaming => Stir yeast into warm water, about 40 °C (105 °F), with a pinch of sugar. If it gets foamy in 5 to 10 minutes, it's alive. Water that's too hot can kill it.
why didn't my bread rise | bread dough not rising | flat bread fix => Dead or old yeast, water that was too hot or cold, a chilly room, or too much salt touching the yeast are common causes. Try a warmer spot and give it more time.
how do i know when bread is done | bread baked through | internal temperature of bread => Tap the bottom, and it should sound hollow. Most bread is done when the inside reaches about 88 to 99 °C (190 to 210 °F) on a thermometer.
how do i make pizza dough | homemade pizza crust | pizza dough tips => Mix flour, warm water, yeast, salt and a little oil, knead until smooth, and let it rise about an hour. Stretch it by hand, add toppings and bake in a very hot oven, as hot as it goes.
how do i make crispy pizza at home | pizza crust soggy | pizza stone tips => Preheat the oven very hot with a pizza stone or steel or an upside down sheet pan inside for at least 30 minutes, and go light on sauce and wet toppings.
how do i make pie crust | flaky pie dough | why is my pie crust tough => Cut cold butter into flour until pea sized, add just enough ice water to hold it together, and chill before rolling. Warm butter and overworking the dough make it tough instead of flaky.
how do i stop a pie crust from burning | pie crust edges too dark | pie shield => Cover the edges with strips of foil or a pie shield partway through baking once they're golden.
how do i make biscuits fluffy | flaky biscuits | tall biscuit tips => Use very cold butter and buttermilk, mix gently, fold the dough over itself a few times for layers, and press the cutter straight down without twisting so they rise tall.
how do i make banana bread | banana bread tips | how ripe should bananas be for bread => Use very ripe bananas with lots of brown spots, since they're sweeter. Mash them, mix with melted butter, sugar, egg, then flour and baking soda, and bake in a loaf pan until a toothpick comes out clean.
how do i make cupcakes | cupcake tips | how full should cupcake liners be => Make cake batter, fill liners about two thirds full, and bake around 18 to 22 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean. Let them cool fully before frosting.
how do i make a cheesecake not crack | cheesecake cracking | water bath for cheesecake => Mix on low so you don't beat in air, bake at a low temperature, often in a water bath, and let it cool slowly in the turned off oven with the door cracked.
how do i make meringue | meringue tips | why is my meringue weeping => Beat clean egg whites to soft peaks, then add sugar slowly until stiff and glossy. Weeping often comes from undissolved sugar or humid weather. Bake low and slow.
how do i make caramel | homemade caramel sauce | wet vs dry caramel => Melt sugar in a heavy pan until it turns amber, then carefully whisk in warm cream and butter, it bubbles up hard. Hot sugar burns badly, so an adult should do this or help closely.
what is tempering chocolate | why temper chocolate | shiny chocolate coating => Tempering means melting and cooling chocolate to exact temperatures so it sets shiny and snaps. Untempered chocolate can turn streaky and soft. Candy melts don't need tempering.
why do you grease and flour a pan | greasing pans | how to grease a pan => Greasing and flouring creates a barrier so cakes release cleanly. Rub butter or shortening all over the inside, add a spoonful of flour, tilt and tap it to coat, and dump the extra.
can i open the oven while baking | opening oven door cake | peeking in the oven => Try not to open it in the first two thirds of baking, especially for cakes and souffles, because the temperature drops and they can sink. Use the oven light and window to check.
what rack should i bake on | middle rack baking | top vs bottom oven rack => The middle rack gives the most even heat and is best for most baking. The top browns tops faster, and the bottom crisps bottoms, like pizza crust.
why do my cookies burn on the bottom | burnt bottom cookies | dark baking sheet => Dark pans absorb more heat and burn bottoms faster. Use a light colored pan, line it with parchment, bake on the middle rack and check a few minutes early.
how do i make no bake cookies | no bake desserts | easy treats without an oven => No bake cookies usually boil butter, sugar, milk and cocoa for about a minute, then stir in oats and peanut butter and scoop onto wax paper to set. Ask an adult to help with the boiling sugar.
how do i make rice krispie treats | rice crispy treats | marshmallow bars => Melt butter in a big pot over low heat, stir in marshmallows until smooth, take it off the heat and fold in the cereal. Press it into a greased pan with a buttered spatula and let it set before cutting.
what is high altitude baking | does altitude change baking | baking in the mountains => At high altitude, air pressure is lower, so water boils at a lower temperature and doughs rise faster. Bakers often add a bit more flour or liquid, use less leavening and sugar, and bake slightly hotter.
how do i make pancakes fluffy | thick fluffy pancakes | why are my pancakes flat => Don't overmix the batter, leave small lumps, let it rest 5 to 10 minutes, use fresh baking powder and cook on medium heat. Buttermilk makes them extra fluffy.
how do i make waffles crispy | crispy waffles | soggy waffles fix => Use a batter with a little more fat or some cornstarch, preheat the iron fully, and don't stack hot waffles, which steams them. Keep them on a rack in a low oven.
how do i make crepes | crepe tips | crepes vs pancakes => Crepes use a thin batter with no baking powder. Pour a little into a hot buttered pan and swirl it thin, cook about a minute until the edges lift, flip and cook 20 seconds more.
how do i decorate cookies | cookie decorating tips | piping icing for beginners => Outline the cookie with thicker icing, let it set a few minutes, then fill in with thinner icing and spread it with a toothpick. Add sprinkles while it's wet.
how do i use a piping bag | piping frosting | make a piping bag from a ziplock => Fill the bag halfway, twist the top closed, and squeeze from the top while guiding with your other hand. A zip top bag with a corner cut off works as a simple piping bag.
