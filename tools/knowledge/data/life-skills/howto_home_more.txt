# tag: life.howto.home
how do i take out the trash properly | trash day | bag the trash => Tie the bag closed, check the bin for spills, and put a new bag in. Separate recycling, and take bins to the curb the night before or morning of trash day.
how do i hang clothes on hangers | hanging shirts | closet hangers => Slide the hanger in through the neck hole, not by stretching the collar, and line the shoulders up with the hanger ends. Fold pants over the bar or clip them at the waist.
how do i unshrink clothes | shrunken sweater | fix shrunk clothes => For cotton or wool, soak the item in lukewarm water with a little hair conditioner or baby shampoo for about 30 minutes, gently stretch it back into shape, and let it dry flat. It often helps but may not fully fix it.
how do i dust | dusting tips | dust top to bottom => Start high and work down so dust falls onto areas you haven't cleaned yet. Use a slightly damp microfiber cloth to trap dust instead of spreading it, then vacuum last.
how do i get rid of fruit flies | fruit flies | fruit fly trap => Throw out overripe fruit, take out the trash and clean sink drains. Trap them with a small cup of apple cider vinegar and a drop of dish soap, covered with plastic wrap poked with tiny holes.
how do i get rid of ants | ants in house | ant trail => Wipe up crumbs and sticky spills, store food sealed, and clean the ant trail with soapy water or vinegar to erase their scent path. Ask an adult before using ant baits.
how do i catch a spider | spider in room | cup and paper spider => Put a cup over the spider, slide a stiff piece of paper under the cup, and carry it outside to let it go. Most house spiders are harmless and eat other bugs.
how do i make my room cozy | cozy room | room vibes => Add soft lighting like string lights or a lamp, a throw blanket and pillows, a rug, and things you love on the walls. Keeping it tidy makes it feel calmer too.
how do i use a drill | power drill basics | drill bit => With an adult, pick the right bit, tighten it in the chuck, wear safety glasses, hold the drill straight with both hands and start slowly. Use the reverse switch to back screws out.
how do i assemble furniture | flat pack furniture | ikea instructions => Lay out and count all the parts first, read the whole instructions once, and work on a soft surface. Leave screws a bit loose until everything is together, then tighten them.
how do i set an alarm clock | alarm clock | wake up alarm => On most digital clocks, hold the alarm button and press the hour and minute buttons to set the time, then switch the alarm on. Check AM and PM, and put it across the room if you snooze a lot.
how do i make my bed every day | bed making habit | make bed fast => Make it right after you get up so it becomes a habit. Pull up the sheets and blanket, smooth them, and put the pillow on top. It takes under two minutes.
how do i tie my hair up | ponytail | bun => Brush hair back with your fingers or a brush, gather it at the height you want, hold it tight and loop a hair tie around it two or three times.
how do i braid hair | braiding | three strand braid => Split hair into three equal sections. Cross the right section over the middle, then the left over the new middle, and keep alternating to the end. Secure it with a hair tie.
how do i french braid | french braid | dutch braid => Start a regular braid at the top of the head, and each time you cross a side section over, add a little loose hair from that side into it. A Dutch braid crosses sections under instead.
how do i hem pants | hemming | pants too long => Try them on and mark the length, fold the bottom up inside, iron the fold, and sew around with small hidden stitches or a sewing machine. Hem tape that irons on is a no sew option.
how do i patch jeans | jean patch | knee hole => Cut a denim patch bigger than the hole, put it inside the jeans behind the hole, and sew around it, or use an iron on patch. Visible patches with fun stitching can look cool too.
how do i iron on a patch | iron on patch | patch jacket => Place the patch where you want it, cover it with a thin cloth, and press a hot iron firmly for about 30 seconds without sliding. Flip and press from the inside, and let it cool. Ask an adult for help with the iron.
how do i make a scrunchie | scrunchie | sew a scrunchie => Cut a fabric strip about 50 cm by 10 cm (20 in by 4 in), sew it into a tube inside out, turn it right side out, thread elastic through, tie the elastic and sew the ends closed.
how do i make a tote bag | tote bag diy | sew a bag => Fold a rectangle of sturdy fabric in half, sew up the two sides, fold and stitch the top edge, then sew on two fabric strips as handles.
how do i make a pillow | sew a pillow | pillow cover => Cut two equal squares of fabric, put them right sides together and sew around the edges, leaving a gap. Turn it right side out, stuff it and sew the gap shut.
how do i knit a scarf | knit scarf | scarf beginner => Use chunky yarn and big needles. Cast on about 20 stitches and knit every row (garter stitch) until it's long enough, then bind off. It's a classic first project.
how do i make lip balm | lip balm | diy lip balm => With an adult, melt beeswax and coconut oil or shea butter together in roughly equal amounts, stir, and pour into small tins or tubes to cool. Only use ingredients safe for lips.
