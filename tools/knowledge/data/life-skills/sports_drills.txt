# tag: sports.drills
what are good basketball drills to do alone | solo basketball drills | basketball practice by myself => Try form shooting close to the hoop, the Mikan drill for layups, two ball dribbling, free throws in sets of 10, and spot shooting around the arc. Track your makes to see progress.
what is the mikan drill | mikan layup drill | basketball layup drill => In the Mikan drill, you stand under the hoop and make alternating layups on each side without letting the ball touch the floor, using correct footwork. It builds touch and footwork.
how do i improve my free throws | free throw tips | free throw routine => Use the same routine every time, like dribbles and a breath, line up your shooting foot with the rim, bend your knees, and follow through with your wrist relaxed. Practice them when tired, like in a game.
what is beef in basketball shooting | bef shooting form | basketball shooting form => BEEF stands for Balance, Eyes, Elbow and Follow through: feet set, eyes on the rim, shooting elbow under the ball, and a smooth follow through with your wrist flicked down.
how do i shoot with my weak hand | left hand layups | weak hand basketball => Practice weak hand layups and dribbling every day, starting close and slow. Brush your teeth or eat with that hand to build coordination. It feels awkward for weeks before it clicks.
how do i box out in basketball | boxing out | rebounding technique => When a shot goes up, find your opponent, turn and put your back against them with a wide, low stance and arms out, then go get the ball. Position beats height for rebounds.
how do i make a good chest pass | basketball passing tips | chest pass technique => Hold the ball at your chest with thumbs behind it, step toward your target, and push your arms out, finishing with thumbs pointing down. Aim at your teammate's chest.
what are good soccer drills to do alone | solo soccer drills | soccer practice by myself => Try wall passing with both feet, cone dribbling with inside and outside touches, juggling, shooting at a target on a wall, and first touch practice by kicking the ball high and controlling it.
how do i improve my first touch in soccer | first touch drills | controlling the ball => Pass the ball against a wall and receive it with different parts of your foot, cushioning it so it stops close. Keep your body relaxed and look up before the ball arrives.
how do i use my weak foot in soccer | weak foot training | kick with both feet => Do every drill with your weaker foot too, starting with short passes against a wall. Many coaches suggest spending extra time on your weak foot since it's the bigger gain.
what are soccer cone drills | dribbling cone drills | soccer cones practice => Set cones in a line or zigzag and dribble through them with small touches, using inside, outside and sole. Try different patterns and time yourself.
how do i take a free kick | curve a soccer ball | free kick technique => To curve it, strike the ball off center with the inside of your foot, wrapping your foot around it and following through across your body. For power, strike through the middle with your laces.
how do i improve my soccer fitness | soccer conditioning | run more in soccer => Soccer needs repeated sprints, so do interval running like sprint 30 seconds, jog 60, plus shuttle runs. Playing small sided games builds game fitness too.
what are good baseball drills at home | baseball practice at home | solo baseball drills => Do tee work into a net, dry swings with focus on form, wall ball for fielding grounders, and throwing at a target with good mechanics. Short, focused reps beat many sloppy ones.
how do i field a ground ball | fielding grounders | ground ball technique => Get in a low athletic stance, glove on the ground in front, bring your throwing hand over the glove, watch the ball in, then shuffle and throw. Move your feet to get in front of it.
how do i stop being scared of the ball | afraid of getting hit baseball | fear of the ball => Start with soft balls, practice getting out of the way of inside pitches by turning away, and wear proper gear. Confidence grows with reps. It's normal to feel this.
what is a good batting stance | batting stance tips | how to stand when batting => Feet about shoulder width apart, knees slightly bent, weight balanced, hands near your back shoulder, and eyes level on the pitcher. Stay relaxed and comfortable.
how do i throw a curveball | curveball for kids | when can kids throw curveballs => Many coaches recommend waiting until about age 13 or 14 or later before throwing curveballs, and focusing first on fastballs and changeups with good mechanics, to protect young arms.
what is a changeup | changeup grip | how to throw a changeup => A changeup looks like a fastball but is slower, which throws off the batter's timing. A common circle changeup grip makes an OK sign on the side of the ball.
how do i run the bases faster | base running tips | rounding the bases => Run hard out of the box, look at your coach, take a slightly curved path before the base so you can cut the corner, and touch the inside corner of the bag.
what are good football drills for kids | flag football drills | football practice drills => Practice route running, catching with your hands away from your body, flag pulling, footwork through ladders, and throwing at targets. Learning plays matters as much as skill.
how do i run a good route | route running tips | wide receiver routes => Run at full speed so the defender can't guess, plant hard on your cut, snap your head around to find the ball, and catch with your hands.
what is a route tree | football routes | slant post curl => A route tree numbers common pass routes, like flat, slant, comeback, curl, out, dig, corner, post and go. Teams use them to call plays quickly.
how do i pull a flag in flag football | flag pulling technique | flag football defense => Stay low, keep your eyes on the runner's hips, close the distance under control, and grab the flag rather than diving. Don't push or hold the runner.
what are good tennis drills | tennis practice alone | solo tennis drills => Hit against a wall to build consistency, practice serves in sets with targets, do shadow swings for form, and do footwork drills like split steps and side shuffles.
what is a split step in tennis | tennis footwork | split step timing => A split step is a small hop landing on both feet just as your opponent hits, so you can push off quickly in any direction. It's used in tennis, badminton and pickleball.
how do i hit a topspin forehand | topspin tennis | brushing up on the ball => Start with the racket low below the ball and swing up and forward, brushing up its back, finishing over your opposite shoulder. Topspin makes the ball dip into the court.
what are good volleyball drills alone | volleyball practice at home | solo volleyball drills => Pass the ball against a wall with forearm passes, set to yourself repeatedly, practice serves at targets, and do footwork for spike approaches.
what is a spike approach | volleyball approach steps | how to jump for a spike => For a right handed hitter, a common approach is left, right, left: a small step, a big fast step, then close with the left and jump off both feet, swinging both arms up.
what are good hockey drills at home | off ice hockey training | stickhandling drills => Stickhandle a ball on a smooth surface around objects, shoot pucks or balls at a net with a shooting pad, and do off ice skating strides and balance work.
how do i improve my stickhandling | hockey stickhandling | puck control => Keep your hands loose and away from your body, roll your wrists to cup the puck, and keep your head up. Practice with a heavy ball and in tight spaces.
what are good swimming drills | swim drills for freestyle | catch up drill => Try catch up drill (one arm waits until the other finishes), kicking with a board, fingertip drag for high elbows, and side kicking for balance and breathing.
how do i get better at running form | running form drills | a skips => Do drills like A skips, B skips, high knees and butt kicks. Run tall, lean slightly from the ankles, land under your hips and keep your arms swinging forward and back.
what are good mountain bike drills | mtb skills practice | bike skills drills => Practice slow speed balance and track stands, braking hard in a straight line, cornering around cones, lifting the front wheel, and riding small obstacles in a parking lot or park.
what are good skateboard drills for beginners | skateboard practice tips | learning skateboard basics => Practice pushing and balance, then kick turns, tic tacs, manuals and stationary ollies on grass or carpet before trying them rolling.
how do i improve reaction time | reaction drills | faster reflexes in sports => Use reaction drills like ball drops, catching a dropped ruler, reacting to a partner's signals, and reaction light apps. Good sleep also speeds up reactions.
what are footwork ladder drills | agility ladder | speed ladder exercises => Agility ladder drills include one foot in each square, two feet in each, lateral shuffles and the Icky Shuffle. Focus on quick, light steps before going fast.
