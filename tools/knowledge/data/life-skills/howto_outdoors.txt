# tag: life.howto.knots
how do i tie a square knot | reef knot | right over left left over right => Cross the right end over the left and tuck it under, then cross the left end over the right and tuck it under. Both ends should come out on the same side, making a flat neat knot.
how do i tie a bowline | bowline knot steps | the rabbit comes out of the hole knot => Make a small loop in the rope, then pass the end up through the loop (the rabbit comes out of the hole), around behind the standing part (around the tree), and back down through the loop (back into the hole). Pull tight. It makes a loop that won't slip.
how do i tie a clove hitch | clove hitch knot | knot for tying rope to a pole => Wrap the rope around the post, cross over itself, wrap around again, then tuck the end under the second wrap so it comes out between the two wraps. Pull both ends tight.
how do i tie a figure eight knot | figure 8 knot | stopper knot => Make a loop, pass the end around behind the standing part, then down through the loop from the front. It looks like an 8 and stops a rope sliding through a hole.
how do i tie a taut line hitch | tent line knot | adjustable knot for tents => Wrap the rope end around a stake, then make two wraps around the standing line inside the loop, and one more wrap outside the loop, tucking it through. You can slide the knot to tighten the line, and it holds under load.
how do i tie a slip knot | slip knot how to | easy loop knot => Make a loop, reach through it and pull a bit of the rope through to make a new loop, then pull tight. Pulling the free end makes it come undone easily.
how do i tie two ropes together | knot to join two ropes | sheet bend knot => A sheet bend joins two ropes, even different thicknesses. Make a bend in the thicker rope, pass the thinner rope up through it, around behind both parts, and tuck it under itself.
what is the strongest knot | which knot should i use | best knot for climbing => There isn't one best knot, each has a job. Climbers use the figure eight follow through for tying in because it's strong and easy to check, but always learn climbing knots from a trained instructor.

# tag: life.howto.outdoors
how do i set up a tent | pitching a tent | tent setup for beginners => Pick a flat spot clear of rocks, lay down a ground sheet, spread the tent out, put the poles together and clip or thread them through, stake the corners at an angle, and add the rain fly. Practice at home first.
how do i pick a campsite | where should i put my tent | good camping spot => Choose flat, high ground so rain doesn't pool under you, away from dead branches overhead and at least 60 m (200 feet) from lakes and streams. Use established sites when there are some.
how do i stay warm while camping | cold at night in a tent | sleeping warm outdoors => Use a sleeping pad because the cold ground pulls heat away, wear a dry hat and dry socks, eat a snack before bed, and don't sleep in sweaty clothes.
what is leave no trace | leave no trace principles | camping rules => Leave No Trace means leaving nature as you found it: plan ahead, stay on trails, pack out all trash, leave plants and rocks, keep fires small and safe, respect wildlife and be considerate of others.
how do i read a compass | using a compass | which way is north on a compass => Hold the compass flat in front of you. The red end of the needle points to magnetic north. Turn your body until the needle lines up with the N on the dial, and now you know every direction.
how do i read a map | map reading for kids | what do map symbols mean => Find the legend or key to learn what symbols mean, use the scale to work out distances, and turn the map so north on it points north in real life. Find landmarks you can see to know where you are.
what are contour lines | how to read a topographic map | topo map lines => Contour lines connect points of the same height. Lines close together mean steep ground, and lines far apart mean gentle slopes. Circles inside circles show hills or mountains.
how do i find north without a compass | navigation without a compass | using the sun to find direction => The sun rises roughly in the east and sets roughly in the west. In the Northern Hemisphere at night, the North Star, Polaris, sits almost exactly over north and lines up with the two outer stars of the Big Dipper's cup.
what do i do if i get lost in the woods | lost while hiking | stuck on a trail => Stop, stay calm, and stay where you are so searchers can find you. Use a whistle, three blasts means help. Stay warm and dry, and if you have a phone with signal, call for help.
how do i stay safe on a hike | hiking safety tips | hiking rules for kids => Stay on the trail, hike with an adult or group, tell someone your plan, bring water, snacks, layers and a whistle, and turn back if the weather changes or it's getting late.
how do i avoid ticks | tick prevention | how to check for ticks => Wear long pants tucked into socks, use an insect repellent made for ticks, stay in the middle of trails, and check your whole body after being outside, including behind ears, armpits and hairline.
what do i do if i find a tick on me | how to remove a tick | tick bite => Use fine tipped tweezers to grab the tick close to the skin and pull straight up with steady pressure. Clean the spot with soap, and tell a parent so they can watch for a rash or fever.
how do i avoid poison ivy | what does poison ivy look like | leaves of three let it be => Poison ivy usually has three leaflets on each stem, and the middle one has a longer stalk. If you touch it, wash with soap and cool water soon, and wash clothes and gear too.
how do i stay safe around bears | what to do if you see a bear | bear safety => Keep food stored properly, make noise on trails, and never approach a bear. If you see one, stay calm, don't run, back away slowly while talking calmly and make yourself look big. Follow local park advice for your area.
what should i do if i see a snake | snake safety | is this snake dangerous => Stay calm, back away slowly and give it plenty of space. Most snakes only bite when stepped on or grabbed. If someone is bitten, keep them still and call 911.
how do i start a campfire safely | campfire safety | how to put out a campfire => Only make fires with an adult, in a fire ring where allowed. Keep water nearby, never leave it, and put it out by drowning it with water, stirring the ashes and drowning again until it's cold to the touch.
how do i purify water when camping | is stream water safe to drink | filter water outdoors => Water from streams and lakes can have germs even if it looks clean. Use a water filter, purification tablets, or boil it at a rolling boil for at least one minute.
how do i build a shelter in the woods | survival shelter | how to make a lean to => A simple lean to uses a long branch propped against a tree, with smaller sticks leaning on one side and leaves piled on top. Practice with an adult at a camp or backyard, not in an emergency first.
how do i skip a stone | skipping rocks | how to skip a rock on water => Pick a flat, smooth stone, crouch low, and throw it sidearm so it spins and hits the water at a shallow angle, around 20 degrees. The spin and low angle make it bounce.
how do i catch a fish | fishing for beginners | how do you fish in real life => Go with an adult and check if you need a fishing license. Bait your hook with a worm or lure, cast it out, wait for a tug, then lift the rod and reel in steadily. Wet your hands before touching fish you'll release.
how do i build a birdhouse | birdhouse tips | making a bird house => Use untreated wood, make the entrance hole the right size for the birds you want, add small drain holes in the bottom and vents near the top, and skip the perch, which helps predators. Mount it out of reach of cats.
how do i fly a kite | kite flying tips | how to get a kite in the air => Stand with your back to the wind, have a friend hold the kite a few meters away, then let it go as you pull the line gently. Let out line slowly as it climbs. Stay far from power lines.
how do i make a compost pile | composting for kids | what can go in compost => Mix green stuff like fruit and veggie scraps and grass with brown stuff like dry leaves and cardboard, keep it damp like a wrung out sponge, and turn it every week or two. No meat, dairy or oily food.
how do i plant seeds | growing plants from seeds | how deep do you plant seeds => Plant seeds about two to three times as deep as the seed is wide, keep the soil moist but not soggy, and give them light once they sprout. The seed packet tells you exact depth and spacing.
how do i grow vegetables | easy vegetables to grow | starting a garden => Easy starters include lettuce, radishes, beans, peas, zucchini and cherry tomatoes. Most vegetables need at least six hours of sun, good soil and regular watering.
how do i keep a cactus alive | cactus care | how often to water a succulent => Give it lots of light and water only when the soil is completely dry, often every one to three weeks. Overwatering is the most common way cacti and succulents die.
how do i grow herbs indoors | windowsill herb garden | growing basil inside => Put pots with drainage holes in your sunniest window, water when the top of the soil feels dry, and pinch off the tips to make them bushy. Basil, mint, chives and parsley are easy.
why are my plant leaves turning yellow | yellow leaves on houseplant | my plant is dying => Yellow leaves are often from overwatering, but can also mean too little light or needing food. Check that the pot drains and the soil isn't soggy, and move it somewhere brighter.
how do i stargaze | stargazing tips | what can i see in the night sky => Find a dark spot away from city lights, give your eyes about 20 minutes to adjust, and use a star map app. Planets don't twinkle much, and binoculars show the Moon's craters and Jupiter's moons.
how do i take care of a bike in winter | riding in the snow | winter bike tips => Wipe off salt and slush after rides, lube the chain more often, use lower tire pressure for grip, and wear warm gloves. Lights are important because it gets dark early.

# tag: life.howto.travel
how do i get through airport security | airport security for kids | what happens at tsa => Have your ticket ready, take liquids and big electronics out if asked, put your bag and jacket in the bins, then walk through the scanner when an officer tells you to.
how do i not get bored on a long flight | things to do on a plane | road trip activities => Download shows, games and music before you go since wifi may not work, bring a book, sketchbook and snacks, and get up to stretch when allowed.
why do my ears hurt on a plane | ear pain when flying | ears feel blocked after flying => Air pressure changes during takeoff and landing. Swallow, yawn, chew gum or sip a drink to help your ears equalize. Gently blowing with your nose pinched and mouth closed can help too.
how do i avoid car sickness | motion sickness tips | i feel sick in the car => Look out the front window at the horizon, avoid reading or screens, get fresh air, and eat a light snack before. Sitting in the middle or front seat if allowed helps.
how do i ride a city bus | taking the bus for the first time | how does public transit work => Check the route and schedule in a transit app, wait at the stop, check the sign on the bus, and pay or tap your card as you get on. Press the stop button or pull the cord before your stop.
what should i do if i get separated from my family at an airport or mall | lost in a store | lost my parents in a crowd => Stop and stay where you are or go to a meeting spot you agreed on. Ask a worker with a name tag, a police officer or a parent with kids for help, and tell them your parent's name and phone number.
how do i memorize my parents phone number | why memorize phone numbers | important numbers to know => Say it in chunks like a rhythm or song, write it a few times and practice dialing it. If you lose your phone, you'll still be able to call home.
how do i use a map app | google maps walking directions | apple maps tips => Type where you want to go, choose walking, biking, transit or driving, and follow the blue line and turn by turn directions. Download offline maps before trips where you may not have signal.
