# tag: life.cooking.tools
what is a ramekin | ramekin | small baking dish => A ramekin is a small, round, oven safe dish used for individual desserts like crème brûlée or soufflés, or for holding prepped ingredients.
what is a springform pan | springform | cheesecake pan => A springform pan has a removable bottom and a side ring with a latch, so you can release delicate cakes like cheesecake without flipping them over.
what is a muffin tin | muffin pan | cupcake liners => A muffin tin is a pan with cups, usually 12, for baking muffins and cupcakes. Paper liners or greasing keep them from sticking.
what is a loaf pan | loaf pan size | bread pan => A loaf pan is a deep rectangular pan for baking bread, banana bread and meatloaf. A common size is about 23 by 13 cm (9 by 5 in).
what is a pie dish | pie plate | glass vs metal pie dish => A pie dish is a shallow round dish with sloped sides. Glass lets you see if the bottom crust is browned, and metal heats faster.
what is a bench scraper | bench scraper | dough scraper => A bench scraper is a flat, rectangular blade with a handle used to cut dough, scrape counters clean and scoop chopped food into a pan.
what is a hand mixer | hand mixer | electric beaters => A hand mixer is a small electric mixer you hold, with two beaters. It's great for whipping cream and mixing cookie dough, but weaker than a stand mixer for heavy doughs.
what is a toaster oven | toaster oven | toaster oven vs oven => A toaster oven is a small countertop oven that can toast, bake and broil. It heats up faster than a big oven and is great for small batches.
what is a panini press | panini | sandwich press => A panini press is a hinged grill that presses and toasts sandwiches from both sides at once, leaving grill marks.
what is a griddle | griddle | griddle vs skillet => A griddle is a large, flat cooking surface, great for pancakes, eggs and grilled cheese since you can cook many at once.
what is a grill pan | grill pan | grill marks indoors => A grill pan is a stovetop pan with raised ridges that give food grill marks and let fat drip away from it.
what is a serrated knife | bread knife | serrated => A serrated knife has a saw like edge that cuts through crusty bread and soft tomatoes without squashing them.
what is the claw grip | claw grip | protect fingers cutting => The claw grip protects your fingers when cutting: curl your fingertips under and rest your knuckles against the side of the knife blade as you slice.
how do i chop herbs | chop herbs | chiffonade basil => Pull leaves off thick stems, bunch them together and rock a sharp knife over them. For basil, stack and roll the leaves, then slice thin ribbons.
how do i cut a carrot | cut carrots | carrot sticks => Peel it, cut off the ends, cut it into shorter pieces, then slice a thin strip off one side so it lies flat and won't roll before cutting sticks or coins.
how do i cut a potato into fries | cut fries | potato wedges => Cut a thin slice off one side so it sits flat, slice it into planks about 1 cm (1/2 in) thick, then cut each plank into sticks.
how do i juice a lemon | juice lemon | get more juice => Roll it firmly on the counter first, cut it in half crosswise and squeeze over your hand or a strainer to catch seeds. A citrus reamer gets more juice.
how do i crack an egg | crack an egg | egg shell in bowl => Tap the egg on a flat surface instead of a bowl edge, which pushes shell into the egg. Press your thumbs into the crack and pull the halves apart.
how many grams in a cup of flour | flour grams | cup flour weight => A cup of all purpose flour weighs about 120 to 125 grams when spooned and leveled. Scooping straight from the bag packs it heavier.
how many grams in a cup of sugar | sugar grams | cup sugar weight => A cup of white granulated sugar weighs about 200 grams. Packed brown sugar is a bit heavier, about 210 to 220 grams.
can i put foil in the air fryer | foil air fryer | parchment air fryer => Yes, but keep it under the food, weigh it down and don't cover the whole basket, or air can't circulate and the foil could fly into the heating element.
