# tag: life.safety.firstaid
how do i treat a minor burn | burned my finger | first aid for a burn => Cool the burn under cool (not icy) running water for about 20 minutes, remove rings or tight items nearby, then cover loosely with a clean non fluffy dressing or plastic wrap. Don't use ice, butter or toothpaste.
what are the degrees of burns | first second third degree burn | how bad is my burn => First degree burns are red and painful, like mild sunburn. Second degree burns blister. Third degree burns go deep and may look white or charred and feel numb. Big, deep, or face, hand or groin burns need emergency care.
what do i do if a tooth gets knocked out | knocked out tooth | tooth fell out from injury => For an adult tooth, hold it by the crown, not the root, rinse briefly if dirty, and try to put it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Get to a dentist within 30 to 60 minutes. Tell an adult right away.
what do i do for a bee sting | wasp sting treatment => Scrape the stinger out sideways with a fingernail or card, wash the area, and use a cold pack. Call 911 if the person has trouble breathing, swelling of the face or throat, or feels faint.
what do i do for a mosquito bite | stop itching bug bites | insect bite itch => Wash it, put on a cold pack or anti itch cream like calamine or hydrocortisone, and try not to scratch, which can cause infection.
what do i do if a spider bites me | spider bite | are spider bites dangerous => Most spider bites are harmless. Wash the area, use a cold pack and watch it. Get medical help for spreading redness, severe pain, cramps or feeling sick, and tell an adult.
what do i do if a snake bites someone | snake bite first aid | rattlesnake bite => Call 911, keep the person calm and still, keep the bite at about heart level, and remove tight items like rings. Don't cut the wound, suck out venom, or use ice or a tourniquet.
what do i do for poison ivy rash | poison ivy treatment | itchy rash from plant => Wash skin with soap and cool water as soon as possible, wash clothes and gear, and use calamine lotion or cool compresses. See a doctor if it's on your face, near your eyes or very widespread.
what do i do if i think a bone is broken | broken arm first aid => Signs include pain, swelling, bruising, a strange shape and not being able to use it. Keep it still, support it, use a cold pack wrapped in cloth, and get an adult and medical help. Call 911 if bone pokes through.
what do i do if someone gets an electric shock | electrocution first aid | someone touched a live wire => Don't touch them while they're still touching the power source. Turn off the power if you can safely, or use something dry and non metal like a wooden broom to move the source away. Call 911.
what do i do if someone has a fever | high fever in kids | when is a fever dangerous => A fever is a body temperature of 38 C (100.4 F) or higher, often from an infection. Rest and fluids help. Tell an adult, and get medical help for a very high fever, a stiff neck, trouble breathing or a fever lasting days.
what do i do if i feel dehydrated | dehydration symptoms => Signs include thirst, dark yellow pee, dry mouth, headache and dizziness. Drink water slowly, rest in a cool place, and get help if you're confused, can't keep fluids down or stop peeing.
what is heat stroke | heat stroke vs heat exhaustion => Heat stroke is an emergency where the body overheats, often above 40 C (104 F), with confusion, fainting or hot skin. Call 911 and cool the person quickly with cool water and shade. Heat exhaustion is milder.
what do i do if someone is having an allergic reaction to food | food allergy emergency | anaphylaxis signs => Signs of a severe reaction include trouble breathing, swelling of lips or throat, hives all over, vomiting or fainting. Use their epinephrine auto injector if they have one and call 911 right away.
what do i do if someone is vomiting | throwing up what to do | feeling sick to my stomach => Help them sit up or lean forward so they don't choke, then give small sips of water after a little while. Tell an adult. Get medical help if there's blood, it won't stop, or they seem very sick.
how do i get rid of hiccups | stop hiccups fast | hiccup cures => Try holding your breath for a bit, sipping cold water slowly, or breathing into cupped hands. Most hiccups stop on their own within minutes.
what do i do if i swallow something i shouldnt | swallowed a coin | swallowed a battery => Tell an adult immediately. Button batteries and small magnets are emergencies because they can burn or damage the gut, so go to the ER. For other things, call Poison Control or a doctor.
why are button batteries dangerous | button battery safety | coin battery swallowed => If swallowed, a button battery can burn through the throat or stomach in about 2 hours. Keep them away from little kids and pets, tape battery covers shut, and go to the ER right away if one is swallowed.
how do i help someone who is panicking | panic attack help | someone cant breathe from panic => Stay calm, speak slowly, help them sit down, and breathe slowly with them, like in for 4 counts and out for 6. Tell a trusted adult. If you're unsure whether it's a medical problem, call 911.
what is a tourniquet | when to use a tourniquet | stop the bleed => A tourniquet is a tight band placed above a wound on an arm or leg to stop life threatening bleeding. Stop the Bleed classes teach how. For most bleeding, firm direct pressure with a cloth works.
what is the first thing to do in an emergency | emergency steps | check call care => Check that the scene is safe, call 911 or get an adult, then care for the person if you can. Don't put yourself in danger.
how do i treat a jammed finger | jammed finger | finger hurts after ball hit => Ice it for 15 to 20 minutes and keep it raised. If it's very swollen, bent strangely or you can't straighten it, see a doctor, since it may be broken.
what do i do if i get a black eye | black eye treatment | hit in the eye => Use a cold pack wrapped in cloth for 15 to 20 minutes at a time, without pressing on the eye. Get medical help for vision changes, blood in the eye, or severe pain.
what do i do if chemicals get on my skin or eyes | chemical in eye | chemical burn first aid => Rinse with lots of lukewarm running water for at least 15 to 20 minutes and remove contaminated clothing. Call Poison Control or 911, and tell an adult.
what is a good first aid course for kids | first aid class for teens | where to learn first aid => The Red Cross and similar groups offer first aid, CPR and babysitting courses for kids and teens. Scouts and schools often run them too.
# tag: life.safety.disasters
what should i do after an earthquake | aftershocks | after an earthquake safety => Expect aftershocks. Check for injuries, watch for broken glass, and leave if the building is damaged. If you smell gas, leave and tell an adult. Near the coast, move to high ground in case of a tsunami.
how long is food safe in a power outage | fridge without power | freezer food after outage => A closed fridge keeps food safe for about 4 hours. A full closed freezer stays safe about 48 hours, a half full one about 24. When in doubt, throw it out.
what does aqi mean | air quality index | when is air quality bad => The Air Quality Index runs from 0 to 500. 0 to 50 is good, 101 to 150 is unhealthy for sensitive groups, and over 150 is unhealthy for everyone. On bad days, limit hard exercise outside.
what should i do when wildfire smoke is bad | smoky air => Stay indoors with windows closed, run an air purifier or HVAC with a good filter, avoid hard outdoor exercise, and wear an N95 mask if you must go out.
what is a severe thunderstorm warning | thunderstorm safety | what to do in a thunderstorm => A warning means a severe storm is happening or about to. Go indoors, stay away from windows, avoid using water like showers during lightning, and unplug electronics if you can.
what should i do if i am caught outside in lightning | lightning outside | no shelter in a storm => Get inside a building or hard topped car if at all possible. If you can't, avoid open fields, hilltops, tall lone trees and water, and keep moving toward shelter. Crouching is a last resort.
what should i do during hail | hail storm safety | hail outside => Get indoors, away from windows and skylights. If you're in a car, stay inside, cover your head, and stay away from windows.
what is a flash flood | flash flood safety | turn around dont drown => A flash flood is fast flooding that can happen within minutes of heavy rain. Never walk or drive through floodwater: just 15 cm (6 inches) of moving water can knock you down. Turn around, don't drown.
what is an avalanche | avalanche safety | what to do in an avalanche => An avalanche is a mass of snow sliding down a slope. Stay in marked areas at ski resorts. Backcountry travelers carry beacons, probes and shovels and take avalanche training.
what should i do during a volcano eruption | volcano safety | volcanic ash => Follow evacuation orders, stay away from lava, rivers and valleys, and stay inside with windows closed if ash falls. Wear a mask and goggles to protect from ash.
what should i do in extreme cold | wind chill safety | cold weather danger => Dress in layers, cover your head, hands and face, stay dry, and limit time outside. Shivering, numbness and pale or waxy skin are warnings to get warm.
what is a family emergency plan | emergency plan for family | where to meet in an emergency => Agree on a meeting spot near home and one outside your area, an out of town contact everyone can call, and how to reach each other. Practice it and keep a copy in your bag.
what is an emergency alert on my phone | wireless emergency alerts | amber alert => Phones get official alerts for extreme weather, AMBER alerts for missing children, and other emergencies. Read them carefully and follow the instructions.
gas leak | rotten egg smell at home => Leave the house right away, don't switch lights on or off or use anything that could spark, and once outside call 911 or the gas company. Natural gas has a rotten egg smell added on purpose.
# tag: life.safety.everyday
how do i stay safe in a crowd | crowd safety | crowd crush => Know where the exits are, agree on a meeting spot with your group, and stay near the edges. If a crowd starts pushing, move diagonally away from the pressure, and keep your arms up to protect your chest.
what should i do if i get lost at an amusement park | lost at a theme park | lost at a concert => Stay calm, find a staff member in uniform or a mom with kids, or go to a lost and found or guest services. Agree on a meeting spot before you split up.
how do i stay safe on halloween | trick or treat safety | halloween safety tips => Go with friends or an adult, carry a flashlight, wear reflective tape or light colors, cross streets at corners, only visit lit houses and never go inside, and check candy before eating.
how do i stay safe at a playground | playground safety | playground injuries => Use equipment for your age, check that surfaces are soft like wood chips or rubber, don't push on slides or ladders, and watch for hot metal in summer.
how do i safely approach a dog | can i pet that dog | dog safety for kids => Always ask the owner first, let the dog sniff your closed hand, and pet its back or side gently. Don't hug, chase or bother dogs while they eat or sleep.
what should i do if a dog chases me | dog running at me | aggressive dog => Don't run or scream. Stand still like a tree, keep your arms in and look away. If you get knocked down, curl into a ball and protect your face and neck.
what do i do if i see a wild animal | wild animal safety | animal acting strange => Keep your distance, never feed or touch it, and back away slowly. An animal acting strangely, like a raccoon out in daytime acting tame, could be sick, so tell an adult.
what do i do if i meet a mountain lion | cougar encounter | mountain lion safety => Don't run. Stand tall, make yourself look big, keep eye contact, back away slowly and make loud noise. If it attacks, fight back.
how do i protect my hearing | hearing safety => Keep headphone volume under about 60 percent and take breaks, follow the 60/60 rule (60 percent volume, 60 minutes at a time), and wear earplugs at concerts and around loud tools.
how do i protect my eyes | eye safety | safety glasses when needed => Wear safety glasses for building projects, sports goggles when needed, sunglasses with UV protection outside, and never look directly at the sun or a laser.
why shouldnt i point a laser at someone | laser pointer safety | laser in eyes => Laser pointers can damage eyes permanently, even in a moment. Never aim one at people, animals, cars or aircraft. Shining lasers at planes is a serious crime.
how do i choose sunscreen | what spf should i use | sunscreen tips => Use broad spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher, apply about a shot glass worth for your body 15 minutes before going out, and reapply every 2 hours and after swimming or sweating.
how do i use a ladder safely | ladder safety | climbing a ladder => Set it on firm, level ground, keep three points of contact (two hands and a foot, or two feet and a hand), don't stand on the top steps, and have someone hold it.
how do i stay safe with cleaning products | dangerous cleaning chemicals | mixing chemicals => Read labels, use products in a ventilated area, wear gloves, and never mix products. Bleach plus ammonia or acids makes toxic gas. Store chemicals in their original containers.
what do warning labels mean | danger warning caution | hazard symbols => On products, Danger means it can cause serious harm, Warning means moderate harm, and Caution means mild harm. Symbols like a skull, flame or corroded hand show the type of hazard.
how do i stay safe at a pool party | pool party rules | swimming party safety => Always have an adult watching who isn't distracted by a phone, walk don't run, don't dive in shallow ends, and stay out of the deep end if you're not a strong swimmer.
how do i stay safe boating | boating safety | life jacket on a boat => Wear a properly fitting life jacket the whole time, stay seated, keep hands and feet inside, and listen to the captain. Know the weather before you go.
how do i stay safe on a lawn mower | lawn mower safety | riding mower kids => Experts suggest kids be at least 12 to push a mower and 16 for a riding mower. Wear closed shoes and eye and ear protection, clear the lawn first, and never put hands or feet near the blade area.
how do i stay safe when meeting an online friend in person | meeting someone from online | meeting internet friend => Only with a parent's knowledge and okay, with a parent there, in a public place. People online aren't always who they say they are.
what should i do if someone is following me | being followed | someone following me home => Go to a busy public place like a store, don't go home, call a trusted adult or 911, and tell an employee. Trust your gut.
what is a safe place to go if i need help | safe places for kids | where to go if lost => Good choices include a police or fire station, library, school, a store with a cashier, or a trusted neighbor's home. Some businesses show a Safe Place sign.
how do i call for help without a phone | no phone emergency | get help without a phone => Shout for help, go to the nearest home or business, ask a store employee, or use an emergency call box. Many phones without a plan can still call 911.
what should i do if i see bullying happen | witness bullying | how to stop bullying as a bystander => Don't join in or cheer. If it's safe, say something like "That's not cool," help the person get away, and tell a trusted adult. Later, check in with the person who was bullied.
what is a medical id on iphone | emergency contacts on phone | medical id setup => In the Health app, Medical ID can show your allergies, conditions and emergency contacts on the lock screen, so first responders can see them. Set it up with a parent.
