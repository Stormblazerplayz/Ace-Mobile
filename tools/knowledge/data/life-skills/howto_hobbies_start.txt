# tag: life.howto.hobbies
how do i start collecting stamps | stamp collecting | philately => Save stamps from mail, soak them off envelopes in water, dry them flat and keep them in an album. Many collectors pick a theme, like animals, space or one country.
how do i do a coin trick | coin vanish | coin magic => A simple vanish: pretend to take a coin from one hand with the other, but secretly let it stay in the first hand. Then open the empty hand. Practice in a mirror until it looks natural.
how do i get faster at the rubik's cube | speedcubing | cfop => Learn finger tricks, look ahead to the next step while turning, and move from the beginner method to a speed method like CFOP. Practice with a timer and a smooth speed cube.
how do i start model rocketry | model rockets | estes rocket => Start with a beginner kit and engines rated for small rockets, launch in a big open field away from trees and power lines, and follow safety codes with an adult. Clubs often host launches.
how do i start building scale models | scale models | model kits => Begin with a beginner snap together kit, then try glue kits. Get a hobby knife, sanding sticks and cement, and paint parts before assembly when possible.
how do i start lego building | lego moc | build your own lego => Sort bricks by color or type, follow a few sets to learn techniques, then try building your own creations, called MOCs. Online communities share ideas and instructions.
how do i start woodworking | woodworking for kids | beginner woodworking => Learn with an adult, start with hand tools like a saw, hammer and sandpaper, wear safety glasses, and build simple projects like a box or birdhouse first.
how do i start metal detecting | metal detecting | metal detector kids => Start with a beginner detector, practice in your yard, and always get permission before searching on private land. Many parks and beaches have rules, so check first.
how do i start parkour | parkour basics | parkour safety => Begin with basics like safe landings, rolls and balance on low objects, near the ground. A parkour gym or coach is the safest way to learn bigger moves.
how do i start fencing | fencing for kids | fencing club => Find a fencing club with beginner classes. Clubs usually lend gear like masks, jackets and weapons at first, and most kids start with foil.
how do i start archery | archery for kids | archery range => Take a class at an archery range where coaches teach safety and form and lend equipment. Never shoot without supervision.
how do i start running | start running | couch to 5k => Start with short run walk intervals, like 1 minute running and 2 minutes walking, three times a week. Build up slowly, and wear good running shoes.
how do i start golf | golf for beginners | learn golf => Try a driving range or putting green, take a beginner lesson or junior clinic, and use junior clubs sized for you. Par 3 courses are great for first rounds.
how do i start tennis | tennis for beginners | learn tennis => Take a beginner class or play with a friend on a public court. Start with slower, low pressure balls, which bounce lower and are easier to control.
how do i start bowling | bowling beginners | bowling league kids => Pick a ball you can control, use bumpers at first if you need them, and focus on rolling straight. Many bowling alleys have youth leagues.
how do i start a blog | start a blog | blogging kids => With a parent's okay, pick a topic you love, use a kid friendly or private blogging platform, and post regularly. Never share personal details like your address or school.
how do i start a newsletter | newsletter | email newsletter => Pick a topic and a schedule, write short, fun issues, and send them to family and friends who sign up. Ask a parent before using any email service.
how do i start writing a novel | write a novel | novel for teens => Start with a character who wants something and a problem in the way. Outline the main events or just start writing, set a small daily word goal and finish a draft before editing.
how do i start learning sign language | sign language | asl basics => Learn the alphabet and common signs with videos or apps, practice daily, and if possible learn from Deaf teachers or community events. Each country has its own sign language.
how do i learn morse code | morse code | sos morse => Learn the letters by their sounds, not by reading dots and dashes. Start with common letters like E (.) and T (-), and SOS is ... --- ... Apps and practice tools help.
how do i learn the phonetic alphabet | nato alphabet | alpha bravo charlie => The NATO alphabet gives each letter a word: Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot and so on to Zulu. Practice spelling your name with it.
how do i grow a sunflower | grow sunflowers | giant sunflowers => Plant seeds about 2.5 cm (1 in) deep in full sun after the last frost, water regularly and support tall ones with a stake. Many bloom in 2 to 3 months.
how do i make a bouquet | flower bouquet | arrange flowers => Pick a few main flowers, add filler flowers and greenery, cut stems at an angle to similar lengths, and tie them together. Put them in water right away.
how do i identify trees | tree identification | leaf identification => Look at the leaf shape, how leaves are arranged on twigs, the bark, and any fruit, nuts or cones. Field guides and plant ID apps help.
how do i identify birds by sound | bird calls | bird song app => Learn a few common birds' songs first, use a bird sound app that listens and suggests matches, and repeat the songs in your head with memory phrases.
how do i identify bugs | insect identification | bug id => Count legs and wings, and note color, size and where you found it. Field guides and insect ID apps help. Don't touch bugs you can't identify.
how do i photograph the moon | moon photo | phone moon picture => On a phone, tap the moon and lower the exposure until you see detail, zoom carefully and steady the phone on something. A tripod and telephoto lens help even more.
how do i press leaves | pressed leaves | preserve leaves => Place leaves between sheets of paper inside a heavy book, add more books on top and wait a week or two until they're flat and dry.
how do i make a pinecone craft | pinecone crafts | pinecones => Collect dry pinecones and turn them into bird feeders, ornaments, owls or mini trees with paint, glue, glitter and pom poms.
how do i make a fairy garden | fairy garden | miniature garden => Use a pot or a corner of the garden, add soil, tiny plants, moss and pebble paths, and decorate with small houses and figures made from twigs and stones.
how do i find water in the wild | find water survival | survival water => Follow valleys and low ground, listen for running water, and look for green plants and animal trails. Always boil, filter or purify water before drinking.
how do i signal for help in the wild | signal for help | sos signal => Blow a whistle three times, use a mirror to flash sunlight at planes or searchers, wear or spread bright colors, and make a big X or SOS on the ground. Stay put.
how do i make a fire without matches | fire without matches | ferro rod => With an adult, a ferro rod (fire steel) scraped with its striker throws hot sparks onto dry tinder. Only make fires where it's allowed and safe.
how do i navigate by the stars | star navigation | celestial navigation => In the Northern Hemisphere, find the Big Dipper and follow its two pointer stars to Polaris, the North Star, which marks north. In the south, the Southern Cross helps find south.
how do i use a gps device | handheld gps | gps hiking => Turn it on outside so it finds satellites, mark your starting point as a waypoint, and follow the map or arrow to your destination. Carry a paper map in case the battery dies.
