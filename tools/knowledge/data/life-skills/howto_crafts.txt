best paper airplane design | paper plane that flies far => For a classic dart, fold a sheet in half lengthwise, fold the top corners to the center line, fold the new slanted edges to the center again, fold in half, then fold each side down to make wings. Keep it symmetrical.

Make forward knots with the left strand across each strand in turn, then repeat with the next strand to create diagonal stripes.

how do i crochet | crochet for beginners | how to start crocheting => Start with a slip knot and a chain of stitches, then learn single crochet. Use a medium yarn and a 5 mm (H) hook. Granny squares and coasters are good first projects.
what is the difference between knitting and crochet | knit vs crochet | is crochet easier => Knitting uses two needles and keeps many loops live at once. Crochet uses one hook and works one loop at a time, which many beginners find easier to fix.
how do i tie dye a shirt | tie dye patterns | how to do tie dye => Wash a cotton shirt, twist or fold it and wrap with rubber bands, squirt dye on the sections, wrap it in plastic and let it sit 6 to 24 hours. Rinse in cold water until clear, then wash separately. Wear gloves.

how do i work with polymer clay | sculpey clay | how to bake polymer clay => Knead the clay until soft, sculpt your design, then bake it in the oven at the temperature on the package, usually around 110 to 135 C (230 to 275 F). It hardens permanently.
what is air dry clay | does air dry clay need baking => Air dry clay hardens by drying in the air over 24 to 72 hours, with no oven needed. It's more fragile and not waterproof, so seal it with varnish. Polymer clay needs baking and is stronger.
