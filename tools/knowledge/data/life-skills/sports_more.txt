# tag: sports.winter
what do green blue and black ski runs mean | ski trail colors | double black diamond => In North America, a green circle is easiest, a blue square is intermediate, a black diamond is advanced, and a double black diamond is expert. Europe uses green, blue, red and black.
what is a pizza and french fries in skiing | snowplow skiing | how do beginners stop on skis => Pizza, or snowplow, means pointing your ski tips together in a wedge to slow down and stop. French fries means skis parallel, which goes faster. Beginners learn the wedge first.
how do i get on a chairlift | riding a ski lift | chairlift tips => Wait at the loading line, look back as the chair comes, sit down, and lower the safety bar once seated. To get off, raise the bar near the top, keep tips up, stand and glide away.
what should i wear skiing | ski clothes | how to dress for snowboarding => Wear layers: a moisture wicking base layer, a warm middle layer, and a waterproof jacket and pants. Add a helmet, goggles, gloves and ski socks. Avoid cotton, which stays wet.
what is a ski helmet for | should i wear a helmet skiing | helmet snowboarding => Helmets greatly reduce head injuries in falls and collisions on the slopes. Most skiers and snowboarders wear them now, and many ski schools require them for kids.
what is the skier responsibility code | ski slope rules | who has right of way skiing => Stay in control, give way to people ahead of you downhill, stop only where you're visible, look uphill before starting, use devices to stop runaway equipment, and obey signs.
what is moguls skiing | mogul bumps | freestyle skiing events => Moguls are bumps on a slope. In freestyle mogul skiing, athletes ski fast through bumps and do two jumps, scored on turns, air and speed.
what is slalom skiing | slalom vs giant slalom | alpine skiing events => Alpine events include slalom (tight gates, quick turns), giant slalom (wider turns), super G, and downhill (the fastest, over 130 km/h at the top level).
what is cross country skiing | nordic skiing | classic vs skate skiing => Cross country skiing means traveling over mostly flat or rolling snow using your own power. Classic style glides in tracks, while skate skiing pushes off sideways like ice skating.
what is ski jumping | how far do ski jumpers fly | ski flying record => Ski jumpers speed down a ramp and fly, scored on distance and style. On the biggest ski flying hills, jumps can pass 250 m.
what is a halfpipe | halfpipe snowboarding | superpipe => A halfpipe is a U shaped snow ramp where snowboarders and skiers ride wall to wall doing tricks in the air. Olympic superpipes have walls about 6.7 m (22 ft) high.
what is slopestyle | slopestyle vs big air | snowboard events => Slopestyle is a course with rails, boxes and jumps where riders do tricks in a row. Big air is one huge jump with one trick per run.
what is regular or goofy on a snowboard | snowboard stance | which foot forward snowboard => Regular means your left foot is in front, goofy means your right foot is. Try sliding in socks on a smooth floor and notice which foot leads.
how do i stop on a snowboard | snowboard falling leaf | snowboard heel edge => Beginners use the heel edge: shift weight to your heels and lift your toes to dig the uphill edge in. The falling leaf drill teaches control by sliding side to side on one edge.
what are figure skating jumps | axel lutz flip salchow | hardest figure skating jump => The six main jumps are the toe loop, salchow, loop, flip, lutz and axel. The axel is hardest because it takes off forward and has an extra half turn.
what is a quad axel | first quad axel | ilia malinin => A quadruple axel has four and a half turns in the air. American Ilia Malinin landed the first one in competition in 2022.
what is speed skating | long track vs short track | speed skating speed => Long track speed skaters race in pairs on a 400 m oval. Short track skaters race in packs on a 111 m track with lots of passing and crashes. Top speeds reach about 60 km/h.
how do i sled safely | sledding safety | safe sledding tips => Sled feet first sitting up, on hills away from roads, trees, fences and water, with a flat area to stop. Never sled on a hill that ends near traffic. A helmet adds protection.
what is snowshoeing | how do snowshoes work | snowshoe hiking => Snowshoes spread your weight over a larger area so you don't sink deep into snow. Snowshoeing is like hiking in winter and easy to learn.
what is ice fishing | how does ice fishing work => In ice fishing, people drill a hole in frozen lakes and fish through it. Experts generally say ice should be at least 4 inches (10 cm) of clear ice to walk on, and only with an adult.
# tag: sports.cycling
what do the tour de france jerseys mean | yellow jersey meaning | polka dot jersey => In the Tour de France, yellow is the overall leader, green is the points (sprint) leader, polka dot is the best climber, and white is the best young rider under 26.
how long is the tour de france | how many stages tour de france | tour de france distance => The Tour de France has 21 stages over about 23 days, covering around 3,300 to 3,500 km, with two rest days. It ends in Paris.
what are the three grand tours | giro vuelta tour | grand tours cycling => Cycling's three Grand Tours are the Giro d'Italia (May), the Tour de France (July) and the Vuelta a Espana (late August to September). Each lasts three weeks.
what is the tour de france | tour de france explained | famous bike race in france => The Tour de France is the world's most famous bike race, held every July since 1903 (except during the World Wars). Riders race in teams, and the rider with the lowest total time wins.
who won the 2025 tour de france | tour de france 2025 winner | tadej pogacar tour wins => As of 2025, Tadej Pogacar of Slovenia won the 2025 Tour de France, his fourth title after 2020, 2021 and 2024.
what is drafting in cycling | why do cyclists ride in a line | peloton meaning => Drafting means riding close behind another rider to save energy, up to about 30 percent, because they block the wind. The big main group of riders is called the peloton.
what is a breakaway in cycling | cycling breakaway | domestique meaning => A breakaway is a small group that rides ahead of the peloton. A domestique is a team rider who works for the leader by fetching water, blocking wind and chasing breaks.
what is a velodrome | track cycling | banked cycling track => A velodrome is an oval track with steeply banked turns for track cycling. Olympic tracks are 250 m around, and track bikes have one fixed gear and no brakes.
what is the hour record | cycling hour record | how far in an hour on a bike => The hour record is the farthest a cyclist can ride on a track in one hour. As of 2025, the men's record is 56.792 km by Filippo Ganna (2022).
what is a road bike | road bike vs mountain bike | road bike features => Road bikes are lightweight with skinny tires and drop handlebars for speed on pavement. Mountain bikes have wider knobby tires, flat bars and suspension for trails.
what is cyclocross | cyclocross racing | cx bike => Cyclocross is racing on short laps through grass, mud, sand and obstacles, where riders sometimes jump off and carry the bike. It's mostly raced in fall and winter.
what is bmx racing | bmx racing vs freestyle | olympic bmx => BMX racing has up to eight riders on a dirt track with jumps and banked turns, starting from a gate on a ramp. BMX freestyle is about tricks in a park and is judged.
how do i ride a bike in traffic | bike road rules | cycling on roads safety => Ride on the right in the same direction as cars, obey traffic lights and signs, use hand signals, be visible with lights and bright clothes, and watch for car doors opening.
what is cadence in cycling | how fast should i pedal | rpm cycling => Cadence is how many times your pedals go around per minute. Many cyclists aim for about 80 to 95 rpm, using gears to keep a steady, comfortable spin.
what is a century ride | how long is a century bike ride | metric century => A century ride is 100 miles (161 km) in one day. A metric century is 100 km (62 miles).
# tag: sports.combat
what is judo | judo rules | how do you win in judo => Judo is a Japanese martial art and Olympic sport focused on throws and holds. A perfect throw that lands an opponent on their back scores ippon and wins instantly.
what is taekwondo | taekwondo kicks | taekwondo olympics => Taekwondo is a Korean martial art known for fast, high kicks. In Olympic taekwondo, fighters score points with kicks and punches to the body protector and kicks to the head.
what is brazilian jiu jitsu | bjj meaning | jiu jitsu belts => Brazilian jiu jitsu is a grappling martial art focused on ground fighting, holds and submissions. Adult belts go white, blue, purple, brown, black, and promotions take years.
what is kung fu | kung fu styles | shaolin kung fu => Kung fu is a broad term for many Chinese martial arts, like Shaolin, Wing Chun and Tai Chi. Wushu is the modern sport version with forms and sparring.
what is tai chi | tai chi benefits | slow martial art => Tai chi is a Chinese martial art practiced as slow, flowing movements. It's popular for balance, flexibility and calm, especially among older adults.
what is mma | mixed martial arts | ufc meaning => MMA, or mixed martial arts, combines striking and grappling from different fighting styles. The UFC is the largest MMA organization. It's an adult sport with real injury risks.
what are boxing weight classes | heavyweight meaning | boxing divisions => Boxers compete against others of similar weight, in classes from minimumweight to heavyweight. Pro boxing has 17 main divisions, and heavyweight has no upper limit.
what is a knockout in boxing | ko vs tko | technical knockout => A knockout (KO) is when a boxer is knocked down and can't get up by the count of 10. A technical knockout (TKO) is when the referee stops the fight because a boxer can't safely continue.
what is sumo wrestling | sumo rules | how do you win in sumo => Sumo is Japanese wrestling where you win by forcing your opponent out of the circle, called the dohyo, or making any part of their body other than their feet touch the ground.
what is kickboxing | kickboxing vs muay thai | muay thai meaning => Kickboxing combines punches and kicks. Muay Thai, from Thailand, is called the art of eight limbs because it also uses elbows and knees.
what martial art is best for kids | should i do karate or taekwondo | starting martial arts => The best martial art is one with a good, safe instructor near you. Karate, taekwondo, judo and jiu jitsu all teach discipline, fitness and confidence. Try a free class first.
is martial arts good for self defense | can martial arts help bullying | learning self defense => Martial arts build confidence and awareness, which helps you avoid fights. Self defense mostly means staying aware, walking away and getting help. Fighting is a last resort.
what is a dojo | dojo etiquette | bowing in martial arts => A dojo is a training hall for Japanese martial arts. Students usually bow when entering and to their teacher and partners as a sign of respect, and keep the mat clean.
what is a sensei | sensei meaning | martial arts teacher title => Sensei is the Japanese word for teacher, used in karate, judo and other martial arts. Taekwondo teachers are often called sabeomnim, or master.
# tag: sports.water
what is rowing | rowing vs kayaking | crew sport => Rowing uses oars attached to the boat, and rowers face backward. In kayaking and canoeing, paddlers face forward and hold a paddle that isn't attached.
what is a coxswain | what does the cox do in rowing | rowing coxswain => The coxswain, or cox, sits in the boat facing forward, steers, calls out the rhythm and strategy, and motivates the rowers. They don't row.
how do i paddle a kayak | kayaking for beginners | kayak paddling technique => Hold the paddle with hands a bit wider than your shoulders and knuckles lined up with the blade edges. Twist your body to pull each blade from your feet back to your hips. Always wear a life jacket.
what is stand up paddleboarding | sup meaning | how to paddleboard => Stand up paddleboarding (SUP) means standing on a big board and paddling. Start kneeling, stand with feet shoulder width apart in the middle, keep knees bent and look ahead. Wear a leash and life jacket.
what is sailing | parts of a sailboat | port and starboard => Sailing uses wind on sails to move a boat. Port is the left side and starboard is the right side when facing the front (bow). The back is the stern.
what is the americas cup | sailing race americas cup | oldest international sports trophy => The America's Cup is a famous sailing race first won in 1851. It's often called the oldest international sports trophy.
what is water skiing | wakeboarding vs water skiing | how to water ski => Water skiing and wakeboarding mean being pulled behind a boat on skis or a board. Start in the water with knees bent and arms straight, and let the boat pull you up.
what is white water rafting | rafting rapids classes | river rapid ratings => White water rafting means riding river rapids in an inflatable raft. Rapids are rated Class I (easy) to Class VI (extremely dangerous). Kids usually start on Class I to III with a guide.
# tag: sports.other
what is the kitchen in pickleball | non volley zone | pickleball kitchen rule => The kitchen is the 7 foot area on each side of the pickleball net. You can't hit the ball out of the air while standing in it, but you can enter to hit a ball that has bounced.
how is pickleball scored | pickleball scoring | pickleball to 11 => Games usually go to 11, win by 2, and only the serving team scores. In doubles, the score is called as three numbers: serving team score, receiving team score and server number.
how do you play table tennis | table tennis scoring => Games go to 11, win by 2, and players switch serve every 2 points. The serve must bounce once on each side, and you hit after the ball bounces once on your side.
what is the fastest ball sport | fastest ball speed in sports | jai alai speed => Badminton shuttlecocks reach the highest speeds, over 500 km/h on smashes. Among balls, jai alai and golf drives are among the fastest, with jai alai balls passing 300 km/h.
what is cheerleading | is cheer a sport | competitive cheer => Competitive cheerleading combines stunts, tumbling, jumps and dance in a timed routine judged by a panel. It requires a lot of strength and has a real injury risk, so good coaching matters.
what is softball | how is softball different => Softball uses a bigger ball, a smaller field and underhand pitching. Fastpitch games are usually 7 innings, compared with 9 in baseball.
what is australian rules football | aussie rules | afl meaning => Australian rules football is played on a big oval field with 18 players per side. Players kick the ball through posts for points, and the main league is the AFL.
what is rugby league vs rugby union | difference between rugby league and union | rugby 13 vs 15 => Rugby union has 15 players per side and rucks and lineouts. Rugby league has 13 players and a limit of six tackles before the ball is handed over.
what is sevens rugby | rugby sevens | olympic rugby => Rugby sevens has 7 players per side and 7 minute halves on a full size field, so it's fast and high scoring. It has been an Olympic sport since 2016.
what is hurling | irish sport hurling | fastest field sport => Hurling is an ancient Irish sport played with a wooden stick called a hurley and a small ball called a sliotar. It's known as one of the fastest field sports.
what is polo | how is polo played | water polo vs polo => Polo is played on horseback, with riders hitting a ball with long mallets into goals. Games are split into periods called chukkas.
what is equestrian | horse riding sport | olympic horse events => Equestrian sports include dressage (precise movements), show jumping (jumping fences) and eventing (a mix including cross country). In the Olympics, men and women compete together.
what is motocross | dirt bike racing | supercross vs motocross => Motocross is off road motorcycle racing on outdoor dirt tracks with jumps. Supercross is similar but held on tighter man made tracks inside stadiums.
what is a dirt bike | can kids ride dirt bikes | youth dirt bikes => A dirt bike is an off road motorcycle. Kids can ride youth sized bikes on private land or tracks with full protective gear, adult supervision and training.
what is go kart racing | karting | how f1 drivers start => Karting is racing small open wheel karts on tracks. Most Formula 1 drivers started in karting as kids.
what is darts scoring | how do you play darts | bullseye points => A dartboard has 20 numbered sections. The thin outer ring doubles a score and the inner ring triples it. The outer bull is 25 and the bullseye is 50. In 501, players count down to exactly zero, finishing on a double.
how do you play pool | 8 ball rules | billiards basics => In 8 ball, one player takes solids (1 to 7) and the other stripes (9 to 15). Sink all yours, then the 8 ball in a called pocket. Sinking the 8 too early loses the game.
what is snooker | snooker vs pool | snooker scoring => Snooker uses a bigger table, 15 reds and six colored balls. Players alternate potting a red (1 point) and a color (2 to 7 points). A perfect break is 147.
how do you play cornhole | cornhole scoring | cornhole rules => Players toss bean bags at a board with a hole. A bag in the hole scores 3 and a bag on the board scores 1. Points cancel out each round, and the first to 21 wins.
how do you play spikeball | roundnet rules | spikeball scoring => In spikeball (roundnet), two teams of two hit a ball off a small round net. Each team gets up to three touches, and you score when the other team can't return it. Games usually go to 21.
how do you play tetherball | tetherball rules | tetherball tips => Two players hit a ball on a rope tied to a pole in opposite directions. You win by wrapping the rope all the way around the pole. You can't touch the rope or pole.
how do you play four square | four square rules | 4 square => Four players stand in four squares and bounce a ball into each other's squares. If you miss or hit it out, you move to the lowest square and a new player comes in.
what is capture the flag | capture the flag rules | how to win capture the flag => Two teams each hide a flag on their side. Players try to grab the other team's flag and bring it back without getting tagged. Tagged players go to jail until a teammate frees them.
what is the fastest sport in the world | most dangerous sport | toughest sport => People argue about this, but motorsports and downhill speed sports are among the fastest. Sports like rugby, boxing and motocross have high injury rates.
what are the most popular sports in the world | most played sport | most watched sport => Soccer is the world's most popular sport, with billions of fans. Cricket, basketball, field hockey, tennis and volleyball also have huge followings.
