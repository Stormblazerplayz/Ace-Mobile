what is offside in soccer | offside rule explained | how does offside work => A player is offside if, when a teammate passes the ball to them, they're in the opponent's half and closer to the goal line than both the ball and the second to last defender, and they get involved in play. Being level is onside, and you can't be offside from a throw in, goal kick or corner.
what is a yellow card | yellow card meaning | what gets you a yellow card => A yellow card is a caution for things like reckless fouls, unsporting behavior, arguing with the referee or wasting time. Two yellows in one game equal a red card.
what is a red card | red card in soccer | what happens when you get a red card => A red card sends a player off for serious foul play, violent conduct, denying an obvious goal scoring chance or a second yellow. The team plays one player short and the player usually misses the next game too.
what is a penalty kick | when do you get a penalty | penalty rules soccer => A penalty kick is given when the defending team commits a foul or handball inside its own penalty area. It's a shot from the penalty spot with only the goalkeeper to beat, who must have at least part of one foot on the goal line.
what is a direct free kick | direct vs indirect free kick | how does an indirect free kick work => A direct free kick can be scored straight into the goal. An indirect free kick, shown by the referee's raised arm, must touch another player before going in. Opponents must stand at least 10 yards (9.15 m) away.
what is a corner kick | when do you get a corner | corner kick rules => A corner kick is awarded when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses their own goal line without a goal. The attackers kick it from the corner arc, and you can score directly from a corner.
what is a goal kick | goal kick rules | when is it a goal kick => When the attacking team last touches the ball before it crosses the goal line without a goal, the defending team gets a goal kick from anywhere in its goal area.
how do you do a throw in | throw in rules | foul throw soccer => Use both hands, take the ball from behind and over your head, face the field, and keep part of both feet on the ground on or behind the sideline. Breaking those rules gives the throw to the other team.
what is a handball in soccer | when is it handball | can you touch the ball with your arm => It's a handball if a player deliberately touches the ball with their hand or arm, or makes their body unnaturally bigger with their arm. Accidental touches close to the body are often allowed, but a handball that directly leads to a goal disallows it.
what are the positions in soccer | soccer positions explained | what does a midfielder do => The main positions are goalkeeper, defenders (center backs and full backs), midfielders (defensive, central and attacking) and forwards (wingers and strikers). Midfielders connect defense and attack and cover the most ground.
what does a striker do | striker vs winger | center forward role => A striker plays at the front and mainly scores goals. Wingers play wide, beat defenders and cross or cut inside to shoot.
what does a goalkeeper do | goalie rules soccer | can the goalie pick up a back pass => The goalkeeper guards the goal and is the only player who can use hands, but only inside their own penalty area. They can't pick up a ball a teammate deliberately kicked back to them, or a throw in from a teammate.
what is a formation in soccer | 4 4 2 vs 4 3 3 | what does 4 3 3 mean => A formation shows how players line up, counting defenders, then midfielders, then forwards, not counting the goalie. 4-3-3 means four defenders, three midfielders and three forwards.
how big is a soccer goal | soccer goal size | how tall is a soccer net => A full size soccer goal is 8 yards wide and 8 feet tall (7.32 m by 2.44 m). Youth goals are smaller.
what size soccer ball should i use | soccer ball sizes | size 4 vs size 5 => Size 5 is for ages 12 and up and adults, size 4 is for about ages 8 to 12, and size 3 for younger kids. Check your league's rules.
what is extra time in soccer | what happens if a soccer game is tied | penalty shootout rules => In knockout games tied after 90 minutes, teams usually play two 15 minute halves of extra time. If still tied, a penalty shootout of five kicks each decides it, then sudden death.
what is stoppage time | added time in soccer | injury time meaning => Stoppage time is extra minutes the referee adds at the end of each half for time lost to injuries, substitutions, goal celebrations and delays. The fourth official shows the minimum added time on a board.
how many substitutions in soccer | soccer subs rules | can players come back in after a sub => Most top competitions allow 5 substitutions per game in up to three stoppages, plus extra in extra time and for concussions. Unlike youth leagues, players who come off can't return.

what is a perfect hat trick | perfect hat trick meaning | left foot right foot header hat trick => A perfect hat trick is one with the left foot, one with the right foot and one header.

how do i shoot harder in soccer | soccer shooting technique | kick with laces => Plant your non kicking foot beside the ball pointing at the target, lock your ankle with toes pointed down, strike the center of the ball with your laces and follow through. Lean over the ball to keep it low.
how do i pass better in soccer | soccer passing tips | inside foot pass => For short passes, use the inside of your foot, lock your ankle, plant your other foot beside the ball and follow through toward your target. Look up before you receive the ball so you know where to pass.
how do i dribble better in soccer | soccer dribbling drills | close control tips => Keep the ball close with small touches using both feet and all parts of the foot, keep your head up and change speed and direction. Cone drills and juggling build touch.

how do i get better at soccer | soccer practice at home | improve soccer skills => Touch the ball every day with juggling and wall passes, practice with your weaker foot, work on fitness, watch good players, and play small sided games where you get lots of touches.
