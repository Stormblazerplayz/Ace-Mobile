# tag: sports.mtb
what is a tabletop jump | tabletop vs double | mtb jump types => A tabletop has a flat top connecting the takeoff and landing, so coming up short is safe. A double has a gap between takeoff and landing, so you must clear it. Learn on tabletops first.
what is a step up and step down jump | step up jump | step down jump => A step up lands higher than the takeoff, so it needs less speed than you'd think. A step down lands lower, so you'll fly farther. Both need scouting before you hit them.
what is a gap jump | gap jump mtb | when am i ready for gaps => A gap jump has open space between takeoff and landing that you must clear. Be consistent on tabletops of the same size and know your speed before trying gaps.
what are rollers on a trail | pumping rollers | how to ride rollers => Rollers are smooth bumps in a row. Instead of pedaling, pump them: push the bike down into the backsides and let it come up on the faces to gain speed.
what is a skinny on a trail | skinny bridge mtb | riding narrow features => A skinny is a narrow log or wooden plank you ride along. Look at the end, not your front wheel, keep steady speed, and stay relaxed.
what is a wall ride | wallride mtb | wall ride technique => A wall ride is riding across a steep or vertical wooden wall. Approach with speed and a slight upward angle, keep your weight centered, and turn off the wall by looking where you want to go.
what is a flow trail | flow trail vs tech trail | what is technical trail => A flow trail is built with smooth berms, rollers and jumps that let you carry speed. A technical trail has rocks, roots and steep, tricky sections that need precise handling.
what is a rock garden | riding rock gardens | rocky trail tips => A rock garden is a section full of rocks. Look ahead for a line, keep some speed, stay off the brakes in the roughest bits, stand in a loose attack position, and let the bike move under you.
how do i ride steep descents | riding steeps mtb | steep trail technique => Lower your saddle, get your hips back but stay low, heels down, look far ahead, and brake smoothly with more front brake before the steep part and gentle control on it. Don't lock the front wheel.
how do i wheelie on a mountain bike | mtb wheelie | learn to wheelie => In an easy gear, pedal hard from a slow roll while leaning back, with a finger covering the rear brake to stop going over backward. Balance side to side with your knees. Practice on grass or a gentle uphill.
how do i get better at cornering on flat turns | flat corner technique | unbermed turns => Brake before the turn, lean the bike more than your body, drop your outside pedal and press weight into it, point your hips and chest toward the exit, and look through the corner.
what are clipless pedal tips | learning clipless | falling with clipless pedals => Practice clipping in and out on grass first, set the release tension low, unclip before you stop, and twist your heel outward to release. Almost everyone tips over once.
what is bike geometry | head tube angle meaning | slack vs steep bike => Geometry is a bike's frame angles and lengths. A slacker head angle (lower number, like 64 degrees) is more stable downhill, while a steeper one (like 69 degrees) steers quicker for climbing and XC.
what is reach on a bike | reach and stack | bike fit reach => Reach is the horizontal distance from the bottom bracket to the top of the head tube. It mostly decides how stretched out you feel standing. Stack is the vertical distance.
what is a mullet bike | mixed wheel mtb | 29 front 27.5 rear => A mullet bike has a 29 inch front wheel and a 27.5 inch rear wheel. The big front rolls over obstacles, and the smaller rear gives more clearance and quicker turning.
what is compression damping | high speed vs low speed compression | suspension compression => Compression damping controls how fast suspension compresses. Low speed compression affects pedaling, braking and body movement, while high speed handles sharp hits like rocks and landings.
what is a lockout on a fork | suspension lockout | when to lock out suspension => A lockout firms up the suspension so it barely moves, saving energy on smooth climbs and roads. Unlock it before descending or rough terrain.
how often should i service my suspension | fork service | lower leg service => Many makers suggest a lower leg service every 50 to 100 hours of riding and a full damper service around every 100 to 200 hours. Wipe stanchions clean after every ride.
how often should i add tubeless sealant | tubeless sealant refill | sealant dried out => Check and top up tubeless sealant every 2 to 6 months, more often in hot, dry weather, since it dries out. Shake the wheel and listen for sloshing.
how do i set up tubeless tires | tubeless setup | seat tubeless tire => You need tubeless ready rims and tires, rim tape, valves and sealant. Seat the tire with a strong burst of air, often a floor pump with a charge tank or compressor, add sealant, then spin and shake it to coat.
what are tire inserts | cushcore | do i need tire inserts => Tire inserts are foam rings inside tubeless tires that protect rims from dents, prevent pinch flats and let you run lower pressure. They add weight, so they're mostly for rough enduro and downhill riding.
what do tire widths mean on mtb | 2.4 vs 2.6 tires | wider tires mtb => Wider tires give more grip and cushion at lower pressure but add weight and rolling resistance. Many trail riders use about 2.3 to 2.5 inch tires.
how do i bleed hydraulic brakes | brakes feel spongy | brake bleed => Spongy brakes usually have air in the system. Bleeding means pushing fresh fluid through to remove bubbles. Use the right fluid, since some brands use mineral oil and others DOT fluid, never mixed. A shop can do it.
how do i bed in disc brakes | bedding in brake pads | new pads not grabbing => Bed in new pads by doing about 10 to 20 repeated stops from moderate speed to walking pace, then a few harder ones, without locking the wheels. It transfers pad material onto the rotor for full power.
what are pivot bearings | frame bearings worn | creaky full suspension => Full suspension bikes have bearings at their pivots. Worn bearings cause creaks or play in the rear end. Check by lifting the back wheel and wiggling it side to side.
what is a dropper post lever | dropper not working | dropper post sticking => A dropper post lever is on the handlebar. If it sticks or won't return, the cable may need tension adjustment, or the post may need service. Keep the post clean.
what is enduro racing | enduro mtb | how does enduro racing work => Enduro racing has timed downhill stages, with untimed climbs (transfers) between them. The rider with the lowest total stage time wins. Bikes are long travel but still pedal uphill.
what is downhill racing | dh mtb | downhill world cup => Downhill racing is one timed run down a steep, rough course with jumps and rock gardens. Riders use full face helmets, body armor and bikes with about 200 mm of travel.
what is xc racing | cross country mtb | xco vs xcm => Cross country (XC) racing is the Olympic mountain bike event. XCO is laps on a short loop, and XCM is a long marathon race. XC bikes are light and fast climbers.
who won olympic mountain biking in 2024 | paris 2024 mtb results | olympic xc winners => At the Paris 2024 Olympics, Tom Pidcock of Great Britain won the men's cross country, defending his Tokyo title, and Pauline Ferrand Prevot of France won the women's race.
what is slopestyle mtb | crankworx | freeride mtb events => Slopestyle MTB is a judged competition of big jumps and tricks down a course. Crankworx is a famous festival series with slopestyle, downhill, pump track and whip off events.
what is a whip on a bike | how to whip a jump | mtb tricks names => A whip is turning the bike sideways in the air and bringing it back before landing. Other tricks include tabletops (laying the bike flat), tail whips, no hands and backflips.
how do i learn mtb tricks safely | mtb tricks progression | learning bike tricks => Learn on a dirt jump line or with a foam pit or resi ramp at a bike park, master the basics first, wear full protection, and progress one small step at a time with someone nearby.
what is a resi ramp | foam pit mtb | airbag landing => A resi ramp is a landing covered with a soft padded surface, and foam pits and airbags give soft landings so riders can practice new tricks with less risk.
what body armor do i need for mtb | knee pads mtb | protective gear downhill => Knee pads are the most common. Downhill and bike park riders add full face helmets, goggles, elbow pads, gloves and sometimes back protectors or neck braces.
how do i fit a mountain bike to me | mtb fit | saddle and bar setup => Set saddle height so your leg is almost straight at the bottom of the pedal stroke, level the saddle, set brake levers so your wrists stay straight when standing, and adjust bar height for comfort.
how do i train for mountain biking | mtb fitness training | get fitter for biking => Ride regularly, mix long easy rides with hill climbs and short hard intervals, and add core, leg and upper body strength work. Skills practice counts as training too.
what is a trail rating app | trailforks | find mtb trails near me => Apps like Trailforks and MTB Project show trails, difficulty, conditions and reviews. Always check local trail status after rain, since riding muddy trails damages them.
why shouldnt i ride wet trails | muddy trail damage | trail closed after rain => Riding muddy trails creates ruts, widens trails and damages them. Many trails close after rain. If your tires leave deep marks, the trail is too wet.
what is imba | mtb advocacy groups | international mountain bicycling association => IMBA, the International Mountain Bicycling Association, works to build, protect and improve trail access for mountain bikers and supports local trail groups.
what is the history of mountain biking | who invented mountain bikes | first mountain bikes => Mountain biking grew in the 1970s in Marin County, California, where riders like Gary Fisher, Joe Breeze and Charlie Kelly modified old cruisers to race down Mount Tamalpais. Joe Breeze built some of the first purpose built mountain bikes in 1977.
what is repack | repack race | first mountain bike race => Repack was a downhill race on Mount Tamalpais in the late 1970s. Coaster brakes got so hot that riders had to repack their hubs with grease afterward, giving the race its name.
how do i transport my bike | bike rack for car | hitch vs roof rack => Hitch racks are easy to load and good for heavy bikes. Roof racks keep the rear clear but are harder to reach. Tailgate pads work for pickup trucks. Secure bikes so they can't swing.
what should i bring to a bike park | bike park packing list | first time at a bike park => Bring a full face helmet, knee and elbow pads, gloves, goggles, water, snacks, a multi tool and spare tube. Start on green trails to warm up and learn the park.
