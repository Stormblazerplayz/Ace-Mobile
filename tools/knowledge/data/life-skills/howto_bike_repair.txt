# tag: life.howto.bike
how do i fix a flat bike tire | how to patch a bike tube | my bike tire is flat how do i fix it => Take the wheel off, use tire levers to pop one side of the tire off, and pull out the tube. Find the hole, patch it or use a new tube, check the inside of the tire for thorns, then put the tube back in, reseat the tire and pump it up.
how do i find the hole in a bike tube | where is my puncture | find a leak in an inner tube => Pump the tube up a little and listen or feel for air hissing out. If you can't find it, dunk it in a bucket of water and look for bubbles, then mark the spot.
how do i use a bike patch kit | glue patch vs sticker patch | how to stick a patch on a tube => Rough up the area around the hole with the sandpaper, then for a glue patch spread a thin layer of glue, wait until it's tacky, and press the patch on hard. Sticker patches go on clean, dry rubber with no glue.
how do i use tire levers | how to get a tire off the rim | removing a bike tire => Let all the air out, hook a lever under the tire bead opposite the valve and hook it on a spoke, then slide a second lever around the rim to pop that side off. Don't use a screwdriver, it can rip the tube.
how do i put a bike tire back on | reinstall a tire on a rim | tire won't go back on the rim => Put a little air in the tube so it holds its shape, tuck it in starting at the valve, then push the tire bead over the rim with your thumbs, working both ways around and finishing opposite the valve. Make sure the tube isn't pinched.
how do i take the wheel off my bike | remove a bike wheel | quick release how to use => Shift to the smallest rear cog, open the brake if it's a rim brake, then flip the quick release lever open or unscrew the thru axle. For the rear, pull the derailleur back so the wheel drops out of the chain.
how much air should be in my bike tires | what psi for bike tires | how to know tire pressure => The safe range is printed on the side of the tire. Mountain bike tires often run around 25 to 35 psi, and road bike tires much higher. Never go over the maximum printed on the tire.
what is the difference between presta and schrader valves | which bike valve do i have | presta valve how to pump => Schrader valves are wide like car tire valves. Presta valves are thin with a little nut on top you unscrew before pumping and tighten after. Many pumps fit both or have an adapter.
why does my bike tire keep going flat | slow leak in bike tire | tire flat again after fixing => Something is probably still stuck in the tire, like a thorn or glass, or the rim tape has slipped and a spoke hole is rubbing the tube. Run your fingers carefully around the inside of the tire and check the rim tape.
what is a tubeless tire | should i go tubeless on my mountain bike | how does tubeless sealant work => Tubeless tires have no inner tube. Liquid sealant inside plugs small holes as you ride, and you can run lower pressure for more grip. They need tubeless ready rims and tires and topping up sealant every few months.
how do i fix a bike chain that fell off | chain came off my bike | put a chain back on => Shift to a smaller front ring, then lay the chain on the bottom of the front teeth and slowly turn the pedals backward so it climbs back on. Your hands will get greasy, so wash with dish soap after.
how do i oil a bike chain | how often should i lube my chain | best way to lube a bike chain => Wipe the chain clean with a rag, then put one drop of bike chain lube on each roller while turning the pedals backward. Let it soak in a few minutes and wipe off the extra so it doesn't collect dirt. Do it every few rides or after riding in rain.
can i use wd40 on my bike chain | is wd40 a lubricant | what lube for a bike chain => Regular WD-40 is mostly a cleaner, not a long lasting chain lube, so it dries out fast. Use a bike specific chain lube: wet lube for rainy or muddy rides and dry lube for dusty, dry ones.
how do i clean a bike chain | degrease my chain | my chain is black and gunky => Use a chain cleaning tool or a rag with bike degreaser, scrub the chain and the gears, rinse lightly, let it dry, then re lube it. A clean chain shifts better and lasts longer.
how do i know if my bike chain is worn out | when to replace a bike chain | chain stretch checker => Chains wear and stretch over time. A cheap chain checker tool tells you when it's time, and replacing a worn chain early saves your more expensive gears.
why is my bike chain skipping | gears slipping when i pedal | chain jumps under power => Common causes are a dirty or worn chain, a worn cassette, or the gears needing adjustment. Clean and lube the chain first, and if it still skips, have the derailleur indexed or check for wear.
why is my bike making a clicking noise | bike ticking sound when pedaling | creaking noise on my bike => Clicking often comes from gears needing adjustment, a dry chain, loose pedals or a loose crank. Lube the chain, check that pedals and bolts are tight, and take it to a shop if it keeps happening.
how do i adjust my bike seat height | how high should my bike seat be | seat height for riding => When the pedal is at the very bottom, your leg should be almost straight with a slight bend in the knee. Loosen the seat clamp, move the post, line it up straight and tighten it, never past the minimum insertion mark.
how do i adjust bike brakes | my brakes are loose | brake lever pulls all the way to the handlebar => For cable brakes, turn the barrel adjuster near the lever out a few turns to tighten them. If the lever on hydraulic disc brakes feels spongy, they may need bleeding, which is a good job for a bike shop.
why are my disc brakes squeaking | squeaky bike brakes | how to stop brake squeal => Squealing usually means oil or dirt got on the pads or rotor, or they're worn or misaligned. Clean the rotor with isopropyl alcohol and never touch it with greasy fingers. Contaminated pads may need replacing.
how do i know if my brake pads are worn | when to change brake pads | brake pads thin => Look at the pads. If the pad material is about 1 mm thick or less, or rim brake pads have no grooves left, replace them. Worn pads stop poorly and can damage rims or rotors.
how do i check my bike before riding | pre ride bike check | abc quick check => Do an ABC check: Air in the tires, Brakes squeeze firmly, and Chain clean and running smoothly. Also check that quick releases are closed and nothing is loose.
how do i shift gears on a bike | when should i change gears | how do bike gears work => Shift to an easier gear before hills and a harder gear on flat ground or downhill. Keep pedaling lightly while you shift, and don't shift while pushing really hard on the pedals.
what does the front and rear shifter do | left or right shifter | which shifter is which on a bike => On most bikes the left shifter controls the front rings for big jumps in difficulty, and the right controls the rear cassette for small changes. Many mountain bikes only have one front ring and a right shifter.
what is cross chaining | should i use big ring and big cog | why is my chain rubbing => Cross chaining is using the biggest front ring with the biggest rear cog, or smallest with smallest. It bends the chain at a sharp angle, causing rubbing and wear, so pick gears closer to a straight line.
how do i wash my bike | best way to clean a mountain bike | can i pressure wash my bike => Rinse with a gentle hose, scrub with bike wash or soapy water and a brush, rinse again and dry with a towel, then lube the chain. Don't use a pressure washer, it pushes water into the bearings.
how do i fix a wobbly bike wheel | my wheel is bent | wheel rubbing on brakes => First check that the wheel is seated straight in the frame. A small wobble can be trued by adjusting spoke tension with a spoke wrench, but it's tricky, so a bike shop is the best bet for a badly bent wheel.
how do i tighten my bike handlebars | handlebars are loose | handlebars twisted after a crash => Line the bars up with the front wheel, then tighten the stem bolts evenly with the right size hex key. Many have a torque value printed on the stem, so don't overtighten them, especially on carbon parts.
what tools should i carry on a bike ride | bike repair kit | what to bring on a mountain bike ride => Carry a spare tube or plug kit, tire levers, a mini pump or CO2, a multi tool with hex keys, and a chain quick link. Bring water, a snack and a phone too.
what is a multi tool for bikes | bike hex keys sizes | which allen keys do i need for a bike => A bike multi tool folds up several hex keys, usually 2 to 8 mm, plus screwdrivers and sometimes a Torx key and chain tool. The 4, 5 and 6 mm sizes fit most bolts.
what is a quick link on a bike chain | master link chain | how to join a chain => A quick link is a special link that snaps together by hand or with pliers so you can remove and rejoin a chain without a chain tool. It must match your chain's speed, like 11 speed or 12 speed.
how often should my bike get serviced | bike tune up how often | do bikes need maintenance => Clean and lube after muddy rides, check tires and brakes every ride, and get a full tune up about once or twice a year depending on how much you ride.
what is torque on a bike bolt | do i need a torque wrench for my bike | nm on bike parts => The Nm number printed on parts tells you how tight to make the bolt. A torque wrench helps you hit it exactly, which matters a lot on carbon parts that can crack if overtightened.
how do i pump up a bike tire | how to use a bike pump | floor pump how to use => Take the valve cap off, unscrew the tip if it's Presta, press the pump head on straight and flip the lever to lock it. Pump to a pressure inside the range printed on the tire, then remove the head quickly and replace the cap.
how do i store my bike | where should i keep my bike | can i leave my bike outside => Keep it inside or under a cover so rain doesn't rust the chain and parts. Hanging it on a wall hook or using a stand keeps it out of the way and keeps tires off a wet floor.

# tag: life.howto.repairs
how do i use a screwdriver | phillips vs flathead | which screwdriver do i need => A flathead has a straight flat tip and a Phillips has a cross shaped tip. Pick the size that fits the screw snugly, push in firmly and turn. Righty tighty, lefty loosey.
what does righty tighty lefty loosey mean | which way do you turn to loosen | which way tightens a screw => Turning clockwise (to the right) tightens most screws, bolts and lids, and turning counterclockwise (to the left) loosens them. A few parts, like the left bike pedal, use reverse threads.
what is an allen key | what is a hex key | what are allen wrenches for => An Allen key, or hex key, is an L shaped tool with six sides that fits screws with a hexagon hole. They're used on furniture, bikes and many gadgets.
how do i use a hammer safely | how to hammer a nail | hammering tips => Hold the nail near its top, tap gently to start it, then move your fingers away and hit with the hammer's flat face, holding the handle near the end. Wear safety glasses and keep your eyes on the nail.
how do i fix a squeaky door | door hinge squeaks | stop a door from squeaking => Put a little lubricant like silicone spray or a few drops of oil on the hinge pins, then open and close the door several times. Wipe away any drips.
how do i fix a loose screw in furniture | screw keeps coming out of wood | stripped screw hole fix => If the hole is stripped, push a couple of wooden toothpicks dipped in wood glue into the hole, break them off flush, let it dry and screw back in. A slightly bigger screw also works.
how do i remove a stripped screw | screw head stripped | how to get out a stuck screw => Put a wide rubber band between the screwdriver and the screw to add grip, press hard and turn slowly. If that fails, a screw extractor bit can grab it, and an adult can help.
how do i put together flat pack furniture | assembling furniture tips | building ikea furniture => Lay out all the parts and check them against the list first. Follow the steps in order, keep screws loose until the frame is together, then tighten everything. Two people make big pieces much easier.
how do i hang a picture on the wall | picture hanging tips | how to put a nail in the wall => Ask an adult first. Mark where you want it with a pencil, use a picture hook or nail for light frames, or removable adhesive strips for rentals and walls you don't want holes in. Use a level so it's straight.
how do i reset a tripped breaker | power went out in one room | circuit breaker tripped => Have an adult find the breaker box, look for the switch that's flipped to the middle or off, and push it fully off and then back on. Unplug what you were using first, because overloading the circuit is a common cause.
how do i change a light bulb | replacing a light bulb safely | what light bulb do i need => Turn the light off and let the bulb cool, then unscrew it counterclockwise and screw in a new one clockwise until snug. Use the same base type and don't go above the fixture's maximum watts.
what is a led bulb | why use led bulbs | led vs incandescent => LED bulbs use much less electricity than old incandescent bulbs and last many years longer. They also stay cooler to the touch.
how do i check if a battery is dead | test aa batteries | how to know if batteries are good => Use a battery tester or multimeter. An AA or AAA should read about 1.5 volts when new, and around 1.2 volts or less means it's mostly used up. Rechargeable ones read about 1.2 volts when full.
how do i dispose of batteries | can i throw batteries in the trash | battery recycling => Many places want batteries recycled at stores or drop off points, and lithium batteries should never go in the trash because they can start fires. Tape the ends of lithium batteries before recycling.
how do i fix a running toilet | toilet keeps running | toilet won't stop filling => Lift the tank lid and check if the flapper at the bottom is sealing, or if the chain is caught. Jiggle the handle or straighten the chain. If the flapper is worn, an adult can replace it cheaply.
how do i unclog a sink | sink won't drain | clogged bathroom sink fix => Remove and clean the stopper first, since hair often collects there. Then try a cup plunger, or a plastic drain snake. Avoid harsh chemical drain cleaners unless an adult handles them.
how do i use a tape measure | reading a tape measure | what do the little lines mean on a ruler => Hook the metal end on the edge and pull the tape straight. Big numbers are inches or centimeters. On inch tapes the lines between are halves, quarters, eighths and sixteenths, getting shorter as they get smaller.
how do i use a level | what is a spirit level | how to tell if something is straight => Put the level on the surface. When the bubble sits exactly between the two lines, it's level. Most levels also have a vial for checking if something is straight up and down.
what does wd40 do | what is wd40 used for | what can i use wd40 on => WD-40 loosens stuck and rusty parts, drives out moisture, quiets squeaks for a while and cleans off sticky gunk. It's not great as a long term lubricant.
how do i fix a zipper that came off one side | zipper broke | zipper won't zip => If the slider came off one side, cut a tiny slit or use a fork to guide the teeth back into the slider, then zip it up. If the slider is loose and teeth don't close, gently squeeze the slider with pliers.
how do i fix a sticky zipper | zipper stuck | zipper hard to pull => Rub a pencil tip, a bar of soap or a little lip balm along the teeth, then work the zipper back and forth gently. Check that no fabric is caught in it.
how do i glue things together | which glue should i use | best glue for plastic => White glue is great for paper and wood, super glue for small hard pieces, hot glue for crafts, and epoxy for strong repairs. Check the label says it works on your material.
how do i use a hot glue gun safely | hot glue burns | hot glue gun tips => Let it heat up on its stand, keep fingers away from the nozzle and fresh glue, and unplug it when done. If glue gets on skin, run it under cold water right away, don't peel it off hot.
how do i solder | soldering for beginners | how to solder wires => Ask an adult first. Heat the joint with the iron's tip for a second or two, then touch solder to the joint, not the iron, so it flows in. Use eye protection, good airflow and a stand, and never touch the tip.
how do i strip a wire | wire stripping | how to take insulation off a wire => Use wire strippers set to the wire's gauge, clamp about 1 cm from the end, and pull the insulation off. Only work on unplugged, low voltage things, like batteries and project wires.
