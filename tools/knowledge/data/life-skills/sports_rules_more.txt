what is a scrum in rugby | rugby scrum | why do rugby players push => A scrum restarts play after small mistakes like a forward pass. The forwards bind together and push against the other team while the ball is rolled in between them.
what is a lineout in rugby | rugby lineout | lifting in rugby => A lineout restarts play after the ball goes out of bounds. Players line up, and teammates lift a jumper to catch the ball thrown in.
