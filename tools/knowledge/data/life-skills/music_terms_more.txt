# tag: life.music.terms
what is 6 8 time | compound meter | 6 8 feel => In 6/8 time there are six eighth notes per measure, usually felt as two big beats of three: ONE two three FOUR five six. It gives a lilting, rolling feel.
what is da capo | d c al fine | dal segno => Da capo, written D.C., means go back to the beginning. D.C. al Fine means play from the start until the word Fine. Dal segno, D.S., means go back to the sign.
what is crescendo | crescendo decrescendo | hairpin music => Crescendo means gradually get louder, and decrescendo or diminuendo means gradually get softer. They're often written as long open wedge shapes called hairpins.
what is the alto clef | viola clef | c clef => The alto clef is a C clef whose center marks middle C on the middle line of the staff. Viola players read it most.
what is a perfect fifth | fifth interval | power chord fifth => A perfect fifth is the distance of seven half steps, like C up to G. It sounds strong and stable and is the basis of power chords and the circle of fifths.
what is a major chord made of | major triad | building a chord => A major chord is a root, a major third (4 half steps up) and a perfect fifth (7 half steps up). C major is C, E and G.
what is a sus chord | suspended chord | sus2 sus4 => A suspended chord replaces the third with the second (sus2) or the fourth (sus4). It sounds open and unresolved, so it often moves back to a regular chord.
what is a diminished chord | augmented chord | diminished sound => A diminished chord stacks two minor thirds, like B, D and F, and sounds tense. An augmented chord stacks two major thirds, like C, E and G sharp, and sounds dreamy and unsettled.
what is the four chord song | axis progression | 1 5 6 4 => Many pop songs use the same four chords: I, V, vi and IV, like C, G, A minor and F in C major. The comedy group Axis of Awesome made it famous by medleying dozens of hits.
what is relative minor | relative major minor | a minor c major => Every major key has a relative minor that shares the same notes and key signature. Its root is the 6th note of the major scale, so C major's relative minor is A minor.
what is a melody vs harmony | melody and harmony | harmony meaning => The melody is the main tune you hum. Harmony is the other notes played or sung at the same time that support it, like chords or backing vocals.
what is timbre | tone color | why instruments sound different => Timbre, or tone color, is what makes a flute and a violin sound different playing the same note at the same volume. It comes from the mix of overtones each instrument makes.
what is a solo | guitar solo | improvisation => A solo is a section where one musician plays or sings the spotlight part, often improvised, while others accompany. Guitar solos are famous in rock.
what is a jam session | jamming | jam band => A jam session is when musicians play together informally, making up music on the spot or playing songs everyone knows. It's great practice and fun.
what is a cover song | cover version | covering a song => A cover is a new performance or recording of a song originally made by someone else. Many famous hits were actually covers.
what is a mashup | music mashup | mashup song => A mashup blends two or more existing songs, often the vocals of one over the instrumental of another, into a new track.
what is a music producer | what does a producer do | record producer => A producer guides a recording, helping shape the song's sound, arrangement and performances. In hip hop and electronic music, the producer often makes the beat.
what is royalties music | music royalties | how musicians get paid => Royalties are payments to songwriters, artists and labels each time their music is sold, streamed, played on the radio or used in shows and ads.
what is a single vs album | ep vs lp | ep meaning => A single is one song released on its own. An EP is a short collection, often 3 to 6 songs, and an album or LP is a full length collection.
what is vinyl | vinyl records | rpm records => Vinyl records store sound as tiny grooves that a turntable needle traces. Big albums usually spin at 33 1/3 RPM and smaller singles at 45. They've become popular again.
what is a cassette | cassette tape | mixtape => A cassette is a small plastic case of magnetic tape that recorded and played music, popular in the 1980s and 1990s. People made mixtapes by recording songs onto them.
what is a concerto | concerto meaning | soloist with orchestra => A concerto is a piece for a solo instrument, like a piano or violin, accompanied by an orchestra. It usually has three movements.
what is a barbershop quartet | barbershop | quartet singing => A barbershop quartet is four singers, lead, tenor, baritone and bass, singing close harmony without instruments. It became popular in the US in the late 1800s and early 1900s.
what is a drumline | drum line | marching percussion => A drumline is the percussion section of a marching band, with snare drums, tenor drums, bass drums and cymbals playing tight, precise rhythms.
what is a jazz band | big band | jazz combo => A jazz band can be a small combo of a few players or a big band with saxophones, trumpets, trombones and a rhythm section of piano, bass and drums.
what is a string quartet | quartet instruments | chamber music => A string quartet is two violins, a viola and a cello playing together. It's one of the most popular kinds of chamber music.
what is a duet | duet trio quartet | ensemble sizes => A duet is music for two performers. Three is a trio, four a quartet, five a quintet, six a sextet, seven a septet and eight an octet.
what is a busker | busking | street performer => A busker is a street performer who plays music or does acts in public places for tips. Some cities require a permit.
what is a gig | gig meaning | first gig => A gig is a live performance, especially a paid one. Musicians use it for any show, from a coffee shop to an arena.
what is a setlist | set list | encore => A setlist is the list of songs a band plans to play in a show, in order. Bands often tape it to the stage floor.
what is an encore | encore meaning | why encores => An encore is an extra song or two performed after a show seems to end, when the crowd cheers for more. Encore is French for again.
what is a mosh pit | mosh pit safety | crowd surfing => A mosh pit is an area near the stage at rock and punk shows where people push and jump into each other. It can be dangerous, so kids are better off staying out.
what is lip syncing | lip sync | lip sync battle => Lip syncing is moving your lips along with a recorded song so it looks like you're singing. It's used in videos, TV shows and fun lip sync battles.
what is a vocoder | vocoder | robot voice music => A vocoder is an effect that blends a voice with a synthesizer, making a robotic singing sound. Bands like Kraftwerk and Daft Punk used it in famous songs.
what is a turntable | dj turntable | scratching => A turntable is a record player. DJs use two turntables and a mixer to blend songs and make scratching sounds by moving records back and forth.
what is a drop in edm | the drop | build up and drop => In electronic dance music, the drop is the moment after a buildup when the full beat and bass suddenly kick in. It's the high energy payoff of the song.
what is a lullaby | lullabies | lullaby meaning => A lullaby is a soft, gentle song sung to help babies and young children fall asleep. They exist in almost every culture.
