# tag: life.sports.terms
what is a face off in lacrosse | lacrosse face off | lacrosse start => A face off starts each quarter and restarts play after every goal in men's lacrosse. Two players crouch at midfield with the ball between their stick heads and battle for it on the whistle. Women's lacrosse uses a draw instead.
what is a penalty corner in field hockey | short corner field hockey | field hockey corner => A penalty corner is awarded for certain defensive fouls in the shooting circle. An attacker pushes the ball from the backline, and up to five defenders, including the goalie, start behind the goal line.
how is badminton scored | badminton rally scoring | badminton game to 21 => Badminton uses rally scoring, so every rally scores a point. Games go to 21, win by 2, capped at 30, and matches are best of three games.
what is a shuttlecock made of | shuttlecock feathers | birdie badminton => Top level shuttlecocks have 16 overlapping goose or duck feathers fixed into a rounded cork base. Plastic ones are cheaper and last longer for casual play.
what is a turkey in bowling | three strikes bowling | bowling slang => A turkey is three strikes in a row. The name comes from old tournaments where a live turkey was sometimes given as a prize.
what is a split in bowling | 7 10 split | bowling split => A split is when your first ball knocks down the headpin but leaves pins with a gap between them. The 7 10 split, the two back corners, is one of the hardest to pick up.
how long is a triathlon | olympic triathlon distance | sprint triathlon => A sprint triathlon is a 750 m swim, 20 km bike and 5 km run. The Olympic distance doubles that: 1.5 km swim, 40 km bike and 10 km run.
what is an ironman triathlon | ironman distance | ironman race length => An Ironman is a 3.86 km (2.4 mi) swim, 180 km (112 mi) bike ride and a full 42.2 km (26.2 mi) marathon, done in a row. Athletes usually have 17 hours to finish.
what is the peloton | peloton cycling | cycling pack => The peloton is the main pack of riders in a bike race. Riding close behind others blocks wind and saves a lot of energy.
what is a domestique | domestique cycling | cycling team roles => A domestique is a cycling teammate who works for the team leader by blocking wind, setting the pace, chasing breakaways and fetching food and water bottles.
what do the flags mean in f1 | formula 1 flags | checkered flag racing => Green means go, yellow means danger and no passing, red stops the session, blue tells a driver to let a faster car pass, and the checkered flag ends the race. A black flag disqualifies a driver.
what is a pit stop | f1 pit stop time | pit crew => A pit stop is when a race car pulls into the pit lane for new tires or repairs. Formula 1 crews can change all four tires in about 2 to 3 seconds.
what is drs in f1 | drag reduction system | f1 overtaking => DRS (drag reduction system) was a flap on the rear wing a driver could open on straights when within 1 second of the car ahead, making passing easier. As of 2026, new F1 rules replaced it with movable wings and an extra electric boost for overtaking.
how many races in an f1 season | f1 calendar | formula 1 season length => As of 2025, the Formula 1 season had 24 races around the world, running from about March to December.
what is the triple crown in horse racing | kentucky derby preakness belmont | horse racing triple crown => The Triple Crown is winning the Kentucky Derby, Preakness Stakes and Belmont Stakes in one year with a 3 year old horse. Only 13 horses have done it, most recently Justify in 2018, as of 2025.
how is boxing scored | 10 point must system | boxing judges => Most pro boxing uses the 10 point must system. Each judge gives the round winner 10 points and the loser 9 or fewer, with points taken off for knockdowns.
what is olympic wrestling | freestyle vs greco roman | wrestling styles => Olympic wrestling has two styles. Freestyle allows holds on the legs, while Greco Roman bans holds below the waist. You win by pinning both of the opponent's shoulders to the mat or by points.
what are the judo belt colors | judo belts | judo ranks => Colors vary by country and club, but a common order is white, yellow, orange, green, blue, brown, then black. Black belts then advance through degrees called dan.
what is a dan in martial arts | black belt degrees | first degree black belt => A dan is a black belt rank, used in judo, karate and taekwondo. First dan is the first black belt level, and ranks below black belt are often called kyu or gup.
what are climbing grades | bouldering v scale | yosemite decimal system => Bouldering in the US uses the V scale, starting at V0 for beginners and going up to about V17. Roped climbs use the Yosemite system like 5.10a, while France and Europe use grades like 6a.
what is bouldering | bouldering vs rope climbing | bouldering crash pad => Bouldering is climbing short walls or rocks without ropes, with thick crash pads below. Each route is called a problem.
what is a terrain park | ski terrain park | park etiquette => A terrain park is an area of a ski hill with jumps, rails and boxes for tricks. Features are often marked by size, so start small and look before you drop in.
what is snowboard cross | boardercross | ski cross => In snowboard cross, four to six riders race side by side down a course of jumps, rollers and banked turns. The first across the line wins. Ski cross is the ski version.
what is nordic combined | nordic combined events | ski jumping plus cross country => Nordic combined mixes ski jumping and cross country skiing. Jump results set the starting order for the ski race, and the first skier to the finish wins.
what is a brace in soccer | scored a brace | two goals soccer => A brace means one player scoring two goals in a single game.
what is a grand slam in baseball | grand slam home run | bases loaded homer => A grand slam is a home run hit with the bases loaded, scoring 4 runs at once, the most possible on one swing.
what is a golden slam | career grand slam | calendar grand slam => A Golden Slam is winning all four tennis majors plus Olympic gold. Steffi Graf is the only player to do it in one year, in 1988. Others, like Novak Djokovic, have done it across their careers.
what is an eagle in golf | eagle albatross | condor golf => An eagle is finishing a hole in two strokes under par. Three under is an albatross, or double eagle.
what is the green jacket | masters green jacket | masters tournament => The green jacket is given to the winner of the Masters golf tournament at Augusta National. Champions can take it home for a year, then it stays at the club.
what is the ryder cup | ryder cup format | usa vs europe golf => The Ryder Cup is a men's golf team event between the US and Europe held every two years, using match play. As of 2025, Europe won the most recent edition at Bethpage Black in New York.
