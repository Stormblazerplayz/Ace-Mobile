# tag: sports.mtb
what are the types of mountain biking | xc vs trail vs enduro | downhill mountain biking => Cross country (XC) is about fitness and climbing on light bikes. Trail riding is all round fun on mixed terrain. Enduro is racing timed downhill sections with untimed climbs, and downhill is riding steep, rough tracks on heavy bikes, usually with lifts.
what is the difference between a hardtail and full suspension | hardtail vs full sus | should i get a hardtail => A hardtail has front suspension only, which makes it lighter, cheaper and simpler. A full suspension bike also has a rear shock for more grip and comfort on rough trails. Hardtails are great for learning skills.
what does suspension travel mean | how much travel do i need | 120mm vs 160mm travel => Travel is how far the suspension can compress, in millimeters. XC bikes have around 100 to 120 mm, trail bikes around 120 to 150 mm, enduro bikes 150 to 180 mm and downhill bikes about 200 mm.
what is sag on a mountain bike | how to set suspension sag | sag percentage => Sag is how much your suspension compresses when you sit on the bike in your gear. Many riders set about 25 to 30 percent sag in the rear and 15 to 20 percent in the fork, adjusting the air pressure. Check the manufacturer's chart.
what is rebound on a fork | rebound vs compression | how to set rebound => Rebound controls how fast the suspension springs back after compressing, and compression controls how easily it squishes. Too fast rebound feels bouncy, too slow and it packs down over repeated bumps.
what is a dropper post | do i need a dropper post | how does a dropper seatpost work => A dropper post lowers your seat with a lever on the handlebar, so you can get it out of the way for descents and raise it for climbing. It's one of the best upgrades for trail riding.
what size wheels should my mountain bike have | 29er vs 27.5 | wheel sizes mtb => 29 inch wheels roll over obstacles more easily and hold speed. 27.5 inch wheels are more nimble and playful. Many kids and smaller riders use 24 or 26 inch wheels, and some bikes mix a 29 front with a 27.5 rear.
what size mountain bike do i need | bike frame size for my height | is my bike too big => Frame size depends mostly on your height and the brand's size chart. You should be able to stand over the bike with a little room and reach the bars comfortably. Test ride if you can.
what is the attack position | how to stand on a mountain bike | body position on descents => The attack position is standing on level pedals with knees and elbows bent, hips back and centered over the bike, and eyes looking ahead down the trail. It lets your body absorb bumps.
how do i corner on a mountain bike | cornering tips mtb | how to turn on trails => Brake before the turn, not in it. Look through the turn to where you want to go, drop your outside pedal, lean the bike more than your body and push your weight into the tires.
how do i use my brakes on a mountain bike | front brake vs back brake | braking tips mtb => Your front brake has most of the stopping power, so use both brakes smoothly, with one finger each on hydraulic brakes. Avoid grabbing hard in loose turns, and brake on straight sections before obstacles.
how do i climb hills on a mountain bike | mtb climbing tips | how to not lose traction going uphill => Shift to an easier gear early, stay seated to keep weight on the rear tire, lean forward a bit so the front doesn't lift, and pedal smoothly. Look ahead to pick the best line.
how do i ride over roots and rocks | technical trail tips | roots are slippery => Stay loose in the attack position, keep some speed, unweight the front wheel as it hits the obstacle, then the back. Cross wet roots as straight as possible and don't brake on them.
how do i bunny hop | bunny hop on a mountain bike | how to jump a bike without a ramp => First learn to lift the front wheel by pushing down and then pulling back with your hips. Then, as the front rises, push the bars forward and lift your feet and hips so the back wheel follows. Practice on low obstacles with flat pedals.
how do i manual a bike | manual vs wheelie | how to ride on the back wheel => A manual is balancing on the back wheel without pedaling. Shift your hips back with straight arms, bend your knees, and use your rear brake to stop from looping out. A wheelie uses pedaling instead.
how do i do drops on a mountain bike | riding off a drop | small drops tips => Start tiny. Approach with enough speed, stay in the attack position, give the bike a small push forward as the front wheel leaves the edge, and land with both wheels together or back wheel slightly first, with knees and elbows bent.
how do i jump on a mountain bike | mtb jumping tips | how to hit a jump => Start on small tabletop jumps. Ride up the lip in the attack position, compress into it, then let the bike rise and follow it with your body. Don't pull up hard on the bars. Keep speed steady and look at the landing.
what is a pump track | how to ride a pump track | pumping on a bike => A pump track is a loop of rollers and berms you ride without pedaling, pushing the bike down into the backsides of bumps to gain speed. It's great for building skills.
what is a berm | how to ride a berm | banked turn mtb => A berm is a banked turn. Look through it, lean the bike into the bank and push your weight into it so the tires grip. You can carry a lot of speed through a good berm.
what do trail difficulty ratings mean | green blue black trails | mtb trail colors => In the US, green circle trails are easiest, blue squares are intermediate, black diamonds are difficult and double black diamonds are extremely difficult. Ratings vary by area, so start easier at new places.
who has the right of way on a trail | trail etiquette | mountain bike yield rules => The common rule is that bikes yield to hikers and horses, and downhill riders yield to riders climbing uphill. Slow down, say hello and pass carefully.
what gear do i need for mountain biking | mtb safety gear | what to wear mountain biking => Always wear a helmet. Gloves, eye protection, knee pads and padded shorts help a lot. For downhill and bike parks, add a full face helmet and more armor. Bring water, a snack and a repair kit.
what is a full face helmet | full face vs half shell | when should i wear a full face => A full face helmet has a chin bar that protects your face and jaw. Wear one at bike parks, on downhill trails and for big jumps. Half shell helmets are lighter for regular trail riding.
how do i know if my bike helmet fits | helmet fit test | how tight should a helmet be => It should sit level, about two fingers above your eyebrows, not tilted back. The side straps form a V under each ear, and the chin strap is snug enough that only one or two fingers fit underneath. Replace a helmet after any crash.
should i use flat pedals or clipless | flats vs clipless mtb | what are clipless pedals => Flat pedals let you put your foot down easily and teach good technique, which makes them great for learning and jumping. Clipless pedals lock your shoes in for efficient pedaling, popular in cross country.
what is a 1x drivetrain | 1x vs 2x | why do mountain bikes have one chainring => A 1x drivetrain has a single front chainring and a wide range cassette in back. It's simpler, lighter, with less chain drop, which is why most modern mountain bikes use it.
what tire pressure should i run on my mountain bike | mtb tire pressure | low pressure for grip => Many mountain bike riders run around 20 to 30 psi depending on weight, tire width and tubeless or tube setup. Lower pressure grips better but risks pinch flats and rim damage. Stay within the tire's printed range.
what is a pinch flat | snake bite flat | how to avoid pinch flats => A pinch flat is when the tube gets squeezed between the rim and a rock or root, leaving two small holes like a snake bite. Running slightly higher pressure or going tubeless helps prevent them.
what is an e mountain bike | ebike classes | are e bikes allowed on trails => An e-MTB has a motor that helps when you pedal. In the US, class 1 e-bikes assist only while pedaling up to 20 mph. Rules on which trails allow them vary, so check local rules.
what is a bike park | lift access mountain biking | bike park for beginners => A bike park is a place with built trails, jumps and berms, often at a ski resort using chairlifts in summer. Start on green trails, wear a full face helmet and pads, and consider a lesson.
what is mountain biking in the olympics | olympic mountain bike race | xco olympics => Cross country mountain biking (XCO) has been an Olympic sport since 1996. Riders race several laps of a technical course with climbs, descents and rock gardens.
what is red bull rampage | rampage mountain biking | freeride mtb => Red Bull Rampage is a famous freeride mountain bike event in Utah where riders build lines down steep desert ridges and do huge drops and tricks, judged on difficulty and style.
how do i clean my mountain bike after a muddy ride | washing mtb | mud on my bike => Rinse off mud with a low pressure hose before it dries, scrub the frame and drivetrain with a brush and bike cleaner, rinse, dry the chain, then lube it. Avoid spraying directly into bearings and suspension seals.
how do i get better at mountain biking | mtb skills for beginners | improve trail riding => Practice basic skills in a parking lot or grass, like braking, cornering, track stands and wheel lifts. Ride with better riders and follow their lines, session tricky sections, and take a skills clinic.
what is a track stand | how to balance on a bike without moving | track stand tips => A track stand is balancing on a stopped bike. Stand on level pedals, turn the front wheel slightly, and make tiny pedal pushes and brake taps to hold your balance. It helps with slow technical moves.
how do i ride switchbacks | tight turns on a trail | switchback tips => Slow down before the turn, look around the corner to the exit, take a wide line on the outside, and turn your hips and shoulders. Shift to an easier gear first for uphill switchbacks.
what should i do if i crash on my bike | mtb crash what to do | fell off my bike => Stay still for a moment and check for injury. Get off the trail if you can move, and check your helmet and bike. If you hit your head, feel dizzy or have bad pain, stop riding and get help. Replace a helmet after a crash.
how do i carry water on a bike ride | hydration pack vs bottle | camelbak for mtb => Use a bottle cage on the frame, or a hydration pack or hip pack with a water bladder for longer rides. Drink small sips often, especially in the heat.
how do i ride in the rain | wet trail riding | should you ride muddy trails => Many trails get damaged when ridden muddy, so check local trail status and skip wet trails if they're closed. On wet surfaces, brake gently, go slower and avoid sudden turns on roots and rocks.
what is strava | how does strava work | strava segments => Strava is an app that tracks rides and runs with GPS. Segments are sections of trail or road where you can compare your time with others and your past rides. Keep your profile private and hide your home location.
what is a gravel bike | gravel vs mountain bike | cyclocross bike => A gravel bike looks like a road bike with drop bars but has wider, knobby tires for dirt roads and light trails. Mountain bikes handle rougher trails with suspension and wider bars.

# tag: sports.skateboarding
what are the parts of a skateboard | skateboard parts names | what are trucks on a skateboard => A skateboard has a deck (the board), grip tape on top, two trucks (the metal axles that turn), four wheels, and bearings inside the wheels that let them spin.
am i regular or goofy | regular vs goofy stance | how to find my skateboard stance => Regular means your left foot is forward, and goofy means your right foot is forward. To find yours, have someone gently push you from behind and see which foot you step forward with.
how do i ride a skateboard | skateboarding for beginners | how to push on a skateboard => Put your front foot over the front bolts pointing forward, push with your back foot on the ground, then put it on the tail and turn your front foot sideways. Keep your knees bent and look ahead.
how do i stop on a skateboard | skateboard braking | foot brake skateboard => The easiest way is a foot brake: take your back foot off and drag the sole lightly on the ground. You can also step off safely at slow speed. Heel drags and powerslides come later.
how do i turn on a skateboard | carving skateboard | how to steer a skateboard => Lean with your toes or heels to turn, called carving. For sharp turns, put a bit of weight on the tail and pivot, called a kickturn.
how do i ollie | ollie tutorial | how to jump with a skateboard => Put your back foot on the tail and front foot near the middle. Snap the tail down hard, jump, and slide your front foot up toward the nose so the board rises, then level it out with both feet and land with knees bent. Practice standing still on grass first.
how do i do a kickflip | kickflip tips | how to flip a skateboard => Set up like an ollie with your front foot a bit angled near the heel side edge. Pop the tail, then flick your front foot off the nose corner so the board spins, jump, catch it with your feet when the grip tape faces up and land. Learn ollies first.
what is a shove it | pop shove it | easy skateboard tricks => A shove it is spinning the board 180 degrees under you without it flipping, using your back foot to push the tail around. A pop shove it adds a pop so the board jumps.
how do i drop in on a ramp | dropping in skateboard | scared to drop in => Put the tail on the coping with your back foot, front foot on the front bolts, then lean forward and push your front foot down with commitment so all wheels touch at once. Leaning back is what makes people fall. Wear pads and a helmet.
what safety gear do i need for skateboarding | skateboard helmet and pads | skatepark safety => Wear a helmet, and use knee pads, elbow pads and wrist guards while learning. Closed toe skate shoes with flat soles give the best grip.
how do i fall safely on a skateboard | falling tips skating | how not to get hurt skateboarding => Try to roll out of a fall instead of catching yourself with straight arms, which can break wrists. Knee sliding on pads helps on ramps. Wrist guards reduce injuries a lot.
what size skateboard should i get | skateboard deck width | best beginner skateboard => Deck width depends mostly on shoe size and style. Many teens and adults use around 7.75 to 8.25 inches. A complete board from a skate shop is better than a cheap toy store board.
what are soft vs hard skateboard wheels | wheel durometer | best wheels for cruising => Wheel hardness is measured on a durometer scale. Softer wheels, around 78a to 87a, are smooth and quiet for cruising on rough ground. Harder wheels, around 99a and up, slide better for park and street tricks.
what is a longboard | longboard vs skateboard | is a longboard easier => A longboard is longer with softer, bigger wheels, made for cruising and downhill. It's stable and easy to ride, but harder for flip tricks.
when did skateboarding become an olympic sport | skateboarding olympics | olympic skateboarding events => Skateboarding debuted at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, held in 2021, with street and park events. It returned at Paris 2024.
who is tony hawk | first 900 skateboarding | tony hawk famous trick => Tony Hawk is a legendary skateboarder who landed the first 900, two and a half spins in the air, at the 1999 X Games. The Tony Hawk's Pro Skater games made him famous worldwide.
how do i maintain my skateboard | skateboard bearings cleaning | tighten skateboard trucks => Keep it dry, check bolts and nuts, and adjust the big truck nut, the kingpin, to make turning looser or tighter. Clean or replace bearings if wheels slow down or grind.
what is a manual in skateboarding | skateboard manual | nose manual => A manual is balancing on the back two wheels while rolling, like a wheelie. A nose manual is balancing on the front wheels.
what is skatepark etiquette | skatepark rules | snaking at a skatepark => Wait your turn, watch where others are going, don't stand in the landing zones, and don't cut in on someone's line, called snaking. Encourage newer skaters.
how do i ride a scooter tricks | scooter tricks for beginners | how to tail whip => Start with bunny hops, then 180s, manuals and barspins. A tailwhip spins the deck around the stem with your foot kick, so learn it on flat ground with pads and a helmet.
how do i rollerblade | inline skating for beginners | how to stop on rollerblades => Stand with knees bent and feet in a V, push out to the side with one skate and glide. Most rollerblades have a heel brake: put the braking foot forward and lift the toe to drag the brake.
