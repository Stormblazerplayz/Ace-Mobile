# tag: life.cooking.tools
what kitchen tools does a beginner need | basic kitchen tools list | must have cooking tools => A good start is a chef's knife, cutting board, nonstick pan, a medium pot, a sheet pan, mixing bowls, measuring cups and spoons, a spatula, wooden spoon, whisk, tongs and a meat thermometer.
what is a chef's knife | which knife should i use | types of kitchen knives => A chef's knife is the main all purpose knife for chopping, slicing and dicing. A paring knife is small for peeling and detailed work, and a serrated bread knife has teeth for crusty bread and tomatoes.
what is a paring knife used for | small kitchen knife | paring knife uses => A paring knife is a small knife for jobs held in your hand or on a board, like peeling apples, hulling strawberries and cutting small things.
how do i sharpen a knife | knife sharpening for beginners | honing vs sharpening => Honing with a steel rod straightens the edge and can be done often. Sharpening actually grinds new metal with a whetstone or sharpener. Ask an adult to show you, and always move the blade away from your body.
what is a nonstick pan | how to take care of a nonstick pan | can you use metal on nonstick => Nonstick pans have a slick coating so eggs and pancakes don't stick. Use wooden or silicone tools, medium heat or lower, and hand wash them to make the coating last.
what is a cast iron skillet good for | why use cast iron | cast iron pan benefits => Cast iron holds heat really well, so it's great for searing steak, cornbread and crispy potatoes. It gets naturally nonstick with seasoning, and it lasts for generations if kept dry and oiled.
what is a dutch oven | dutch oven uses | what do you cook in a dutch oven => A Dutch oven is a heavy pot with a tight lid, usually cast iron. It's great for soups, stews, braising meat and even baking crusty bread, on the stove or in the oven.
what is a sheet pan | sheet pan vs baking sheet | what is a rimmed baking sheet => A sheet pan is a flat metal pan with low sides, used for cookies, roasting vegetables and sheet pan dinners. The rim keeps juices from dripping into the oven.
what is a spatula | rubber spatula vs turner | types of spatulas => A turner spatula is flat and stiff for flipping pancakes and burgers. A rubber or silicone spatula is flexible for scraping bowls and folding batter.
what are tongs used for | kitchen tongs | why use tongs => Tongs let you grab and flip food like pasta, meat and veggies without poking holes or getting close to hot oil.
what is a colander | colander vs strainer | how to drain pasta => A colander is a bowl with holes for draining pasta and rinsing produce. A fine mesh strainer has tiny holes for things like rice or sifting flour. Pour pasta away from you so the steam doesn't burn your face.
what is a whisk for | balloon whisk | whisk vs fork => A whisk beats air into eggs and cream and mixes batters smoothly without lumps. It works faster and better than a fork for most mixing.
what is a ladle | kitchen ladle uses | soup spoon big => A ladle is a deep spoon with a long handle for scooping soup, stew and sauce, and pouring pancake batter.
what is a slotted spoon | slotted spoon uses | spoon with holes => A slotted spoon has holes so liquid drains away while you lift food out, like boiled eggs, poached eggs or vegetables.
what is a peeler | how to use a vegetable peeler | y peeler vs straight peeler => A vegetable peeler shaves off thin skin from carrots, potatoes and apples. Pull it away from you along the vegetable, and keep fingers behind the blade.
what is a grater | box grater sides | how to grate cheese safely => A box grater has different sides: big holes for shredding cheese, small holes for fine grating, a slicing side, and sometimes a tiny zesting side. Stop before your fingers get close and use the last bit in another way.
what is a microplane | microplane uses | fine grater for zest => A Microplane is a very fine, sharp grater for citrus zest, hard cheese like parmesan, garlic, ginger and nutmeg.
what is a mandoline | is a mandoline safe | mandoline slicer => A mandoline slices vegetables very thin and even, but it's one of the most dangerous kitchen tools. Always use the hand guard or cut resistant gloves, and kids should only use one with an adult.
what is a stand mixer | stand mixer vs hand mixer | do i need a mixer => A stand mixer sits on the counter and mixes, whips and kneads while you do other things. A hand mixer is cheaper and smaller, great for cookies, cakes and whipped cream.
what is a food processor | food processor vs blender | what is a food processor used for => A food processor chops, slices, shreds and makes thick mixtures like hummus or pie dough. A blender is best for liquids like smoothies and soups.
what is an immersion blender | stick blender uses | blending soup in the pot => An immersion blender is a handheld stick blender you put right in the pot to puree soup, or in a cup for smoothies. Keep the blade under the surface so it doesn't splatter.
how do i use a blender safely | blender safety | blending hot liquids => Put the lid on before starting, never put your hand or a spoon inside while it's plugged in, and unplug it before clearing a jam. Hot liquids expand, so only fill it partway and vent the lid with a towel over it.
what is a rice cooker | how does a rice cooker work | do i need a rice cooker => A rice cooker boils rice and water, then switches to keep warm when the water is absorbed, which it senses by temperature. It makes perfect rice without watching it.
what is a slow cooker | crockpot uses | how does a slow cooker work => A slow cooker cooks food at a low, steady temperature for many hours, which is great for stews, chili and pulled meat. You add ingredients in the morning and dinner's ready later.
what is an instant pot | pressure cooker how does it work | is a pressure cooker safe => An Instant Pot is an electric pressure cooker. Sealing the pot raises the pressure, so water boils hotter and food cooks much faster. Modern ones have safety locks, but always let an adult open it.
what is a kitchen scale used for | why weigh ingredients | digital kitchen scale => A kitchen scale weighs ingredients, which is more accurate than cups, especially for flour. Press tare to zero out the bowl first, then add the ingredient.
what is a candy thermometer | candy vs meat thermometer | thermometer for sugar => A candy thermometer clips to the pot and reads very high temperatures for candy, caramel and frying oil. A meat thermometer is for checking the inside of meat.
what is a rolling pin used for | how to use a rolling pin | rolling out dough evenly => A rolling pin flattens dough for cookies, pie crust and pizza. Dust it and the counter with flour, roll from the center outward and turn the dough often so it stays round and even.
what is a pastry brush | basting brush uses | brushing egg wash => A pastry brush spreads egg wash, butter or glaze on dough, bread and meat. Silicone ones are easy to clean.
what is a cooling rack | why cool cookies on a rack | wire rack uses => A wire cooling rack lets air flow all around baked goods so the bottoms don't get soggy. It's also handy for oven bacon over a sheet pan.
what is parchment paper | parchment vs wax paper | what is baking paper used for => Parchment paper is heatproof, nonstick paper for lining pans so things don't stick. Wax paper isn't heat safe and shouldn't go in the oven.
what is aluminum foil used for | can foil go in the oven | shiny side of foil => Foil covers dishes, wraps food and lines pans, and it's oven safe. The shiny and dull sides work about the same. Don't put foil in the microwave.
what is plastic wrap for | cling film uses | covering food in the fridge => Plastic wrap seals bowls and wraps food so it doesn't dry out or pick up smells in the fridge. Don't let it touch hot pans or go in the oven.
what is a can opener | how to use a can opener | manual can opener steps => Clamp the opener's wheel onto the rim of the can, squeeze the handles, and turn the knob to cut around the lid. Lift the lid off carefully because the edge is sharp.
what is a garlic press | do i need a garlic press | how to use a garlic press => A garlic press squeezes a peeled garlic clove through little holes to mince it instantly. You can also mince garlic with a knife.
what is a salad spinner | how does a salad spinner work | drying lettuce => A salad spinner spins washed lettuce in a basket so water flies off, using the same idea as a washing machine spin cycle. Dry greens make dressing stick better.
what is a waffle iron | waffle maker tips | how to use a waffle maker => A waffle iron is two hot patterned plates that cook batter into waffles. Grease it, pour batter in the middle, close and wait until steam mostly stops, usually 3 to 5 minutes.
what pans can go in the oven | is my pan oven safe | oven safe pans => Metal pans with metal handles, cast iron, and glass or ceramic baking dishes are usually oven safe. Plastic handles and some nonstick pans have limits, so check the bottom of the pan or the manual.
what is a double boiler | how to make a double boiler | melting chocolate on the stove => A double boiler is a bowl set over a pot of simmering water, without touching the water, for gently melting chocolate or cooking delicate sauces. Any heatproof bowl that fits on a pot works.
what is a skillet vs a saucepan | pan types explained | frying pan vs pot => A skillet, or frying pan, is wide and shallow with sloped sides for frying and searing. A saucepan is deeper with straight sides for sauces, boiling eggs and heating soup.
what is a wok | why is a wok shaped like that | wok cooking => A wok is a round bottomed pan used in Chinese cooking. Its shape keeps a small super hot area at the bottom and lets you toss food up the sides, perfect for stir frying.
how do i use oven mitts | oven mitt vs pot holder | wet oven mitt burns => Use dry oven mitts, since a wet mitt passes heat straight through and can burn you. Mitts cover your whole hand, while pot holders are flat pads for grabbing handles.
what is a timer for in cooking | why set a kitchen timer | forgetting food in the oven => A timer stops you forgetting food and burning it. Set it for a little less than the recipe time and check early, since ovens vary.
