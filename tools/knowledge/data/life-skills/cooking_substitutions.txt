# tag: life.cooking.substitute
what can i use instead of eggs in baking | egg substitute | no eggs for baking what do i use => For each egg, try 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tablespoons water and left 5 minutes, or a quarter cup of mashed banana or applesauce. These work best in muffins, pancakes and brownies, not recipes that need lots of eggs.
what can i use instead of buttermilk | buttermilk substitute | how to make buttermilk at home => Put 1 tablespoon of lemon juice or white vinegar in a measuring cup, add milk to make 1 cup, stir and let it sit 5 to 10 minutes until it thickens a little.
what can i use instead of butter | butter substitute for baking | no butter what can i use => In many baking recipes you can use the same amount of margarine or plant based butter, or about three quarters as much oil for melted butter. For cooking, oil works almost anywhere.
can i use oil instead of butter | substitute oil for butter in cookies | swap butter for vegetable oil => Yes in many recipes, using about three quarters as much oil as butter. Muffins and cakes work well, but cookies may spread more and taste less buttery.
what can i use instead of baking powder | no baking powder substitute | make baking powder at home => Mix 1/4 teaspoon baking soda with 1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar to replace 1 teaspoon of baking powder. You can also use baking soda with an acidic ingredient like buttermilk or yogurt.
out of baking soda what now | no baking soda | baking soda substitute => You can use about three times as much baking powder as the baking soda the recipe calls for, but it may taste a bit different and not brown as much. It's best for small amounts.
what can i use instead of brown sugar | brown sugar substitute | make brown sugar => Mix 1 cup of white sugar with 1 tablespoon of molasses for light brown sugar or 2 tablespoons for dark. In a pinch, plain white sugar works, but cookies will be less chewy.
what can i use instead of powdered sugar | make powdered sugar | icing sugar substitute => Blend regular sugar in a clean, dry blender until it's a fine powder. Adding a teaspoon of cornstarch per cup stops clumping, like store powdered sugar.
what can i use instead of sour cream | sour cream substitute | greek yogurt instead of sour cream => Plain Greek yogurt works as a one to one swap in most recipes, dips and toppings.
what can i use instead of milk | milk substitute in baking | can i use water instead of milk => Plant milks like oat, soy or almond swap one to one. Water works in some recipes, like pancakes, but the result is less rich, so add a little melted butter if you can.
what can i use instead of heavy cream | heavy cream substitute | no cream for pasta sauce => For cooking, mix 3/4 cup milk with 1/4 cup melted butter for 1 cup of cream. It works in sauces and soups, but won't whip.
what can i use instead of cornstarch | thicken without cornstarch | cornstarch substitute => Use about twice as much all purpose flour, or the same amount of arrowroot or potato starch. Flour sauces need to simmer a few minutes so they don't taste raw.
what can i use instead of self rising flour | make self raising flour | self rising flour substitute => For 1 cup, mix 1 cup all purpose flour with 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
what can i use instead of cake flour | cake flour substitute | no cake flour => Measure 1 cup all purpose flour, remove 2 tablespoons and replace them with 2 tablespoons cornstarch, then sift it together.
can i use all purpose flour instead of bread flour | bread flour substitute | difference between bread flour and all purpose => Yes, it works fine in most recipes. Bread flour has more protein, which makes chewier bread, so all purpose gives a slightly softer loaf.
what can i use instead of breadcrumbs | breadcrumb substitute | no breadcrumbs for meatballs => Crushed crackers, crushed cornflakes, rolled oats or panko all work. Toasting and blending stale bread also makes your own breadcrumbs.
what can i use instead of vanilla extract | no vanilla substitute | can i skip vanilla => Maple syrup or a little almond extract can stand in, or you can just leave it out of most recipes. The flavor will be a little different but it won't ruin the recipe.
what can i use instead of lemon juice | lemon juice substitute | lime instead of lemon => Lime juice swaps one to one. In baking or dressings, white vinegar or apple cider vinegar can work in small amounts, using about half as much.
what can i use instead of honey | honey substitute | can i use maple syrup instead of honey => Maple syrup, agave syrup or corn syrup can replace honey one to one in most recipes.
what can i use instead of mayonnaise | mayo substitute | no mayo for sandwiches => Plain Greek yogurt, mashed avocado or hummus work well on sandwiches and in salads like chicken or tuna salad.
what can i use instead of tomato paste | tomato paste substitute | ketchup instead of tomato paste => Use about 3 tablespoons of tomato sauce or puree for each tablespoon of paste, and cook it down a bit. Ketchup works in a pinch but is sweeter.
what can i use instead of wine in cooking | cooking without wine | wine substitute for kids => Use the same amount of broth, plus a splash of vinegar or lemon juice for tang. Grape juice with a little vinegar works for red wine in sauces.
what can i use instead of fresh herbs | dried herbs vs fresh | how much dried herbs for fresh => Use about 1 teaspoon of dried herbs for every 1 tablespoon of fresh, since dried are stronger. Add dried herbs earlier in cooking so they soften.
what can i use instead of garlic cloves | garlic powder for fresh garlic | how much garlic powder equals a clove => About 1/8 teaspoon garlic powder equals one clove of fresh garlic. Pre minced jarred garlic works too, about half a teaspoon per clove.
what can i use instead of onion | onion substitute | onion powder instead of fresh => About 1 tablespoon of onion powder can replace one medium onion for flavor, though you lose the texture. Shallots, leeks or green onions also work.
what can i use instead of chocolate chips | no chocolate chips | substitute for chips in cookies => Chop up a chocolate bar, or use M&Ms, raisins, dried cranberries, nuts or butterscotch chips.
what can i use instead of oil for brownies | applesauce instead of oil | healthier brownie swap => Applesauce can replace some or all of the oil one to one, making brownies cakier and less fudgy. Replacing half is a good middle ground.
what can i use instead of a rolling pin | no rolling pin | how to roll dough without a rolling pin => A clean, smooth bottle, like a wine bottle or tall drinking glass, works well. Dust it with flour so the dough doesn't stick.
what can i use instead of parchment paper | no parchment paper | can i use foil instead of parchment => Grease and flour the pan, or use foil sprayed with oil for many things. Don't use wax paper in the oven, the wax can melt and smoke.
what can i use instead of a cookie cutter | no cookie cutter | cut cookies without a cutter => Use the rim of a drinking glass for circles, or make a paper template and cut around it with a butter knife.
what can i use instead of a whisk | no whisk what do i use | mixing eggs with a fork => A fork works for eggs and thin batters. Shaking cream in a sealed jar can whip it, and an electric mixer works for bigger jobs.
what can i use instead of a muffin tin | no muffin pan | bake cupcakes without a tin => Stand sturdy foil cupcake liners on a baking sheet, doubled up, or bake the batter as a loaf or square cake and adjust the time.
what can i use instead of a steamer basket | steam without a steamer | make a steamer at home => Set a metal colander or heatproof plate on three balls of foil inside a pot with a little water, then cover. A microwave with a covered bowl and splash of water also steams.
what can i use instead of soy sauce | soy sauce substitute | low sodium soy alternative => Tamari is a close swap and usually gluten free. Coconut aminos are sweeter and lower in salt. Worcestershire works in small amounts for flavor.
what can i use instead of ricotta | ricotta substitute | cottage cheese instead of ricotta => Blended cottage cheese is a close swap. Mascarpone or cream cheese thinned with a little milk also work but are richer.
what can i use instead of parmesan | parmesan substitute | no parmesan cheese => Pecorino Romano is the closest, a bit saltier. Any hard aged cheese works, and nutritional yeast gives a cheesy taste without dairy.
what can i use instead of evaporated milk | evaporated milk substitute | make evaporated milk => Simmer milk gently until it reduces by more than half, stirring often. Or use the same amount of half and half or light cream.
what can i use instead of sweetened condensed milk | condensed milk substitute | make condensed milk at home => Simmer 2 cups of milk with about 2/3 cup sugar on low, stirring, until it reduces to about 1 cup and thickens, which takes a while. Let it cool before using.
what can i use instead of yeast | no yeast bread | bread without yeast => Make a quick bread or soda bread that uses baking soda or baking powder to rise. It won't taste exactly like yeast bread, but it's ready much faster.
what can i use instead of corn syrup | corn syrup substitute | no corn syrup for candy => Honey, maple syrup or golden syrup can work in many recipes. For candy, the result may crystallize more easily.
what can i use instead of cream of tartar | cream of tartar substitute | what does cream of tartar do => Cream of tartar steadies whipped egg whites and adds tang. Use about three times as much lemon juice or white vinegar in its place for egg whites.
what can i use instead of ketchup | ketchup substitute | make ketchup at home => Simmer tomato paste with a little vinegar, sugar, salt and onion powder for a quick homemade ketchup. Barbecue sauce or salsa also work on burgers.
what can i use instead of eggs for breading | egg wash substitute | breading without eggs => Use milk, buttermilk, a thin mix of flour and water, or a thin layer of mayonnaise or mustard to make the crumbs stick.
what can i use instead of eggs in pancakes | eggless pancakes | pancakes with no eggs => Use a quarter cup of mashed banana or applesauce, or a flax egg, per egg. Or just add a little extra milk and baking powder, pancakes still turn out fine.
can i use salted butter instead of unsalted | salted vs unsalted butter baking | does it matter if butter is salted => Yes, just cut the added salt in the recipe a little, about 1/4 teaspoon per stick of butter. Bakers like unsalted because it gives more control.
can i use quick oats instead of rolled oats | old fashioned vs quick oats | instant oats in cookies => Usually yes. Quick oats are cut smaller and cook faster, so cookies will be a bit softer and less chewy. Steel cut oats don't swap in baking.
can i use frozen fruit instead of fresh | frozen berries in muffins | baking with frozen fruit => Yes. For muffins, keep berries frozen and toss them in a little flour so they don't sink or bleed. In smoothies, frozen fruit makes them thicker and colder.
can i use regular yogurt instead of greek yogurt | swap greek yogurt => Yes, but regular yogurt is thinner. Strain it in a coffee filter or paper towel lined strainer for an hour to thicken it, or use less of the other liquids.
can i use margarine instead of butter in cookies | margarine for baking | is margarine same as butter => Stick margarine with at least 80 percent fat works, but spreads and tubs have more water and can make cookies spread and turn cakey. Flavor is less buttery.
what can i use instead of a cake pan | bake a cake without a cake pan | different pan size for cake => Any oven safe dish of similar size works, like a glass baking dish or loaf pan. Change baking time: deeper pans take longer, shallow ones less. Start checking early.
