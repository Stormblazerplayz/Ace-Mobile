# tag: life.art.drawing
how do i draw lips | drawing mouths | lips shading => Draw the line where the lips meet first, shaped like a stretched M with a dip in the middle. Add the upper lip, usually thinner, and a fuller lower lip, then shade under the lower lip.
how do i draw ears | drawing ears | ear shape => Ears sit between the eyebrow line and the bottom of the nose. Draw a C shape, then add the inner curves like a backward question mark.
how do i draw a side profile | profile face | side view face => Draw a circle for the skull, add a jaw line slanting down to the chin, then mark the brow, nose, lips and chin from the front edge. The ear sits just behind the middle of the head.
how do i draw a smile | drawing a smile | smiling face => Curve the mouth up at the corners, push the cheeks up and slightly squint the lower eyelids. Real smiles show in the eyes, not just the mouth.
how do i draw feet | drawing feet | shoes drawing => Start with a wedge or triangle shape for the foot, add the ankle bumps and the toes as small rounded boxes. The big toe is separate, and the others curve down in size.
how do i draw clothes folds | clothing folds | drapery drawing => Folds come from where fabric is pulled or bunched, like elbows, knees and waist. Draw lines radiating from those tension points and shade the inner side of each fold.
how do i draw a running pose | running pose | action pose drawing => Use a strong diagonal line of action, lean the body forward, and swing the opposite arm and leg forward together. Bend the knees and elbows a lot for energy.
how do i draw a superhero | superhero drawing | heroic proportions => Use heroic proportions about 8 to 9 heads tall, broad shoulders, a strong pose with a clear line of action, and a bold costume with a simple logo.
how do i draw a dinosaur | dinosaur drawing | t rex drawing => Start with simple shapes: an oval body, a long tapering tail, a neck and head. Add thick legs with the right number of toes, then texture like scales.
how do i draw a bird | bird drawing | birds flying drawing => Start with an egg shape for the body and a small circle for the head. Add a beak, a wing shape along the body, tail feathers and thin legs.
how do i draw a fish | fish drawing | underwater drawing => Draw a long oval or teardrop for the body, add a tail fin, top and bottom fins, an eye and gill line, then curved lines of scales.
how do i draw a wolf | wolf drawing | wolf head => Start with circles for the head, chest and hips, connect them with a back line, add a long muzzle, pointed ears and a bushy tail. Use short strokes for fur.
how do i draw grass | drawing grass | grass texture => Use quick, upward flicks of the pencil in clumps, with some blades overlapping. Make blades bigger and darker in front and smaller and lighter in the distance.
how do i draw a sunset | sunset drawing | sunset colors => Blend warm colors from yellow near the horizon through orange and pink to purple or blue higher up. Make silhouettes of land, trees or buildings dark.
how do i draw the ocean | ocean waves drawing | sea drawing => Draw a horizon line, make waves smaller and closer together near the horizon and bigger up close, and leave white for foam and highlights.
how do i draw smoke | smoke drawing | steam drawing => Draw soft, curling, overlapping shapes that get wider and fainter as they rise. Blend edges softly and erase highlights.
how do i draw rain | rain drawing | rainy scene => Draw thin, slanted lines in the same direction, with darker clouds above. Add splashes and ripples on the ground and reflections on wet surfaces.
how do i draw snow | snow drawing | winter scene => Leave the paper white for snow, shade only shadows with light blues or grays, and add snow piled on top of branches and roofs.
how do i draw a castle | castle drawing | medieval castle => Start with box shapes for walls and cylinders for towers, add battlements along the tops, a gate, small windows and flags. Use perspective for depth.
how do i draw a house | house drawing | house in perspective => Draw a box for the walls and a triangle or sloped shape for the roof, then add a door, windows and chimney. Use two point perspective to make it look 3D.
how do i draw a road in perspective | road perspective | road vanishing point => Put a vanishing point on the horizon and draw the road's edges as two lines meeting there. Dashes in the middle get smaller and closer together as they go back.
how do i draw a bike | bicycle drawing | bike wheels drawing => Start with two equal circles for wheels, then draw the frame as two triangles connecting them, add the seat, handlebars, pedals and chain.
how do i draw a plane | airplane drawing | jet drawing => Draw a long tube for the body, add swept wings across the middle and a tail with a fin, then windows and engines under the wings.
how do i draw a drone | drone drawing | quadcopter sketch => Draw a small center body, four arms in an X shape, and a circle or ellipse at the end of each arm for the propellers. Add a camera underneath.
how do i draw a spaceship | spaceship drawing | sci fi ship => Start with simple 3D shapes like cylinders, wedges and boxes, add engines with glowing exhaust, panels and windows. Keep the silhouette clear and interesting.
how do i draw stars and galaxies | galaxy drawing | space painting => On dark paper or a dark background, spatter white paint with a toothbrush for stars, blend soft swirls of purple, blue and pink for nebulas, and add a few bigger stars with sparkle lines.
how do i draw a sword | sword drawing | weapon drawing => Draw a long straight or curved blade with a center ridge, a crossguard, a wrapped grip and a pommel at the end. Add shine lines on the blade.
how do i draw armor | armor drawing | knight armor => Draw the body first, then wrap overlapping plates around it like tubes. Add rivets, edges and strong highlights to show the metal is shiny.
how do i draw food | food drawing | cute food => Start with simple shapes, add texture like crumbs or drips, and use warm colors and highlights to make it look tasty.
how do i draw shiny metal | metal shading | chrome drawing => Use strong contrast with very dark darks right next to bright white highlights, and show reflections of the surroundings in the surface.
how do i draw glass | glass drawing | transparent objects => Draw the outline lightly, show what's behind the glass slightly distorted, and add sharp white highlights and a few dark edges.
how do i draw a sphere | shading a sphere | ball shading => Draw a circle, pick a light direction, leave a highlight, shade gradually darker away from the light, add a core shadow, reflected light along the edge and a cast shadow.
how do i draw a cylinder | cylinder drawing | ellipses => Draw two ellipses of the same size, one above the other, and connect their sides with straight lines. Shade one side darker for a rounded look.
how do i draw an ellipse | drawing ellipses | ellipse in perspective => Move your whole arm, not just your wrist, and trace the shape in the air a few times before touching the paper. Ellipses are flatter the closer they are to eye level.
how do i draw straight lines | straight lines freehand | ghosting lines => Mark the start and end points, practice the motion above the paper, then draw quickly from your shoulder in one confident stroke.
how do i draw smooth curves | smooth lines | confident lines => Draw quickly and confidently instead of slowly, use your elbow and shoulder, and rotate the paper so the curve feels natural.
how do i draw a map | fantasy map | draw a world map => Sketch the coastline with uneven, natural edges, add mountains, rivers flowing to the sea, forests and cities, then a compass rose, a legend and names.
how do i draw a cute character | chibi | kawaii style => Use a big head, small body, large eyes set low on the face, rounded shapes and simple features. This style is often called chibi or kawaii.
how do i draw a monster | monster drawing | creature design => Mix parts from different animals, play with odd proportions, add features like horns, extra eyes or teeth, and keep the silhouette strong and easy to read.
how do i draw a pokemon | pokemon drawing | draw pikachu => Look at a reference, break the character into simple shapes like circles and ovals, sketch lightly, then refine and color. Drawing fan art is great practice.
how do i draw a minecraft character | minecraft drawing | pixel character => Draw a cube head on a rectangle body with rectangle arms and legs. Use graph paper to color in square pixels for the face and clothes.
how do i blend colored pencils | colored pencil blending | layering colored pencils => Layer colors lightly, building up slowly, then blend with a colorless blender pencil, a white pencil or by burnishing with firm pressure.
how do i use markers without streaks | marker streaks | coloring with markers => Color in small circles or quick strokes while the ink is still wet, keep moving, and go over areas in one direction. Alcohol markers blend best.
how do i paint with oils | oil painting beginners | oil paint drying time => Oil paints dry slowly, from days to weeks, so you can blend for a long time. Use the fat over lean rule, adding more oil in upper layers. Use good ventilation and an adult's help with solvents.
how do i paint trees | painting trees | tree painting => Paint the trunk and main branches first, then dab leaves with a sponge or round brush in groups of color, darker underneath and lighter where light hits.
what paper should i use for watercolor | watercolor paper | cold press vs hot press => Use thick watercolor paper of at least 300 gsm (140 lb) so it doesn't warp. Cold press has texture and is great for beginners, while hot press is smooth.
what is a mannequin for drawing | drawing mannequin | wooden figure => A drawing mannequin is a small wooden figure with movable joints that you pose to practice body positions and proportions.
how do i draw from imagination | draw without reference | imagination drawing => Build a mental library by drawing from life and reference a lot, break ideas into simple shapes, and look up references for details you're unsure of.
how do i stay consistent drawing a character | consistent character | same character different angles => Make a character sheet showing your character from the front, side and back with key details, colors and proportions, and refer to it every time.
what is a speedpaint | speed painting | timelapse art => A speedpaint is a video of an artwork being made, sped up so you can watch the whole process in a few minutes.
what is fan art | fan art | is fan art legal => Fan art is artwork based on characters from shows, games, books or movies. It's great practice, though selling it can break copyright rules.
what is illustration | illustrator | illustration jobs => Illustration is art made to explain or go with something, like pictures in books, magazines, games or ads. Illustrators often work digitally.
what is an art career | jobs in art | art careers => Art careers include illustrator, animator, graphic designer, concept artist, game artist, art teacher and tattoo artist. Building a portfolio is key.
what is art school | art school | going to art school => Art schools teach drawing, painting, design, animation and more. Many people also learn through classes, online courses and lots of practice.
