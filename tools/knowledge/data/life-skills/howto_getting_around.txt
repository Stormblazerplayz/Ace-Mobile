# tag: life.howto.transport
how do i read a bus schedule | bus timetable | reading a transit schedule => Find your route number, then your stop or the nearest listed timepoint, and read across or down for times. Check whether it's a weekday, Saturday or Sunday schedule, since they differ.
how do i ride a subway | taking the metro | how does the subway work => Buy a ticket or tap a card or phone at the gate, check the line color or number and the direction (named by the last stop), and watch the station signs. Stand behind the yellow line and let people off before boarding.
how do i take a train | riding amtrak | train travel tips => Buy a ticket online or at the station, arrive early, check the board for your track or platform, and keep your ticket ready for the conductor. Store big bags on the racks.
what is a transfer on public transit | how do bus transfers work | switching buses => A transfer lets you change from one bus or train line to another, often for free within a time limit. Many cards and apps apply transfers automatically.
how do i plan a trip with transit | using maps for bus routes | find the best bus route => Map apps like Apple Maps or Google Maps have a transit option that shows routes, transfers, walking parts and times. Check the last bus time so you don't get stuck.
is it safe for kids to ride the bus alone | when can i take the bus alone | riding transit by myself => It depends on your age, maturity, the route and your parents' rules. Practice the trip with an adult first, carry a charged phone, know your stops and who to call, and sit near the driver.
what do i do if i miss my bus stop | missed my stop | got off at the wrong stop => Stay calm, get off at the next stop, and take a bus going the other way, or call a parent. Don't accept rides from strangers. Ask the driver or station staff for help.
what do traffic light colors mean | what does a yellow light mean | traffic signals => Green means go if it's safe, yellow means the light is about to turn red, so stop if you safely can, and red means stop. A flashing red works like a stop sign.
what do the walk signals mean | flashing hand at crosswalk | countdown crosswalk signal => A walking person means you may start crossing. A flashing hand or countdown means don't start crossing, but finish if you're already in the street. A solid hand means don't walk.
what shape is a stop sign | road sign shapes | what do sign colors mean => A stop sign is a red octagon. A yield sign is a downward triangle. Yellow diamonds are warnings, orange signs mean construction zones, and green signs give directions.
how old do you have to be to drive | when can i get my learners permit | driving age us => It depends on the state. In most US states you can get a learner's permit at 15 or 16 and a full license later after supervised practice. A few states allow permits at 14.
what is drivers ed | do i need drivers ed | drivers education => Driver's education teaches road rules and safe driving through classes and behind the wheel lessons. Many states require it for teens under 18.
what are the parts of a car | basic car parts | car parts names => Key parts include the engine (or electric motor and battery), transmission, brakes, steering, tires, suspension, battery, radiator and exhaust. The dashboard shows speed, fuel and warning lights.
what do car dashboard lights mean | check engine light | warning lights in a car => Red warning lights, like oil pressure or temperature, mean stop soon and safely. Yellow or orange, like check engine or low tire pressure, mean get it checked soon. Blue or green lights are just information.
how do i check tire pressure on a car | car tire pressure | psi for car tires => Use a tire gauge on the valve when tires are cold, and compare it to the number on the sticker inside the driver's door, not the number on the tire, which is the maximum.
how do i pump gas | filling up a car | gas station steps => Turn off the engine, pay at the pump or inside, pick the right fuel grade, put the nozzle in the tank and squeeze the handle. It stops on its own when full. Don't use your phone near the pump.
how does an electric car work | ev vs gas car | electric car charging => Electric cars use a big battery to power electric motors, and plug in to charge at home or at stations. They have no tailpipe emissions and fewer moving parts than gas cars.
what is a hybrid car | hybrid vs electric | how does a hybrid work => A hybrid has both a gas engine and an electric motor with a battery. It switches between them or uses both to save fuel. Plug in hybrids can also be charged from an outlet.
how should a seat belt fit | wearing a seatbelt correctly | seat belt across neck => The lap belt should sit low across your hips, not your stomach, and the shoulder belt across your chest and shoulder, not your neck. Never put the shoulder belt behind you.
how do i be a good passenger on a road trip | road trip tips | things to do on a road trip => Help with snacks and navigation, keep your area tidy, use headphones, and don't distract the driver. Download podcasts, books or games before you leave.
what are fun car games | games to play in the car => Try I Spy, 20 Questions, the license plate game (spot plates from different states), the alphabet game (find letters on signs in order) and Would You Rather.
how do i get a passport | kids passport | passport for a minor => For a US passport under 16, both parents or guardians usually go with the child to an acceptance facility with the application, proof of citizenship like a birth certificate, ID and a photo. Kids' passports last 5 years.
what is a passport for | do i need a passport | passport vs visa => A passport is an official ID that proves your citizenship and lets you travel between countries. A visa is permission from a country to enter, which some countries require on top of a passport.
what is jet lag | how to get over jet lag | jet lag tips => Jet lag is tiredness and messed up sleep after flying across time zones, because your body clock doesn't match local time. Get sunlight in the day, stay awake until local bedtime, and drink water.
what is a layover | layover vs connecting flight | how long is a layover => A layover is a stop between two flights where you wait, often changing planes. Short ones can be under an hour, so walk quickly to your next gate and check the boards.
how do i find my gate at the airport | reading an airport departure board | boarding pass explained => Your boarding pass shows your flight number, gate, boarding time and seat. Gates can change, so check the departure screens using your flight number.
what is a boarding group | when do i board the plane | airplane boarding zones => Airlines board in groups or zones printed on your boarding pass. Wait until your group is called, have your pass ready, and store big bags overhead and small ones under the seat in front.
what can i bring on a plane | carry on rules | what is not allowed on a plane => In the US, liquids in carry ons must be 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less in one quart sized bag. Sharp objects, large tools and fireworks aren't allowed. Spare batteries and power banks must go in carry ons, not checked bags.
what is a checked bag | checked vs carry on | bag weight limit => A checked bag goes in the plane's hold and you pick it up at baggage claim. Many airlines limit checked bags to 50 pounds (23 kg) and charge fees. Carry ons come into the cabin with you.
what do i do if my luggage is lost | lost bag at airport | airline lost my suitcase => Go to the airline's baggage service desk before leaving the airport and file a report with your bag tag number. Most lost bags are found and delivered within a few days.
what is an unaccompanied minor | flying alone as a kid | can a 12 year old fly alone => Many airlines have an unaccompanied minor service for kids flying alone, often required for ages 5 to 14, where staff escort the child. Rules and fees vary by airline.
how do i stay comfortable on a long flight | long flight tips => Bring headphones, downloaded shows, a sweater, a refillable water bottle (empty through security), snacks and a neck pillow. Get up and stretch when allowed.
how do i convert time zones | what time is it in another time zone | time difference => Find how many hours apart the zones are and add or subtract. Pacific time is 3 hours behind Eastern, so noon in New York is 9 a.m. in Los Angeles.
what is gmt and utc | utc meaning | coordinated universal time => UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the world's standard reference time, based on atomic clocks. GMT, Greenwich Mean Time, is the time zone in London in winter and nearly the same as UTC.
what should i do in a new city | exploring a new city | things to do when traveling => Look up top sights, parks and local foods, ride public transit, visit a museum or market, and try at least one food you've never had. Stay with your group and agree on a meeting spot.
how do i use a hotel key card | hotel tips for kids | staying in a hotel => Tap or insert the key card at the door, then put it in the slot inside if there is one to turn on the lights. Know your room number, keep the door locked and don't open it for strangers.
how do i exchange money for travel | foreign currency | spending money abroad => You can exchange cash at banks or airports, or use cards that work abroad. Airport exchange counters often have worse rates. Learn roughly what the local money is worth before you go.
how do i learn some words before a trip | travel phrases | basic phrases in another language => Learn hello, please, thank you, excuse me, yes, no, where is the bathroom, and how much. A translation app helps, and locals usually appreciate the effort.
what is an adapter for travel | do i need a power adapter | plug types around the world => Countries use different plug shapes and sometimes different voltages. A plug adapter changes the shape. Most phone and tablet chargers work on 100 to 240 volts, so they only need an adapter, not a converter.
how do i read a subway map | metro map colors | understanding transit maps => Each line has a color or number. Dots are stations, and stations where lines meet are transfer points. Transit maps aren't to scale, so distances can look wrong.
how do i ask for directions | asking a stranger for directions | how to ask where something is => Ask a store worker, staff member or police officer first. Say "Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to..." and repeat the directions back to check. Don't go anywhere with a stranger.
how do i give directions to someone | giving directions clearly | explaining how to get somewhere => Use landmarks and simple steps: "Go straight two blocks, turn left at the gas station, and it's on the right." Mention distances or how many lights or stops.
what is a roundabout | how do roundabouts work | traffic circle rules => A roundabout is a circular intersection where traffic flows one way around a center island. In the US, cars go counterclockwise and yield to traffic already in the circle.
why do some countries drive on the left | left side driving countries | uk drive on left => About a third of the world's people live in countries that drive on the left, including the UK, Ireland, Japan, India, Australia and New Zealand. It mostly comes from old British customs.
what is carpooling | why carpool | carpool lane => Carpooling means sharing rides, like several families taking turns driving kids to practice. It saves money and fuel. Many highways have carpool lanes for cars with 2 or more people.
how do i lock up my bike | best way to lock a bike | u lock vs cable lock => Use a sturdy U lock through the frame and back wheel, attached to a solid rack in a visible spot. Add a cable for the front wheel. Thin cable locks alone are easy to cut.
how do i register my bike | bike serial number | stolen bike recovery => Write down the serial number, usually stamped under the bottom bracket where the pedals connect, and take photos of your bike. Registering it with a site like Bike Index helps if it's stolen.
what is an e scooter | can kids ride electric scooters | e scooter rules => Electric scooters have a motor and battery. Rental e scooters usually require riders to be 18, and many places have age limits for private ones too. Always wear a helmet.
what is an e bike | e bike classes | how fast do e bikes go => An e bike has a motor that helps you pedal. In the US, class 1 helps only while pedaling up to 20 mph, class 2 has a throttle up to 20 mph, and class 3 assists up to 28 mph. Age rules vary by state.
