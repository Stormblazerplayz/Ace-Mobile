# tag: art.movements
what is impressionism | impressionist painters | impressionism art style => Impressionism started in France in the 1860s and 1870s. Artists like Claude Monet, Pierre Auguste Renoir and Edgar Degas painted light and everyday scenes with quick, visible brushstrokes, often outdoors.
where did the name impressionism come from | impression sunrise name | why is it called impressionism => A critic mocked Claude Monet's 1872 painting Impression, Sunrise, calling the artists "impressionists." They adopted the name.
what is post impressionism | post impressionist artists | after impressionism => Post Impressionism (about 1886 to 1905) built on Impressionism with bolder color, shapes and emotion. Key artists were Vincent van Gogh, Paul Cezanne, Paul Gauguin and Georges Seurat.
what is surrealism | surrealist art | dreamlike art movement => Surrealism, starting in the 1920s, painted dreamlike, strange scenes from the unconscious mind. Famous surrealists include Salvador Dali, Rene Magritte and Max Ernst.
what is pop art | pop art artists | andy warhol style => Pop Art, from the 1950s and 1960s, used images from popular culture like ads, comics and products. Andy Warhol and Roy Lichtenstein are famous pop artists.
what is renaissance art | renaissance painting style | characteristics of renaissance art => Renaissance art (about 1400 to 1600) focused on realism, perspective, human anatomy and classical themes from Greece and Rome. Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael are its stars.
what is baroque art | baroque painting | caravaggio style => Baroque art (about 1600 to 1750) is dramatic, with strong light and dark contrast, movement and emotion. Caravaggio, Rembrandt and Bernini are well known baroque artists.
what is abstract expressionism | jackson pollock drip painting | action painting => Abstract Expressionism started in New York in the 1940s. Jackson Pollock dripped and splattered paint on canvases on the floor, and Mark Rothko painted big glowing blocks of color.
what is expressionism | expressionist art | emotional art movement => Expressionism, in the early 1900s, used bold colors and distorted shapes to show emotion rather than how things look. Edvard Munch's The Scream influenced it.
what is realism in art | realism art movement | realist painters => Realism, in the mid 1800s, showed ordinary people and everyday life truthfully, instead of heroes and myths. Gustave Courbet led the movement in France.
what is romanticism in art | romantic painting | romanticism characteristics => Romanticism (about 1800 to 1850) celebrated emotion, nature and the sublime, like stormy seas and wild mountains. J. M. W. Turner and Caspar David Friedrich are well known romantic painters.
what is art nouveau | art nouveau style | alphonse mucha => Art Nouveau (about 1890 to 1910) used flowing, curvy lines inspired by plants and flowers in posters, buildings and jewelry. Alphonse Mucha's posters are famous examples.
what is art deco | art deco style | art deco buildings => Art Deco, popular in the 1920s and 1930s, used bold geometric shapes, symmetry and shiny materials. New York's Chrysler Building is a famous Art Deco skyscraper.
what is minimalism in art | minimalist art | less is more art => Minimalism, from the 1960s, uses very simple shapes, few colors and no decoration, focusing on the object itself.
what is fauvism | fauvist painters | wild beasts art => Fauvism, around 1905, used wild, unnatural bright colors. Its leader was Henri Matisse. Fauves means "wild beasts" in French, a critic's insult.
what is pointillism | pointillism technique | painting with dots => Pointillism uses tiny dots of pure color that blend in your eye from a distance. Georges Seurat and Paul Signac developed it in the 1880s.
what is ukiyo e | japanese woodblock prints | floating world art => Ukiyo e, meaning "pictures of the floating world," were Japanese woodblock prints from the 1600s to 1800s showing actors, landscapes and daily life. Hokusai and Hiroshige are famous ukiyo e artists.
what is the bauhaus | bauhaus design | bauhaus school => The Bauhaus was a German art and design school (1919 to 1933) that mixed art, crafts and technology. Its simple, functional style shaped modern furniture, buildings and graphic design.
what is folk art | folk art examples | traditional art => Folk art is made by people, often without formal training, using traditional styles of their community, like quilts, carvings, painted pottery and murals.
what is indigenous art | aboriginal dot painting | native american art => Indigenous peoples around the world have rich art traditions, like Aboriginal Australian dot paintings, Northwest Coast totem poles and Navajo weaving, often tied to stories and land.
what is anime art style | anime vs cartoon style | features of anime art => Anime style, from Japan, often features large expressive eyes, simplified noses, detailed hair, dynamic camera angles and speed lines. Styles vary a lot between studios and artists.
what is contemporary art | modern vs contemporary art | art today => Modern art usually means art from about the 1860s to the 1970s. Contemporary art means art made by living artists from about the 1970s to today, in any medium.
what is performance art | performance art examples | art as action => Performance art is art made through live actions by the artist, like movement, sound or endurance, instead of a finished object.
what is installation art | art installation | immersive art => Installation art is a large artwork that fills a space you can walk through, using objects, light, sound or video. Immersive light rooms are popular examples.
# tag: art.design
what is graphic design | what do graphic designers do | graphic design basics => Graphic design is creating visuals that communicate, like logos, posters, websites, game menus and book covers. Designers use type, color, images and layout.
what is typography | fonts vs typefaces | what is a font => Typography is the art of arranging text. A typeface is the design, like Helvetica, and a font is a specific style and size of it, like Helvetica Bold 12 point.
what is serif vs sans serif | serif fonts | sans serif fonts => Serifs are the small feet at the ends of letter strokes, like in Times New Roman. Sans serif fonts like Arial and Helvetica don't have them and look cleaner on screens.
how do i choose fonts for a poster | font pairing | how many fonts should i use => Use 2 fonts at most: a bold one for the title and a simple, readable one for the text. Make the title big and make sure there's strong contrast with the background.
what is white space in design | negative space design | why empty space matters => White space is empty space around and between elements. It makes designs easier to read and look more professional. Crowded designs feel messy.
what is visual hierarchy | design hierarchy | what to look at first => Visual hierarchy guides the viewer's eye in order of importance, using size, color, contrast and position. The most important thing should stand out most.
what is alignment in design | align text and images | grid in design => Alignment means lining elements up along common edges or centers. Using a grid makes layouts look organized and intentional.
what is a mood board | how to make a mood board | design inspiration board => A mood board is a collage of images, colors, textures and fonts that sets the look and feel of a project before you start designing.
what is a vector vs raster image | svg vs png => Raster images like PNG and JPG are made of pixels and get blurry when enlarged. Vector images like SVG use math shapes and scale to any size without losing quality.
what is concept art | concept artist | concept art for games => Concept art is early artwork used to explore and plan characters, worlds and objects for games, films and animation before they're built.
what is industrial design | product design | designing objects => Industrial design is designing everyday products like phones, bikes, chairs and controllers so they look good, are easy to use and can be manufactured.
what is architecture | what do architects do | how to become an architect => Architecture is designing buildings and spaces. Architects plan how buildings look, work and stay safe, and usually need a degree and a license.
what is ux design | user experience design => UI (user interface) design is how an app looks: buttons, colors and layout. UX (user experience) design is how it works and feels to use, so it's easy and enjoyable.
what is a color palette for a brand | brand colors | choosing brand colors => A brand palette is a small set of colors, often 1 to 3 main colors plus neutrals, used everywhere so the brand is recognizable.
# tag: art.photography
how do i take better photos with my ipad | phone photography tips | better photos on phone => Clean the lens, use natural light, tap to focus, use the grid for the rule of thirds, get closer instead of zooming, and try different angles like low or overhead.
what is the golden hour | golden hour photography | best light for photos => The golden hour is the time shortly after sunrise and before sunset, when sunlight is soft, warm and low, making photos glow.
what are leading lines in photography | leading lines | composition lines => Leading lines are lines in a scene, like roads, fences or rivers, that guide the viewer's eye toward the subject.
what is depth of field | blurry background photo | portrait mode => Depth of field is how much of a photo is in sharp focus. A shallow depth of field blurs the background to make the subject stand out, which portrait mode on phones imitates.
what is aperture shutter speed and iso | exposure triangle | camera settings basics => Aperture controls how much light enters and the background blur, shutter speed controls motion blur, and ISO controls brightness sensitivity but adds grain when high.
how do i take action photos | freeze motion photography | photos of mountain biking => Use a fast shutter speed like 1/1000 second, burst mode, and focus ahead where the rider will be. Shooting from low angles makes jumps look bigger.
what is long exposure photography | light trails | silky water photos => Long exposure uses a slow shutter speed, often seconds long, with the camera on a tripod, to blur moving things like car lights or waterfalls.
how do i take good photos of my art | photographing artwork | scan vs photo art => Use even, soft daylight without glare, hold the camera straight above or level with the art, fill the frame, and adjust brightness and crop afterward. A scanner works for flat drawings.
what is photo editing | basic photo edits | how to edit photos => Common edits are cropping, straightening, adjusting brightness and contrast, warmth, and saturation. Light edits usually look better than heavy filters.
