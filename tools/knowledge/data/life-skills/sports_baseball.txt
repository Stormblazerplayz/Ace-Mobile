# tag: sports.baseball
how does baseball work | baseball rules for beginners | how do you play baseball => Two teams take turns batting and fielding. Batters try to hit the pitched ball and run around four bases to score runs, and the fielding team tries to get three outs to end the half inning. After nine innings, the team with more runs wins.
how many strikes is an out | three strikes rule | how many balls is a walk => Three strikes is a strikeout. Four balls, pitches outside the strike zone the batter doesn't swing at, is a walk, sending the batter to first base.
what is the strike zone | how is a strike called | strike zone size => The strike zone is over home plate, from about the middle of the batter's chest down to the hollow just below the knees. A pitch through it is a strike even if the batter doesn't swing.
what is a foul ball | can a foul ball be strike three | foul ball rules => A foul ball lands outside the foul lines. It counts as a strike if the batter has fewer than two strikes, but a foul can't be strike three, except a foul bunt.
how far apart are the bases | distance between bases | how far is it from home to first => In MLB and high school, the bases are 90 feet (27.4 m) apart. In Little League they're 60 feet apart.
how far is the pitcher's mound from home plate | pitching distance | mound to plate distance => In MLB it's 60 feet 6 inches (18.4 m) from the pitching rubber to home plate. In Little League it's 46 feet.
what are the positions in baseball | baseball position numbers | what does a shortstop do => The nine positions are pitcher (1), catcher (2), first base (3), second base (4), third base (5), shortstop (6), left field (7), center field (8) and right field (9). The shortstop plays between second and third, covering lots of ground.
how do scorekeepers write plays | scoring 6 4 3 double play | what does 6 4 3 mean => Scorekeepers number positions to write plays quickly. A 6-4-3 double play goes from shortstop to second baseman to first baseman.
what is a double play | how to turn a double play | triple play meaning => A double play is getting two outs on one play, like a ground ball where the fielders get the runner at second and the batter at first. A triple play gets three outs and is very rare.
what is a force out | force play vs tag | when do you have to tag the runner => On a force play, the runner must move to the next base because the batter became a runner, so fielders just touch the base with the ball. When the runner isn't forced, fielders must tag them with the ball.
what is tagging up | tag up rule | can you run on a fly ball => On a caught fly ball, runners must go back and touch their base after the catch before running to the next base. That's tagging up, and it's how sacrifice flies score runs.
what is a sacrifice fly | sac bunt meaning | what is a sacrifice in baseball => A sacrifice fly is a caught fly ball that lets a runner tag up and score. A sacrifice bunt is a bunt that moves a runner up while the batter is put out.
what is the infield fly rule | infield fly explained | why is there an infield fly rule => With runners on first and second or the bases loaded and fewer than two outs, an easy pop up in the infield is called an automatic out. It stops fielders from dropping it on purpose to get a double play.
what is a balk | balk rule | why is it a balk => A balk is an illegal motion by the pitcher with runners on base, like starting a pitch and stopping. Every runner moves up one base.
what is a designated hitter | dh rule | does the national league have the dh => The designated hitter bats instead of the pitcher. Since 2022, both MLB leagues use the DH.
what is the pitch clock | mlb pitch clock rules | how long does a pitcher have => As of 2025, MLB pitchers have 15 seconds to start their pitch with the bases empty and 18 seconds with runners on. The pitch clock started in 2023 and made games much shorter.
what is a home run | inside the park home run | grand slam meaning => A home run is a hit that lets the batter circle all the bases and score, usually by hitting it over the outfield fence. A grand slam is a home run with the bases loaded, scoring four runs.
what is an rbi | runs batted in | what does rbi mean => RBI stands for runs batted in, the number of runs that score because of a batter's hit, walk, sacrifice or other play.
what is a batting average | how is batting average calculated | what is a good batting average => Batting average is hits divided by at bats. A .300 average means three hits every ten at bats, which is very good in MLB.
what is an era in baseball | earned run average | what is a good era => ERA, earned run average, is how many earned runs a pitcher allows every nine innings. Under 3.00 is excellent in MLB.
what is a no hitter | perfect game vs no hitter | how many perfect games in mlb => A no hitter means a pitcher or team allows no hits in a full game. A perfect game means no batter reaches base at all. As of 2025, there have been 24 official perfect games in MLB history.
what is a walk off | walk off home run | walk off win => A walk off is when the home team scores the winning run in the bottom of the last inning, ending the game instantly.
what is a bunt | how do you bunt | squeeze play => A bunt is holding the bat out and gently tapping the ball into the infield instead of swinging. A squeeze play is bunting to bring a runner home from third.
what is a stolen base | how do you steal a base | stealing home => A stolen base is when a runner advances while the pitcher throws home, without a hit. Watch the pitcher's front foot or first move, then sprint and slide.
what is a slider | types of pitches | what is a curveball => A fastball is thrown hard and straight, a curveball spins forward and drops, a slider breaks sideways late, and a changeup looks like a fastball but arrives slower to fool the batter.
how fast is the fastest pitch | fastest pitch ever | aroldis chapman fastest pitch => Aroldis Chapman threw a 105.8 mph pitch in 2010, the fastest officially recorded in MLB as of 2025.
how many teams are in mlb | number of mlb teams | al vs nl => MLB has 30 teams, split into the American League and National League, each with East, Central and West divisions.
what is the world series | how does the world series work | mlb championship => The World Series is MLB's championship, a best of seven series between the American League and National League pennant winners.

which team has won the most world series | most world series titles | yankees championships => As of 2025, the New York Yankees have won the most World Series titles with 27.

who was babe ruth | why is babe ruth famous | the bambino => Babe Ruth was a legendary slugger and pitcher, mostly with the New York Yankees in the 1920s and 1930s. He hit 714 home runs, the record for nearly 40 years, and changed baseball into a power hitting game.
who was the last player to hit 400 | 400 batting average | ted williams 406 => Ted Williams of the Boston Red Sox hit .406 in 1941, the last time an MLB player batted .400 or better for a full season, as of 2025.
