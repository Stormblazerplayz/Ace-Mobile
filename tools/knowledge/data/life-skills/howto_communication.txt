# tag: life.howto.writing
how do i write an email | email writing for kids | how to write a proper email => Write a short subject that says what it's about, start with a greeting like "Hi Ms. Lee," say what you need in a few clear sentences, then end with "Thanks," and your name. Reread it before sending.
how do i write an email to my teacher | emailing a teacher | what to say in an email to a teacher => Use a clear subject like "Question about science homework", greet them by name, say which class you're in, ask your question politely and briefly, and sign off with thanks and your full name.
what should the subject line of an email say | how to write a subject line | email subject tips => Keep it short and specific so they know what it's about before opening it, like "Late assignment for Period 3" or "Can I borrow the drill Saturday".
how do i end an email | email sign off examples | how to close an email => Common sign offs are "Thanks," "Thank you," "Best," or "Sincerely," followed by your name on the next line. "Thanks" is friendly and works almost everywhere.
how do i write a thank you note | thank you card for a gift | what to write in a thank you card => Greet the person, thank them for the specific gift, say how you'll use it or why you like it, add a warm line about them, then sign off. For example: "Thank you for the drone, I've already flown it in the park!"
how soon should i send a thank you note | how long do i have to send a thank you card | is it too late for a thank you note => Try to send it within a week or two of getting the gift. A late thank you is still much better than none.
how do i thank someone for money | thank you note for a gift card | what to say for a cash gift => Thank them for their generosity and say what you plan to use it for, like "I'm saving it toward a new bike helmet." It's polite to mention it without naming the amount.
how do i write a letter | parts of a friendly letter | how to format a letter => A friendly letter has the date, a greeting like "Dear Grandma," the body paragraphs, a closing like "Love," or "Your friend," and your signature.
how do i address an envelope | where do you write the address on an envelope | how to mail a letter => Write the receiver's name and address in the middle of the front, your return address in the top left corner, and put a stamp in the top right corner.
how do i write a formal letter | business letter format | letter to a company => Put your address and the date at the top, then the receiver's address. Start with "Dear Mr. or Ms. Name," explain your reason clearly in short paragraphs, and close with "Sincerely," and your name.
how do i write a complaint politely | complaining to a company | product broke what do i write => Say what you bought and when, explain the problem with facts, say what you'd like, like a refund or replacement, and stay polite. Include photos or order numbers if you have them.
how do i write an apology letter | sorry note | how to write a letter saying sorry => Say clearly what you did, say you're sorry without excuses, explain how it affected them, and say what you'll do differently. Keep it short and sincere.
how do i write a birthday card | what to write in a birthday card | birthday message ideas => Wish them a happy birthday, mention something you like about them or a fun memory, and wish them a great year. Sign it with your name.
how do i write a get well card | get well soon message | what to write to someone who is sick => Keep it short and kind: say you're sorry they aren't feeling well, you're thinking of them, and you hope they feel better soon. A funny line can cheer them up.
how do i write a sympathy card | what to write when someone dies | condolence message for a friend => Keep it simple: "I'm so sorry about your grandpa. I'm thinking of you." Sharing a kind memory of the person is nice too. You don't need to fix their sadness.
how do i reply to a group chat politely | group chat manners | texting etiquette => Keep messages short, don't spam, avoid inside jokes that leave people out, and never share screenshots or photos of others without permission. If something gets mean, step away.
how do i text politely | texting manners | rules for texting => Don't text really late or early, reread before sending, use full sentences with adults, and don't say anything you wouldn't say in person. Tone can be misread in text, so add clarity if needed.

# tag: life.howto.manners
what are good manners | basic manners for kids | why are manners important => Good manners include saying please and thank you, waiting your turn, not interrupting, holding doors, greeting people and cleaning up after yourself. They show respect and make people want to be around you.
how do i introduce myself | introduce myself to someone new | what to say when meeting someone => Smile, make eye contact and say "Hi, I'm Storm," then ask their name. Repeating their name back, like "Nice to meet you, Sam," helps you remember it.
how do i introduce two people | introducing friends to each other | how to make an introduction => Say each person's name and one thing they have in common, like "Alex, this is Jordan. Jordan also builds robots." That gives them something to talk about.
how do i shake hands | how to give a good handshake | handshake tips => Make eye contact, smile, grip firmly but not too hard, shake two or three times and let go. Say "nice to meet you" while you do it.
what are good table manners | dinner table manners | how to eat politely => Chew with your mouth closed, don't talk with food in your mouth, put your napkin on your lap, ask people to pass things instead of reaching across, and wait until everyone has food to start.
how do i behave at a restaurant | restaurant manners | how to order at a restaurant => Use an indoor voice, say please and thank you to the server, stay at the table, and look at the menu before they come back. Tell them clearly what you'd like and any allergies.
how do i order food by myself | ordering at a restaurant for kids | i'm nervous to order => Decide first, look at the server and say "Can I please have the cheeseburger with fries?" They'll ask questions like how you want it cooked, so just answer and say thanks.
how do i be a good guest at someone's house | manners at a friend's house | visiting a friends house rules => Greet the parents, follow their house rules, ask before using things, help clean up and thank them when you leave. Taking your shoes off if they do is polite.
how do i be a good host | having friends over tips | hosting a playdate => Greet them at the door, offer a drink or snack, let them help choose what to do, and make sure everyone is included. Walk them to the door when they leave.
how do i interrupt politely | how to get someone's attention | excuse me manners => Wait for a pause, then say "Excuse me" and ask your question. If it's an emergency, it's okay to interrupt right away.
how do i say no politely | turning someone down nicely | how to decline an invitation => Thank them, say no clearly and kindly, and give a short reason if you want: "Thanks for inviting me, but I can't make it Saturday. Have fun!"
how do i rsvp to a party | what does rsvp mean | do i need to rsvp => RSVP comes from French and means "please respond." Tell the host yes or no by the date on the invitation so they know how many people to plan for.
how do i respect someone's personal space | what is personal space | standing too close to people => Personal space is the area around someone they like to keep to themselves, often about an arm's length with people you don't know well. Ask before hugging or touching.
how do i be polite in public | manners in public places | how to act in a store => Keep your voice down, don't block the way, hold the door for the person behind you, wait your turn in lines and clean up after yourself.
how do i talk to adults | talking to grown ups | i get nervous talking to adults => Make eye contact, speak clearly, use their name like Mr. or Ms. unless they tell you otherwise, and answer with full sentences. Adults usually love when kids are confident and polite.
how do i say thank you properly | ways to say thank you | showing gratitude => Be specific about what you're thankful for: "Thanks for helping me fix my bike chain, I couldn't have done it without you." Specific thanks mean much more.
when should i say excuse me | when to say excuse me vs sorry | excuse me or pardon => Say excuse me when you need to get past someone, interrupt, or after a burp or sneeze. Say sorry when you've bumped into or upset someone.
how do i cover a sneeze or cough | sneeze into your elbow | why sneeze into your arm => Cough or sneeze into a tissue or the inside of your elbow, not your hands, so germs don't get on everything you touch. Then wash your hands or use sanitizer.

# tag: life.howto.calls
how do i answer the phone politely | phone manners | what to say when you answer the phone => Say "Hello?" or "Hello, this is Storm." If the call is for someone else, say "One moment please" and go get them instead of yelling.
how do i make a phone call | i'm nervous to call someone | calling a store tips => Write down what you want to say and any questions first. Say hi, your name and why you're calling, then listen and take notes. Say thanks and goodbye at the end.
how do i leave a voicemail | voicemail tips | what to say in a voicemail => Say your name, who you are, why you're calling and your phone number slowly. Repeat the number at the end, and keep it under 30 seconds.
how do i take a phone message | taking a message for my parents | writing down a phone message => Ask for their name, number and what it's about, write it down with the time, read the number back to check it and tell the person as soon as you can.
what should i not say to a stranger on the phone | home alone and the phone rings | phone safety for kids => Never say you're home alone or give your address. Say your parent is busy and can't come to the phone and offer to take a message.
how do i act on a video call | video call manners | zoom etiquette for kids => Find a quiet spot with light in front of you, mute when you're not talking, look at the camera when you speak and don't eat or walk around. Wait for a pause to talk, or use the raise hand button.
how do i look good on a video call | best lighting for video calls | camera at eye level => Face a window or lamp so the light is in front of you, not behind. Put the camera at eye level by stacking books, and check what's visible behind you.
how do i mute myself on a call | muting on facetime | how to turn off my mic => In most apps, tap the microphone icon so it shows a line through it. On FaceTime on iPad, tap the screen and then the microphone button. Tap it again to unmute.
how do i join an online class | online school tips | how to do well in virtual class => Join a few minutes early, test your mic and camera, have your materials ready, close other tabs and take notes like you would in person.

# tag: life.howto.speaking
how do i speak in front of the class | public speaking tips for kids | presenting to my class => Practice out loud several times, know your first lines really well, look at a few friendly faces, speak a little slower than normal and pause between points. Nerves are normal and usually go away once you start.
how do i stop shaking when i present | hands shaking during a speech | physical nerves when presenting => Take a few slow deep breaths before you start, hold your notes or rest your hands on the desk, and plant your feet. The shaking feels bigger to you than it looks to the audience.
how do i stop saying um | too many ums | filler words in speeches => Pause silently instead of saying um, it sounds more confident. Practicing your talk helps because you know what comes next, and recording yourself shows where you tend to say it.
how do i make eye contact during a speech | where should i look when presenting | eye contact tips => Look at one person for a sentence, then another in a different part of the room. If eye contact is hard, look at foreheads or just above people's heads.
how do i make a speech interesting | engaging presentation tips | how to grab the audience's attention => Start with a hook, like a surprising fact, a question or a short story. Use examples, pictures and a little humor, and change your voice to keep it lively.
how do i remember my speech | forgetting what to say in a speech | using note cards => Use note cards with a few key words for each point instead of full sentences. Practice the order of your points, and if you lose your place, pause, glance at your card and keep going.
what do i do if i mess up during a presentation | i forgot my words on stage | mistake in front of the class => Take a breath, smile and just pick up from where you remember. Most people won't notice, and those who do will forget fast. Everyone has been there.
how loud should i talk when presenting | speak louder in class | my voice is too quiet => Speak loud enough that the person in the back row can hear you easily. Breathe from your belly, stand tall and aim your voice at the back wall.
how do i answer questions after a presentation | q and a tips | what if i don't know the answer => Listen to the whole question, repeat it if needed, and answer briefly. If you don't know, it's fine to say "Good question, I'm not sure, but I can find out."
how do i give a speech for student council | student council speech ideas | how to run for class president => Introduce yourself, share two or three specific ideas you'd really work on, show why you care, add a little humor, and keep it short. End with a catchy line asking for their vote.
how do i prepare for an interview | interview tips for kids | what do they ask in an interview => Research the place, practice answering common questions like "tell me about yourself" and "why do you want this", dress neatly, arrive early, and have a question ready to ask them.
how do i talk about myself | tell me about yourself answer | how to describe myself => Share a few things: what you like to do, something you're good at or proud of, and what you're working toward. For example: "I build games and robots, and I'm learning C++ this year."
how do i debate | debate tips | how to win an argument politely => State your point clearly, back it up with evidence, listen to the other side, and answer their points calmly. Attack ideas, never the person.
how do i persuade someone | how to convince my parents | persuasive tips => Know what they care about, give clear reasons and facts, admit any downside and how you'll handle it, and stay calm and respectful. Offering a fair compromise helps a lot.
how do i ask my parents for something | convince my parents to get me something | how to ask for a new phone => Pick a calm time, explain why you want it and how it helps, show you've thought about cost and rules, and offer to help pay or earn it. Accept their answer politely, which makes a yes more likely next time.
