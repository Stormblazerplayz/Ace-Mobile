# tag: life.safety.body
what are body safety rules | private parts rules | safe and unsafe touch => Your body belongs to you. No one should touch your private parts or ask you to touch theirs, except a doctor or parent for health reasons with a trusted adult present. If anyone does, say no, get away and tell a trusted adult.
what is the difference between a secret and a surprise | secrets vs surprises | should i keep a secret => A surprise, like a birthday gift, is fun and eventually told. A secret that makes you feel uncomfortable, scared or confused, especially from an adult, should be told to a trusted adult. Safe adults don't ask kids to keep secrets.
what are tricky people | tricky people vs strangers | stranger danger => Most strangers are fine. Tricky people are anyone, even someone you know, who asks a kid for help, asks you to keep a secret, or tries to get you to go somewhere without your parent's okay. Say no and tell an adult.
what is consent | consent meaning for kids | asking before touching => Consent means asking and getting a clear yes before doing something that affects someone, like hugging or borrowing their stuff. People can change their minds, and no means no.
what should i do if someone makes me feel uncomfortable | uncomfortable around an adult | someone gives me a bad feeling => Trust your gut. Get away from the person, go somewhere safe with other people, and tell a trusted adult. You won't be in trouble for speaking up.
why is vaping bad for kids | is vaping safe | vape dangers => Most vapes contain nicotine, which is very addictive and can harm the developing teen brain, affecting attention and learning. Vapes can also contain harmful chemicals. It's illegal to sell vapes to anyone under 21 in the US.
how do i say no to vaping or drugs | refuse a vape | peer pressure drugs => Try a simple "No thanks, I'm good," give a reason like "I have a game tomorrow," change the subject or leave. Real friends will accept it. You can also blame your parents, which is totally fine.
why are pills from friends dangerous | fake pills | counterfeit pills fentanyl => Pills that don't come from a pharmacy can be fake and may contain fentanyl, a drug so strong a tiny amount can kill. Never take any pill not prescribed to you or given by a trusted adult.
why is alcohol bad for teens | underage drinking | alcohol and the teen brain => The brain keeps developing until around age 25, and alcohol can harm memory and learning, lead to dangerous choices and cause poisoning. It's illegal for people under 21 to drink in the US.
what are signs of alcohol poisoning | someone drank too much | alcohol emergency => Signs include confusion, vomiting, slow or irregular breathing, pale or blue skin and passing out. Call 911, keep them on their side and don't leave them alone.
how do i read a medicine label | medicine dosage | over the counter medicine safety => Check the active ingredient, the dose for your age or weight, how often to take it and warnings. Only take medicine with a parent's okay, and use the measuring cup that comes with it.
why shouldnt i take someone elses medicine | sharing prescriptions | taking other peoples meds => Prescription medicine is chosen and dosed for one person. It may be the wrong strength, interact with other medicines or cause allergic reactions in someone else.
are vitamins safe to take like candy | gummy vitamins | too many vitamins => Gummy vitamins look like candy, but taking too many can be harmful, especially vitamins with iron, A or D. Only take the dose on the label.
what should i do if i accidentally take too much medicine | medicine overdose | took two doses => Tell an adult right away and call Poison Control at 1 800 222 1222 in the US. If someone is very sleepy, confused or having trouble breathing, call 911.
how do i read food labels for allergens | allergen labels | contains nuts label => US food labels must list major allergens, like milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. "May contain" means possible cross contact.
what is cross contact for allergies | allergy cross contamination | sharing food with allergies => Cross contact is when a tiny bit of an allergen gets into safe food, like using the same knife for peanut butter and jelly. For someone with a serious allergy, it can cause a reaction.
how do i help a friend with a food allergy | friend has a peanut allergy | allergy friend tips => Don't share food unless you're sure it's safe, wash hands after eating, know where their epinephrine is, and tell an adult right away if they show signs of a reaction.
how do i stay safe from mosquitoes | mosquito repellent | prevent mosquito bites => Use insect repellent with DEET, picaridin or oil of lemon eucalyptus (for ages 3 and up), wear long sleeves at dawn and dusk, and dump standing water where mosquitoes breed.
what should i do if i find a bat | bat in the house | can bats have rabies => Don't touch it. Bats can carry rabies. Leave the room, close the door and tell an adult. If anyone might have touched or been bitten by it, they need to see a doctor quickly.
what is rabies | how do you get rabies | rabies symptoms => Rabies is a virus spread through bites or scratches from infected animals like bats, raccoons, skunks and foxes. It's almost always deadly once symptoms appear, but shots right after exposure prevent it.
how do i stay safe from germs at school | avoid getting sick | stop spreading germs => Wash hands often with soap for 20 seconds, cough into your elbow, don't share drinks or utensils, keep your hands away from your face, and stay home when you're sick.
does hand sanitizer work | hand sanitizer vs soap | alcohol hand sanitizer => Sanitizer with at least 60 percent alcohol kills many germs, but soap and water works better on dirty or greasy hands and against some germs. Use sanitizer when you can't wash.
# tag: life.safety.places
why is it dangerous to play near train tracks | train track safety | how long does a train take to stop => Trains are quieter and faster than they seem, and a train can take a mile or more to stop. Only cross at marked crossings, never walk on tracks, and obey gates and lights.
why are hot cars dangerous | kids left in cars | car heats up fast => A car's inside can get dangerously hot within minutes, even on a mild day, and kids heat up faster than adults. Never stay in or let a child or pet stay in a parked car, and never play in cars or trunks.
what should i do if i get locked in a car | trapped in a car | honk the horn if stuck => Honk the horn over and over or turn on the hazard lights to get attention. Most newer cars have a glow in the dark trunk release handle inside.
how do i stay safe in a parking lot | parking lot safety | cars backing up => Hold an adult's hand if you're young, walk, stay alert, watch for backup lights and remember drivers may not see you. Never run between parked cars.
why shouldnt i go into construction sites | construction site danger | playing in abandoned buildings => Construction sites and abandoned buildings have holes, falling objects, sharp materials, unstable floors and machines. They're dangerous and often illegal to enter.
how do i stay safe during hunting season | hunting season hiking | wear orange in the woods => During hunting season, wear bright orange when hiking in the woods, stay on trails, and keep pets in orange too. Check local season dates.
are hot tubs safe for kids | hot tub safety | how long can kids stay in a hot tub => Hot water can overheat kids quickly. Many experts advise kids stay out of hot tubs or keep it short, around 5 to 10 minutes, at lower temperatures, with an adult present.
why is swimming in a quarry dangerous | quarry swimming | swimming in a pond => Quarries can have freezing cold deep water, steep drop offs, hidden rocks and machinery. Cold water shock can cause drowning even in strong swimmers. Swim only where there are lifeguards.
how do i stay safe on a zipline or ropes course | ropes course safety | zipline safety => Use only professional courses with trained staff, wear a fitted harness and helmet, follow every instruction, and never unclip unless told to.
how do i stay safe at an amusement park ride | ride safety | roller coaster rules => Follow height rules, keep hands, arms and legs inside, hold on, stay seated until the ride stops completely, and don't ride if you have conditions the warning signs list.
how do i stay safe at a sleepover | sleepover safety | when to call home => Know your parent's number, agree on a code word to call home if you feel unsafe, and never feel pressured to do anything you're uncomfortable with. You can always leave.
what is a family safe word | code word for kids | safety code word => A family code word is a secret word only your family knows. If someone says they're picking you up, ask for the code word. If they don't know it, don't go with them.
how do i stay safe walking home | walking home alone tips | safe route home => Use the same known route, walk with friends when you can, stay in well lit busy areas, keep headphones low, let a parent know when you leave and arrive, and trust your gut.
what should i do if i lose my phone | lost my phone | find my iphone => Use Find My on another device or iCloud.com to see it on a map, play a sound or mark it as lost. Tell your parents and change passwords if it might be stolen.
what should i do if i lose my wallet | lost wallet | lost debit card => Retrace your steps, check lost and found, and tell a parent. Freeze or cancel any cards right away through the bank app, and report any missing ID.
how do i stay safe at an atm | atm safety tips | using an atm safely => Use ATMs in busy, well lit places, cover the keypad when entering your PIN, put cash away right away, and look for anything stuck on the card slot that looks odd.
how do i stay safe on a school field trip | field trip safety | lost on a field trip => Stay with your buddy and group, know your chaperone, wear your name tag if given one, and if you get lost, stay where you are and ask staff or a police officer for help.
what should i do during a fire drill | fire drill rules | school fire alarm => Stop what you're doing, leave your stuff, walk quietly in line to the exit, go to your class's meeting spot and stay with your teacher for attendance.
what should i do if my friend gets hurt | friend got injured | friend fell and is hurt => Stay calm, don't move them if they might have a neck or back injury, get an adult right away, and call 911 for serious injuries. Stay with them and keep them calm.
what should i do if a little kid i am babysitting gets hurt | babysitting emergency | child injured while babysitting => Stay calm, give first aid if you know how, call 911 for serious injuries, then call the parents. Keep emergency numbers and the home address written down.
what should i do if a little kid swallows something | toddler swallowed something | child choking babysitting => If they're choking and can't cough or breathe, call 911 and give back blows and chest or abdominal thrusts if trained. If they swallowed something like a battery or medicine, call Poison Control or 911.
