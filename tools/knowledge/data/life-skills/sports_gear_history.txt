# tag: sports.gear
what size baseball bat should i use | bat size chart | how to pick a bat => A common test: stand the bat next to your leg, and the knob should reach about the middle of your palm with your arm hanging down. Check your league's bat rules, since many require USA or USSSA stamps.
what size baseball glove do i need | glove size for kids | infield vs outfield glove => Youth gloves are often 9 to 11.5 inches. Infielders use smaller gloves for quick transfers, and outfielders use longer ones for reach. Catchers and first basemen use special mitts.
what are cleats for | soccer vs football cleats | can i use soccer cleats for baseball => Cleats grip grass and dirt. Soccer cleats have no toe stud, which is required in soccer. Baseball and football cleats often have a front toe stud. Metal cleats are banned in many youth leagues.
how do i choose a hockey stick | hockey stick flex | hockey stick length => A common rule is the stick should reach your chin without skates or your nose on skates. Flex should be about half your body weight in pounds, and lower flex for kids makes shots easier.
how do i choose a tennis racket | tennis racket size for kids | racket length => Kids under 10 often use 19 to 25 inch rackets based on height. Most kids around 10 and up move to 26 inch, and adults use 27 inch. A bigger head is more forgiving.
what are shin guards for | how should shin guards fit | do i need shin guards => Shin guards protect your shins from kicks in soccer and field hockey and are required in most leagues. They should cover from just below the knee to above the ankle, under your socks.
how do i choose swim goggles | goggles that dont leak | swim goggle fit => Press the goggles to your eyes without the strap. If they stick for a second, they seal well. Adjust the strap so it's snug, not tight, and pick tinted lenses for outdoors.
what is a wetsuit for | how does a wetsuit work | wetsuit thickness => A wetsuit traps a thin layer of water against your skin that your body warms, keeping you warm in cold water. Thicker suits, like 4/3 or 5/4 mm, are for colder water.
how do i choose a skateboard deck size | deck width | skateboard width guide => Deck width is the main size. Kids often ride about 7.5 to 8 inches, and adults 8 to 8.5. Wider decks are more stable for ramps, and narrower ones flip easier for street tricks.
how do i choose ski length | ski size chart | how long should my skis be => Beginners often use skis that reach between the chin and nose. Longer skis are faster and more stable, and shorter ones turn easier. A ski shop can fit you.
how do i choose a snowboard size | snowboard length | snowboard sizing => A snowboard standing on end usually reaches between your chin and nose. Your weight matters more than height, and boot size affects board width.
what is a jockstrap and cup | athletic cup | do i need a cup => An athletic cup is a hard protective shell worn in a supporter or compression shorts to protect the groin. It's recommended in baseball, hockey, lacrosse and martial arts for boys.
what is a sports bra | sports bra fit | why wear a sports bra => A sports bra supports breasts during exercise to reduce movement and discomfort. It should be snug but let you breathe, with straps that don't dig in.
what is compression clothing | do compression clothes help | compression shorts => Compression clothing is tight stretchy gear. It can feel supportive and reduce chafing, but studies show only small effects on performance. Use what's comfortable.
why do athletes wear tape | kinesiology tape | athletic tape => Athletic tape supports joints like ankles and fingers. Stretchy kinesiology tape, often colorful, is used for support and comfort, though research on its benefits is mixed.
how do i clean my sports gear | smelly hockey gear | cleaning pads and helmets => Air out gear after every use, wash removable liners and jerseys, wipe hard gear with mild soap or sports gear spray, and let everything dry fully before packing it.
how long does a bike helmet last | when to replace a helmet | helmet expiration => Replace a helmet after any crash, even if it looks fine, since the foam may be crushed. Many makers suggest replacing it every 3 to 5 years.
# tag: sports.history
where did soccer come from | history of soccer | who invented soccer => Ball kicking games are thousands of years old, like cuju in ancient China. Modern soccer rules were written in England in 1863, when the Football Association was formed.
who invented baseball | history of baseball | abner doubleday myth => Baseball grew from English bat and ball games like rounders. The story that Abner Doubleday invented it in 1839 is a myth. Alexander Cartwright's Knickerbocker club wrote early rules in 1845.
who invented american football | history of american football | walter camp => American football grew out of rugby and soccer at US colleges in the 1800s. Walter Camp, called the father of American football, introduced the line of scrimmage and downs in the 1880s.
where did hockey come from | history of ice hockey | first hockey game => Modern ice hockey developed in Canada. The first recorded indoor game was played in Montreal in 1875. The NHL was founded in 1917.
where did golf come from | history of golf | st andrews golf => Modern golf developed in Scotland in the 1400s. St Andrews in Scotland is called the home of golf, and its Old Course is one of the oldest in the world.
who invented snowboarding | history of snowboarding | snurfer => The Snurfer, a toy invented by Sherman Poppen in 1965, helped start snowboarding. Snowboarding joined the Winter Olympics in 1998.
where did bmx come from | history of bmx | bmx meaning => BMX, short for bicycle motocross, started in California in the late 1960s and early 1970s when kids raced bikes on dirt tracks, copying motocross. BMX racing joined the Olympics in 2008.
when were women allowed in the olympics | first women olympians | women olympics history => Women first competed in the modern Olympics in 1900 in Paris, in sports like tennis and golf. Charlotte Cooper became the first female Olympic champion, in tennis.
what was the miracle on ice | miracle on ice 1980 | do you believe in miracles => At the 1980 Winter Olympics in Lake Placid, a young US hockey team of college players beat the heavily favored Soviet Union 4 to 3, then won the gold medal.
who is lebron james | lebron james records | lebron scoring record => LeBron James is an NBA star who won four championships and in 2023 passed Kareem Abdul Jabbar to become the NBA's all time leading scorer. He was the first player to pass 40,000 and then 50,000 career points, counting playoffs for the latter.
who is lionel messi | messi achievements | messi world cup => Lionel Messi is an Argentine forward who has won 8 Ballon d'Or awards, the most ever, and led Argentina to the 2022 World Cup title. As of 2025 he plays for Inter Miami in MLS.
who is cristiano ronaldo | ronaldo goals record | cr7 => Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese forward who, as of 2025, holds the record for most official career goals in men's soccer, with over 900, and the most international goals.
who is shohei ohtani | ohtani two way player | ohtani 50 50 => Shohei Ohtani is a Japanese star who both pitches and hits at an elite level. In 2024 he became the first MLB player with 50 home runs and 50 stolen bases in one season.
who is caitlin clark | caitlin clark records | caitlin clark scoring => Caitlin Clark became the all time leading scorer in NCAA Division I basketball history, men or women, at Iowa in 2024, then was the 2024 WNBA Rookie of the Year with the Indiana Fever.
