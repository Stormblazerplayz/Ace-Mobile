# tag: life.safety.online
how do i make my social media private | private account settings | who can see my posts => Go to your account's privacy settings and set your profile to private, so only people you approve can see your posts. Turn off location sharing and limit who can message you.
how old do you have to be for social media | age limit for instagram | why is the age 13 => Most big social media apps, like Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat, require users to be at least 13, mostly because of US privacy laws about collecting data from kids.
what is a digital footprint | does stuff online last forever | digital footprint meaning => Your digital footprint is everything about you online: posts, comments, photos and accounts. Things can be screenshotted and shared even after you delete them, so think before posting.
what should i do if someone online makes me uncomfortable | creepy person online | someone online is being weird => Stop replying, block and report them, and tell a trusted adult. You won't be in trouble for telling. Save screenshots in case they're needed.
what is grooming online | warning signs of online predators | online stranger red flags => Grooming is when an adult builds trust with a kid online to take advantage of them. Red flags include lots of compliments, gifts or game currency, asking for secrets, photos or to move to a private app, and asking to meet. Tell a trusted adult right away.
should i send photos to someone i met online | someone asked me for a picture | is it okay to send pictures to online friends => No. Never send photos of yourself to people you only know online, and never any private photos to anyone. If someone asks, block them and tell a trusted adult.
what is sextortion | someone is threatening to share my photos | blackmail online what do i do => If anyone threatens to share pictures or demands money or more pictures, stop replying, don't pay, save the messages and tell a trusted adult right away. It's not your fault, and in the US you can report it to NCMEC's CyberTipline.
how do i block someone | blocking on games | reporting someone in an app => Most apps and games have a block or report option in the person's profile or the chat menu. Blocking stops them contacting you, and reporting tells the platform to review them.
what is screen time addiction | am i on my phone too much | signs of too much screen time => Warning signs include losing sleep, skipping homework or activities, getting upset when you have to stop, and feeling worse after scrolling. Setting limits and doing offline activities helps.
how do i turn on screen time limits | ipad screen time settings | app limits on ipad => On iPad, go to Settings, then Screen Time, where you can see your usage, set app limits and downtime. Parents can manage it with a passcode through Family Sharing.
is it safe to use public wifi | public wifi risks | using wifi at a cafe => Public Wi-Fi can be less secure, so avoid logging into important accounts or entering passwords on sites without https. Using your phone's data or a trusted VPN is safer.
how do i spot fake news | is this news real | checking facts online => Check who made it, look for the same story on several trusted news sites, check the date, and be suspicious of headlines meant to make you angry. Reverse image search can show if a photo is old or edited.
how do i know if a photo is fake or ai made | spotting ai images | is this picture photoshopped => Look for odd hands, warped text, strange shadows or backgrounds that don't make sense. Search for the original source. AI images keep getting better, so trusted sources matter most.
what is clickbait | clickbait meaning | why do videos have clickbait titles => Clickbait is a title or thumbnail made to grab clicks with exaggeration or mystery, often not matching what's inside. It's a reason to be careful what you believe.
what should i do if i'm being cyberbullied | cyberbullying help | someone is posting mean things about me => Don't respond, take screenshots, block and report the person, and tell a trusted adult. If you feel unsafe or it's threatening, adults can involve the school or police.
is it okay to post where i am | location sharing safety | should i turn off location on snapchat => It's safer not to share your live location publicly or post where you are in real time. Turn off location sharing in apps like Snapchat Map, or share only with family.
how do i keep my gaming account safe | keep my game login safe => Use a strong unique password, turn on two step verification, never share your login or click free item links, and don't log in on unofficial sites. Link an email you control.
what is online gaming chat safety | voice chat with strangers | game chat rules => Keep personal info private, use a nickname that doesn't show your real name, mute or block anyone mean or creepy, and don't move chats to other apps with strangers.
what is a good username | safe username ideas | should my username have my name => Pick a username that doesn't include your real name, age, school, birthday or location. Something fun and creative works best.
what should i do if i see someone posting about hurting themselves | friend posted something scary online | someone online talked about suicide => Take it seriously and tell a trusted adult right away. You can also report the post to the platform. In the US, 988 is the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline for calls or texts.

# tag: life.safety.water
how do i stay safe at the beach | beach safety tips | swimming in the ocean safety => Swim near a lifeguard, check the flags for warnings, never swim alone and stay out during storms. Watch for rip currents and protect yourself from the sun.
what is a rip current | how do you escape a rip current | how to spot a rip current => A rip current is a narrow, fast channel of water flowing away from shore. If caught, stay calm, don't fight it, swim parallel to the shore until you're out of it, then swim back at an angle. Float and wave for help if tired.
what do beach warning flags mean | red flag at the beach | beach flag colors => Flag colors vary, but generally green means calm, yellow means moderate surf or currents, red means high hazard, double red means the water is closed, and purple means dangerous marine life.
is it safe to swim in a lake or river | lake swimming safety | river currents => Lakes and rivers can have cold water, hidden rocks, drop offs and currents. Swim only in designated areas, wear a life jacket if you're not a strong swimmer and never dive into unknown water.
when should i wear a life jacket | life jacket rules | how should a life jacket fit => Wear one on boats, kayaks, paddleboards and in open water if you're not a strong swimmer. It should be US Coast Guard approved, fit snugly and not ride up over your chin when you lift the shoulders.
why shouldn't you dive into shallow water | diving safety | is it safe to dive in a pool => Diving into shallow or unknown water can cause serious neck and spine injuries. Only dive in deep areas marked as safe for diving, and enter feet first in unknown water.
what does drowning look like | drowning is quiet | signs someone is drowning => Drowning is usually quiet. The person may be upright in the water, head back with mouth at the surface, unable to call for help or wave. Get help right away.
how do i stay safe on ice | is the ice thick enough | falling through ice => Stay off ice unless an adult checks it's thick enough. Many guides say clear ice should be at least 10 cm (4 inches) thick for walking. If someone falls through, call 911 and reach with a pole or rope from shore instead of going on the ice.
what is cold water shock | why is cold water dangerous | falling into cold water => Sudden cold water makes you gasp and breathe fast for about a minute, which can cause you to swallow water. Keep your head above water, float and control your breathing, then try to get out or signal for help.
how do i stay safe kayaking or paddleboarding | paddle sports safety | kayak safety tips => Always wear a life jacket, stay close to shore, check the weather and wind, go with an adult or buddy, and know how to get back on if you fall off.
what is pool safety | pool rules for kids | pool drain safety => Walk, don't run, swim with a buddy and an adult watching, stay in your depth, and stay away from drains. Never swim in a pool without permission or when no one is around.
what should i do if a jellyfish stings me | jellyfish sting treatment | does peeing on a jellyfish sting work => Get out of the water, rinse with seawater, not fresh water, remove tentacles with tweezers or a card, then soak in hot water as hot as you can comfortably stand. Peeing on it doesn't help. Get help for trouble breathing.

# tag: life.safety.sports
how do i prevent sports injuries | avoiding injuries in sports | stay safe playing sports => Warm up before playing, wear the right gear, drink water, rest between hard days, and learn proper technique. Speak up when something hurts instead of playing through it.
why do i need a mouthguard | mouthguard for sports | which sports need mouthguards => Mouthguards protect teeth, lips and jaws in contact sports like football, hockey, basketball, lacrosse and martial arts. Custom or boil and bite ones fit best.
why do athletes need rest days | overtraining in kids | can you exercise too much => Muscles and bones get stronger during rest, not during training. Too much without rest can cause overuse injuries and burnout. Most young athletes should take at least one or two days off a week.
what are overuse injuries | shin splints | growth plate injuries => Overuse injuries come from repeating the same motion too much, like shin splints from running or elbow pain from pitching. Kids' growth plates are more easily hurt, so variety and rest help.
what is a heat illness in sports | practicing in the heat | heat safety for athletes => Hard exercise in heat can cause heat exhaustion or heat stroke. Drink water before, during and after, take breaks in shade, wear light clothing, and tell a coach if you feel dizzy, sick or confused.
should i play sports when i'm sick | exercising with a cold | when to sit out of practice => A common guide is the neck check: with mild symptoms above the neck, like a stuffy nose, light activity may be okay. With fever, body aches or chest symptoms, rest until better.
