# tag: life.games.play
how do you play hide and seek | hide and seek rules | hide and seek tips => One person counts with eyes closed while everyone hides, then searches. The first found is often the next seeker. Good hiders stay still and quiet and pick spots others overlook.
how do you play sardines | sardines game | reverse hide and seek => In sardines, one person hides and everyone else searches. When you find the hider, you squeeze into the hiding spot with them. The last person to find the group is the next hider.
how do you play tag | tag game variations | types of tag => One person is "it" and chases others. Tagged players become it. Variations include freeze tag (tagged players freeze until freed), shadow tag and flashlight tag at night.
how do you play freeze tag | freeze tag rules | how to unfreeze in freeze tag => Tagged players freeze in place. Free players unfreeze them by tagging them or crawling between their legs. The taggers win if everyone is frozen.
how do you play red light green light | red light green light rules | how to win red light green light => One player is the stoplight facing away. On "green light," others move toward them. On "red light," the stoplight turns and anyone caught moving goes back to the start. First to tag the stoplight wins.
how do you play kick the can | kick the can game | kick the can rules => A can is placed in the open and one player guards it while others hide. The guard finds and tags people, sending them to jail. A free player can kick the can to release everyone.
how do you play ghost in the graveyard | ghost in the graveyard rules | night hiding game => One player, the ghost, hides at dusk. Others count "one o'clock, two o'clock" up to "midnight," then search. When someone spots the ghost, they yell and everyone races back to base without getting tagged.
how do you play simon says | simon says rules | simon says ideas => The leader gives commands. Players only follow if the command starts with "Simon says." Anyone who follows a command without it, or doesn't follow one with it, is out.
how do you play duck duck goose | duck duck goose rules | duck duck goose game => Players sit in a circle. One walks around tapping heads saying "duck" until they say "goose." The goose chases them around the circle. If the walker sits in the goose's spot first, the goose is the new walker.
how do you play charades | charades rules | charades ideas => One player acts out a word or phrase without speaking while their team guesses. Use signals like holding up fingers for the number of words and tugging your ear for "sounds like."
how do you play pictionary | pictionary rules | drawing guessing game => One player draws a word without using letters or numbers while their team guesses before time runs out. Simple shapes and quick sketches work best.
how do you play twenty questions | 20 questions game | 20 questions tips => One person thinks of something, and others ask up to 20 yes or no questions to guess it. Start broad, like "Is it alive?", and narrow down.
how do you play would you rather | would you rather questions | would you rather for kids => Players pick between two choices, like "Would you rather fly or be invisible?", and explain why. Keep the questions fun and kind.
how do you play two truths and a lie | two truths and a lie ideas | icebreaker game => Each person says three statements about themselves, two true and one false, and others guess the lie. It's a great way to get to know people.
how do you play telephone | telephone game | whisper game => Players sit in a line. The first whispers a phrase to the next, and it passes down. The last person says it out loud, and it's usually hilariously different.
how do you play i spy | i spy game | i spy rules => One player says "I spy with my little eye something that is..." and gives a color or letter. Others guess what it is.
how do you play mash | mash game | mash fortune => Write categories like home, job and car with options. Draw a spiral until told to stop, count the lines, then cross off every option at that count until one is left in each category. MASH stands for Mansion, Apartment, Shack, House.
how do you play dots and boxes | dots and boxes strategy | dot game => Players take turns drawing a line between two dots on a grid. Whoever completes a box writes their initial in it and goes again. Avoid drawing the third side of a box.
how do you play hot potato | hot potato game | hot potato rules => Players pass a ball quickly around a circle while music plays. Whoever is holding it when the music stops is out.
how do you play musical chairs | musical chairs rules | musical chairs game => Set up one fewer chair than players. Walk around while music plays, and when it stops, everyone sits. The player without a chair is out, and one chair is removed each round.
how do you play heads up seven up | seven up game | heads down thumbs up => Seven people stand at the front. Everyone else puts heads down with thumbs up. The seven each press one thumb down, then return. The chosen players guess who picked them.
how do you play mafia | mafia game | werewolf party game => In Mafia, or Werewolf, a narrator secretly assigns roles. At "night," the mafia secretly pick someone out, and during "day," everyone discusses and votes on who they think is mafia. Townspeople win by finding all the mafia.
how do you play spoons | spoons card game | spoons rules => Put one fewer spoon than players in the middle. Pass cards quickly, trying to collect four of a kind. When someone does, they grab a spoon, and everyone else grabs one too. The player without a spoon gets a letter.
how do you play crazy eights | crazy eights rules | card game crazy eights => Match the top card by number or suit. Eights are wild and let you change the suit. First to get rid of all cards wins. UNO is based on similar rules.
how do you play slapjack | slapjack rules | slap card game => Players take turns flipping cards onto a pile. When a jack appears, everyone slaps it. The first to slap it wins the pile. The player with all the cards wins.
how do you play egyptian ratscrew | egyptian ratscrew rules | slap card game rules => Players flip cards to a pile. Face cards challenge the next player to play a face card within a set number of tries. Slap doubles or sandwiches to win the pile.
how do you play rummy | rummy rules | gin rummy basics => Players draw and discard to make sets (three or four of a kind) and runs (three or more in a row of the same suit). The first to arrange all cards wins.
how do you play hearts | hearts card game | shooting the moon => In Hearts, players try to avoid taking tricks with hearts (1 point each) and the queen of spades (13 points). Taking all of them, called shooting the moon, gives everyone else 26 points.
how do you play poker hands ranking | poker hand rankings | what beats what in poker => From best to worst: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. Friendly games can use chips or candy instead of money.
how do you play blackjack | blackjack rules | 21 card game => Try to get cards totaling closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards are 10 and aces are 1 or 11. Play it for fun with no money involved.
how do you play cribbage | cribbage basics | cribbage board => Cribbage is a card game scored by moving pegs on a board, getting points for combinations like fifteens, pairs and runs. The first to 121 points wins.
how do you play clue | clue game rules | cluedo => In Clue, players move through a mansion, make suggestions about the suspect, weapon and room, and use other players' cards to rule things out. The first to correctly accuse wins.
how do you play catan | settlers of catan basics | catan rules => Players collect resources like wood, brick and wheat from dice rolls, trade, and build roads, settlements and cities. The first to 10 victory points wins.
how do you play ticket to ride | ticket to ride rules | train board game => Players collect colored train cards and claim routes between cities to complete destination tickets. Longer routes give more points.
how do you play risk | risk board game | risk strategy => Players use armies to conquer territories on a world map, rolling dice for battles. Holding whole continents gives bonus armies. It can take hours.
how do you play jenga | jenga rules | jenga tips => Players take turns pulling one block from the tower with one hand and placing it on top. Whoever makes it fall loses. Tap blocks gently to find the loose ones.
how do you play spot it | spot it game | dobble card game => In Spot It (also called Dobble), every two cards share exactly one matching symbol. Players race to find and call out the match.
how do you play exploding kittens | exploding kittens rules | exploding kittens card game => Players draw cards until someone draws an Exploding Kitten and is out, unless they have a Defuse card. Other cards let you skip, peek or attack. The last player left wins.
how do you play codenames | codenames rules | codenames tips => Two teams each have a spymaster who gives one word clues and a number to help teammates guess their team's words on a grid, while avoiding the other team's words and the assassin.
what are good board games for families | best family board games | board games for kids and adults => Popular choices include Ticket to Ride, Catan, Codenames, Sushi Go, Carcassonne, Azul, Splendor, Kingdomino and Dixit.
what are good strategy board games for kids | strategy games for a 12 year old | thinking board games => Try chess, Catan, Ticket to Ride, Carcassonne, Azul, Splendor, Stratego and Robo Rally for strategy and planning.
what are good two player games | games for two people | two player board games => Good picks include chess, checkers, Battleship, Connect Four, Stratego, cribbage, Patchwork, Jaipur and Lost Cities.
how do you play capture the flag at night | glow capture the flag | flashlight games => Use glow sticks to mark flags and players, set clear boundaries, and walk instead of run in the dark. Flashlight tag is another great night game.
how do you play wall ball | wall ball rules | butts up game => Players throw a tennis ball against a wall and must catch or field it cleanly. Rules vary by school, but dropping it means you must race to touch the wall before getting hit by the ball, or you're out.
how do you play hopscotch | hopscotch rules | hopscotch court => Toss a marker onto square 1, hop over it on one foot (two feet on side by side squares), turn at the end and hop back, picking up the marker. Then throw to square 2, and so on.
how do you play marco polo | marco polo rules | pool game marco polo => In the pool, the player who is "it" closes their eyes and calls "Marco." Others answer "Polo," and "it" tries to tag them by sound. A tagged player becomes "it."
how do you play kickball rules basics | kickball bases | kickball outs => Kickball is like baseball, but the pitcher rolls a big rubber ball and the kicker kicks it. Runners are out if tagged, forced out, or hit by a thrown ball below the shoulders, depending on rules.
how do you play horse in basketball | horse basketball game | pig basketball => Players take turns shooting. If a player makes a shot, the next must copy it. Missing a copied shot earns a letter. Spell H O R S E and you're out. PIG is a shorter version.
how do you play knockout in basketball | knockout basketball | lightning basketball => Players line up at the free throw line with two balls. If the person behind you makes their shot before you, you're out. The last player standing wins.
how do you play around the world basketball | around the world shots | basketball shooting game => Shoot from a set of spots in a circle around the hoop. Make it to move to the next spot. The first to make it all the way around and back wins.
how do you play 500 | 500 throwing game | catching game 500 => One person throws or hits a ball to a group. Catching a fly ball is worth 100 points, one bounce 75, two bounces 50 and grounders 25. The first to 500 becomes the thrower.
how do you play pickle | pickle baseball game | hot box => Two players stand on bases and throw a ball back and forth while a runner tries to run between bases without getting tagged. It's great practice for rundowns.
