# tag: life.safety.firstaid
when should i call 911 | what counts as an emergency | when to call for help => Call 911 when someone's life or safety is in danger: a fire, someone not breathing or not waking up, bad bleeding, a serious injury, a crime happening, or a car crash. If you're unsure and it seems serious, call.
what do i tell the 911 operator | steps for calling 911 | what happens when you call 911 => Stay calm, say what the emergency is and give your address or location. Answer the dispatcher's questions, follow their instructions, and don't hang up until they tell you to.
what if i call 911 by accident | accidentally called 911 | butt dialed 911 => Don't hang up. Stay on the line and tell the dispatcher it was an accident and everyone is okay, so they don't have to call back or send help.
can i text 911 | text to 911 | what if i can't talk on the phone => In many US areas you can text 911 if calling isn't safe or possible, but not everywhere, so calling is best whenever you can. If texting doesn't work where you are, you'll get a message back saying to call.
what is the emergency number in other countries | 112 emergency number | what number is 999 => It depends on the country. 911 is used in the US and Canada, 112 across Europe and on many mobile phones worldwide, 999 in the UK, and 000 in Australia.
what is emergency sos on iphone | how to call emergency on ipad | emergency call iphone shortcut => On an iPhone, pressing and holding the side button and a volume button brings up Emergency SOS, which can call emergency services. iPads without cellular service can't call 911 directly, so use a phone if you can.
how do i help someone who is choking | choking first aid steps | heimlich maneuver how to => If they can cough, encourage them to keep coughing. If they can't cough, speak or breathe, shout for an adult and call 911. Trained rescuers give back blows and abdominal thrusts. Even a few minutes of training in a first aid class teaches this well.
what do i do if i am choking and alone | choking by myself | self heimlich => Try to cough hard. If you can't, call 911 if possible, then push your upper belly hard against the back of a sturdy chair or counter edge to force the object out.
how do i stop a nosebleed | nosebleed first aid | what to do for a bloody nose => Sit up and lean forward, not back, so blood doesn't run down your throat. Pinch the soft part of your nose firmly for 10 to 15 minutes without letting go. Get help if it won't stop after 20 minutes or followed a hard hit to the head.
why do i get nosebleeds | what causes nosebleeds | frequent nosebleeds kids => Dry air, picking your nose, colds, allergies and bumps are the most common causes in kids. A humidifier and a little petroleum jelly inside the nostrils can help. Tell a parent if they happen a lot.
how do i treat a scrape | cleaning a scrape | road rash first aid => Wash your hands, rinse the scrape under clean running water to get dirt out, gently wash around it with soap, then cover it with a bandage or nonstick dressing. Change it daily and watch for signs of infection.
how do i know if a cut needs stitches | does my cut need stitches | when to go to the doctor for a cut => See a doctor if the cut is deep, gaping open, longer than about 1.3 cm (half an inch), on the face or a joint, won't stop bleeding after 10 minutes of pressure, or came from something dirty or rusty.
how do i stop bleeding | bad bleeding first aid | applying pressure to a wound => Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage and keep pressing. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top without lifting the first. For heavy bleeding, call 911 right away.
what are signs of an infected cut | is my cut infected | infection symptoms wound => Watch for growing redness, swelling, warmth, pus, red streaks spreading out, worsening pain or a fever. Tell a parent so they can get it checked by a doctor.
how do i treat a blister | should i pop a blister | blister on my foot => Don't pop it if you can avoid it, since the skin protects against infection. Cover it with a bandage or blister pad. If it pops, wash it, leave the skin flap on and cover it.
how do i treat a bruise | how to make a bruise heal faster | why do bruises change color => Put a cold pack wrapped in a cloth on it for 10 to 20 minutes at a time in the first day or two. Bruises change from red or purple to blue, green and yellow as your body breaks down the blood.
what should i do if someone faints | someone passed out | fainting first aid => Help them lie down on their back and raise their legs about 30 cm (12 inches) if there's no injury. Loosen tight clothing and check they're breathing. If they don't wake up within a minute, call 911.
what are signs of a concussion | did i get a concussion | concussion symptoms kids => Signs include headache, dizziness, feeling foggy, nausea, confusion, blurry vision, trouble with balance, or feeling extra emotional or sleepy after a hit to the head. Stop playing and tell an adult right away, and see a doctor.
what should i do after hitting my head | bumped my head hard | head injury warning signs => Tell an adult. Go to the emergency room or call 911 for passing out, repeated vomiting, a worsening headache, confusion, seizures, slurred speech, or one pupil bigger than the other.
what is cpr | what does cpr stand for | how does cpr work => CPR, cardiopulmonary resuscitation, is pushing hard and fast on the chest of someone whose heart has stopped, keeping blood flowing to the brain until help arrives. Anyone who sees it should call 911 first.
how do i do hands only cpr | cpr steps for kids | chest compressions how to => Call 911 and get an adult. If the person isn't responsive and isn't breathing normally, push hard and fast in the center of the chest, about 100 to 120 times a minute, like the beat of "Stayin' Alive". Keep going until help arrives. A CPR class teaches it properly.
what is an aed | how do you use an aed | defibrillator meaning => An AED, automated external defibrillator, is a device in many schools and public places that can shock a stopped heart back into rhythm. Turn it on and it speaks step by step instructions. Anyone can use it.
can kids learn cpr | cpr class for kids | at what age can you learn cpr => Yes. Many kids learn hands only CPR around middle school, and groups like the American Red Cross and American Heart Association offer classes. Knowing it can save a life.
what is the recovery position | how to put someone in the recovery position | unconscious but breathing => If someone is unconscious but breathing normally and has no spine injury, roll them onto their side with their top knee bent and head tilted back slightly, so they can breathe and won't choke. Call 911.
what should i do if someone is having a seizure | seizure first aid | someone is shaking on the ground => Stay calm, move hard objects away, put something soft under their head, and turn them on their side when you can. Never put anything in their mouth or hold them down. Time it and call 911 if it lasts over 5 minutes.
what is an allergic reaction | signs of a severe allergic reaction | what is anaphylaxis => Anaphylaxis is a severe allergic reaction that can include trouble breathing, swelling of the lips or throat, hives all over, dizziness or vomiting. It's an emergency: use an epinephrine auto injector if they have one and call 911.
how do you use an epipen | epinephrine auto injector | when to use an epipen => An adult or the person usually gives it into the outer thigh following the label instructions, holding it in place for a few seconds. Call 911 after using it, because symptoms can come back.
what do i do if someone is having an asthma attack | asthma attack help | someone can't breathe asthma => Help them sit upright, stay calm and use their rescue inhaler as they've been taught. If breathing doesn't improve quickly, their lips turn blue or they can't talk, call 911.
how do i treat a sunburn | sunburn relief | what helps a sunburn => Get out of the sun, cool the skin with a cool shower or damp cloth, use aloe or a gentle moisturizer and drink water. See a doctor for big blisters, fever, chills or feeling very sick.
what is heat exhaustion | heat stroke signs | too hot what to do => Heat exhaustion causes heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache and nausea: move to shade or AC, drink water and cool down. Heat stroke is an emergency with a very high temperature, confusion or fainting. Call 911 and cool them right away.
what is hypothermia | signs of hypothermia | too cold what to do => Hypothermia is when the body gets dangerously cold. Signs include strong shivering, confusion, slurred speech and clumsiness. Get them somewhere warm, remove wet clothes, wrap in blankets and call 911 for serious signs.
what is frostbite | frostbite signs | how to treat frostbite => Frostbite is frozen skin, often on fingers, toes, ears and nose, which turns numb, white or grayish and hard. Get indoors, warm it gently with lukewarm water, not hot, don't rub it, and get medical help.
what do i do if i get something in my eye | dust in my eye | how to get something out of your eye => Don't rub it. Blink a lot, and rinse your eye with clean water or saline, holding it open. If it's still there, hurts a lot, or it's a chemical, get help from an adult right away. For chemicals, rinse for at least 15 minutes and call for help.
what should i do if someone swallows something poisonous | poison control number | swallowed cleaning product => In the US, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222, available 24/7, or call 911 if the person is unconscious, having trouble breathing or having a seizure. Don't make them throw up unless told to.
what should i do if i get bitten by a dog | dog bite first aid | animal bite what to do => Wash the bite well with soap and running water, cover it, and tell an adult. See a doctor for deep bites, bites from an unknown animal, or signs of infection, and report the animal so they can check for rabies.
what do i do if i get a splinter | how to get a splinter out | splinter removal => Wash the area, then use clean tweezers to pull it out at the same angle it went in. If it's deep or under a nail, ask an adult or doctor for help. Wash and cover it afterward.
what is a sprain vs a fracture | how do i know if it's broken | signs of a broken bone => A sprain is a stretched or torn ligament, and a fracture is a broken bone. Signs of a break include a bent shape, a snap sound, not being able to use it, severe pain and swelling. Keep it still and get medical help.
what goes in a first aid kit | first aid kit list | build a first aid kit => A basic kit has adhesive bandages, gauze pads, medical tape, antiseptic wipes, tweezers, scissors, gloves, an elastic bandage, a cold pack, and any personal medicines. Keep one at home, in the car and in your bike or hiking bag.
how do i help someone who is hurt | first aid steps | what to do first in an emergency => First check that the scene is safe for you. Then check the person, call or shout for help or call 911, and give simple care like pressing on bleeding until help arrives. Don't move someone who might have a neck or back injury.
what should i do if i see a car accident | witnessing a crash | stopping to help at an accident => Stay safely away from traffic and call 911 with the location. Don't run into the road. Tell an adult, and only help if it's safe and you know how.
what do i do if someone is drowning | how to help a drowning person | reach or throw don't go => Shout for help and call 911. Reach with a pole or throw something that floats, like a ring buoy or cooler, while staying on land. Don't jump in, because a panicking person can pull you under. Swimmers who are drowning often can't shout or wave.
