# tag: life.cooking.baking
what are the types of cake | cake types | butter cake vs sponge cake => Butter cakes, like yellow cake, are rich and tender. Sponge cakes rely on whipped eggs for lightness. Chiffon cakes use oil and whipped whites, and angel food cake uses only egg whites.
what is a bundt cake | bundt pan | how to get a bundt cake out => A bundt cake is baked in a ring shaped pan with fluted sides. Grease every crevice well, cool it in the pan about 10 minutes, then flip it out.
what is a pound cake | why is it called pound cake | pound cake recipe => Pound cake was originally made with a pound each of flour, butter, sugar and eggs. It's dense, buttery and keeps well.
what is angel food cake | angel food cake pan | why cool angel food cake upside down => Angel food cake is a light, fat free cake made with whipped egg whites, sugar and flour. It's cooled upside down so it doesn't collapse while it sets.
what is the difference between cake and cupcake batter | can i make cupcakes from cake batter | cake batter as cupcakes => Most cake batters work as cupcakes. Fill liners about two thirds full and bake for less time, usually 18 to 22 minutes, checking with a toothpick.
what is a sheet cake | sheet cake vs layer cake | sheet cake size => A sheet cake is baked in one large flat pan and frosted on top, easy for parties. A layer cake stacks two or more round cakes with frosting between.
how do i make a layer cake level | leveling a cake | cake dome => Cut off the domed top with a long serrated knife, using a turntable if you have one. Baking strips around the pan help cakes bake flatter.
what is a crumb coat | crumb coating a cake | first layer of frosting => A crumb coat is a thin layer of frosting that traps crumbs. Chill the cake for 15 to 30 minutes, then add the final, smooth layer.
what is fondant | fondant vs buttercream | how to use fondant => Fondant is a smooth sugar dough rolled out to cover cakes or shape decorations. Buttercream is creamier and tastier to many people, while fondant gives a flat, polished look.
what is ganache | chocolate ganache | ganache ratio => Ganache is chocolate melted with hot cream. Equal parts by weight makes a glaze or filling, and more chocolate, like 2 to 1, makes it firm enough to roll into truffles.
what is cream cheese frosting | cream cheese frosting tips | frosting for carrot cake => Cream cheese frosting mixes softened cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar and vanilla. Use cold cream cheese block style for best texture and keep frosted cakes in the fridge.
what are the types of cookies | drop cookies vs bar cookies | cookie types => Drop cookies are spooned onto pans, like chocolate chip. Bar cookies bake in a pan and are cut, like brownies. Rolled cookies are cut with shapes, and icebox cookies are sliced from a chilled log.
how do i make sugar cookies that hold their shape | cut out cookies spreading | sugar cookie tips => Chill the dough, roll it between parchment, cut shapes, then chill the cut cookies again for 10 to 15 minutes before baking. Don't add too much baking powder.
how do i make royal icing | royal icing for cookies | cookie decorating icing => Mix powdered sugar with meringue powder or egg whites and water. Thicker icing outlines cookies and thinner icing fills them, called flooding.
what are snickerdoodles | snickerdoodle recipe | cream of tartar cookies => Snickerdoodles are soft cookies rolled in cinnamon sugar. Cream of tartar gives them a slight tang and chewy texture.
what is shortbread | shortbread cookies | why is it called shortbread => Shortbread is a buttery Scottish cookie made from just butter, sugar and flour. "Short" means crumbly and tender because of all the fat.
what is a pie vs a tart | pie vs tart | what is a tart => Pies usually have deeper, sloped dishes and can have top crusts. Tarts are shallow with straight sides and a firm crust, often removed from the pan to serve.
how do i make a lattice pie crust | lattice crust | weaving pie crust => Cut dough into strips, lay half in one direction, fold back every other strip, lay a strip across, unfold, and repeat to weave. Crimp the edges together.
what is a cobbler crisp and crumble | cobbler vs crisp | fruit dessert types => All are baked fruit desserts. A cobbler has a biscuit or batter topping. A crisp has a crunchy topping usually with oats, and a crumble is similar, often without oats.
what is choux pastry | cream puffs | eclairs dough => Choux pastry is made by cooking water, butter and flour, then beating in eggs. Steam puffs it up into hollow shells for cream puffs and eclairs.
what is puff pastry | puff pastry vs phyllo | laminated dough => Puff pastry has many thin layers of butter and dough that puff when baked. Phyllo (filo) is paper thin sheets brushed with butter, used in baklava.
what are cinnamon rolls | how to make cinnamon rolls | cinnamon rolls rising => Cinnamon rolls are sweet yeast dough rolled with butter, cinnamon and sugar, sliced, risen and baked, then often topped with icing. Use dental floss to cut neat slices.
how do i make soft dinner rolls | homemade rolls | fluffy rolls tips => Use a soft, enriched dough with milk, butter and a little sugar, let it rise until doubled, shape tight balls, let them rise again, and brush with butter after baking.
what is focaccia | focaccia bread | dimples in focaccia => Focaccia is a flat Italian bread baked in an oiled pan. Press dimples into the dough with your fingers before baking to hold olive oil and toppings.
what is a quick bread | quick bread vs yeast bread | examples of quick breads => Quick breads rise with baking soda or baking powder instead of yeast, so no waiting to rise. Banana bread, muffins, biscuits and cornbread are quick breads.
how do i make cornbread | cornbread recipe | sweet vs savory cornbread => Mix cornmeal, flour, baking powder, salt, egg, milk and melted butter, and bake in a hot greased pan. Southern cornbread is usually less sweet than Northern style.
how do i make scones | scone tips | flaky scones => Cut cold butter into flour, sugar and baking powder, stir in cream until just combined, pat into a circle, cut into wedges and bake hot. Don't overwork the dough.
# tag: life.cooking.breakfast
how do i make hard boiled eggs | hard boiled egg time | perfect boiled eggs => Put eggs in boiling water and cook 10 to 12 minutes, or start them in cold water, bring to a boil, cover off the heat for 10 to 12 minutes. Move them to ice water to stop cooking.
how do i make a breakfast burrito | breakfast burrito ideas | freezer breakfast burritos => Fill a warm tortilla with scrambled eggs, cheese, cooked potatoes, and sausage or beans, then roll it up. Wrap extras in foil and freeze for quick breakfasts.
how do i make a breakfast sandwich | egg sandwich | bacon egg and cheese => Toast an English muffin or bagel, cook an egg flat in a pan, add a slice of cheese to melt, and stack with bacon or ham.
how do i make hash browns | crispy hash browns | shredded potatoes => Shred potatoes, squeeze out as much water as possible in a towel, then cook in a hot oiled pan in a thin layer without stirring until golden, about 5 minutes per side.
how do i make granola | homemade granola | granola clusters => Mix oats, nuts or seeds, a pinch of salt, oil and honey or maple syrup, spread on a pan and bake at about 160 C (325 F) for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring once. Let it cool without stirring for clusters.
how do i make crispy waffles from a mix | better boxed pancakes | upgrade pancake mix => Add an extra egg, use milk instead of water, add a little melted butter and vanilla, and don't overmix. Let the batter rest a few minutes.
what is a dutch baby | dutch baby pancake | german pancake => A Dutch baby is a big puffy pancake baked in a hot cast iron pan. Blend eggs, milk and flour, pour into the sizzling buttered pan and bake until it puffs.
what is eggs benedict | eggs benedict parts | what is on eggs benedict => Eggs Benedict is a toasted English muffin topped with ham or Canadian bacon, a poached egg and hollandaise sauce.
what is a good protein breakfast | high protein breakfast ideas | breakfast for sports days => Try eggs, Greek yogurt with fruit, peanut butter toast, a breakfast burrito, cottage cheese, or a smoothie with milk and yogurt.
# tag: life.cooking.lunch
how do i keep hot food hot for lunch | thermos lunch | hot lunch in a thermos => Fill an insulated food jar with boiling water for 5 minutes, dump it, then add steaming hot food and close it tight. It should stay hot until lunch.
what are lunch ideas besides sandwiches | sandwich free lunch | creative lunch ideas => Try pasta salad, wraps, leftover pizza, quesadillas, hummus with veggies and pita, a bento box with cheese, crackers and fruit, or soup in a thermos.
what is a bento box | japanese lunch box | bento ideas => A bento is a Japanese style packed lunch with small portions in compartments, like rice, protein, vegetables and fruit. It makes a balanced, colorful lunch.
how do i make pasta salad | easy pasta salad | cold pasta salad => Cook short pasta, rinse it in cold water, and toss with chopped vegetables, cheese and Italian dressing or vinaigrette. Chill before serving.
how do i make chicken salad | chicken salad recipe | leftover chicken ideas => Mix chopped cooked chicken with mayo or Greek yogurt, diced celery, a little mustard, salt and pepper. Add grapes or apples for sweetness. Serve on bread or crackers.
how do i make soup from scratch | basic soup method | homemade soup => Cook onion, carrot and celery in oil, add garlic and spices, pour in broth, add vegetables, beans, meat or noodles, and simmer until tender. Season at the end.
how do i make tomato soup | homemade tomato soup | tomato soup and grilled cheese => Cook onion and garlic in butter, add canned tomatoes and broth, simmer 15 minutes, blend until smooth, and stir in a splash of cream. Perfect with grilled cheese.
how do i make ramen from scratch | homemade ramen | ramen broth basics => Real ramen has a long simmered broth, seasoning (tare), noodles, oil and toppings. A quick version simmers chicken broth with soy sauce, garlic and ginger, then adds noodles, egg and green onion.
how do i make stir fry sauce | easy stir fry sauce | stir fry sauce ratio => Mix soy sauce, a little sugar or honey, rice vinegar, garlic, ginger and a spoon of cornstarch in water. Add it at the end and stir until glossy and thick.
how do i make a homemade pizza | pizza at home tips | homemade pizza toppings => Stretch the dough thin, add a thin layer of sauce, cheese and toppings, and bake on the hottest oven setting, ideally on a preheated pizza stone or steel, until the crust is browned.
how do i make a burger juicy | juicy burger tips | best burger meat => Use 80/20 ground beef, handle it gently, season just before cooking, make a thumb dent in the middle, don't press it while cooking, and rest it a minute before eating.
how do i make a sloppy joe | sloppy joes | sloppy joe sauce => Brown ground beef with onion, drain the fat, then stir in ketchup, a little mustard, brown sugar and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer until thick and spoon onto buns.
how do i make mac and cheese from a box better | upgrade boxed mac and cheese | boxed mac hacks => Use milk and butter as directed, add a handful of shredded cheese, a little extra butter, black pepper or garlic powder, and mix in cooked broccoli or bacon.
how do i make chicken noodle soup | homemade chicken noodle soup | soup when sick => Simmer chicken broth with carrots, celery and onion until tender, add cooked chicken and egg noodles, and cook until the noodles are done. Season with salt, pepper and parsley.
