# tag: life.howto.tools
what are the different types of screwdrivers | screwdriver types => A flathead (slotted) screwdriver has a single flat blade. A Phillips has a cross shaped tip. Others include Torx (star shaped), hex and Robertson (square). Use the right size so the screw doesn't strip.
what are the different types of saws | which saw should i use | hand saw types => A crosscut hand saw cuts across wood grain, a coping saw cuts curves, a hacksaw cuts metal and plastic, and a miter saw makes angled cuts. Power saws should only be used with adult supervision.
how do i use a hand saw | sawing wood straight | saw technique => Mark your line, clamp the wood down, start with a few gentle back strokes to make a groove, then use long smooth strokes and let the saw do the work. Keep your free hand well away from the blade.
what is a clamp for | types of clamps | why use clamps => Clamps hold pieces still while you cut, drill or wait for glue to dry. Common types are C clamps, bar clamps and spring clamps. Clamping keeps your hands safely away from tools.
what do sandpaper numbers mean | sandpaper grit | which sandpaper to use => The grit number tells how fine sandpaper is. Low numbers like 60 to 80 are coarse for shaping, 120 to 150 is medium, and 220 and up is fine for smoothing before painting or finishing.
how do i sand wood | sanding tips | sand with the grain => Start with a coarser grit and move to finer ones, sanding with the direction of the wood grain. Wrap paper around a block for flat surfaces, and wipe the dust off between grits. Wear a dust mask.
should i use nails or screws | nails vs screws | when to use screws => Screws hold more strongly and can be removed, so they're good for furniture and things you might take apart. Nails are faster and flex a bit, so they're common for framing and trim.
what is a pilot hole | why drill a pilot hole | pilot hole size => A pilot hole is a small hole drilled before putting in a screw. It keeps wood from splitting and makes the screw easier to drive. Use a drill bit a little thinner than the screw's core.
how do i use a power drill | drill for beginners | cordless drill tips => With an adult's okay, put on safety glasses, tighten the bit in the chuck, clamp your workpiece, start slowly, and keep the drill straight. Use the clutch setting for screws so they don't strip.
what is the clutch on a drill | drill numbers on the collar | drill clutch setting => The numbered ring on a drill is the clutch. It stops turning the screw once it reaches a certain resistance, so low numbers are for small screws and soft materials, and higher numbers for bigger screws.
how do i use wood glue | does wood glue work | how long does wood glue take to dry => Spread a thin even layer on both surfaces, press together, clamp for about 30 to 60 minutes, and wipe off squeeze out with a damp cloth. Let it cure about 24 hours before stressing the joint.
what is a stud in a wall | how do i find a wall stud | stud finder => Studs are the vertical wood or metal beams inside walls, usually 16 inches (40 cm) apart in the US. Find them with a stud finder or by knocking, since a stud sounds solid. Heavy things should be screwed into studs.
what is drywall | what are walls made of | drywall anchor => Many indoor walls are drywall: gypsum plaster pressed between paper. It's soft, so heavy things need wall anchors or screws into studs, not just a nail.
how do i fix a hole in drywall | patch a nail hole | spackle how to => For small nail holes, push spackle in with a putty knife, scrape it flat, let it dry, sand lightly and paint. Bigger holes need a mesh patch.
how do i paint a wall | painting a room tips | how to paint a wall evenly => Clean the wall, tape the edges with painter's tape, cover the floor, brush the corners and edges first, then roll the rest in overlapping W shapes. Usually two coats are needed.
what is caulk | how do i caulk | caulking a bathtub => Caulk is a flexible sealant that fills gaps around tubs, sinks and windows to keep water and air out. Cut the tip small, run a smooth bead along the gap, and smooth it with a wet finger.
what is duct tape good for | duct tape uses | duct tape vs electrical tape => Duct tape is strong cloth backed tape good for quick fixes, bundling and patching. Electrical tape is stretchy plastic used to insulate wires. Painter's tape peels off cleanly without damaging paint.
what is a wrench | adjustable wrench | wrench vs pliers => A wrench grips and turns nuts and bolts. Adjustable wrenches fit many sizes. Pliers grip, bend and cut wire, but can round off nuts, so wrenches are better for those.
what is a socket set | how to use a socket wrench | ratchet meaning => A socket set has a ratchet handle and different sized sockets that snap on to fit nuts and bolts. The ratchet lets you turn back and forth without lifting it off.
what is metric vs imperial tools | mm vs inch wrenches | sae tools => Metric tools are sized in millimeters and imperial (SAE) tools in fractions of an inch. Bikes and most electronics use metric. Using the wrong one can strip a bolt.
what should be in a basic toolbox | toolbox essentials | first tools to buy => A good starter toolbox has a hammer, screwdrivers (Phillips and flathead), pliers, an adjustable wrench, Allen keys, a tape measure, a utility knife, a level, duct tape and safety glasses.
how do i use a utility knife safely | box cutter safety | cutting with a craft knife => Always cut away from your body on a stable surface, keep your other hand behind the blade, use a sharp blade since dull ones slip, and retract the blade when you're done.
how do i use pliers | types of pliers | needle nose pliers => Pliers grip, twist and bend. Needle nose pliers reach into small spaces and bend wire, slip joint pliers grip larger objects, and wire cutters snip wire.
how do i tighten a loose doorknob | doorknob is wobbly | fix a loose door handle => Find the screws on the inside plate or a small set screw on the knob's neck and tighten them with a screwdriver or Allen key. Don't overtighten.
how do i fix a door that wont close | door sticks | door rubbing frame => Check and tighten the hinge screws first, since loose hinges make doors sag. If the door rubs because of humidity, it may need light sanding on the edge by an adult.
how do i fix a wobbly table | table legs uneven | wobbly chair fix => Tighten any loose screws or bolts first. If one leg is short, put a felt pad or folded cardboard under it, or use adjustable feet.
how do i stop furniture from scratching the floor | felt pads | furniture floor protectors => Stick felt pads on the bottom of chair and table legs, and lift furniture instead of dragging it. Clean the floor first so pads stick well.
how do i change a smoke detector battery | smoke alarm chirping | why is my smoke detector beeping => A chirp every minute or so usually means a low battery. With an adult, twist the alarm off its base, replace the battery and press the test button. Replace the whole alarm after 10 years.
how do i shut off the water in my house | main water shut off | where is the water valve => The main shut off valve is usually where the water line enters the house, often in a basement, garage or near the water heater. Turn it clockwise to close. Sinks and toilets also have small valves underneath.
how do i stop a toilet from overflowing quickly | toilet water shut off valve | toilet valve behind => Turn the small valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise to stop water flowing into the tank. You can also lift the tank lid and push the flapper down.
what is a gfci outlet | outlet with buttons | reset button on outlet => A GFCI outlet has test and reset buttons and cuts power fast if electricity leaks, like into water. They're used in kitchens, bathrooms and outdoors. If one stops working, press reset.
why should i not overload outlets | power strip safety | too many plugs in one outlet => Plugging too many high power devices into one outlet or power strip can overheat wires and start a fire. Don't chain power strips together, and keep cords out from under rugs.
how do i replace a battery in a remote | changing batteries | which way do batteries go => Open the cover, match the flat end (minus) to the spring and the bump end (plus) to the flat contact, following the plus and minus marks. Replace all batteries at once with the same type.
what battery sizes are there | aa vs aaa | common battery types => Common household sizes are AA, AAA, C, D and 9 volt, plus coin cells like CR2032. AA and AAA are both 1.5 volts, but AA is bigger and lasts longer.
how do i unclog a drain without chemicals | baking soda and vinegar drain | natural drain cleaner => Pour boiling water down first, then try a plunger, or pull hair out with a drain snake. Baking soda and vinegar fizz can loosen light buildup. Chemical drain cleaners are harsh, so let an adult decide.
how do i clean a shower drain of hair | hair clogging drain | drain hair catcher => Remove the drain cover, pull out hair with gloves or a plastic drain snake, and rinse with hot water. A cheap hair catcher prevents clogs.
how do i fix a leaky faucet | dripping tap | faucet drips => A dripping faucet usually has a worn washer or cartridge inside. Turn off the water under the sink first. It's a common adult DIY fix, but get help if you're unsure.
how do i change an air filter | furnace filter | how often to change hvac filter => Turn off the system, slide out the old filter, check the arrow showing airflow direction, and slide in a new one of the same size. Many filters should be changed every 1 to 3 months.
how do i defrost a freezer | freezer full of ice | frost in freezer => Move food to a cooler, unplug the freezer, put towels down, and leave the door open until the ice melts. Don't chip ice with sharp tools, since you could puncture it.
how do i fix a squeaky floor | creaky floorboards | floor squeaks => Squeaks happen when floorboards rub against nails or each other. Powder like talc brushed into gaps can help, while bigger fixes involve screwing boards to joists, usually by an adult.
how do i hang shelves | install a shelf | wall shelf how to => With an adult, find studs or use proper wall anchors, mark level holes, drill pilot holes, screw in the brackets, then attach the shelf. Check that it's level before tightening fully.
how do i assemble a lego set faster | sorting lego pieces | lego building tips => Sort pieces by color or type in trays before building, follow the instructions one bag at a time, and use the brick separator tool. Check each step's picture closely before moving on.
what is a workbench | how to set up a workshop | kid workshop setup => A workbench is a sturdy table for building. A good space has bright light, a clamp or vise, tools on a pegboard, safety glasses nearby and a way to clean up dust.
what safety gear do i need for building projects | workshop safety | wood shop rules => Wear safety glasses, tie back long hair, avoid loose sleeves and jewelry, use hearing protection with loud tools, wear a dust mask when sanding, and only use power tools with an adult.
what is a vise | bench vise | how to use a vise => A vise is a clamp attached to a bench that holds work tightly while you saw, file or drill. Turn the handle to open and close the jaws. Use soft pads to avoid marking your project.
what is a file tool | how to use a metal file | rasp vs file => A file is a hardened steel bar with ridges for smoothing and shaping metal, plastic or wood. A rasp is a coarser file for wood. Push the file forward, since most cut only in one direction.
how do i measure and mark wood | measure twice cut once | marking a cut line => Measure carefully, mark with a sharp pencil, use a square to draw straight lines, and check again before cutting. "Measure twice, cut once" saves wood and time.
what is a carpenter square | speed square | how to use a square => A square is an L shaped tool for marking and checking 90 degree angles. A speed square is a triangle that also marks common angles like 45 degrees.
what is plywood | plywood vs mdf | types of wood for projects => Plywood is thin wood layers glued with the grain alternating, so it's strong and stable. MDF is made of pressed wood fibers, smooth but heavy and weak with water. Pine is a cheap, easy softwood for beginners.
what is hardwood vs softwood | is pine hard or soft wood | oak vs pine => Hardwoods come from leafy trees like oak and maple and are usually denser. Softwoods come from conifers like pine and cedar and are easier to cut. Balsa is a hardwood, though it's very soft.
what is wood stain | stain vs paint | how to finish wood => Stain soaks in to color wood while showing the grain. Finishes like polyurethane or oil protect it. Paint covers the grain completely. Work in a ventilated area.
how do i fix a broken mug or plate | glue for ceramics | fixing broken ceramic => Use a strong two part epoxy or super glue made for ceramics, press the pieces together, and hold or tape them while it sets. Glued dishes shouldn't be used for food or hot drinks unless the glue says food safe.
what is super glue | how to use super glue | super glue on skin => Super glue (cyanoacrylate) bonds fast with a tiny drop. If it gets on skin, soak in warm soapy water and gently peel it, or use acetone nail polish remover. Never pull skin apart forcefully.
what is epoxy | two part epoxy | epoxy vs super glue => Epoxy is a glue made by mixing a resin and a hardener. It's very strong, fills gaps and works on many materials, but takes longer to set than super glue.
how do i remove a sticker from something | sticker residue | get sticky goo off => Peel slowly after warming it with a hair dryer, then rub leftover goo with a little cooking oil, rubbing alcohol or a sticker remover, and wipe clean.
how do i fix a squeaky chair | chair squeaks | office chair noise => Tighten all screws and bolts, then spray a little silicone lubricant or WD 40 on moving parts like the swivel and joints. Wipe off extra.
how do i fix a broken zipper pull | zipper pull broke off | replace zipper tab => Clip a small key ring or paperclip through the slider's hole as a temporary pull, or buy a replacement zipper pull that snaps on.
how do i sharpen a pencil without a sharpener | sharpen pencil with knife | no sharpener => With an adult's okay, carefully shave the wood away from you with a craft knife, rotating the pencil, then rub the tip on sandpaper to point it.
how do i fix a stuck jar lid | jar wont open | open a tight jar => Run the lid under hot water for 30 seconds, grip it with a rubber glove or rubber band, or tap the edge of the lid gently on the counter to break the seal.
how do i reset a garbage disposal | garbage disposal stopped | disposal humming not spinning => Turn it off first. Many have a red reset button on the bottom. Never put your hand inside. An adult can use the Allen key slot underneath to free a jam.
