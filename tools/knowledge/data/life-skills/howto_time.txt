# tag: life.howto.time
how do i stop wasting time | i waste too much time | how to be more productive => Write down the three most important things to do today and start with the first one. Notice what eats your time, like scrolling or videos, and save it for after your work is done.
how do i make a to do list that works | to do list tips | best way to write a to do list => Keep it short, three to five things a day, and make each item a clear action, like "write the intro" instead of "essay". Put the most important item at the top and cross off as you go.
what is the eisenhower matrix | urgent vs important | how do i decide what to do first => Sort tasks into four boxes: urgent and important (do now), important but not urgent (plan it), urgent but not important (do quickly or get help), and neither (drop it).
how do i break a big project into steps | big project overwhelm | how to plan a long project => Write the final goal, then list every step backward from the due date, like research, outline, draft, edit. Give each step its own mini deadline on your calendar.
how do i plan my day | daily schedule ideas | how to make a schedule => Block out fixed things first, like school, practice and dinner. Then add homework time, free time and a set bedtime. Leave some gaps for things that take longer than planned.
what is time blocking | how does time blocking work | block schedule for homework => Time blocking means giving each task a set slot on your calendar, like 4:00 to 4:45 math, so you know exactly what to work on and when.
how do i stop procrastinating on a project | putting off my project | how to start something i keep avoiding => Make the first step tiny, like opening the document and writing one sentence. Set a timer for 10 minutes and promise yourself you can stop after, most times you'll keep going.
what is the two minute rule | do it now if it takes two minutes | two minute rule productivity => If something takes less than two minutes, like putting your plate away or replying to a message, do it right away instead of adding it to a list.
how do i balance school and sports | too busy with practice and homework | balancing activities => Use a weekly planner, do homework in short blocks before or right after practice, and use small gaps like the bus ride for flashcards. If it's too much, talk to a parent about cutting one thing.
how do i say no when i'm too busy | too many activities | overscheduled kid => It's okay to turn things down. Say thanks, explain you're busy, and offer another time if you want to. Protecting time for sleep and rest is important.
how do i set goals | how to make a goal | goal setting for kids => Make it specific and measurable, like "practice guitar 15 minutes a day for a month" instead of "get better at guitar". Write it down, set a date and check your progress each week.
what are smart goals | smart goal meaning | how do i write a smart goal => SMART goals are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time bound. For example: "I'll finish a 3 minute animation for my channel by the end of the month."
how do i stick to my goals | i always give up on goals | how to stay consistent => Make it small enough to do even on bad days, track it on a calendar, and tell a friend or parent. If you miss a day, just do it the next day instead of quitting.
how do i track my progress | habit tracker idea | how to see if i'm improving => Mark each day you do it on a calendar or habit app, or keep a log with numbers, like minutes practiced or times run. Seeing a streak keeps you motivated.
how much screen time should i have on school nights | screen time rules for homework | balancing gaming and homework => A good rule is homework and chores first, then games or videos, and screens off about an hour before bed. Agree on limits with your parents so there's no arguing each night.
how do i manage time on youtube or gaming | i play games too long | how to stop gaming when i should => Decide how long before you start, set a timer, and stop at a natural break like the end of a match. Doing it right after homework makes it a reward instead of a distraction.
what do i do when i have too much homework | overwhelmed by homework | so much homework help => List everything with due dates, do what's due soonest first, and work in short focused blocks with breaks. If it's always too much, talk to your teacher or a parent.
how do i plan for a test a week away | study plan for a test | test in 7 days => Split the material into chunks across the days, review a bit each day, and quiz yourself rather than rereading. Do a practice test two days before, and sleep well the night before.
how do i stay focused for longer | short attention span | how to concentrate better => Remove distractions, work in short sessions with breaks, drink water, move around between sessions, and get enough sleep. Focus is like a muscle that grows with practice.
what is multitasking | is multitasking bad | can i do two things at once => Your brain mostly switches quickly between tasks rather than doing both at once, and each switch costs time and focus. Doing one thing at a time usually gets things done faster and better.
how do i use a calendar app | google calendar for school | putting events in my calendar => Add each class, practice and test with its time, and turn on reminders a day before for big things. Use different colors for school, sports and fun.
how do i set reminders on my ipad | ipad reminders app | remind me to do homework => Open the Reminders app and tap New Reminder, type it, then tap the info button to add a date and time. You can also ask Siri, like "remind me to pack my bag at 8 pm".
how do i wake up without feeling tired | feeling groggy in the morning | how to feel awake faster => Keep the same sleep schedule every day, get sunlight soon after waking, drink a glass of water and move around. Hitting snooze often makes grogginess worse.
how do i be on time | i'm late for everything | always running late tips => Set alarms for when to start getting ready and when to leave, not just when you need to arrive. Plan for about 10 extra minutes because things always take longer than expected.

# tag: life.howto.friends
how do i make new friends at a new school | i'm the new kid | making friends when you move => Smile, say hi to people near you, and ask simple questions like "what did you get for number 3" or "what games do you play". Join a club or team, since doing activities together makes friends fast.
how do i start a conversation | what do i say to someone new | conversation starters for kids => Ask about something you both see or share, like the class, a game, or something they're wearing or doing. Ask open questions that can't be answered with just yes or no, then listen and ask a follow up.
how do i keep a conversation going | running out of things to say | awkward silence help => Ask follow up questions about what they just said, share a little about yourself that connects, and show you're listening. People like talking about their interests.
how do i join a group that's already talking | how to join in at lunch | walking up to a group => Stand nearby, listen for a moment, then add something to the topic or ask "can I sit here?" Most people are happy to make room.
how do i make friends if i'm shy | i'm too shy to talk to people | tips for shy kids => Start small, like saying hi or giving a compliment, and find one person who seems friendly or likes the same things. Clubs, teams and online groups for your hobbies make it easier because you already share an interest.
how do i be a good friend | what makes a good friend | being a better friend => Listen, keep their secrets unless someone is in danger, show up when they need you, be honest but kind, and celebrate their wins. Small things like remembering what they told you matter a lot.
how do i know if someone is a real friend | true friend vs fake friend | signs of a good friend => A real friend is kind to you most of the time, respects your no, doesn't make fun of you to look cool, and is happy when good things happen to you. You feel good, not worse, after being with them.
how do i apologize to a friend | how to say sorry | making up with a friend => Say what you did, that you're sorry, and how you'll do better, without "but" excuses. For example: "I'm sorry I left you out at lunch. That wasn't fair and I won't do it again."
how do i accept an apology | someone said sorry to me | do i have to forgive them => You can say "thanks for apologizing" and that you're okay, or that you need a little time. Forgiving means letting go of being mad, but you can still set limits if it keeps happening.
how do i make friends online safely | online friends safety | is it okay to have internet friends => Online friends can be real friends, but keep personal info private, use only safe platforms with your parents knowing, and never meet in person without a parent's okay and being there.
how do i invite someone to hang out | asking a friend to hang out | how to make plans with a friend => Be specific: "Want to come over Saturday to play Minecraft?" Check with your parents first, then make a plan with a time and place.
how do i make friends with similar hobbies | find people who like what i like | clubs for my interests => Join school clubs, teams, coding or robotics groups, art classes, or community centers. Making something together, like a robot or a video, is a great way to bond.
what do i do if i have no friends | i feel like i have no friends | nobody wants to be my friend => That's a really hard feeling, and lots of people go through times like this. Try one new activity where people share your interests, reach out to one person at a time, and talk to a parent or school counselor who can help.
how do i deal with a friend who is mean sometimes | friend makes fun of me | my friend is rude to me => Tell them calmly how it makes you feel, like "it hurts when you joke about that, please stop". If it keeps happening, spend more time with friends who treat you well.
how do i handle a friend who copies me | friend copies everything i do | someone keeps copying me => Copying often means they admire you, so it's not always bad. If it bugs you, mention it kindly, or just focus on your own stuff. For schoolwork copying, don't let them, it can get you both in trouble.
what do i do if my friends are pressuring me | peer pressure what to do | friends keep pushing me to do stuff => Say no clearly and simply, like "nah, I'm good", suggest something else, or leave. Real friends accept your no. If the pressure is about something dangerous, tell a trusted adult.
how do i deal with being left out | friends left me out | not invited to a party => It hurts and that's normal. It may not have been on purpose, so you could ask kindly. Spend time with other friends, and talk to someone you trust about it.
how do i stop gossip | someone is spreading rumors about me | what to do about rumors => Don't spread it further, stay calm and tell close friends the truth. If it keeps going or turns mean, tell a teacher, counselor or parent who can help stop it.
how do i be a good listener | listening skills | how to show i'm listening => Look at the person, put your phone down, don't interrupt, nod, and ask questions about what they said. Repeating back the main idea shows you got it.
how do i give a compliment | nice things to say to friends | how to compliment someone => Be specific and genuine, like "your drawing of that dragon is awesome, the shading is so good". Compliment things people choose, like their skills or effort, more than looks.
how do i take a compliment | someone said something nice what do i say back | i don't know how to accept compliments => Just smile and say "thank you!" You don't need to argue or say it was nothing.
how do i deal with a bully | someone is bullying me | what should i do if i'm being bullied => Tell a trusted adult like a parent, teacher or counselor, it's not tattling. Stay calm, say "stop" firmly and walk away, and stay near friends or adults. If it's online, don't reply, take screenshots and block them.
what should i do if i see someone being bullied | how do i help a kid being bullied | being an upstander => If it's safe, tell the bully to stop or help the kid walk away, then tell an adult. Later, check on the kid and be friendly. Even just sitting with them helps.
how do i stop being a bully | i think i was mean to someone | i want to stop being mean => Noticing it is a great first step. Apologize to the person, think about what made you act that way, and talk to a trusted adult who can help you handle those feelings.
how do i handle a fight with my best friend | best friend is mad at me | we had an argument => Give it a little time to cool down, then talk privately. Listen to their side, explain yours calmly, say sorry for your part, and figure out what to do differently.
how do i stay friends after moving | friend moved away | long distance friendship => Video call, play online games together, send messages, and plan visits when you can. Friendships can last across distance if you both make a little effort.
how do i make friends with my teammates | bond with my team | getting along with my team => Cheer for them, help after practice, talk before and after games and hang out off the field too. Being positive and working hard earns respect fast.
how do i get along with my siblings | fighting with my brother | sibling arguments help => Take turns, ask before using their stuff, and walk away when you're getting angry. Find something you both like doing. If it keeps getting bad, ask a parent to help set fair rules.

# tag: life.howto.teamwork
how do i be a good teammate | what makes a good teammate | being a team player => Show up on time, work hard, cheer for others, accept the coach's choices, and own your mistakes without blaming. Lift people up after a bad play.
how do i lose gracefully | how to be a good loser | what to do after losing a game => Shake hands, say "good game", and don't make excuses or blame teammates or the ref. It's okay to feel upset later, then think about what you can work on.
how do i win without bragging | being a good winner | how to celebrate without showing off => Celebrate with your team, then shake hands and say "good game" to the other side. Give credit to your teammates and the other team.
what is good sportsmanship | sportsmanship examples | why does sportsmanship matter => Sportsmanship means playing fair, following the rules, respecting the refs, coaches and other team, and being gracious whether you win or lose.
how do i deal with a teammate who hogs the ball | teammate won't pass | ball hog on my team => Talk to them calmly after practice, or ask the coach to work on passing drills. Keep making good passes yourself and get open so passing to you is easy.
how do i talk to my coach about playing time | i don't get to play much | ask coach for more minutes => Ask the coach privately when they're not busy, "What can I work on to earn more playing time?" Then work hard on that. It shows maturity and they'll notice.
how do i deal with making a mistake in a game | i messed up in the game | how to get over a bad play => Everyone makes mistakes, even pros. Take a breath, say "next play" to yourself, and focus on what's happening now. Learn from it after the game, not during.
how do i be a good team leader | how to be a captain | leadership tips for kids => Lead by example: work the hardest and stay positive. Encourage quieter teammates, listen to ideas, and handle problems calmly and fairly.
how do i work well in a group project | group project tips | how to split up group work => Agree on a goal, split tasks so everyone has a clear part, set mini deadlines and check in often. Use a shared doc so everyone can see progress.
what do i do if someone isn't helping in a group project | lazy partner in group work | group member not doing anything => Talk to them first and ask what's going on, then give them a clear task with a deadline. If they still don't help, tell the teacher early and politely, with what you've tried.
how do i give feedback nicely | how to tell someone their work needs fixing | constructive criticism => Start with something good, be specific about one thing to improve and how, then end positively. Talk about the work, not the person.
how do i take criticism | someone criticized my work | how to handle feedback => Listen without arguing, thank them, and think about whether it helps. Good feedback is a shortcut to getting better, even when it stings a bit.
how do i share fairly | taking turns | how to split things fairly => Take turns, set a timer, or use the "one cuts, the other chooses" trick so both people get a fair share.
