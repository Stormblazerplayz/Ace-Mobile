# tag: life.sports.gear
what size surfboard should i get | beginner surfboard size | foam board => Beginners do best on a long, wide, thick foam board, often around 2.4 to 2.7 m (8 to 9 ft), because it floats well and catches waves easily. Shorter boards come later.
what size golf clubs do i need | junior golf clubs | golf club length => Junior clubs are sized by height. When you stand holding the club, the sole should rest flat on the ground with your hands at your hips. Most brands have height charts.
how should soccer cleats fit | cleat fit | cleats too big => Cleats should fit snugly with about a thumb's width or less of room at the toe, no heel slipping, and no pinching. They usually feel tighter than sneakers.
what is the difference between turf shoes and cleats | turf shoes | indoor soccer shoes => Cleats have studs for grass. Turf shoes have many small rubber nubs for artificial turf, and indoor shoes have flat soles for gym floors.
what are knee pads for | knee pads | volleyball knee pads => Knee pads cushion your knees from hard falls and floors. Volleyball players, skaters and wrestlers use them a lot.
how often should i replace running shoes | running shoe mileage | worn out shoes => Running shoes usually last about 500 to 800 km (300 to 500 mi). Replace them sooner if the cushioning feels flat or the treads are worn smooth.
what are swim fins for | swim fins | kickboard => Swim fins make your kick stronger and help build leg strength and good kicking technique. Short training fins are common for swimmers.
what is a pull buoy | pull buoy | swim training tools => A pull buoy is a foam float you squeeze between your thighs so your legs float without kicking, letting you focus on arm strokes.
what is a rash guard | rash guard | swim shirt => A rash guard is a stretchy, quick drying swim shirt that protects your skin from the sun, board rubbing and scrapes. Surfers wear them a lot.
what is chalk for in climbing | climbing chalk | chalk bag => Climbing chalk soaks up sweat on your hands so you grip holds better. It's carried in a small bag on your harness.
what are climbing shoes | climbing shoes | climbing shoes tight => Climbing shoes have sticky rubber soles and a snug fit so you can stand on tiny edges. They should be tight but not painful.
what is a harness | climbing harness | harness fit => A climbing harness wraps around your waist and legs and connects you to the rope. It should be snug, with the waist belt above your hip bones and buckles double checked.
what is a carabiner | carabiner | locking carabiner => A carabiner is a strong metal clip with a spring gate used in climbing. Locking carabiners have a sleeve that screws closed for important connections.
what is a helmet rating | helmet certification | cpsc helmet => Helmets are tested to safety standards. In the US, bike helmets must meet CPSC rules, and you'll see that label inside. Use a helmet made for your sport.
what is mips | mips helmet | rotational impact => MIPS is a low friction layer inside some helmets that lets the helmet slide slightly on impact, which can reduce twisting forces on the brain.
what are elbow pads | elbow pads | pads for skating => Elbow pads have hard plastic caps that slide when you fall, protecting your elbows. Skaters, BMX riders and hockey players wear them.
what is a hockey jock | hockey equipment list | hockey gear order => A hockey jock is protective shorts with a hard cup. Full hockey gear also includes shin guards, pants, shoulder and elbow pads, gloves, a helmet with a cage and skates.
what does a goalie wear | goalie equipment | goalie pads => Hockey goalies wear big leg pads, a blocker and catcher glove, a chest protector, a mask with a cage, and a special stick. Soccer goalies wear padded gloves and a different color jersey.
what is a cricket bat made of | cricket bat | willow bat => Cricket bats are made of willow wood, usually English or Kashmir willow, with a cane handle wrapped in a rubber grip.
what is a rugby ball shape | rugby ball | rugby vs football ball => A rugby ball is an oval, larger and rounder at the ends than an American football, which makes it easier to pass with two hands.
what is a volleyball made of | volleyball | indoor vs beach volleyball ball => Indoor volleyballs have leather or synthetic leather panels and are heavier and firmer. Beach volleyballs are a bit larger and softer, with lower pressure.
what is a tennis ball made of | tennis ball | why tennis balls are fuzzy => A tennis ball is a hollow rubber ball covered in felt. The fuzz slows it down and helps players control spin.
why are tennis balls yellow | tennis ball color | optic yellow => Tennis balls became yellow in the 1970s because it's easier to see on color TV. Wimbledon switched from white to yellow in 1986.
what is a pickleball paddle made of | pickleball paddle | pickleball ball holes => Pickleball paddles are solid, without strings, usually made of a honeycomb core covered by fiberglass or carbon fiber. The ball is plastic with holes, like a wiffle ball.
what is a bowling ball made of | bowling ball weight | bowling ball inside => Bowling balls have a dense core inside a shell of plastic, urethane or resin. They weigh up to 7.3 kg (16 lb), and kids usually use about 10 percent of their body weight.
what is a basketball made of | basketball material | indoor vs outdoor basketball => Basketballs have a rubber bladder inside and an outer cover of leather, composite (synthetic) leather or rubber.
what is the difference between indoor and outdoor basketballs | indoor basketball | composite leather => Indoor balls are often leather and feel softer but wear out on rough surfaces. Outdoor balls are rubber or durable composite to survive asphalt.
how do i pump up a ball | ball pump | needle ball pump => Wet the needle a little, push it straight into the valve, and pump to the pressure printed on the ball. Don't overinflate, which can damage it.
how much air should a basketball have | basketball pressure | ball psi => Most basketballs list a pressure of about 7.5 to 8.5 psi. A good test: dropped from your head height, it should bounce up to about your waist.
what is a jersey number rule | jersey numbers | why players pick numbers => Leagues have number rules. In the NFL, numbers once showed position groups, though rules loosened in 2021. Many players pick numbers for meaning, and some famous numbers are retired.
why do athletes wear compression sleeves | compression sleeve | arm sleeve => Compression sleeves give snug support, keep muscles warm and protect skin from scrapes. Studies on performance benefits are mixed, but many athletes like how they feel.
what are batting gloves for | batting gloves | why wear batting gloves => Batting gloves improve grip on the bat, reduce stinging on cold days and help prevent blisters.
what is pine tar | pine tar bat | grip on bat => Pine tar is a sticky substance baseball hitters put on the bat handle for better grip. MLB limits how far up the bat it can go.
what is a rosin bag | rosin bag | pitcher rosin => A rosin bag holds a powder made from tree sap that pitchers pat on their hands to dry sweat and improve grip on the ball.
what is grip tape | grip tape | skateboard grip tape => Grip tape is the sandpaper like sheet stuck on top of a skateboard deck so your shoes don't slip.
what is overgrip | tennis overgrip | racket grip tape => An overgrip is a thin, cushioned tape wrapped over a racket handle for better grip and sweat absorption. Players replace it often.
what is wax for surfboards | surf wax | why wax surfboard => Surf wax makes the board's top sticky so your feet don't slip. Different waxes are made for different water temperatures.
what is ski wax | waxing skis | snowboard wax => Ski and snowboard wax goes on the base to help it glide faster and protect it. Boards are waxed every few days of riding.
what are ski goggles for | ski goggles | goggle lens colors => Ski goggles protect your eyes from wind, snow, glare and UV light. Lens tints help see in bright sun or flat, cloudy light.
what are ski boots | ski boot fit | ski boots hurt => Ski boots are stiff plastic boots that clip into bindings and transfer your movements to the skis. They should fit snug with toes just brushing the front.
what are bindings | ski bindings | snowboard bindings => Bindings connect your boots to skis or a snowboard. Ski bindings are set to release your boot in a fall to help prevent injuries.
what is a snowboard stance width | stance width | binding angles => Stance width is the distance between your bindings, usually about shoulder width. Angles are often a little forward on the front foot and zero or slightly back on the rear.
what is a bike computer | cycling computer | bike gps => A bike computer mounts on your handlebars and shows speed, distance, time and sometimes maps and heart rate, often using GPS.
what is a heart rate monitor | heart rate monitor | chest strap => A heart rate monitor measures your heartbeats per minute, using a chest strap or a watch sensor, to show how hard you're working.
what is a resistance band | resistance bands | band workouts => A resistance band is a stretchy band used for strength exercises. Lighter bands are easier, and they're great for warmups and home workouts.
what is a medicine ball | medicine ball | med ball throws => A medicine ball is a weighted ball used for throws, twists and core exercises. Athletes use it to build power.
what is a foam roller | foam roller | foam rolling => A foam roller is a firm foam cylinder you roll your muscles over to loosen tight spots, often after workouts.
what is a balance board | balance board | balance training => A balance board is a board on a roller or rounded base that you stand on to train balance, popular with skaters, surfers and snowboarders.
what is a pitchback | rebounder net | pitch back net => A pitchback or rebounder is a springy net that throws the ball back to you, so you can practice catching, throwing or passing alone.
what is a ball machine | tennis ball machine | pitching machine => A ball machine launches balls for practice, like tennis ball machines or baseball pitching machines, so you can repeat shots without a partner.
