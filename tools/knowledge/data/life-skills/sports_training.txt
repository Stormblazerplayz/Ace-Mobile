# tag: sports.training
what is a good warm up before sports | how to warm up | dynamic warm up exercises => Do 5 to 10 minutes of light jogging, then moving stretches like leg swings, high knees, butt kicks, arm circles and walking lunges. Then do a few practice moves from your sport.
should i stretch before or after exercise | static stretching vs dynamic | when to stretch => Before exercise, use moving (dynamic) stretches to warm up. Save holding stretches (static) for after, when muscles are warm, holding each for about 20 to 30 seconds.
how much should a kid my age train | how many days a week should i practice | too much training for kids => Kids are encouraged to get at least 60 minutes of activity a day. For a single sport, taking at least 1 or 2 rest days a week and some months off helps prevent overuse injuries and burnout.
is it bad to play only one sport | should i specialize in one sport | playing multiple sports => Playing several sports while young builds more all around skills and lowers the risk of overuse injuries and burnout. Many experts suggest not specializing in one sport until the mid teens.
how do i get better at a sport fast | how to improve at sports | practice tips for sports => Practice the basic skills often with focus, get feedback from a coach, watch skilled players, play against slightly better opponents, and work on fitness too. Short daily practice beats one long session a week.
what is deliberate practice | how to practice effectively | practice smarter => Deliberate practice means working on a specific weakness with full focus and quick feedback, instead of just repeating what you're already good at. It's harder but improves skills much faster.
how do i get stronger as a kid | strength training for kids => Supervised strength training is safe for kids when it focuses on good form. Start with bodyweight moves like push ups, squats, planks and lunges, and use light weights only with a trained adult.
how do i do a proper squat | squat form | how to squat correctly => Stand with feet shoulder width apart, push your hips back like sitting in a chair, keep your chest up and knees in line with your toes, go as low as is comfortable, then stand up through your heels.
how do i improve my balance | balance exercises | better balance for sports => Stand on one foot for 30 seconds, then try it with eyes closed or on a pillow. Yoga, slacklining and single leg exercises also help. Good balance helps in nearly every sport.
how do i improve my agility | agility drills | change direction faster => Try ladder drills, cone drills like zigzags, shuttle runs and side shuffles. Stay low with bent knees and take short, quick steps when changing direction.
how do i improve hand eye coordination | hand eye coordination drills | better reflexes => Play catch against a wall, juggle, catch tennis balls with one hand, use a reaction ball, or play ping pong. A little daily practice helps.
what should i eat before a game | pregame meal | food before sports => Eat a meal with carbs, some protein and little fat 2 to 3 hours before, like pasta with chicken or a turkey sandwich. A small snack like a banana works about 30 to 60 minutes before.
how much water should i drink during sports | hydration for athletes => Drink water before you start, sip during practice, especially in heat, and drink after. For most kids' practices under an hour, water is enough. Pale yellow pee means you're hydrated.
how much sleep do athletes need | does sleep help sports | sleep and performance => Teens need about 8 to 10 hours. Good sleep improves reaction time, accuracy and recovery, and lowers injury risk. Many pro athletes treat sleep as part of training.
why are rest days important | do i need rest days | overtraining signs => Muscles get stronger while you rest, not while you train. Signs of overtraining include constant soreness, tiredness, worse performance, crankiness and getting sick often.
how to treat a sprain | sprained ankle care => For minor sprains, many people follow rest, ice (20 minutes at a time, wrapped in cloth), compression with a bandage, and elevation. See a doctor if you can't put weight on it, it looks deformed, or it doesn't improve.
what is a concussion in sports | hit my head playing sports => A concussion is a brain injury from a hit to the head or body. Signs include headache, dizziness, confusion, nausea and blurry vision. Stop playing right away, tell an adult, and get checked by a doctor before returning.
how do i get rid of a muscle cramp | leg cramp during sports | charley horse => Gently stretch and massage the cramping muscle. Drink water, and if you've been sweating a lot, have something with salt. Most cramps pass in a minute or two.
how do i deal with nerves before a game | pregame jitters | nervous before a competition => Some nerves help you focus. Take slow deep breaths, stick to your warm up routine, focus on what you can control, and picture yourself doing well. Even pros get nervous.
how do i bounce back after losing a game | losing a game feels bad | dealing with a loss in sports => It's normal to feel down. Give yourself time, then think about one thing that went well and one thing to practice. Every great athlete has lost many times.
how do i deal with a bad referee call | ref made a bad call | arguing with the referee => Stay calm and keep playing. Arguing rarely changes a call and can get you penalized. Let your coach handle it, and refocus on the next play.
how do i handle a coach yelling at me | my coach is too harsh | coach is mean to me => Listen for the useful part of the feedback. If a coach insults, embarrasses or hurts players, or it makes you dread going, talk to a parent or another trusted adult about it.
how do i tell my parents i want to quit a sport | want to quit my team | should i quit a sport => Think about why: is it the sport, the team, or something else? Talk honestly with your parents. Many families agree to finish the season before stopping, since teammates count on you.
how do i become team captain | being a good team captain | leadership in sports => Work hard at every practice, encourage teammates, stay positive when things go wrong, know the rules, and communicate with the coach. Captains lead by example more than by talking.
how do i make the team at tryouts | tips for tryouts | sports tryout advice => Show up early and ready, hustle on every drill, listen closely, encourage others, and show you're coachable. Coaches notice effort and attitude as much as skill.
what if i didnt make the team | cut from the team | didnt make tryouts => It hurts, and that's okay. Ask the coach what to work on, practice those skills, and try again next time. Many great athletes were cut early, and you can also try a rec league.
what is cross training | why do athletes cross train | cross training examples => Cross training means doing other activities to support your main sport, like swimming or cycling for a runner. It builds all around fitness and reduces overuse injuries.
what is interval training | hiit for kids | interval workout example => Interval training alternates hard bursts with easier recovery, like 30 seconds sprint, 90 seconds walk, repeated 6 times. It builds speed and fitness efficiently.
how do i track my fitness progress | measure sports improvement | fitness log => Keep a log of workouts, times, distances or reps, and test yourself every few weeks. A watch or app can help, but a notebook works fine too.
what is muscle memory | how does muscle memory work | practice until automatic => Muscle memory is when repeated practice makes a movement automatic, because your brain builds stronger pathways for it. It's why practicing correct form matters.
how do i visualize for sports | visualization in sports | mental rehearsal => Close your eyes and picture yourself doing the skill perfectly, with as much detail as you can: sights, sounds and feelings. Many athletes use this along with real practice.
how do i stay motivated to exercise | no motivation to work out | make exercise fun => Pick activities you enjoy, set small goals, exercise with friends, track progress, and mix things up. Biking, hiking or dancing count as exercise too.
what is a good fitness goal for a kid | fitness goals ideas | set a sports goal => Pick specific goals, like running a mile without stopping, doing 10 push ups, or landing a new bike trick. Write them down with a date and track progress.
how fast can a kid run a mile | good mile time for a 12 year old | average mile time kids => Many active 12 year olds run a mile in about 8 to 11 minutes, while well trained young runners can go under 6. Focus on improving your own time.
how to train for a 5k | training plan for my first 5k | get ready for a 5k run => To train for a 5K, run or run walk 3 times a week and slowly increase distance over 6 to 8 weeks.
how do i improve my throwing arm | throwing technique => Step toward your target with the opposite foot, turn your hips and shoulders, lead with your elbow, and follow through across your body. Build up throwing gradually and rest your arm.
how do i catch a ball better | stop dropping the ball => Watch the ball all the way into your hands, keep your hands relaxed, and pull them in slightly as you catch to soften it. Practice with a partner or against a wall.
how do i get better at defense | defensive stance | play better defense => Stay low with knees bent and feet shoulder width apart, keep your eyes on the opponent's hips or the ball, and move with quick shuffles instead of crossing your feet.
why do athletes wear mouthguards | do i need a mouthguard => Mouthguards protect teeth, lips and jaws in contact sports like football, hockey, lacrosse and basketball. A properly fitted one is much cheaper than fixing a broken tooth.
what sports gear should i always wear | protective gear for sports | safety equipment sports => Wear the gear your sport requires, such as a helmet for biking, skating and football, shin guards for soccer, and a mouthguard for contact sports. Gear should fit well and be in good condition.
what are good running shoes for kids | how to choose running shoes | running shoe fit => Look for shoes that fit with about a thumb's width of space at the toe, feel comfortable right away and suit your sport. Replace them when the cushioning wears out, often around 300 to 500 miles.
how do i lace shoes for running | heel lock lacing | stop heel slipping in shoes => Use the extra top eyelet to make a loop on each side, then thread each lace through the opposite loop before tying. This heel lock keeps your heel from slipping.
what is an athletic trainer | athletic trainer vs coach | sports medicine => An athletic trainer is a health professional who prevents and treats sports injuries. A coach teaches skills and strategy.
what is a sports physical | do i need a physical for sports | pre participation exam => Many schools and leagues require a yearly sports physical, where a doctor checks your health, heart and joints to make sure it's safe to play.
what is heat exhaustion in sports | practicing in hot weather => In heat, drink water often, take breaks in the shade, and wear light clothes. Dizziness, heavy sweating, nausea or headache can mean heat exhaustion. Stop, cool down and tell an adult.
how do i run in cold weather | winter running tips => Dress in layers you can remove, cover your hands, ears and head, and warm up indoors. Watch for ice. You still need to drink water in cold weather.
how do i do a backflip safely | learn a backflip | is a backflip dangerous => Learn backflips only with a trained coach using mats, spotting and progressions like back rolls and trampoline work. Trying one alone can cause serious neck injuries.
how do i do a front roll | forward roll | somersault how to => Squat with hands on the mat, tuck your chin to your chest, push with your legs and roll over the back of your shoulders, not your head. Come up to your feet.
what is yoga good for | yoga for kids | is yoga exercise => Yoga builds flexibility, balance and strength and can help you calm down and focus. Many athletes use it to recover and prevent injuries.
what are core exercises | why is core strength important | core workout for kids => Core exercises strengthen your stomach, back and hips, like planks, side planks, bird dogs and dead bugs. A strong core helps with balance, power and injury prevention.
what does a coach do | what makes a good coach | role of a coach => A coach teaches skills, plans practices, sets strategy, motivates players and helps them grow as athletes and people. Good coaches are fair, encouraging and clear.
what is a scrimmage | scrimmage meaning | practice game => A scrimmage is a practice game where the score doesn't count, often against teammates or another team, to work on skills in game situations.
what is a varsity team | jv vs varsity | junior varsity meaning => In US high schools, varsity is the main top team, and junior varsity (JV) is a level below it, often for younger or developing players.
what are travel teams | rec league vs travel team | club sports => Rec (recreational) leagues are local, cheaper and open to all. Travel or club teams are competitive, require tryouts, cost more and travel to tournaments.
how do sports scholarships work | athletic scholarship | college sports scholarship => Colleges can give scholarships to talented athletes to pay part or all of tuition. Only a small percentage of high school athletes get them, so good grades matter a lot too.
what is the ncaa | college sports organization | ncaa divisions => The NCAA (National Collegiate Athletic Association) runs most US college sports. It has three divisions, I, II and III, with Division I being the most competitive.
how do people become pro athletes | how to go pro in sports | chances of going pro => Pro athletes train for years, play at high levels like college or academies, and get drafted or signed. Very few make it, so enjoy the sport and keep other plans too.
