# tag: life.cooking.meat
what are the cuts of beef | beef cuts explained | most tender beef cut => Tender cuts come from muscles that move less, like tenderloin (filet mignon), ribeye and strip, and are best cooked fast. Tougher cuts like chuck, brisket and short ribs are best cooked low and slow.
what is filet mignon | tenderloin steak | most expensive steak => Filet mignon is cut from the tenderloin, the most tender part of the cow, because that muscle does very little work. It's lean, buttery soft and pricey.
what is a ribeye | ribeye vs sirloin | marbling in steak => A ribeye comes from the rib area and has lots of marbling, the thin white streaks of fat that melt and add flavor and juiciness. Sirloin is leaner and a bit chewier.
what is brisket | how to cook brisket | brisket barbecue => Brisket is a tough cut from the cow's chest. Cooking it low and slow for many hours, by smoking or braising, breaks down its connective tissue so it becomes tender. It's famous in Texas barbecue.
what is ground beef 80 20 | lean ground beef | ground beef fat percent => The numbers show lean meat to fat. 80/20 is 80 percent lean, 20 percent fat, juicy for burgers. 90/10 or 93/7 is leaner, good for sauces and tacos where you don't want grease.
what are the cuts of chicken | chicken parts | white meat vs dark meat => Chicken cuts include breasts, wings, thighs, drumsticks and tenderloins. Breast is white meat, lean and quick to dry out. Thighs and drumsticks are dark meat, juicier and more forgiving.
why is chicken thigh better than breast | chicken thighs vs breasts | dark meat juicy => Thighs have more fat and connective tissue, so they stay juicy even if slightly overcooked and have more flavor. Breasts are leaner and dry out quickly if overcooked.
how do i cook chicken wings | crispy wings | baked chicken wings => Pat wings very dry, toss with a little baking powder (not soda) and salt, and bake on a rack at about 220 C (425 F) for 40 to 50 minutes, flipping once. Cook to at least 74 C (165 F).
how do i make chicken tenders | homemade chicken nuggets | breaded chicken strips => Cut chicken into strips, dip in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs or panko. Bake at about 220 C (425 F) for 15 to 20 minutes, or pan fry until golden and cooked to 74 C (165 F).
how do i cook a whole chicken | roast chicken | roasting a chicken => Pat the chicken dry, season well inside and out, and roast at about 220 C (425 F) for roughly 1 to 1.5 hours, until the thickest part of the thigh reads 74 C (165 F). Rest 10 to 15 minutes before carving.
how do i carve a turkey | carving a chicken | how to carve poultry => Let it rest, then cut through the skin between leg and body and pull the leg away to pop the joint. Separate drumstick and thigh, remove wings, then slice each breast off the bone and cut across.
how long does it take to cook a turkey | turkey cooking time | how long to roast a turkey => At about 165 C (325 F), an unstuffed turkey takes roughly 13 minutes per pound (450 g). A 14 pound turkey takes about 3 hours. It's done when the thigh reaches 74 C (165 F).
how do i thaw a turkey | turkey thawing time | defrost turkey in fridge => Thaw it in the fridge, allowing about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, so a 15 pound turkey needs 3 or more days. For faster thawing, submerge it wrapped in cold water, changing water every 30 minutes.
what is pulled pork | how to make pulled pork | pork shoulder => Pulled pork is pork shoulder cooked low and slow, by smoking, roasting or slow cooking, until it's so tender you can pull it apart with forks, usually around 90 to 95 C (195 to 205 F) inside.
what is bacon made from | what part of the pig is bacon | turkey bacon => US bacon is cured, usually smoked pork belly cut into strips. Canadian bacon and back bacon come from the pork loin. Turkey bacon is made from pressed turkey meat.
what is ham | ham vs pork | is ham cooked => Ham is pork from the back leg, usually cured with salt and often smoked. Most store hams are fully cooked and just need reheating.
what is a hot dog made of | hot dog ingredients | are hot dogs healthy => Hot dogs are made from finely ground meat, like beef, pork or chicken, with salt, spices and curing agents, stuffed into casings. They're processed meat, so they're best as an occasional food.
what is sausage | types of sausage | how to cook sausage => Sausage is ground meat mixed with fat, salt and spices in a casing. Raw sausages like bratwurst and Italian sausage must be cooked to 71 C (160 F). Others like salami are cured and ready to eat.
what is jerky | how is jerky made | beef jerky => Jerky is thin strips of lean meat that are seasoned and dried at low heat until most moisture is gone, so it lasts a long time without refrigeration.
how do i make meatballs | homemade meatballs | meatballs falling apart => Mix ground meat with breadcrumbs soaked in milk, an egg, grated parmesan, garlic, salt and herbs. Don't overmix. Roll into balls and bake or brown them, then simmer in sauce until cooked through.
how do i make tacos | taco meat | how to make ground beef tacos => Brown ground beef, drain extra fat, stir in taco seasoning with a splash of water and simmer until thick. Serve in warm tortillas with toppings like lettuce, cheese, salsa and sour cream.
how do i make a quesadilla | quesadilla in a pan | cheese quesadilla => Put shredded cheese and any fillings on half a flour tortilla, fold it over, and cook in a pan over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese melts.
how do i make chili | simple chili | chili con carne => Brown ground beef with onion and garlic, add chili powder and cumin, then canned tomatoes and beans. Simmer at least 30 minutes, stirring now and then. It tastes even better the next day.
how do i use a slow cooker | crock pot tips | slow cooker safety => Fill it half to two thirds full, put firm vegetables at the bottom and meat on top, add some liquid, and keep the lid on. Low takes about 7 to 8 hours, high about 3 to 4. Don't put frozen meat in.
how do i use a pressure cooker | instant pot basics | is an instant pot safe => A pressure cooker traps steam to raise the temperature, cooking food much faster. Always use enough liquid, never overfill, make sure the valve is set right, and let an adult handle releasing pressure.
# tag: life.cooking.seafood
what are the types of fish for cooking | mild fish for beginners | white fish vs oily fish => Mild white fish like cod, tilapia and halibut are flaky and gentle in flavor. Oily fish like salmon, mackerel and sardines are richer and high in omega 3 fats.
how do i cook shrimp | shrimp cooking time | when is shrimp done => Shrimp cook fast, in 2 to 3 minutes per side, and are done when they turn pink and opaque and curl into a C shape. An O shape means they're overcooked and rubbery.
how do i devein shrimp | what is the vein in shrimp | cleaning shrimp => The dark line along a shrimp's back is its digestive tract. Cut a shallow slit along the back with a small knife and pull it out. It's safe to eat but can be gritty.
what is canned tuna | is canned tuna healthy | tuna in water vs oil => Canned tuna is cooked tuna packed in water or oil. It's cheap and full of protein. Light tuna has less mercury than albacore, so it can be eaten more often.
why should i limit some fish | mercury in fish | which fish have mercury => Big predatory fish like swordfish, shark, king mackerel and bigeye tuna have more mercury. Kids are advised to choose lower mercury fish like salmon, cod, shrimp and canned light tuna.
what are fish sticks made of | fish fingers | are fish sticks real fish => Fish sticks are usually made from white fish like pollock or cod, cut into strips, breaded and frozen. They're real fish, just processed.
how do i cook mussels | cooking clams | steaming mussels => Rinse and scrub them, throw away any with broken shells or that stay open when tapped. Steam in a covered pot with a little liquid for about 5 to 7 minutes until they open. Discard any that don't open.
how do lobsters turn red | why do lobsters change color when cooked | lobster color => Lobster shells contain a red pigment called astaxanthin bound to proteins that make it look blue green or brown. Heat breaks those proteins, releasing the bright red color.
what is surimi | imitation crab | what is fake crab made of => Imitation crab is surimi: white fish like pollock that's minced, mixed with starch and flavor, and shaped and colored to look like crab legs. It's fully cooked.
what is smoked salmon | lox vs smoked salmon | is smoked salmon raw => Lox is salmon cured in salt but not cooked or smoked. Cold smoked salmon is cured and smoked at low temperatures, so it's still silky. Hot smoked salmon is cooked and flaky.
# tag: life.cooking.sauces
what are the five mother sauces | french mother sauces | list the mother sauces => The five French mother sauces are bechamel (milk and roux), veloute (light stock and roux), espagnole (brown sauce), hollandaise (butter and egg yolk) and tomato sauce. Many other sauces are made from them.
what is bechamel | white sauce | how to make bechamel => Bechamel is a white sauce made by cooking butter and flour into a roux, then whisking in warm milk until thick. It's the base for mac and cheese, lasagna and gratins.
what is hollandaise | hollandaise sauce | eggs benedict sauce => Hollandaise is a rich sauce of egg yolks, melted butter and lemon juice whisked into an emulsion. It's served on eggs Benedict and vegetables. It can split if it gets too hot.
how do i make cheese sauce | homemade cheese sauce | mac and cheese sauce => Make a roux with butter and flour, whisk in warm milk until thick, take it off the heat, then stir in shredded cheese a handful at a time. Freshly grated cheese melts smoother than bagged.
how do i make mac and cheese from scratch | homemade mac and cheese | stovetop mac and cheese => Boil pasta until just al dente, make a cheese sauce from butter, flour, milk and cheddar, then stir in the pasta. For baked, top with breadcrumbs and bake until bubbly.
how do i make tomato sauce | easy pasta sauce | marinara sauce => Gently cook garlic in olive oil, add canned crushed tomatoes, salt and a pinch of sugar if they're sour, and simmer 15 to 20 minutes. Add basil at the end.
what is marinara vs pizza sauce | marinara vs spaghetti sauce | pizza sauce difference => Marinara is a simple, quick tomato sauce. Pizza sauce is often uncooked, thicker and seasoned with oregano. Spaghetti sauce is a broader term that can include meat and vegetables.
what is bolognese | meat sauce | ragu vs bolognese => Bolognese is a slow cooked Italian meat sauce, ragu alla bolognese, from Bologna, with onion, carrot, celery, ground meat, a little tomato and milk. It's traditionally served with tagliatelle.
what is alfredo sauce | fettuccine alfredo | how to make alfredo => Original fettuccine Alfredo from Rome is pasta tossed with butter and parmesan. The creamy American version adds heavy cream and garlic.
how do i make gravy | gravy from drippings | homemade gravy => Pour off most of the fat from the roasting pan, stir a few tablespoons of flour into the remaining fat over medium heat, then whisk in broth slowly and simmer until thick. Season with salt and pepper.
why is my gravy lumpy | fix lumpy gravy | smooth gravy => Lumps form when flour hits hot liquid too fast. Whisk hard, strain it through a sieve, or blend it. Next time, add the liquid slowly while whisking.
what is barbecue sauce | types of bbq sauce | kansas city vs carolina bbq => Barbecue sauces vary by region: Kansas City sauce is sweet, thick and tomato based, Carolina sauces can be vinegar or mustard based, Memphis is tangy, and Alabama has a white mayo based sauce.
what is ketchup made of | history of ketchup | ketchup ingredients => Ketchup is made from tomatoes, vinegar, sugar, salt and spices. The word likely comes from a Chinese fish sauce called ke tsiap. Tomato ketchup became popular in the US in the 1800s.
what is mayonnaise made of | how is mayo made | homemade mayo => Mayonnaise is an emulsion of oil, egg yolk and an acid like lemon juice or vinegar. Whisking oil in slowly lets the yolk hold tiny oil droplets suspended in water.
what is ranch dressing | ranch ingredients | where did ranch come from => Ranch dressing is made from buttermilk, mayonnaise or sour cream, garlic, onion and herbs like dill and parsley. It was created in the 1950s at Hidden Valley Ranch in California.
what is a vinaigrette | vinaigrette ratio | how to make vinaigrette => A vinaigrette is a dressing of oil and vinegar, usually about 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar, with salt and pepper. Shake it in a jar with a little mustard to help it stay mixed.
what is hot sauce made of | types of hot sauce | sriracha vs tabasco => Hot sauce is made from chili peppers, vinegar and salt. Tabasco is thin and vinegary, sriracha is thicker with garlic and sugar, and Mexican style sauces like Cholula are milder.
what is the scoville scale | how spicy is a jalapeno | hottest pepper in the world => The Scoville scale measures pepper heat. Bell peppers are 0, jalapenos about 2,500 to 8,000, habaneros 100,000 to 350,000, and as of 2025 the hottest recognized pepper, Pepper X, averages about 2.69 million.
what is soy sauce vs tamari | light vs dark soy sauce | low sodium soy sauce => Light soy sauce is thinner and saltier for seasoning. Dark soy sauce is thicker, sweeter and used for color. Tamari is a Japanese style soy sauce, often made with little or no wheat.
what is fish sauce | how to use fish sauce | fish sauce smell => Fish sauce is a salty, savory liquid made from fermented fish and salt, used in Thai and Vietnamese cooking. It smells strong in the bottle but adds deep flavor in small amounts.
what is hoisin sauce | hoisin vs oyster sauce | hoisin uses => Hoisin is a thick, sweet and salty Chinese sauce made from fermented soybeans, garlic and spices, used on Peking duck and in stir fries. Oyster sauce is savory and made with oyster extract.
what is teriyaki sauce made of | homemade teriyaki | teriyaki ingredients => Teriyaki sauce is made from soy sauce, mirin or sake, and sugar, simmered until glossy. Teriyaki means glossy grilled in Japanese.
what is chimichurri | argentina green sauce | chimichurri ingredients => Chimichurri is an Argentine sauce of chopped parsley, garlic, oregano, olive oil, vinegar and red pepper flakes, served with grilled steak.
what is guacamole made of | how to make guacamole | guac ingredients => Mash ripe avocados with lime juice and salt, then mix in diced onion, cilantro, tomato and a little jalapeno if you like heat. Taste and adjust lime and salt.
what is queso | queso dip | how to make queso => Queso is a melted cheese dip, from the Spanish word for cheese. A simple version melts American or cheddar cheese with milk and canned diced tomatoes with green chiles.
what is a pan sauce | how to make a pan sauce | sauce from drippings => After searing meat, pour off extra fat, add a liquid like broth to dissolve the browned bits, simmer to reduce, then swirl in a little cold butter off the heat.
how do i fix a broken sauce | sauce separated | split sauce fix => If a butter or cream sauce separates, take it off the heat, add a spoonful of warm water or cream and whisk hard. For mayo or hollandaise, whisk the broken sauce slowly into a fresh egg yolk.
