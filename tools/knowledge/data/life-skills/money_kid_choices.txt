# tag: life.money.choices
how do i save up for a bike | saving for a new bike | buy a mountain bike myself => Find the price of the bike you want, decide how much you can save each week from allowance or jobs, and track it on a chart. Check used bikes too, which can save a lot.
how do i save up for a gaming pc | saving for a pc | pc savings plan => Price out the parts or a prebuilt PC, set a weekly savings amount, and consider starting with a cheaper build you can upgrade later. Selling old games or gear can help.
how do i save up for a phone | saving for a phone | buy my own phone => Talk with your parents about the phone and the monthly plan cost, then set a savings goal and timeline. Refurbished or older models cost much less.
how long will it take to save up | savings time calculator | how many weeks to save => Divide the price by how much you save each week. A $300 item at $15 a week takes 20 weeks. Saving more or finding extra jobs shortens it.
is it worth buying used | buying used stuff | secondhand pros => Often yes. Used bikes, games, books and sports gear can cost much less and still work well. Check the condition, ask questions and compare to the new price.
how do i buy something used safely | buying used online | meet up to buy => With a parent, meet in a public place like a police station lobby, inspect and test the item before paying, and never send money before seeing it.
how do i price used items | pricing used stuff | how much to sell for => Search for the same item on resale sites and look at what similar ones actually sold for, then price yours a bit lower if it's worn or missing parts.
what is a fair price | is this a good deal | price check => A fair price is close to what others pay for the same item in similar condition. Compare a few stores or listings before buying.
how do i tell if a deal is real | fake discount | sale tricks => Check the price history on a price tracker or compare stores. Some "sales" raise the original price first so the discount looks bigger.
what are marketing tricks | stores trick you | impulse buying tricks => Stores use tricks like prices ending in .99, "limited time" countdowns, buy one get one deals and putting candy by the checkout to get you to buy more.
how do i make a wish list | wish list | wants list => Write down things you want with their prices and where to buy them. Waiting a while before buying often shows which ones you still really want.
how do i know if a charity is real | charity scam | check a charity => Look it up on charity rating sites, check that it's a registered nonprofit, and be wary of pressure or requests for gift cards. Give directly through the charity's own website.
how do streamers make money | twitch money | streamer income => Streamers earn from subscriptions, viewer donations and tips, ads, sponsorships and selling merch. Most need a big audience before it adds up to much.
how do i charge for a service | charging for help | pricing a service => Find out what others nearby charge, think about your skill and time, and agree on the price before you start. Be clear about what's included.
how much should i charge for babysitting | babysitting rates | babysitter pay => Rates depend a lot on where you live, your age and experience, and how many kids there are. Ask other babysitters or families nearby what's normal, and charge a bit more for extra kids.
how much should i charge for tutoring | tutoring rates | teen tutor => Teen tutors usually charge less than adult tutors, based on local rates and the subject. Ask what others charge, and start lower until you have happy families recommending you.
how do i keep track of money i earn | tracking earnings | money log => Write down each job, date, and amount in a notebook, spreadsheet or app. It shows how fast you're reaching your goals.
what is a trademark | trademark meaning | trademark vs copyright => A trademark protects names, logos and slogans that identify a brand, like a company's logo. It stops others from using them in confusing ways.
what is an llc | llc meaning | start a company => An LLC, or limited liability company, is a type of US business that protects the owner's personal money if the business gets into debt or legal trouble.
why are sneakers so expensive | sneaker resale | limited sneakers => Brand names, athlete deals, advertising and limited releases push prices up. Rare sneakers can resell for much more than their original price because of high demand.
what is reselling | resellers | flipping items => Reselling means buying items, like sneakers, cards or used goods, and selling them for a higher price. It can make money, but prices can drop and you can lose money too.
what is a collectible worth | collectible value | rare items value => A collectible's value depends on rarity, condition and demand. Check recent sold prices on resale sites, not asking prices, to see what people actually pay.
are trading cards a good investment | pokemon card value | card investing => Cards can gain value, but prices go up and down with trends, and most cards aren't worth much. Collect what you enjoy, and don't spend money you can't afford to lose.
how do i grade a trading card | card grading | psa grading => Grading companies examine a card's corners, edges, surface and centering and give it a score, usually out of 10, then seal it in a case. It costs a fee, so it's only worth it for valuable cards.
what is a recurring charge | recurring payment | monthly charge => A recurring charge is a payment taken automatically on a schedule, like a monthly app subscription. Keep a list of yours so you can cancel ones you don't use.
how much does it cost to own a car | car costs | gas insurance car => Owning a car costs much more than its price. Gas, insurance, repairs, registration and loss of value often add up to several thousand dollars a year or more in the US.
how much does a family spend on groceries | grocery budget | food costs => It varies a lot, but as of 2025 a US family of four often spends somewhere around $1,000 to $1,500 a month on groceries, depending on what they buy.
how much does rent cost | rent cost | apartment rent => Rent depends heavily on location. As of 2025, typical US apartment rents were around $1,500 to $2,000 a month, and much higher in big cities.
what is a flea market | flea market | swap meet => A flea market is a big market where many sellers set up tables to sell used items, antiques, crafts and cheap goods. Haggling is common.
what is a rewards program | loyalty program | points card => A rewards or loyalty program gives you points or perks for shopping at a store. They can save money, but are designed to keep you coming back and spending.
what is an overpayment scam | overpayment | send back extra money => In an overpayment scam, a buyer pays you too much, then asks you to send back the difference. Their payment later bounces, and you lose the money you sent.
what is a fake check scam | fake check | check scam => A scammer sends a check, asks you to deposit it and send some money back. The check is fake, bounces days later, and you owe the bank the full amount.
