# tag: life.howto.room
how do i make my room look cooler | room makeover ideas | bedroom upgrade on a budget => Rearrange furniture, add LED strip lights, hang posters or your own art, use a shelf to display builds and trophies, add a rug and some plants, and clear clutter. Small changes add up.
how do i put up led strip lights | led strips on wall | led strip tips => Clean the surface with rubbing alcohol, measure first, cut only at the marked cut lines, peel the backing slowly and press firmly. Stick them under shelves or behind a desk for a glow without glare.
how do i hang posters without damaging walls | poster putty | removable hooks => Use removable poster strips, poster putty or washi tape instead of thumbtacks or regular tape. Follow the package directions when removing, like pulling the tab straight down slowly.
how do i set up a gaming or desk setup | desk setup ideas | good desk setup => Put the monitor or iPad at eye level an arm's length away, keep your elbows at about 90 degrees, use cable clips to tidy wires, add a lamp, and keep your favorite tools within reach.
how do i manage cables on my desk | cable management | hide cords => Use cable clips, a cable tray under the desk, velcro ties and a power strip mounted under the desk. Label chargers so you can find the right one.
how do i make a reading nook | cozy corner ideas | reading corner => Pick a quiet corner, add a beanbag or cushions, a small lamp, a blanket and a shelf or basket for books.
how do i arrange furniture in my room | bedroom layout | rearranging my room => Measure the room and furniture first, keep paths clear, put the bed where it doesn't block the door, and place your desk near natural light. Sketch the plan before moving heavy stuff with help.
how do i display my lego or models | display shelves | showing off builds => Use floating shelves or a bookcase with LED lighting, keep builds out of direct sunlight to prevent fading, and dust them with a soft makeup brush.
how do i display my medals and trophies | medal hanger | trophy shelf => A medal hanger, a shadow box or a dedicated shelf works well. Group them by sport or year.
how do i make my room smell better | room smells bad | freshen my room => Open a window, wash bedding and clothes, take out trash, empty snack wrappers, put sports gear somewhere to air out, and put an open box of baking soda in the room.
how do i keep my room cool in summer | hot bedroom | cooling a room without ac => Close blinds during the day, open windows at night, use a fan, point a box fan out the window to pull hot air out, and turn off electronics you aren't using.
how do i keep my room warm in winter | cold bedroom | warm up my room => Use thicker blankets and flannel sheets, close curtains at night, put a rug down, and wear warm socks. Only use a space heater with adult permission and keep it 3 feet from anything that can burn.
how do i share a room with a sibling | sharing a bedroom | room sharing tips => Divide space fairly, give each person their own storage, agree on quiet times and lights out, and respect each other's stuff. A curtain or bookshelf divider can give privacy.
how do i pack for moving | moving house tips | packing boxes => Pack one room at a time, put heavy things in small boxes and light things in large ones, label each box with the room and contents, and pack a first night bag with essentials.
how do i deal with moving to a new house | moving to a new home | first night in new house => Unpack your room first so it feels like yours, explore the neighborhood, and keep in touch with old friends. It's normal for a new place to feel strange for a while.
how do i paint my bedroom | picking paint colors for my room | bedroom paint ideas => Get permission first. Pick colors by testing samples on the wall and looking at them in day and night light. Lighter colors make rooms look bigger, and an accent wall adds style.
how do i make my bed look nice | bed styling | make bed look like a hotel => Smooth the sheets tight, fold the top sheet over the blanket at the head, fluff and stack pillows, and add a folded throw blanket at the foot.
# tag: life.howto.style
how do i figure out my style | finding my style | what clothes suit me => Notice clothes you feel comfortable and confident in, look at styles you like for ideas, and build around a few favorite colors. Style is about feeling like yourself.
what is a capsule wardrobe | fewer clothes more outfits | mix and match clothes => A capsule wardrobe is a small set of clothes that all go together, so you can make many outfits from fewer pieces. Neutral colors help.
what colors go together in clothes | matching clothes colors | outfit color tips => Neutrals like black, white, gray, navy and khaki go with almost everything. Add one bright color, or use colors next to each other on the color wheel for a calm look.
how should jeans fit | jeans fit guide | types of jeans fits => Jeans should fit your waist without a belt being needed, be comfortable when sitting, and end around your shoe tops. Common fits include slim, straight, relaxed and skinny.
how should a t shirt fit | t shirt fit | shirt too big => A good t shirt's shoulder seams sit at your shoulders, the sleeves hit mid bicep, and it ends around your belt line or a little below.
how do i layer clothes | layering outfits | dressing in layers => Start with a light base like a tee, add a middle layer like a hoodie or flannel, and top with a jacket. You can remove layers as the day warms up.
what should i wear to a job interview | interview outfit | what to wear for an interview => Dress a step nicer than the job's everyday clothes. For most teen jobs, clean pants or khakis and a neat shirt or polo work. Make sure clothes are clean and wrinkle free.
what should i wear to a wedding | wedding outfit for kids | dress code wedding => Follow the invitation's dress code. For most weddings, nice pants and a button up shirt, or a dress, plus clean dress shoes. Avoid white for girls and women unless asked.
what should i wear to a funeral | funeral clothes | dress for a funeral => Wear simple, dark, modest clothes, like black, navy or gray dress pants and a shirt, or a plain dress. Neat and respectful is the goal.
what do dress codes mean | formal semi formal casual | black tie meaning => Casual means everyday clothes. Smart casual is neat but relaxed. Semi formal means dressy, like a suit or nice dress. Formal or black tie means tuxedos and long gowns.
how do i know my shoe size | measure my foot | shoe size chart => Stand on paper, trace your foot, measure from heel to longest toe, and compare to a size chart. Measure both feet in the evening, when feet are biggest, and use the larger one.
what is the difference between kids and adult shoe sizes | youth shoe sizes | big kid sizes => US kids' sizes go up to about 13, then restart at 1 to 7 for big kids (youth). Youth sizes are about the same as adult men's sizes with the same number.
how do i take care of sneakers | clean white sneakers | keep shoes looking new => Brush off dirt, scrub with mild soap and a soft brush, use a magic eraser on white soles, stuff with paper to dry and avoid the dryer. Rotate pairs so they last longer.
how do i keep my hoodie from pilling | fabric pilling | remove pills from sweater => Wash it inside out on gentle with cold water and air dry. Remove pills with a fabric shaver or by gently running a razor over the fabric.
how do i care for a rain jacket | waterproof jacket care | jacket not waterproof anymore => Wash it with a technical gear wash, not fabric softener, then tumble dry low or iron on low to reactivate the water repellent coating. Spray on treatments refresh it.
how do i choose a good backpack | backpack for school | backpack features => Look for two padded shoulder straps, a padded back, a laptop or iPad sleeve, a chest strap if it's heavy, and a size that doesn't hang below your waist.
how do i choose sunglasses | uv sunglasses | polarized lenses => Choose ones labeled 100 percent UV protection or UV400. Polarized lenses cut glare from water and roads, which helps on bikes and at the beach.
how do i care for glasses | glasses care | keep glasses from breaking => Clean them with lens cleaner and a microfiber cloth, use both hands to put them on and off, store them in a case, and never leave them on a car dashboard or seat.
what are contact lenses | can kids wear contacts | contact lens care => Contacts are thin lenses worn on the eye. Many kids can wear them if responsible. Always wash hands before touching them, never sleep or swim in them unless told, and follow the replacement schedule.
how do i style my hair | hair styling for boys | how to use hair product => Start with towel dried hair, use a small amount of product, about a dime sized amount, rub it in your hands, and work it through from back to front. Match the product to the look, like clay for matte or gel for shine.
what is a fade haircut | low mid high fade | taper vs fade => A fade is a haircut that gradually goes from very short or shaved at the bottom to longer on top. A low fade starts near the ears, a high fade near the top of the head. A taper is a softer version.
how often should i get a haircut | haircut frequency | how long between haircuts => Short styles often need a trim every 3 to 6 weeks to stay sharp. Longer styles can go 8 to 12 weeks.
how do i get rid of helmet hair | helmet hair fix | hair after bike ride => Ride with dry hair, wear a thin cycling cap or buff under the helmet, and carry a small comb or a little product to fix it after.
