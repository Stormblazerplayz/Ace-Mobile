# tag: life.money.investing
what does investing mean | how does investing work | why do people invest => Investing means putting money into something, like stocks or funds, hoping it grows over time. It can earn much more than a savings account over many years, but its value can also go down.
what is an index fund | index fund for beginners | what is an s&p 500 fund => An index fund buys a little of every company in a market index, like the S&P 500's 500 large US companies. It spreads out risk and has low fees, so many experts recommend it for long term investing.
what is the s&p 500 | s&p 500 meaning | what does the s&p measure => The S&P 500 is a list of about 500 of the biggest US companies. Its value is used to show how the overall US stock market is doing.
what is the dow jones | dow jones meaning | what is the nasdaq => The Dow Jones Industrial Average tracks 30 large US companies, and the Nasdaq Composite tracks thousands of stocks listed on the Nasdaq exchange, many of them tech companies.
what is a mutual fund | mutual fund vs etf | what is an etf => Both pool money from many investors to buy a mix of investments. ETFs trade on the stock market during the day like a stock, while mutual funds are bought at one price after the market closes.
what is diversification | why not put all your eggs in one basket | spreading out investments => Diversification means owning lots of different investments so one bad one doesn't sink you. It's the idea behind "don't put all your eggs in one basket".
what is risk in investing | risk vs reward | why do stocks go up and down => Risk is the chance an investment loses value. Investments that can grow more, like stocks, usually swing up and down more. Safer ones, like savings accounts, grow slowly.
what is a bond | stocks vs bonds | how do bonds work => A bond is a loan you give to a company or government. They pay you interest and return the money later. Bonds are usually less risky than stocks but grow less.
what is a dividend | dividend stocks | how do dividends work => A dividend is a share of a company's profits paid to people who own its stock, often every three months.
what is a bull market and bear market | bull vs bear market | what is a market crash => A bull market is when prices rise for a long time, and a bear market is when they fall 20 percent or more from a recent high. A crash is a sudden, big drop.
can kids invest in stocks | how can a kid start investing | custodial account => Kids can't open their own brokerage account, but a parent can open a custodial account in the kid's name and manage it until the kid is an adult. Some apps offer teen accounts with a parent.
what is the rule of 72 | how long to double money | doubling money interest => Divide 72 by the yearly growth rate to estimate how many years it takes money to double. At 8 percent a year, money doubles in about 9 years.
why is starting to save early so powerful | invest young benefits | time in the market => Because of compound growth, money invested early has many more years to grow. Small amounts at 12 can grow bigger than larger amounts started at 30.
what is a retirement account | what is a 401k | what is an ira => Retirement accounts help adults save for when they stop working, with tax benefits. A 401(k) is offered through a job, and an IRA is opened by a person. A Roth IRA can even be opened for a teen with earned income.
what is a stock price | why do stock prices change | how is a stock price set => A stock's price is what buyers and sellers agree on at that moment. It changes with news, company profits and how many people want to buy or sell.
what is a stock ticker | ticker symbol meaning | what does aapl mean => A ticker is a short code for a company's stock, like AAPL for Apple or MSFT for Microsoft.
what is an ipo | going public meaning | what does ipo stand for => An IPO, initial public offering, is when a private company first sells shares to the public on a stock exchange.
is day trading a good idea | can you get rich trading stocks fast | is trading risky => Day trading means buying and selling quickly to profit from small price moves. Studies show most day traders lose money. Long term investing in diversified funds has worked better for most people.
what is a market cap | market capitalization | most valuable company => Market cap is a company's total value on the stock market: the share price times the number of shares. Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia have all been among the most valuable companies in the 2020s.
what is gold as an investment | is gold a good investment | why do people buy gold => Some people buy gold because it tends to hold value over long periods and during crises. It doesn't pay interest or dividends, and its price can swing a lot.
what is real estate investing | investing in property | rental property meaning => Real estate investing means buying property to rent out or sell later for more. It can earn money but takes a lot of cash, work and risk.
what is a recession | why do recessions happen | recession meaning => A recession is a period when the economy shrinks, businesses sell less and more people lose jobs. It can be caused by things like a financial crisis, a pandemic or high interest rates.
what is the economy | how does the economy work | economy for kids => The economy is all the buying, selling, making and working in a place. When people buy more and businesses grow, the economy grows. When they spend less, it slows.
what is gdp | gross domestic product meaning | which country has the biggest economy => GDP is the total value of goods and services a country produces in a year, used to measure the size of its economy. As of 2025, the US has the largest economy, followed by China.
what is the federal reserve | what does the fed do | who sets interest rates => The Federal Reserve is the US central bank. It sets key interest rates, which affect loans and savings, to try to keep prices stable and jobs strong.
what is an exchange rate | how do exchange rates work | converting money in another country => An exchange rate is how much one currency is worth in another, like how many euros you get for a dollar. Rates change every day.
what is a monopoly in real life | monopoly meaning economics | why are monopolies bad => A monopoly is when one company controls almost all of a market. Without competition, it can charge high prices or not improve, so governments have laws against unfair monopolies.
what is competition in business | why competition lowers prices | competitor meaning => Competition is when businesses try to win the same customers. It pushes them to lower prices, improve quality and come up with new ideas.

# tag: life.money.scams
signs of a scam | scam red flags | warning signs someone is scamming me => Watch for deals that seem too good to be true, pressure to act fast, requests for gift cards, crypto or passwords, messages from strangers, and links to sites that don't look right. When in doubt, show a parent.
phishing email examples | fake email from a company | how to spot a phishing message => Phishing is a fake message pretending to be from a real company or person to trick you into giving passwords or money. Check the sender's address, look for spelling mistakes and urgent threats, and go to the site yourself instead of clicking the link.
why do scammers ask for gift cards | gift card scam | someone wants payment in gift cards => Real companies and government agencies never ask you to pay with gift cards. Scammers like them because the money is hard to trace and get back. Anyone asking for gift card codes is a scammer.
what is a fake giveaway scam | free iphone giveaway scam | i won a prize online => Messages saying you won something you never entered are almost always scams. They want your personal info, a fee or a click on a bad link. Real contests don't charge you to claim a prize.
what is a trading scam in games | roblox trading scam | someone wants to trade items => Common game scams include asking you to give items first, fake middleman offers, and links to fake trading sites. Only use the game's official trade system and never share your login.
someone is offering free skins if i log in | free skins scam | is this skin generator real => No. Sites or people offering free skins, robux, V-Bucks or items for your login are trying to steal your account. Only get items through the official game or store.
what is a tech support scam | pop up says my computer has a virus | fake virus warning => A tech support scam shows a scary pop up or calls you saying your device is infected, then asks you to call a number or install something. Close the window, don't call, and tell a parent.
what is an impersonation scam | someone pretending to be my friend online | fake account of my friend => Scammers make fake accounts that copy a friend or famous person and ask for money, codes or personal info. Check with the real person another way, like in person or a known phone number.
what is a too good to be true deal | super cheap stuff online scam | fake online stores => Fake shops sell popular items at very low prices, then send nothing or junk. Check reviews, look up the store's name with the word scam, and only buy from well known stores with a parent.
what is a verification code scam | someone asked for my code | should i share my 2fa code => Never share a verification code you get by text or email. Scammers ask for it so they can take over your account. Real companies never ask you to send them that code.
what is identity theft | how to protect my identity | someone stole my identity => Identity theft is when someone uses your personal info, like your name, birthday or Social Security number, to open accounts or steal money. Keep that info private and shred papers that have it.
what should i do if i got scammed | i got scammed online | someone took my money => Tell a parent right away. Stop contacting the scammer, change your passwords, and report it to the platform. Your parents can contact the bank or card company quickly to try to stop or reverse charges.
what is a pyramid scheme | mlm scam | get rich quick scheme => A pyramid scheme makes money by recruiting people who pay to join, not by selling real things. Almost everyone except the people at the top loses money. Be careful of anyone promising easy money for signing up friends.
what is a crypto scam | crypto doubling scam | someone promises to double my bitcoin => Nobody can guarantee to double your money. Messages promising crypto giveaways, doubling or "guaranteed" returns are scams, even if they use a famous person's name or photo.
what is a money mule | someone wants to send money through my account | free money to pass along => A money mule moves stolen money for criminals, often tricked by offers of easy cash. Never let anyone use your bank account or payment app to receive or send money for them. It's illegal.
should i give my info to win a contest | online quiz asking my birthday | personal info in quizzes => Be careful. Quizzes and contests often collect info like your birthday, pet's name or first school, which are common security question answers. Skip them or ask a parent first.

# tag: life.money.shopping
how do i return something to a store | return policy | can i return without a receipt => Bring the item in its original condition with the receipt or order email, before the return deadline. Without a receipt, some stores give store credit, and some don't take returns.
what is a warranty | how does a warranty work | is an extended warranty worth it => A warranty is a promise to fix or replace a product if it breaks from a defect within a set time. Extended warranties cost extra and often aren't worth it for cheaper items.
what is a refund | refund vs exchange vs store credit | how do refunds work => A refund gives your money back. An exchange swaps the item for another, and store credit gives you money to spend only at that store.
how do i buy something online safely | safe online shopping | is it safe to shop online => Shop only on well known sites, check the web address starts with https, never pay by wire, gift card or crypto, and have a parent handle payments. Read reviews before buying.
what is shipping and handling | why is shipping so expensive | free shipping minimum => Shipping and handling is the cost to pack and send an order. Many stores give free shipping over a certain total, but don't buy extra stuff just to reach it unless you need it.
should i buy used or new | buying used items | is refurbished good => Buying used can save a lot on things like bikes, games, books and electronics. Refurbished items are repaired and tested by the seller and often have a warranty, a good middle ground.
how do i compare products before buying | research before buying | how to choose the best one => Decide what features you really need, read reviews from several places, compare prices across stores and look at warranty and return policies before you buy.
what is buyers remorse | regret buying something | i wasted my money => Buyer's remorse is feeling regret after a purchase. Check if you can return it, and next time wait a day before buying to see if you still want it.
how do i haggle | negotiating prices | can you bargain at a yard sale => At yard sales and markets, it's normal to offer a bit less than the price. Be polite, know what it's worth, offer a fair lower price and be ready to walk away.
what is a receipt for at the store | check my receipt | mistakes on receipts => A receipt shows what you bought, the prices and what you paid. Check it before leaving to catch mistakes, and keep it for returns.

# tag: life.money.earning
what is minimum wage | what is the federal minimum wage | minimum wage meaning => Minimum wage is the lowest hourly pay employers are allowed to give. As of 2025, the US federal minimum is $7.25 an hour, but many states and cities set higher minimums.
how old do you have to be to get a job | working at 14 | first job age in the us => In the US, most non farm jobs require you to be at least 14, with limits on hours until 16. Kids can do jobs like babysitting, yard work and delivering newspapers younger.
what are good first jobs for teens | jobs for 14 year olds | teen job ideas => Common first jobs include working at grocery stores, fast food restaurants, movie theaters, amusement parks, camps, lifeguarding (with certification), tutoring and pet sitting.
how do i write a resume as a teen | first resume tips | what goes on a resume => Include your name and contact info, school, any jobs or volunteer work, skills like coding or languages, and activities like sports or clubs. Keep it to one neat page.
what is volunteering | why volunteer | volunteer ideas for kids => Volunteering is doing helpful work without pay, like at food banks, animal shelters, parks cleanups or helping neighbors. It builds skills, looks great on applications and feels good.
what is a paycheck stub | reading a pay stub | what are deductions => A pay stub shows your hours, pay rate, gross pay, deductions like taxes and your net pay. Check it to make sure you were paid correctly.
how do people get raises | asking for a raise | what is a promotion => People usually get raises by doing great work, learning new skills, taking on more responsibility, or moving to a better job. A promotion moves you to a higher position, usually with more pay.
what is passive income | how to earn money while sleeping | passive income ideas => Passive income is money earned with little ongoing work after setup, like ad money from old videos, selling digital designs or interest from savings. Most of it takes a lot of work up front.
what is a commission | commission based pay | what is a tip vs commission => A commission is pay based on how much you sell, usually a percentage. Tips are extra money given by customers for service.
