# tag: life.howto.thinking
how do i make a hard decision | decision making tips | cant decide => List your options, write pros and cons for each, think about what matters most to you, and imagine how you'd feel with each choice next week. Talking it through with someone helps too.
what is a pros and cons list | pros and cons | how to weigh options => A pros and cons list puts the good points (pros) and bad points (cons) of a choice side by side. Some points matter more than others, so give important ones more weight.
how do i solve a problem step by step | problem solving steps | how to solve problems => Define the problem clearly, brainstorm possible solutions, pick the best one, try it, and check if it worked. If not, learn why and try another.
how do i brainstorm ideas | brainstorming tips | coming up with ideas => Write down every idea without judging, even silly ones, for a set time like 10 minutes. Quantity leads to quality. Then pick the best few and combine them.
how do i be more creative | creativity tips | get better ideas => Try new things, mix ideas from different areas, ask "what if," keep an idea notebook, and give yourself time to be bored. Creativity grows with practice.
what is critical thinking | how to think critically | critical thinking examples => Critical thinking means questioning information, asking for evidence, spotting bias, and considering other explanations before deciding what's true.
how do i tell if something online is true | fact check something | is this real or fake => Check who made it and why, look for the same info on trusted sources, check the date, look for evidence, and be extra careful with things that make you very angry or excited.
what is a logical fallacy | common fallacies | bad arguments => A logical fallacy is a flaw in reasoning. Examples include attacking the person instead of the argument, a slippery slope, and "everyone does it" (bandwagon).
how do i plan a big project | project planning | planning a build => Define the goal, list the steps, estimate time and materials, set mini deadlines, start with the hardest or most uncertain part, and review progress each week.
what is a prototype | why make a prototype | prototype meaning => A prototype is an early, rough version of something, like a cardboard model of a robot, made to test ideas quickly before building the real thing.
what is the engineering design process | design process steps | engineering steps => The engineering design process is: ask about the problem, imagine solutions, plan, create a prototype, test it, and improve it, then repeat.
how do i learn from my mistakes | learning from failure | what went wrong => After a mistake, ask what happened, why, and what you'd do differently. Write it down so you remember. Mistakes are information.
what is a post mortem | project review | what went well what didnt => A post mortem is a review after a project ends, listing what went well, what went wrong and what to change next time. Game studios and engineers use them a lot.
how do i think outside the box | out of the box thinking | lateral thinking => Question the usual rules of a problem, look at it from another person's view, flip it around, or combine unrelated ideas. Some of the best solutions break assumptions.
what is first principles thinking | first principles meaning | thinking from scratch => First principles thinking means breaking a problem down to its basic truths and building up from there, instead of copying how things are usually done.
how do i make a mind map for ideas | mind map brainstorming | idea map => Write the main topic in the center, draw branches for subtopics, and add smaller branches for details. Use colors and pictures to make connections clear.
how do i remember things better | improve memory | memory tips => Sleep well, review things a few times spread out over days, connect new information to what you already know, teach it to someone, and use pictures or stories.
how do i teach someone something | how to explain things | teaching a skill => Find out what they already know, explain one step at a time, show an example, let them try, and be patient. Ask them to explain it back.
how do i ask good questions | asking better questions | curious questions => Ask open questions that start with why, how or what if, be specific about what you don't understand, and listen fully to the answer.
how do i change my mind gracefully | admitting i was wrong | saying i was wrong => Say something like "Good point, I hadn't thought of that. I think you're right." Changing your mind when you learn something new is a sign of a smart thinker.
how do i disagree respectfully | disagreeing politely | arguing nicely => Listen fully, restate their point, use calm words like "I see it differently because...," focus on ideas not the person, and be okay agreeing to disagree.
how do i negotiate | negotiation tips | getting a fair deal => Know what you want and your limit, listen to what the other person needs, look for a win win, and be willing to compromise or walk away.
how do i compromise | what is a compromise | meeting in the middle => A compromise is when each side gives up a little so both get something they want, like watching one person's movie today and the other's tomorrow.
how do i handle a problem i cant solve | stuck on a problem | getting unstuck => Take a break, explain the problem out loud to someone or even a rubber duck, try a smaller version, look for similar solved problems, and ask for help.
how do i set priorities | what should i do first | prioritize tasks => Rank tasks by importance and deadline. Do important and urgent things first, schedule important but not urgent things, and drop or shorten tasks that don't matter much.
what is a habit loop | cue routine reward | how habits work => A habit loop has a cue (trigger), a routine (the action) and a reward. Changing the routine while keeping the cue and reward is a way to swap a bad habit for a good one.
what is a growth plan | personal growth plan | improve myself plan => Pick one area to grow in, set a specific goal, list small daily actions, track progress, and review each week. Small consistent steps add up.
how do i become a leader | leadership skills | leading a group => Listen to your group, set a clear goal, divide tasks fairly, encourage others, take responsibility when things go wrong, and lead by example.
how do i be more independent | becoming independent | doing things on my own => Take on small responsibilities, like making your lunch, managing your schedule or doing laundry. Learn to solve problems before asking for help, and show you're reliable.
how do i be more responsible | showing responsibility | being responsible => Keep your promises, do your chores and homework without reminders, take care of your things, admit mistakes and fix them. Trust grows when you're reliable.
how do i build trust with my parents | earn my parents trust | getting more freedom => Be honest, do what you say you'll do, check in when you're out, and handle small freedoms well. Trust and freedom usually grow together.
how do i be honest when it is hard | telling the truth | lying makes it worse => Tell the truth early, calmly and simply. Lies usually grow and cause more trouble later. People respect honesty, even when the truth isn't fun.
what is integrity | integrity meaning | doing the right thing when nobody is watching => Integrity means doing what's right and honest even when no one is watching, and keeping your values the same in every situation.
what is a role model | good role models | who should i look up to => A role model is someone whose actions inspire you to be better. Good role models show kindness, hard work and honesty, not just fame.
how do i find a mentor | what is a mentor | mentor for kids => A mentor is a more experienced person who gives advice and encouragement. Coaches, teachers, club leaders and relatives can be mentors. Ask someone you admire for advice on a specific skill.
what do i want to be when i grow up | choosing a career | how to find a career => Notice what you love doing and what you're good at, try related activities, and ask adults about their jobs. Many people change careers, so you don't have to decide now.
what jobs combine bikes and engineering | careers in bikes | bike engineer jobs => Careers include bicycle design engineer, bike mechanic, product designer, suspension engineer, frame builder, and pro rider or coach. Mechanical engineering is a common path.
what jobs use animation and coding | careers in game development | game design jobs => Game studios need programmers, animators, technical artists, designers, sound designers and testers. Technical animators and tools programmers mix art and code.
what do engineers do | types of engineers | engineering careers => Engineers design and build solutions. Mechanical engineers work on machines, electrical on circuits and power, civil on bridges and buildings, software on programs, and aerospace on aircraft and rockets.
