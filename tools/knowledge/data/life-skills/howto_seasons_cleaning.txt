# tag: life.howto.seasons
how do i get ready for winter | winter prep checklist | preparing for cold weather => Check that coats, gloves and boots still fit, put an emergency kit and blanket in the car, test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and store outdoor gear like bikes somewhere dry.
how do i get ready for summer | summer prep | things to do before summer => Check bikes and outdoor gear, stock up on sunscreen, make a list of summer goals and projects, and plan some camps, trips or activities with your family.
what can i do in summer break | summer bucket list ideas | summer activities for kids => Ideas include building something big, learning a new trick or skill, camping, a bike trip, a summer reading challenge, making a short film, volunteering, or starting a small business.
how do i avoid the summer slide | keep learning in summer | summer learning loss => Read regularly, do a little math practice through games or real projects, keep a journal, visit museums and libraries, and pick a fun project to learn about.
what can i do on a snow day | snow day ideas | snow day activities => Build a snow fort or snowman, go sledding safely, have a snowball fight with rules, make snow ice cream with clean fresh snow, do a puzzle, bake cookies, or have a movie marathon.
how do i build a snow fort | snow fort tips | igloo building => Pack snow into blocks using a bin or bucket, stack them in a circle, staggering the joints, and lean them slightly inward. Never tunnel into big snowbanks, since they can collapse.
how do i build a snowman | snowman tips | snow for snowman => Use wet, packing snow. Roll a small ball and push it around to grow it, make three balls in decreasing size, stack them, and pack snow between them to keep them steady.
what is spring cleaning | spring cleaning checklist | how to spring clean my room => Spring cleaning is a deep clean once a year: wash bedding and curtains, dust high and low, clean under furniture, go through clothes and donate what doesn't fit.
how do i get ready for fall | fall activities | things to do in autumn => Rake leaves into piles to jump in, visit a pumpkin patch or apple orchard, go on a hike to see fall colors, carve pumpkins and get your gear ready for cooler weather.
how do i stay busy during a long break | holiday break ideas | what to do over winter break => Mix rest with a project, like a build, a drawing challenge or learning a skill. Plan outdoor time each day, see friends, and keep a loose routine so the return to school is easier.
how do i get back into a school routine after break | back to school after holidays | adjust sleep schedule => Start moving bedtime and wake up time earlier a few days before school, pack your bag the night before, and plan a fun thing for the first week back.
# tag: life.howto.cleaning
how do i clean my bike helmet | wash helmet pads | smelly helmet => Remove the pads and wash them in soapy water, then wipe the shell and straps with mild soap. Air dry, away from heat, which can damage the foam. Never use harsh chemicals.
how do i clean a game controller | sticky controller | sanitize a controller => Turn it off, wipe it with a cloth slightly dampened with rubbing alcohol, clean gaps with a cotton swab or toothpick, and let it dry. Never spray liquid directly on it.
how do i clean my laptop screen | clean a computer monitor | screen cleaning tips => Turn it off, wipe gently with a dry microfiber cloth, and if needed use a cloth lightly dampened with water. Avoid paper towels, glass cleaner and spraying directly on the screen.
how do i clean a 3d printer bed | clean pei sheet | prints not sticking clean bed => Wash a PEI sheet with warm water and dish soap, or wipe with isopropyl alcohol. Don't touch the surface with your fingers, since skin oils stop prints sticking.
how do i clean my retainer | retainer cleaning | smelly retainer => Brush it gently with a soft toothbrush and cool water each day, and soak it in a retainer cleaner as your orthodontist suggests. Hot water can warp it.
how do i clean a reusable straw | straw cleaning brush | clean metal straw => Rinse right after use and scrub inside with a thin straw brush and soapy water. Many are dishwasher safe.
how do i clean a lunch thermos | thermos smells | clean insulated bottle => Fill it with warm water and a spoonful of baking soda or a few drops of vinegar, let it sit an hour, scrub with a bottle brush, rinse and dry with the lid off.
how do i clean the oven | oven cleaning | dirty oven => Many ovens have a self cleaning setting, which gets very hot, so let an adult run it. For manual cleaning, a paste of baking soda and water left overnight, then wiped with vinegar, loosens grime.
how do i clean a blender | blender cleaning trick | clean blender quickly => Fill it halfway with warm water and a drop of dish soap, blend for 30 seconds, rinse, and let it dry. Be careful with the sharp blades.
how do i clean a cutting board after raw meat | sanitize cutting board | cutting board after chicken => Wash it with hot soapy water right away, rinse and dry. For extra sanitizing, use a diluted bleach solution, about 1 tablespoon per gallon of water, then rinse.
how do i clean grout | dirty tile grout | grout cleaning => Scrub with a paste of baking soda and water using an old toothbrush, then rinse. For tougher stains, an adult can use a grout cleaner.
how do i clean a trash can | smelly trash can | wash garbage bin => Rinse it outside with a hose, scrub with soapy water and a brush, rinse, and let it dry in the sun. Sprinkle baking soda in the bottom to fight odors.
how do i clean a ceiling fan | dusty fan blades | pillowcase fan trick => Turn it off, slide an old pillowcase over each blade and pull it back to catch the dust inside. Wipe with a damp cloth after.
how do i clean blinds | dusty blinds | clean window blinds => Close the blinds and wipe with a microfiber cloth or a duster, then flip them and do the other side. A sock on your hand works too.
how do i make a homemade cleaner | diy all purpose cleaner | vinegar cleaner => Mix equal parts white vinegar and water in a spray bottle for glass and many surfaces. Don't use vinegar on stone countertops, and never mix cleaners with bleach.
how often should i clean things in my room | cleaning schedule | how often to dust => A rough plan: tidy daily, wash sheets weekly, dust and vacuum weekly, wipe electronics and doorknobs weekly, and deep clean under furniture every few months.
