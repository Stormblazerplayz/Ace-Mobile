# tag: life.feelings.stress
how do i calm down when i'm stressed | ways to calm down fast | quick stress relief => Try slow breathing: breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4, and repeat a few times. Then take a short walk, drink water or stretch. If stress keeps building up, talk to a trusted adult.
what is box breathing | breathing exercise for anxiety | 4 4 4 4 breathing => Box breathing is breathing in for 4 seconds, holding 4, breathing out 4 and holding 4, like tracing the sides of a box. It slows your heart rate and helps your body calm down.
what is the 5 4 3 2 1 grounding trick | grounding technique | how to stop panicking => Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste. Focusing on your senses pulls your mind back to the present when you feel overwhelmed.
why do i feel stressed all the time | always stressed | too much stress kids => Lots of homework, activities, friendship problems, family changes or not enough sleep can keep stress high. Try to sleep enough, move your body and make time for fun, and talk to a parent or school counselor about what's weighing on you.
what are signs of stress | how do i know if i'm stressed | stress symptoms in kids => Stress can show up as headaches, stomach aches, trouble sleeping, feeling grumpy or teary, not wanting to do things you usually like, or finding it hard to focus. Noticing it is the first step to handling it.
how do i handle too much pressure | pressure from parents | everyone expects too much from me => Tell someone you trust how you feel, honestly and calmly. Remind yourself that doing your best is enough, break big things into small steps, and ask for help deciding what can wait or be dropped.
how do i stop worrying so much | i worry about everything | constant worrying => Try setting a short worry time each day, writing worries down, then asking what you can actually do about each one. Worries you can't control are okay to let go. If worry takes over your days, tell a trusted adult.
how do i stop overthinking | overthinking everything | my brain won't stop thinking => Notice when you're going in circles and gently switch to something active, like a walk, music, drawing or talking with someone. Writing thoughts down can get them out of your head.
what is anxiety | is it normal to feel anxious | anxious vs nervous => Anxiety is your body's alarm system, a feeling of worry or fear, often with a fast heartbeat or butterflies. Everyone feels it sometimes. If it's very strong, happens a lot or stops you from doing things, a doctor or counselor can help.
what helps with butterflies in my stomach | nervous stomach | tummy ache when nervous => Butterflies are a normal body reaction to nerves. Slow belly breathing, a little water, and reminding yourself "I'm excited" can help. They usually fade once you get started.
how do i relax | ways to relax | relaxing things to do => Try reading, listening to music, drawing, going outside, taking a warm shower, stretching or playing with a pet. Doing something calm with your hands helps your mind slow down.
what is mindfulness | how to be mindful | mindfulness for kids => Mindfulness means paying attention to the present moment without judging it, like noticing your breathing, sounds or how your feet feel on the floor. Even a minute or two a day can help you feel calmer.
how do i meditate | meditation for beginners | how long should i meditate => Sit comfortably, close your eyes or look down, and focus on your breathing. When your mind wanders, which always happens, gently bring it back. Start with 2 to 5 minutes.
does exercise help with stress | why does exercise make you feel better | exercise and mood => Yes. Moving your body releases chemicals like endorphins that lift your mood and helps burn off stress. Even a 10 minute walk or bike ride can help.
does music help with stress | music for calming down | songs to relax => Music can change your mood. Calm songs can slow your breathing and heart rate, and upbeat songs can boost energy. Making a playlist for different moods is a fun tool.
how do i handle a really bad day | today was terrible | worst day ever => Bad days happen to everyone. Be gentle with yourself, do something comforting, like a favorite show, snack or hobby, talk to someone and get some sleep. Tomorrow is a fresh start.
how do i deal with change | moving to a new house | big changes in my life => Change can feel scary even when it's good. Keep some routines the same, find out what you can about what's coming, and talk about how you feel. It usually gets easier after a few weeks.
how do i cope with my parents divorcing | my parents are getting divorced | parents splitting up => It's really hard, and all your feelings are okay. It's not your fault, and both parents still love you. Talk to them or a school counselor about your worries, and keep doing things that help you feel normal.
how do i deal with a sick family member | my grandparent is sick | someone i love is in the hospital => It's normal to feel scared or sad. Ask adults to explain what's happening in a way you understand, find small ways to help, like making a card, and keep talking about your feelings with someone you trust.
how do i deal with someone dying | grief help for kids | my grandpa died => Losing someone hurts a lot, and there's no right way to grieve. You might feel sad, angry, numb or even laugh sometimes, and that's all okay. Share memories, talk with family, and ask a school counselor for support.
how do i deal with my pet dying | my dog died | sad about my pet => Losing a pet is a real loss, and it's okay to cry and miss them. Make a memory book or draw them, share stories, and talk with family. The sadness gets softer over time.

# tag: life.feelings.nerves
how do i stop being nervous before a game | pregame nerves | sports anxiety => Nerves mean you care. Stick to a warm up routine, take slow breaths, focus on your first job, like your first pass, and remember a time you played well. Nerves usually disappear once the game starts.
how do i calm nerves before a performance | stage fright | nervous before a recital => Practice a lot, breathe slowly before you go on, and focus on sharing something you love rather than being perfect. Picture it going well. Most audiences want you to do great.
how do i stop being nervous on the first day of school | first day of school nerves | new school anxiety => Lots of kids are nervous too. Plan your outfit and bag the night before, visit or look at a map of the school, and have a simple line ready to say to someone new. It gets easier fast.
how do i deal with nerves before a big test | panicking during a test | blank mind during exam => Prepare well, sleep, eat breakfast and arrive early. If you blank, take three slow breaths, skip to an easier question and come back. Your memory often returns once you calm down.
how do i get over a fear | how to face my fears | overcoming fear => Face it in small steps, starting with something just a little scary and working up as it gets easier. Breathe slowly, remind yourself you're safe, and celebrate each step. A trusted adult can help with big fears.
why do i get scared at night | afraid of the dark | can't sleep because i'm scared => Lots of people feel more nervous at night because it's quiet and dark. A night light, calm music, reading something happy before bed and reminding yourself you're safe can help.
how do i stop being afraid of going to the doctor | scared of shots | needle fear => Ask what will happen so there are no surprises, take slow breaths, look away and squeeze something during a shot. It's over in seconds, and telling the nurse you're nervous helps them help you.
how do i get over fear of failure | scared to fail | afraid of messing up => Everyone fails sometimes, including the best athletes and inventors. Failing means you tried something hard. Focus on what you'll learn, and remember most mistakes can be fixed.
how do i be braver | how to have courage | i want to be more confident => Courage isn't feeling no fear, it's doing something even though you're scared. Start with small brave acts, like raising your hand, and each one makes the next easier.
how do i build confidence | low self confidence | how to feel more confident => Do things you're good at, learn new skills step by step, keep promises to yourself, and notice your progress. Stand tall and speak clearly even when you don't feel it yet, confidence often follows action.
what do i do if i feel embarrassed | something embarrassing happened | i embarrassed myself => Most people forget embarrassing moments much faster than you think, because they're focused on themselves. Laugh it off if you can, and remember everyone has these moments.
how do i stop caring what people think | worried what others think of me | people pleaser => It's normal to care, but you can't make everyone happy. Focus on the opinions of people who know and care about you, and on whether you're proud of your own choices.

# tag: life.feelings.anger
how do i control my anger | anger management for kids | how to calm down when angry => When you feel anger rising, stop and take a few slow breaths, count to ten, or walk away for a bit. Once you're calmer, say how you feel and what you need. Anger is okay, hurting people or things isn't.
why do i get angry so easily | always mad | short temper => Being tired, hungry, stressed or feeling unheard makes anger come faster. Notice your early warning signs, like a hot face or tight fists, and take a break before it builds. If anger feels out of control, talk to a trusted adult.
what can i do with angry energy | safe ways to let out anger | release anger without hurting anyone => Go for a run or bike ride, squeeze a stress ball, scribble hard on paper, punch a pillow, or write what you're mad about and then rip it up.
how do i say i'm angry without yelling | i statements | how to tell someone they made me mad => Use an I statement: "I feel angry when you take my stuff without asking, and I need you to ask first." It explains your feelings without blaming, so people listen better.
what do i do if i said something mean when angry | i yelled at my mom | regret what i said => Give it a little time, then apologize sincerely for what you said, without excuses. Explain how you felt, and talk about how to handle it better next time.
how do i deal with someone who is angry at me | someone yelled at me | someone is mad at me => Stay calm, don't yell back, and listen to understand. If they're too heated, say you'll talk later and step away. If you made a mistake, own it and apologize.
how do i handle a sibling who makes me mad | my sister annoys me | brother keeps bugging me => Ask them calmly to stop, then move away if they don't. Find a space of your own for a while, and ask a parent for help if it keeps happening. Getting even usually makes it worse.
how do i deal with being frustrated | i'm so frustrated | frustrated when things don't work => Take a short break, then come back and try a different way or a smaller step. When stuck on code or a build, explaining the problem out loud often helps you spot the fix.
how do i not rage at video games | gaming rage | i get angry when i lose games => Take a break after a loss or two, remember it's supposed to be fun, and notice your body getting tense. Play with friends who are positive, and don't play when you're already tired or upset.
is it okay to feel angry | is anger bad | should i hide my anger => Anger is a normal feeling, and it can tell you something isn't fair. What matters is what you do with it. Hiding it all the time isn't healthy either, so find safe ways to express it.

# tag: life.feelings.boredom
what do i do when i'm bored at home | bored at home ideas | nothing to do at home => Build something with LEGO or cardboard, draw or animate a short scene, learn a card trick, bake something, ride your bike, start a story, or clean up your room and find things you forgot you had.
what do i do when i'm bored outside | bored outside ideas | things to do outdoors => Go on a bike ride, build a fort, do a scavenger hunt, try to identify birds or bugs, practice tricks, play catch, or fly a kite.
what can i do when i'm bored without screens | screen free activities | no screens what to do => Read, draw, build, play a board game, write a comic, do a puzzle, learn origami, bake, play music or go outside. Boredom is often when creative ideas show up.
what do i do when i'm bored in class | bored at school | class is boring => Take notes in a fun way, like with drawings or colors, ask a question about the topic, or challenge yourself to find one interesting thing in the lesson. Moving your feet quietly can help you stay alert.
what can i do when i'm bored with friends | things to do with friends | fun activities with friends => Make a video or short film, build something together, have a cooking challenge, play board games, go to a park or skatepark, or do a mini Olympics in the backyard.
is being bored good for you | why boredom is good | benefits of boredom => Some boredom can be good. It gives your brain space to daydream and come up with creative ideas, and it pushes you to find new things to do.
what should i do on a rainy day | rainy day activities | stuck inside rainy day => Build a blanket fort, have a movie or game marathon, bake cookies, do a puzzle, start an art project, make a stop motion video, or have an indoor scavenger hunt.
what can i do on a long car ride | road trip games | bored in the car => Play I Spy, 20 questions or the license plate game, listen to audiobooks or podcasts, draw, or download shows and games before you leave.
what are some new hobbies to try | hobby ideas for kids | how to find a hobby => Try drawing, animation, coding, robotics, photography, cooking, an instrument, skateboarding, chess, gardening or making videos. Pick something that sounds fun and try it for a few weeks.
