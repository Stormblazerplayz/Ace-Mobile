# tag: life.cooking.storage
how should i store bread | does bread go in the fridge | keeping bread fresh => Keep bread at room temperature in a bag or bread box for a few days, or freeze it for longer. The fridge actually makes bread go stale faster, though it slows mold.
how do i freeze bread | can you freeze bread | toasting frozen bread => Slice it first, put it in a freezer bag with the air squeezed out, and freeze for up to about 3 months. Toast slices straight from frozen.
should tomatoes go in the fridge | how to store tomatoes | keep tomatoes fresh => Keep whole, unripe tomatoes on the counter so they keep their flavor. Once ripe or cut, put them in the fridge and use them within a few days.
how do i store potatoes | should potatoes go in the fridge | why are my potatoes sprouting => Store potatoes in a cool, dark, dry place with airflow, like a pantry, not the fridge. Keep them away from onions, which make them sprout faster.
how do i store onions | onions in the fridge or pantry | keeping onions fresh => Keep whole onions in a cool, dry, dark place with airflow, not in plastic. Cut onions go in a sealed container in the fridge for about a week.
how do i store berries | keeping strawberries fresh | why do my berries get moldy fast => Store berries unwashed in the fridge, in a container lined with a paper towel, and wash them just before eating. Throw out moldy ones right away so it doesn't spread.
how do i store lettuce | keep salad greens fresh | lettuce wilting fix => Wash and dry it well, then store in a container or bag with a paper towel to soak up moisture. Limp lettuce can perk up in a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes.
how do i store fresh herbs | keeping herbs fresh | herbs in water like flowers => Stand soft herbs like parsley and cilantro in a jar of water in the fridge with a bag loosely over the top. Keep basil on the counter, since cold turns it black. Wrap hardy herbs in a damp paper towel.
how do i store bananas | keep bananas from ripening | separate bananas => Keep them on the counter away from other fruit. Wrapping the stems slows ripening a little, and once ripe, the fridge keeps the inside firm though the peel darkens.
which fruits should not be stored together | ethylene gas fruit | why do apples ripen other fruit => Apples, bananas, avocados, tomatoes and pears give off ethylene gas, which speeds up ripening of nearby produce. Keep them apart from greens and berries unless you want to ripen something faster.
how do i ripen fruit faster | paper bag ripening | make avocado ripen => Put the fruit in a paper bag with a banana or apple and fold it closed. The trapped ethylene gas speeds ripening. Check it daily.
how long do eggs last in the fridge | egg storage | should eggs be refrigerated => In the US, eggs must be refrigerated and usually last 3 to 5 weeks past purchase. Keep them in their carton on a shelf, not the door, where temperature changes more.
how long does milk last after opening | milk going bad | how to tell if milk is spoiled => Opened milk usually lasts about a week in the fridge. Sour smell, lumps or a sour taste mean it's gone bad. Keep it on a shelf, not in the door.
how should i store cheese | keeping cheese from drying out | cheese in plastic wrap => Wrap hard cheese in wax paper or parchment, then loosely in a bag, so it can breathe but not dry out. Keep it in the cheese drawer of the fridge.
how long does cooked chicken last | leftover chicken fridge | is 4 day old chicken okay => Cooked chicken lasts about 3 to 4 days in the fridge in a sealed container, or 2 to 6 months in the freezer.
how long does raw chicken last in the fridge | raw meat fridge time | how long can ground beef stay in the fridge => Raw chicken and ground meat should be cooked or frozen within 1 to 2 days. Raw steaks and chops last about 3 to 5 days.
how do i freeze food properly | freezing leftovers tips | how to prevent freezer burn => Cool food first, use airtight containers or freezer bags with the air pressed out, label them with the name and date, and freeze flat to save space and thaw faster.
what is freezer burn | is freezer burned food safe | why does frozen food get ice crystals => Freezer burn happens when air dries out frozen food, leaving dry, grayish spots. It's safe to eat but tastes worse. Cut off bad spots and wrap food tightly next time.
how long does frozen food last | can frozen food go bad | how long is food good in the freezer => Food kept frozen at -18 °C (0 °F) stays safe indefinitely, but quality drops over time. Most cooked leftovers are best within 2 to 3 months, and raw meats from about 4 to 12 months.
what foods can't be frozen | what doesn't freeze well | can you freeze lettuce => Lettuce, cucumbers, raw potatoes, mayo based salads, cream sauces and fried foods don't freeze well because they get mushy, watery or separate.
how do i store cookies | keep cookies soft | why are my cookies hard the next day => Store cookies in an airtight container at room temperature. A slice of bread in the container keeps soft cookies soft. Keep crispy and soft cookies apart.
how do i store a cake | does cake need to go in the fridge | keeping cake fresh => Most frosted cakes keep 2 to 3 days at room temperature under a cover. Cakes with cream cheese frosting, whipped cream or fresh fruit go in the fridge.
how do i store leftover pizza | pizza in the fridge | how long does pizza last => Put leftover pizza in the fridge within 2 hours, in a container or wrapped. It lasts about 3 to 4 days.
how do i store opened canned food | can i keep food in the open can | leftover canned beans => Move leftovers into a glass or plastic container with a lid, then refrigerate and use within a few days. Once opened, cans can give food a metallic taste.
how do i store flour and sugar | pantry bugs in flour | keep flour fresh => Store them in airtight containers in a cool, dry pantry. Whole wheat flour lasts longer in the fridge or freezer because its oils can go rancid.
how long do spices last | do spices expire | old spices still good => Ground spices are best within about 1 to 3 years, and whole spices last longer. They don't usually go bad, they just lose flavor. Smell them: no smell means little taste.
do condiments need to go in the fridge | does ketchup need refrigerating | mustard after opening => Ketchup, mustard and mayo should go in the fridge after opening to stay good longest, and mayo always must. Soy sauce, honey and vinegar are fine in the pantry.
does peanut butter need to be refrigerated | storing natural peanut butter | peanut butter oil on top => Regular peanut butter is fine in the pantry for a few months. Natural peanut butter separates and keeps longer in the fridge, so stir in the oil and then chill it.
how do i keep chips from going stale | stale chips fix | crispy chips after opening => Roll the bag tightly and clip it, or move chips to an airtight container. Stale chips can be crisped in the oven at about 175 °C (350 °F) for a few minutes.
how do i label leftovers | why date food in the fridge | first in first out fridge => Write the food name and date on tape on the container. Put newer food behind older food so you use the older stuff first.
how do i organize a fridge | where should food go in the fridge | fridge shelf order => Keep ready to eat food on top shelves, raw meat on the bottom shelf in a container so it can't drip on anything, and fruit and veggies in the drawers. The door is the warmest spot, so keep condiments there.
how do i store avocados after cutting | half an avocado browning | save half an avocado => Leave the pit in, brush the cut side with lemon or lime juice, wrap tightly and refrigerate. A thin brown layer on top can just be scraped off.
how do i store cut fruit | prepping fruit ahead | how long does cut fruit last => Keep cut fruit in a sealed container in the fridge, usually good for 2 to 4 days. Apples and pears brown, so toss them in a little lemon juice.
how do i store rice and pasta | uncooked rice shelf life | how long does dry pasta last => Store them in airtight containers in a cool, dry pantry. Dry pasta and white rice last for years, while brown rice lasts about 6 months because of its oils.
how do i store ice cream | ice cream freezer burn | keep ice cream from getting icy => Keep it in the back of the freezer where it's coldest, not the door. Pressing plastic wrap on the surface before closing the lid helps stop ice crystals.
how long can you keep a lunch in the fridge | packing lunch the night before | meal prep how long => Most meal prepped lunches stay good about 3 to 4 days in the fridge. Pack lunches the night before and keep them chilled until you leave.

# tag: life.cooking.measuring
how do i measure flour correctly | spoon and level flour | why weigh flour => Spoon flour into the measuring cup, then level it off with a knife. Scooping straight from the bag packs it down and can add too much flour. A kitchen scale is the most accurate.
how do i measure brown sugar | packed brown sugar meaning | firmly packed sugar => Press brown sugar firmly into the measuring cup until it's level. It should hold the cup's shape when you tip it out.
how do i measure butter | tablespoons in a stick of butter | butter wrapper lines => In the US, the wrapper is marked in tablespoons. One stick is 1/2 cup, which is 8 tablespoons or about 113 grams.
how do i measure liquids | liquid measuring cup vs dry | read a measuring cup at eye level => Use a clear liquid measuring cup on a flat counter and bend down so your eye is level with the line. Dry measuring cups are for filling to the top and leveling off.
how do i measure sticky stuff like honey | measuring honey without sticking | peanut butter measuring trick => Spray or lightly oil the measuring cup or spoon first, and the honey, syrup or peanut butter will slide right out.
what is the difference between tsp and tbsp | teaspoon or tablespoon abbreviation | big t small t recipe => Tsp or a small t means teaspoon, and tbsp or a big T means tablespoon. One tablespoon equals three teaspoons, so mixing them up can ruin a recipe.
what does a heaping cup mean | level vs heaping | rounded teaspoon => Level means scraped flat across the top. Heaping or rounded means piled up above the rim. If a recipe doesn't say, use level.
why are baking measurements so important | can i guess measurements | why baking is a science => Baking depends on chemistry, so the right amounts of flour, leavening, fat and liquid matter for rising and texture. Cooking on the stove is more forgiving, and you can adjust as you go.
how do i measure without measuring cups | no measuring spoons | eyeballing measurements => A regular spoon from the drawer holds roughly a teaspoon, and a big soup spoon roughly a tablespoon, though they vary. A standard mug often holds more than 1 cup, so it's best for rough cooking, not baking.
