# tag: music.practice
how do i practice an instrument effectively | good practice routine => Warm up, then work on the hardest small section slowly, using a metronome, and gradually speed up. Short daily practice beats one long weekly session. End by playing something you enjoy.
how do i stay motivated to practice music | dont want to practice instrument | make practicing fun => Set small goals, learn songs you love, record yourself to hear progress, play with friends, and track practice days. It's normal for motivation to go up and down.
how do i stop making mistakes when playing | keep messing up a part | practice slowly to play fast => Slow down until you can play the hard part perfectly several times, then speed up a little. Practicing mistakes teaches them, so slow and correct wins.
what is sight reading | how to get better at sight reading | sight reading practice => Sight reading is playing music you've never seen before. Practice a little every day with easy pieces, look ahead, keep steady time, and don't stop for mistakes.
how do i memorize music | memorize a piano piece | playing without sheet music => Learn the piece in small sections, understand its chord patterns and structure, practice from different starting points, and play it away from the instrument in your head.
how do i get over stage fright for a recital | nervous for a music performance | recital tips => Practice performing for family or friends, play through mistakes without stopping, take slow breaths before you start, and remember the audience wants you to do well.
how do i play in a band | starting a band | tips for a kid band => Find friends who play different instruments, pick a few easy songs, practice regularly, listen to each other, agree on who leads, and keep volume reasonable for your neighbors.
how do i write a song | songwriting tips | how to write song lyrics => Start with a hook, a short catchy line or melody. Pick a simple chord progression, write a verse and chorus, and make the chorus the most memorable part. Record ideas on your phone before you forget.
how do i write lyrics that rhyme | song rhymes | rhyme schemes in songs => Common rhyme schemes are AABB (pairs of lines rhyme) and ABAB (every other line rhymes). Near rhymes, like "time" and "mine," sound natural. Focus on meaning first, then rhyme.
what is a pre chorus | pre chorus meaning | build up before chorus => A pre chorus is a short section between the verse and chorus that builds tension and leads into the chorus.
how do i make a beat in garageband | garageband for beginners | making music on ipad => In GarageBand, start a project, choose Drummer or Live Loops for a beat, add a bass and chords with Smart Instruments, record a melody, and arrange sections in the tracks view.
what is a loop in music production | using loops => A loop is a short repeating piece of music, like a drum pattern or chord riff. Producers layer and arrange loops to build songs.
what is mixing in music | mixing vs mastering | what does a mix engineer do => Mixing balances the volume, tone and position of each track so they sound good together. Mastering is the final polish on the whole song so it sounds good on all speakers.
what is eq in music | equalizer | how to use eq => EQ (equalization) turns up or down specific frequencies, like bass, mids or treble. Cutting muddy low frequencies from non bass instruments makes a mix clearer.
what is reverb | reverb vs delay | echo effect => Reverb simulates the sound bouncing in a room or hall, adding space. Delay repeats the sound after a short time, like an echo.
what is compression in audio | audio compressor | why compress vocals => A compressor reduces the difference between loud and quiet parts, making sound more even and punchy. It's used on vocals, drums and whole mixes.
what is autotune | how does autotune work | pitch correction => Auto Tune is software that corrects the pitch of singing to the nearest note. Used subtly, it fixes small errors, and pushed hard, it makes the robotic sound in many pop and hip hop songs.
what is a stem in music | song stems | stems vs tracks => Stems are groups of tracks mixed together, like all drums or all vocals, exported separately. They're used for remixing, mastering and live shows.
what is a remix | how to make a remix | remix vs cover => A remix reworks an existing recording, often changing the beat, tempo or arrangement. A cover is a new performance of someone else's song.
can i use copyrighted music in my videos | music copyright | royalty free music => Using copyrighted songs usually needs permission, and platforms may block or demonetize videos. Use royalty free music libraries like YouTube's Audio Library, or make your own.
how do i record vocals at home | home recording tips | recording singing at home => Record in a small room with soft things like pillows and blankets to cut echo, stand about a hand's width or two from the mic, use a pop filter, and record several takes.
what is a pop filter | why use a pop filter | plosives => A pop filter is a mesh screen in front of a mic that softens bursts of air from P and B sounds, called plosives, so they don't thump in recordings.
how do i make a song sound professional | better mixes | music production tips => Start with good recordings, keep levels balanced, cut unnecessary low end, use reference tracks, take breaks to rest your ears, and check on headphones, speakers and a phone.
what is lofi production | how to make lofi beats | lofi beat tips => Lofi beats use relaxed tempos around 70 to 90 BPM, jazzy chords, swung drums, vinyl crackle, and slightly muffled sound with soft filters.
what is a key and bpm for a beat | choosing tempo for a song | song tempo => Pick a tempo that fits the mood, like 70 to 90 BPM for chill songs, 120 to 130 for dance, and 140 to 170 for drum and bass or trap (often felt at half time).
# tag: music.reading
what are the dynamics markings in order | pp p mp mf f ff | music volume symbols => From softest to loudest: pianissimo (pp), piano (p), mezzo piano (mp), mezzo forte (mf), forte (f) and fortissimo (ff).
what are tempo markings | largo adagio andante | music speed words => From slowest to fastest, common ones are largo (very slow), adagio (slow), andante (walking pace), moderato (moderate), allegro (fast) and presto (very fast).
what is an accent mark in music | accent symbol | marcato => An accent (>) above a note means play it with extra emphasis. A marcato (^) is an even stronger accent.
what is a key signature | how to read a key signature | sharps at the start of music => A key signature is the group of sharps or flats at the start of each staff, telling you which notes are always sharp or flat in that piece. No sharps or flats means C major or A minor.
what is a coda in music | coda symbol | to coda => A coda is an ending section. When you reach "To Coda," jump to the coda symbol, which looks like a circle with a cross through it.
what is a natural sign | natural in music | cancel a sharp => A natural sign cancels a sharp or flat, returning the note to its normal pitch for the rest of that measure.
what is a grace note | grace notes | tiny notes in music => A grace note is a small, quick ornamental note played just before the main note.
what is a trill | trill in music | tr symbol => A trill, marked "tr," is rapidly alternating between a note and the one just above it.
what is a chord chart | lead sheet | chord symbols above lyrics => A chord chart or lead sheet shows lyrics or melody with chord names above, like C, G and Am, so players can strum or comp along.
what does am mean in chords | chord names explained | what does maj7 mean => A capital letter alone means a major chord, like C. "m" means minor, like Am. "7" means dominant seventh, and "maj7" means major seventh. "sus4" replaces the third with the fourth.
# tag: music.voices
how do i sing in tune | singing off key | pitch matching => Practice matching single notes from a piano or app, record yourself to check, and work slowly with simple songs. Most people can improve their pitch with practice.
what is head voice and chest voice | chest voice vs head voice | falsetto => Chest voice is your lower, fuller speaking range. Head voice is higher and lighter. Falsetto is a breathy, airy higher sound. Training helps blend them smoothly.
what is breath support in singing | breathing for singing | diaphragm breathing => Breath support means breathing low into your belly and ribs, then letting air out steadily as you sing. It gives a stronger, steadier sound.
how do i take care of my voice | vocal health | sore throat from singing => Drink water, warm up first, don't scream or strain, rest your voice when it's tired, and avoid singing through pain or hoarseness.
what is karaoke | where did karaoke come from | karaoke meaning => Karaoke is singing along to recorded music with the words on a screen. It started in Japan in the early 1970s, and the word means "empty orchestra."
# tag: music.misc
what is a musical | broadway musicals | what is musical theater => A musical is a play that tells a story through songs, spoken lines and dance. Broadway in New York and London's West End are the most famous theater districts.
what is hamilton the musical | hamilton musical | lin manuel miranda => Hamilton is a hip hop influenced Broadway musical by Lin Manuel Miranda about US founding father Alexander Hamilton. It opened on Broadway in 2015 and won 11 Tony Awards.
what is the longest running broadway show | longest broadway musical | phantom of the opera broadway => The Phantom of the Opera is the longest running show in Broadway history, playing from 1988 to 2023 with almost 14,000 performances.
how is an orchestra arranged | orchestra seating | where do instruments sit in an orchestra => Strings usually sit in front around the conductor, woodwinds behind them, brass behind those, and percussion at the back.
what is a marching band | marching band instruments | drumline => A marching band plays while marching in formations, often at football games and parades. It uses brass, woodwinds and percussion. The drumline is its percussion section.
what is a concert band vs orchestra | band vs orchestra difference | wind ensemble => An orchestra includes strings like violins and cellos. A concert band is mainly woodwinds, brass and percussion, without most string instruments.
what is video game music | famous video game composers | game soundtrack => Video game music changes with gameplay and often loops. Famous composers include Koji Kondo (Super Mario, Zelda), Nobuo Uematsu (Final Fantasy), Toby Fox (Undertale) and C418 (Minecraft).
what is a film score | how movie music works | film composers => A film score is original music written to match a movie's scenes and emotions. Composers like John Williams, Hans Zimmer and Ludwig Goransson write scores that use recurring themes for characters.
what is a jingle | catchy jingle | ad music => A jingle is a short catchy song used in ads to make a brand memorable.
what are the best selling music artists | best selling artist of all time | who sold the most records => The Beatles are generally named the best selling music act of all time. Elvis Presley and Michael Jackson are usually listed among the top solo artists.
what is the most streamed song | most streamed song on spotify | most played song => As of 2025, The Weeknd's "Blinding Lights" was the most streamed song on Spotify, with over 4 billion streams.
how does a vinyl record work | how do records play music | turntable needle => A vinyl record has a long spiral groove with tiny wiggles that match the sound wave. A needle rides in the groove, vibrates, and those vibrations are turned into electrical signals and amplified.
what is an mp3 | mp3 vs wav | audio file formats => MP3 is a compressed audio format that makes files small by removing sounds people can barely hear. WAV and FLAC keep full quality but are larger.
what is perfect pitch vs relative pitch | relative pitch | can you learn pitch => Perfect pitch is naming a note without any reference, and it's rare. Relative pitch is recognizing notes by their distance from a known note, and anyone can train it.
what is music therapy | music therapy benefits | how music helps people => Music therapy uses music, guided by a trained therapist, to help people with stress, pain, communication, movement and emotions.
