how often should i reapply sunscreen | reapply sunscreen | sunscreen water resistant => Reapply sunscreen at least every 2 hours, and right after swimming, sweating or toweling off, even if it says water resistant. Use about a shot glass full for your whole body.
is it safe to look at a solar eclipse | eclipse glasses | eclipse eye damage => Only with certified eclipse glasses or a pinhole projector. Regular sunglasses aren't safe, and looking at the partly covered sun can permanently damage your eyes. Only during totality is it safe to look briefly without glasses.
how do i stay safe in strong wind | high wind safety | windstorm => Stay indoors away from windows, keep clear of trees and power lines outside, and bring in or tie down loose things like trampolines and patio furniture.
how do i stay safe in fog | fog safety | walking in fog => Wear bright or reflective clothes, use lights on your bike, stay on sidewalks and listen carefully for cars, since drivers can't see far in fog.
how do i stay safe on icy sidewalks | walking on ice | penguin walk => Walk like a penguin: short, flat footed steps with your weight over your front foot and hands out of your pockets for balance. Wear boots with good grip.
how do i stay safe at a water park | water park safety | waterslide rules => Follow height rules and lifeguards, go down slides feet first in the right position, wait until the bottom is clear, and wear a life jacket in wave pools if you're not a strong swimmer.
how do i stay safe at a fair | carnival safety | fair rides => Pick a meeting spot, follow ride height rules, keep hands and feet inside rides, stay with your group and drink water on hot days.
how do i stay safe at a parade | parade safety | parade crowds => Stay behind the curb or barriers, never run into the street for candy, and keep track of your group in the crowd.
how do i stay safe at a sports event | stadium safety | big event safety => Know where your seats and exits are, set a meeting spot, stay with your group, and watch for foul balls or pucks flying into the stands.
how do i stay safe on a hike with my dog | hiking with dog | dog hiking safety => Bring water for both of you, keep your dog leashed, check paws and fur for ticks and burrs, and avoid hot pavement and the hottest hours.
how do i stay safe while fishing | fishing safety | hooks safety => Watch your backcast, handle hooks carefully, wear a life jacket on boats and near deep water, and cut the line instead of yanking a snag toward you.
how do i stay safe while camping in a tent | tent safety | heater in tent => Never use a stove, grill or fuel heater inside a tent, since carbon monoxide can kill. Keep the fire far from the tent, and store food away from it.
how do i stay safe snorkeling | snorkeling safety | snorkel tips => Snorkel with a buddy and an adult, wear a life jacket or snorkel vest if you're not a strong swimmer, stay in calm, marked areas, and don't touch coral or sea creatures.
how do i stay safe on a jet ski | jet ski safety | pwc age => Many places have minimum ages for driving one. Always wear a life jacket and attach the safety lanyard, which stops the engine if you fall off. Ride with a trained adult.
how do i stay safe tubing on a boat | tubing safety | towed tube => Wear a life jacket, use hand signals with the boat's spotter, hold on with both hands, and let go and stay still if you fall so the boat can circle back.
how do i stay safe in a sauna | sauna safety | sauna kids => Saunas get very hot, so kids should only use them briefly with an adult, if at all. Drink water and get out right away if you feel dizzy or sick.
how do i stay safe on a farm | farm safety | farm animals safety => Stay away from machinery, never climb on equipment or into grain bins, wash your hands after touching animals, and follow the farmer's rules.
how do i stay safe around horses | horse safety | behind a horse => Approach from the side where they can see you, speak calmly, never stand directly behind a horse, and wear a helmet when riding.
how do i stay safe around cows | cow safety | cattle field => Walk calmly around the edges of fields, keep dogs on a leash, and never get between a cow and her calf. Leave the field if cattle approach.
how do i stay safe at a petting zoo | petting zoo | wash hands animals => Follow the rules, don't eat or drink around animals, keep fingers flat when feeding, and wash your hands with soap and water afterward.
how do i stay safe around bees | bee safety | swarm of bees => Stay calm, don't swat, and move away slowly from hives or swarms. Avoid wearing strong perfumes outdoors, and tell an adult right away if you're allergic.
what do i do if bees chase me | bees chasing | run from bees => Run away in a straight line to a building or car and cover your face. Don't jump into water, since bees wait for you to come up.
what is deet | deet safe kids | insect repellent types => DEET is a common ingredient in bug repellent that keeps mosquitoes and ticks away. Kids should use lower concentrations and have an adult apply it, avoiding hands and face.
how do i stay safe from scorpions | scorpion sting | scorpion safety => In scorpion areas, shake out shoes and clothes before putting them on, wear gloves when moving rocks or wood, and use a UV flashlight at night, since scorpions glow.
how do i stay safe from alligators | alligator safety | gator => Stay far from the water's edge in alligator areas, never feed them, keep pets away, and only swim in designated areas during daylight.
how do i stay safe from sharks | shark safety | shark attack odds => Shark bites are very rare. Swim in groups at guarded beaches, avoid swimming at dawn, dusk or near fishing areas, and get out if a shark is seen.
how do i stay safe from coyotes | coyote safety | coyote encounter => Never feed coyotes, keep small pets inside at night, and if one approaches, make yourself big and loud, wave your arms and back away slowly.
how do i stay safe from moose | moose safety | moose encounter => Moose can be dangerous if they feel threatened. Give them lots of space, and if one charges, run and get behind a tree, rock or car.
how do i stay safe from wild boars | wild pig | boar safety => Stay away, especially from mothers with young. If one charges, climb onto something high or get behind a solid object.
how do i stay safe from raccoons | raccoon safety | raccoon rabies => Never touch or feed raccoons, secure trash cans, and tell an adult about one acting strangely in daytime, since it could be sick.
how do i stay safe from geese | aggressive geese | goose attack => Geese can get aggressive, especially near nests. Face them, back away slowly and don't run or turn your back.
what plants are poisonous to touch | poison oak | poison sumac => Poison ivy, poison oak and poison sumac cause itchy rashes. Giant hogweed sap can cause bad burns in sunlight. Learn what they look like where you live.
what plants are poisonous to eat | poisonous plants | toxic berries => Many plants are poisonous, like foxglove, oleander, lily of the valley, nightshade berries and many wild mushrooms. Never eat wild plants unless an expert adult says they're safe.
how do i stay safe biking on trails | trail biking safety | mtb safety => Wear a helmet and gloves, ride within your skills, check your bike first, carry water and a repair kit, and ride with a buddy or tell someone your route.
how do i stay safe biking in the rain | rain biking | wet brakes => Slow down, brake earlier and gently since wet brakes grab less, avoid painted lines and metal grates, and use lights to be seen.
how do i stay safe crossing at a roundabout | roundabout crossing | pedestrian roundabout => Cross only at marked crosswalks, which are set back from the circle, and cross one direction of traffic at a time, using the island in the middle.
how do i stay safe near big trucks | truck blind spots | no zone => Trucks have big blind spots called no zones. If you can't see the driver in their mirror, they can't see you, so stay well clear on a bike.

how do i stay safe at a train station | train platform safety | yellow line => Stay behind the yellow line, never run for a train, hold the handrail on stairs and stay with your adult.
how do i stay safe on a subway | subway safety | subway doors => Stand behind the platform line, let people off first, hold on while the train moves and know your stop. Plan with your adult what to do if you get separated.
how do i stay safe in a city | city safety | big city tips => Stay aware and keep your phone out of sight while walking, know your route, stay with your group, and ask a store worker or police officer for help if needed.
how do i stay safe at the library | library safety | alone at library => Libraries are generally safe, but follow your family's rules about being there alone, know where staff are, and don't leave with anyone you don't know.

how do i stay safe at the gym | gym safety | weights kids => Follow the rules for your age, learn proper form from a trainer, start light, put equipment back, and wipe down machines.
