# tag: life.cooking.snacks
how do i make trail mix | trail mix ideas | homemade trail mix => Mix nuts or seeds, dried fruit, a crunchy cereal or pretzels, and a few chocolate chips. Use seeds instead of nuts if anyone has a nut allergy. Great for bike rides and hikes.
how do i make energy bites | no bake energy balls | protein balls recipe => Mix rolled oats, peanut butter or sunflower butter, honey, and add ins like chocolate chips or flaxseed. Chill for 30 minutes, roll into balls and keep them in the fridge.
how do i make ants on a log | ants on a log snack | celery peanut butter raisins => Spread peanut butter or cream cheese in celery sticks and line raisins on top like ants. Try other "bugs," like chocolate chips or dried cranberries.
how do i make nachos | easy nachos | oven nachos => Spread tortilla chips on a sheet pan, top with shredded cheese and beans or cooked meat, bake at about 200 C (400 F) for 5 to 8 minutes until melted, then add salsa, guacamole and sour cream.
how do i make a yogurt parfait | parfait layers | yogurt parfait ideas => Layer yogurt, fruit and granola in a glass or jar. Add granola right before eating so it stays crunchy.
how do i make a smoothie bowl | thick smoothie bowl | acai bowl at home => Blend frozen fruit with just a little liquid so it's thick enough to eat with a spoon, pour into a bowl and top with sliced fruit, granola, seeds or coconut.
how do i make banana ice cream | nice cream | one ingredient ice cream => Freeze ripe sliced bananas, then blend them in a food processor until smooth and creamy, scraping down the sides. Add cocoa or peanut butter for flavor.
how do i make frozen grapes | frozen grapes snack | frozen fruit snacks => Wash and dry grapes, spread them on a tray and freeze for a few hours. They taste like little sorbet bites. Cut them in half for little kids, since whole grapes are a choking risk.
how do i make apple chips | baked apple chips | dried apple slices => Slice apples very thin, sprinkle with cinnamon, and bake on parchment at about 100 C (200 F) for 1 to 2 hours, flipping halfway, until dry and crisp.
how do i make popcorn toppings | popcorn seasoning ideas | flavored popcorn => Toss warm popcorn with melted butter and try parmesan, cinnamon sugar, ranch powder, chili lime, or nutritional yeast for a cheesy flavor without cheese.
how do i make homemade popsicles | diy popsicles | fruit popsicles => Blend fruit with juice or yogurt, pour into molds or small cups, add sticks, and freeze at least 4 to 6 hours. Run the mold under warm water to release them.
how do i make ice cream in a bag | ice cream in a bag | homemade ice cream science => Put 1 cup half and half, 1 tablespoon sugar and half a teaspoon vanilla in a small sealed bag. Put it in a big bag with ice and 1/3 cup rock salt and shake for 5 to 10 minutes. Salt makes the ice colder.
how do i make chocolate covered strawberries | dipping strawberries in chocolate | chocolate strawberries => Wash and dry strawberries completely, melt chocolate gently, dip each berry, let extra drip off, and set them on parchment. Chill for 15 to 30 minutes to harden.
how do i make pudding from scratch | homemade chocolate pudding | stovetop pudding => Whisk sugar, cocoa, cornstarch and a pinch of salt in a pot, whisk in milk, then cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick. Stir in vanilla and chill.
how do i make jello | gelatin dessert | how long does jello take to set => Dissolve the jello powder in boiling water, stir in cold water, then refrigerate about 4 hours until firm. Fresh pineapple and kiwi stop it from setting because of their enzymes.
how do i make s'mores | smores | how to roast a marshmallow => Toast a marshmallow over hot coals, not big flames, turning slowly until golden. Sandwich it with a piece of chocolate between two graham crackers. Watch out, since melted marshmallow is very hot.
how do i make s'mores without a fire | indoor smores | oven smores => Put graham crackers topped with chocolate and marshmallows on a sheet pan and broil for 1 to 2 minutes, watching closely, or microwave a marshmallow for about 10 seconds.
how do i make a pb and j | peanut butter and jelly sandwich | pbj tips => Spread peanut butter on both slices of bread so the jelly doesn't soak in, put jelly in the middle, and press together. Sunflower butter works for nut free lunches.
how do i make a wrap | lunch wrap | how to roll a wrap => Warm the tortilla so it's flexible, put fillings in a line just below the center, fold in the sides, then roll it up tightly from the bottom. Cut it in half on a slant.
how do i make a fruit salad | fruit salad tips | keep fruit salad fresh => Cut a mix of fruits into bite sized pieces, and toss with a little lemon or orange juice to keep apples and bananas from browning. Add bananas last.
how do i make deviled eggs | deviled eggs recipe | stuffed eggs => Hard boil and peel eggs, cut in half, mash the yolks with mayo, a little mustard, salt and pepper, then spoon the mixture back in and sprinkle with paprika.
how do i make garlic bread | homemade garlic bread | garlic butter bread => Mix softened butter with minced garlic, parsley and a pinch of salt, spread it on sliced bread, and bake at about 200 C (400 F) for 8 to 10 minutes until golden.
how do i make cinnamon toast | cinnamon sugar toast | cinnamon sugar ratio => Toast bread, spread with butter, and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar, about 1 part cinnamon to 4 parts sugar.
how do i make avocado toast | avocado toast ideas | avocado toast toppings => Toast bread, mash ripe avocado with salt, a squeeze of lemon and pepper, spread it on, and top with an egg, chili flakes or tomatoes.
# tag: life.cooking.outdoor
how do i make foil packet dinners | foil packet meals | hobo dinner => Put sliced potatoes, veggies, and meat or sausage on heavy foil with butter and seasoning, fold it into a sealed packet, and cook on coals or a grill for about 20 to 30 minutes, flipping once.
how do i make campfire breakfast | camping breakfast ideas | breakfast while camping => Easy options are pancakes on a griddle, eggs and bacon in a cast iron pan, oatmeal packets with hot water, or breakfast burritos wrapped in foil and warmed on the grate.
how do i keep a cooler cold longer | cooler tips camping | ice for cooler => Pre chill the cooler, use block ice which melts slower than cubes, freeze water bottles as ice packs, keep it in the shade, and open it as little as possible. Pack drinks in a separate cooler.
how do i use a camp stove | camping stove | backpacking stove safety => Set it on flat ground outside, never in a tent, attach the fuel canister carefully, light it with an adult, and keep the pot stable. Let it cool before packing.
what food should i bring camping | camping food list | easy camp meals => Bring foods that are easy to cook and keep, like pasta, tortillas, peanut butter, oatmeal, trail mix, hot dogs, foil packet ingredients and s'mores supplies.
how do i store food from bears | bear proof food storage | camping food bears => In bear country, store all food, trash and anything smelly, like toothpaste, in bear proof lockers, canisters or hung bags well away from your tent. Never keep food in your tent.
how do i light a charcoal grill | charcoal chimney | lighting charcoal => Adults usually use a chimney starter: fill it with charcoal, light crumpled newspaper underneath, wait about 15 to 20 minutes until the top coals are ashy, then dump them in the grill.
what is direct vs indirect heat grilling | two zone grilling | grilling methods => Direct heat cooks food right over the flames, great for burgers and thin foods. Indirect heat cooks beside the heat with the lid closed, like an oven, for thick foods. Two zone setups give you both.
how do i get grill marks | grill marks on steak | crosshatch marks => Preheat the grill very hot, oil the grates, place food at an angle and don't move it for a few minutes, then rotate it 90 degrees for crosshatch marks before flipping.
how do i grill hot dogs | grilling hot dogs | hot dogs on grill => Grill over medium heat, turning often, for about 5 to 7 minutes until heated through with light char marks. Toast the buns for the last minute.
how do i grill corn | grilled corn on the cob | corn in the husk => Grill it in the husk after soaking for 15 minutes, for about 15 to 20 minutes, or husk it, brush with butter, and grill about 10 minutes, turning until charred in spots.
how do i clean a grill | cleaning grill grates | grill brush safety => Heat the grill, then scrub grates with a grill brush or crumpled foil held in tongs. Check for loose wire bristles, which can stick in food and be swallowed.
how do i make a picnic | picnic food ideas | packing a picnic => Pack easy foods like sandwiches, wraps, cut fruit, veggies and dip, chips and cookies. Bring a blanket, napkins, a trash bag, and an ice pack for anything that needs to stay cold.
how do i pack food for a bike ride | food for a long bike ride | bike ride snacks => For rides over an hour, bring easy carbs like bananas, granola bars, fig bars or trail mix, and plenty of water. Eat a little every 30 to 45 minutes before you feel hungry.
what is bonking in cycling | hitting the wall | ran out of energy on a ride => Bonking is suddenly running out of energy on a long ride or run because your body is low on stored carbs. Eat and drink regularly during long efforts to avoid it.
