what is ravioli | ravioli pasta | stuffed pasta => Ravioli are Italian pasta pillows stuffed with fillings like cheese, meat or vegetables, usually served with sauce.
what is carbonara | carbonara sauce | real carbonara => Carbonara is a Roman pasta dish made with eggs, hard cheese like pecorino, cured pork such as guanciale, and black pepper. The traditional version has no cream.
what is bruschetta | bruschetta | italian toast => Bruschetta is Italian grilled bread rubbed with garlic and drizzled with olive oil, often topped with chopped tomatoes and basil.
what is ciabatta | ciabatta bread | ciabatta holes => Ciabatta is an Italian bread with a crisp crust and a chewy inside full of big holes, made from a very wet dough. Its name means slipper.
what is shepherd's pie | shepherd's pie vs cottage pie | potato topped pie => Shepherd's pie is a British dish of minced lamb and vegetables in gravy, topped with mashed potato and baked. Made with beef, it's called cottage pie.
what is haggis | haggis | scottish dish => Haggis is Scotland's national dish, made of sheep's heart, liver and lungs minced with oats, onion and spices, traditionally cooked in a sheep's stomach. It's often eaten on Burns Night.
what is a full english breakfast | full english | fry up => A full English breakfast usually includes eggs, bacon, sausages, baked beans, grilled tomatoes, mushrooms and toast, sometimes with black pudding.
what is moussaka | moussaka | greek eggplant dish => Moussaka is a Greek baked dish of layered eggplant, spiced minced meat in tomato sauce, and a creamy béchamel topping.
what is shawarma | shawarma | spit roasted meat => Shawarma is Middle Eastern meat, like chicken, lamb or beef, stacked on a rotating spit, slow roasted and shaved off thin, often wrapped in pita with sauces.
what is tabbouleh | tabbouleh | parsley salad => Tabbouleh is a Levantine salad made mostly of finely chopped parsley with tomatoes, mint, onion and bulgur, dressed with lemon juice and olive oil.
what is couscous dish morocco | tagine | moroccan stew => In Morocco, couscous is often steamed and served with a stew of vegetables and meat. A tagine is a slow cooked stew named after the cone shaped clay pot it's cooked in.
what is samosa | samosa | fried pastry india => A samosa is a fried or baked pastry, usually triangle shaped, filled with spiced potatoes, peas or meat. It's a popular snack in India and many other countries.
what is tikka masala | chicken tikka masala | tikka masala origin => Chicken tikka masala is chunks of grilled, spiced chicken in a creamy, orange tomato sauce. It's hugely popular in the UK, where it's often said to have been created.
what is ramen originally | ramen types | tonkotsu shoyu miso ramen => Ramen came to Japan from China in the late 1800s and early 1900s. Popular broth styles include shoyu (soy sauce), miso, shio (salt) and tonkotsu (pork bone).
what is okonomiyaki | okonomiyaki | japanese pancake => Okonomiyaki is a savory Japanese pancake made of batter and cabbage with fillings like pork or seafood, topped with a sweet sauce, mayo and bonito flakes. The name means grilled as you like it.
what is takoyaki | takoyaki | octopus balls => Takoyaki are round Japanese snacks of batter with a piece of octopus inside, cooked in a special molded pan and topped with sauce and bonito flakes. They come from Osaka.
what is korean fried chicken | korean fried chicken | double fried chicken => Korean fried chicken is usually fried twice, making it extra crispy, then often coated in sweet and spicy sauces like gochujang glaze or soy garlic.
what is a taco | taco types | hard vs soft taco => A taco is a corn or flour tortilla folded around a filling like meat, beans or fish, topped with things like onion, cilantro and salsa. Tacos come from Mexico, where soft corn tortillas are traditional.
what is churro | churros | fried dough cinnamon => Churros are fried dough sticks with ridges, rolled in cinnamon sugar and often dipped in thick hot chocolate. They're popular in Spain and Latin America.
what is feijoada | feijoada | brazilian bean stew => Feijoada is a hearty Brazilian stew of black beans with pork and sausage, often served with rice, collard greens and orange slices. It's considered Brazil's national dish.
what is a butter tart | butter tart | canadian dessert => A butter tart is a Canadian pastry shell filled with a sweet, gooey mix of butter, sugar and eggs, sometimes with raisins or pecans.
what is mac and cheese history | macaroni and cheese | baked mac and cheese => Pasta baked with cheese dates back to medieval Europe. In the US, macaroni and cheese became popular in the 1800s, and boxed versions took off in the 1930s.
what is new york style pizza | ny pizza | foldable slice => New York style pizza has a thin, foldable crust with a crisp bottom, sold in big wide slices. People often fold a slice in half lengthwise to eat it.
what is a corn dog | corn dog | corn dog batter => A corn dog is a hot dog on a stick dipped in cornmeal batter and deep fried. It's a classic fair food in the US.
what is a pavlova | pavlova | meringue dessert => A pavlova is a meringue dessert with a crisp outside and soft, marshmallowy middle, topped with whipped cream and fruit. Australia and New Zealand both claim it.
what is a lamington | lamington | australian cake => A lamington is an Australian sponge cake square dipped in chocolate and rolled in coconut, sometimes with jam in the middle.
what is vegemite | vegemite | vegemite taste => Vegemite is a dark, salty Australian spread made from leftover brewer's yeast. It's spread very thinly on buttered toast.
what is a meat pie australia | aussie meat pie | pie floater => The Australian meat pie is a hand sized pastry pie filled with minced meat and gravy, a classic at sports games, often with tomato sauce on top.
