# tag: art.color
primary colors in paint | red yellow blue primary => In traditional painting, the primary colors are red, yellow and blue, which can't be mixed from other colors. Printers use cyan, magenta and yellow, and screens use red, green and blue light.
what are tertiary colors | how to make tertiary colors | intermediate colors => Tertiary colors are made by mixing a primary color with a neighboring secondary color, like red orange, yellow green and blue violet.
what is rgb vs cmyk | screen colors vs print colors | why do printers use cmyk => RGB mixes red, green and blue light for screens, and adding all three makes white. CMYK mixes cyan, magenta, yellow and black ink for printing, and the more ink, the darker it gets.
what is hue saturation and value | hsv meaning | what is saturation in art => Hue is the color itself, like blue. Saturation is how strong or dull it is. Value is how light or dark it is.
what is a tint shade and tone | tint vs shade | how to make a color lighter => A tint adds white to a color to make it lighter, a shade adds black to make it darker, and a tone adds gray to make it softer.
what colors make pink | how do i make pink paint | mixing pink => Mix red with white. Add more white for a lighter pink, or a tiny bit of blue for a cooler pink.
what colors make gray | how to mix gray | mixing grey paint => Mix black and white, or mix complementary colors like blue and orange, then add white. Mixed grays look richer than black and white alone.
how do i make black paint | can you mix black | mix black from colors => Mixing dark complementary colors, like ultramarine blue with burnt umber, or all three primaries, makes a rich near black that many painters prefer over tube black.
how do i make skin tones | mixing skin color paint | how to paint skin => Start with white, yellow and red mixed to a peachy base, then adjust with a touch of blue or brown for depth. Real skin tones vary a lot, so mix several and compare to your reference.
what colors make teal | how to make turquoise | mixing teal paint => Mix blue with a little green, or blue with a touch of yellow. Add white for turquoise.
what is a color scheme | color harmony | how to pick colors that go together => A color scheme is a planned set of colors. Common ones are complementary (opposites), analogous (neighbors on the wheel), triadic (three evenly spaced) and monochromatic (shades of one color).
what is analogous color | analogous color scheme | colors next to each other => Analogous colors sit next to each other on the color wheel, like blue, blue green and green. They look calm and harmonious together.
what is a monochromatic color scheme | one color art | monochrome painting => A monochromatic scheme uses one hue in different tints, shades and tones. It gives a unified, moody look.
what is a triadic color scheme | triad colors | three color scheme => A triadic scheme uses three colors evenly spaced around the color wheel, like red, yellow and blue, or orange, green and violet. It's bold and balanced.
what do colors mean | color psychology | what feelings do colors give => Colors often carry feelings: red can feel exciting or urgent, blue calm, green natural, yellow cheerful and purple royal or mysterious. Meanings change between cultures.
why do colors look different next to each other | simultaneous contrast | color illusion => Colors change how they look depending on their neighbors. Gray looks warmer next to blue and cooler next to orange. Artists use this to make colors pop.
what is color temperature in lighting | warm light vs cool light | kelvin color temperature => Light color is measured in kelvins. Lower numbers, around 2700 K, look warm and yellow like a lamp, and higher numbers, around 5000 to 6500 K, look cool and blue like daylight.
what is a palette | color palette meaning | how to make a color palette => A palette is the set of colors an artist uses in a piece, and also the board for mixing paint. Picking a small, limited palette often makes art look more unified.

# tag: art.painting
what are the types of paint | acrylic vs watercolor vs oil | which paint is best for beginners => Watercolor is thin and see through, acrylic dries fast and is opaque and water based, oil dries slowly and blends smoothly but needs solvents. Acrylic and watercolor are great for beginners.
how do i paint with acrylics | acrylic painting for beginners | acrylic paint tips => Use a palette and keep a cup of water to clean brushes. Work fast because acrylic dries quickly, paint from dark to light or background to foreground, and wash brushes before the paint dries in them.
how do i paint with watercolors | watercolor tips for beginners | watercolor techniques => Use thick watercolor paper, work from light to dark since you can't easily cover dark areas, and leave the paper white for highlights. Wet on wet makes soft blends, and wet on dry gives sharp edges.
what is wet on wet watercolor | wet on wet vs wet on dry | watercolor blending => Wet on wet means painting onto wet paper so colors spread and blend softly, great for skies. Wet on dry means painting on dry paper for crisp edges and details.
how do i paint a sky | painting a sunset | gradient sky painting => Work while the paint is wet, blending from one color to the next, like dark blue at the top to lighter near the horizon, or yellow to orange to pink for a sunset. Add clouds last.
what brushes do i need for painting | paint brush types | round vs flat brush => A round brush makes lines and details, a flat brush makes broad strokes and sharp edges, and a filbert does both with soft edges. A few sizes of each are enough to start.
how do i clean paint brushes | cleaning acrylic brushes | brush care => Rinse brushes in water right after using acrylics or watercolors, wash with mild soap, reshape the tips and dry them lying flat or tip down. Never leave them standing on their bristles in water.
how do i get acrylic paint out of a brush | dried paint in brush | save a hard brush => Soak it in warm soapy water or rubbing alcohol for a while, then work the paint out gently. Brush cleaner made for acrylic also helps. Prevention is easier: rinse often while painting.
what is a canvas | canvas vs paper | what do you paint on => A canvas is fabric stretched over a frame and primed, usually with gesso, for acrylic and oil painting. Watercolor needs special absorbent paper. Canvas boards are a cheap option.
what is gesso | why prime a canvas | gesso meaning => Gesso is a white primer painted on canvas or boards so paint sticks well and doesn't soak in. Most store canvases come already primed.
what is impasto | thick paint technique | palette knife painting => Impasto is applying paint so thickly that brushstrokes or knife marks stand out. Van Gogh used it a lot. Palette knives spread thick paint in bold strokes.
what is glazing in painting | glazing oil paint | thin layers of paint => Glazing is painting thin, see through layers over dry paint to change its color and add glow. Old masters used it for rich skin and light.
what is gouache | gouache vs watercolor | is gouache like poster paint => Gouache is like an opaque watercolor. It dries matte and flat, can be rewetted and covers lighter colors, making it popular for illustration.
what is a mural | how to paint a mural | murals and street art => A mural is a painting done directly on a wall. Artists usually sketch a small version, scale it up with a grid, and use exterior paints for outdoor walls. Always get permission.
what is spray paint art | graffiti vs street art | is graffiti legal => Spray paint art uses aerosol cans, often on walls. Graffiti on property without permission is illegal, but many cities have legal walls and mural programs. Wear a mask and work outdoors.
what is printmaking | linocut printing | how do prints work => Printmaking makes art that can be printed many times. In linocut, you carve a design into linoleum, roll ink on the raised parts and press paper onto it. The design prints reversed.
what is a collage | how to make a collage | collage art => A collage is art made by gluing pieces of paper, photos, fabric or other things onto a surface. Plan a layout, cut shapes, arrange them before gluing, and layer for depth.
what is clay sculpting | air dry clay vs polymer clay | sculpting for beginners => Air dry clay hardens on its own over a day or two. Polymer clay stays soft until baked in a home oven following the package. Start with simple forms and use toothpicks for details.
what is pottery | how is pottery made | pottery wheel => Pottery is shaping clay into pots and dishes by hand or on a spinning wheel, then firing them in a very hot kiln to harden them. Glaze adds a glassy, colored coating.
what is 3d art | digital sculpting | zbrush and blender sculpting => 3D art creates objects and scenes in 3D software. Digital sculpting, in apps like Blender or ZBrush, shapes models like virtual clay, often used for characters and 3D prints.
what is photography composition | how to take better photos | photo tips for beginners => Use the rule of thirds, find good light, clean up the background, get close, try unusual angles and keep the camera steady. Morning and evening light often look best.
what is the rule of thirds | rule of thirds in art | where to put the subject => The rule of thirds divides an image into a 3 by 3 grid and places important things along the lines or where they cross, which often looks more interesting than dead center.
what is composition in art | good composition tips | how to arrange a picture => Composition is how you arrange things in an artwork. Use a clear focal point, lead the eye with lines, balance big and small shapes, and leave some breathing space.
what is a focal point | focal point in art | how to make something stand out => The focal point is where the viewer's eye goes first. Make it stand out with strong contrast, more detail, brighter color or by placing it where lines lead.
what is contrast in art | high contrast art | value contrast => Contrast is the difference between elements, like light and dark, big and small, or warm and cool colors. High contrast grabs attention, and low contrast feels soft.
what are the elements of art | 7 elements of art | line shape form => The seven elements of art are line, shape, form, space, color, value and texture. They're the building blocks of any artwork.
what are the principles of design | principles of art | balance emphasis movement => The principles of design include balance, contrast, emphasis, movement, pattern, rhythm and unity. They describe how artists arrange the elements.
what is texture in art | implied texture | how to draw texture => Texture is how something feels or looks like it would feel. Real texture can be touched, like thick paint, and implied texture is drawn, like fur or bark made with small marks.
what is shape vs form | 2d shape vs 3d form | form in art => A shape is flat and two dimensional, like a circle. A form is three dimensional or looks it, like a sphere drawn with shading.
what is a self portrait | how to draw a self portrait | self portrait tips => A self portrait is an artwork of yourself. Use a mirror or a photo, map out face proportions lightly, and focus on what makes your expression unique.
