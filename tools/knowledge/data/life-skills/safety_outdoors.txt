# tag: life.safety.outdoors
what does stop mean if you get lost | s.t.o.p. hiking | stop think observe plan => S.T.O.P. stands for Stop, Think, Observe, Plan. It reminds lost hikers to calm down, look around and make a careful plan instead of rushing off.
why should i carry a whistle hiking | whistle for emergencies | three whistle blasts => A whistle carries much farther than your voice and doesn't wear you out. Three blasts is the universal signal for help.
what should i tell someone before i go hiking | trip plan before hiking | leave a trip plan => Tell a trusted adult where you're going, your route, who's with you and when you'll be back, so they know when and where to look if you're late.
how much water should i bring on a hike | water for hiking | how much to drink hiking => A common guide is about half a liter (16 oz) per hour of moderate hiking, more in heat or on steep trails. Bring extra in case you're out longer than planned.
what is bear spray | how does bear spray work | bear spray vs pepper spray => Bear spray is a strong pepper spray made to stop a charging bear, sprayed in a cloud toward its face. Adults carry it in bear country and should learn how to use it.
how do i keep bears away from my campsite | bear safe camping | food storage bears => Store food, trash and anything with a scent, like toothpaste, in a bear canister, bear locker or hung bag far from tents. Never keep food in your tent.
what do i do if i see a snake on the trail | snake on trail | rattlesnake encounter => Stop, stay calm and back away slowly, giving it lots of space. Most snakes only bite if bothered, so never try to touch or move one.
how do i stay safe from lightning outside | lightning while hiking | what to do in a thunderstorm outside => Get inside a building or a hard topped car. If you can't, get off peaks and ridges, away from tall lone trees, water and metal. When thunder roars, go indoors.
what is the 30 30 rule for lightning | how long to wait after thunder | lightning wait time => Wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back outside, because lightning can strike far from a storm.
how do i stay safe near a river | river safety | crossing a creek safely => Never cross fast or deep water, especially above your knees. Currents are stronger than they look. Find a bridge or turn back, and stay with an adult.
how do i stay safe in the desert | desert hiking safety | hiking in hot weather => Hike early or late, bring lots of water and salty snacks, wear a hat and light clothes, and rest in shade. Turn back before you've used half your water.
how do i stay safe hiking in cold weather | winter hiking safety | hiking in snow => Dress in layers that stay warm when wet, cover your head, hands and feet, bring extra food and water, and turn back early since days are short.
what is altitude sickness | high altitude headache | mountain sickness => Altitude sickness can happen high up where air has less oxygen. Signs include headache, nausea and tiredness. Going down lower is the best fix, and climbing slowly helps prevent it.
how do i stay safe on a frozen lake | ice thickness safety | is the ice safe => Only go on ice an adult has checked. A common guide is at least 10 cm (4 in) of clear solid ice for walking. Avoid ice near moving water, and never go alone.
how do i stay safe with fireworks | firework safety | sparkler safety => Only adults should light fireworks, and many places ban them. Stand back, never relight a dud, keep water nearby, and remember sparklers burn hot enough to cause bad burns.
how do i stay safe at a campground | campground safety | camping safety tips => Stay with your group, know your site number, use a headlamp at night, keep away from the fire edge, store food properly and know the way to the bathrooms.
what plants and berries are safe to eat in the wild | wild berries safe | can i eat wild berries => Never eat wild plants, berries or mushrooms unless an expert adult says they're safe. Many look like edible ones but are poisonous.
how do i stay safe around wild animals | wildlife safety | can i feed wild animals => Watch from a distance and never feed, touch or chase wild animals. Feeding them makes them lose fear of people and can hurt them.
how do i stay safe rock climbing | climbing safety | belay safety => Climb with trained adults, check knots and harness buckles before every climb, wear a helmet outdoors, and say clear commands like "on belay" and "climbing."
how do i stay safe walking near traffic | walking near roads safety | walking without a sidewalk => Use sidewalks, or walk facing traffic on the edge if there aren't any. Cross at corners, make eye contact with drivers, and don't use your phone while crossing.
how do i stay safe flying a kite | kite safety | power lines kite => Fly kites in open fields far from power lines, roads and airports, and never fly in storms. If it gets stuck on a line, let it go and tell an adult.
how do i stay safe during a heat wave | heat wave safety | extreme heat tips => Drink water often, stay in shade or air conditioning, limit hard activity in the hottest hours, and check on pets and older neighbors.
