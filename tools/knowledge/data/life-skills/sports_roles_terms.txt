# tag: life.sports.roles
what does a number 10 do | attacking midfielder | playmaker => A number 10, or attacking midfielder, plays behind the strikers and creates chances with clever passes, dribbles and shots. They're often a team's playmaker.
what does a sweeper keeper do | sweeper keeper | goalkeeper with feet => A sweeper keeper is a goalkeeper who comes far off the line to clear through balls and helps build attacks with good passing, almost like an extra defender.
what does a tight end do | tight end | te role => A tight end lines up next to the offensive line and does two jobs: blocking for runners and catching passes. Strong, big players with good hands fit the role.
what does a defensive end do | defensive end | pass rusher => A defensive end lines up on the outside of the defensive line, rushing the quarterback and stopping runs to their side. Sacks are a big part of the job.
what does a long snapper do | long snapper | snapper => A long snapper is a specialist who snaps the ball back to the punter or the holder on field goals and extra points, quickly and accurately.
what does a closer do | closer baseball | save opportunity => A closer is a relief pitcher who usually pitches the final inning of a close game to protect a lead. Finishing the win earns them a save.
what is a utility player | utility player | plays many positions => A utility player can play several positions well, like infield and outfield in baseball. Coaches love them for filling gaps.
what does an enforcer do in hockey | enforcer | hockey fighting => An enforcer was a tough player whose job was to protect teammates and respond to rough play, sometimes by fighting. The role has become much less common.
what does a prop do in rugby | prop rugby | front row => Props are the two big players on either side of the hooker in the front row of the scrum. They push hard in scrums and lift teammates in lineouts.
what does a scrum half do | scrum half | number 9 rugby => The scrum half, number 9, is the link between forwards and backs. They feed the ball into scrums and fire quick passes from rucks.
what does a fly half do | fly half | number 10 rugby => The fly half, number 10, is rugby's main decision maker, choosing whether to pass, kick or run, and often taking goal kicks.
what does a hooker do in rugby | hooker rugby | throw in lineout => The hooker is in the middle of the front row and hooks the ball back with their foot in scrums. They also throw the ball in at lineouts.
what does an attack do in lacrosse | lacrosse attack | attackman => Attackers stay mostly in the offensive half, using stick skills to feed passes and score goals.
what does a midfielder do in lacrosse | lacrosse middie | midfielder lacrosse => Midfielders, or middies, play both offense and defense, running the whole field and often taking face offs.
what does a goalie do in lacrosse | lacrosse goalie | crease => The lacrosse goalie defends a 1.8 m (6 ft) square goal from the crease, using a stick with a big head to block shots, then starts the clear up the field.
what does a wicketkeeper do | wicketkeeper | keeper cricket => The wicketkeeper crouches behind the stumps wearing gloves and pads, catching balls the batter misses, taking catches and stumping batters out of their crease.
what does an all rounder do | all rounder cricket | batting and bowling => An all rounder is a cricket player who is good at both batting and bowling, making them extra valuable to a team.
what does an assistant coach do | assistant coach | coaching staff => Assistant coaches help the head coach by running drills, focusing on certain positions or skills, studying opponents and helping players one on one.
what does a linesman do | assistant referee | linesman flag => A linesman or assistant referee watches the sidelines and signals with a flag when the ball goes out of play or a player is offside.
what is a sixth man | sixth man | bench scorer => A sixth man is basketball's best player coming off the bench, giving the team energy and scoring. The NBA gives a Sixth Man of the Year award.
what is a rookie | rookie meaning | rookie of the year => A rookie is a first year player in a league. Leagues give a Rookie of the Year award to the best one.
what is a veteran in sports | veteran player | vet => A veteran is a player with many seasons of experience. Veterans often mentor younger players.
what is a trade in sports | sports trade | traded player => A trade is when teams swap players, draft picks or both. Players can be traded during the season until a trade deadline.
what is a contract extension | contract extension | rookie contract => A contract extension adds more years, and often more money, to a player's existing contract before it runs out, so the team keeps them longer.
what is a hall of fame | sports hall of fame | inducted => A sports hall of fame honors the greatest players, coaches and contributors in a sport. Getting inducted is a career highlight.
what is an all star game | all star game | all star voting => An All Star game is an exhibition game featuring the best players from a league, often chosen by fan and player votes. It's usually held mid season.
what is a round robin | round robin | group stage => In a round robin, every team plays every other team once, and standings decide who wins or advances. The World Cup group stage works this way.
what is a single elimination tournament | single elimination | knockout tournament => In single elimination, you're out after one loss, and winners move on until one champion remains, like March Madness.
what is a double elimination | double elimination | losers bracket => In double elimination, a team must lose twice to be knocked out. Teams that lose once drop into a losers bracket and can still win it all.
what is a bye in sports | first round bye | bye week => A bye means a team skips a round or week without playing, often given to top seeds in tournaments or to every NFL team once a season.
what is a rivalry | sports rivalry | biggest rivalries => A rivalry is an intense competition between two teams or players, often because of history, location or big past games, like the Yankees and Red Sox.
what is a derby in soccer | local derby | derby match => A derby is a match between two rival teams, often from the same city or region, like Manchester United vs Manchester City.
what is a cinderella team | cinderella story | underdog run => A Cinderella team is an unexpected underdog that goes on a surprising winning run in a tournament, like a low seed in March Madness.
what is an underdog | underdog | upset win => An underdog is the team or player expected to lose. When they win, it's called an upset.
