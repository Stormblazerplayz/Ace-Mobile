# tag: life.money.scenarios
should i spend my birthday money on a game or save it | spend or save birthday money | what to do with gift money => Try splitting it: spend part on something you'll enjoy now and save the rest toward a bigger goal. Waiting a day or two before buying helps you know if you really want it.
is it worth buying a more expensive bike | cheap bike vs good bike | should i buy a better bike => A better bike usually has stronger parts, lighter weight and better brakes and suspension, so it lasts longer and rides better. A good used bike can give more value than a cheap new one.
how do i buy a used bike safely | used bike checklist | buying a second hand bike => Meet in a public place with a parent, check the frame for cracks, test brakes and gears, spin the wheels for wobbles, check the chain, and ask for the serial number to make sure it isn't stolen.
how do i know if a deal is actually good | is this a good price | price check before buying => Compare prices at several stores, check price history tools, read reviews, and think about quality and how long it will last. A sale price isn't a deal if you didn't need it.
what is the 24 hour rule for buying | wait before buying | avoid impulse buys rule => Before buying something you didn't plan, wait 24 hours, or a week for bigger things. If you still want it after waiting, it's more likely a good choice.
what is cost per use | price per use | is it worth it cost per use => Cost per use is the price divided by how many times you'll use it. A 100 dollar helmet used 200 times costs 50 cents a ride, while a 20 dollar toy used twice costs 10 dollars a use.
should i lend money to my sibling | lending money to family | sibling owes me money => Only lend what you'd be okay not getting back. Agree on when they'll pay you back, and maybe write it down. Ask a parent to help if it causes arguments.
what should i do if a friend owes me money | friend wont pay me back | getting money back from a friend => Remind them politely and suggest a plan, like paying a little each week. If they don't, decide whether the friendship matters more than the money, and don't lend again.
how do i split costs for a group gift | group gift money | splitting a present => Agree on the gift and total first, divide it evenly or by what each can give, and have one person collect and track who paid.
how do i save money as a kid with no job | saving without a job | ways to save as a kid => Save part of allowance and gifts, avoid small daily purchases, sell things you don't use with a parent's help, and do extra chores or small jobs for neighbors.
how do i convince my parents to buy something | get parents to buy me something | asking parents for something expensive => Explain why you want it, show you've researched it, offer to pay part with your savings or extra chores, and accept their answer respectfully.
should i buy the newest phone or console | is the newest version worth it | upgrade or wait => New models are usually small upgrades and cost the most at launch. Prices often drop later, and older models still work well. Upgrade when your current one doesn't meet your needs.
should i buy digital or physical games | digital vs physical games | game discs vs downloads => Physical games can be resold, lent or traded, while digital games are convenient and often go on sale. Digital games are tied to your account and can't be resold.
are expensive headphones worth it | cheap vs expensive headphones | headphone value => Mid range headphones often give most of the sound quality of expensive ones. Pay more for features you'll use, like noise cancelling, and protect your hearing with lower volume.
how do i budget for a school trip | saving for a trip | trip spending money => Find the total cost, set a weekly savings amount, bring a set amount of spending money, and keep it in a safe place on the trip.
how much should i spend on a hobby | hobby budget | expensive hobbies => Set a monthly hobby budget that fits your savings, and look for used gear, rentals or borrowing to try a hobby before buying a lot.
how do i rent instead of buy | renting gear | should i rent or buy skis => Renting is smart for things you use only a few times a year or are still growing into, like skis or instruments. Buy when you'll use it often and it fits long term.
what do i do if i get charged twice | double charged | wrong charge on a card => Tell a parent and contact the store or bank with your receipt. Most double charges are fixed quickly, and banks can dispute errors.
what if i get the wrong change | cashier gave me too much change | short changed => Count your change before leaving the counter. If it's wrong either way, politely tell the cashier. Returning extra change is the honest thing to do.
what do i do if i find money on the ground | found money | finders keepers => For a small amount on the street, it's usually fine to keep it. If you find a wallet or a lot of cash, turn it in to the store, school office or police so the owner can get it back.
what do i do if i lose money | lost my money | lost my allowance => Retrace your steps, check lost and found, and learn from it, like using a wallet or zipper pocket. It's frustrating, but it happens to everyone.
should i buy in game currency | buying robux or vbucks | is buying game currency worth it => Decide on a spending limit with your parents, buy only items you'll really use, and remember game currency usually can't be refunded or turned back into money.
how do i avoid overspending on in app purchases | in app purchase limits | stop spending on games => Use a gift card with a set amount instead of a linked card, turn on purchase approvals, and set a monthly limit. Track what you spend.
how do i sell my old games or gear | trade in games | selling used gear => Options include store trade ins (easy but lower value), selling to friends, or online marketplaces with a parent's help (more money, more work). Clean items and include all parts.
how do i make money from my art or videos as a teen | earning from creative work | monetizing hobbies => Options include selling prints or commissions, YouTube once old enough and eligible, and local craft fairs. A parent needs to help with payments and accounts.
should i put my money in a bank or a piggy bank | bank vs piggy bank | where to keep my savings => A bank is safer from loss or theft and can pay interest. A piggy bank is fine for small amounts and helps younger kids see their money grow.
what is a good first big purchase to save for | saving goal ideas | what to save up for => Good goals are things you'll use a lot, like a bike upgrade, a drone, a tablet, a 3D printer or a camera. Pick something specific and track your progress.
how do i talk to my parents about money | asking parents about money | learning about family finances => Ask questions like how they budget, how they save, and what they wish they'd learned at your age. Many parents are happy to teach money skills.
