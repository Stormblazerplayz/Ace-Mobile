# tag: life.music.techniques
what is a hammer on on guitar | guitar hammer on | how to do a hammer on => A hammer on is when you pluck one note, then press a higher fret on the same string hard enough to make a second note sound without picking again.
what is a pull off on guitar | guitar pull off | how to do a pull off => A pull off is the opposite of a hammer on: you fret a note, pick it, then pull that finger off the string with a slight flick so a lower fretted or open note rings.
what is a string bend on guitar | how to bend a guitar string | guitar bending => A bend is pushing a fretted string up or down across the fretboard to raise its pitch, often by a half or whole step. Use two or three fingers together for strength.
what is a slide on guitar | guitar slide technique | sliding between frets => A slide is moving a fretted finger along the string to another fret while keeping pressure, so the pitch glides smoothly between notes.
what is palm muting | how to palm mute | palm mute guitar => Palm muting is resting the side of your picking hand lightly on the strings near the bridge while you pick, giving a short chunky sound used a lot in rock and metal.
what is alternate picking | how to alternate pick | down up picking => Alternate picking means switching between downstrokes and upstrokes with your pick on every note, which is faster and smoother than only picking down.
what is a barre chord | how to play barre chords | barre chord hurts => A barre chord uses one finger pressed flat across several strings at the same fret, letting you move chord shapes anywhere on the neck. They feel hard at first and get easier with practice.
what is drop d tuning | how to tune to drop d | drop d guitar => Drop D lowers only the thickest string from E down to D. It gives a heavier sound and lets you play power chords with one finger.
what is a paradiddle | drum paradiddle | paradiddle sticking => A paradiddle is a drum rudiment played R L R R, L R L L. It trains both hands evenly and is used in grooves and fills.
what is a flam on drums | flam rudiment | how to play a flam => A flam is two strokes played almost at the same time, a quiet grace note just before a louder main note, making a thicker sound.
what is a drum fill | how to play drum fills | fills between beats => A fill is a short burst of drumming that breaks from the main beat, often at the end of a section, to lead into the next part of the song.
what is a basic rock beat | first drum beat to learn | simple drum beat => A basic rock beat plays hi hat on every eighth note, the bass drum on beats 1 and 3, and the snare on beats 2 and 4.
how do i hold drumsticks | drumstick grip | matched grip => Most drummers use matched grip: hold each stick about a third of the way from the end between thumb and first fingers, letting the stick bounce freely.
how do i find notes on a piano | piano keys names | white keys letters => White keys follow A B C D E F G and repeat. C is always just left of a group of two black keys, and F is just left of a group of three.
what is proper hand position on piano | piano hand position | curved fingers piano => Keep fingers curved like holding a ball, wrists level, and play with your fingertips. Sit so your forearms are roughly level with the keys.
what are finger numbers in piano | piano fingering numbers | piano finger 1 => In piano music, your thumb is 1, index 2, middle 3, ring 4 and pinky 5 on both hands.
how do i play with both hands on piano | piano hand independence | piano two hands => Practice each hand alone first, then put them together very slowly, a measure at a time. Speed up only when it's smooth.
what is a piano scale fingering | c major scale fingering | thumb under piano => For C major in the right hand, play 1 2 3, tuck the thumb under to play 1 2 3 4 5. The thumb tuck lets you play smoothly past five notes.
what is a chord inversion | piano inversions | first inversion chord => An inversion is a chord with a note other than the root on the bottom. C major is C E G, its first inversion is E G C, and second inversion is G C E.
what is a falsetto | falsetto voice | head voice vs falsetto => Falsetto is a light, breathy high voice made when the vocal folds vibrate only at their edges. It lets singers, especially men, reach notes above their normal range.
how do i play the ukulele | ukulele beginners | ukulele tuning gcea => Standard ukulele tuning is G C E A. Start with C, A minor, F and G chords, which cover many songs.
how do i hold a violin | violin posture | violin for beginners => The violin rests on your left shoulder with your chin on the chinrest, and your left hand supports the neck. Hold the bow loosely with a bent thumb.
what is rosin for | why put rosin on a bow | rosin violin => Rosin is hardened tree sap rubbed on bow hair to give it grip, so it catches the strings and makes them vibrate.
what is a recital | music recital | piano recital => A recital is a concert where students or musicians perform pieces, often one at a time, for family and friends.
