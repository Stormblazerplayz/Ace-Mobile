how do i solve a rubik's cube | rubiks cube beginner method | how to solve a 3x3 cube => The beginner method goes layer by layer: make a white cross, put in the white corners, solve the middle layer edges, make a yellow cross on top, then position and turn the yellow corners and edges. Each step uses a short move sequence you can learn from a tutorial.
what is the rubik's cube world record | fastest rubiks cube solve | who solved a rubik's cube the fastest => As of 2025, the official 3x3 single solve record is 3.05 seconds, set by Xuanyi Geng in 2025. Records are tracked by the World Cube Association.
what does rubik's cube notation mean | what is r u r prime | cube move letters => Each letter is a face: R right, L left, U up, D down, F front, B back. A letter alone means turn that face clockwise as you look at it, a prime (R') means counterclockwise, and a 2 means turn it twice.
how many combinations does a rubik's cube have | how many ways can a rubiks cube be scrambled | rubiks cube possibilities => A 3x3 cube has about 43 quintillion possible positions, that's 43,252,003,274,489,856,000. Yet any position can be solved in 20 moves or fewer.
how do i juggle | learn to juggle three balls | juggling for beginners => Start with one ball, tossing it in an arc from hand to hand at about eye height. Then use two, throwing the second when the first peaks. For three, keep that rhythm, always throwing a new ball when one peaks. Practice over a bed so drops don't roll away.
how do i do yo yo tricks | yo yo for beginners | how to make a yo yo sleep => Tie the string loop around your middle finger, throw the yo yo down with your palm facing down and a flick of the wrist, and let it spin at the bottom (that's a sleeper). Give a small tug to bring it back. Walk the dog and around the world build on that.
how do i whistle with my fingers | loud whistle with fingers | two finger whistle => Make an OK sign with one hand, or use two fingers from each hand, put the tips under your tongue to push it back, close your lips tightly around your fingers, and blow over the tip of your tongue. It takes lots of practice to find the angle.
how do i snap my fingers | can't snap my fingers | finger snap trick => Press your middle finger against your thumb, push hard, then slide the middle finger down quickly so it hits the base of your thumb. The sound comes from your finger hitting your palm, so keep that hand cupped.
how do i do a cartwheel | cartwheel steps | learning to cartwheel => Stand facing sideways with arms up, step forward with your strong leg, place your hands on the ground one after the other in a line, kick your legs over in a wide V and land one foot then the other. Practice on grass or a mat.
how do i do a handstand | handstand for beginners | how to balance in a handstand => Start against a wall: put your hands shoulder width apart, kick up gently and hold, with your body straight and your eyes looking between your hands. Practice on a soft surface and learn to roll or cartwheel out.
how do i ride a bike without training wheels | teaching a kid to ride a bike | learning to balance on a bike => Lower the seat so feet touch the ground, take the pedals off or ignore them and push along with your feet to learn balance, then add pedaling. Look ahead, not down, and practice on a gentle grassy slope.
how do i swim | learning to swim | how to float in water => Learn from a swim instructor in a pool. Start with floating on your back with ears in the water and belly up, blowing bubbles, and kicking while holding the wall. Never swim without an adult watching.
how do i tread water | treading water technique | staying afloat in deep water => Keep your body upright, sweep your hands back and forth in front of you, and kick with an eggbeater or slow scissor kick. Stay calm and breathe slowly, it takes less energy than you'd think.
how do i do a magic trick | easy magic tricks for kids | simple card trick => Learn one trick well, practice it in a mirror, and never reveal how it works. A classic is a forced card trick, where you secretly know which card they'll pick. The patter, or story you tell, makes it fun.
how do i shuffle cards | riffle shuffle | how to shuffle a deck => For a riffle shuffle, split the deck in half, hold each half with your thumbs at the inner ends, bend them up and let the cards fall together, then push them together. An overhand shuffle is easier to start with.
how do i make slime | slime recipe | how to make slime with glue => A common recipe mixes white school glue with a little baking soda and contact lens solution that contains boric acid, stirring until it pulls away from the bowl, then kneading it. Have an adult help, and wash hands after.
how do i make a paper fortune teller | cootie catcher | how to fold a fortune teller => Fold a square sheet corner to corner both ways, fold all four corners into the center, flip it, fold corners into the center again, then fold in half and push the four flaps up so your fingers fit inside. Write colors, numbers and fortunes on the flaps.
how do i fold a paper boat | origami boat | paper boat steps => Fold a rectangle in half from top to bottom, fold the top corners down to the middle, fold the bottom strips up on each side, then open the middle into a square, fold it into a triangle and pull the sides apart to make the boat.
how do i make an origami crane | paper crane folding | how to fold a crane => Start with a square base, fold the edges into a bird base, then fold the two bottom flaps up as the neck and tail, fold down a head on one, and pull the wings open. It takes practice, so follow a step by step diagram the first time.
how do i draw a cube | how to draw a 3d box | draw a cube in perspective => Draw a square, then draw another square the same size overlapping it, up and to the right. Connect the matching corners with straight lines and erase hidden lines if you want it solid.
how do i learn to code | how do i start programming | best first programming language => Start with something visual like Scratch or a beginner friendly language like Python, and build small projects you care about. Copy examples, change them to see what happens, and keep going even when bugs happen.
how do i get better at video games | how to improve at gaming | get better at fortnite => Practice with a purpose, like aiming drills or building, watch good players and your own replays to spot mistakes, adjust your settings, and take breaks. Rest and sleep improve reaction time too.
how do i learn something new fast | how to learn faster | best way to learn a new skill => Break it into small parts, practice the hardest part on purpose, get quick feedback, and practice a little every day. Teaching someone else what you learned helps you remember it.
how do i stay patient when learning something hard | i'm not good at it yet | how to keep trying => Everyone is bad at things before they're good. Notice small progress, compare yourself to your past self, not others, and take breaks when frustrated. Saying "I can't do it yet" helps.
what is a growth mindset | fixed vs growth mindset | how to have a growth mindset => A growth mindset is believing you can get better at things with effort and good strategies. A fixed mindset thinks ability can't change. Mistakes are information on how to improve.

# tag: life.games.board
how do you play chess | chess rules for beginners | how does chess work => Each player has 16 pieces. White moves first, and players take turns moving one piece. The goal is checkmate, which means the enemy king is attacked and can't escape. Each piece moves its own way.
how does each chess piece move | chess piece moves | how does the knight move => The king moves one square any way. The queen moves any distance in any straight line. Rooks move straight, bishops move diagonally, and the knight jumps in an L shape. Pawns move forward one square, two on their first move, and capture diagonally.
what is castling in chess | how do you castle | rules for castling => Castling moves the king two squares toward a rook, then the rook jumps over to the other side of the king. You can only castle if neither piece has moved, nothing is between them, and the king isn't in check or passing through an attacked square.
what is en passant | en passant chess rule | how does en passant work => If a pawn moves two squares forward and lands right beside an enemy pawn, that enemy pawn can capture it as if it had moved only one square. It must be done right away on the next move.
what is stalemate in chess | what does stalemate mean | is stalemate a win => Stalemate is when the player whose turn it is has no legal moves but their king isn't in check. It's a draw, not a win.
what happens when a pawn reaches the end | pawn promotion | can a pawn become a queen => When a pawn reaches the far row, it is promoted to a queen, rook, bishop or knight. Most people pick a queen because it's the strongest.
how do you set up a chessboard | chess board setup | where does the queen go => Put a white square in the bottom right corner. Rooks go in the corners, then knights, then bishops, and the queen goes on her own color, so the white queen goes on a white square. Pawns fill the second row.
what are good chess opening tips | how do i get better at chess | chess strategy for beginners => Control the center with your pawns and pieces, develop your knights and bishops early, castle to keep your king safe, and don't move the same piece over and over. Always check what your opponent's last move threatens.
how much are chess pieces worth | chess piece values | what is a bishop worth => A common scoring guide is pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5 and queen 9. The king can't be traded, so it has no point value.
what is checkmate | check vs checkmate | how do you win at chess => Check means the king is under attack and must get out of it. Checkmate means it's in check and there's no legal way out, which ends the game.
how do you play checkers | checkers rules | how to win at checkers => Pieces move diagonally forward one square on dark squares. You capture by jumping over an opponent's piece into an empty square, and if you can jump you usually must. Reach the far side to become a king, which can move backward. Capture or block all their pieces to win.
how do you play uno | uno rules | what do the uno cards do => Match the top card by color or number. Skip skips the next player, Reverse changes direction, Draw Two makes the next player draw two and lose their turn, Wild changes the color, and Wild Draw Four changes color and makes the next player draw four. Say UNO when you have one card left.
how much money do you start with in monopoly | monopoly starting money | how much do you get for passing go => In the classic US Monopoly, each player starts with $1,500 and collects $200 every time they pass Go.
how do you play go fish | go fish rules | card game go fish => Deal five or seven cards each. On your turn, ask a player for a rank you have, like "Do you have any 7s?" If they do, they give them all and you go again. If not, they say "Go fish" and you draw. Sets of four go down, and most sets wins.
how do you play war card game | war rules | simple card games for two => Split the deck evenly face down. Both players flip a card and the higher one takes both. If they tie, it's war: each puts three cards down and flips a fourth, and the higher card wins the whole pile. Whoever gets all the cards wins.
how many cards are in a deck | how many cards in a standard deck | what is in a deck of cards => A standard deck has 52 cards in four suits: hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades. Each suit has 13 cards, ace through 10 plus jack, queen and king, and many decks add two jokers.
how do you play solitaire | klondike solitaire rules | how to win solitaire => Deal seven piles with the top card face up. Build down in alternating colors, like a red 6 on a black 7, and move aces to the foundation piles to build up by suit from ace to king. Flip new cards from the stock when stuck.
how do you play tic tac toe and never lose | tic tac toe strategy | unbeatable tic tac toe => If you go first, take a corner or the center. Always block when your opponent has two in a row, and look for moves that make two threats at once. With perfect play by both sides, every game is a tie.
how do you play connect four | connect 4 strategy | how to win connect four => Drop discs in turns to get four in a row any way. Play the middle column early, block your opponent's threes, and set up two threats at once so they can only block one.
how do you play battleship | battleship rules | battleship game how to win => Each player hides five ships on a grid. Take turns calling a square, like B4, and the other player says hit or miss. Sink all the opponent's ships to win. Spread your guesses in a checkerboard pattern first.
how do you play hangman | hangman rules | best letters to guess in hangman => One player picks a word and draws blanks. The other guesses letters, and wrong guesses add parts to the drawing. Start with common letters like E, A, R, I, O, T, N and S.
how do you play scrabble | scrabble rules | how does scrabble scoring work => Make words on the board that connect to existing words, using your seven letter tiles. Add up the letter values and any bonus squares. Using all seven tiles in one turn earns a 50 point bonus.
how do you play dominoes | dominoes rules | how to play dominos => Each player draws tiles. Take turns matching an end of your tile to an open end of the line, with the same number of dots. If you can't play, draw from the pile or pass. First to play all their tiles wins the round.
how do you play rock paper scissors to win | rock paper scissors strategy | best move in rock paper scissors => Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. Random is the safest strategy. Many people throw rock first, and players often switch after losing, which you can watch for.
how do you play sudoku | sudoku rules | how to solve sudoku => Fill the 9 by 9 grid so each row, each column and each 3 by 3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. Start with rows, columns or boxes that are nearly full, and pencil in options.
how do you play mancala | mancala rules | how to win mancala => Pick up all the stones in one of your pits and drop one in each pit going counterclockwise, including your store but not your opponent's. Landing in your store gets another turn. Most stones in your store at the end wins, though house rules vary.
