where did hamburgers come from | hamburger history | why is it called a hamburger => The hamburger is named after Hamburg, Germany, whose minced beef inspired it. The burger in a bun became popular in the US in the late 1800s and early 1900s, and several places claim to have invented it.
where did ice cream come from | history of ice cream | first ice cream => Frozen treats go back thousands of years in China and Persia. Ice cream as we know it developed in Europe in the 1600s and became widely available with refrigeration in the 1800s.
where does vanilla flavor come from | real vs imitation vanilla | vanillin => Real vanilla comes from orchid pods. Imitation vanilla uses vanillin, the main flavor compound, made in labs, which is much cheaper.
why should babies not eat honey | honey babies botulism | honey under 1 year => Honey can contain botulism spores that a baby's immature gut can't handle, so babies under 1 year shouldn't eat it.
what is the hottest part of a chili pepper | where is the heat in a pepper | pepper seeds spicy => The heat (capsaicin) is concentrated in the white ribs (placenta) that hold the seeds, not mainly the seeds themselves. Removing the ribs makes peppers milder.
why do we have food cravings | food cravings | craving sweets => Cravings come from habits, emotions, stress, tiredness and hunger. Eating regular balanced meals and getting enough sleep can reduce them.
why does cilantro taste like soap to some people | cilantro soap gene | cilantro taste => Some people have a gene variant that makes them very sensitive to aldehydes in cilantro, which are also found in soap, so it tastes soapy to them.

what is the most expensive food | expensive foods | why is caviar expensive => Some of the priciest foods include saffron, white truffles, caviar, Wagyu beef and certain melons. Rarity, difficult harvesting and slow growth make them expensive.

what is a truffle in food | truffle mushroom | truffle pigs => Truffles are rare underground fungi with a strong aroma, found near tree roots. Trained dogs, and traditionally pigs, sniff them out. Chocolate truffles are named for their look.
