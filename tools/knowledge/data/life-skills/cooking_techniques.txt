how do i fry an egg | fried egg how to | how to cook a sunny side up egg => Heat a pan on medium with a little butter or oil, crack the egg in gently, and cook until the white is set, about 2 to 3 minutes. For over easy, flip it and cook 20 to 30 seconds more.
what is the difference between over easy and over hard | over medium egg | egg styles explained => Over easy is flipped briefly so the yolk stays runny, over medium has a jammy yolk, and over hard is cooked until the yolk is fully firm. Sunny side up is never flipped.
how do i poach an egg | poached egg how to | poaching eggs in water => Bring water to a gentle simmer with small bubbles, crack the egg into a small cup, stir the water into a gentle swirl and slide the egg in. Cook about 3 to 4 minutes until the white is set, then lift it out with a slotted spoon.
how do i crack an egg without getting shell in it | cracking eggs cleanly | how to crack an egg one handed => Tap the egg on a flat counter instead of the bowl edge, which pushes shell inward. Open it with your thumbs over the bowl. If shell falls in, scoop it out with a bigger piece of shell.
how do i separate egg yolks and whites | separating an egg | how to get the yolk out => Crack the egg over a bowl and pass the yolk back and forth between the shell halves, letting the white drip down. Or crack it into your clean hand and let the white slip through your fingers.
how do i make an omelette | omelet how to | folding an omelette => Whisk 2 or 3 eggs with a pinch of salt, pour into a buttered pan on medium low, and gently push the edges in as it sets. When the top is almost set, add fillings on one half and fold it over.
how do i make soft boiled eggs | runny egg yolk boiled | jammy eggs => Lower eggs into already boiling water and cook about 6 minutes for a runny yolk or 7 minutes for jammy, then move them into cold water to stop the cooking.
why are my hard boiled eggs hard to peel | easy peel boiled eggs | peeling eggs tips => Very fresh eggs are hardest to peel. Start eggs in boiling water instead of cold, then chill them in ice water, and peel under running water starting from the wide end.
why does my boiled egg yolk have a green ring | green yolk | gray ring around yolk => That green gray ring forms when eggs are overcooked or cooled slowly. It's safe to eat. Cook for less time and chill them in cold water right away to prevent it.
how do i boil water faster | does a lid make water boil faster | quick way to boil water => Put a lid on the pot, use only as much water as you need, and start with hot tap water from a kettle if you have one. An electric kettle is usually fastest.
what is the difference between boiling and simmering | simmer meaning | what does a simmer look like => Boiling is big, rolling bubbles that don't stop when you stir. A simmer is lower heat with small, gentle bubbles coming up now and then, used for soups, sauces and stews.
what does it mean to saute | how do you saute vegetables | saute vs fry => Sauteing means cooking food quickly in a little oil or butter over medium high heat while moving it around. Cut things evenly and don't crowd the pan so they brown instead of steaming.
what does it mean to sear meat | why sear a steak | searing tips => Searing is cooking the surface of meat over high heat to make a brown, flavorful crust. Pat the meat dry, get the pan very hot with a little oil, and don't move it until it releases easily.
what is the maillard reaction | why does food turn brown when cooked | browning food flavor => The Maillard reaction is when proteins and sugars react in high heat, turning food brown and creating lots of tasty flavors. It's why toast, seared steak and roasted veggies taste so good.
what is caramelization | how do you caramelize onions | why does sugar turn brown => Caramelization is sugar breaking down and browning with heat, giving a sweet nutty taste. To caramelize onions, cook sliced onions slowly in butter or oil on medium low, stirring now and then, for 30 to 45 minutes.
how do i roast vegetables | oven roasted veggies | roasting vegetables temperature => Cut veggies into similar sizes, toss with oil, salt and pepper, and spread them out on a sheet pan without crowding. Roast at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 20 to 40 minutes, flipping halfway, until browned.
why are my roasted vegetables soggy | crispy roasted vegetables | how to get veggies crispy in the oven => Crowding the pan makes them steam instead of roast. Use a bigger pan or two pans, dry them well after washing, use enough oil and a hot oven.
how do i steam vegetables | steaming broccoli | steam veggies without a steamer => Put an inch or so of water in a pot, bring it to a boil, set the veggies in a steamer basket above the water, cover and cook until just tender, about 4 to 7 minutes for broccoli. A microwave with a covered bowl and a splash of water works too.
what is blanching | how do you blanch vegetables | why blanch green beans => Blanching means boiling food for a short time, then plunging it into ice water to stop the cooking. It keeps veggies bright and crisp and helps peel tomatoes.
how do i bake a potato | baked potato in the oven | how long to bake a potato => Scrub it, poke it a few times with a fork, rub with oil and salt, and bake at about 200 °C (400 °F) for 45 to 60 minutes until a knife slides in easily.
how do i make mashed potatoes fluffy | why are my mashed potatoes gluey | mashing potatoes tips => Use starchy potatoes like russets, cook until very tender, drain well, then mash with warm butter and milk. Overmixing, especially with a blender, makes them gluey.
how do i make crispy bacon | bacon in the oven | cooking bacon on the stove => On the stove, start bacon in a cold pan and cook on medium, flipping, until crisp. In the oven, lay it on a sheet pan and bake at about 200 °C (400 °F) for 15 to 20 minutes. Drain on paper towels.
how do i cook a steak | steak in a pan | how long to cook steak => Let it sit out briefly, pat it dry and salt it. Sear in a very hot oiled pan for about 3 to 5 minutes per side for a 2.5 cm (1 inch) steak, then check the temperature and rest it for 5 minutes before cutting.
why do you rest meat after cooking | resting a steak | should i let meat rest => Resting lets the juices settle back into the meat, so they don't all run out when you cut it. Rest steaks about 5 minutes and big roasts 10 to 20 minutes.
what does cut against the grain mean | slicing meat against the grain | which way to cut steak => The grain is the direction the muscle fibers run, looking like lines. Cutting across those lines makes short fibers, so the meat is more tender to chew.
how do i cook ground beef | browning ground beef | how to cook hamburger meat => Put the meat in a hot pan, break it up with a spoon, and cook, stirring, until no pink remains and it reaches 71 °C (160 °F). Drain extra fat into a can, not the sink.
how do i make a burger patty | forming hamburger patties | why do burgers puff up => Gently shape the meat into patties a bit wider than the bun without packing it too hard, and press a small dent in the middle so it stays flat as it cooks. Salt just before cooking.
how do i cook chicken breast so it isn't dry | juicy chicken breast | dry chicken fix => Pound it to an even thickness, season it, cook on medium high until it reaches 74 °C (165 °F) inside, then let it rest. A meat thermometer stops you from overcooking it.
how do i bake chicken | oven baked chicken temperature | how long to bake chicken breasts => Bake boneless breasts at about 200 °C (400 °F) for roughly 20 to 25 minutes, and bone in pieces longer, until the inside reaches 74 °C (165 °F) on a thermometer.
how do i cook fish | pan fried fish | how do i know when fish is done => Pat it dry, season and cook in a hot oiled pan a few minutes per side. Fish is done when it turns opaque and flakes easily with a fork, at 63 °C (145 °F).
how do i bread something | breading chicken steps | flour egg breadcrumbs order => Use three plates: first flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs. Coat the food in each in that order, pressing the crumbs on. Use one hand for wet and one for dry so your fingers don't get clumpy.
how do i deep fry safely | deep frying at home | is deep frying dangerous => Deep frying should only be done with an adult. Use a heavy pot no more than half full of oil, keep it around 175 to 190 °C (350 to 375 °F) with a thermometer, lower food in gently, and never use water on an oil fire.
how do i cook pasta perfectly | pasta tips | how much salt in pasta water => Use a big pot of boiling, well salted water, stir in the pasta so it doesn't stick, and taste it a minute or two before the package time. Save a cup of the starchy water to loosen your sauce.
should i add oil to pasta water | does oil stop pasta sticking | why does my pasta stick together => Oil mostly floats on top and can make sauce slide off. Stirring in the first couple of minutes and using plenty of water stops sticking better.
what does al dente mean | how do i know pasta is done | al dente pasta => Al dente is Italian for "to the tooth," meaning pasta that's cooked through but still has a slight bite in the middle. Bite a piece to check.
should i rinse pasta after cooking | rinsing noodles | do you rinse spaghetti => Usually no, because the starch helps sauce stick. Rinse only for cold pasta salads or noodles you want to stop cooking right away.
why is my rice mushy | rice too sticky | how to make fluffy rice => Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, use the right water amount, keep the lid on while it simmers, then let it rest covered for 10 minutes and fluff with a fork.
how do i make fried rice | fried rice tips | why use day old rice for fried rice => Cold leftover rice works best because it's drier and fries without clumping. Stir fry it in a very hot pan with a little oil, veggies and egg, then add soy sauce.
how do i stir fry | stir fry technique | how to stir fry vegetables => Cut everything small and even before you start, heat the pan very hot with a little oil, and cook in small batches, stirring constantly. Add sauce at the end.
how do i make a grilled sandwich crispy | crispy panini at home | toasting a sandwich in a pan => Butter the outside of the bread or use mayo, cook on medium low so the inside melts before the outside burns, and press with a spatula or another pan.
how do i make toast without a toaster | toast in a pan | oven toast => Toast bread in a dry or lightly buttered pan on medium, flipping when golden, or under the oven broiler for a minute or two per side, watching closely so it doesn't burn.
how do i make french toast | french toast how to | why is my french toast soggy => Whisk eggs, milk, a little sugar and cinnamon, dip slightly stale bread for a few seconds per side, and cook on a buttered pan on medium until golden. Soaking too long or high heat makes it soggy inside.
how do i flip a pancake | when should i flip pancakes | perfect pancakes tips => Flip when bubbles pop on top and the edges look dry, usually 2 to 3 minutes. Slide the spatula fully under, lift and turn quickly. Keep the heat medium, since the first pancake often tests the pan.
how do i melt chocolate | melting chocolate in the microwave | why did my chocolate seize => Microwave chopped chocolate in short 20 to 30 second bursts, stirring between, until almost melted, then stir until smooth. Even a drop of water can make it seize into a thick lump.
how do i melt butter | melting butter in the microwave | softened vs melted butter => Microwave it cut into pieces, covered, in 10 to 15 second bursts, or melt on the stove over low heat. Softened butter is not melted, just room temperature so a finger leaves a dent.
how do i soften butter quickly | butter too cold to bake | fast way to soften butter => Cut it into small cubes and let it sit 15 minutes, or grate it. You can microwave in 5 second bursts but watch closely so it doesn't melt.
how do i whip cream | homemade whipped cream | soft peaks vs stiff peaks => Beat cold heavy cream with a little sugar until it thickens. Soft peaks droop over when you lift the whisk, and stiff peaks stand straight up. Stop there or it turns to butter.
how do i whip egg whites | beating egg whites | why won't my egg whites get fluffy => Use a very clean bowl with no grease, and make sure there's no yolk in the whites. Beat until foamy, then add sugar slowly if the recipe says, and keep beating until they form peaks.
what does fold in mean | how do you fold ingredients | folding batter => Folding means gently mixing a light ingredient, like whipped cream or egg whites, into something heavier without losing the air. Cut down through the middle with a spatula, sweep along the bottom and turn it over, rotating the bowl.
what does cream butter and sugar mean | creaming method | how long to cream butter => Creaming is beating softened butter and sugar together until pale and fluffy, about 2 to 4 minutes with a mixer. It adds air that helps cookies and cakes rise.
what does knead mean | how to knead dough | how long do you knead bread => Kneading is pushing dough with the heel of your hand, folding it over and turning it, again and again. It builds gluten so bread is chewy. Knead about 8 to 10 minutes by hand until smooth and stretchy.
how do i know if bread dough has risen enough | proofing dough | poke test for dough => Most dough should about double in size. Gently poke it with a floured finger: if the dent slowly springs back partway, it's ready. If it springs right back, wait more.
why are my cookies flat | cookies spread too much | how to make thicker cookies => Butter that's too soft or melted, too little flour, or a hot baking sheet can make cookies spread. Chill the dough for 30 minutes, measure flour correctly and use a cool pan.
how do i make cookies chewy | soft chewy cookies | crispy vs chewy cookies => More brown sugar, an extra egg yolk and slightly underbaking make cookies chewy. More white sugar, more butter and baking longer make them crispier.
how do i know when cookies are done | when to take cookies out | underbaked cookies => Take them out when the edges are set and golden but the middles still look a little soft. They keep cooking on the hot pan for a few minutes.
how do i know when a cake is done | toothpick test cake | is my cake cooked inside => Stick a toothpick or skewer into the center. If it comes out clean or with a few dry crumbs, it's done. The top should spring back when lightly pressed.
why did my cake sink in the middle | cake fell in the middle | sunken cake reasons => Common causes are opening the oven too early, underbaking, too much baking powder or soda, or overmixing. Check doneness with a toothpick before pulling it out.
how do i keep a cake from sticking to the pan | greasing a cake pan | how to get a cake out of the pan => Grease the pan with butter or spray, dust with flour or line the bottom with parchment paper. Let the cake cool in the pan about 10 minutes, run a knife around the edge and flip it out.
why should i preheat the oven | do i need to preheat | how long does an oven take to preheat => Preheating means the oven is already at the right temperature when food goes in, so it cooks the right way and on time. Most ovens take about 10 to 15 minutes.
what is the difference between bake and broil | broiler what is it | how to use the broiler => Bake heats the oven all around for even cooking. Broil uses strong heat from the top element only, like an upside down grill, for browning cheese or toasting tops in a few minutes. Watch it closely.
what is a convection oven | convection vs regular oven | do i lower the temperature for convection => A convection oven uses a fan to move hot air, so food cooks faster and browns more evenly. Many cooks lower the temperature by about 15 °C (25 °F) or check food earlier.
how does an air fryer work | what is an air fryer | is an air fryer the same as deep frying => An air fryer is a small convection oven that blows very hot air around food, making it crispy with little oil. It's not true frying, but it gets fries and nuggets nicely crunchy.
how do i use an air fryer | air fryer tips | how long to air fry fries => Don't overfill the basket, shake or flip food halfway, and spray or toss with a little oil for crispiness. Frozen fries often take about 15 to 20 minutes at around 200 °C (400 °F), but check the package.
how do i use a microwave safely | microwave safety | what can't go in a microwave => Use microwave safe dishes, no metal or foil, and cover food loosely to stop splatters. Stir halfway so it heats evenly, and be careful of steam when removing covers. Never run it empty.
why does microwaved food have cold spots | microwave heating unevenly | how to reheat food evenly => Microwaves heat unevenly. Spread food in a ring around the edge of the plate, cover it, stir or flip halfway, and let it stand a minute after heating so the heat spreads.
how do i reheat pizza | best way to reheat pizza | soggy microwave pizza => Reheat slices in a covered pan on medium for a few minutes to keep the crust crispy, or in the oven or air fryer at about 180 °C (350 °F). Microwaving makes the crust chewy.
how do i reheat rice safely | is it safe to reheat rice | leftover rice food safety => Rice is safe to reheat if it was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge, ideally eaten within a day or so. Heat it until steaming hot all the way through and only reheat it once.
how do i chop an onion | dicing an onion | how to cut an onion => Cut off the top, halve it through the root, peel it, lay each half flat, then make cuts toward the root without cutting through it, then slice across. The root keeps it together while you dice.
how do i hold a knife safely | knife skills for kids | how to cut without cutting yourself => Hold the handle firmly, and curl the fingers of your other hand into a claw so your knuckles guide the blade and fingertips stay tucked. Cut on a stable board and go slowly. Ask an adult to teach you first.
how do i stop my cutting board from sliding | cutting board slipping | wet towel under cutting board => Put a damp paper towel or dish towel under the cutting board. It grips the counter so the board stays still, which is much safer.
why is a sharp knife safer than a dull one | dull knife danger | do i need a sharp knife => A sharp knife cuts with less force, so it's less likely to slip off food and into your hand. Dull knives need you to push hard, which causes accidents.
what is the difference between dice chop and mince | cutting terms | julienne meaning => Chopping is rough pieces, dicing is even cubes, and mincing is very tiny pieces, like for garlic. Julienne means thin matchstick strips, and slicing means flat pieces.
how do i peel garlic | easy garlic peeling | how to mince garlic => Press the clove with the flat side of a knife until it cracks, and the skin comes right off. To mince, slice it thin, then rock the knife back and forth across it until it's tiny.
how do i cut an avocado | opening an avocado safely | how to get the pit out of an avocado => Cut around the pit lengthwise, twist the halves apart, then scoop the pit out with a spoon instead of whacking it with a knife, which causes lots of hand injuries. Scoop the flesh out with a spoon.
how do i know if an avocado is ripe | ripe avocado test | how to ripen an avocado faster => A ripe avocado gives a little when you gently squeeze it. To ripen faster, put it in a paper bag with a banana or apple at room temperature.
how do i cut a mango | slicing a mango | mango pit how to cut => Stand it on its end and slice down on each side of the flat pit. Score each half in a grid without cutting the skin, then push the skin up and the cubes pop out.
how do i cut a pineapple | pineapple cutting | peeling a pineapple => Cut off the top and bottom, stand it up and slice off the skin, then cut out the hard core. A ripe pineapple smells sweet at the bottom and is golden, not hard and green.
how do i zest a lemon | what is citrus zest | lemon zest without a zester => Zest is the colorful outer skin of citrus, full of flavor oils. Rub the fruit over a fine grater or zester, taking only the colored part, not the bitter white pith.
how do i get more juice from a lemon | juicing a lemon | lemon juice tips => Roll it firmly on the counter, or microwave it for about 10 seconds, before cutting. Then squeeze it with a juicer or a fork twisted inside the half.
how do i season food | when to add salt | food tastes bland => Salt a little at the start and taste as you go, adjusting at the end. A squeeze of lemon or a splash of vinegar can also make bland food taste brighter.
what does season to taste mean | how much salt is a pinch | to taste meaning => Season to taste means add salt, pepper or spices little by little, tasting as you go, until you like it. A pinch is the small amount you can hold between your thumb and finger.
what do i do if i put too much salt in | food too salty fix | oversalted soup => Add more of the unsalted ingredients, like more broth, water, potatoes or rice, to dilute it. A little acid like lemon juice can also help balance it.
what do i do if food is too spicy | made it too spicy | how to cool down spicy food => Add dairy like sour cream or yogurt, something sweet, more of the base ingredients, or a squeeze of lime. Drinking milk helps your mouth more than water.
how do i make a basic sauce thicker | thicken a sauce | cornstarch slurry => Simmer it longer so water evaporates, or stir in a slurry of 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cold water and bring it to a simmer for a minute.
what is a roux | how do you make a roux | flour and butter sauce => A roux is equal parts butter and flour cooked together to thicken sauces like mac and cheese sauce and gravy. Melt the butter, whisk in the flour and cook a minute, then slowly whisk in milk or stock.
how do i deglaze a pan | what does deglaze mean | brown bits in the pan => After searing, pour a little liquid like broth into the hot pan and scrape up the brown stuck bits, called fond. They melt into the liquid and make a tasty sauce.
what does reduce mean in cooking | reducing a sauce | how to reduce liquid => Reducing means simmering a liquid uncovered so water evaporates, making it thicker and more flavorful.
what does marinate mean | how long should i marinate chicken | what is a marinade => A marinade is a flavorful liquid, often with oil, acid and seasoning, that you soak food in. Chicken often marinates 30 minutes to a few hours in the fridge. Very acidic marinades can make meat mushy if left too long.
what does brining do | how do you brine chicken | wet brine vs dry brine => Brining means soaking meat in salty water, or salting it ahead of time for a dry brine, which helps it stay juicy and seasoned when cooked.
what does it mean to toast spices | toasting nuts | how to toast seeds => Toasting means warming spices, nuts or seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, stirring, for a few minutes until they smell nutty. Watch closely, they burn quickly.
how do i make popcorn on the stove | stovetop popcorn | popcorn without a microwave => Heat a few tablespoons of oil in a big pot with a lid, add a few kernels, and when they pop, add about a third of a cup more. Cover and shake now and then until popping slows to a few seconds apart.
how do i cook frozen vegetables | frozen veggies taste better how | do i thaw frozen vegetables first => You don't need to thaw them. Roast, saute or steam them straight from frozen, and roasting at high heat gives the best flavor and texture.
how do i make a hard taco shell crispy | warming tortillas | how to heat tortillas => Warm tortillas in a dry pan for 20 to 30 seconds per side, or wrap a stack in a damp paper towel and microwave for about 30 seconds. Bake hard shells briefly in the oven.
how do i make instant ramen better | ramen upgrade ideas | hack for instant noodles => Add a soft boiled or poached egg, frozen veggies, leftover chicken, green onions, a little soy sauce or a slice of cheese. Using only part of the seasoning packet cuts the salt.
how do i grill food | grilling basics | how to grill burgers => Have an adult light and run the grill. Clean and oil the grates, cook food over direct heat to sear and move it to cooler indirect heat to finish if it's thick, and check temperatures with a thermometer.
what is mise en place | getting ingredients ready before cooking | prep before cooking => Mise en place is French for "everything in its place". It means measuring and chopping all your ingredients before you start cooking, so you never rush or forget something.
how do i follow a recipe | recipe tips for beginners | reading a recipe => Read the whole recipe first, get out all the ingredients and tools, measure everything, then follow the steps in order. Set timers and taste as you go.
how do i double a recipe | scaling a recipe | can you double a cake recipe => Multiply each ingredient by two. Cooking times may change, especially for baking where a bigger pan takes longer, so check early and often. Baking is trickier to scale than cooking.
