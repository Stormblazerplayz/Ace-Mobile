# tag: life.money.words
what is an asset | assets vs liabilities | what is a liability => An asset is something you own that has value, like savings, a bike or a house. A liability is something you owe, like a loan.
what is net worth | how do you calculate net worth | net worth meaning => Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. If you have $500 in savings and owe a friend $50, your net worth is $450.
what is principal on a loan | principal vs interest | loan principal meaning => The principal is the original amount borrowed. Interest is the extra you pay for borrowing it.
what is collateral | secured vs unsecured loan | what is a secured loan => Collateral is something valuable a borrower promises to the lender, like a car or house, which the lender can take if the loan isn't paid. Loans with collateral are secured loans.
what is simple interest | simple vs compound interest | how to calculate simple interest => Simple interest is paid only on the original amount. If you have $100 at 5 percent simple interest, you earn $5 each year. Compound interest also earns interest on past interest, so it grows faster.
what is deflation | deflation vs inflation | is deflation good => Deflation is when prices fall across the economy. It sounds nice, but it can make people delay spending, which slows the economy and can cost jobs.
what is a currency | different currencies | why do countries have different money => A currency is the money a country uses, like the US dollar, euro, yen or pound. Each country or group manages its own currency and its value.
what is the euro | which countries use the euro | eurozone => The euro is the shared currency of the eurozone, a group of European Union countries. As of 2025, 20 EU countries use it, and Bulgaria is set to join in 2026.
what is cash flow | positive cash flow meaning | money in money out => Cash flow is the money moving in and out over time. Positive cash flow means more is coming in than going out.
what is a fee | hidden fees | why do things have service fees => A fee is a charge for a service, like an ATM fee or a ticket service fee. Hidden fees are ones you don't see until checkout, so always look at the final total.
what is a down payment | how much is a down payment | down payment meaning => A down payment is the part of a big purchase, like a car or house, you pay up front, with the rest borrowed. A bigger down payment means a smaller loan.
what is a lease | leasing vs buying a car | rent vs lease => A lease is a contract to use something, like an apartment or car, for a set time while making regular payments, without owning it.
what is renting vs buying a home | should you rent or buy | pros of renting => Renting means paying a landlord to live somewhere, with fewer repairs to worry about and easier moving. Buying builds ownership over time but costs more up front, and you pay for repairs.
what are utilities | utility bills | what bills do houses have => Utilities are services like electricity, water, gas, trash pickup and often internet. Households pay for them every month.
what is insurance | how does insurance work | why do people buy insurance => Insurance is paying a company a regular amount, called a premium, so that if something bad happens, like a car crash or illness, the company helps pay the big costs.
what is a deductible | insurance deductible meaning | premium vs deductible => A deductible is the amount you pay yourself before insurance starts paying. A higher deductible usually means a lower premium.
what kinds of insurance are there | types of insurance | car health home insurance => Common types are health, car, home or renters, life and disability insurance. Phones and electronics can also be insured.
what is a budget deficit | government debt | what is the national debt => A deficit is when a government spends more than it collects in a year. The national debt is the total it owes from borrowing over many years.
what is a tariff | tariffs explained | who pays for tariffs => A tariff is a tax on goods imported from another country. Importers pay it at first, and they often pass some of the cost on to shoppers through higher prices.
what are imports and exports | trade between countries | why countries trade => Imports are goods a country buys from other countries, and exports are goods it sells to them. Countries trade because each can make some things more cheaply or better.
what is a supply chain | supply chain problems | how do products get to stores => A supply chain is every step that gets a product to you: raw materials, factories, shipping, warehouses and stores. A problem at any step can cause shortages.
what is scarcity in economics | scarcity meaning | why are resources limited => Scarcity means there isn't enough of something for everyone to have all they want, so people have to make choices. It's a basic idea in economics.
what is a goods vs services | goods and services examples | what is a service => Goods are things you can touch, like shoes or a bike. Services are work done for you, like a haircut, a bike repair or a lesson.
what is a producer and consumer | consumer meaning | who are producers => Producers make goods or provide services. Consumers buy and use them. Most people are both, like a baker who also buys groceries.
what is a nonprofit | nonprofit vs for profit | how do charities make money => A nonprofit is an organization that uses its money for a mission, like helping people or animals, instead of paying profits to owners. It gets money from donations, grants and fees.
how do i choose a charity to give to | donating wisely | is this charity legit => Pick a cause you care about, check that the charity is real and registered, and look it up on rating sites like Charity Navigator. Local food banks and shelters are good places to start.
what is a barter | barter system | trading without money => Bartering is trading goods or services directly without money, like trading your old game for a friend's book. It's how people traded before money was common.
what is a credit report | who can see my credit report | free credit report => A credit report lists someone's borrowing history. In the US, adults can get free reports from the three credit bureaus at AnnualCreditReport.com. Kids normally shouldn't have one, so a report in a child's name can mean identity theft.
what is bankruptcy | what happens when you go bankrupt | bankruptcy meaning => Bankruptcy is a legal process for people or companies that can't pay their debts. Some debts get wiped out or reorganized, but it hurts credit for years.
what is a coin collection worth | rare coins | how to know if a coin is valuable => Most everyday coins are worth face value, but rare dates, mint errors and coins in great condition can be worth more. A coin guide book or a coin dealer can check yours.
what is a budget app | money apps for kids | apps to track allowance => Budget apps help track spending and savings automatically. Some are made for kids and teens with parent controls, letting you set goals and see your balance.
what is cryptocurrency risk | is bitcoin safe to invest in | why crypto prices swing => Crypto prices can rise or crash very quickly, and there's no bank insurance if an exchange fails or you lose your keys. Only adults should invest, with money they can afford to lose.
what is a blockchain | how does blockchain work | blockchain explained simply => A blockchain is a shared digital record of transactions copied across many computers. New entries are added in blocks linked together, which makes old records very hard to change.
what is an nft | nft meaning | are nfts worth anything => An NFT is a digital certificate on a blockchain showing ownership of a digital item, like art. Their prices rose a lot in 2021, then many fell sharply, so they're very risky.
what is a crypto wallet | seed phrase meaning | private key crypto => A crypto wallet holds the keys that control your crypto. The seed phrase is a list of words that can recover it. Anyone with it can take everything, so never share it with anyone.

# tag: life.money.kids
how can i earn money online as a kid | online jobs for kids | make money on the internet safely => With a parent's help, kids can sell crafts or prints, make videos, design graphics or offer simple services to people they know. A parent should handle accounts, payments and contacts with strangers.
should i lend money to my friends | lending money to friends | friend won't pay me back => Only lend what you'd be okay not getting back, and agree clearly when it'll be repaid. Money problems can hurt friendships, so many people prefer just saying no politely.
how do i say no when a friend asks for money | friend keeps asking for money | refusing to lend money => You can say kindly, "Sorry, I'm saving for something and can't lend money." You don't owe a long explanation.
what should i do with birthday money | spending birthday money wisely | gift money ideas => Try splitting it: save part for a bigger goal, spend some on something you'll really enjoy, and give a little if you want. Thank the person who gave it.
how do i count money quickly | counting coins and bills | adding up change => Sort by type, start with the biggest bills, then coins from largest to smallest, and count in groups like stacks of ten or rolls of coins. Recount once to check.
how do i pay at a store | using cash at a store | how to pay with a card => Put your items on the counter, the cashier scans them and tells you the total. Hand over cash and wait for change, or tap or insert a card and enter a PIN if asked. Check your change and receipt.
how do i know if i was given the right change | counting change | cashier gave wrong change => Subtract the total from what you paid, then count the change you got. If it's wrong, politely tell the cashier right away before walking away.
what is pocket money | allowance vs pocket money | is pocket money the same as allowance => Pocket money is the British name for an allowance, a regular amount of money parents give kids to spend or save.
should kids get paid for chores | allowance for chores | paid chores vs family chores => Families do it differently. Some pay for extra jobs but not for everyday chores that help the family, like cleaning your own room. Talk with your parents about what works.
how do i get my parents to trust me with money | showing responsibility with money | prove i can handle money => Track what you spend, stick to a plan, save for goals, pay back anything you borrow and be honest about purchases. Showing good habits with small amounts earns more freedom.
what is a money goal | short term vs long term goals | setting savings goals => A short term goal is something you can save for in weeks or months, like a game. A long term goal takes a year or more, like a computer, a car or college.
how do i stop wasting money on snacks | small purchases add up | latte factor => Small daily buys add up: $3 a day is over $1,000 a year. Bring snacks from home and a water bottle, and save the treats for special times.
what is financial literacy | why learn about money | money skills for kids => Financial literacy means understanding how to earn, save, spend, borrow and invest wisely. Learning it early helps you avoid debt and reach your goals.
how much does a gaming pc cost | building a pc budget | cheap gaming pc => Prices vary a lot. A basic gaming PC might cost several hundred dollars, while high end builds can cost a few thousand. Set a budget first and spend most of it on the graphics card and processor.
should i buy a new phone every year | upgrading phones | how long should a phone last => Most phones get software updates for several years, so upgrading every year usually isn't needed. Using a phone longer and protecting it with a case saves a lot of money.
