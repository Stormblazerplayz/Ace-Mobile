# tag: life.cooking.storage
can i freeze milk | freezing milk | frozen milk texture => Yes, for about 3 months. Leave room in the container since it expands. Thawed milk can separate a little, so shake it well, and it's best for cooking, cereal or smoothies.
can i freeze cheese | freezing cheese | frozen shredded cheese => Hard and shredded cheeses freeze well for about 6 months, though they may crumble, so they're best for cooking. Soft cheeses like brie get mushy.
can i freeze eggs | freezing eggs | freeze raw eggs => Not in the shell, since they crack. Beat whole eggs together, or separate whites and yolks, and freeze them in a container or ice cube tray for up to about a year.
can i freeze cooked rice | freezing rice | frozen rice reheat => Yes. Cool it quickly, freeze it in flat bags or containers for about a month, and reheat until steaming hot, adding a splash of water.
can i freeze pasta | freezing cooked pasta | frozen pasta => Cooked pasta freezes well for 1 to 2 months, especially in sauce or casseroles like lasagna. Cooking it slightly firm keeps it from getting mushy.
can i freeze soup | freezing soup | soup freezer => Most soups freeze well for 2 to 3 months. Leave space for expansion, and add noodles or cream when reheating, since they get mushy or separate in the freezer.
can i freeze cookie dough | freezing cookie dough | frozen dough balls => Yes. Scoop dough into balls, freeze them on a tray, then store them in a bag for about 3 months. Bake from frozen, adding a minute or two.
can i freeze cake | freezing cake | frozen cake layers => Unfrosted cake layers freeze well for 2 to 3 months if cooled and wrapped tightly in plastic wrap. Thaw them wrapped so they stay moist.
can i freeze pancakes | freezing pancakes | frozen waffles homemade => Yes. Cool them, freeze in a single layer, then stack with parchment between them in a bag. Reheat in a toaster or microwave for a quick breakfast.
can i freeze avocado | freezing avocado | frozen avocado => Yes, mashed or in halves with a little lemon juice. Thawed avocado is softer, so it's best for guacamole, smoothies or spreads, not slices.
can i freeze yogurt | freezing yogurt | frozen yogurt cubes => Yes, but it gets grainy when thawed, so use it in smoothies or baking. Frozen yogurt bark with fruit is a fun snack.
can i freeze potatoes | freezing potatoes | frozen raw potatoes => Raw potatoes don't freeze well because they turn grainy and dark. Cook or blanch them first. Mashed potatoes and fries freeze well.
can i freeze tomatoes | freezing tomatoes | frozen tomatoes => Yes, whole or chopped, for cooking sauces and soups. They turn mushy when thawed, but the skins slip right off.
can i freeze herbs | freezing herbs | herbs in ice cube trays => Chop fresh herbs and freeze them in ice cube trays covered with olive oil or water, then drop a cube into soups and sauces.
can i freeze butter | freezing butter | frozen butter grate => Yes. Butter freezes well for several months if wrapped tightly. Frozen butter can be grated straight into pastry dough.
can i freeze tortillas | freezing tortillas | frozen tortillas => Yes. Put parchment between them, seal them in a bag, and thaw for a few minutes on the counter or warm them in a pan.
can i freeze sandwiches | freezing sandwiches | make ahead sandwiches => Sandwiches like peanut butter and jam or meat and cheese freeze well. Leave off lettuce, tomato and mayo, and pack it frozen so it thaws by lunch.
can i freeze pizza | freezing leftover pizza | frozen pizza slices => Yes. Wrap slices tightly and freeze for 1 to 2 months. Reheat in an oven, air fryer or covered pan for a crisp crust.
how long does fish last in the fridge | fresh fish fridge | raw fish days => Raw fish is best cooked within 1 to 2 days. Cooked fish lasts about 3 to 4 days in the fridge.
how long does pasta last in the fridge | cooked pasta fridge | leftover pasta days => Cooked pasta lasts about 3 to 5 days in a sealed container in the fridge.
how long does cheese last | hard cheese fridge | moldy cheese => Opened hard cheeses like cheddar last about 3 to 4 weeks in the fridge, while soft cheeses like brie or ricotta last about a week. Wrap them well.
how long does deli meat last | lunch meat fridge | deli meat days => Opened deli meat lasts about 3 to 5 days in the fridge. Unopened packages are good until the date on the package.
how long does a hard boiled egg last | hard boiled eggs fridge | boiled egg storage => Hard boiled eggs last about 1 week in the fridge, in or out of the shell. Don't leave them out for more than 2 hours.
how long do apples last | apples fridge | store apples => Apples last about a week on the counter and 1 to 2 months in the fridge crisper drawer.
how long does salsa last | open salsa | salsa fridge => Opened jarred salsa usually lasts about 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge. Fresh homemade salsa lasts about 5 to 7 days.
how long does ketchup last | ketchup fridge | refrigerate ketchup => Opened ketchup keeps about 6 months in the fridge. It's acidic, so it lasts a long time, but cold storage keeps the best flavor.
how long does peanut butter last | peanut butter expire | natural peanut butter fridge => Opened regular peanut butter lasts about 2 to 3 months in the pantry. Natural peanut butter should go in the fridge after opening.
how long does jam last | jam fridge | open jelly => Opened regular jam usually lasts about 6 months to a year in the fridge. Low sugar jams spoil faster, so check for mold.
how long does mayo last | mayonnaise fridge | mayo left out => Opened mayonnaise lasts about 2 months in the fridge. Don't leave it out for more than 2 hours.
how long does hummus last | hummus fridge | hummus expire => Opened store bought hummus lasts about 5 to 7 days in the fridge. Homemade hummus lasts about 3 to 5 days.
how long does juice last | open juice | juice fridge => Opened juice that needs refrigeration usually lasts about 7 to 10 days in the fridge.
how long does soda last | flat soda | soda expire => Unopened soda stays safe well past the date but slowly loses fizz and flavor. Once opened, it goes flat within a day or two.
how do i keep chips fresh | stale chips | chip clip => Squeeze out the air, roll the bag down tightly and clip it, or pour chips into an airtight container. Stale chips can be crisped in the oven for a few minutes.
how do i keep cereal fresh | stale cereal | cereal container => Close the inner bag tightly or move cereal to an airtight container. Stale cereal can be toasted for a few minutes in the oven.
how do i keep brown sugar soft | hard brown sugar | soften brown sugar => Store it in an airtight container. To soften hard brown sugar, add a slice of bread for a day, or microwave it with a damp paper towel for 20 seconds.
how do i ripen bananas | ripen bananas fast | green bananas => Put green bananas in a paper bag with an apple or ripe banana, which give off ethylene gas that speeds ripening. Keep them at room temperature.
how do i tell if meat is bad | spoiled meat | meat smells => Spoiled meat may feel slimy or sticky, smell sour or off, or turn gray or green. Never taste it to check, and when in doubt, throw it out.
how do i open a coconut | coconut opening | coconut water => Ask an adult. Poke a screwdriver into the soft eye to drain the water, then tap firmly around the middle with the back of a heavy knife or hammer until it cracks open.
