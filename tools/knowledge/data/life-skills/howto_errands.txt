# tag: life.howto.errands
how many stamps do i need | stamp for a letter | forever stamp => A standard letter in the US under 1 ounce needs one Forever stamp, which is always valid for first class mail even after prices rise. Heavier or oddly shaped mail needs more postage.
what is a zip code | zip code meaning | why do we have zip codes => A ZIP code is a 5 digit US postal code that helps mail get sorted and delivered. ZIP stands for Zone Improvement Plan, introduced in 1963. ZIP+4 adds 4 more digits for more precision.
how do i ship a package | mailing a box | send a package => Pack the item with padding in a sturdy box, tape it well, write or print the address label, and take it to the post office or a shipping store, where they weigh it and tell you the price.
what is a tracking number | track a package | where is my package => A tracking number is a code for a package that lets you see where it is on the shipper's website or app, from pickup to delivery.
what is a po box | post office box | po box meaning => A PO box is a locked mailbox you rent at a post office to receive mail, useful when people don't want mail sent to their home address.
how do i make a doctors appointment | booking an appointment | calling the doctor => Call the office or use their app or website, say who the appointment is for, the reason and what days work. Have insurance information ready. Parents usually handle this for kids.
what happens at a checkup | yearly physical | well child visit => At a checkup, a doctor measures your height, weight and blood pressure, checks your eyes, ears, heart and lungs, asks about health and habits, and may give vaccines. It's a good time to ask questions.
what happens at the dentist | dental checkup | dentist visit for kids => A hygienist cleans and polishes your teeth, may take X rays, and the dentist checks for cavities and gum problems. Most people go every 6 months.
what do i say at the barber | how to ask for a haircut | getting a haircut => Bring a photo of the style you want, say how much to take off, like "an inch on top, short on the sides," and ask for a fade or scissor cut if you know. Speak up if something seems off.
how do i get a library card | library card for kids | sign up for library => Visit your local library with a parent and proof of address. Many libraries give free cards to kids, and some let you sign up online.
what can i do at the library | library services | more than books at the library => Besides books, many libraries offer ebooks, audiobooks, movies, video games, 3D printers, maker spaces, homework help, coding clubs and free events.
how do i borrow ebooks from the library | libby app | free ebooks and audiobooks => Use an app like Libby with your library card to borrow ebooks and audiobooks for free on an iPad or phone. They return automatically when the loan ends.
how do i find a book in the library | dewey decimal system | library call numbers => Use the library catalog to search, then find the call number on the shelf. Nonfiction uses the Dewey Decimal System, so science books are in the 500s, and fiction is sorted by author's last name.
how do i order at a fast food counter | ordering food at a counter | fast food ordering => Look at the menu first, step up when it's your turn, say your order clearly with any changes, like "no pickles," answer any questions about size and drink, then pay and wait for your number.
how do i order at a coffee shop | coffee shop ordering | drinks for kids at coffee shops => Say the size, the drink and any changes. Kid friendly options include hot chocolate, steamers (steamed milk with syrup), lemonade, smoothies and caffeine free drinks.
how do i shop for groceries | grocery shopping tips | how to grocery shop => Make a list sorted by store section, eat before you go, compare unit prices, check expiration dates, and get cold and frozen items last so they stay cold.
how is a grocery store laid out | where things are in a grocery store | grocery store sections => Most stores put fresh produce, meat, dairy and bakery around the edges, and packaged foods in the middle aisles. Basics like milk are often in the back so you pass more items.
how do i use self checkout | self checkout tips | scanning groceries => Scan each item's barcode, put it in the bagging area, look up produce by name or code, then pay. Press the help button if something goes wrong.
what is a barcode | how do barcodes work | upc code => A barcode is a pattern of lines that a scanner reads as numbers, identifying the product so the register knows the item and price. The UPC barcode was first scanned in 1974, on a pack of gum.
how do i call a store to ask a question | calling a business | phone call to a store => Say hello, ask your question clearly, like "Do you have this in stock?", and have a pen ready. Say thank you before hanging up.
how do i ask for help in a store | finding an item in a store | asking a store employee => Look for someone in a uniform or name tag, say "Excuse me, could you help me find..." and describe what you need.
how do i get my bike fixed at a shop | bike shop visit | bike tune up cost => Explain what's wrong, like "the gears skip," and ask for a quote before work starts. A basic tune up adjusts brakes and gears and often costs around 60 to 120 dollars.
how do i get my shoes resoled | shoe repair | fix worn shoes => A cobbler, or shoe repair shop, can replace soles, heels, fix stitching and stretch shoes. It's often cheaper than buying good shoes again.
how do i recycle electronics | e waste | old phone disposal => Don't put electronics or batteries in the trash. Take them to an e waste drop off, an electronics store with a recycling program, or a local hazardous waste day. Erase personal data first.
how do i donate old clothes and toys | where to donate | donating stuff => Wash and check items, then take them to a thrift store, shelter, school or donation bin. Toys should be clean and have all their parts.
how do i sell stuff online as a kid | selling things online | selling used stuff => Kids usually need a parent to sell on sites like eBay or Facebook Marketplace, since most require users to be 18. Take clear photos, describe honestly and meet buyers in safe public places with a parent.
how do i fill out a form | filling out paperwork | form tips => Read the whole form first, print clearly in blue or black ink, fill every required field, write N/A if something doesn't apply, and double check spelling, dates and signatures.
how do i write the date | date formats | month day year => In the US, dates are often written month/day/year, like 3/14/2026. Most other countries use day/month/year, like 14/3/2026. The format 2026-03-14 is used internationally to avoid confusion.
how do i write my address | address format | what goes in an address => Write your name, then house number and street, apartment number if any, then city, state and ZIP code. Only share your address with people and places your parents approve.
how do i sign my name | signature ideas | how to make a signature => A signature is how you write your name to approve something. It can be cursive or printed, but should be consistent. Only sign things you understand.
how do i read a receipt | what is on a receipt | receipt explained => A receipt lists each item and price, the subtotal, tax, total, how you paid, any change, the date, and often the return policy.
how do i use a vending machine | vending machine tips | vending machine stuck => Check the price, insert money or tap a card, and type the item's code. If it gets stuck, don't shake or tip the machine, since they can fall. Tell staff.
how do i use a washing machine at a laundromat | laundromat tips | using a laundromat => Bring detergent and quarters or a card, sort clothes, pick an empty machine, add detergent, choose the cycle and pay. Stay nearby and move clothes promptly when they're done.
what is a coin counting machine | cashing in coins | coinstar fees => Coin machines in some stores count your coins and give a voucher, often for a fee around 12 percent. Many banks count coins free for customers.
how do i keep track of my stuff when out | not losing things | stuff to check before leaving => Before you leave a place, do a quick check: phone, keys, wallet, water bottle and bag. Keep important things in the same pocket each time.
what should i carry in my backpack every day | everyday carry for kids | backpack essentials => Pack a water bottle, pencils, charger, a small snack, tissues, a few bandages, and a card with emergency contact numbers in case your phone dies.
