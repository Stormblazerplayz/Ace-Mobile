# tag: life.howto.basics
how do i read roman numerals on a clock | iiii on clocks | roman numeral clock => Each hour is a Roman numeral: I is 1, V is 5 and X is 10, so IX is 9 and XII is 12. Many clocks show 4 as IIII instead of IV, a long tradition for looks.
how do i write in cursive | learn cursive | cursive letters => Learn lowercase letters in groups that share strokes, like a, c, d, g and o, then practice joining them. Write slowly on lined paper and keep a steady slant.
how do i use a compass for drawing circles | drawing compass | draw a perfect circle => Set the distance between the point and pencil to your radius, press the point in the center, and twist the top to swing the pencil around.
how do i mail a package | shipping a package | post office package => Pack it in a sturdy box with padding, tape it well, write the recipient's address in the center and yours in the top left, then take it to the post office or a shipping store to pay.
how do i write a postcard | postcard format | sending postcards => Write your message on the left side and the recipient's address on the right. Put the stamp in the top right corner. Keep it short since anyone can read it.
how do i fold a letter | fold letter into envelope | tri fold letter => For a standard envelope, fold the page into thirds: bring the bottom up a third, then the top down over it, so it opens like a brochure.
how do i tie my shoes faster | ian knot | fast shoe tying => Try the Ian knot: make loops with both laces at the same time, cross them and pull each through the other loop. It's faster than the usual way once practiced.
how do i keep shoelaces from untying | double knot | shoelaces come untied => Double knot the bow, or check that your knot isn't a granny knot. If the bow sits crooked across the shoe, wrap the first loop the opposite way.
how do i fix a hole in clothes | mend a hole | sew a rip => For a small rip on a seam, turn the item inside out and stitch the seam closed with small stitches. For holes in fabric, use an iron on patch or a sewn patch.
how do i change a light bulb safely | change lightbulb | lightbulb types => Turn off the switch and let the bulb cool. Use a sturdy step stool, not a chair, unscrew counterclockwise and replace it with one of the same type and wattage or lower.
how do i light a match safely | striking a match | match safety => Only with an adult's permission. Close the matchbook, hold the match away from you and strike it away from your body. Blow it out and put it in water before tossing it.
how do i check a car's tire pressure | tire pressure gauge | psi tires => Unscrew the valve cap, press a tire gauge firmly on the valve and read the number. Compare it with the pressure on the sticker inside the driver's door, and check when tires are cold.
how do i jump start a car | jumper cables | dead car battery => It's a job for an adult. Usually red clamps go on the dead battery's positive and then the good battery's positive, black on the good battery's negative, and the last black clamp on bare metal of the dead car. Then start the good car.
how do i check oil in a car | dipstick oil | car oil level => With the engine off and cool on flat ground, pull the dipstick, wipe it, push it back in and pull it out again. The oil should be between the two marks.
how do i tie a bowline knot | bowline | knot that won't slip => Make a small loop in the rope, bring the end up through it, around behind the main line, and back down through the small loop. Remember: the rabbit comes out of the hole, around the tree, and back in.
how do i coil a rope | coiling rope | rope storage => Hold one end and loop the rope in even circles over your hand or elbow. Wrap the last part around the coil a few times and tuck it through to hold it.
how do i roll a sleeping bag | stuff sack sleeping bag | pack a sleeping bag => Zip it up, fold it in half lengthwise, squeeze out air as you roll from the foot end, then tie it or slide it into its sack. Many bags can just be stuffed into a stuff sack.
how do i pack a backpack for hiking | backpack packing order | hiking pack weight => Put light, bulky things like a sleeping bag at the bottom, heavy items like food and water in the middle close to your back, and things you'll need often on top or in pockets.
how do i sharpen a pocket knife | sharpening stone | knife sharpening angle => With an adult, hold the blade at about a 20 degree angle on a sharpening stone and push it across as if slicing a thin layer, working evenly on both sides.
how do i use a pocket knife safely | knife safety | blood circle => Always cut away from your body, close the blade when you're not using it, keep a clear space around you, and pass it handle first. Ask an adult before using one.
how do i do a backbend | bridge pose | backbend kids => Warm up first. Lie on your back, put your hands by your ears with fingers toward your shoulders, feet flat, and push up into a bridge. Practice with a spotter before trying from standing.
how do i do the splits | learning splits | splits stretching => Warm up, then stretch gently every day with lunges and hamstring stretches. Slide down slowly with support only until you feel a stretch, never pain. Most people take weeks or months.
how do i throw a frisbee | frisbee backhand | frisbee forehand => For a backhand throw, grip the rim with your thumb on top, bring the disc across your body, and snap your wrist as you release it flat toward your target.
how do i shoot a free throw | free throw form | bef => Line up your feet, bend your knees, keep your elbow under the ball and push it up with one hand, guiding with the other. Follow through like reaching into a cookie jar.
how do i kick a soccer ball | instep kick | shoot a soccer ball => To pass, use the inside of your foot. To shoot, plant your other foot beside the ball, lock your ankle and strike with your laces, leaning over the ball to keep it low.
how do i serve a volleyball | underhand serve | overhand serve volleyball => For an underhand serve, hold the ball at waist height in front of your hitting arm, step forward and swing your arm like a pendulum, hitting the ball with the heel of your hand.
how do i swim freestyle | front crawl | freestyle breathing => Lie flat, kick from your hips, and reach forward with one arm at a time, pulling back under your body. Turn your head to the side to breathe every two or three strokes.
how do i dive into a pool | diving headfirst | pool dive => Only dive into deep water marked for diving with an adult around. Point your arms over your head, tuck your chin, and lean forward to enter hands first. Never dive into shallow or unknown water.
how do i ride a scooter | kick scooter | scooter tricks => Keep one foot on the deck and push with the other, then stand with both feet on as you glide. Steer with the handlebars and use the rear brake by pressing it with your foot. Wear a helmet.
how do i ride a unicycle | unicycle learning | unicycle tips => Start next to a wall or rail, keep your weight on the seat, sit up tall and pedal in small half turns. It takes many hours of practice, so be patient.
how do i do a wheelie | bike wheelie | wheelie practice => In an easy gear, ride slowly, then pedal hard while pulling up on the bars and leaning back. Cover the rear brake to stop going over backward, and wear a helmet.
how do i finger knit | finger knitting | knitting without needles => Weave yarn around your fingers in a figure eight, then lift the lower loops over the top ones and over each finger. Keep going to make a long, chunky chain.
