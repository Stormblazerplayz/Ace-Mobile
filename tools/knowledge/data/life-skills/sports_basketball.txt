# tag: sports.basketball
who invented basketball | when was basketball invented | where did basketball start => James Naismith invented basketball in December 1891 at a YMCA school in Springfield, Massachusetts. The first games used peach baskets as hoops.
what are the positions in basketball | basketball positions explained | what does a point guard do => The five positions are point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward and center. The point guard runs the offense and passes, guards shoot and handle the ball, forwards score and rebound, and the center plays near the basket.
what does a center do in basketball | center position | what is a big man in basketball => The center is usually the tallest player, playing close to the hoop to rebound, block shots, score inside and set screens.
how long is the shot clock | nba shot clock seconds | what is a shot clock => In the NBA and international FIBA games, a team has 24 seconds to shoot. In NCAA men's and women's college basketball it's 30 seconds. The ball must hit the rim to reset it.
how many fouls before you foul out | fouling out rule | how many fouls in the nba => NBA players foul out with 6 personal fouls. In college, high school and FIBA, it's 5.
what is traveling in basketball | travel violation | how many steps can you take in basketball => Traveling is moving your pivot foot or taking too many steps while holding the ball without dribbling. Usually you can take two steps after you stop dribbling, then you must pass or shoot.
what is a double dribble | double dribble rule | can you dribble with two hands => A double dribble is dribbling with both hands at once, or stopping your dribble and then starting again. Either gives the ball to the other team.
what is a carry in basketball | palming the ball | carrying violation => Carrying, or palming, is when your hand goes under the ball and you briefly hold it while dribbling, then keep dribbling. It's a turnover.
what is the three second rule | three seconds in the key | lane violation basketball => An offensive player can't stay in the painted lane for more than 3 seconds while their team has the ball. The NBA also has a defensive 3 second rule.
what is a backcourt violation | over and back basketball | half court violation => Once the offense brings the ball past half court, they can't take it back into the backcourt. Teams also have 8 seconds in the NBA and FIBA, or 10 in NCAA men's, to get the ball past half court.
what is goaltending | goaltending rule | what is basket interference => Goaltending is blocking a shot when the ball is on its way down toward the hoop, or touching it while it's directly above the rim. If the defense does it, the basket counts.
what is a free throw | how far is the free throw line | how many points is a free throw => A free throw is an unguarded shot worth 1 point, taken from a line 15 feet (4.6 m) from the backboard after certain fouls.
how far is the three point line | nba three point line distance | three point line in college => The NBA three point line is 23 feet 9 inches (7.24 m) at the top and 22 feet (6.7 m) in the corners. FIBA uses 6.75 m, and NCAA uses the same 22 feet 1.75 inches as FIBA.
how big is a basketball court | nba court size | basketball court dimensions => An NBA court is 94 feet long and 50 feet wide (about 28.7 by 15.2 m). FIBA courts are 28 by 15 m. High school courts are often 84 feet long.
what size basketball should i use | basketball sizes by age | size 6 vs size 7 basketball => Size 7 (29.5 inch) is for men and boys about 15 and older, size 6 (28.5 inch) for women and boys about 12 to 14, and size 5 (27.5 inch) for kids about 9 to 11.
what is a foul in basketball | personal foul | what counts as a foul => A personal foul is illegal contact, like pushing, holding, tripping or hitting someone's arm while they shoot. Fouls on a shooter give free throws.
what is a technical foul | technical foul basketball | what gets you a tech => A technical foul is for unsportsmanlike behavior or certain rule breaks, like arguing with refs or hanging on the rim. The other team gets a free throw.
what is a flagrant foul | flagrant 1 vs flagrant 2 | dangerous foul in basketball => A flagrant foul is unnecessary or excessive contact. In the NBA, a flagrant 2 is the most severe and gets the player ejected. The fouled team gets free throws and the ball.
what is an and one | and 1 basketball | what does and one mean => An and one is when a player is fouled while making a shot. The basket counts, and they get one free throw, a chance for a three point play, or four on a made three.
what is a charge in basketball | charge vs block | taking a charge => A charge is when an offensive player runs into a defender who had already set their position, so it's an offensive foul. If the defender was still moving, it's a blocking foul on the defense.
what is a triple double | double double meaning | triple double stats => A double double is getting 10 or more in two stat categories, like points and rebounds. A triple double is 10 or more in three, usually points, rebounds and assists.
what is a rebound | offensive vs defensive rebound | how to get more rebounds => A rebound is grabbing the ball after a missed shot. Offensive rebounds are your own team's misses, and defensive rebounds are the other team's. Box out by putting your body between your opponent and the hoop.
what is an assist | assist in basketball | how do you get an assist => An assist is a pass that leads directly to a teammate scoring.
what is a pick and roll | screen and roll | how does a pick and roll work => A teammate sets a screen, or pick, on the defender guarding the ball handler, then rolls toward the basket. The ball handler can shoot, drive, or pass to the rolling player.
what is a screen in basketball | setting a pick | moving screen foul => A screen is when an offensive player stands still in a defender's path to free up a teammate. If the screener moves into the defender, it's a moving screen foul.
what is zone defense | man to man vs zone | 2 3 zone => In man to man, each defender guards one player. In a zone, each defender guards an area, like a 2-3 zone with two players up top and three near the basket.
what is a fast break | fast break basketball | transition offense => A fast break is pushing the ball up the court quickly after a rebound or steal, before the defense sets up, for an easy shot.
what is an alley oop | alley oop meaning | how does an alley oop work => An alley oop is when a player throws the ball near the rim and a teammate jumps, catches it in the air and dunks or lays it in before landing.
what is a layup | how to do a layup | right handed layup steps => A layup is a close shot banked softly off the backboard. For a right handed layup, step right foot then left foot, jump off your left foot, lift your right knee and shoot with your right hand.
how do i shoot a basketball better | shooting form tips | bef shooting => Use BEEF: Balance with feet shoulder width, Eyes on the rim, Elbow under the ball, and Follow through with your wrist snapping down. Practice close to the hoop and move back slowly.
how do i dribble better | basketball dribbling drills | ball handling tips => Dribble with your fingertips, keep the ball low and your eyes up, and practice with both hands daily. Drills like crossovers, between the legs and two ball dribbling build control.
how do i get better at basketball | basketball practice at home | improve at basketball fast => Practice ball handling and shooting form every day, play pickup games, work on your weaker hand, and watch how good players move without the ball. Conditioning helps you play hard the whole game.
how do i play better defense in basketball | defense tips | how to guard someone => Stay low with knees bent, keep your hands active, slide your feet instead of crossing them, and stay between your player and the basket. Watch their hips, not the ball.
how long is a college basketball game | ncaa game length | halves or quarters in college basketball => NCAA men's basketball uses two 20 minute halves. NCAA women's basketball uses four 10 minute quarters. Overtime periods are 5 minutes.
how long is a high school basketball game | high school quarters length | how long is each quarter => Most US high school games use four 8 minute quarters. NBA quarters are 12 minutes and FIBA quarters are 10.
what happens if a basketball game is tied | overtime in basketball | how long is overtime in the nba => If the score is tied at the end, teams play a 5 minute overtime period, and keep playing more overtimes until someone wins.
how many timeouts in the nba | basketball timeouts | how many timeouts does each team get => In the NBA, each team gets 7 timeouts per game, with limits on how many can be used late in the fourth quarter.
what is a jump ball | how does a basketball game start | tip off => Games start with a jump ball, or tip off, where the referee tosses the ball up between two players at center court and they try to tap it to a teammate.
what is the possession arrow | alternating possession | held ball rule => In college and high school, after the opening tip, held balls and other ties are settled by an arrow that alternates which team gets the ball. The NBA uses jump balls instead.
what is a bounce pass | chest pass vs bounce pass | types of passes in basketball => A chest pass goes straight from your chest to a teammate's. A bounce pass hits the floor about two thirds of the way to them, which is harder to steal. Overhead passes go over defenders.
what is a dunk | when was dunking banned | how high do you have to jump to dunk => A dunk is putting the ball down through the hoop from above. College basketball banned dunking from 1967 to 1976. Most people need to reach about 6 inches above the 10 foot rim to dunk.
how many teams make the nba playoffs | nba playoff format | what is the play in tournament => Sixteen teams make the NBA playoffs, eight from each conference. Seeds 7 to 10 in each conference play a play in tournament for the last two spots. Every round is best of seven.
what is the nba finals | how does the nba finals work | best of seven series => The NBA Finals is the championship series between the Eastern and Western Conference winners. The first team to win four games takes the title.
who won the 2025 nba finals | 2025 nba champions | who won the nba championship in 2025 => The Oklahoma City Thunder won the 2025 NBA Finals, beating the Indiana Pacers in seven games. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander was Finals MVP.
which nba team has the most championships | most nba titles | celtics vs lakers championships => As of 2025, the Boston Celtics have the most NBA titles with 18, and the Los Angeles Lakers have 17.
who is the nba all time leading scorer | most points in nba history | who has scored the most nba points => LeBron James is the NBA's all time leading scorer. He passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar in February 2023 and, as of 2025, has more than 42,000 career points.
who scored 100 points in an nba game | most points in one game | wilt chamberlain 100 points => Wilt Chamberlain scored 100 points for the Philadelphia Warriors against the New York Knicks on March 2, 1962. It's still the NBA single game record as of 2025.
how many points did kobe score in one game | kobe 81 points | second most points in a game => Kobe Bryant scored 81 points against the Toronto Raptors on January 22, 2006, the second highest single game total in NBA history.
who has the most three pointers ever | most 3 pointers nba | steph curry three point record => Stephen Curry holds the NBA record for career three pointers. He passed Ray Allen in 2021 and became the first to make 4,000 in March 2025.
who has the most nba rings as a player | bill russell championships | most titles by one player => Bill Russell won 11 NBA championships with the Boston Celtics between 1957 and 1969, the most by any player.
how many championships did michael jordan win | michael jordan rings | jordan finals record => Michael Jordan won 6 NBA championships with the Chicago Bulls, in 1991 to 1993 and 1996 to 1998, and was Finals MVP all six times.
who has the most assists in nba history | career assists leader | john stockton assists => John Stockton holds the NBA record with 15,806 career assists, as of 2025. Chris Paul is second.
who has the most rebounds in nba history | career rebounds record | wilt rebounds => Wilt Chamberlain holds the NBA career rebound record with 23,924. He also once grabbed 55 rebounds in a single game in 1960.
what is the wnba | when did the wnba start | women's basketball league => The WNBA is the top professional women's basketball league in the US. It was founded in 1996 and played its first season in 1997.
what is march madness | how many teams are in march madness | ncaa tournament explained => March Madness is the NCAA college basketball tournament held each spring. The men's and women's tournaments each have 68 teams, with single elimination games until a champion is crowned.
what is a bracket | how do march madness brackets work | filling out a bracket => A bracket is a chart of every tournament matchup. People predict the winner of each game, and points are given for correct picks as the tournament goes on.
what is a buzzer beater | buzzer beater meaning | shot at the buzzer => A buzzer beater is a shot released just before time runs out on the clock that goes in after the buzzer sounds. It counts if it left the shooter's hand in time.
what is a turnover in basketball | turnovers meaning | how do you lose possession => A turnover is losing the ball to the other team before shooting, like a bad pass, a steal, stepping out of bounds or a violation.
what is a steal | how to get steals | steal in basketball => A steal is when a defender legally takes the ball away, by tipping a pass or poking it loose from a dribbler, without fouling.
what is a block | blocked shot rules | how to block a shot => A block is when a defender legally deflects a shot attempt. Jump straight up with your arm up, time it, and don't hit the shooter's arm or touch the ball on its way down.
what is the paint in basketball | the key or lane | why is it called the paint => The paint, also called the key or lane, is the painted rectangle under the basket. It got the name because it's often painted a different color.
what is a hook shot | sky hook | who invented the sky hook => A hook shot is a one handed shot swung up in an arc with the body turned sideways to protect it. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's version, the sky hook, was almost impossible to block.
what is a euro step | euro step move | how to do a euro step => A Euro step is a layup move where you take one step in one direction, then a quick second step the other way to get around a defender, all within the legal two steps.
what is a crossover dribble | how to do a crossover | best crossover tips => A crossover is quickly switching the dribble from one hand to the other in front of you to change direction and get past a defender. Keep it low and fast, and sell the fake with your shoulders.
how tall do you have to be to play basketball | can short people play basketball | shortest nba player => You don't have to be tall to play and have fun. Muggsy Bogues, the shortest NBA player ever at 5 feet 3 inches, played 14 NBA seasons.
who are the tallest nba players ever | tallest basketball player | 7 foot 7 nba => The tallest NBA players ever were Gheorghe Muresan and Manute Bol, both listed at 7 feet 7 inches (2.31 m).
what is an nba draft | how does the nba draft work | nba draft lottery => The NBA Draft is when teams pick new players, usually from college or overseas, over two rounds. The worst teams have the best odds in a lottery for the top picks.
what is the nba all star game | how are all stars picked | nba all star weekend => The All Star Game is a mid season exhibition with the league's best players, chosen by fan, player and media votes plus coaches. All Star weekend also has the dunk contest and three point contest.
what is mvp in basketball | nba most valuable player | who won the 2025 nba mvp => The MVP award goes to the best regular season player. As of 2025, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander of the Oklahoma City Thunder won the 2024 to 2025 MVP.
what is 3 on 3 basketball | 3x3 basketball rules | 3x3 olympics => 3x3 is a half court game with three players per team, a 12 second shot clock and a game to 21 points or 10 minutes. It became an Olympic sport at the Tokyo 2020 Games.
what is a full court press | press defense | how to beat a press => A full court press is when the defense guards the other team tightly the whole length of the court to force turnovers. Beat it with quick passes, spacing and players coming back to help.
what is the difference between nba and fiba rules | international basketball rules | olympic basketball rules => FIBA games have 10 minute quarters, a slightly shorter three point line, 5 fouls to foul out, and allow players to touch the ball once it hits the rim. NBA games have 12 minute quarters and 6 fouls.
what is a bank shot | using the backboard | when to bank a shot => A bank shot bounces off the backboard into the hoop. It's useful from angles near the basket, aiming at the top corner of the small square.
what does the square on the backboard do | backboard square meaning | shooting square => The rectangle on the backboard helps players aim bank shots and layups. Hitting its upper corner on the way up often guides the ball in.
how do i make a basketball team | basketball tryout tips | how to prepare for tryouts => Work on fundamentals like dribbling with both hands, shooting form and defense, get in shape for running, and at tryouts hustle, communicate and stay positive. Coaches love effort and coachability.
what does pivot foot mean | how to pivot in basketball | pivoting rules => After stopping, one foot is your pivot foot and must stay planted while you turn on it. You can step around with the other foot to protect the ball or find a pass.
what is a jump stop | two foot stop basketball | jump stop benefits => A jump stop is landing on both feet at the same time after a dribble. It lets you choose either foot as your pivot, which gives you more options and fewer travels.
