# tag: life.feelings.everyday
how do i deal with a friend who brags | bragging friend | show off friend => Bragging often comes from wanting to feel good enough. Be happy for them when it's real, change the subject when it's too much, and you can kindly say it bugs you.
how do i deal with a bossy friend | bossy friend | friend always in charge => Speak up calmly, like "Let's take turns picking," and suggest ideas of your own. If they never listen, spend time with other friends too.
how do i deal with a friend who copies my work | friend copying homework | cheating friend => Say no kindly, like "I can help explain it, but I can't let you copy." Sharing answers can get you both in trouble, so offer to study together instead.
how do i deal with being teased about my name | teased about name | name jokes => Try a calm, bored response like "Yep, that's my name," since teasers want a reaction. If it keeps happening, tell a teacher or trusted adult.
how do i deal with being teased about my glasses | teased glasses | braces teasing => Glasses help you see, and lots of people wear them. Act unbothered or confident, and tell a trusted adult if the teasing doesn't stop.
how do i deal with acne embarrassment | acne confidence | pimples embarrassing => Acne is super common in the preteen and teen years, so you're far from alone. A gentle face wash routine helps, and a doctor can help with acne that bothers you a lot.
how do i deal with sports pressure from parents | parents pressure sports | sports parents => Pick a calm time to tell them how the pressure feels and what you enjoy about the sport. Most parents want you to be happy and just need to hear it.
how do i deal with a performance slump | slump | playing badly lately => Slumps happen to every athlete. Go back to basics, focus on effort instead of results, get good sleep and ask your coach for one thing to work on.
how do i deal with a sports injury emotionally | injured sad | can't play injured => Feeling sad or frustrated is normal. Follow your recovery plan, stay connected with your team, set small goals, and talk to someone if you feel down for a long time.
how do i deal with a project not working | project failed | robot won't work frustrated => Take a break, then break the problem into small parts and test each one. Write down what you've tried. Every builder hits failures, and they're how you learn.
how do i deal with frustration when coding | coding frustration | bug won't go away => Step away for a few minutes, then explain the problem out loud or to a rubber duck. Read the error message carefully and test one small change at a time.
how do i stay motivated on a big project | motivation project | lose interest project => Break it into small milestones, celebrate each one, and work on it a little every day. Sharing progress with a friend helps you keep going.
how do i deal with negative comments on my videos | mean youtube comments | hate comments => Don't reply to mean comments. Delete, block or filter them, and focus on helpful feedback and the people who enjoy your work. Tell a trusted adult if someone threatens you.
how do i deal with low views | no views | nobody watches my videos => Almost every creator starts with low views. Focus on making things you enjoy and getting better each time, and learn from what does well.
how do i deal with comparing my work to others online | others are better | online comparison => Remember you're comparing your behind the scenes to someone else's highlight reel. Compare yourself to your past work instead and notice how you've grown.
how do i deal with liking things others think are childish | childish interests | outgrow hobbies => It's okay to like what you like at any age. Real friends accept your interests, and lots of adults still love cartoons, toys and games.
what is neurodiversity | neurodivergent | adhd autism differences => Neurodiversity is the idea that brains naturally work in different ways, including people with ADHD, autism or dyslexia. Different brains have different strengths and challenges.
how do i deal with a change in routine | change in plans upset | routine change => Ask for details about what's changing, make a simple plan, and keep parts of your routine that stay the same. It's okay to feel unsettled at first.
how do i deal with fear of the dentist | dentist fear | scared dentist => Tell the dentist you're nervous so they can explain each step. Breathing slowly, bringing headphones and agreeing on a hand signal to pause can help.
how do i deal with fear of flying | scared to fly | plane fear => Learning how safe flying is helps, since it's one of the safest ways to travel. Bumps are normal and planes are built for them. Slow breathing and distractions help.
how do i deal with climate anxiety | climate worry | worried about the planet => Feeling worried about the planet shows you care. Focus on actions you can take, like saving energy or joining a group, and talk with adults about your worries. Many people are working on solutions.
how do i deal with a parent losing their job | parent lost job | family money worries => It's normal to worry. Ask your parents what's going on in a way that makes sense, help where you can, and remember it's not your job to fix it.
how do i deal with living in two homes | two houses | shared custody => Keep important things like chargers and favorite items in both places, use a calendar for the schedule, and tell your parents what helps you feel settled.
what do i do if someone posts a photo of me | photo posted without permission | embarrassing photo online => Ask them to take it down. If they won't, report it on the app and tell a trusted adult, especially if it's embarrassing or private.
how do i deal with being pranked | prank went too far | mean prank => If a prank hurt or embarrassed you, it's okay to say "That wasn't funny to me." Pranks that hurt, scare or humiliate people aren't harmless, so tell an adult if it keeps happening.
how do i deal with a sibling who takes my stuff | sibling takes my things | privacy from sibling => Ask calmly for them to ask first, keep special things put away, and agree on rules with your parents' help if it keeps happening.
how do i deal with an older sibling who is mean | mean older sibling | older brother bullies => Stay calm and walk away when you can. Tell your parents if it's often or hurtful, since being mean or hurting you isn't okay just because they're older.
how do i recharge my social battery | social battery | tired after socializing => After lots of socializing, take quiet time doing something you enjoy alone, like reading, drawing or building. It's normal to need recovery time.
how do i celebrate small wins | small wins | celebrate progress => Notice progress, like finishing a chapter or landing a trick, and mark it with something you enjoy, like a favorite snack or telling a friend. Small wins add up.
