# tag: life.howto.projects
how do i build a cardboard fort | cardboard fort | box fort => Collect big boxes from appliances or moving, cut doors and windows with an adult's help, and connect boxes with packing tape or slotted joints. Decorate with markers and add a blanket roof.
how do i build a blanket fort | blanket fort | pillow fort => Drape sheets or blankets over chairs, a table or the couch, hold them with clothespins or heavy books, and fill the inside with pillows, string lights and snacks.
how do i build a treehouse | treehouse | building a treehouse safely => It's a project for adults and kids together. Pick a healthy, strong tree, build the platform low, use proper bolts made for trees, add sturdy railings, and check local rules first.
how do i build a skate ramp | diy skate ramp | quarter pipe build => With an adult, start with a small launch ramp or quarter pipe made from plywood over a frame of 2x4s, with curved sides cut from plywood. Plans online give exact measurements.
how do i build a pump track in my yard | backyard pump track | dirt jumps backyard => Get permission first, plan a loop of small rollers and two banked turns, pile and pack dirt firmly, and keep it damp while packing. Start small and add features once the basic loop rides well.
how do i build a marble run | marble run | cardboard marble run => Tape paper towel tubes, cut pool noodles or folded cardboard strips to a wall or box in a zigzag, angled downhill, with funnels and drops. Test as you go so marbles don't jump off.
how do i make a mousetrap car | mousetrap car | mousetrap car tips => Attach a mousetrap to a light frame with wheels, tie a string from the trap's arm to the drive axle, and wind it up. A longer lever arm and big, light wheels go farther. Watch your fingers.
how do i make a paper bridge | paper bridge challenge | strongest paper bridge => Fold paper into shapes like accordions, tubes or triangles, which are much stronger than flat paper. Test how many coins it holds between two books.
how do i build a spaghetti tower | spaghetti marshmallow tower | marshmallow challenge => In the marshmallow challenge, teams build the tallest freestanding tower from dry spaghetti, tape and string with a marshmallow on top. Triangles make strong bases, and test early.
how do i make a hovercraft | cd hovercraft | balloon hovercraft => Glue a bottle cap with a hole in it onto the center hole of an old CD, blow up a balloon and stretch it over the cap. Open the cap and air pushes out underneath, letting it glide.
how do i make a periscope | periscope | diy periscope => Cut openings at opposite ends of a long box or milk carton, on opposite sides, and fix small mirrors inside at 45 degree angles facing each other. You can see over walls.
how do i make a kaleidoscope | kaleidoscope | diy kaleidoscope => Tape three equal strips of mirror card into a triangle tube, put it in a paper towel tube, and cap one end with clear plastic holding beads or sequins. Look through the other end and turn.
how do i make a weather vane | weather vane | wind direction => Push a pencil eraser side up into a base, stick a pin through a straw into the eraser, and attach a paper arrowhead and a bigger tail. Mark N, S, E and W on the base with a compass.
how do i make an anemometer | anemometer | wind speed cups => Attach four paper cups to the ends of two crossed straws, pin the center to a pencil eraser so it spins, and mark one cup. Count spins per minute to compare wind speeds.
how do i make a worm farm | worm farm | vermicomposting => Layer damp soil and sand in a clear jar or bin with air holes, add a few earthworms and some food scraps, and keep it dark and moist. Release the worms outside after a few weeks.
how do i make a bug hotel | bug hotel | insect hotel => Fill a wooden box or stacked bricks with hollow sticks, bamboo, pinecones, bark and straw, and place it in a sheltered spot. It gives bees and helpful insects places to live.
how do i make a butterfly garden | butterfly garden | plants for butterflies => Plant nectar flowers like zinnias, coneflowers and lantana for adults, and host plants like milkweed for caterpillars. Choose plants native to your area and skip pesticides.
how do i build a raised garden bed | raised bed | garden bed wood => Build a rectangle frame of untreated boards about 30 cm (12 in) tall, set it in a sunny spot, line the bottom with cardboard and fill it with garden soil and compost.
how do i make a bird bath | bird bath | diy bird bath => Set a wide, shallow dish or plant saucer on a stump or upside down pot, add a rock for perching, and change the water every few days to keep it clean.
how do i make a scarecrow | scarecrow | build a scarecrow => Stuff old clothes with straw or newspaper, tie off the ends, attach a stuffed sack or pillowcase head with a drawn face, and tie it to a wooden cross frame.
how do i make a snow globe | snow globe | diy snow globe => Glue a small waterproof figure to the inside of a jar lid, fill the jar with water, a little glycerin and glitter, then seal the lid tight with glue and flip it over.
how do i make a mask | paper mask | cardboard mask => Cut a mask shape from cardstock or a paper plate, cut eye holes, decorate it, and attach elastic or a stick to hold it. Paper mache over a balloon makes a sturdier one.
how do i make a prop sword | cardboard sword | foam sword => Cut a sword shape from cardboard or foam board, layer pieces for strength, wrap the handle with tape, and paint the blade silver. Foam is safer for play fights.
how do i make a marionette | string puppet | marionette strings => Make a puppet with jointed parts from cardboard, felt or wood, tie strings to the head, hands and knees, and connect them to a cross shaped wooden control bar.
how do i make a stop motion set | stop motion set | miniature set => Build a small scene on a table with cardboard, clay and craft supplies, light it with a lamp that won't flicker, and keep the camera on a stand so it never moves.
how do i make a diorama | diorama | shoebox diorama => Turn a shoebox on its side, paint or print a background on the back wall, and add figures and scenery made from clay, paper and natural items to show a scene.
how do i make a solar system model | solar system model | planets model => Use foam balls or clay in different sizes for the Sun and eight planets, paint them, and hang them from a hanger or mount them on sticks in order from the Sun.
how do i make a model of a cell | cell model | edible cell model => Use a container or cake for the cell and candies, clay or small objects for the parts, like a gumball nucleus and gummy worm mitochondria. Label each part.
how do i make a bubble wand | bubble wand | giant bubble wand => Bend a pipe cleaner or wire into a loop with a handle. For giant bubbles, tie a loop of cotton string between two sticks so it opens wide.
how do i make sidewalk chalk | homemade chalk | sidewalk chalk paint => Mix plaster of Paris with water and a little paint, pour it into molds like toilet paper tubes lined with paper, and let it dry for a day or two.
how do i make a chalk art 3d illusion | 3d chalk art | chalk illusion => Draw a shape stretched long in the direction away from the viewer, add strong shading and cast shadows, and view it from one spot. Photograph it from that angle.
how do i make a zoetrope | zoetrope | spinning animation => Make a cardboard drum with evenly spaced slits, place a strip of drawings showing a motion in steps inside, and spin it while looking through the slits.
how do i make a thaumatrope | thaumatrope | spinning card illusion => Draw two related pictures on opposite sides of a paper disc, like a bird and a cage, attach strings to both sides and twirl it fast. Your eyes blend them into one image.
how do i make a vision board | vision board | goals board => Collect pictures and words that show your goals and dreams, arrange them on a poster or corkboard, and hang it where you'll see it every day.
how do i make a board game | design board game | homemade board game => Choose a theme and goal, sketch a board path or grid, write simple rules, make cards and pieces, and playtest with friends to fix what's boring or unfair.
how do i make a card game | invent card game | card game rules => Decide the goal and how you win, design a small set of cards with clear actions, write short rules, then playtest and adjust until it's fun and balanced.
how do i make an escape room at home | diy escape room | home escape room => Pick a theme, write a story, and create a chain of puzzles where each one unlocks a clue to the next, like codes, hidden keys and riddles. Test it and set a time limit.
how do i make a treasure map | treasure map | old map look => Draw the area with landmarks, mark a path with an X, then crumple the paper and stain it with cold tea or coffee to make it look old.
how do i make a secret compartment | hidden compartment | secret box => Hollow out an old book by cutting a box in the pages with a craft knife (ask an adult), or add a false bottom to a box or drawer with a piece of cardboard.
how do i make a lock box | combination box | puzzle box => Use a small box with a cheap combination lock or padlock, or make a puzzle box from cardboard with sliding panels that must be moved in the right order.
how do i make a maze | draw a maze | maze puzzle => Draw a solution path first, then add dead end branches around it and fill the rest of the grid with walls. Graph paper makes it easier.
how do i make a crossword | crossword puzzle | make crossword => Pick words and clues, place the longest word first on graph paper, cross other words where letters match, then number the starts and write clues for across and down.
how do i make a word search | word search | make word search => Make a grid, write your words in it across, down, diagonally and backward, then fill empty squares with random letters and list the words below.
how do i make a quiz | trivia quiz | make a quiz game => Pick a topic, write clear questions with one right answer each, mix easy and hard ones, add multiple choice options, and keep an answer key.
how do i make a domino run | domino chain | domino tricks => Stand dominoes on a flat surface about two thirds of a domino's height apart. Leave gaps every so often while building so an accident doesn't knock them all down.
how do i make a rube goldberg machine | rube goldberg | chain reaction machine => Chain simple actions together, like a ball rolling down a ramp that tips dominoes that pull a string, to do a simple task in an overly complicated way. Test each step separately.
how do i make a cardboard arcade game | cardboard arcade | caine's arcade => Cut holes in boxes for skee ball, build a claw machine from cardboard and string, or make a basketball toss. The famous Caine's Arcade started this way.
how do i make a pinball machine | cardboard pinball | diy pinball => Use a shallow box tilted slightly, add flippers made from cardboard and brass fasteners, bumpers from bottle caps and a plunger from a pen spring or rubber band.
how do i make a cardboard guitar | box guitar | rubber band guitar => Cut a hole in a box, stretch rubber bands of different thicknesses across it, and glue a cardboard tube as the neck. Thicker or looser bands make lower notes.
how do i make a drum | homemade drum | diy drum => Stretch a balloon or thick paper tightly over an empty can or oatmeal container and secure it with rubber bands or tape. Use pencils or wooden spoons as sticks.
how do i make a shaker | rice shaker | maraca craft => Fill a plastic egg, small bottle or paper roll with rice, beans or beads, seal it well and decorate it. Different fillings make different sounds.
how do i make a pan flute | straw pan flute | pan pipes => Cut drinking straws into lengths that get shorter step by step, line them up and tape them together. Blow across the tops, and shorter straws make higher notes.
