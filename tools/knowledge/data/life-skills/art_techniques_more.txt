# tag: life.art.techniques
what is dry brush technique | dry brushing | dry brush painting => Dry brushing is painting with a brush that has very little paint and almost no water, dragged lightly so it skips over the paper's texture. It's great for grass, wood and fur.
what is a wash in watercolor | flat wash | graded wash => A wash is a smooth layer of watery paint over a large area. A flat wash is one even color, and a graded wash fades from dark to light, like a sky.
how do i fix a watercolor mistake | lift watercolor | watercolor correction => While it's wet, soak up paint with a clean damp brush or tissue. Once dry, wet the spot gently and blot to lift some color. Some pigments stain, so test first.
what is masking fluid | masking fluid watercolor | save whites => Masking fluid is a rubbery liquid you paint on areas you want to stay white. After painting over it and letting it dry, you rub it off to reveal clean paper. Use an old brush.
what is underpainting | underpainting | tone the canvas => An underpainting is a first layer, often in one color, that sets up the light and dark values before you add full color on top.
what is scumbling | scumbling technique | broken color => Scumbling is brushing a thin, broken layer of light paint over a dry darker layer so bits of the bottom show through, softening and adding texture.
what is sgraffito | sgraffito | scratching paint => Sgraffito is scratching through a top layer of paint, clay or pastel to reveal the color underneath, making lines and texture.
what is stippling | stippling drawing | dots shading => Stippling is shading with lots of tiny dots. Dots packed close look dark and spread out dots look light. It takes patience but looks striking.
what is blending stump | tortillon | blending stump use => A blending stump, or tortillon, is a tightly rolled paper stick used to smudge and blend pencil or charcoal smoothly without getting skin oils on the paper.
what pencil grades mean | 2b vs hb | pencil hardness => H pencils are hard and light, and B pencils are soft and dark. HB is in the middle, like a normal school pencil. Artists use 2H for sketching lines and 2B to 6B for shading.
what is charcoal drawing | vine vs compressed charcoal | charcoal smudging => Charcoal makes rich, dark marks that smudge easily. Vine charcoal is light and erases well, and compressed charcoal is darker. A spray fixative stops smudging when you're done.
what are soft pastels vs oil pastels | oil pastels | soft pastels => Soft pastels are powdery sticks that blend like chalk and need fixative. Oil pastels are waxy and creamy, smear less, and are great for bright, bold layers.
what are alcohol markers | copic markers | alcohol vs water markers => Alcohol markers use alcohol based ink that dries fast and blends smoothly without streaks, popular for illustration and manga. They bleed through paper, so use marker paper.
what is three point perspective | three point perspective | worm's eye view => Three point perspective adds a third vanishing point above or below the horizon, making tall buildings look like they tower up or drop away, like a bird's or worm's eye view.
what is atmospheric perspective | aerial perspective | distant mountains blue => Atmospheric perspective is making faraway things lighter, bluer and less detailed, because the air between you and them softens them. It makes landscapes feel deep.
what is the golden ratio in art | golden ratio | divine proportion => The golden ratio is about 1.618 to 1. Some artists use it to place things in a pleasing way, though how much famous artists used it is often exaggerated.
what is chiaroscuro | chiaroscuro | strong light and dark => Chiaroscuro is the use of strong contrast between light and dark to make figures look 3D and dramatic. Caravaggio and Rembrandt were masters of it.
what is a cast shadow | cast shadow vs form shadow | core shadow => A cast shadow is the shadow an object throws onto other surfaces. The form shadow is the darker side of the object itself, and the core shadow is the darkest band on it.
what is a highlight in drawing | highlight | reflected light => A highlight is the brightest spot on an object where light hits it most directly. Leaving it white or erasing it out makes things look shiny and round.
what is balance in art | symmetrical balance | asymmetrical balance => Balance is how visual weight is spread across an artwork. Symmetrical balance mirrors both sides, while asymmetrical balance uses different things that feel equal in weight.
what is rhythm in art | pattern and rhythm | movement in art => Rhythm is the repeating of shapes, colors or lines to create a sense of movement that leads your eye through the artwork, like a beat in music.
how do i draw a body | figure proportions | heads tall => Start with a stick figure for the pose, then add simple shapes: an oval for the chest, a box for the hips and cylinders for limbs. Adults are about 7 to 8 heads tall.
how do i draw a horse | horse drawing | horse legs => Start with circles for the chest and hips connected by a back line, then a smaller shape for the head on a thick neck. Horse legs bend like a backward knee at the hock, so use reference.
how do i draw a cat | drawing cats | cat sketch => Start with a circle for the head and a bean shape for the body. Add triangle ears near the top of the head, eyes about halfway down, and a long tail for balance.
how do i draw a room | drawing a room | interior perspective => Use one point perspective: draw the back wall as a rectangle, put a vanishing point inside it, then draw lines from its corners outward for the floor, ceiling and side walls.
how do i draw a city | cityscape drawing | buildings skyline => Draw a horizon line and vanishing points, then sketch buildings as boxes using perspective lines. Add windows, signs and details last, and make far buildings simpler.
what is a speech bubble | speech balloon | thought bubble => A speech bubble holds what a character says, with a tail pointing to the speaker. Thought bubbles are cloud shaped with little circles leading to the thinker.
what is a webcomic | webcomic | post comics online => A webcomic is a comic published online, often in episodes. Many artists start by posting short strips regularly, and some build big audiences.
what is art block | art block | no drawing ideas => Art block is feeling stuck or uninspired. Try drawing from life, using prompts, copying a favorite artist to study, switching mediums or taking a short break.
what is a drawing prompt | drawing prompts | inktober => A drawing prompt is a word or idea that gives you something to draw. Challenges like Inktober give one prompt per day for a month.
what is a drawing tablet | drawing tablet | pen display => A drawing tablet lets you draw on a computer with a pen. Some are plain pads you look away from, and pen displays let you draw right on a screen.
what is line art | line art | inking => Line art is the clean outline drawing of an image, usually done over a rough sketch before coloring. Doing it in ink is called inking.
what is linocut | linocut | lino carving => Linocut is carving a design into a block of linoleum, rolling ink on the raised parts and pressing paper on it to print. Carve away from your hand, since the tools are sharp.
what is the rule of thirds in drawing | thirds grid art | composition thirds => Imagine your page split into a 3 by 3 grid. Placing your main subject along those lines or where they cross usually looks more interesting than putting it dead center.
what is an art portfolio | art portfolio | portfolio for art school => A portfolio is a collection of your best artwork, used to apply to art programs or jobs. Pick strong pieces that show your skills and include some drawings from life.
what is an art show | art exhibit | gallery opening => An art show or exhibit displays artwork for people to view, at a gallery, school or fair. Many schools and libraries host student shows.
how do i sign my artwork | sign artwork | where to sign painting => Sign neatly in a bottom corner where it doesn't distract, using your name, initials or an artist signature. Some artists add the year.
how do i frame art | framing art | mat board => Pick a frame a little bigger than your art, add a mat to give it space, and use acid free materials so the paper doesn't yellow. Ready made frames work fine.
how do i store artwork | store drawings | protect art => Store drawings flat in a folder or portfolio, with paper between them so they don't smudge. Keep them away from sunlight, damp places and heat.
