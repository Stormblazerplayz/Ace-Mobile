# tag: life.sports.skills
how do i shoot a layup | layup steps | right handed layup => For a right handed layup, step right foot then left, jump off your left foot, lift your right knee and gently bank the ball off the top corner of the square on the backboard. Flip it for lefty.
how do i dunk | how to dunk | dunk height => Dunking takes jumping high enough to get your hand above the 3 m (10 ft) rim. Practice on a lower hoop, build leg strength with jumps and squats, and work on grip and timing.
how do i trap a soccer ball | first touch | receive a pass soccer => Get in line with the ball and cushion it with the inside of your foot, thigh or chest by relaxing as it arrives. A good first touch pushes it slightly where you want to go next.
how do i play goalie in soccer | goalkeeper tips | diving save => Stay on the balls of your feet, center yourself between the ball and goal, and keep your hands ready. Catch with your hands in a W shape and talk to your defenders.
how do i do a step over | step over soccer | soccer moves => Swing your foot around the front of the ball without touching it, shift your weight as if going that way, then push the ball off in the other direction with your other foot.
how do i pitch a baseball | pitching basics | pitch count kids => Stand on the rubber, step toward home plate with your glove side foot, rotate your hips, and bring your arm over the top, following through toward the catcher. Youth leagues limit pitch counts to protect arms.
how do i bunt | sacrifice bunt | bunting baseball => Square up or pivot to face the pitcher, slide your top hand up the bat to the label, hold the bat level at the top of the strike zone and let the ball hit it softly.
how do i slide into a base | sliding baseball | feet first slide => For a feet first slide, start a few steps out, bend one leg under the other like a figure 4, lean back and slide on your backside with your hands up. Learn it with a coach on soft ground first.
how do i punt a football | punting | punt drop => Hold the ball out at waist height, take two steps, drop it onto your foot and kick with the top of your foot, swinging your leg up and through.
how do i kick a field goal | place kick | soccer style kick => A holder places the ball upright. Approach at an angle, plant your non kicking foot beside the ball, and strike just below the middle with the top of your foot, following through toward the target.
how do i tackle safely | safe tackling | heads up tackling => Keep your head up and to the side, never leading with the top of your helmet. Hit with your shoulder, wrap your arms around the ball carrier and drive with your legs.
how do i hit a forehand | tennis forehand | forehand grip => Turn your shoulders sideways as the ball comes, swing low to high, make contact in front of your body and follow through over your opposite shoulder.
how do i hit a backhand | tennis backhand | two handed backhand => Turn your shoulders so your back is partly toward the net, swing low to high and contact the ball in front of you. Many beginners use two hands for control.
how do i volley in tennis | net volley | tennis volley => Near the net, keep your racket up in front, take a short punch at the ball before it bounces, and don't swing big. Use your feet to step toward it.
how do i hold a ping pong paddle | table tennis grip | shakehand grip => The shakehand grip is most common: hold the handle like shaking hands, with your index finger along the bottom of the back rubber and your thumb on the front.
how do i put spin on a ping pong ball | topspin table tennis | backspin serve => Brush the ball instead of hitting it flat. Brush up the back for topspin and under the ball for backspin. The faster the brush, the more spin.
how do i putt | putting tips | golf putting stroke => Set your eyes over the ball, rock your shoulders like a pendulum with your wrists still, and swing back and through the same distance. Focus on speed as much as line.
how do i chip in golf | chip shot | chipping => Use a short swing with your weight on your front foot and hands ahead of the ball, so it pops up briefly and then rolls toward the hole.
how do i bowl a strike | bowling strike tips | pocket shot => For a righty, aim for the pocket between the 1 and 3 pins. Watch the arrows on the lane instead of the pins, and keep the same approach and release every time.
how do i hook a bowling ball | bowling hook | spin bowling ball => Keep your wrist firm and release with your thumb coming out first, then roll your fingers up and to the side, like a handshake. The spin makes the ball curve into the pins.
how do i hold a bowling ball | bowling grip | finger holes => Put your middle and ring fingers in the two finger holes and your thumb fully in the thumb hole. Pick a ball heavy enough to control but light enough that your arm doesn't strain.
how do i swim backstroke | backstroke technique | backstroke flags => Float on your back with your ears in the water, kick steadily from the hips and rotate your arms one at a time, entering pinky first. Flags overhead warn you that the wall is 5 m away.
how do i swim breaststroke | breaststroke kick | frog kick => Pull your hands out and around in a heart shape, breathe as you pull, then kick with a frog style whip kick and glide with arms stretched forward.
how do i do a long jump | long jump technique | long jump takeoff => Sprint down the runway, hit the board with your strongest foot without stepping over the line, drive your knee up and swing your arms, then reach your feet forward to land.
how do i run hurdles | hurdle technique | lead leg hurdles => Attack the hurdle with your lead leg straight out, snap your trail leg out to the side and over, and keep your body leaning forward. Most hurdlers take three steps between hurdles.
how do i skate backward | skating backwards | backward crossovers => Bend your knees, push out with one skate in a C shape, then the other, making a series of curves like a lemon shape. Look over your shoulder to see where you're going.
how do i fall safely snowboarding | falling snowboard | wrist guards => Fall onto your butt when falling backward, and onto your knees and forearms when falling forward. Don't reach out with straight arms, and wrist guards help a lot.
how do i surf | learn to surf | pop up surfing => Take a lesson on a soft foam board. Paddle hard as a small wave comes, then pop up in one move to a low stance with feet sideways and eyes forward.
how do i paddleboard | stand up paddle | sup basics => Start on your knees in calm water, then stand up one foot at a time with feet hip width apart. Keep your eyes on the horizon, not your feet, and wear a leash and life jacket.
how do i kayak | kayak paddling | kayak stroke => Hold the paddle with both hands about shoulder width apart, dip one blade near your toes and pull back to your hip, then switch sides. Use your core, not just your arms.
how do i row a boat | rowing a rowboat | oars technique => Sit facing the back, dip both oars in, lean back as you pull the handles toward your chest, then lift the oars out and reach forward. Pull harder on one side to turn.
how do i cast a fishing rod | casting spinning reel | cast fishing line => On a spinning reel, hold the line with your finger, open the bail, bring the rod back to about 1 o'clock, flick it forward and let go of the line as the rod points ahead.
how do i tie a fishing knot | improved clinch knot | hook knot => The improved clinch knot is popular: thread the line through the hook eye, wrap it around itself 5 to 7 times, pass the end through the small loop near the eye and then through the big loop, wet it and pull tight.
how do i bait a hook | worm on hook | live bait => Thread a worm onto the hook several times so it covers most of the hook but leaves a little dangling. Be careful of the sharp point and barb.
how do i climb a rock wall | climbing technique | climbing feet => Use your legs to push yourself up instead of pulling with your arms, keep your hips close to the wall, and place your feet carefully on holds. Climb with a trained belayer.
how do i belay | belaying | belay device => Belaying is managing the rope that protects a climber, using a belay device to catch falls. It must be learned in person from a certified instructor and checked before you belay anyone.
how do i do a lunge | lunge form | walking lunges => Step forward with one foot and lower until both knees bend to about 90 degrees, keeping your front knee over your ankle. Push back up and switch legs.
how do i do a burpee | burpee steps | burpees form => From standing, squat and put your hands down, jump your feet back into a plank, do a push up if you want, jump your feet back in, then jump up with arms overhead.
how do i do a sit up | sit up vs crunch | crunches form => Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, cross your arms on your chest and curl up using your stomach muscles. Don't pull on your neck.
how do i do a cartwheel on one hand | one handed cartwheel | aerial cartwheel => Once your two handed cartwheel is straight and strong, practice placing only your first hand down, kicking your legs over faster. Use a mat and a coach.
how do i do a round off | round off gymnastics | round off steps => Run, hurdle step, place both hands down like a cartwheel, snap your legs together over the top and land on both feet facing back the way you came. Learn it with a coach.
how do i do a back walkover | back walkover | walkover steps => From standing, lift one leg, arch back into a bridge, then kick your legs over one at a time to stand. You need a strong bridge and flexibility first, and a spotter.
how do i do a front flip | front flip | front tuck => Learn front flips with a coach on a trampoline, into a foam pit or onto soft mats. You jump up, tuck tight and rotate forward, and practice on hard ground can cause serious injuries.
how do i do a back handspring | back handspring | back handspring drills => A back handspring should be learned with a gymnastics coach and spotter. Drills include strong bridges, handstands and back handspring practice over a barrel or mat.
how do i do a kip | kip bars | glide kip => A glide kip on bars starts with a swing forward, bringing your toes to the bar at the end of the glide, then pulling your legs up along the bar like pulling on pants as you pull up to front support.
how do i jump rope tricks | double unders | criss cross jump rope => Once basic jumping is easy, try crossing your arms in front (criss cross), side swings, or double unders, where the rope passes under your feet twice in one jump.
how do i hula hoop | hula hooping | keep hula hoop up => Hold the hoop against your back, give it a spin, and shift your weight forward and back, not in circles, to keep it up. A heavier, bigger hoop is easier to learn with.
how do i spin a basketball on my finger | spin ball on finger | finger spin => Spin the ball up into the air with a quick flick, catch it on the tip of your index finger, and keep it going by tapping the side with your other hand.
how do i ride a ripstik | ripstik | caster board => Start next to a wall, put your front foot on the front deck, push off, add your back foot and twist your hips and shoulders back and forth to move forward. Wear a helmet and pads.
how do i do an ollie on a scooter | scooter bunny hop | scooter jump => Roll forward, crouch, then jump while pulling up the handlebars and lifting your back foot so the deck rises with you. Keep your feet on the deck and land with bent knees.
