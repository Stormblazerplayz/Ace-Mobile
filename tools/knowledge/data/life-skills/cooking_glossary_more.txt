# tag: life.cooking.glossary
why save pasta water | starchy pasta water | what is pasta water for => The starchy cooking water helps sauce cling to pasta and turn silky. Scoop out a cup before draining and stir in a splash at a time.
what does sear mean | how to sear meat | searing locks in juices => Searing is browning the surface of food in a very hot pan for flavor and color. It doesn't actually seal in juices, but the browned crust tastes great.
how do i thicken soup | soup too thin | thickening a stew => Simmer it uncovered so water evaporates, blend part of it, or stir in a cornstarch slurry, mashed potato, beans or a little roux.
what is carryover cooking | carryover heat | meat keeps cooking after => Carryover cooking is food continuing to cook after you take it off the heat, as heat keeps moving inward. Big roasts can rise several degrees, so remove them a little early.
what is a dry rub | spice rub | rub vs marinade => A dry rub is a mix of salt, spices, herbs and sometimes sugar rubbed onto meat before cooking. It forms a flavorful crust, while a marinade is wet.
what does fold mean in baking | folding in egg whites | fold vs stir => Folding is gently mixing with a spatula by cutting down through the middle and lifting the batter over itself, so you keep air bubbles in.
why should ingredients be room temperature for baking | room temperature eggs | softened butter => Room temperature butter, eggs and milk blend smoothly and trap air better. Cold eggs can make creamed butter seize and curdle.
how do i know bread dough is kneaded enough | windowpane test | kneading bread => Kneaded dough is smooth and springs back when poked. For the windowpane test, stretch a small piece thin; if light shows through without tearing, it's ready.
how do i keep fried food crispy | soggy fried food | wire rack fried food => Drain fried food on a wire rack instead of paper towels, don't cover it, and keep batches warm in an oven at about 95°C (200°F).
what happens if oil gets too hot | oil smoking | oil too hot => Oil that smokes has passed its smoke point, tastes burnt and can catch fire. Turn off the heat. If it flames, cover it with a lid and never use water.
how do i know oil is hot enough to fry | oil temperature test | wooden spoon oil test => Most frying is done at 175 to 190°C (350 to 375°F), so a thermometer is best. Bubbles forming steadily around a wooden spoon handle also mean it's hot.
how do i stop rice sticking | sticky rice fix | rinse rice => Rinse rice to remove extra starch, use the right water amount, don't stir while it cooks, and let it rest covered for 5 to 10 minutes before fluffing.
what is harissa | harissa paste | north african chili paste => Harissa is a spicy North African chili paste made with garlic, olive oil and spices like cumin, coriander and caraway.
what is chinese five spice | five spice powder | five spice ingredients => Chinese five spice is a blend usually made of star anise, cloves, Chinese cinnamon, Sichuan pepper and fennel seeds. It's used on meats and in stir fries.
what is tamarind | tamarind paste | tamarind taste => Tamarind is a pod fruit with sticky, sweet and sour pulp. It's used in pad thai, chutneys, drinks and Worcestershire sauce.
what is ghee | ghee vs butter | clarified butter => Ghee is butter simmered until the water cooks off and the milk solids brown and are strained out. It tastes nutty, comes from Indian cooking and handles high heat.
what is creme fraiche | creme fraiche vs sour cream | creme fraiche taste => Crème fraîche is thick cultured cream that's richer and less tangy than sour cream, and it doesn't curdle when heated in sauces.
what is mascarpone | mascarpone cheese | tiramisu cheese => Mascarpone is a soft, rich Italian cream cheese. It's the creamy layer in tiramisu and is great in desserts.
what is tempeh | tempeh vs tofu | fermented soybean cake => Tempeh is a firm cake of fermented whole soybeans from Indonesia. It has a nutty flavor and more texture than tofu.
what is seitan | wheat meat | seitan protein => Seitan is a chewy meat substitute made from wheat gluten. It's high in protein but not safe for people who avoid gluten.
what is jackfruit used for | jackfruit meat substitute | young jackfruit => Jackfruit is the largest tree fruit. Young unripe jackfruit shreds like pulled meat and is used as a meat substitute, while ripe jackfruit is sweet and fruity.
what is xanthan gum | xanthan gum gluten free | why xanthan gum => Xanthan gum is a thickener made by fermenting sugar with bacteria. In gluten free baking, a small amount helps hold dough together.
what is molasses | molasses in brown sugar | blackstrap molasses => Molasses is the thick, dark syrup left over from making sugar. Brown sugar is white sugar with some molasses mixed back in.
what is corn syrup | corn syrup vs high fructose | light corn syrup => Corn syrup is a sweet syrup made from corn starch. It keeps candy smooth by stopping sugar crystals. High fructose corn syrup is a different, processed version.
what is condensed milk | sweetened condensed vs evaporated | evaporated milk => Sweetened condensed milk is milk cooked down with lots of sugar until thick. Evaporated milk is cooked down too but has no added sugar, so they can't be swapped.
what is half and half | half and half cream | half and half substitute => Half and half is a mix of milk and cream, richer than milk but lighter than cream. It's common in coffee and won't whip.
what is a chipotle pepper | chipotle in adobo | smoked jalapeno => A chipotle is a ripe jalapeño that has been smoked and dried. Canned chipotles in adobo sauce add smoky heat to dishes.
what is a habanero | habanero heat | habanero vs jalapeno => A habanero is a small, fruity, very hot chili, usually around 100,000 to 350,000 Scoville units, far hotter than a jalapeño. Wear gloves when cutting them.
what is a serrano pepper | serrano vs jalapeno | serrano heat => A serrano is a slim green chili that's a few times hotter than a jalapeño. It's common in salsas and pico de gallo.
what is a shallot | shallot vs onion | shallot substitute => A shallot is a small onion relative with a milder, sweeter flavor. It grows in clusters like garlic and is great in dressings and sauces.
what is the difference between scallions and green onions | scallions green onions | spring onion => Scallions and green onions are the same thing: young onions with long green tops and small white bottoms. Spring onions are similar but have bigger bulbs.
what is a leek | how to clean leeks | leeks dirt => A leek is a large, mild onion relative. Use the white and light green parts, and slice then rinse them well because dirt hides between the layers.
what is lemongrass | how to use lemongrass | lemongrass flavor => Lemongrass is a tall grass with a lemony flavor used in Thai and Vietnamese food. Use the tender lower stalk and bruise it to release flavor.
what is bok choy | how to cook bok choy | baby bok choy => Bok choy is a Chinese cabbage with crisp white stalks and dark green leaves. It cooks in minutes in stir fries, soups or steamed.
what is daikon | daikon radish | daikon taste => Daikon is a large, mild white radish used in Asian cooking. It's eaten raw, pickled, or simmered in soups and stews.
what is jicama | jicama taste | how to eat jicama => Jicama is a round root vegetable with crisp, juicy, slightly sweet flesh. Peel off the brown skin and eat it raw in sticks or salads.
what is a plantain | plantain vs banana | how to cook plantains => A plantain is a starchy cooking banana. Green ones are starchy and fried into tostones, and very ripe dark ones are sweet when fried.
what is yuca | cassava | is cassava poisonous raw => Yuca, or cassava, is a starchy tropical root. It must be peeled and fully cooked because raw cassava has natural compounds that can be toxic. Tapioca is made from it.
what is okra | slimy okra | how to cook okra => Okra is a green seed pod used in Southern US, African, Caribbean and Indian cooking, like gumbo. It gets slimy when cut and simmered, but roasting or frying keeps it crisp.
what is a parsnip | parsnip taste | parsnip vs carrot => A parsnip is a pale, cream colored root related to carrots. It tastes sweet and nutty, especially when roasted.
what is kohlrabi | kohlrabi taste | how to eat kohlrabi => Kohlrabi is a round bulb in the cabbage family that tastes like a mild, sweet broccoli stem. Peel it and eat it raw or roasted.
what is arugula | rocket lettuce | arugula taste => Arugula, also called rocket, is a salad green with a peppery, slightly bitter flavor.
what is orzo | orzo pasta | is orzo rice => Orzo is a small pasta shaped like grains of rice. It's often used in soups and salads.
what is wild rice | is wild rice rice | wild rice grass => Wild rice isn't really rice. It's the seed of aquatic grasses that grow in North American lakes and rivers, and it's chewy and nutty.
what is sticky rice | glutinous rice | does sticky rice have gluten => Sticky rice, or glutinous rice, is a short grain rice that gets very sticky when cooked. Despite the name, it has no gluten.
what is tapioca | tapioca pearls | boba pearls made of => Tapioca is starch from the cassava root. It's used to thicken pies and puddings and to make the chewy pearls in bubble tea.
what is sauerkraut | sauerkraut made | sauerkraut vs kimchi => Sauerkraut is finely shredded cabbage fermented with salt until sour. It's common in German and Eastern European food.
what is kombucha | kombucha scoby | does kombucha have alcohol => Kombucha is a fizzy fermented tea made with a culture of bacteria and yeast called a SCOBY. It contains a tiny bit of alcohol, usually under 0.5% in store versions.
what is kefir | kefir vs yogurt | kefir grains => Kefir is a tangy fermented milk drink made with kefir grains, which are clusters of bacteria and yeast. It's thinner than yogurt.
what is prosciutto | prosciutto vs ham | cured ham => Prosciutto is Italian dry cured ham, sliced paper thin and usually eaten uncooked.
what is chorizo | mexican vs spanish chorizo | chorizo sausage => Spanish chorizo is a cured, firm sausage flavored with smoked paprika. Mexican chorizo is usually fresh ground pork that must be cooked.
what is pancetta | pancetta vs bacon | italian bacon => Pancetta is Italian cured pork belly. It's like bacon but usually not smoked.
what is a flank steak | cutting against the grain | slice against grain => Flank steak is a lean, flavorful cut from the belly. Cook it hot and fast and slice it thinly across the grain so it isn't chewy.
what is nori | seaweed sheets | nori sushi => Nori is dried seaweed pressed into thin sheets, used to wrap sushi and onigiri or crumbled on rice.
what is wasabi really | fake wasabi | horseradish wasabi => Real wasabi comes from the root of a Japanese plant and is rare and expensive. Most wasabi outside Japan is horseradish, mustard and green coloring.
what is salsa verde | green salsa | tomatillo => Mexican salsa verde is a green salsa made from tomatillos, chilies, onion and cilantro. Italian salsa verde is a different herb sauce.
what is sriracha | sriracha sauce | sriracha heat => Sriracha is a hot sauce made from chilies, garlic, vinegar, sugar and salt. It's named after the town of Si Racha in Thailand.
what is ponzu | ponzu sauce | citrus soy sauce => Ponzu is a tangy Japanese sauce made with citrus juice, usually mixed with soy sauce. It's used as a dipping sauce or dressing.
