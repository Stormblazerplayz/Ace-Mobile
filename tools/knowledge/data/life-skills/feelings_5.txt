# tag: life.feelings.situations
how do i be a good winner | gracious winner | bragging after winning => Shake hands or say "good game," say something nice about how the other side played, and celebrate without rubbing it in. People remember how you act when you win.
how do i deal with being embarrassed | embarrassing moment | cringe memory => Everyone has embarrassing moments, and other people forget them much faster than you do. Laughing it off, taking a breath and moving on usually works best.
how do i talk to my parents about something hard | hard conversation parents | tell parents something => Pick a calm time, maybe in the car or on a walk, and start with "Can I talk to you about something?" If saying it is hard, writing a note or text works too.
how do i deal with moving to a new school | new school nervous | first day new school => Feeling nervous is normal. Join a club or team, say hi to people near you, and ask a teacher or counselor for help finding your way. It usually gets easier after a few weeks.
what do i do if my pet died | pet died | grieving a pet => Losing a pet really hurts, and it's okay to cry and miss them. Talking about your favorite memories, drawing them or making a little memorial can help. Share your feelings with family.
how do i deal with a grandparent dying | grandparent died | grief kids => Grief can feel like sadness, anger, numbness or all of them. Talk with family, share stories about your grandparent, and let a trusted adult know if the sad feelings stay heavy for a long time.
what is grief | grieving | stages of grief => Grief is the mix of feelings after losing someone or something you love. It can come in waves, and there's no right way or timeline. Talking to people you trust helps.
how do i help a friend who is being bullied | friend bullied | stand up for friend => Stay with them, don't join in or laugh, and tell a trusted adult what you saw. Later, check on your friend and let them know it's not their fault.
what is an upstander | upstander vs bystander | be an upstander => An upstander is someone who sees bullying or unfairness and does something to help, like supporting the target or telling an adult, instead of just watching.
what do i do if i'm being bullied online | cyberbullying what to do | mean comments online => Don't reply, take screenshots, block and report the person, and tell a trusted adult. It's not your fault, and you don't have to handle it alone.
how do i join a conversation | joining a group | start talking to group => Walk up, listen for a moment, then add something related or ask a question about what they're talking about. Smiling and making eye contact helps.
what do i do if i feel like crying at school | crying at school | hold back tears => Take slow breaths, ask to get a drink of water or use the restroom for a minute, and talk to a teacher, nurse or counselor if you need to. Crying is normal and nothing to be ashamed of.
how do i deal with a teacher i don't like | teacher doesn't like me | unfair teacher => Stay respectful and do your best work. If something feels unfair, talk to the teacher calmly after class, or ask a parent or counselor for help.
what do i do if i feel pressured to vape | vaping pressure | friends vaping => You can just say "No thanks" or "I'm good," or blame a parent or sport. Vapes contain nicotine, which is very addictive for teens. Real friends accept your no.
how do i deal with worry before bed | can't sleep worried | worry at night => Write your worries down to deal with tomorrow, try slow breathing, and do something calming without screens. If worries keep you up a lot, tell a trusted adult.
what is a worry time | worry time | schedule worries => Worry time is setting aside about 15 minutes during the day to think about worries. When one pops up at other times, you write it down and save it for later, which helps it feel more manageable.
how do i deal with boredom | always bored | nothing to do => Boredom can lead to creativity. Try making something, going outside, reading, learning a new trick, or making a list of things to do next time you're bored.
how do i deal with screen time fights | screen time arguments | parents limit screen time => Talk calmly with your parents at a non heated moment, make a plan together with clear limits and times, and stick to it to build trust.
how do i cope with a bad day | bad day | everything went wrong => Do something comforting, like a shower, a snack, music or time outside, and talk to someone. Tomorrow is a fresh start, and one bad day doesn't define you.
what are feelings for | why do we have emotions | emotions purpose => Feelings give us information. Fear warns of danger, anger shows something feels unfair, sadness shows what we care about, and happiness shows what matters to us.
what is the difference between sad and depressed | sad vs depression | always sad => Sadness is a normal feeling that passes. Depression is a health condition where low mood, tiredness or loss of interest lasts most days for weeks. If it feels like that, tell a trusted adult.
what is a panic attack | panic attack | sudden fear => A panic attack is a sudden wave of intense fear with a racing heart, fast breathing, shaking or dizziness. It feels scary but passes, usually within minutes. Slow breathing helps, and tell a trusted adult.
how do i show gratitude | gratitude | thankful list => Say thank you specifically, write a note, help someone back, or list three good things each day. Gratitude can boost your mood.
how do i handle someone being rude | someone rude to me | rude people => Stay calm, don't match their rudeness, and walk away if needed. Sometimes people are rude because of their own bad day, and you don't have to take it personally.
what do i do if an adult makes me uncomfortable | adult uncomfortable | creepy adult => Trust your feelings. Get away if you can, say no, and tell a parent or another trusted adult right away. You won't be in trouble for telling, even if they told you to keep it secret.
what is a crush | having a crush | crush feelings => A crush is having strong feelings of liking someone, often with butterflies and thinking about them a lot. It's very common and normal, and you don't have to act on it.
how do i deal with someone liking me when i don't like them | someone has a crush on me | turn down crush => Be honest but kind, like "I like you as a friend, but not that way." Don't tease them or spread it around, since telling you took courage.
what do i do if i'm stressed about grades | grade stress | pressure good grades => Break work into small pieces, ask teachers for help early, and talk to your parents about how you feel. Grades matter, but so do sleep, fun and your health.
how do i take a break from social media | social media break | social media makes me sad => Mute or log out for a set time, move apps off your home screen, and fill the time with hobbies and friends. Notice how you feel without it.
how do i deal with fomo | fomo | fear of missing out => FOMO, fear of missing out, is feeling others are having more fun without you. Remember posts show only highlights, and focus on enjoying what you're doing now.
what is assertive vs aggressive | assertive | passive aggressive => Being assertive is clearly and calmly saying what you need while respecting others. Being aggressive is pushing your way with yelling, threats or put downs.
how do i deal with gaming rage | rage quit | gaming anger => Notice when you're getting heated, take a break, breathe slowly and step away from the screen. Losing is part of getting better, and games are meant to be fun.
how do i deal with toxic players online | toxic gamers | mean people in games => Don't argue back. Mute, block and report them, and play with friends or in safer modes. Tell a trusted adult if someone threatens you or won't leave you alone.
