# tag: life.business.basics
how do i start a small business as a kid | business ideas for kids | how can a 12 year old start a business => Pick something people around you need that you're good at, like pet sitting, yard work, making crafts or 3D prints, or tech help. Ask a parent to help with money and safety, start small, and keep track of what you spend and earn.
what is a business plan | how do i write a business plan | simple business plan for kids => A business plan answers what you'll sell, who will buy it, how much it costs to make, what you'll charge, how people will find out, and what your goal is. One page is plenty to start.
what is revenue | revenue vs profit | what's the difference between sales and profit => Revenue is all the money that comes in from sales. Profit is what's left after you subtract your costs, like materials and fees.
what are business expenses | what counts as costs in a business | fixed vs variable costs => Expenses are what you spend to run the business. Variable costs change with how much you make, like filament or lemons, and fixed costs stay the same, like a website fee each month.
how do i price my products | how much should i charge | pricing tips for small business => Add up your cost for one item, including materials and a share of tools and fees, then add profit on top. Check what similar things sell for, and don't forget to value your time.
what is a profit margin | how to calculate margin | what percent profit should i make => Profit margin is profit divided by the price, shown as a percent. If you sell something for $10 and it costs $4 to make, your profit is $6 and your margin is 60 percent.
what is break even | when does a business break even | break even point meaning => Break even is when your total sales equal your total costs, so you've earned back what you spent. Every sale after that is profit.
how do i keep track of money for my business | business bookkeeping for kids | tracking sales and costs => Use a simple spreadsheet with columns for the date, what it was, money in and money out. Save receipts and add it up each week so you know if you're making a profit.
how do i advertise my business | marketing ideas for kids | how to get customers => Start with people you know, like family, neighbors and friends of your parents. Make simple flyers, ask happy customers to tell others, and with a parent's help, post in local groups or online.
what is a target customer | who is my audience | target market meaning => Your target customer is the type of person most likely to buy, like dog owners for a dog walking business or gamers for custom controller grips. Knowing them helps you decide what to make and where to advertise.
how do i name my business | business name ideas | what makes a good brand name => Pick a name that's short, easy to say and spell, and hints at what you do. Check that nobody nearby or online is already using it.
what is a logo | how do i make a logo | logo design tips => A logo is a simple symbol or styled name that represents your brand. Keep it simple so it works small, use one or two colors, and make sure it looks good in black and white.
what is customer service | how do i treat customers | handling an unhappy customer => Customer service means helping buyers and making things right when there's a problem. Listen, say sorry, and offer a fix like a replacement or refund. Happy customers come back and tell friends.
what is a brand | what does branding mean | how do i build a brand => A brand is how people recognize and feel about your business: your name, logo, colors, style and how you treat people. Keeping them consistent makes you easy to remember.
do kids have to pay taxes on business money | taxes on money i earn | do i pay tax as a kid => Possibly. In the US, if a kid earns money from their own business or side jobs, they may need to file taxes once self employment income passes about $400 in a year. Have a parent look into it.
what is a receipt | why keep receipts | do i need to give receipts => A receipt is proof of a purchase showing what was bought, the price and the date. Keep receipts for what you buy for your business, and give customers one if they ask.
how do i get paid online as a kid | can kids use paypal | taking payments as a kid => Most payment apps like PayPal require users to be 18, so a parent usually has to set up and own the account. Cash is simplest for local sales.
what is a contract | do i need a contract for a job | what is an agreement in business => A contract is an agreement about what each side will do, like what you'll make, the price and the deadline. For small kid jobs, writing it down in a simple message helps avoid confusion.
what is a startup | what does startup mean | startup vs small business => A startup is a new company, usually aiming to grow very fast with a new product or idea, often with investors. A small business usually grows steadily and may stay small on purpose.
what is an entrepreneur | how do you become an entrepreneur | what do entrepreneurs do => An entrepreneur is someone who starts a business and takes a risk to build it. You become one by spotting a problem, creating a solution people will pay for and learning as you go.
what is supply and demand | how does supply and demand affect prices | why do prices change => When lots of people want something and there isn't much of it, prices go up. When there's plenty and fewer people want it, prices go down.
how do i get my first customers | no one is buying my stuff | first sale tips => Ask friends and family to try it and give honest feedback, offer a small launch deal, and show your product where people can see it in action. Use their feedback to improve.
what is a review and why does it matter | ask customers for reviews | how to get good reviews => Reviews are what customers say about your product. Good ones make new buyers trust you, so ask happy customers to leave one, and use bad ones to improve.
what is a side hustle | side hustle ideas for teens | small ways to make money => A side hustle is a small business or job you do on top of school or a main job. For kids it can be pet sitting, crafts, tutoring younger kids, or selling things you make.
what is wholesale vs retail | what does wholesale mean | selling to stores => Retail means selling directly to customers at full price. Wholesale means selling many items to a store at a lower price per item so they can resell them.
what is inventory | how much stock should i make | managing inventory => Inventory is the stock of things you have ready to sell. Start small so money isn't stuck in things that don't sell, and make more of what sells best.

# tag: life.business.lemonade
how do i run a lemonade stand | lemonade stand tips | how to start a lemonade stand => Pick a busy, safe spot with a parent's okay, make a clear sign with prices, keep the lemonade cold, and have change ready. Be friendly, keep things clean, and offer a snack like cookies to earn more.
how much should i charge for lemonade | lemonade stand prices | what to charge for a cup of lemonade => Add up what you spent on lemons, sugar and cups, divide by how many cups you can make, and charge more than that. Many stands charge a dollar or so a cup, and simple round prices make change easy.
where is the best place for a lemonade stand | lemonade stand location | best spot to sell lemonade => Choose a spot with lots of people walking by, like near a park, a sports game or a garage sale, with a parent's permission. Shade keeps you and the lemonade cool.
how do i make a lemonade stand sign | lemonade sign ideas | what to put on my stand sign => Use big bright letters people can read from the street, show the price clearly, and add a fun picture. Mention any special, like "ice cold" or "cookies too".
do i need a permit for a lemonade stand | is a lemonade stand legal | lemonade stand rules => It depends on where you live. Many places allow kids' stands, but some require permits for selling food. Ask a parent to check your local rules.
how do i make change at my stand | counting change back | how to give change => Count up from the price to what they gave you. If lemonade is $1 and they give $5, hand back one dollar at a time saying "2, 3, 4, 5". Start with a cash box with small bills and coins.
what else can i sell besides lemonade | stand ideas for kids | bake sale tips => Popcorn, cookies, iced tea, crafts, bracelets or slime can sell well. Bake sales do great at events. Keep food covered, wash hands, and label anything with nuts.

# tag: life.business.3dprints
how do i sell 3d prints | selling 3d printed stuff | how to start a 3d printing business => Start with useful or fun prints people want, like phone stands, desk organizers, fidget toys or custom name tags. Price for filament, electricity, printer wear and your time, and make sure you have the right to sell the designs.
can i sell things i print from thingiverse | 3d model licenses | is it legal to sell other people's 3d models => Only if the license allows it. Many free models use licenses like Creative Commons NonCommercial, which means no selling. Look for models that allow commercial use, buy a commercial license, or design your own.
how do i price a 3d print | how much to charge for 3d printing | 3d print cost calculator => Multiply the grams of filament used by your filament cost per gram, add a bit for electricity and printer wear, then add your design and finishing time and a profit. Your slicer shows the grams and print time.
how much does filament cost per gram | cost of pla per print | how do i figure filament cost => Divide the spool price by its weight in grams. A $20 spool of 1 kg PLA works out to 2 cents per gram, so a 50 gram print uses about $1 of plastic.
what 3d prints sell best | popular 3d prints to sell | best selling 3d printed items => Personalized items often sell well, like name keychains, signs and gifts, along with desk organizers, articulated toys, cosplay parts and replacement parts people can't find. Things that solve a real problem sell best.
etsy or craft fair for prints | best place to sell 3d prints | sell prints online or local => Start locally with friends, family, school events and craft fairs. Online shops like Etsy work too, but you need to be 18 or have a parent run the account, and you pay listing and sales fees.
how do i make my 3d prints look more professional | finishing 3d prints | make prints sell better => Dial in your settings to avoid stringing and blobs, sand or trim rough spots, pick good colors, and package them nicely with a little card. Clear photos with good light help them sell.
how do i take good product photos | photographing things to sell | product photo tips => Use natural light near a window or a cheap light box, put the item on a plain background, fill the frame and show it from a few angles plus one photo showing its size in a hand.
how do i handle custom orders | custom 3d print requests | taking commission orders => Write down exactly what they want, including size, color and text, agree on the price and date, and send a preview before printing. Ask for payment or a deposit up front through a parent.
can i sell 3d prints of characters | is it okay to print pokemon and sell it | trademark and copyright for fan art => Selling prints of characters from games, movies or brands without permission can break copyright and trademark rules, even if you made the model. Original designs are the safe choice.

# tag: life.business.youtube
how do i start a youtube channel | starting a youtube channel as a kid | how to make a youtube channel => In the US you must be at least 13 to have your own YouTube account, and younger kids need a parent's account or supervised setup. Pick a topic you love, plan your first few videos, and focus on making each one a bit better than the last.
how do i come up with youtube video ideas | what should i make videos about | youtube ideas for kids => Make videos about things you know and love, like your builds, games, animations or bike rides. Look at what people search for in your topic and ask your viewers what they want next.
how do i make a good youtube thumbnail | thumbnail tips | what makes people click a video => Use a clear close up image, bright colors, a big readable face or object, and three words or fewer of text. Make sure it still makes sense when tiny on a phone screen.
how do i write a good youtube title | youtube title tips | how to name my video => Make it clear and interesting in under about 60 characters, use words people would search for, and make sure it matches the video so viewers don't feel tricked.
how do i get more subscribers | grow my youtube channel | how to get views on youtube => Post regularly, hook viewers in the first few seconds, make titles and thumbnails people want to click, and keep improving. Reply to comments and make videos in a series so people come back.
how do i hook viewers at the start of a video | first 10 seconds of a video | how to keep people watching => Skip long intros. Show the most exciting moment or say what they'll get right away, like "I built a drone out of a 3D printer, and it actually flies". Keep pace up by cutting pauses.
what is audience retention | why do people stop watching my video | youtube retention graph => Audience retention shows how much of your video people watch. Look for big drops in YouTube Studio's analytics, and cut or speed up those parts in future videos.
what is youtube shorts | how long can a youtube short be | shorts vs regular videos => Shorts are vertical videos up to 3 minutes long, made for phones. They're great for quick ideas, clips and animations, and can bring new viewers to your longer videos.
how do i make a youtube short | vertical video tips | making shorts on youtube => Film or edit in a 9:16 vertical format, grab attention in the first second, keep it fast with captions, and loop the end back to the start so people rewatch.
how do kids make money on youtube | how does youtube pay | youtube partner program requirements => As of 2025, the YouTube Partner Program needs 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in a year or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days for ad money. You must be 18 or have a parent or guardian manage the payment account.
how often should i upload on youtube | upload schedule | is it better to post more often => Consistency matters more than speed. Once a week or every two weeks is a good start, and quality is more important than quantity.
what equipment do i need for youtube | best camera for beginner youtubers | do i need a fancy camera => A phone or iPad camera is enough to start. Good sound matters most, so a cheap clip on mic helps a lot, and filming facing a window gives nice light.
how do i make my audio sound better | microphone tips for videos | bad audio in my videos => Get the mic close to your mouth, record in a small room with soft things like a bed or curtains to cut echo, and avoid noisy fans. Even a basic lapel mic beats a built in one far away.
what editing software should i use | free video editing apps | best editor for youtube => Free choices include DaVinci Resolve on a computer, CapCut, and iMovie on iPad and Mac. DaVinci Resolve is powerful and used by pros, while iMovie is the easiest to learn.
what is youtube studio | how to see my channel stats | youtube analytics for beginners => YouTube Studio is where you upload videos and see analytics like views, watch time, retention and where viewers came from. Check which videos did best and figure out why.
can i use music in my youtube videos | copyright music on youtube | what music is safe for youtube => Using popular songs without permission can get a copyright claim, which may block your video or give the money to the music owner. Use the free YouTube Audio Library or music you have a license for.
what is a copyright strike | youtube copyright claim vs strike | how do i avoid copyright problems => A claim means a rights owner found their content in your video and may take the ad money or block it. A strike is a formal takedown, and three active strikes can get a channel deleted. Use only content you made or have rights to.
how do i stay safe as a kid youtuber | youtube safety tips | not sharing personal info on youtube => Never share your full name, school, address or exact location, watch for things in the background like mail or street signs, and have a parent help manage comments. Don't meet viewers in person.
how do i handle mean comments | hate comments on my video | someone left a rude comment => Don't argue with them. Delete, block or hide the comment and focus on the people who enjoy your stuff. If something is threatening or really upsetting, show a parent.
how do i make a youtube banner and profile picture | channel art size | channel branding => Use a simple logo or face for the profile picture since it shows tiny. The banner shows on phones, tablets, computers and TVs, so keep important text in the center safe area, which YouTube Studio shows when you upload.
how do i collab with other youtubers | youtube collaboration | working with another creator => Find creators around your size who make similar content, watch their stuff and comment, then message them, with a parent's okay, with a specific idea that helps you both.
how do i write a video script | scripting a youtube video | do i need a script => Write a strong hook, the main points in order, and a quick ending. Bullet points work if you like talking naturally. Reading it out loud helps cut boring parts.
what is a youtube niche | should my channel have one topic | choosing a channel theme => A niche is a focused topic, like drone builds or Minecraft redstone. Focused channels are easier to grow because viewers know what to expect and subscribe for more.
how do i use b roll | what is b roll footage | b roll ideas => B roll is extra footage that shows what you're talking about, like close ups of your build while you narrate. It keeps videos visually interesting and hides cuts.
how do i make jump cuts | what is a jump cut | cutting pauses in my video => A jump cut removes pauses or mistakes so the video skips ahead. Most YouTube videos use them to keep the pace quick and energy high.
what is the youtube algorithm | how does youtube recommend videos | how does youtube choose what to show => YouTube recommends videos that people click on and keep watching, and that keep them satisfied on the platform. Strong thumbnails, titles and good retention help your videos get shown more.
what is a call to action | asking viewers to subscribe | how to end a youtube video => A call to action asks viewers to do something, like subscribe, comment or watch another video. A short ask at the right moment works better than begging at the start.

# tag: life.business.jobs
how much should i charge for pet sitting | pet sitting for kids | dog walking business => Check what others nearby charge and price a bit lower when you're starting. Get the owner's phone number, feeding instructions and vet info, and do a trial visit while they're home.
how do i start a lawn mowing business | yard work for money | raking leaves business => Ask neighbors you know with a parent's help, offer a fair price per yard, show up on time and do a careful job. Raking, weeding and shoveling snow are great jobs too.
how do i start a babysitting business | babysitting at 12 | do i need a babysitting course => Many kids start babysitting around 12 or 13, often as a parent's helper first. A babysitting and first aid course, like ones from the Red Cross, teaches safety and makes parents trust you more.
how can i earn money with tech skills | tech help business for kids | making money with computers => You could help neighbors set up devices, teach older people to video call, edit videos, design logos or make simple websites. Always have a parent know who you're helping.
how do i ask a neighbor for a job | asking for odd jobs | what to say when offering to work => Go with a parent, introduce yourself, say what you offer and your price, and leave a flyer with a parent's phone number. Be polite even if they say no.
how do i do a garage sale | yard sale tips | how to sell old stuff => Gather things to sell, clean them, price them with stickers, and advertise with signs and local posts. Have small change ready and be okay with people bargaining.
how do i sell my old toys or games | selling used stuff online | where to sell old video games => With a parent's help, you can sell at a garage sale, trade at game stores, or list them on local marketplace apps. Clean them and take clear photos, and meet buyers in safe public places with an adult.
