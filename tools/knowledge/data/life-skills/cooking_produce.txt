how do i cook broccoli | best way to cook broccoli | roasted broccoli => Roast florets tossed in oil and salt at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 15 to 20 minutes until the edges are crispy, or steam them for 4 to 5 minutes until bright green and just tender.
how do i cook carrots | roasted carrots | how long to boil carrots => Roast carrot pieces with oil and salt at about 200 °C (400 °F) for 25 to 35 minutes, or boil slices for 5 to 8 minutes until a fork goes in easily. A little butter and honey makes glazed carrots.
how do i cook corn on the cob | boiling corn on the cob | how long to boil corn => Husk the corn, drop it into boiling water and cook about 4 to 7 minutes until bright yellow and tender. You can also grill it or microwave it in the husk for about 3 to 4 minutes per ear.
how do i cook green beans | sauteed green beans | how to trim green beans => Snap or cut off the stem ends, then steam or boil for 4 to 6 minutes until bright green, or saute in oil with garlic for about 6 to 8 minutes.
how do i cook asparagus | how to trim asparagus | roasted asparagus => Bend a spear near the bottom and it snaps where the tough part ends, then trim the rest to match. Roast with oil and salt at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 8 to 12 minutes depending on thickness.
how do i cook spinach | sauteed spinach | why does spinach shrink so much => Saute spinach in a little oil or butter with garlic for 1 to 2 minutes until just wilted. It shrinks a lot because it's mostly water, so a huge pile cooks down to a small bowl.
how do i cook mushrooms | browning mushrooms | should you wash mushrooms => Wipe them with a damp towel or rinse quickly. Cook in a hot pan with oil, spread out and not stirred too much, until browned. Salt them near the end so they brown instead of steaming.
how do i cook zucchini | sauteed zucchini | grilled zucchini => Slice it, toss with oil and salt, and saute or grill a few minutes per side until tender and lightly browned. It's mostly water, so high heat keeps it from getting mushy.
how do i cook cauliflower | roasted cauliflower | cauliflower rice how to make => Roast florets with oil and spices at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 20 to 30 minutes. For cauliflower rice, pulse raw florets in a food processor until rice sized and saute for 5 minutes.
how do i make brussels sprouts taste good | roasted brussels sprouts | why do brussels sprouts taste bitter => Cut them in half and roast cut side down with oil and salt at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 20 to 25 minutes until crispy. Roasting brings out sweetness, while boiling them too long makes them bitter and smelly.
how do i cook bell peppers | roasted peppers | sauteed peppers and onions => Slice them and saute in oil for 5 to 8 minutes until soft, or roast whole peppers until the skin blackens, then cover them so the skin peels off easily.
how do i cook sweet potatoes | baked sweet potato | sweet potato fries in the oven => Bake whole sweet potatoes pricked with a fork at about 200 °C (400 °F) for 45 to 60 minutes until soft. For fries, cut into sticks, toss with oil and a little cornstarch, and roast at high heat, flipping once.
how do i make homemade french fries | crispy oven fries | how to make fries from potatoes => Cut potatoes into sticks, soak in cold water for 30 minutes to remove starch, dry them very well, toss with oil and salt, and bake at about 220 °C (425 °F) for 30 to 40 minutes, flipping halfway.
how do i make mashed potatoes | boiling potatoes for mash | how long to boil potatoes => Peel and cut potatoes into even chunks, start them in cold salted water, bring to a boil and cook 15 to 20 minutes until a fork slides in easily. Drain, then mash with butter, warm milk and salt.
how do i cook peas | frozen peas how long | cooking fresh peas => Frozen peas only need 2 to 3 minutes in boiling water or the microwave. Fresh peas need popping out of their pods and cook in about 3 to 5 minutes.
how do i cook cabbage | sauteed cabbage | roasted cabbage wedges => Slice it thin and saute in butter or oil for 8 to 10 minutes until tender and a little browned, or roast thick wedges brushed with oil at high heat until the edges crisp.
how do i cook eggplant | why is eggplant bitter | roasted eggplant => Cube or slice it, toss with oil and salt, and roast at high heat until soft and browned. Modern eggplants are rarely bitter, but salting slices for 30 minutes draws out water.
how do i make a salad | how to build a good salad | salad tips => Start with clean, dry greens, add crunch like nuts or croutons, something sweet like fruit, some protein like chicken, beans or cheese, and dress it right before serving so it doesn't wilt.
how do i make salad dressing | basic vinaigrette | oil and vinegar ratio => A classic vinaigrette is about 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar or lemon juice, plus salt, pepper and a little mustard or honey to help it mix. Shake it in a jar.
how do i pick a ripe watermelon | how to tell if watermelon is ripe | watermelon thump test => Look for a creamy yellow spot on the bottom where it sat on the ground, a dull rather than shiny rind, and a heavy feel for its size. A deep hollow sound when tapped is a common clue.
how do i pick a good cantaloupe | ripe cantaloupe | melon ripeness => A ripe cantaloupe smells sweet at the stem end, feels heavy, and the stem end gives slightly when pressed. The netting should be raised with a tan rather than green color under it.
how do i know if a pineapple is ripe | picking a pineapple | pineapple leaf pull test => Ripe pineapples smell sweet at the base and look more golden than green. Pulling leaves isn't a reliable test. Pineapples don't get sweeter after picking, just softer.
how do i pick good strawberries | sweet strawberries | choosing berries at the store => Look for bright red berries all the way to the green top, with fresh leaves and no mushy or moldy spots. Check the bottom of the container too.
how do i pick a ripe peach | soft vs hard peaches | how to ripen peaches => A ripe peach smells fragrant and gives slightly near the stem. Hard ones ripen on the counter in a day or two, faster in a paper bag.
how do i know if a mango is ripe | mango ripeness | green mango ripe or not => Squeeze gently: a ripe mango gives a little like a ripe peach and smells sweet near the stem. Color isn't a good guide since some varieties stay green.
how do i cut a watermelon | slicing watermelon | easy watermelon cutting => Wash the rind, cut off both ends so it stands flat, slice it in half and then into wedges or slabs. For cubes, cut off the rind first, then dice the flesh.
how do i peel an orange easily | peeling oranges | orange peel trick => Roll it on the counter to loosen the peel, then start at the top with your thumbnail and peel in a spiral. Clementines and navel oranges are the easiest.
how do i cut an apple | slicing an apple | how to core an apple => Cut the apple into quarters through the stem, then cut the core out of each quarter at an angle, and slice. An apple slicer tool does it in one push.
how do i peel a kiwi | how to eat a kiwi | kiwi skin edible => Cut it in half and scoop it out with a spoon, or slice off the ends and peel with a peeler. The fuzzy skin is actually edible and has fiber.
how do i peel a hard boiled egg fast | peel an egg with a spoon | quick egg peeling => Crack it all over on the counter, then slide a spoon under the shell and membrane and run it around the egg. Peeling under running water helps too.
how do i cut a bell pepper | removing pepper seeds | slicing peppers => Cut off the top and bottom, slice down one side and unroll the pepper flat, then cut away the white ribs and seeds and slice into strips.

how do i cut a tomato | tomato slicing tips | how to dice a tomato => Use a sharp or serrated knife since tomato skin is slippery. Cut out the core, slice it, then stack and cut slices into strips and cubes.
how do i cut corn off the cob | removing corn kernels | corn cob kernels => Stand the cob up in a big bowl, or on the center of a bundt pan, and slice down the sides with a knife to cut off the kernels.
how do i wash leafy greens | dirty spinach or lettuce | washing kale => Fill a big bowl with cold water, swish the leaves around, then lift them out so the dirt stays at the bottom. Repeat if gritty, then dry them in a salad spinner or towel.
how do i cook kale | kale chips how to make | making kale less bitter => Remove the tough stems, tear the leaves and massage them with a little oil and salt for salad, or saute them for a few minutes. For chips, bake dry, oiled pieces at about 150 °C (300 °F) until crisp.
how do i cook beets | roasted beets | do beets stain => Wrap scrubbed beets in foil and roast at about 200 °C (400 °F) for 45 to 60 minutes until tender, then rub off the skins. Their juice stains, so wear gloves and use a plastic board.
how do i make applesauce | homemade applesauce | cooking apples down => Peel and chop apples, simmer with a splash of water and a little cinnamon for about 20 minutes until soft, then mash or blend. Add sugar only if needed.
