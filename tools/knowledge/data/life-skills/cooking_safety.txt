# tag: life.cooking.safety
what temperature should ground beef be cooked to | safe temp for hamburgers | is pink burger safe => Ground beef should reach 71 °C (160 °F) inside. Color isn't a reliable sign, so use a meat thermometer in the thickest part.
what temperature should pork be cooked to | is pink pork safe | safe pork temperature => Whole cuts of pork like chops and roasts are safe at 63 °C (145 °F) with a 3 minute rest, and can still be a little pink. Ground pork needs 71 °C (160 °F).
what temperature should steak be cooked to | steak doneness temperatures | rare medium well temps => Food safety guidance says whole beef cuts should reach at least 63 °C (145 °F) with a 3 minute rest, which is about medium rare. Many chefs serve rare at around 52 °C (125 °F), which carries a little more risk.
what temperature should fish be cooked to | safe fish temperature | how to tell salmon is cooked => Fish should reach 63 °C (145 °F), when it turns opaque and flakes easily with a fork. Shrimp and lobster turn pink and firm.
what temperature should leftovers be reheated to | reheating leftovers safely | how hot should leftovers be => Reheat leftovers until they're steaming hot all the way through, at least 74 °C (165 °F). Stir in the middle of reheating so there are no cold spots.
what temperature is the danger zone | food danger zone | why can't food be left out => The danger zone is between about 4 °C and 60 °C (40 °F and 140 °F), where bacteria grow fastest. Keep cold food cold and hot food hot, and don't leave food in that range for long.
how cold should my fridge be | what temperature should a refrigerator be | fridge temperature setting => Keep your fridge at 4 °C (40 °F) or below, and your freezer at -18 °C (0 °F). A cheap fridge thermometer helps you check.
how do i use a meat thermometer | where do you put a meat thermometer | instant read thermometer tips => Push the tip into the thickest part of the meat, not touching bone, fat or the pan. Wait for the number to settle, and clean it between uses, especially between raw and cooked food.
how do i know chicken is done without a thermometer | chicken still pink in the middle | no thermometer how to check chicken => The safest check is a thermometer reading 74 °C (165 °F). Without one, the juices should run clear and the meat should be white all the way through, with no pink at the thickest part, but color can fool you.
how long can leftovers stay in the fridge | how long do leftovers last | are 5 day old leftovers safe => Most cooked leftovers are good for 3 to 4 days in the fridge. If you won't eat them by then, freeze them. When in doubt, throw it out.
how long can cooked rice stay in the fridge | leftover rice how long | is old rice dangerous => Cool rice quickly and refrigerate it within about an hour or two, then eat it within about a day or so. Rice can carry bacteria that make toxins if it sits warm too long.
how do i thaw frozen meat safely | defrosting chicken | can i thaw meat on the counter => Thaw meat in the fridge, which takes about a day for most cuts, in cold water changed every 30 minutes, or in the microwave if you cook it right after. Never thaw it on the counter.
can i refreeze thawed meat | refreezing food | is it safe to refreeze chicken => Meat thawed in the fridge can be refrozen, though it may lose some quality. Meat thawed in cold water or the microwave should be cooked before refreezing.
what is cross contamination | how do i stop cross contamination | raw meat and vegetables same board => Cross contamination is when germs move from raw meat, poultry, seafood or eggs to food that won't be cooked, like salad. Use separate cutting boards, wash hands and tools with hot soapy water after raw meat, and never put cooked food on a plate that held raw meat.
should i wash raw chicken | do you rinse chicken before cooking | washing meat before cooking => No. Rinsing raw chicken splashes germs around your sink and counter. Cooking it to 74 °C (165 °F) kills the bacteria.
should i wash fruits and vegetables | how do you wash produce | do i need soap to wash fruit => Rinse fruits and veggies under running water and rub them with your hands, or scrub firm ones like potatoes with a brush. Don't use soap. Wash even ones you'll peel, since germs can move to the inside when you cut.
can i eat raw cookie dough | is cookie dough safe to eat | why can't you eat raw batter => Raw dough can make you sick from raw eggs and also from raw flour, which can carry E. coli. Some store dough is made safe to eat raw, but homemade usually isn't.
is it safe to eat raw eggs | raw eggs in smoothies | can you get sick from raw eggs => Raw or undercooked eggs can carry salmonella. Cook eggs until whites and yolks are firm, or use pasteurized eggs for recipes that stay raw.
what are signs that food has gone bad | how do i know if food is spoiled | is this food still good => Bad smells, slime, mold, strange colors, a puffy or leaking package, or a sour taste are signs it's spoiled. Some harmful germs don't change how food looks or smells, so follow storage times too.
can i cut mold off cheese | moldy bread can i eat it | is it okay if i remove the mold => For hard cheese like cheddar, you can cut off at least 2.5 cm (1 inch) around the mold. Soft foods like bread, soft cheese, yogurt and berries should be thrown out because mold spreads through them.
what does best by mean | expiration date vs best before | is food bad after the best by date => "Best by" and "sell by" are mostly about quality, not safety, so many foods are fine for a while after, if stored well. "Use by" dates matter more, especially on things like baby formula.
how long can food sit out at a party | buffet food safety | picnic food safety => Perishable food shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour if it's hotter than 32 °C (90 °F), like at a summer picnic. Keep cold food on ice.
how should i pack a safe lunch | keeping lunch cold | does my lunch need an ice pack => Use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack for things like meat sandwiches, yogurt and cut fruit. Keep hot foods hot in a thermos filled with hot water first.
how do i prevent food poisoning | food poisoning causes | how to cook safely => Follow four steps: clean hands and surfaces, separate raw meat from other food, cook to the right temperature, and chill leftovers within 2 hours.
what are common food allergies | most common food allergens | top food allergies => The most common in the US are milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish, wheat, soy and sesame. Always ask before serving food to friends and check labels.
how do i cook for someone with a food allergy | allergy safe cooking | nut free cooking => Read every label, keep allergy ingredients completely out of the dish, and clean all tools, boards and surfaces first. Cross contact with even a small amount can cause a reaction.
how do i put out a grease fire | oil fire in a pan | can you use water on a grease fire => Never use water, it makes burning oil explode. Turn off the burner, slide a metal lid or baking sheet over the pan to smother it and leave it covered. If it doesn't go out, get everyone out and call 911.
what should i do if something catches fire in the microwave | microwave fire | fire in the oven => Keep the door closed and turn it off or unplug it, which cuts off the fire's air. Tell an adult. If it doesn't go out quickly, leave and call 911.
how do i stay safe in the kitchen | kitchen safety rules | cooking safety for kids => Ask an adult before using the stove, oven or knives. Turn pot handles inward, use oven mitts, never leave cooking food alone, keep towels away from burners, and wipe up spills right away.
why should pot handles face inward | pot handle safety | turning handles on the stove => Handles sticking out over the edge of the stove can be bumped or grabbed, tipping hot food onto someone. Turning them inward over the counter prevents that.
what do i do if i burn myself in the kitchen | kitchen burn first aid | touched a hot pan => Run cool, not icy, water over the burn for about 20 minutes, then cover it loosely with a clean nonstick bandage. Don't use ice or butter. Get an adult, and get medical help for big, deep or blistering burns.
what do i do if i cut myself while cooking | knife cut first aid | sliced my finger => Press on it with a clean cloth until it stops bleeding, rinse with clean water and put on a bandage. Tell an adult. If it won't stop bleeding after 10 minutes of pressure or it's deep, get medical help.
why does oil pop and splatter | hot oil spitting | how to stop oil from splattering => Oil spits when water hits it, like from wet food. Pat food dry first, lower it in gently away from you, and use a splatter screen or lid.
can you microwave plastic containers | is it safe to heat food in plastic | microwave safe plastic => Only use containers labeled microwave safe. Other plastics can warp or melt. Many experts suggest glass or ceramic for heating, and never microwave takeout containers with metal handles.
should i let hot food cool before putting it in the fridge | putting hot food in fridge | cooling leftovers quickly => Get food in the fridge within 2 hours. Big batches cool faster if you split them into shallow containers. Very hot pots can be cooled briefly first so they don't warm other food.
how do i clean a cutting board | wooden vs plastic cutting board | sanitizing a cutting board => Wash with hot soapy water after each use, especially after raw meat, and dry it fully. Plastic boards can go in the dishwasher. Replace boards with deep grooves that are hard to clean.
can dogs and cats be in the kitchen while cooking | pets in the kitchen safety | dog under my feet while cooking => It's safer to keep pets out while cooking, so you don't trip carrying hot or sharp things. Many foods like onions, garlic, grapes and chocolate are also toxic to pets.
are canned foods safe if the can is dented | bulging can safety | dented can food => Small dents are usually fine, but throw out cans with deep dents on the seams, bulging, rust or leaks, or that spurt when opened. Those can mean dangerous bacteria.
can i eat food that fell on the floor | five second rule | is the 5 second rule real => The five second rule isn't real, bacteria can stick to food right away. Whether it's risky depends on the floor and the food, and wet foods pick up more germs.
how long can milk stay out of the fridge | milk left out overnight | can milk go bad on the counter => Milk shouldn't be left out more than about 2 hours. Milk left out overnight should be thrown away.
is it safe to eat sushi | raw fish safety | can kids eat sushi => Sushi from a good restaurant or store is usually safe for healthy people. Raw fish has a small risk of germs and parasites, so many kids start with cooked or veggie rolls like California rolls.
what is botulism | why can't babies have honey | is honey safe for kids => Botulism is a rare but serious illness from a toxin made by bacteria. Babies under 1 shouldn't have honey because it can carry botulism spores, but it's fine for older kids and adults.
can you eat potatoes that are green | sprouted potatoes safe | green potato skin => Green skin and sprouts have more of a natural toxin called solanine. Cut away green parts and sprouts, and throw out potatoes that are very green or soft.
can i eat apple seeds | are cherry pits poisonous | fruit seeds dangerous => Apple seeds and cherry pits contain a compound that can release cyanide, but swallowing a few by accident is very unlikely to hurt you, especially whole. Just don't crush and eat lots of them.
what kitchen jobs can a 12 year old do | cooking skills for kids my age | what can kids cook alone => Around 12, many kids can measure and mix, use the microwave and toaster, peel and chop with a safe knife, crack eggs and, with an adult nearby, cook simple things on the stove like eggs, pasta and grilled cheese.
