# tag: life.howto.stains
how do i get gum out of my hair | gum stuck in hair | how to remove chewing gum from hair => Rub a little peanut butter, vegetable oil or coconut oil into the gum and hair, wait a few minutes, then slide the gum out with your fingers or a comb. Wash your hair with shampoo afterward.
how do i get gum off my clothes | gum stuck on my pants | remove gum from fabric => Put the clothing in a plastic bag in the freezer for an hour or rub it with an ice cube until the gum is hard, then scrape it off with a spoon or dull knife. Treat any leftover spot with stain remover and wash.
how do i get gum off my shoe | stepped in gum | gum on the bottom of my sneaker => Rub ice on the gum until it hardens, then scrape it off with a stick or old butter knife. A little cooking oil helps loosen any sticky bits.
how do i get ketchup out of a shirt | ketchup stain removal | tomato sauce stain => Scrape off extra ketchup, rinse the back of the stain with cold water, then rub in a little dish soap or stain remover and wash in cold water. Hot water can set tomato stains.
how do i get grass stains out | grass stain on my jeans | remove grass stains from pants => Rub in some laundry detergent or stain remover, let it sit 15 to 30 minutes, scrub gently with an old toothbrush, then wash in the warmest water the label allows. Check it's gone before drying.
how do i get blood out of clothes | blood stain removal | nosebleed on my shirt => Use cold water only, because hot water cooks the stain in. Rinse from the back, rub with soap or stain remover, and soak in cold water if needed before washing.
how do i get grease out of clothes | oil stain on shirt | how to remove a greasy stain => Blot extra oil, sprinkle baking soda or cornstarch to soak it up for 15 minutes, brush it off, then rub dish soap into the spot and wash in the warmest water the label allows.
how do i get chocolate out of clothes | chocolate stain removal | melted chocolate on my shirt => Scrape off what you can, rinse from the back with cold water, rub in detergent or dish soap and let it sit, then wash. Repeat before drying if it's still there.
how do i get ink out of clothes | pen exploded on my shirt | ballpoint ink stain => Put a paper towel under the stain and dab rubbing alcohol on it with a cotton ball so the ink moves into the towel. Rinse, rub in detergent and wash. Ask an adult before using alcohol.
how do i get marker off my skin | sharpie on my hand | how to remove permanent marker from skin => Rub it with rubbing alcohol, hand sanitizer or a little baby oil, then wash with soap and water. It also fades on its own in a day or two.
how do i get sharpie off a wall | marker on the wall | remove crayon from walls => Try a melamine sponge like a magic eraser with a little water, rubbing gently. For crayon, a hair dryer on low can soften it so it wipes off. Test a hidden spot first so you don't take the paint off.
how do i get mud out of clothes | muddy pants after biking | how to wash mud stains => Let the mud dry, then brush or scrape off as much as you can. Rinse from the back with cold water, rub in detergent and wash. Washing wet mud just spreads it.
how do i get sweat stains out | yellow armpit stains | how to clean pit stains => Make a paste of baking soda and water, or use a stain remover, scrub it into the spot, let it sit 30 minutes and wash. Antiperspirant buildup causes a lot of yellowing, so let it dry before dressing.
how do i get slime out of clothes | slime stuck on carpet | remove slime from fabric => Pick off as much as you can, then soak the spot with white vinegar to break down the glue, rub with a brush and rinse. Wash the item normally afterward.
how do i get paint out of clothes | acrylic paint on my shirt | how to remove washable paint => Rinse wet paint out with cold water and soap right away. Washable kids paint comes out in the wash, but dried acrylic is hard, so try rubbing alcohol and scrubbing, and ask an adult.
how do i get a stain out of the carpet | carpet stain removal | how to clean a carpet spot => Blot up as much as you can without rubbing. Mix a little dish soap in warm water, blot it on, then blot with plain water and press dry with a towel.
how do i get wax off fabric | candle wax on the tablecloth | remove crayon wax from clothes => Let the wax harden and scrape off what you can. Ask an adult to lay a paper towel over the spot and press with a warm iron so the wax melts into the paper towel.
how do i get juice out of a shirt | fruit juice stain | berry stain on clothes => Rinse right away from the back with cold water, then rub with liquid detergent and wash. A bit of white vinegar helps on fruit stains. Don't dry it until the stain is gone.
how do i get coffee stains out | tea stain on shirt | how to remove coffee from fabric => Rinse with cold water from the back, then rub in detergent and wash. For old stains, soak in cool water with an oxygen based stain remover first.
how do i get makeup off clothes | lipstick stain removal | foundation on my collar => Dab with a little dish soap or makeup remover, work it in gently, then rinse and wash. For lipstick, blot with rubbing alcohol on a cotton ball first.
how do i get superglue off my fingers | glue on my skin | stuck my fingers with super glue => Don't pull stuck skin apart. Soak in warm soapy water and gently roll the edges apart, or dab with nail polish remover that has acetone and ask an adult. Keep it away from eyes and lips.
how do i get pen off my hands | ink on my skin | remove ink from fingers => Scrub with soap and warm water, then rub a little hand sanitizer or rubbing alcohol on any leftover ink and wash again.
why should i never put a stained shirt in the dryer | does the dryer set stains | can you fix a stain after drying => Dryer heat sets most stains so they become almost permanent. Always check that a stain is gone before drying, and if not, treat it and wash it again.
does hot or cold water remove stains better | which water for stains | what water temperature for blood or food stains => Cold water is safest for most stains, especially blood, ketchup and anything with protein, because heat sets them. Warm or hot water works better on greasy and oily stains once you've pretreated them.
how do i get bike grease off my hands | chain grease on my hands | clean bike oil from skin => Rub dish soap into dry hands before adding water, then scrub and rinse. A little cooking oil or sugar mixed with soap works great too. Wash with soap afterward.
how do i get bike grease off my clothes | chain oil stain on pants | bike chain mark on leg of jeans => Rub dish soap into the stain, let it sit 10 minutes, scrub with an old toothbrush and wash in the warmest water allowed. A spray stain remover can help on old marks.
how do i get gum off carpet | gum in the rug | chewing gum stuck in carpet => Press ice cubes in a plastic bag on the gum until it hardens, then scrape it off with a spoon and trim tiny bits with scissors. Clean the spot with soapy water after.
how do i get a sticker residue off | sticky label gunk | remove sticker glue => Rub a little cooking oil or rubbing alcohol on the sticky spot, wait a minute, then scrape gently with a plastic card and wash with soap. Warming it with a hair dryer helps peel stickers off.

# tag: life.howto.clothes
how do i tie a tie | how to tie a necktie | simple tie knot => For the four in hand knot: drape the tie with the wide end longer on your right, cross wide over narrow, wrap it around behind, bring it across the front again, then up through the neck loop and down through the front knot. Tighten by sliding the knot up.
how long should a tie be | where should a tie end | tie length rule => The tip of the tie should reach about the middle of your belt buckle. If it's too long or short, untie it and change how long the wide end is before you start.
how do i tie a bow tie | bow tie knot | how to tie a self tie bow tie => Make one end a bit longer, cross it over and up through the neck loop. Fold the short end into a bow shape, drop the long end over it, then push a fold of the long end through the loop behind and tighten both bows. Practice in a mirror.
how do i tie my shoes the bunny ears way | easiest way to tie shoelaces | bunny ears shoe tying => Tie a starting knot, then make a loop with each lace like two bunny ears. Cross the ears, tuck one under and through the hole, and pull both loops tight.
how do i stop my shoelaces coming undone | why do my laces keep untying | double knot shoes => Make sure you tie a true square knot, where the loops sit across the shoe, not up and down. If the bow twists, start your first knot the other way. A double knot also helps.
how do i lace my shoes | shoe lacing styles | how to relace sneakers => Criss cross lacing is the standard. Start at the bottom eyelets, cross the laces over and go up through the next pair, and repeat until the top. Bar lacing looks neater for dress shoes.
how do i sew a button back on | button fell off | how to sew on a button => Thread a needle and knot the end. Push it up from the back through one hole and down through the opposite hole, about 6 to 8 times, then wrap thread around the base under the button a few times and knot it at the back.
how do i thread a needle | threading a needle | can't get the thread through the needle => Cut the thread end clean at an angle and wet it or roll it between your fingers so it's pointy. Hold the needle up to light and push the thread through the eye, or use a needle threader.
how do i tie a knot at the end of thread | sewing knot | how to knot thread => Wrap the thread end around your fingertip once or twice, roll it off with your thumb so it twists, then pull the twist down into a small knot.
how do i fix a small hole in my shirt | sew a hole in clothes | patch a ripped seam => For a seam, turn the item inside out and stitch small running stitches along the old seam line. For a small hole, sew the edges together with tiny stitches, or iron on a patch from the inside.
how do i know my clothing size | what size shirt should i get | how to measure for clothes => Measure your chest around the widest part, your waist where pants sit, and your inseam from crotch to ankle. Compare those numbers to the brand's size chart, since sizes differ between brands.
how do i know if shoes fit | how much room should my shoes have | are my shoes too small => There should be about a thumb's width of space past your longest toe, and your heel shouldn't slip much. Shop later in the day when your feet are slightly bigger and wear the socks you'll use.
how do i break in new shoes | new shoes hurt | blisters from new shoes => Wear them around the house for short periods first, with thick socks. Put bandages or blister pads on rub spots early, before a blister forms.
how do i dress for cold weather | what to wear when it's freezing | layering clothes for winter => Wear layers: a base layer that moves sweat away, a warm middle layer like fleece, and a windproof or waterproof outer jacket. Add a hat, gloves and warm socks, since you lose heat fast from your head, hands and feet.
how do i dress for a nice event | what does dress up mean | what to wear to a fancy party => Dressing up usually means clean pressed pants or a skirt, a collared shirt or nice top, and real shoes instead of sneakers. When unsure, ask the host or go slightly fancier.
what does business casual mean | business casual for kids | what is smart casual => Business casual means neat but not a full suit, like chinos or nice pants, a collared shirt or sweater, and clean shoes. Smart casual is a little more relaxed, like nice jeans with a button up shirt.
how do i polish my shoes | shoe shine steps | how to make leather shoes shiny => Brush off dirt, rub a little shoe polish on with a cloth in small circles, let it dry a few minutes, then buff with a brush or clean cloth until it shines.
how do i get static out of clothes | why do my clothes have static | static cling fix => Static builds up in dry air and the dryer. Use a dryer sheet, take clothes out while slightly damp, or rub a metal hanger over the fabric.
how do i make my shoes stop smelling | stinky shoes | why do my sneakers smell => Let shoes dry fully between wears, wear clean socks, and sprinkle baking soda inside overnight, then shake it out. Washable insoles can be cleaned or replaced.
how do i put on a belt | which way does a belt go | how tight should a belt be => Thread it through the loops starting at the left front, going around the back, and buckle in front. It should hold your pants up without squeezing, with room for one finger.
how do i roll up my sleeves | how to roll sleeves neatly | rolling sleeves on a button up => Unbutton the cuff, fold it back once the width of the cuff, then fold again one or two more times until it's above your elbow or where you want it.
how do i fold clothes to save space in a drawer | file folding clothes | konmari folding => Fold shirts into small rectangles that stand up on their edge, then line them up in the drawer like files. You can see everything at once and nothing gets buried.
