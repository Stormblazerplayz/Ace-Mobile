# tag: sports.swimming
what are the four swimming strokes | types of swim strokes | competitive swimming strokes => The four competitive strokes are freestyle (front crawl), backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly.
what is the individual medley order | im order swimming | what is an im => The individual medley is one swimmer doing all four strokes in this order: butterfly, backstroke, breaststroke, freestyle.
what is the medley relay order | medley relay swimming | why does backstroke go first in a relay => The medley relay order is backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, freestyle. Backstroke goes first because those swimmers start in the water.
what is the fastest swimming stroke | which stroke is fastest | is freestyle the fastest => Freestyle, the front crawl, is the fastest stroke, followed by butterfly, backstroke and breaststroke.
how do i breathe while swimming freestyle | freestyle breathing tips | bilateral breathing => Turn your head to the side as your arm pulls back, take a quick breath with one goggle still in the water, then turn back and breathe out slowly underwater. Breathing every three strokes lets you breathe on both sides.
how do i swim faster | swimming tips | improve my freestyle => Keep your body long and flat, rotate your hips and shoulders, reach far forward, pull all the way back to your thigh, and kick from your hips with fairly straight legs. Good technique matters more than strength.
how do i do a flip turn | flip turn steps | swimming turns => Swim toward the wall, tuck your chin, do a quick somersault when you're about an arm's length away, plant your feet on the wall, push off on your back or side and rotate to your stomach as you glide.
how do i stop my goggles from fogging | foggy goggles fix | anti fog for goggles => Use anti fog spray or a drop of baby shampoo rinsed off, don't touch the inside of the lenses, and rinse them in the pool water before putting them on. Old goggles lose their coating.
how do i stop water getting in my nose | water up my nose swimming | breathing out through nose underwater => Breathe out slowly through your nose while your face is underwater, which keeps water out. A nose clip helps for backstroke and flips.
what is a dolphin kick | how to do a dolphin kick | butterfly kick => A dolphin kick moves both legs together in a wave, starting from your chest and hips. It's used in butterfly and underwater after starts and turns in other strokes.
what is long course vs short course | 25 yard pool vs 50 meter | swimming pool lengths => Long course pools are 50 meters, like at the Olympics. Short course pools are 25 meters or 25 yards, common for practice and US high school swimming.
who is the most decorated swimmer | michael phelps gold medals | phelps olympic records => Michael Phelps won 28 Olympic medals, 23 of them gold, more than any other athlete. He won 8 golds at the 2008 Beijing Games.
who is katie ledecky | katie ledecky medals | best female distance swimmer => Katie Ledecky is an American distance freestyle swimmer. As of 2025, she has won 14 Olympic medals, 9 of them gold, the most golds by any female swimmer.
what is the 100m freestyle world record | fastest 100 freestyle | pan zhanle world record => As of 2025, the men's long course 100 m freestyle world record is 46.40 seconds, set by China's Pan Zhanle at the 2024 Paris Olympics.
why do swimmers shave | swim caps why | why do swimmers wear caps => Swim caps and shaving reduce drag in the water, which can save fractions of a second. Caps also keep hair out of your face and protect it from chlorine.
why does chlorine turn hair green | swimmers green hair | protect hair from chlorine => The green tint comes from copper in pool water sticking to hair, not from chlorine itself. Wet your hair with clean water before swimming, wear a cap, and use a swimmer's shampoo.
how long should i wait to swim after eating | swimming after eating myth | can you get cramps swimming after eating => There's no evidence that swimming after eating causes dangerous cramps. A big meal might make you feel sluggish, so a short break is fine but not required.
what is open water swimming | open water vs pool | marathon swimming => Open water swimming happens in lakes, rivers or the ocean, without lane lines or walls. It's harder because of waves, currents and cold, so always swim with supervision and a bright cap or buoy.
what is synchronized swimming | artistic swimming | how does artistic swimming work => Artistic swimming, once called synchronized swimming, is a sport where swimmers perform choreographed routines to music, judged on difficulty, execution and artistry.
what is diving in the olympics | platform vs springboard | how high is olympic diving => Olympic divers compete from a 3 meter springboard and a 10 meter platform, judged on takeoff, flight and a clean entry with little splash.

# tag: sports.track
what are the events in track and field | track and field events list | what is a field event => Track events are races: sprints, middle distance, long distance, hurdles, relays and the steeplechase. Field events are jumps (high jump, pole vault, long jump, triple jump) and throws (shot put, discus, javelin, hammer).
how long is a running track | how many meters is one lap | 400 meter track => A standard outdoor track is 400 meters around in lane 1, so four laps is 1,600 meters, a bit under a mile.
why do runners start staggered | staggered start in track | why are lanes different starting points => Outer lanes are longer around curves, so runners in outer lanes start farther ahead. That way everyone runs the same distance.
what is the 200m world record | fastest 200 meters | usain bolt records => Usain Bolt holds the men's 200 m world record of 19.19 seconds and the 100 m record of 9.58, both set in 2009, as of 2025.
what is the women's 100m world record | fastest woman ever | florence griffith joyner record => Florence Griffith Joyner set the women's 100 m world record of 10.49 seconds in 1988, and it still stands as of 2025.
what is the marathon world record | fastest marathon ever | kelvin kiptum record => As of 2025, the men's marathon world record is 2:00:35, set by Kenya's Kelvin Kiptum in Chicago in 2023.
what is the mile world record | fastest mile ever | sub 4 minute mile => The men's mile world record is 3:43.13, set by Hicham El Guerrouj in 1999, as of 2025. Roger Bannister ran the first mile under four minutes in 1954.
what is the long jump world record | longest long jump | mike powell long jump => Mike Powell's long jump of 8.95 m (29 feet 4.25 inches), set in 1991, is still the men's world record as of 2025.
what is the high jump world record | highest high jump ever | javier sotomayor => Javier Sotomayor of Cuba set the men's high jump world record of 2.45 m in 1993, still standing as of 2025.
who is mondo duplantis | pole vault world record | how high is the pole vault record => Armand "Mondo" Duplantis of Sweden is the greatest pole vaulter ever. He has broken the world record many times, and as of 2025 it's over 6.2 m.
what is the fosbury flop | how do high jumpers jump | back first high jump => The Fosbury Flop is jumping over the bar head first and backward, arching your back. Dick Fosbury made it famous at the 1968 Olympics, and nearly every high jumper uses it now.
what is a decathlon | decathlon events | heptathlon events => The decathlon is 10 track and field events over two days, usually for men. The heptathlon is 7 events, usually for women. Points are scored in each event.
what is the steeplechase | steeplechase water jump | 3000m steeplechase => The steeplechase is a 3,000 m race with barriers and a water jump that runners leap over on each lap.
what is a false start in track | false start rule | one false start disqualification => A false start is moving before the starting gun. In top level track, one false start gets the runner disqualified.
how do relay handoffs work | relay baton pass | relay exchange zone => The baton must be passed within a marked exchange zone, 30 m long in most sprint relays. The incoming runner hands it off while the next runner is already speeding up.
how do i start a sprint | sprint start technique | starting blocks tips => In blocks, put your strong foot forward, hands just behind the line, then on "set" raise your hips a bit higher than your shoulders. On the gun, drive out low and push hard with both legs, rising gradually.
how do i run a mile faster | mile training | how to improve my mile time => Mix easy runs, interval workouts like 400 m repeats and longer steady runs. Learn to pace evenly instead of sprinting the start, and build up mileage slowly.
what is cross country running | cross country vs track | how long is a middle school cross country race => Cross country is distance running on grass, trails and hills instead of a track. Middle school races are often around 2 to 3 miles, and high school races are often 5 km.
how do i pace myself in a race | race pacing tips | don't start too fast => Start a little slower than you think and settle into a steady pace you can hold. Most people run their best times with even splits or a slightly faster second half.
what is a personal record | pr meaning in running | what does pb mean => A personal record, PR or personal best, PB, is your fastest time or best mark ever in an event.
how do i do a shot put | shot put technique | how heavy is a shot put => Hold the shot at the base of your fingers against your neck, keep your elbow up, push it out from your neck instead of throwing it, using your legs and hips. The men's shot is 7.26 kg, and youth shots are lighter.
what are running spikes | do i need track spikes | spikes vs running shoes => Spikes are light shoes with small metal pins on the front for grip on the track or cross country course. They help, but aren't required to start.
what is a 5k race | what is a 10k | fun run => A 5K is 5 kilometers, about 3.1 miles, and a 10K is 10 kilometers, about 6.2 miles. Fun runs are relaxed races, often for charity, great for a first race.
what is a side stitch | side cramp when running | how to stop a stitch => A side stitch is a sharp pain under the ribs while running. Slow down, breathe deeply, press on the spot and stretch the side. Avoiding big meals before running helps prevent them.

# tag: sports.golf
what is a birdie eagle and albatross | golf scores below par | what is a double eagle => A birdie is one under par on a hole, an eagle is two under, and an albatross, or double eagle, is three under. A hole in one on a par 4 counts as an albatross.
what is a hole in one | odds of a hole in one | ace in golf => A hole in one, or ace, is hitting the ball from the tee into the hole with one stroke. It's rare and usually happens on short par 3 holes.
what are golf clubs called | types of golf clubs | how many clubs can you carry in golf => The main types are woods (the driver is the biggest), hybrids, irons, wedges and the putter. Rules allow up to 14 clubs in the bag.
what is a handicap in golf | golf handicap meaning | how does a golf handicap work => A handicap is a number showing roughly how many strokes over par a golfer usually shoots. It lets players of different skill compete fairly by giving the weaker player extra strokes.
what are the four golf majors | golf major championships | what is the masters => The four men's majors are the Masters, the PGA Championship, the US Open and The Open Championship. The Masters is played every April at Augusta National in Georgia, and the winner gets a green jacket.
who has won the most golf majors | jack nicklaus majors | tiger woods majors => Jack Nicklaus won the most men's majors with 18. Tiger Woods is second with 15, as of 2025.
who won the 2025 masters | masters 2025 winner | rory mcilroy grand slam => Rory McIlroy won the 2025 Masters in a playoff, completing his career Grand Slam of all four majors.
why do golfers yell fore | what does fore mean | fore in golf => Golfers yell "fore" to warn people that a ball may be heading toward them. If you hear it, cover your head and duck.
what are the parts of a golf course | fairway rough green | what is a bunker => A hole starts at the tee box, then the fairway, the short mown path, with longer grass called the rough on the sides. The green has the hole, and bunkers are sand traps.
why do golf balls have dimples | golf ball dimples | how many dimples on a golf ball => Dimples make a thin layer of turbulent air that cling to the ball, reducing drag so it flies much farther than a smooth ball would. Most balls have around 300 to 400 dimples.
what is stroke play vs match play | golf formats | how does match play work => In stroke play, the lowest total strokes wins. In match play, players win individual holes, and whoever wins the most holes wins the match.
what is a mulligan in golf | mulligan meaning | is a mulligan allowed => A mulligan is a do over shot that friends sometimes allow in casual games. It's not allowed in official rules.
how do i hold a golf club | golf grip types | interlocking vs overlap grip => For a right handed golfer, put your left hand on top with the thumb pointing down the shaft, then the right hand below. In the overlap grip, the right pinky rests on the left index finger, and in the interlocking grip they hook together.
how do i get better at golf | golf tips for beginners | how to swing a golf club => Take a lesson to learn grip, stance and posture, keep your head steady, and swing smoothly instead of hard. Spend lots of time on putting and short shots, where most strokes happen.
what is mini golf | putt putt rules | how to win at mini golf => Mini golf is putting only, through fun obstacle holes. Most courses cap each hole at 6 strokes, and the lowest total wins. Read the slope and hit gently.
what is disc golf | disc golf rules | how does disc golf work => Disc golf is like golf but with flying discs thrown into metal chain baskets. Fewest throws wins, and many parks have free courses.
