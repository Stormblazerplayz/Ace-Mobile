# tag: life.howto.sleep
how do i get better sleep | tips for better sleep | how can i sleep better at night => Go to bed and wake up at the same times every day, keep your room cool, dark and quiet, put screens away about an hour before bed, and avoid caffeine in the afternoon and evening.
how many hours of sleep does a 12 year old need | sleep for preteens | how long should a kid my age sleep => Health experts recommend 9 to 12 hours a night for kids aged 6 to 12, and 8 to 10 hours for teens 13 to 18.
why can't i sleep on sunday nights | can't sleep before school | trouble sleeping after the weekend => Sleeping in late on weekends shifts your body clock, so Sunday night you aren't tired yet. Keeping your wake up time within about an hour of school days fixes most of this.
do screens keep you awake | does my ipad mess up my sleep | blue light and sleep => Using screens late keeps your brain busy and the light can delay sleepiness. Exciting games and videos make it worse, so stop screens about an hour before bed or switch to a book.
is it bad to sleep with my phone in my room | phone in bed at night | should i charge my phone in my bedroom => Notifications and the urge to check it can cut into sleep. Charging it outside your room, or at least across the room on Do Not Disturb, helps you sleep better.
what should i do if i wake up in the middle of the night | can't get back to sleep | woke up at 3am => Stay calm, keep lights low, and don't check your phone. Breathe slowly or think about something boring. If you're still awake after about 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet in dim light until you feel sleepy.
how do i stop having nightmares | i had a bad dream | scary dreams help => Nightmares are common and not real. Talk about it with someone, think of a happier ending to the dream, keep a calm bedtime routine and avoid scary shows before bed. If they happen a lot, tell a parent.
is it okay to take naps | how long should a nap be | do naps help => Short naps of 20 to 30 minutes can refresh you. Long naps or naps late in the afternoon can make it harder to fall asleep at night.
why do i feel tired even after sleeping | still tired after 8 hours | always tired in the morning => You might need more sleep than you're getting, your schedule may be shifting, or sleep quality may be poor from screens or noise. If you're tired a lot even with enough sleep, tell a parent so they can talk to a doctor.
what is a sleep schedule | how do i fix my sleep schedule | my sleep schedule is messed up => A sleep schedule means going to bed and waking up at the same times each day. To fix it, move your bedtime 15 minutes earlier every few nights and get sunlight soon after waking.
does caffeine affect sleep | can soda keep me awake | energy drinks and kids => Yes, caffeine can keep you awake for hours. It's in coffee, many sodas, iced tea and energy drinks. Pediatricians recommend kids avoid energy drinks completely.
what is the best room temperature for sleep | is a cold room better for sleep | too hot to sleep => A cool room, around 18 to 20 °C (65 to 68 °F), is best for most people. A fan helps on hot nights and blocks out noise too.
why do we dream | what are dreams | do dreams mean anything => Dreams mostly happen in REM sleep, when your brain is very active. Scientists think dreaming may help with memory and processing emotions, but nobody knows for sure what dreams mean.
what is sleepwalking | why do people sleepwalk | is sleepwalking dangerous => Sleepwalking is when someone walks or does things while still asleep, and it's more common in kids. It's usually harmless, but tell a parent so they can make sure the house is safe.
can i catch up on sleep on the weekend | does sleeping in fix being tired | sleep debt => A little extra sleep helps, but sleeping way in shifts your body clock. A steady schedule with enough sleep each night works much better than catching up.
what should i do before bed to relax | relaxing bedtime ideas | how to calm my mind at night => Take a warm shower, read a paper book, stretch gently, listen to calm music or try slow breathing. Writing tomorrow's to do list can stop your brain from going over it in bed.
how do i stop overthinking at night | thoughts keep me awake | my brain won't turn off => Write down what's on your mind in a notebook before bed so you can let it go. Try slow breathing, counting backward from 100 by threes, or imagining a calm place in detail.

# tag: life.howto.hygiene
how do i brush my teeth properly | right way to brush teeth | brushing technique => Use a pea sized bit of fluoride toothpaste, hold the brush at an angle to your gums and use gentle small circles on every tooth surface for two minutes. Brush your tongue too, and spit but don't rinse heavily so the fluoride keeps working.
when should i replace my toothbrush | how often to get a new toothbrush | toothbrush lifespan => Every three to four months, or sooner if the bristles are frayed or after you've been sick.
how do i floss | flossing steps | right way to floss => Wind about 45 cm (18 inches) of floss around your fingers, slide it gently between two teeth, curve it into a C around one tooth and move it up and down, then do the other tooth. Use a fresh section for each gap.
how do i get rid of bad breath | my breath smells | fresh breath tips => Brush twice a day, brush or scrape your tongue, floss, and drink water. Bad breath that doesn't go away can mean a tooth or gum problem, so tell a parent so they can check with a dentist.
when should i start wearing deodorant | do i need deodorant | deodorant vs antiperspirant => Start when you notice body odor, which often happens around ages 9 to 13 as puberty starts. Deodorant covers smell, and antiperspirant also reduces sweat.
how do i stop body odor | why do i smell | how to not smell after sports => Shower daily and after sports, wash armpits and feet with soap, wear clean clothes and socks, and use deodorant. Sweaty clothes left in a bag smell, so wash them soon.
how do i wash my face | face washing routine | how to stop pimples => Wash with lukewarm water and a gentle cleanser twice a day and after sweating, pat dry, and don't pick at pimples because it can scar. If acne bothers you, ask a parent about seeing a doctor.
how do i wash my hair | how often should i wash my hair | shampoo and conditioner how to use => Wet your hair, rub a small amount of shampoo into your scalp, rinse, then put conditioner on the ends and rinse. How often depends on your hair, from daily for oily hair to a few times a week for dry or curly hair.
how do i cut my nails | trimming fingernails | how to cut toenails => Use clean clippers after a shower when nails are soft. Cut fingernails in a gentle curve and toenails straight across to avoid ingrown nails, and don't cut too short.
how do i stop biting my nails | nail biting habit | quit biting nails => Keep nails short, notice when you do it, like when bored or nervous, and keep your hands busy with a fidget toy. Some people use a bitter tasting nail polish made for this.
how do i take a shower | how long should a shower be | shower routine => Wash your hair, then your body with soap, focusing on armpits, feet and private areas, rinse off and dry fully. About 5 to 10 minutes is plenty and saves water.
how do i clean my ears | should i use q tips in my ears | earwax removal => Ears clean themselves, so just wipe the outer ear with a washcloth. Don't stick cotton swabs inside, they push wax deeper and can hurt your eardrum.
how do i take care of braces | braces cleaning tips | what can't you eat with braces => Brush after meals, clean around the brackets with a small brush, and floss with a threader. Avoid hard, sticky and chewy foods like hard candy, caramel, gum and popcorn kernels.
how do i stay clean at camp | hygiene when camping | no shower at camp => Use wipes or a washcloth for your face, armpits and feet, change socks and underwear daily, brush teeth twice a day and use hand sanitizer before eating.
times i need to wash my hands | do i need to wash hands after using my phone => Wash before eating or cooking, after the bathroom, after touching animals or raw meat, after blowing your nose, and when you come home from public places.
how do i prevent athlete's foot | itchy feet after sports | stinky feet fix => Dry your feet well, especially between toes, wear clean socks that wick sweat, let shoes dry out between wears, and wear flip flops in locker rooms and pool showers.
what is puberty | when does puberty start | what happens during puberty => Puberty is when your body changes from a kid's to an adult's, usually starting between about 8 and 14. Growth spurts, body hair, sweat, voice changes and acne are all normal. A parent, doctor or school nurse can answer questions.

# tag: life.howto.fitness
how do i get stronger | how can a kid build muscle | exercises to get strong => Body weight exercises like push ups, squats, lunges, planks and pull ups build strength safely. Eat enough protein, get good sleep, and add a little more each week.
how do i do a proper push up | push up form | how to do push ups right => Hands a bit wider than shoulders, body in a straight line from head to heels, lower your chest close to the floor, then push up. If it's too hard, start on your knees or against a wall.
how do i do a pull up | can't do a pull up | how to get my first pull up => Practice dead hangs, slow lowering from the top (negatives), and pull ups with a resistance band for help. Doing a few sets several times a week builds up to a full pull up.
how do i do a squat | squat form for beginners | proper squat technique => Stand with feet shoulder width apart, push your hips back like sitting in a chair, keep your chest up and knees in line with your toes, go down as low as feels okay, then stand back up.
how do i do a plank | how long should i hold a plank | plank form => Rest on your forearms and toes with your body in a straight line, squeeze your stomach and don't let your hips sag. Start with 20 to 30 seconds and build up.
how do i get faster at running | how to run faster | sprint tips => Do short sprints with full rest, practice good form with arms pumping and quick light steps, and add hill runs. Strength exercises for legs help too.
how do i build endurance | get more stamina | how to run longer without getting tired => Run or bike a little longer each week, about 10 percent more, at an easy pace where you can still talk. Mix in some faster intervals once a week.
how do i stretch properly | stretching routine | when should i stretch => Before exercise, do dynamic stretches like leg swings and arm circles to warm up. After, do static stretches, holding each for about 20 to 30 seconds without bouncing.
how do i warm up before sports | warm up exercises | why warm up => Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes with light jogging, jumping jacks and dynamic stretches. It raises your heart rate and makes muscles ready, which helps prevent injuries.
how do i cool down after exercise | cool down routine | why cool down => Slowly walk or jog lightly for a few minutes so your heart rate comes down, then do some gentle stretches and drink water.
is it okay for kids to lift weights | weight training for kids | at what age can you lift weights => Strength training can be safe for kids with good form, light weights and adult supervision. Focus on technique and body weight moves first, and avoid trying for max lifts.
how do i get a six pack | abs workout for kids | how to get abs => Ab exercises like planks and crunches strengthen your core, but whether abs show mostly depends on body fat and genetics. A strong core matters more than visible abs, especially while you're growing.
what should i eat before sports | pre game snack | food before a game => Eat a meal with carbs and some protein 2 to 3 hours before, or a light snack like a banana or toast about an hour before. Avoid heavy, greasy food right before playing.
what should i eat after exercise | post workout snack | recovery food => Within an hour or two, have carbs and protein, like chocolate milk, a turkey sandwich, yogurt with fruit or eggs and toast. Drink plenty of water too.
do i need sports drinks | gatorade vs water | are sports drinks good for kids => For most activities under an hour, water is all you need. Sports drinks can help in long, hot, sweaty exercise, but they have lots of sugar.
how much water should i drink when exercising | staying hydrated in sports | how to not get dehydrated => Drink water before you start, sip every 15 to 20 minutes while active, and drink after. Drink more on hot days, and don't wait until you're very thirsty.
what are sore muscles after exercise | why am i sore two days later | doms meaning => That's called delayed onset muscle soreness, or DOMS. It's normal after new or harder exercise and usually peaks one to two days later. Gentle movement and stretching help, and it fades in a few days.
how do i know if an injury is serious | when to stop playing hurt | sports injury signs => Stop and tell a coach or parent if you have sharp pain, swelling, can't put weight on it, hear a pop, or the pain doesn't get better with rest. Never play through a possible head injury.
what is rice for injuries | how to treat a sprained ankle | what to do for a twisted ankle => For a mild sprain, rest it, put ice wrapped in a cloth on it for about 15 to 20 minutes at a time, use a compression wrap, and keep it raised. See a doctor if it's very swollen or you can't walk on it.
how do i jump higher | vertical jump training | jump higher for basketball => Do squat jumps, box jumps, lunges and calf raises, and practice your jump technique with a strong arm swing. Rest well between jump sessions.
how do i get more flexible | flexibility training | how to do the splits => Stretch a little every day after you're warm, holding each stretch about 30 seconds and breathing slowly. Never force it into pain. Splits take weeks or months of steady practice.
how much exercise should i get each day | daily activity for kids | 60 minutes of exercise => Health guidelines say kids and teens should get at least 60 minutes of moderate to vigorous activity every day, including muscle and bone strengthening activities like climbing and jumping at least three days a week.
what are fun ways to exercise | exercise that isn't boring | fun workouts for kids => Ride your bike, play tag or a sport with friends, swim, dance to music, hike, skateboard, jump rope or play active video games. The best exercise is one you enjoy enough to keep doing.
how do i do jumping jacks | what is a burpee | easy home exercises => For a jumping jack, jump your feet out wide while raising your arms overhead, then jump back. A burpee is a squat, then jump your feet back into a push up position, jump them in and leap up.
how do i jump rope | jump rope for beginners | jumping rope tips => Hold the handles at hip height, swing the rope with your wrists rather than your whole arms, and make small hops just high enough to clear it. Start with short sets and build up.
