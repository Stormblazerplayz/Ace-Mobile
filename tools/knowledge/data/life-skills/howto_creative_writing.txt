# tag: life.howto.writing
how do i write a short story | short story tips | writing a story for fun => Start with a character who wants something, add a problem that gets in the way, raise the stakes, and end with how it's solved or changed. Keep it focused on one main event.
how do i come up with story ideas | story idea generator | what should i write about => Ask "what if" questions, like "What if my bike could talk?" Combine two unrelated things, borrow from your own life, or start with an interesting character or place.
how do i create a good character | character development | making characters interesting => Give your character a goal, a fear, a flaw and something that makes them unique. Show who they are through their choices, not just descriptions.
how do i write good dialogue | writing dialogue tips | dialogue in stories => Make each character sound different, keep lines short, cut boring small talk, and use "said" most of the time. Reading dialogue out loud shows if it sounds natural.
what does show dont tell mean | show dont tell examples | showing vs telling => Instead of telling "He was scared," show it: "His hands shook as he gripped the handlebars." It lets readers feel the emotion themselves.
how do i write a good opening line | first line of a story | hook the reader => Start with action, a surprising statement, a question or a strong voice. Drop readers into a moment instead of starting with background.
how do i end a story | story ending ideas | good endings => A good ending resolves the main problem and shows how the character has changed. It can be happy, sad or surprising, but should feel earned.
how do i fix writers block | writers block tips | cant think what to write => Write badly on purpose, change your location, freewrite for 10 minutes, skip to a scene you're excited about, or go for a walk or ride and come back.
what is a first draft | rough draft tips | should my first draft be good => A first draft is your first full version, and it's supposed to be messy. Finish it first, then improve it in revisions.
how do i revise my writing | editing my story | revision tips => Read it out loud, cut parts that don't move the story, make verbs stronger, check that characters act consistently, and fix spelling and grammar last.
how do i build a fantasy world | worldbuilding tips | creating a fictional world => Decide the rules of your world, like magic or technology, its geography, cultures, history and conflicts. Reveal details through the story instead of long info dumps.
what is fan fiction | can i write fan fiction | fan fiction rules => Fan fiction is stories fans write using characters and worlds from books, games or shows. It's a fun way to practice writing. Sharing it free is common, but selling it can break copyright.
how do i write a comic script | writing for comics | comic script format => Break the story into pages and panels, describe what's in each panel, and write the dialogue and captions. Keep text short so art can do the work.
how do i write a script for a youtube video | youtube script tips | video script => Start with a strong hook in the first few seconds, keep sentences short and conversational, plan visuals for each part, and end with a clear wrap up.
how do i write a funny story | writing humor | how to be funny in writing => Use surprise, exaggeration, unexpected comparisons and characters who take silly things very seriously. Timing matters, so put the funny part at the end of a sentence.
how do i get better at writing | improve writing skills | become a better writer => Read a lot, write often, finish what you start, get feedback, and study writers you like. Copying a favorite author's style for practice can teach you a lot.
how do i share my writing safely | posting stories online | where to share my writing => Share with family, friends or school writing clubs, or use kid safe writing sites with a parent's approval. Use a pen name and don't share personal details.
what is a genre in writing | book genres | types of stories => Genres are categories like fantasy, science fiction, mystery, adventure, horror, historical fiction, realistic fiction and humor. Each has typical patterns readers expect.
how do i write a mystery | mystery story tips | writing a whodunit => Start with a puzzling crime or problem, plant clues and red herrings (false clues), give the detective obstacles, and reveal the answer in a way that's surprising but fair.
how do i write science fiction | sci fi writing tips | writing about the future => Pick one big "what if" about science or technology and explore how it changes people's lives. Keep your world's rules consistent.
# tag: life.howto.reading
how do i find books i will like | book recommendations | what should i read next => Think about books, shows and games you already love, ask a librarian, look at award lists, or try the first chapter of a few books before choosing.
how do i read more | reading habit | get into reading => Keep a book with you, read for 10 to 20 minutes before bed, try audiobooks and graphic novels, and drop books you don't enjoy. Reading what you like counts.
do audiobooks count as reading | audiobooks vs reading | listening to books => Audiobooks build vocabulary, comprehension and love of stories, so they absolutely count. Many people enjoy them on walks or car rides.
do graphic novels count as reading | are comics real reading | graphic novels for kids => Yes. Graphic novels build reading skills, vocabulary and the ability to follow complex stories using words and pictures together.
what is a book club | starting a book club | book club questions => A book club is a group that reads the same book and meets to talk about it. Good questions include what you liked, which character you related to and what you'd change.
how do i remember characters in a long book | keeping track of characters | too many characters in a book => Keep a quick list of characters with one note about each, use a bookmark with names, and look for a character list at the front of the book.
what are the newbery and caldecott medals | book awards for kids | newbery award => The Newbery Medal honors the most distinguished American children's book each year, and the Caldecott Medal honors the best illustrated picture book. Both are awarded by the American Library Association.
how do i write a book review | book review for kids | reviewing a book => Give the title and author, a short summary without spoilers, what you liked and didn't, and who would enjoy it. Give it a rating if you like.
