# tag: life.music.instruments
what is the lowest instrument in the orchestra | lowest pitched instrument | contrabassoon => The contrabassoon and the double bass play the lowest notes in a standard orchestra. Some pipe organs and tubas can go even lower.
what is distortion | overdrive vs distortion | fuzz pedal => Distortion is an effect that clips the guitar signal to make a gritty, crunchy sound, used in rock and metal. Overdrive is a milder version, and fuzz is a thick, buzzy one.
what is an acoustic electric guitar | acoustic electric | plug in acoustic => An acoustic electric guitar is an acoustic guitar with a built in pickup, so it sounds normal unplugged but can also plug into an amp or speaker for louder shows.
what is a classical guitar | nylon strings | classical vs acoustic => A classical guitar uses soft nylon strings and has a wider neck. It sounds warm and mellow and is gentler on beginners' fingers than steel strings.
what is a tambourine | tambourine | jingles => A tambourine is a small frame drum with metal jingles around the edge. You shake it or hit it against your hand.
what is a triangle instrument | triangle | triangle percussion => A triangle is a steel rod bent into a triangle and struck with a metal beater, making a bright ringing sound. It's part of the orchestra's percussion section.
what is a cajon | cajon | box drum => A cajón is a box shaped drum from Peru that you sit on and slap on the front. Hitting the center gives a bass sound and the edges a snappy one.
what is a gong | gong | tam tam => A gong is a large, flat metal disc hit with a padded mallet, making a deep, shimmering crash. Gongs come from East and Southeast Asia.
what is a kalimba | kalimba | thumb piano => A kalimba, or thumb piano, has metal tines on a wooden board or box that you pluck with your thumbs. It comes from African instruments like the mbira.
what is an ocarina | ocarina | zelda ocarina => An ocarina is a small, rounded wind instrument, often made of clay, with finger holes. It became famous with gamers through The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
what is a keytar | keytar | keyboard guitar => A keytar is a keyboard worn with a strap like a guitar, so the player can move around on stage. It was popular in 1980s pop music.
what is a grand piano | grand vs upright piano | piano types => A grand piano has horizontal strings in a long, curved case, giving a rich sound and fast key response. Upright pianos stand vertically and fit in smaller rooms.
how does a piano make sound | piano hammers | inside a piano => Pressing a key makes a felt covered hammer strike strings inside the piano. The strings vibrate, and a big wooden soundboard makes the sound louder.
what is a music stand | music stand | sheet music stand => A music stand is an adjustable holder for sheet music, set at eye level so you can read while playing with good posture.
how do i restring a guitar | restring guitar | string winding => Change one string at a time: loosen and remove it, thread the new one through the bridge, pull it to the right tuning peg, wind it neatly a few turns and tune up. Stretch new strings gently.
how do i clean a flute | clean flute | flute swab => After playing, take it apart and run a cleaning rod with a soft cloth through each piece to remove moisture. Wipe fingerprints off the outside with a soft cloth.
how do i store a guitar | guitar storage | humidity guitar => Keep it in a case or on a stand away from heaters, windows and damp places. Big changes in temperature and humidity can crack wood and change tuning.
what is intonation | guitar intonation | out of tune higher up => Intonation is how in tune an instrument is across all its notes. On a guitar, bad intonation makes chords sound sour higher up the neck, and it's fixed by adjusting the bridge saddles.
what is action on a guitar | string action | high action => Action is how high the strings sit above the frets. Low action is easier to press but may buzz, while high action is harder to play.
what is a fret buzz | fret buzz | buzzing strings => Fret buzz is a rattling sound when a string hits a fret it shouldn't. It can come from pressing too lightly, low action or a bent neck.
what is a whammy bar | tremolo arm | whammy bar => A whammy bar, or tremolo arm, is a lever on some electric guitars that bends all the strings' pitch up or down for wobbly, dive bomb effects.
what is a 7 string guitar | extended range guitar | 7 string => A 7 string guitar adds a lower string, usually tuned to B, below the low E. Metal and jazz players use it for a deeper range.
what is a ukulele size | soprano concert tenor ukulele | ukulele sizes => Ukuleles come in four main sizes: soprano (smallest), concert, tenor and baritone (largest). Larger ones have more room for fingers and a fuller sound.
what is a bugle | bugle | taps bugle => A bugle is a simple brass horn with no valves, so it plays only a few notes. Armies used it for signals, and it's still played for Taps at military funerals.
what is a conch shell instrument | conch | shell horn => Large sea shells like the conch can be blown like a horn after cutting a hole at the tip. They've been used for signals and ceremonies in many cultures.
what is a rainstick | rainstick | rain stick => A rainstick is a hollow tube with small pebbles or seeds inside and pins or thorns lining the walls. Tilting it makes a sound like falling rain.
what is a kazoo | kazoo | hum instrument => A kazoo is a small tube with a thin membrane. You hum into it, not blow, and the membrane buzzes to make a funny sound.
what is a hang drum | handpan | hang drum => A hang, or handpan, is a steel instrument shaped like two bowls stuck together, with tuned dents you tap with your fingers. It makes a soft, bell like sound.
what is the world's most expensive instrument | stradivarius | expensive violin => Some of the most expensive instruments are rare violins and violas made by Antonio Stradivari and Guarneri del Gesù, which have sold for over $10 million.
what is a stradivarius | stradivari | strad violin => A Stradivarius is a string instrument made by Antonio Stradivari in Italy in the late 1600s and early 1700s. Only several hundred survive, and they're prized for their sound.
what is a steinway | steinway piano | steinway => Steinway and Sons is a famous piano maker founded in New York in 1853. Steinway grand pianos are used in many top concert halls.
