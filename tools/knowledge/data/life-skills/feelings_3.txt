# tag: life.feelings.names
what are the basic emotions | list of emotions | how many emotions are there => Researchers often name a few basic emotions like happiness, sadness, fear, anger, surprise and disgust. People feel many more, like pride, guilt, excitement, jealousy and calm, often mixed together.
what is the difference between feelings and emotions | feelings vs emotions | emotion meaning => Emotions are the body and brain's quick reactions, like a racing heart when scared. Feelings are how we notice and name those reactions. People often use the words the same way.
can i feel two emotions at once | mixed feelings | happy and sad at the same time => Yes, mixed feelings are normal, like being excited and nervous before a trip, or happy and sad at a goodbye party.
why do i cry when i am happy | happy tears | crying when not sad => Strong emotions of any kind, including joy, relief or being moved, can trigger tears. It's a normal way the body releases big feelings.
why do i cry when i am angry | angry crying | tears when frustrated => Lots of people cry when angry or frustrated, because the body reacts to strong emotions the same way. It's normal. Taking a break and some deep breaths can help.
what is guilt | why do i feel guilty | guilt vs shame => Guilt is feeling bad about something you did, which can push you to make it right. Shame is feeling bad about who you are. It helps to remember a mistake is something you did, not who you are.
what is pride | feeling proud of myself | is it okay to be proud => Pride is a good feeling about something you or someone you care about did. Being proud of your effort is healthy. Bragging to put others down is different.
what is anxiety vs worry | worry vs anxiety | normal worry => Worry is thinking about something that might go wrong. Anxiety is a bigger feeling of nervousness that can show up in your body too, like a fast heart or stomachache. Everyone feels both sometimes.
what is frustration | why do i get frustrated | handling frustration => Frustration is the feeling when something blocks what you want, like a level you can't beat. Taking a break, breaking the task into steps or asking for help usually works better than pushing harder.
what is envy | envy vs jealousy | why do i feel envious => Envy is wanting what someone else has, like their skills or stuff. Jealousy is usually worry about losing something, like a friend, to someone else. Both are normal and pass faster when you talk about them.
what is loneliness | why do i feel lonely with people around | lonely in a crowd => Loneliness is feeling disconnected, even around people, if you don't feel understood. Having one or two close connections often matters more than many friends.
what is excitement vs anxiety | nervous or excited | turn nerves into excitement => Excitement and anxiety feel similar in the body, with a fast heart and energy. Some people find saying "I'm excited" instead of "I'm nervous" helps them feel more ready.
why do i feel angry when i am hungry | hangry | hungry and grumpy => When you're hungry, your blood sugar drops, which can make you cranky and less patient. People call it being hangry. A snack often fixes the mood.
why do i feel moody when i am tired | tired and grumpy | sleep and mood => Not enough sleep makes it harder to handle emotions, so small things feel bigger. Good sleep is one of the best mood boosters.
how do i name my feelings | feelings words | emotion vocabulary => Try a feelings wheel, which starts with basic emotions and gets more specific, like from mad to frustrated or annoyed. Naming a feeling clearly often makes it feel smaller.
what is a feelings wheel | emotion wheel | how to use a feelings wheel => A feelings wheel is a circle chart with basic emotions in the middle and more specific ones around the outside. It helps you find the exact word for what you feel.
# tag: life.feelings.coping
what are healthy ways to cope | coping skills list | coping strategies for kids => Healthy coping skills include deep breathing, exercise, talking to someone, drawing or writing, music, time outside, and taking a short break. Different ones work in different moments.
how do i journal about my feelings | feelings journal | journaling prompts => Write for a few minutes about what happened, how you felt and what might help. Prompts like "Today I felt... because..." or "One thing that went well was..." make starting easier.
what is a calm down corner | calm down kit | make a calm space => A calm down spot is a quiet place with things that help you settle, like a soft blanket, headphones, a stress ball, a drawing pad and a breathing card.
does going outside help my mood | nature and mood | time outside helps => Time in nature, even a short walk, can lower stress and improve mood. Sunlight in the morning also helps your sleep.
how does exercise help my mood | exercise and feelings | why do i feel better after a bike ride => Exercise releases chemicals like endorphins that boost mood and reduce stress. Even 10 to 20 minutes of biking or walking can help.
how do i calm down fast | quick ways to calm down | calm down in 1 minute => Try a few slow breaths with a longer breath out, splash cold water on your face, squeeze and release your fists, or count backwards from 20.
what is progressive muscle relaxation | tense and relax muscles | muscle relaxation exercise => Progressive muscle relaxation means tensing one muscle group for about 5 seconds, then relaxing it, moving from your toes to your head. It helps your body let go of stress.
what is a body scan | body scan meditation | noticing my body => A body scan means slowly paying attention to each part of your body, from your toes up, noticing how it feels without changing anything. It can help you relax and sleep.
what is positive self talk | talking to myself kindly | self talk examples => Positive self talk is speaking to yourself like a good friend would. Instead of "I'm terrible at this," try "This is hard, but I'm getting better with practice."
how do i stop negative thoughts | negative thinking | thoughts that bring me down => Notice the thought, ask if it's really true, and look for a more balanced view. For example, "Everyone hates me" might really be "One person was rude today."
how do i deal with a big disappointment | things didnt go my way | plans got cancelled => Let yourself feel it, talk about it, then think about what you can still do or look forward to. Disappointment hurts because you cared, and that's a good thing.
how do i handle unfairness | life is not fair | something unfair happened => Name what feels unfair and calmly talk to the person who can help, like a teacher or parent. Some things can be changed, and some can't, and knowing the difference helps.
how do i let go of a grudge | cant stop being mad at someone | forgive someone => Holding a grudge mostly hurts you. Talking it out, writing about it, or deciding to move on can help. Forgiving doesn't mean what happened was okay.
how do i deal with regret | i wish i had done something differently | regret something i did => Think about what you'd do differently next time and make things right if you can. Everyone has regrets. Learning from them is what matters.
how do i stop being so hard on myself | too self critical | beating myself up => Talk to yourself the way you'd talk to a friend who made the same mistake. Notice what you did well, not just what went wrong.
how do i handle a new step parent | blended family | new stepfamily => It's normal to feel unsure. Give it time, be polite, share your feelings honestly with your parent, and find small things in common. Relationships in blended families often take a while to grow.
what if i feel like nobody understands me | no one gets me | feeling misunderstood => That feeling is really common, especially in the teen years. Try sharing with one trusted person, a parent, relative, coach or school counselor. Finding people with your interests helps too.
what do i do if i feel stuck | dont know what to do with my life | feeling lost => It's okay not to have everything figured out. Try small new things, notice what you enjoy, and talk with someone you trust. Interests often grow from trying stuff.
how do i get through a hard time | going through something hard | tough times => Lean on people you trust, keep basic routines like sleep, food and exercise, take it one day at a time, and do small things you enjoy. Hard times don't last forever.
# tag: life.feelings.social
how do i handle drama in my friend group | friend group drama | friends gossiping => Stay out of gossip, don't pick sides in arguments you're not part of, be kind to everyone, and spend time with people who make you feel good.
how do i know if a friendship is healthy | toxic friend signs | bad friendship => A healthy friendship feels mostly good: you're respected, can be yourself and can say no. Warning signs are constant put downs, pressure, controlling behavior or feeling worse after hanging out.
how do i make up with my parents after a fight | fight with my mom or dad | apologize to parents => Wait until everyone is calm, apologize for your part, listen to their side, and explain your feelings without yelling. Most parents appreciate a sincere effort.
how do i tell someone they hurt my feelings | you hurt my feelings | talk about hurt feelings => Use an I statement, like "I felt hurt when you laughed at my drawing." Stay calm, say what you'd like instead, and give them a chance to respond.
what is an i statement | i statements examples | how to use i statements => An I statement shares how you feel without blaming: "I feel ___ when ___ because ___. I'd like ___." It makes people less defensive than "You always..."
how do i support a friend who is sad | friend is going through something | help a friend who is upset => Listen more than you talk, tell them you care, ask what would help, and spend time with them. If they mention hurting themselves, tell a trusted adult right away.
how do i deal with someone who is annoying | someone annoys me | annoying classmate => Take a breath, give yourself space, and be polite even if you're not close. If they're doing something that really bothers you, kindly tell them.
how do i get along with a teacher i dont like | teacher doesnt like me | problems with a teacher => Stay respectful, do your work, and ask for help when you need it. If something feels unfair, talk calmly to them or ask a parent to help.
how do i handle being the new kid | new kid at school | new to a school => Smile, say hi, join a club or team, ask questions about the school, and sit with someone at lunch. It often takes a few weeks to feel settled, which is normal.
what is social anxiety | scared of social situations | nervous around people => Social anxiety is strong worry about being judged in social situations, making it hard to talk or join in. Small steps and support from a trusted adult or counselor help a lot.
how do i say sorry | how to apologize properly | good apology => Say what you did, that you're sorry, how it affected them, and what you'll do differently. Skip "but" excuses. Then give them time.
how do i handle someone who wont accept my apology | apology not accepted | they are still mad => Give them time and space. You've done your part by apologizing sincerely. Show through your actions that you meant it.
how do i stand up for someone else | defend a friend | help someone being picked on => If it's safe, say something like "Leave them alone," invite the person to come with you, and tell a trusted adult. Being an upstander can make a huge difference.
how do i handle being different | feel different from everyone | not fitting in => Everyone feels different sometimes. Your interests and personality are what make you you. Look for people who share your interests, like clubs or online communities with parent approval.
# tag: life.feelings.self
how do i build self esteem | improve self esteem | feeling good about myself => Notice what you're good at, set small goals and reach them, spend time with people who lift you up, take care of your body, and treat yourself kindly.
what are my strengths | how to find my strengths | what am i good at => Think about what you enjoy, what feels easy, and what people ask you for help with. Strengths can be skills like drawing or traits like being kind or curious.
how do i stop worrying about what i look like | body image | dont like my body => Bodies change a lot during growing up, and every body is different. Focus on what your body can do, limit comparing to edited photos online, and talk to a trusted adult if it's bothering you a lot.
does social media affect how i feel | social media and mood | feel bad after scrolling => It can. Seeing highlight reels and edited photos can make people feel worse about themselves. Notice how you feel after scrolling and take breaks or unfollow accounts that make you feel bad.
how do i handle failing at something | i failed | failure is hard => Failing feels bad, but it's how everyone learns. Ask what you can learn from it, try a different approach, and remember one result doesn't define you.
how do i deal with being compared to my sibling | parents compare me | always compared to my brother or sister => It's frustrating. Calmly tell your parents how it makes you feel. Remember you have your own strengths, and you're running your own race.
how do i stop procrastinating because i am scared | fear of starting | avoiding hard tasks => Sometimes we avoid things because we're scared of doing them badly. Start with a tiny step, like 5 minutes, and remember a messy start is better than none.
how do i accept compliments | awkward when complimented | cant take compliments => Just smile and say "Thank you!" You don't need to argue with it or shrink it. Accepting a compliment is kind to the person who gave it.
what makes people happy | science of happiness | how to be happier => Research shows strong relationships, helping others, gratitude, exercise, good sleep, time outside and doing meaningful activities all boost happiness more than buying things.
how do i find a hobby i love | hobby ideas | finding my passion => Try lots of things: building, drawing, music, sports, coding, cooking, filming. Notice what makes you lose track of time. Passions often grow slowly from interests.
what is flow | being in the zone | losing track of time => Flow is when you're so absorbed in an activity, like riding a trail or drawing, that you lose track of time. It happens with challenges that match your skill.
is it okay to spend time alone | liking being alone | introvert vs extrovert => Yes. Some people recharge by being alone (more introverted) and others by being with people (more extroverted), and most are a mix. Both are normal.
what is an introvert | introvert meaning | am i an introvert => An introvert is someone who feels recharged by quiet or alone time and may prefer small groups. Introverts can still be social and confident.
how do i deal with a crush who doesnt like me back | crush rejected me | unrequited crush => It hurts, and it's okay to feel sad. Give yourself time, hang out with friends, and focus on things you enjoy. Rejection doesn't mean you aren't likable.
what should i do if i feel unsafe at home | not safe at home | someone at home hurts me => Tell a trusted adult outside your home, like a teacher, school counselor, coach or relative. If you're in danger, call 911. In the US, the Childhelp hotline is 1 800 422 4453.
what is 988 | suicide and crisis lifeline | crisis hotline number => In the US, 988 is the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. You can call or text 988 any time if you or someone you know is struggling or thinking about self harm. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
what is a crisis text line | text for help | can i text a hotline => In the US, you can text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line, or text 988. Trained counselors respond for free, 24/7.
when should i get help for my feelings | do i need help | feelings that dont go away => Talk to a trusted adult if sad, worried or angry feelings last for weeks, get in the way of school, sleep, eating or friends, or if you have thoughts of hurting yourself. Getting help is a strong move.
