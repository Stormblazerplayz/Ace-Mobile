# tag: life.howto.manners
how do i ask for the check | paying at a restaurant | ask for the bill => Make eye contact with your server, raise your hand slightly or say "Could we get the check, please?" when they come by. In some countries you have to ask, since they won't bring it automatically.
how do i use chopsticks | holding chopsticks | chopsticks for beginners => Rest the bottom chopstick in the crook of your thumb and on your ring finger so it stays still. Hold the top one like a pencil and move only it to pinch food against the bottom one.
which fork do i use | formal place setting | outside in silverware => At a formal table, use silverware from the outside in, one set per course. Forks are on the left, knives and spoons on the right, and dessert silverware is often above the plate.
how do i eat spaghetti neatly | twirl spaghetti | eating pasta politely => Twirl a few strands at a time against the plate or a spoon until they wrap into a small bite. Don't cut it into pieces or slurp, and keep a napkin in your lap.
how do i remember people's names | remembering names | forget names => Repeat the name when you meet them, like "Nice to meet you, Sam," use it once more in conversation, and link it to something about them.
how do i give a gift | giving gifts | gift etiquette => Hand it over with a smile and a quick message, like "I saw this and thought of you." Let them open it without pressure, and include a gift receipt for clothes.
how do i receive a gift i don't like | gift i don't like | polite gift reaction => Thank the person for thinking of you and mention something you appreciate, like the color or the effort. Kindness matters more than the gift itself.
how do i write a text to an adult | texting adults | text a coach => Start with a greeting and your name, keep it clear and polite, use full words instead of slang, and end with thanks. Example: "Hi Coach Lee, this is Sam. I'll be late to practice today. Thanks!"
how do i email a coach | email coach | email club leader => Use a clear subject line, greet them by name, introduce yourself and your team, ask your question briefly, and sign off with your name. Copy a parent if your team asks for that.
how do i ask for a recommendation letter | recommendation letter | ask teacher reference => Ask in person or by polite email at least a few weeks before the deadline. Explain what it's for, give them the details and deadline, and send a thank you note afterward.
how do i congratulate someone | congratulations message | congrats card => Say it sincerely and specifically, like "Congrats on the science fair! Your robot was awesome." A short card or message works too.
how do i give a toast | giving a toast | toast speech => Stand, raise your glass, get people's attention, and say a short, kind message about the person or event, maybe a quick story. End with "Cheers!" and keep it under a minute or two.
how do i tell a good story | storytelling | tell a story out loud => Start with a hook, keep the details that matter, build up to the exciting or funny part, and end with a quick wrap up. Practice it a few times, and watch your listeners' reactions.
how do i be funny | being funny | sense of humor => Notice everyday things that are surprising or silly, keep jokes short, and laugh at yourself. Good humor never puts others down, and timing improves with practice.
how do i end a conversation | leave a conversation | exit conversation politely => Wait for a natural pause, then say something like "It was great talking to you, I've got to go. See you later!" Smile and leave on a friendly note.
how do i say no to a sales person | pushy salesperson | no thanks salesperson => You can say "No thanks, I'm just looking" and keep walking. You don't owe an explanation, and it's fine to repeat yourself politely.
how do i order food delivery | food delivery | delivery app => With a parent's okay, pick a restaurant on an app or call it, choose items, check the address and total including fees and tip, and pay. Meet the driver at the door with an adult nearby.
how do i order takeout | takeout | pick up order => Call or order online, give your name and order, ask how long it'll take, then pick it up and pay at the counter. Check the bag before you leave.
how do i plan a picnic | picnic | picnic checklist => Pick a spot and check the weather, pack easy foods like sandwiches, fruit and snacks, plus water, a blanket, napkins, a trash bag and sunscreen. Keep cold foods in a cooler.
how do i plan a day trip | day trip | outing plan => Choose a destination, check opening times and costs, plan travel time both ways, pack snacks, water and a charger, and leave a buffer for traffic.
how do i entertain myself on a long car ride | car ride boredom | long drive => Download music, podcasts or audiobooks, bring a book or drawing pad, and play car games like I Spy or the license plate game. Look outside if you get queasy.
how do i pass time on a plane | plane boredom | long flight => Bring headphones, downloaded shows and games, a book, snacks and a refillable water bottle. Walk the aisle now and then on long flights and try to sleep on overnight ones.
how do i make friends at camp | camp friends | new friends summer => Introduce yourself to your cabin mates, join activities, sit with new people at meals and ask questions. Everyone's new at first, so being friendly goes a long way.
how do i write a letter home from camp | camp letter | write home => Share what you've been doing, a favorite moment, a new friend and something you're looking forward to. Bring pre addressed, stamped envelopes to make it easy.
how do i do laundry at camp | camp laundry | wash clothes trip => Many camps send clothes home or have a laundry day. Pack enough for the week, bring a mesh bag for dirty clothes, and label everything with your name.
how do i use a public restroom | public bathroom | restroom etiquette => Wait your turn, lock the stall, flush when done, wash your hands with soap for 20 seconds and toss paper towels in the trash. Kids should go with a buddy or adult nearby.
how do i ride an elevator politely | elevator etiquette | elevator manners => Let people get off before you get on, step to the back or side to make room, press buttons for others if you're by the panel, and keep your voice low.
how do i hold the door for someone | holding doors | door manners => If someone is a few steps behind you, hold the door until they reach it. Push doors are held from behind, and pull doors can be held open while you let them through first.
how do i be polite on public transit | bus etiquette | give up seat => Let people off before boarding, move to the back, keep your backpack off seats, use headphones, and offer your seat to older, pregnant or disabled riders.
how do i behave at a movie theater | movie theater etiquette | phone at movies => Silence and put away your phone, keep quiet during the movie, don't kick seats, and clean up your trash when you leave.
how do i behave at a concert | concert etiquette | first concert => Arrive on time, stay with your group, protect your ears with earplugs at loud shows, and don't block others' views by holding up your phone the whole time.
how do i behave at a wedding | wedding etiquette | kids at weddings => Be on time, stay quiet during the ceremony, follow seating and dress instructions, don't take photos if asked not to, and congratulate the couple. Have fun at the reception.
how do i behave at a funeral | funeral etiquette | first funeral => Dress in simple, dark clothes, sit quietly, follow what others do, and keep your phone off. Saying "I'm sorry for your loss" to the family is enough.
how do i behave at a religious service | visiting church | visiting mosque temple => Dress modestly, arrive on time, sit quietly, and follow what others do, like standing or sitting. It's fine not to join prayers you don't know. Ask your host about any customs.
how do i behave at a sports game | fan etiquette | sportsmanship fans => Cheer for your team without booing or insulting players, refs or other fans, stay in your seat during play, and clean up after yourself.
how do i cheer for my team | cheering | good fan => Wear team colors, clap and cheer for good plays, join chants that are positive, and encourage players after mistakes. Never trash talk kids or refs.
how do i volunteer at an event | event volunteer | volunteering day => Sign up ahead, arrive on time, ask what job you have, and follow directions from organizers. A positive attitude and helping wherever needed make you a great volunteer.
how do i run a meeting | club meeting | meeting agenda => Share a short agenda, start on time, let everyone speak, keep the conversation on topic, decide next steps and who does what, and end on time.
how do i make a flyer | flyer | event flyer => Use a big, clear headline, the key details (what, when, where), an eye catching image and lots of empty space. Make text big enough to read from a few steps away.
