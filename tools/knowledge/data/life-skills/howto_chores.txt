# tag: life.howto.laundry
how do i sort laundry | how should i separate my clothes before washing | do i wash whites and colors together => Sort into whites, lights, darks and bright colors, and keep towels apart from clothes. Wash new dark or red clothes on their own the first few times because their dye can bleed onto lighter things.
should i wash clothes in hot or cold water | what water temperature for laundry | cold wash or hot wash => Cold water is fine for most everyday clothes and helps stop shrinking and fading. Use warm or hot for whites, towels, sheets and very dirty or smelly things, but always check the care label first.
how much laundry detergent do i use | how much soap goes in the washer | how much detergent for one load => Use the amount marked on the cap or scoop, usually the first line for a normal load. Too much soap leaves clothes stiff and the machine smelly, it does not get them cleaner.
what do the symbols on clothing tags mean | how do i read a laundry care label | what does the tub symbol on my shirt mean => A tub means machine wash, and the dots inside show the heat (one dot cool, more dots hotter). A hand in the tub means hand wash, a triangle is about bleach, a square with a circle is about the dryer, and an iron is about ironing. A big X through any symbol means don't do it.
how do i use a dryer | how long do clothes go in the dryer | what dryer setting should i use => Clean the lint trap first, put in damp clothes without stuffing it, and pick a setting that matches the care label, like low heat for delicates or normal for towels. Most loads take around 40 to 60 minutes.
why do i need to clean the lint trap | where is the lint filter on a dryer | is dryer lint dangerous => Pull out the lint screen, usually just inside the door or on top, and wipe the fuzz off before every load. A clogged lint trap makes drying slow and can cause a dryer fire.
how do i hang clothes to dry | how do i air dry my clothes | can i dry clothes without a dryer => Shake each item out, then hang it on a hanger or drying rack with space around it so air can move. Lay sweaters flat so they don't stretch, and keep bright colors out of strong sun so they don't fade.
how do i fold a t shirt | best way to fold a shirt | how do you fold a tshirt neatly => Lay the shirt face down and smooth it out. Fold each side in so the sleeves sit along the back, then fold the bottom up to the collar in halves or thirds.
how do i fold pants | how do you fold jeans | best way to fold trousers => Lay the pants flat, fold one leg over the other so they line up, then fold them in half or thirds from the bottom up toward the waist.
how do i fold a fitted sheet | fitted sheet folding trick | how do you fold the sheet with elastic => Turn it inside out, tuck each corner into the next so all four are stacked, then lay it flat as a rectangle and fold it into a square. It doesn't have to be perfect to fit in a closet.
how do i fold socks | should i ball up my socks | how do i keep socks in pairs => Lay the two socks on top of each other, fold them in half or roll them up from the toe. Balling them by turning the cuff over stretches the elastic over time.
how do i hand wash clothes | how to wash something by hand | wash a sweater by hand => Fill a sink with cool or lukewarm water and a little gentle detergent, swish the item around for a few minutes, then rinse in clean water until no soap is left. Press the water out instead of wringing and lay it flat to dry.
why did my clothes shrink | how do i stop clothes from shrinking | why is my shirt smaller after washing => Heat is the main cause, especially hot water and high dryer heat on cotton and wool. Wash in cold water and air dry or use low heat to keep things their size.
my white shirt turned pink in the wash | how do i fix clothes that got dyed in the wash | color ran onto my clothes => Don't put them in the dryer. Rewash right away in cold water with detergent, or soak in a color remover or oxygen bleach made for laundry, following the box directions, and ask an adult to help.
how often should i wash my jeans | do jeans need washing every time | how many wears before washing pants => Jeans can usually be worn several times before washing unless they get dirty or smelly. Wash them inside out in cold water to keep the color.
how often should i wash my sheets | how often to change bed sheets | when should i wash my bedding => About once a week is a good habit, or every two weeks at most. Wash them more often if you are sick or sweat a lot.
how often should i wash towels | when do towels need washing | how many times can you use a towel => Wash bath towels after about three or four uses, and hang them to dry fully between uses so they don't get musty.
how do i get the smell out of my sports clothes | why does my gym shirt still stink after washing | how to get sweat smell out of clothes => Wash them soon after wearing instead of leaving them in a bag, turn them inside out, and use cold or warm water. Soaking in water with a cup of white vinegar for 30 minutes before washing helps, and skip fabric softener on sports fabric because it traps smell.
what does fabric softener do | do i need fabric softener | dryer sheets what are they for => Fabric softener and dryer sheets make clothes feel softer and cut static cling. They are optional, and you should skip them on towels and athletic wear because they make those absorb less.
what is the delicate cycle | when should i use the gentle cycle | what are the washer settings for => Delicate or gentle uses slow spinning for thin or stretchy things. Normal is for everyday clothes, and heavy duty is for very dirty or sturdy items like towels and jeans.
can i put shoes in the washing machine | how do i wash my sneakers | how to clean dirty shoes => Many canvas and fabric sneakers can go in the washer in a pillowcase or mesh bag on a cold gentle cycle, with the laces and insoles out. Let them air dry, never in the dryer, and clean leather shoes by hand with a damp cloth.
how do i iron a shirt | how to use a clothes iron | ironing for beginners => Ask an adult first. Set the iron to the heat on the care label, start with the collar, then cuffs and sleeves, then the body, moving smoothly and never leaving the iron sitting on fabric. Stand it up on its heel and unplug it when done.
how do i get wrinkles out without an iron | no iron how to fix wrinkles | quick way to unwrinkle clothes => Hang the item in the bathroom while you take a hot shower so the steam relaxes it, or toss it in the dryer for 10 minutes with a damp washcloth. Spritzing with water and smoothing it by hand also helps.
what happens if i leave wet clothes in the washer | forgot laundry in the washer | clothes smell musty after washing => Wet clothes left too long start to smell musty or mildewy. Rewash them with detergent, and move clothes to the dryer or rack within an hour or so next time.
how do i wash a stuffed animal | can stuffed animals go in the washer | clean a plush toy => Many can go in a pillowcase on a cold gentle cycle, then air dry. If it has a music box, glued parts or it's very old, spot clean it by hand with a damp cloth and mild soap instead.
how do i wash a hat | how to clean a baseball cap | can you put a cap in the washer => Hand wash it in cool water with a little mild detergent, scrub the sweatband with a soft toothbrush, rinse and let it dry over something round like a bowl so it keeps its shape.
how do i wash a backpack | cleaning a school bag | can a backpack go in the washer => Empty every pocket, shake out crumbs and check the tag. Many fabric backpacks can be hand washed or put on a gentle cold cycle, then hung upside down with the pockets open to dry.

# tag: life.howto.dishes
how do i wash dishes by hand | how to do the dishes | handwashing dishes steps => Scrape food into the trash, fill the sink with hot soapy water, and wash from cleanest to dirtiest: glasses, then plates and bowls, then pots and pans. Rinse in clean water and let them dry on a rack.
what order do you wash dishes | which dishes do you wash first | dish washing order => Start with glasses and cups, then silverware, then plates and bowls, and do greasy pots and pans last. That way the water stays cleaner for the things you drink and eat from.
how do i load a dishwasher | where do things go in a dishwasher | dishwasher loading tips => Cups, glasses and plastic go on the top rack facing down. Plates, bowls and pots go on the bottom facing the center sprayer. Put forks and spoons handle down, but knives handle up so you don't get cut.
can i use dish soap in the dishwasher | what happens if you put dish soap in a dishwasher | regular soap in dishwasher => No. Hand dish soap makes huge amounts of suds that can overflow onto the floor. Only use detergent made for dishwashers.
what can't go in the dishwasher | is it safe to put this in the dishwasher | dishwasher safe meaning => Keep out sharp knives, wooden spoons and cutting boards, cast iron pans, nonstick pans unless marked safe, insulated mugs and anything hand painted. Look for a dishwasher safe label underneath.
how do i clean a burnt pan | how to get burnt food off a pot | scrub a scorched pan => Fill the pan with water and a spoonful of baking soda, let it soak or simmer for a few minutes with an adult, then scrub with a non scratch sponge. Don't use steel wool on nonstick pans.
how do i clean a cast iron pan | can you use soap on cast iron | cast iron care => Wash it with hot water and a little soap if needed, scrub off stuck bits, then dry it right away on the stove. Rub a thin layer of oil on it so it doesn't rust, and never soak it or put it in the dishwasher.
how do i dry dishes | should i towel dry dishes or air dry | drying dishes after washing => Air drying on a rack is the most hygienic. If you towel dry, use a clean, dry towel and put dishes away only when fully dry.
how often should i replace a kitchen sponge | do sponges get dirty | when to throw out a sponge => Kitchen sponges hold lots of bacteria, so replace them about every week or two, and sooner if they smell. Squeeze them out and let them dry between uses.
how do i unload the dishwasher | dishwasher unloading tips | which rack do i empty first => Empty the bottom rack first so water from the top rack cups doesn't drip onto the clean bottom dishes. Make sure everything is dry before stacking it away.
why are my glasses cloudy after the dishwasher | cloudy glasses fix | white film on dishes => Cloudiness is usually hard water minerals. Use a rinse aid and don't use too much detergent, and a soak in white vinegar can remove the film.

# tag: life.howto.bedroom
how do i put a fitted sheet on a bed | how to make a bed with a fitted sheet | put sheets on the bed => Find the corners of the fitted sheet by the elastic seams, pull one corner over a mattress corner, then do the opposite corner, then the last two. Smooth it out so there are no wrinkles.
how do i put on a duvet cover | easiest way to put a comforter in a cover | duvet cover trick => Turn the cover inside out, reach inside and grab the two far corners, then pinch the comforter's corners through the cover. Shake it so the cover flips right side out over the comforter, then button it.
how do i put a pillowcase on | pillowcase trick | how to put a pillow in a case => Fold the pillow in half like a taco, push it into the case all the way to the end, then let it spring open and wiggle the case on the rest of the way.
how do i make hospital corners | what are hospital corners on a bed | tuck sheets neatly => Tuck the end of the flat sheet under the foot of the mattress, then lift the side edge up into a triangle, tuck the hanging part under, and drop the triangle down and tuck it. It looks neat and stays put.
how do i flip my mattress | should i rotate my mattress | how often do you turn a mattress => Many modern mattresses are one sided, so rotate them head to foot every few months instead of flipping. Check the label, and get help because mattresses are heavy.
how do i dust my room | best way to dust | what do i use for dusting => Dust from top to bottom, like shelves before desks before the floor, using a slightly damp cloth or microfiber cloth that traps dust instead of spreading it. Vacuum or sweep last.
how do i vacuum properly | vacuuming tips | how to vacuum a carpet => Pick up small things first, then vacuum slowly in overlapping rows, going over busy spots twice. Use the hose tool for corners and edges, and empty the bin or bag when it's getting full.
how do i sweep the floor | how to use a broom and dustpan | sweeping tips => Sweep from the edges of the room toward the middle in short strokes, gather it into one pile, and sweep it into the dustpan held flat to the floor. A damp paper towel picks up the last line of dust.
how do i mop a floor | how to mop | mopping tips => Sweep or vacuum first, then mop with warm water and a little floor cleaner, wringing the mop well so it's damp, not soaking. Start at the far corner and work back toward the door so you don't step on the wet floor.
how do i clean a mirror without streaks | streak free mirror | wipe a window or mirror clean => Spray glass cleaner or a mix of water and a little vinegar onto a microfiber cloth, wipe top to bottom, then buff with a dry part of the cloth. Too much cleaner causes streaks.
how do i clean under my bed | stuff under my bed | how to keep under the bed clean => Pull everything out, sort into keep, donate and trash, then vacuum or sweep. Store only a few things there in bins so you can clean easily next time.
how do i get rid of a bad smell in my room | why does my room smell | make my room smell nice => Find the source first, like dirty clothes, old food or trash, and get it out. Open a window to air it out, wash bedding, and put an open box of baking soda on a shelf to soak up smells.
how do i keep my room clean | how do i stop my room getting messy | daily habits for a tidy room => Give everything a home, put things back as soon as you're done, and do a 5 minute tidy each night. A laundry basket and a trash can in the room make it much easier.
how do i clean my desk | organize and clean my desk | my desk is cluttered => Clear everything off, wipe the surface, then only put back what you use every day. Put supplies in a cup or drawer and recycle old papers.
how do i clean my computer keyboard | how to clean a keyboard | crumbs in my keyboard => Unplug it or turn it off, turn it upside down and gently shake it, then use a soft brush or compressed air between keys. Wipe the tops with a cloth that's barely damp with rubbing alcohol.
how do i clean my ipad screen | how to clean a tablet screen | wipe fingerprints off ipad => Turn it off, then wipe with a soft, lint free cloth that's slightly damp with water. Apple says 70 percent isopropyl alcohol wipes are okay too, but don't spray liquid directly on it or use harsh cleaners.
how do i clean headphones | clean my earbuds | how to clean airpods => Wipe them with a dry or slightly damp soft cloth, and gently clean mesh and grills with a dry cotton swab or soft brush. Keep water out of the openings.

# tag: life.howto.kitchen
how do i clean the microwave | how to get stuck food out of the microwave | easy microwave cleaning => Heat a microwave safe bowl of water with a splash of vinegar or lemon for about 3 minutes, then leave the door shut for a few minutes. The steam softens the mess so you can wipe it out easily. Be careful, the bowl will be hot.
how do i clean the kitchen counter | wiping down counters | best way to clean kitchen counters => Clear the counter, wipe away crumbs, then clean with warm soapy water or a kitchen spray and dry it. After raw meat touches a counter, use a disinfecting cleaner.
how do i take out the trash | trash day chores | how to change a trash bag => Tie the full bag closed, lift it straight out, and take it to the outdoor bin. Put a new bag in and check that the can is clean. Rinse recycling before it goes in its bin.
how do i clean out the fridge | cleaning the refrigerator | how often should i clean the fridge => Throw out expired or moldy food, take out shelves and drawers to wash in warm soapy water, wipe the inside and dry everything. A quick check once a week and a deep clean every few months works well.
how do i clean a stove top | cleaning the stove | how to clean burner spills => Wait until it's fully cool. Wipe glass tops with a cleaner made for them, and soak removable burner grates in hot soapy water before scrubbing. Spills come off easiest when cleaned soon.
how do i clean a toaster | crumbs in the toaster | how to empty toaster crumbs => Unplug it and let it cool, then slide out the crumb tray at the bottom and empty it. Never stick a fork or knife inside a toaster.
how do i set the table | where does the fork go | how to set a table properly => Put the plate in the middle, the fork on the left, and the knife on the right with the blade facing the plate. The spoon goes right of the knife, the glass above the knife, and the napkin on the left or on the plate.
how do i clear the table after dinner | helping after dinner | after dinner chores => Carry plates to the kitchen, scrape leftover food into the trash or compost, and stack dishes by the sink. Put leftovers in containers in the fridge, then wipe the table.

# tag: life.howto.bathroom
how do i clean a toilet | how to scrub a toilet | toilet cleaning steps => Put on gloves, squirt toilet cleaner under the rim, let it sit a few minutes, then scrub the bowl with a toilet brush and flush. Wipe the seat, lid and outside with a disinfecting wipe or spray.
how do i clean a bathroom sink | bathroom sink cleaning | toothpaste stuck in the sink => Rinse the sink, spray it with bathroom cleaner, scrub with a sponge or old toothbrush around the drain and faucet, then rinse and dry it. Dried toothpaste wipes off easily with warm water.
how do i clean a shower | how to scrub a bathtub | soap scum removal => Spray the walls and tub with a bathroom cleaner and let it sit a few minutes, then scrub and rinse. Squeegeeing the walls after showers keeps soap scum and mildew from building up.
what do i do if the toilet is overflowing | toilet is about to overflow | stop a toilet from flooding => Don't flush again. Take the lid off the tank and push the rubber flapper at the bottom down to stop the water, or turn the valve behind the toilet clockwise to shut off water. Then get an adult.
how do i unclog a toilet | how to use a plunger | toilet is clogged => Use a flange plunger, the kind with an extra rubber flap. Put it over the hole so it seals, then push and pull firmly up and down several times without breaking the seal. Ask an adult if it doesn't clear.
never mix bleach and ammonia | can you mix cleaning products | is it dangerous to mix bleach with other cleaners => Never mix cleaners. Bleach mixed with ammonia, vinegar or other acids makes toxic gases. Use one product at a time, keep the room aired out, and let adults handle bleach.
how do i hang a towel so it dries | why do my towels smell | how to stop towels smelling => Spread towels out flat on a bar or hook instead of bunching them, so air can reach them. Damp bunched towels grow bacteria that smell musty, so wash them if they do.

# tag: life.howto.cleaning
what is the fastest way to clean my room | clean my room in 10 minutes | speed clean my room => Set a timer for 10 minutes. Put trash in a bag, dirty clothes in the hamper, dishes back to the kitchen, then put stuff back in its spots and make the bed. Starting with the biggest things makes the room look clean fast.
how do i start cleaning when it is really messy | my room is too messy to start | overwhelmed by a messy room => Pick one small zone, like just the bed or one corner, and finish it before moving on. Use three piles or boxes: keep, donate and trash, and play music to keep going.
how do i declutter my room | how do i get rid of stuff | what should i throw away => Ask about each thing: do I use it, do I love it, would I buy it again? If not, donate, sell or recycle it. Start with easy things like broken items, trash and clothes that don't fit.
what is a chore chart | how do i make a chore schedule | chore list for kids => A chore chart lists jobs and the days they're done, like making the bed daily and vacuuming on Saturday. Check off each job when it's finished so you can see your progress.
what chores can a 12 year old do | chores for kids my age | what jobs can i do around the house => At around 12 you can do laundry, load and unload the dishwasher, vacuum, take out trash and recycling, clean the bathroom, make simple meals, feed pets and help with yard work.
how do i clean up a spilled drink | wipe up a spill | spilled juice on the carpet => Blot it up right away with paper towels or a cloth, pressing instead of rubbing. For carpet, blot with a little cool water and dish soap mixed, then blot with plain water and dry.
how do i clean up broken glass | i broke a glass | picking up glass safely => Get an adult if you can, and put on shoes. Pick up big pieces carefully, sweep the small bits into a dustpan, then press a damp paper towel or slice of bread on the spot to catch tiny shards. Wrap the glass in paper before throwing it out.
what is the best all purpose cleaner | what do i clean with | what is a good homemade cleaner => Warm water with a little dish soap cleans most surfaces safely. A spray of half water and half white vinegar works on glass and many counters, but not on stone like granite or marble.
how do i get dog hair off the couch | pet hair removal | how to remove cat hair from clothes => Use a lint roller, a slightly damp rubber glove swiped across the fabric, or the vacuum's upholstery tool. For clothes, a dryer cycle with no heat loosens pet hair before you roll it.
how do i wash windows | window cleaning tips | how to clean windows without streaks => Wash on a cloudy day so the cleaner doesn't dry too fast. Wipe off dust, spray glass cleaner, then wipe with a microfiber cloth or squeegee in an S shape from the top down.
how do i clean a whiteboard | dry erase marker won't come off | clean a dry erase board => Use a whiteboard eraser or a dry microfiber cloth, then a whiteboard cleaner or a cloth with a little rubbing alcohol for ghost marks. Writing over an old mark with a dry erase marker and wiping can lift it too.
how do i clean my glasses | how to clean eyeglasses | best way to clean lenses => Rinse them under lukewarm water, rub with a drop of dish soap, rinse and dry with a clean microfiber cloth. Shirts and paper towels can scratch the lenses.
how do i clean a water bottle | my water bottle smells | cleaning a reusable bottle => Wash it every day with hot soapy water and a bottle brush, including the lid and straw. If it smells, soak it with a spoonful of baking soda or some white vinegar and water, then rinse well and let it dry open.
how do i clean my lunch box | lunchbox smells | washing a lunch bag => Empty it every day, wipe the inside with warm soapy water and dry it with the lid open. A little baking soda paste removes stuck smells.

# tag: life.howto.yard
how do i rake leaves | raking tips | best way to rake a yard => Rake in one direction with the wind behind you, pulling leaves into piles on a tarp. Drag the tarp to the bin or compost, and wear gloves so you don't get blisters.
how do i shovel snow | snow shoveling tips | how to shovel a driveway safely => Push the snow instead of lifting when you can, and lift small loads with your legs, not your back. Take breaks, stay warm and drink water.
how do i water plants outside | when should i water the garden | best time to water plants => Water in the early morning so less evaporates and leaves dry during the day. Water deeply at the base of the plant a few times a week instead of a little every day.
how do i pull weeds | weeding the garden | best way to get rid of weeds => Pull weeds after it rains when the soil is soft, grabbing low by the base and pulling slowly to get the roots. Wear gloves, and ask an adult if a plant might be prickly or poisonous.
can a kid mow the lawn | at what age can you use a lawn mower | is mowing safe for kids => Safety groups suggest kids be at least 12 to use a push mower and 16 for a riding mower, with an adult teaching them first. Always wear closed shoes and eye protection, and never put hands near the blade.
how do i wash a car | car washing steps | can i wash my parents car => Rinse the car, then wash from the top down with car soap and a mitt, using one bucket for soap and one for rinsing. Rinse and dry with a microfiber towel, out of direct sun so it doesn't spot. Dish soap can strip the wax.

# tag: life.howto.pets
how often should i feed my dog | how much food does a dog need | feeding a dog => Most adult dogs eat twice a day, and puppies three or four times. The right amount depends on size and the food, so follow the bag's chart and ask the vet.
how do i take care of a goldfish | goldfish care | do goldfish need a filter => Goldfish need a big tank with a filter, not a small bowl, because they make lots of waste. Feed a pinch they can eat in a couple of minutes once or twice a day, and change part of the water weekly.
how often should i clean a fish tank | changing fish tank water | how to clean an aquarium => Change about 10 to 25 percent of the water every week or two, using dechlorinated water at the same temperature. Don't change all the water at once, it removes the good bacteria the fish need.
how do i clean a litter box | how often do you scoop cat litter | litter box chores => Scoop clumps out every day and add fresh litter as needed. Fully empty and wash the box with mild soap and water every couple of weeks. Wash your hands afterward.
how do i walk a dog | dog walking tips | how long should i walk my dog => Use a leash and harness that fit, bring poop bags, and let the dog sniff, which is good exercise for their brain. Many dogs need at least 30 minutes to two hours of activity a day depending on breed.
how do i take care of a hamster | hamster care for beginners | what do hamsters need => Hamsters need a large cage with deep bedding to dig in, a solid wheel, a hide, fresh water and hamster food plus small bits of fresh veggies. Syrian hamsters must live alone and they're awake mostly at night.
how do i give my dog a bath | washing a dog | how often should a dog get a bath => Brush out tangles, wet the dog with lukewarm water, lather with dog shampoo while avoiding the eyes and ears, then rinse really well and towel dry. Most dogs only need a bath every month or two unless they get dirty.
how do i pet a dog i don't know | is it okay to pet a strange dog | how to meet a new dog => Always ask the owner first. Let the dog sniff your closed hand, then pet the side of its neck or chest, not the top of the head. Leave it alone if it backs away, growls or looks stiff.
how do i know if my pet is sick | signs my pet is sick | when should a pet go to the vet => Watch for not eating, hiding, low energy, throwing up, diarrhea, trouble breathing or limping. Tell an adult right away so they can call the vet.
what foods are bad for dogs | can dogs eat chocolate | what can't dogs eat => Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol sweetener, macadamia nuts and alcohol can all be poisonous to dogs. If a dog eats one, tell an adult to call the vet.
how do i train my dog to sit | teach a dog to sit | dog trick training => Hold a treat near the dog's nose, slowly move it up and back over its head so its bottom goes down, then say "sit" and give the treat as soon as it sits. Practice short sessions a few times a day.
