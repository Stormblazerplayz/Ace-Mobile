# tag: life.sports.terms
what is a heelflip | how to do a heelflip | heelflip skateboard => A heelflip is a flip trick where you ollie and flick the board with your front heel off the toe side edge, so it spins the opposite way from a kickflip.
what is a varial kickflip | varial flip | kickflip plus shove it => A varial kickflip combines a pop shove it with a kickflip, so the board spins 180 degrees and flips at the same time.
what is a tre flip | 360 flip skateboard | tre flip meaning => A tre flip, or 360 flip, combines a 360 degree shove it with a kickflip. It's considered one of the classic harder flat ground tricks.
what is a boardslide | skateboard boardslide | how to boardslide a rail => A boardslide is sliding along a rail or ledge on the middle of the board, between the trucks, with the board across the obstacle.
what is a 50 50 grind | 50 50 skateboard | 5050 grind => A 50 50 is grinding along a ledge or rail with both trucks on it. It's usually one of the first grinds skaters learn.
what is a kickturn | how to kick turn | skateboard kickturn => A kickturn is pressing on the tail to lift the front wheels and pivoting the board to turn. It's a key skill for ramps and tight spots.
what is fakie | riding fakie | fakie vs switch => Fakie means rolling backward in your normal stance. Switch means riding in your opposite stance while moving forward, like a righty writing left handed.
what is a half pipe | half pipe skating | vert ramp => A half pipe is a U shaped ramp with two curved walls facing each other. Riders go back and forth, doing tricks at the top. A tall one with vertical sections is called a vert ramp.
what is a tailwhip | bmx tailwhip | scooter tailwhip => A tailwhip is spinning the bike or scooter frame all the way around the handlebars while you stay in the air, then catching the pedals or deck to land.
what is a barspin | bmx barspin | how to barspin => A barspin is spinning the handlebars a full turn in the air and catching them before landing. BMX bikes use special brakes or no brakes so cables don't tangle.
what is a 180 on a bike | bmx 180 | bike one eighty => A 180 is jumping and turning your bike half a circle so you land riding backward or roll into a fakie. A 360 spins all the way around.
what is a hip jump | hip jump mtb | jumping a hip => A hip jump is set at an angle so you take off in one direction and land turned to the side. Riders turn their bike in the air to match the landing.
what is a scrub on a bike | mtb scrub | scrubbing jumps => A scrub is leaning the bike low over a jump's lip to stay closer to the ground and keep speed. Racers use it to save time.
what is a dink in pickleball | pickleball dink | how to dink => A dink is a soft shot hit from near the kitchen line so it drops into the opponent's kitchen. It forces them to hit upward and is key to winning points.
what is the two bounce rule in pickleball | pickleball double bounce rule | pickleball serve rule => The serve must bounce once and the return must bounce once before anyone can volley. After that, players can hit balls in the air outside the kitchen.
what is a yorker in cricket | cricket yorker | yorker ball => A yorker is a delivery aimed to land right at the batter's feet, making it hard to hit. It's a favorite of fast bowlers at the end of innings.
what is a googly in cricket | googly meaning | cricket googly => A googly is a trick ball by a leg spin bowler that spins the opposite way from what the batter expects.
what is a ruck in rugby | rugby ruck vs maul | maul in rugby => A ruck forms when the ball is on the ground and players from both teams push over it. A maul is similar but the ball carrier is still standing.
what is a hat trick in rugby or cricket | hat trick in cricket | three in a row sports => In cricket, a hat trick is a bowler taking wickets with three balls in a row. In many sports it means scoring three times in one game.
what is a yurchenko vault | yurchenko gymnastics | vault entry roundoff => A Yurchenko is a vault entry where the gymnast does a roundoff onto the springboard, a back handspring onto the table, then flips off. It's named for Soviet gymnast Natalia Yurchenko.
what is a pommel horse | pommel horse gymnastics | what do you do on pommel horse => The pommel horse is a men's event on a padded horse with two handles. Gymnasts swing their legs in circles and scissors, supporting themselves only with their hands.
what are the men's gymnastics events | men's artistic events | six gymnastics events => Men compete in six events: floor, pommel horse, still rings, vault, parallel bars and high bar.
what are the women's gymnastics events | women's artistic events | four gymnastics events => Women compete in four events: vault, uneven bars, balance beam and floor exercise.
what is an axel jump | axel figure skating | why is an axel harder => An axel is the only figure skating jump that takes off going forward, so it has an extra half rotation. A triple axel is 3.5 turns.
what is a quad in figure skating | quad jump | quadruple axel => A quad is a jump with four full rotations. Ilia Malinin became the first to land a quadruple axel in competition in 2022.
what is a camel spin | figure skating spins | sit spin => In a camel spin, the skater spins on one leg with the other leg and torso stretched horizontally. In a sit spin, the skater crouches low on one bent leg.
what is a barrel in surfing | getting barreled | tube ride surfing => A barrel or tube is the hollow space inside a breaking wave. Riding inside it is considered one of the best moments in surfing.
what is a duck dive | surfing duck dive | how to duck dive => A duck dive is pushing the nose of a surfboard under an oncoming wave and letting it pass over you, so you can paddle out.
what is a hammer in curling | curling hammer | last stone advantage => The hammer is the advantage of throwing the last stone in an end. Teams try to keep it because it often decides who scores.
what is the house in curling | curling target | how is curling scored => The house is the target of rings at the far end. After each end, only one team scores, getting one point for each stone closer to the center than the other team's closest.
what is a touche in fencing | fencing touch | fencing scoring => A touch is a valid hit on the opponent. Electric scoring boxes light up when the weapon's tip or blade registers a hit.
what is a bullseye worth in archery | olympic archery target | archery scoring => The Olympic target has 10 rings. The inner gold ring scores 10 and the outer white ring scores 1. Olympic archers shoot from 70 m.
what is a recurve bow vs compound bow | recurve vs compound | bow types => A recurve bow has tips that curve away from the archer and is used in the Olympics. A compound bow uses cables and pulleys that make it easier to hold at full draw.
what is sculling vs sweep rowing | sculling oars | sweep rowing => In sculling, each rower uses two oars, one in each hand. In sweep rowing, each rower uses one bigger oar held with both hands.
what is a eskimo roll in kayaking | kayak roll | how to roll a kayak => A kayak roll is flipping a capsized kayak back upright using your hips and paddle without getting out. Learn it with an instructor in a pool.
