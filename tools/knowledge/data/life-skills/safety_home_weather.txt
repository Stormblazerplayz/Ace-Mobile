# tag: life.safety.fire
how do smoke alarms work | how often should i test smoke detectors | smoke alarm beeping => Smoke alarms sense smoke and sound a loud alarm. Test them monthly by pressing the test button. A short chirp every minute or so usually means the battery is low and needs replacing.
what is a fire escape plan | how to make a fire escape plan | family fire drill => Draw your home's layout, mark two ways out of every room, and pick a meeting spot outside, like a mailbox or tree. Practice the plan with your family twice a year.
what should i do if my clothes catch fire | stop drop and roll | clothes on fire what to do => Stop where you are, drop to the ground, cover your face with your hands, and roll over and over until the fire is out. Then cool any burns with water and get help.
how do i get out of a smoky room | crawl low under smoke | why stay low in a fire => Smoke and heat rise, so the cleanest air is near the floor. Get low and crawl to the exit, keeping your mouth covered if you can.
should i open a door during a fire | feel the door for heat | hot doorknob fire => Feel the door and doorknob with the back of your hand first. If it's hot, don't open it, use another way out. If it's cool, open it slowly and be ready to shut it if smoke or fire is there.
what should i do if i'm trapped in a room during a fire | trapped by fire | can't get out in a fire => Close the door and stuff towels or clothes under it to block smoke. Call 911 and tell them where you are, open a window and wave something bright or a flashlight to signal for help.
should i go back inside a burning building | go back for my pet in a fire | getting my stuff in a fire => Never go back inside. Get out, stay out and call 911. Tell the firefighters if a person or pet is still inside, because they have the gear to go in safely.
how does a fire extinguisher work | how to use a fire extinguisher | pass method => Use PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, and Sweep side to side. Only use it on a small fire with a clear exit behind you, and let adults handle it when they can.
what are the classes of fire | which fire extinguisher for which fire | abc extinguisher => Class A fires are ordinary things like wood and paper, B is flammable liquids like gas and grease, and C is electrical. An ABC extinguisher works on all three. Kitchens may also have a Class K for cooking oil.
what causes most house fires | how to prevent fires at home | fire prevention tips => Cooking is the leading cause of home fires, followed by heating equipment, electrical problems and candles. Never leave cooking unattended, keep things 1 m (3 feet) from heaters, and blow out candles.
are candles safe | candle safety | can i have candles in my room => Keep candles away from anything that can burn, on a stable holder, and never leave them burning when you leave the room or fall asleep. Battery candles are a safe swap for bedrooms.
are space heaters safe | space heater safety | heater in my bedroom => Keep space heaters at least 1 m (3 feet) from beds, curtains and anything flammable, plug them directly into the wall, and turn them off when you leave the room or go to sleep.
what is carbon monoxide | carbon monoxide detector | carbon monoxide poisoning signs => Carbon monoxide is an invisible, odorless gas from burning fuel that can be deadly. Signs include headache, dizziness, nausea and sleepiness. If the CO alarm goes off, get everyone outside to fresh air and call 911.
can phone chargers start fires | charging safety | swollen battery danger => Damaged, cheap or knockoff chargers and batteries can overheat and catch fire. Don't charge under a pillow or on your bed, use good chargers, and stop using any battery that's swollen, hot or damaged.
are e bike and scooter batteries dangerous | lithium battery fire | charging an e scooter safely => Lithium batteries can catch fire if damaged, overcharged or using bad chargers. Use the original charger, charge where you can see it and not blocking an exit, and don't charge overnight unattended.
is it okay to play with matches or lighters | found a lighter | kids and fire => No. Matches and lighters are tools for adults. If you find one, give it to an adult. Fires can spread across a room in under a minute.
what are fireworks safety tips | is it safe to use fireworks | fireworks injuries => Fireworks cause thousands of injuries every year, even sparklers, which burn hotter than 1,000 °C. It's safest to watch public shows. If your family uses legal ones, only adults should light them, with water nearby.
how do i stay safe at a campfire or bonfire | bonfire safety | marshmallow roasting safety => Stay back from the fire, never run around it, use long sticks for roasting, and keep water or sand nearby. Only adults should add fuel, and never use gasoline to start or grow a fire.

# tag: life.safety.home
how do i stay safe home alone | home alone rules | what age can you stay home alone => Keep doors locked, don't open the door for anyone you don't expect, don't tell callers or people online you're alone, and know how to reach your parents and 911. Rules on the right age vary by state and family.
what should i do if a stranger is at the door | someone knocking i don't know | delivery person at the door home alone => You don't have to open it. Talk through the closed door or don't answer, and call your parent. Delivery people can leave packages. If someone tries to get in, call 911.
what should i do if i hear someone breaking in | intruder in my house | burglar what to do => Get out if you can do it safely and go to a neighbor's, or lock yourself in a room, stay quiet and call 911. Don't confront the person.
what is a safe adult | who is a safe person to ask for help | trusted adults list => A safe adult is someone you trust to help, like a parent, relative, teacher, coach, school counselor or police officer. Make a list of a few with your parents, including how to reach them.
what is a family code word | safe word for kids | password for pickup => A family code word is a secret word your parents share only with people allowed to pick you up. If someone says your parent sent them but doesn't know the word, don't go with them.
what should i do if a stranger offers me a ride | someone wants me to get in their car | stranger danger tips => Say no loudly, step away and go toward a safe place with people, like a store, and tell an adult right away. Never get in a car without your parent's okay, even if they say your parent sent them.
what should i do if someone tries to grab me | someone grabbed me | how to escape a stranger => Yell loudly things like "This isn't my parent!" or "Help!", kick, pull away and run toward people. Make a scene. Then tell an adult and call 911.
what is a lockdown drill | lockdown at school | what to do in a school lockdown => A lockdown keeps students safe from a danger outside or inside the school. Follow your teacher's directions, stay quiet and out of sight, silence your phone and wait until it's officially over.
how do i store medicine safely | medicine safety for kids | can i take medicine by myself => Only take medicine an adult gives you or says it's okay, and always follow the label. Keep medicines in their original containers, away from younger kids and pets.
how do i use a knife safely | knife safety rules | pocket knife safety => Cut away from your body, keep fingers clear of the blade, use a cutting board, and close a pocket knife before carrying it. Never run with a knife or wave it around, and pass it handle first.
what is electrical safety | electricity safety tips | why not touch outlets => Keep water away from plugs and devices, don't overload power strips, pull plugs by the plug, not the cord, and never stick anything into an outlet. Stay far from downed power lines.
what should i do if i see a downed power line | fallen power line | power line on the ground => Stay far away, at least 10 m (35 feet), and keep others back. Assume it's live even if it isn't sparking. Call 911 or the power company.
how do i use power tools safely | power tool safety for kids | safety glasses when building => Only use power tools with an adult's permission and supervision. Wear safety glasses and hearing protection, tie back long hair, avoid loose sleeves, and clamp your work instead of holding it.
how do i stay safe while 3d printing | 3d printer safety | are 3d printer fumes safe => Don't touch the hot nozzle or bed, keep the printer away from flammable stuff, and don't leave it running unattended for long. Print in a ventilated room, especially with ABS or resin, and wear gloves with resin.
how do i stay safe using a soldering iron | soldering iron burns => Always use a stand, wear safety glasses, work with airflow so you don't breathe the fumes, wash your hands after, and unplug it when done. Hold parts with pliers or clamps, never fingers.
how do i stay safe flying a drone | drone safety rules | where can i fly my drone => Keep it in sight, stay away from people, roads and airports, and in the US fly below 400 feet. Recreational flyers must pass the free FAA TRUST test, and drones over 250 g must be registered.
how do i stay safe on a trampoline | trampoline safety | trampoline injuries => One person at a time is safest. Use nets and pads, no flips unless trained, and climb off instead of jumping off. Most injuries happen when several people jump together.
what are the rules for wearing a seat belt | when can kids sit in the front seat | booster seat age => Always buckle up. Kids should use a booster seat until the seat belt fits right, usually around 4 feet 9 inches tall and 8 to 12 years old. The CDC says kids under 13 should ride in the back seat.

# tag: life.safety.road
how do i ride my bike safely in traffic | biking on the road rules | which side of the road do bikes ride on => Ride on the right side, going the same direction as cars, and follow traffic signs and lights like a car. Use hand signals, be visible with lights and bright clothes, and make eye contact with drivers.
what are bike hand signals | how to signal on a bike | bike turn signals => Point your left arm straight out to turn left. For a right turn, point your right arm out or bend your left arm up at the elbow. To stop, bend your left arm down with your palm back.
do i need lights on my bike | bike lights at night | front and rear bike lights => Yes, if you ride at dusk, at night or in bad weather. Most places require a white front light and a red rear reflector or light. Bright lights in the day help drivers see you too.
how do i stay safe walking at night | walking at night safety | be seen at night => Wear bright or reflective clothing, carry a flashlight, walk facing traffic if there's no sidewalk and stay on well lit routes. Put earbuds away so you can hear cars.
why shouldn't i look at my phone while walking | distracted walking | texting while crossing => Looking at your phone makes you much less aware of cars, bikes and obstacles. Stop on the side to check it, and put it away when crossing streets.
how do i get on and off a school bus safely | school bus safety | crossing in front of a bus => Stand back from the curb while you wait, wait for the bus to stop fully, and if you have to cross, walk at least 10 feet (3 m) in front of the bus where the driver can see you.
how do i ride an electric scooter safely | e scooter safety | electric scooter age rules => Wear a helmet, go at a safe speed, ride where it's allowed, and keep both hands on the bars. Rental e scooters often require riders to be 18, so check the rules.

# tag: life.safety.weather
what should i do in a tornado | tornado safety | where to go in a tornado => Go to a basement, or if there isn't one, a small interior room on the lowest floor without windows, like a bathroom or closet. Cover your head with your arms or a mattress. Never try to outrun a tornado in a car.
what is the difference between a tornado watch and warning | watch vs warning | tornado warning meaning => A watch means conditions are right for tornadoes, so be ready. A warning means a tornado has been seen or shown on radar, so take shelter right away. The same idea applies to other storms.
what should i do in a hurricane | hurricane safety | how to prepare for a hurricane => Follow local officials and evacuate if told to. Have an emergency kit, stay indoors away from windows, and never walk or drive through floodwater.
what should i do in a flood | flood safety | turn around don't drown => Move to higher ground. Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater, because just 15 cm (6 inches) of moving water can knock you down and 30 cm (1 foot) can sweep a car away. Turn around, don't drown.
how do i stay safe in a blizzard | snowstorm safety | winter storm tips => Stay inside if you can. If you go out, dress in layers with a hat, gloves and waterproof boots, and come in if you get very cold or wet. Make sure heaters and generators are used safely.
what should i do during a wildfire | wildfire smoke safety | evacuating for wildfire => Follow evacuation orders right away. If smoke is bad, stay indoors with windows closed and use an air purifier, and a well fitting N95 mask outdoors helps. Check air quality reports.
what should i do in a tsunami warning | tsunami safety | signs of a tsunami => If you're near the coast and feel a strong earthquake, see the sea suddenly pull back, or hear a warning, go to high ground or inland right away. Don't wait or go to look.
what is a heat wave | staying cool in a heat wave | heat safety tips => A heat wave is a long stretch of very hot weather. Drink lots of water, stay in shade or air conditioning, avoid hard exercise in the hottest hours, and never leave anyone or pets in a parked car.
what is lightning safety 30 30 rule | when to go inside for lightning | when thunder roars go indoors => When thunder roars, go indoors, because if you can hear thunder you're close enough to be struck. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back outside.
what goes in an emergency kit | disaster supply kit | emergency go bag => Pack water (about 4 liters or 1 gallon per person per day for several days), non perishable food, a flashlight, batteries, a first aid kit, medicines, a phone charger or power bank, a whistle, copies of important papers and warm clothes.
what is an evacuation | what does evacuate mean | how to evacuate safely => An evacuation is when officials tell people to leave an area because of danger. Leave quickly, take your go bag, pets and medicines, follow the official routes and tell family where you're going.
