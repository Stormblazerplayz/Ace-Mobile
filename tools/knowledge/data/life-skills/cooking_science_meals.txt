# tag: life.cooking.science
why does water boil at a lower temperature on mountains | boiling point at altitude | why does pasta take longer to cook in the mountains => Higher up, air pressure is lower, so water boils at a lower temperature, around 90 °C at about 3,000 m instead of 100 °C at sea level. Food cooks slower in that cooler boiling water.
does salt make water boil faster | why add salt to boiling water | salt in pasta water reason => Salt actually raises the boiling point a tiny bit, not enough to matter. Cooks add it for flavor, since pasta and potatoes soak it up as they cook.
why does a watched pot never boil | how long does water take to boil | boiling water time => It just feels slow when you're watching. A big pot of water on a stove often takes about 8 to 15 minutes to boil, depending on the amount, the burner and whether it has a lid.
why do eggs turn solid when cooked | what happens when you cook an egg | egg proteins => Heat makes egg proteins unfold and link together into a solid network. That's called denaturing, and it can't be reversed.
why does meat shrink when cooked | meat loses water | why is my burger smaller => Heat makes muscle proteins tighten and squeeze out water and melted fat, so the meat shrinks. Cooking at lower heat and not overcooking means less shrinkage.
why does bread toast turn brown | what happens when bread toasts | toasting chemistry => The heat dries the surface and causes the Maillard reaction between sugars and proteins, creating brown color and toasty flavor.
why does popcorn need to be dry inside | why do some kernels not pop | old maids in popcorn => Each kernel holds a little water. Heat turns it to steam that bursts the hard hull. Kernels with cracked hulls or too little moisture don't pop.
why does ice cream melt faster than ice | why is ice cream soft | what makes ice cream creamy => Ice cream has sugar, fat and air mixed in, which lower its freezing point and keep ice crystals small. That's why it's scoopable and creamy, not hard like an ice cube.
why does soda fizz | what makes soda bubbly | why does soda go flat => Soda has carbon dioxide gas dissolved in it under pressure. When you open it, pressure drops and the gas escapes as bubbles. Warm soda goes flat faster because warm liquid holds less gas.
why do mentos make soda explode | diet coke and mentos | mentos geyser science => Tiny bumps on the Mentos surface give dissolved carbon dioxide lots of places to form bubbles at once, so the gas rushes out in a huge foam. Do it outside with an adult and stand back.
why does cut fruit go brown faster in the air | oxidation in food | why does lemon stop browning => Cutting breaks cells so enzymes mix with oxygen and form brown pigments. Lemon juice's acid slows those enzymes, and vitamin C also helps.
why does cooking oil not mix with water | oil and water separate | why oil floats => Water molecules stick strongly to each other but not to oil molecules, so they stay apart. Oil is less dense, so it floats on top.
how does yeast make bread rise | yeast fermentation | why does dough get bigger => Yeast eats sugars in the dough and gives off carbon dioxide gas and a little alcohol. Gluten traps the gas in bubbles, puffing the dough up, and baking sets the bubbles in place.
why do onions get sweet when cooked | cooked onions taste sweet | why raw onions are sharp => Cooking breaks down the sharp sulfur compounds that make raw onions bite, and slow browning caramelizes their natural sugars, making them sweet.
why does chocolate turn white | white film on chocolate | chocolate bloom => That's called bloom. Fat bloom happens when cocoa butter rises to the surface after warm temperatures, and sugar bloom happens from moisture. It looks odd but is safe to eat.
why does a microwave heat food | how does a microwave work | microwaves and water molecules => Microwaves make water molecules in food jiggle back and forth billions of times a second, and that movement creates heat. That's why dry things heat less and wet food gets hot.
why does a cold pan make food stick | preheating the pan | why does food stick to stainless steel => Food sticks when proteins bond to the metal. Heating the pan first, then adding oil, and letting food sear until it releases on its own helps stop sticking.
why does salt make food taste better | salt flavor enhancer | why add salt to sweets => Salt boosts savory and sweet flavors and reduces bitterness, so food tastes fuller. A pinch in desserts makes them taste less flat.
why does spicy food burn | what makes peppers hot | capsaicin => Hot peppers contain capsaicin, which triggers the same heat sensing nerves that detect real heat, so your mouth feels like it's burning even though it isn't damaged.
why does milk help with spicy food | does water help spicy food | casein and capsaicin => Capsaicin dissolves in fat, not water. Milk's fat and a protein called casein help wash it away, while water just spreads it around.
why do beans make you gassy | beans and gas | how to make beans less gassy => Beans have sugars our bodies can't fully digest, so gut bacteria break them down and make gas. Soaking and rinsing dried beans and eating them often helps your body get used to them.
why does pineapple make my mouth tingle | pineapple enzyme | why pineapple ruins jello => Fresh pineapple has bromelain, an enzyme that breaks down proteins, including on your tongue. It also stops gelatin from setting, but canned pineapple is heated so it's fine.
why do we cook food | why is cooked food easier to digest | cooking history => Cooking kills many germs, makes food softer and easier to chew and digest, and lets our bodies get more energy from it. It also creates lots of flavors.
why do noodles get soggy in soup | pasta absorbing liquid | leftover soup noodles => Pasta keeps soaking up liquid even after it's cooked. Cook noodles separately and add them to each bowl if you're saving leftovers.

# tag: life.cooking.meals
what is a balanced meal | how to make a healthy plate | myplate explained => A balanced plate is about half fruits and vegetables, a quarter whole grains and a quarter protein, with some dairy or a calcium rich food on the side. That's the idea behind the USDA's MyPlate.
how do i plan meals for the week | meal planning tips | what is meal prep => Pick a few dinners, write a shopping list by store section, and cook some basics ahead, like rice, chopped veggies and grilled chicken, to mix into meals all week.
what are easy meals i can make myself | simple meals for kids to cook | beginner meals => Good starter meals include scrambled eggs, quesadillas, pasta with sauce, grilled cheese, rice bowls, tacos, wraps, baked potatoes and loaded nachos. Ask an adult before using the stove.
what can i make with eggs | quick egg meals | egg ideas for lunch => Eggs can become scrambled eggs, omelettes, fried egg sandwiches, egg fried rice, breakfast burritos or a frittata. They're cheap and cook fast.
what can i make with leftover rice | leftover rice ideas | what to do with extra rice => Leftover rice is great for fried rice, rice bowls, rice pudding, stuffed peppers or adding to soup.
what can i make with bread | stale bread ideas | what to do with old bread => Make French toast, croutons, breadcrumbs, bread pudding or grilled cheese. Stale bread is often better for these than fresh.
good breakfast foods for school days | healthy breakfast ideas | quick breakfast before school => Good picks have protein and fiber to keep you full: eggs and toast, oatmeal with fruit, yogurt with granola, a peanut butter banana wrap or a smoothie.
what is a good after school snack | snack ideas after school | healthy snacks for kids => Try apple slices with peanut butter, cheese and crackers, yogurt, hummus and veggies, popcorn, a hard boiled egg or trail mix. Pairing protein with fruit or veggies keeps you full.
how do i make a good sandwich | sandwich tips | best way to build a sandwich => Toast the bread if you want crunch, spread a thin layer of butter or mayo to keep wet fillings from making bread soggy, layer protein and cheese, then veggies, and season with salt and pepper.
how do i pack a lunch | lunch packing ideas | what to put in a lunchbox => Include a main like a sandwich, wrap or leftovers, a fruit, a vegetable, and something with protein. Add water, and an ice pack if anything needs to stay cold.
how do i make a grocery list | grocery list tips | what to buy at the grocery store => Check what you already have, plan meals, and list items grouped by store section like produce, dairy, meat and pantry. Sticking to the list saves money.
how do i read a nutrition label | what does a food label mean | serving size on labels => Start with the serving size, since all the numbers are for one serving. Look at calories, protein, fiber and added sugar, and the percent daily value, where 5 percent or less is low and 20 percent or more is high.
what are whole grains | whole grain vs white bread | why eat whole grains => Whole grains keep all parts of the grain, including the fiber rich bran and germ. They keep you full longer than refined grains like white bread. Look for "whole" as the first ingredient.
what is fiber | why do we need fiber | foods high in fiber => Fiber is the part of plant foods your body can't digest. It helps digestion, keeps you full and is good for your heart. Beans, whole grains, fruit, vegetables, nuts and seeds have lots.
what are healthy fats | good fats vs bad fats | are fats bad for you => Your body needs fat. Healthier fats are in nuts, seeds, avocados, olive oil and fish. Limit saturated fat from fatty meats and butter, and avoid trans fats.
how much protein does a kid need | protein for kids | do i need protein shakes => Most kids get enough protein from regular food, like meat, eggs, dairy, beans and nuts. Kids aged 9 to 13 need about 34 grams a day, and protein shakes usually aren't needed.
what are empty calories | what is junk food | is junk food bad => Empty calories come from foods with lots of sugar or unhealthy fat but few nutrients, like soda, candy and chips. They're fine as treats, but shouldn't replace real meals.
how do i cook for my family | making dinner for my parents | surprise dinner ideas => Pick a simple recipe you've practiced, check with a parent about using the stove, prep everything before you start and set the table. Pasta, tacos and breakfast for dinner are great choices.
how do i make a meal kid friendly and healthy | sneaking veggies into food | picky eater tips => Blend veggies into sauces, add them to quesadillas and pasta, offer dips, and let kids help cook, which makes them more willing to try things.
what foods give you energy | best foods for focus | food for studying => Meals with whole grains, protein and healthy fats give steady energy, like oatmeal with nuts or eggs on whole grain toast. Sugary snacks give a quick spike, then a crash.
what is a vegetarian protein | protein without meat | meat free protein foods => Good meat free proteins include beans, lentils, tofu, tempeh, eggs, dairy, nuts, seeds, edamame and whole grains like quinoa.

# tag: life.cooking.drinks
how do i make iced tea | homemade iced tea | cold brew tea => Steep tea bags in hot water for 3 to 5 minutes, remove them, sweeten if you like and chill, then pour over ice. For cold brew, steep bags in cold water in the fridge for 6 to 12 hours.
how do i make a milkshake | milkshake recipe basics | thick milkshake tips => Blend about 3 scoops of ice cream with a splash of milk, adding milk a little at a time. Less milk makes it thicker. Add fruit, cookies or chocolate syrup for flavors.
how do i make hot cocoa from cocoa powder | real hot chocolate | hot cocoa from scratch => Whisk 1 to 2 tablespoons cocoa powder and 1 to 2 tablespoons sugar with a splash of milk into a paste, then whisk in a cup of hot milk and a drop of vanilla.
how do i make flavored water | fruit infused water | water that tastes good => Add sliced fruit like lemon, cucumber, strawberries or oranges, or herbs like mint, to a pitcher of water and chill for a few hours.
how do i make a smoothie thicker | thick smoothie tips | smoothie too runny => Use frozen fruit instead of ice, add a banana, Greek yogurt, oats or nut butter, and use less liquid. Blend liquid in slowly until it's the thickness you want.
how do i make ice cubes clear | cloudy ice cubes | why is my ice cloudy => Cloudy ice comes from trapped air and minerals. Boiling water twice before freezing helps a bit, but freezing water slowly from one direction, like in an insulated cooler, gives the clearest ice.
how do i make lemonade less sour | lemonade too sour fix | balance sweet and sour => Add more simple syrup or sugar dissolved in a little warm water, or dilute it with cold water. Taste as you go.
what is simple syrup | how do you make simple syrup | why use simple syrup in cold drinks => Simple syrup is equal parts sugar and water heated until the sugar dissolves, then cooled. It mixes into cold drinks easily, while plain sugar just sinks to the bottom.
are energy drinks safe for kids | energy drinks for teens | why are energy drinks bad for kids => The American Academy of Pediatrics says kids and teens shouldn't have energy drinks. They have lots of caffeine and other stimulants that can cause fast heartbeat, trouble sleeping and anxiety.
how much caffeine is in soda | caffeine in coke | does sprite have caffeine => A 12 ounce can of Coca Cola has about 34 mg of caffeine. Many clear sodas like Sprite and 7UP have none, while some sodas and energy drinks have much more.
