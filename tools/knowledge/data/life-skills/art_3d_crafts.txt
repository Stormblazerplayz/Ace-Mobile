# tag: art.sculpture
what is an armature in sculpture | sculpture armature | wire armature => An armature is an inner skeleton, often wire or foil, that supports a sculpture so thin parts like arms and legs don't sag or break.
what is relief sculpture | bas relief | relief carving => Relief sculpture sticks out from a flat background instead of standing free. Coins are a common example of low relief.
how is bronze sculpture made | lost wax casting | casting metal sculpture => In lost wax casting, a wax model is covered in a heat proof mold, the wax is melted out, and hot bronze is poured into the space. The mold is broken to reveal the sculpture.
what is soapstone carving | carving soap | easy carving for kids => Soapstone is a soft stone that can be carved with files and sandpaper. Kids can practice carving on a bar of soap with a plastic knife or spoon.
how do i carve wood | whittling for beginners | whittling safety => Whittling uses a small knife to carve soft wood like basswood. Always carve away from your body, wear a cut resistant glove, keep your knife sharp, and learn with an adult.
what is pottery wheel throwing | how to use a pottery wheel | centering clay => On a pottery wheel, you center a lump of wet clay as it spins, open a hole in the middle, then pull up the walls. Centering is the hardest part and takes practice.
what is hand building pottery | pinch pot | coil pot => Hand building makes pottery without a wheel. Pinch pots are shaped with your fingers, coil pots are built from rolled snakes of clay, and slab pots use flat sheets.
what is a kiln | how does a kiln work | firing clay => A kiln is a very hot oven, often over 1,000 C (1,800 F), that fires clay so it becomes hard and permanent. Glazed pieces are fired again to melt the glaze into glass.
what is glaze | pottery glaze | how glaze works => Glaze is a coating of powdered glass and minerals that melts in the kiln into a shiny, waterproof surface. It often looks totally different before firing.
what is bisque | bisqueware | greenware vs bisque => Greenware is unfired dried clay, very fragile. Bisque is clay after its first firing, hard but porous, ready to glaze.
what is a kinetic sculpture | moving sculpture | alexander calder mobile => A kinetic sculpture moves, by wind, motors or touch. Alexander Calder invented the mobile, a hanging sculpture balanced to move in the air.
how do i make a mobile | hanging mobile craft | balance a mobile => Tie shapes to the ends of sticks or wire with thread, then hang each stick from another, adjusting the tie points until each level balances. Work from the bottom up.
what is assemblage art | found object art | sculpture from junk => Assemblage art is made by combining found objects, like old parts, toys or scrap, into a 3D artwork. It's a great way to reuse broken things.
how do i make a sculpture from recycled materials | recycled art | cardboard sculpture => Collect clean cardboard, bottles, caps and scraps, sketch a plan, join pieces with hot glue or tape, and paint it. Robots and creatures are fun subjects.
what is wire sculpture | wire art | how to make wire sculptures => Wire sculpture bends wire into shapes, like drawing in 3D. Use soft aluminum wire and round nose pliers, and wear safety glasses since cut ends can spring.
what is origami tessellation | advanced origami | modular origami => Modular origami joins many folded units into one shape, like a kusudama ball. Origami tessellations fold one sheet into a repeating pattern.
what is ice sculpture | snow sculpture | how are ice sculptures made => Ice sculptors carve large blocks of clear ice with chainsaws, chisels and grinders. Snow sculpting festivals carve giant packed snow blocks.
what is sand sculpture | sandcastle tips | how to build a sandcastle => Use lots of water with sand to pack it firmly, build a strong wide base, pack sand into buckets or forms, and carve from the top down.
# tag: art.printmaking
what is screen printing | how does screen printing work | printing t shirts => Screen printing pushes ink through a fine mesh screen with a stencil onto fabric or paper. Each color needs its own screen. It's how many band and team shirts are made.
what is a monoprint | monotype printing | one of a kind print => A monoprint is made by painting or drawing on a smooth plate and pressing paper onto it, giving a single unique print.
how do i make a stencil | diy stencils | stencil art => Draw a design on card or plastic, cut out the parts where paint should go, keeping "bridges" so islands like the middle of an O don't fall out. Dab paint on with a sponge.
what is a rubbing | crayon rubbing | leaf rubbing => Put paper over a textured surface, like a leaf or coin, and rub the side of a crayon or pencil over it to transfer the texture.
what is marbling paper | paper marbling | shaving cream marbling => Marbling floats paint on water or shaving cream, swirls it into patterns, and transfers it onto paper. Shaving cream marbling is an easy kid version.
what is cyanotype | sun printing | blueprint photography => Cyanotype paper turns blue where sunlight hits it. Place leaves or objects on the paper in the sun, then rinse it to reveal white shapes on blue.
# tag: art.crafts2
what is quilling | paper quilling | quilling craft => Quilling rolls thin paper strips into coils and shapes, then glues them to make designs like flowers and patterns.
what is decoupage | mod podge crafts | decoupage how to => Decoupage glues cut paper images onto objects like boxes or jars, then seals them with layers of glue or varnish like Mod Podge.
what is resin art | epoxy resin crafts | is resin safe => Resin art pours clear epoxy that hardens like glass. It gives off fumes and can irritate skin, so it's an adult supervised craft with gloves and good ventilation.
what is macrame | macrame knots | macrame for beginners => Macrame makes decorations like plant hangers and wall hangings by tying knots in cord. The square knot and half hitch are the basic knots.
what is felting | needle felting | wet felting => Felting mats wool fibers together. Needle felting uses a barbed needle to shape wool into figures, so it needs care since the needle is very sharp. Wet felting uses soap and hot water.
what is cross stitch | cross stitch for beginners | cross stitch pattern => Cross stitch makes a picture of small X shaped stitches on a grid fabric called Aida, following a pattern chart.
what is perler beads | fuse beads | how to iron perler beads => Perler beads are small plastic beads arranged on a pegboard into designs, then fused by ironing over parchment paper. Pixel art and game characters are popular designs, and an adult should help with the iron.
what is diamond painting | diamond art | diamond painting how to => Diamond painting places tiny shiny resin "diamonds" on a sticky numbered canvas to create a sparkly picture, like paint by numbers.
