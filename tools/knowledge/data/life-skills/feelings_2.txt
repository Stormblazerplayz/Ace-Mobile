# tag: life.feelings.sad
what do i do when i feel lonely | i feel lonely | lonely at school => Feeling lonely is really common, even around people. Reach out to one person, a friend, cousin or family member, join an activity you enjoy, and talk to a trusted adult about how you feel. You deserve connection.
what can i do to feel happier | how to be happy | ways to cheer up => Move your body, get outside, spend time with people you like, do something kind for someone, listen to music, and get enough sleep. Small things add up.
why do i feel sad for no reason | sad but don't know why | random sadness => Sometimes sadness shows up because of tiredness, hunger, stress or changes in your body, even without a clear reason. Be gentle with yourself and do something comforting. If it lasts for weeks, tell a parent or counselor.
is it okay to cry | why do people cry | crying is weak => Yes. Crying is a healthy way your body releases strong feelings, and everyone does it. Many people feel calmer afterward.
how do i cheer up a friend | my friend is sad | how to help a sad friend => Ask what's wrong and really listen, without rushing to fix it. Spend time with them, do something fun together, and let them know you care. If they seem in danger or very down for a long time, tell a trusted adult.
what is depression | how is depression different from sadness | signs of depression in kids => Sadness is a normal feeling that passes. Depression is a health condition where sadness, low energy or losing interest in things lasts weeks and makes daily life hard. If that sounds like you or a friend, tell a trusted adult so a doctor or counselor can help.
what should i do if i feel like hurting myself | i want to hurt myself | i don't want to be alive => I'm really glad you said something. Please tell a trusted adult straight away, like a parent, teacher or school counselor. In the US you can call or text 988 any time to talk to someone kind. If you're in danger now, call 911. You matter, and things can get better with help.
what should i do if a friend says they want to hurt themselves | friend talked about suicide | friend is cutting => Take it seriously and tell a trusted adult right away, even if your friend asked you to keep it secret. Keeping them safe matters more than the secret. In the US, 988 is a call or text line for help.
how do i talk to my parents about my feelings | hard to talk to my parents | telling my mom i'm not okay => Pick a calm time, like a car ride or walk, and start simple: "Can I talk to you about something?" You can also write a note or text if saying it is hard. You don't need the perfect words.
what is a school counselor | can i talk to the school counselor | what does a counselor do => A school counselor is trained to help students with feelings, friendships, family problems and school stress. You can usually ask a teacher or the office to see them, and they keep most things private unless someone's safety is at risk.
what do i do if i feel left out at home | parents favor my sibling | i feel ignored at home => Tell a parent calmly how you feel, with specific examples, like "I feel left out when..." They may not realize it. Ask for some one on one time doing something you both like.
how do i deal with homesickness | homesick at camp | missing home on a trip => Homesickness is normal and usually fades after a few days. Keep busy, make friends, write a letter home and bring something comforting from home, like a photo.
how do i deal with jealousy | i'm jealous of my friend | feeling jealous => Jealousy is normal. Notice it, then use it as a clue to what you want, and think about steps toward it. Celebrating your friend's win often makes the jealousy fade.
how do i stop comparing myself to others | comparing myself on social media | everyone is better than me => Remember you're comparing your everyday life to other people's highlight reels. Compare yourself to who you were last month instead, and take breaks from apps that make you feel worse.
how do i feel better about how i look | i don't like how i look | body image for kids => Bodies come in all shapes and change a lot while growing. Focus on what your body can do, like biking or building, and spend less time on media that makes you feel bad. A trusted adult can help if it's on your mind a lot.
how do i be kinder to myself | negative self talk | i'm always hard on myself => Talk to yourself like you'd talk to a good friend. When you think "I'm so dumb," try "I made a mistake, and I can learn from it." Notice things you did well each day.
what is self care | self care ideas for kids | how to take care of myself => Self care is doing things that keep your body and mind healthy, like sleeping enough, eating well, moving, spending time outside, doing hobbies and talking about your feelings.
what is a gratitude journal | how to practice gratitude | does gratitude make you happier => A gratitude journal is writing down a few things you're thankful for each day, big or small. Studies suggest gratitude can boost mood and help you notice good things.
how do i deal with disappointment | i didn't make the team | things didn't go my way => Let yourself feel disappointed, it shows you cared. Then think about what you learned and what you can try next. Lots of successful people faced big disappointments first.
how do i get over losing | i lost the competition | upset after losing => It's okay to be upset for a while. Then look at one thing you did well and one thing to practice. Every loss is information that helps you improve.
how do i deal with being rejected | someone said no to being my friend | rejection hurts => Rejection stings for everyone. It doesn't mean something is wrong with you, just that it wasn't the right fit. Spend time with people who appreciate you and keep putting yourself out there.

# tag: life.feelings.friends
what do i do if my friend is ignoring me | friend stopped talking to me | friend is acting distant => Ask them privately and kindly if everything's okay or if you did something to upset them. They may be dealing with something else. Give them a little space and keep being friendly.
what do i do if my best friend has a new friend | friend replaced me | jealous of my friend's new friend => It's normal to feel worried. Friends can have more than one friend. Try hanging out all together, keep doing things you both enjoy, and make new friends too.
how do i end a friendship | toxic friend | how to stop being friends with someone => If a friendship keeps making you feel bad, you can slowly spend less time with them, or tell them kindly that you need space. You don't have to be mean. A trusted adult can help if it's tricky.
what do i do if my friends are fighting | stuck between two friends | friends asked me to pick a side => You don't have to pick a side. Tell both that you care about them and won't carry messages or talk badly about either one. Encourage them to talk to each other.
how do i make up with a friend after a fight | fixing a friendship | we're not talking after a fight => Reach out first, it shows you care. Say sorry for your part, listen to theirs, and talk about how to move on. Sometimes a fun activity together helps break the ice.
what do i do if a friend told my secret | friend broke my trust | someone shared my secret => Tell them calmly how it made you feel and why trust matters to you. Decide what to share with them in the future. Trust can be rebuilt slowly if they're truly sorry.
what if i have a crush | i like someone at school | how to deal with a crush => Crushes are totally normal and can feel exciting and confusing. You don't have to do anything about it. Be friendly and yourself, and talk to someone you trust if you want advice.
what do i do if someone is jealous of me | people envy me | friend acts weird when i win => Be kind and don't brag. Include them, celebrate their wins too, and if it causes problems, talk about it honestly.
how do i handle a friend who always wants to win | competitive friend | sore loser friend => Pick games where luck matters too, play as teammates sometimes, and say kindly when it stops being fun. If it keeps ruining time together, do other activities with them.
how do i stand up for myself | being assertive | how to not be a pushover => Speak clearly and calmly about what you want or don't want, like "Please stop, I don't like that." Look the person in the eye, stand tall and repeat it if needed. Assertive isn't mean, it's respectful to both of you.
how do i set boundaries with friends | what are boundaries | saying no to friends nicely => Boundaries are your limits, like not sharing your password or not wanting to be teased about something. Tell friends kindly and clearly, and good friends will respect them.
what do i do if my friend lies to me | friend keeps lying | caught a friend lying => Talk to them calmly about it and explain how it affects your trust. Ask why they lied. If it keeps happening, you might want to rely on other friends more.

# tag: life.feelings.family
how do i deal with strict parents | my parents are too strict | parents won't let me do anything => Ask calmly about their reasons, show you're responsible in small ways, and suggest a compromise, like a trial with rules. Arguing usually makes parents less likely to agree.
what do i do if i get in trouble | i got grounded | how to handle being punished => Stay calm, listen, and take responsibility without making excuses. Apologize, and ask how you can make it right. Showing you learned from it helps rebuild trust.
how do i tell my parents i made a mistake | scared to tell my parents something | broke something and scared to tell => Telling the truth early is almost always better. Pick a calm moment, say what happened directly and what you'll do to fix it. Most parents get more upset about hiding than the mistake itself.
how do i deal with a new sibling | new baby in the family | jealous of my baby brother => It's normal to feel left out or jealous. Ask your parents for some time with just you, find ways to help with the baby if you want, and tell them how you feel.
how do i deal with moving schools | changing schools | starting at a new school mid year => Moving is a big change. Ask for a buddy or tour, join a club quickly, introduce yourself to people near you, and stay in touch with old friends. It takes a few weeks to feel settled.
how do i get more independence | asking my parents for more freedom | how to be trusted more => Show responsibility with what you already have, like homework, chores and being on time. Then ask for one specific new freedom with a plan, like biking to a friend's house and texting when you arrive.
what do i do if my parents are fighting | parents arguing a lot | scared when parents argue => It's hard to hear. Go somewhere you feel calm, like your room with headphones. Their arguments aren't your fault. Tell a trusted adult if you ever feel unsafe or it's worrying you a lot.
how do i be a good big brother or sister | being a good older sibling | help my little brother => Be patient, play with them, teach them things you know, and stand up for them. Little siblings look up to you more than you might think.

# tag: life.feelings.growth
how do i handle making mistakes | i keep making mistakes | i'm afraid of making mistakes => Mistakes are how everyone learns. Admit it, fix what you can, think about what you'd do differently, and move on. Even pros make mistakes daily.
how do i become more patient | i'm so impatient | patience tips => Notice when you're getting impatient, take a breath and remind yourself good things take time. Practice by doing slow skills, like learning an instrument or building a model.
how do i stop being a perfectionist | perfectionism | nothing i make is good enough => Aim for done and good, not perfect. Set a time limit, finish, then improve it in the next version. Many creators release imperfect work and get better each time.
how do i stay motivated | lost my motivation | how to get motivated => Connect what you're doing to something you care about, set a small goal for today, and start before you feel like it. Motivation often comes after you begin, not before.
how do i deal with burnout | burned out on a hobby | not fun anymore => Burnout happens when you do too much without rest. Take a real break, do something different, and come back with smaller, more fun goals.
how do i deal with being teased | people make fun of me | kids tease me about my interests => Stay calm, don't let them see they bothered you, and walk away or say something short like "okay" and move on. Spend time with people who share your interests. If it's repeated or hurtful, tell a trusted adult.
how do i stop being shy | how to be less shy | overcome shyness => Shyness gets easier with practice. Set tiny goals, like saying hi to one person a day, ask questions since people love to talk about themselves, and join activities where talking happens naturally.
what is empathy | how to be more empathetic | empathy meaning => Empathy is understanding and caring about how someone else feels, like imagining being in their shoes. You can practice by listening closely and asking how people feel.
what is resilience | how to be more resilient | bouncing back from hard times => Resilience is the ability to bounce back after hard things. It grows from having people you trust, taking care of yourself, solving problems step by step and remembering past challenges you got through.
how do i be more positive | positive thinking | stop being so negative => Notice negative thoughts and ask if they're fully true. Look for one good thing each day, spend time with positive people and do things you enjoy.
why do i feel moody | mood swings | why are my emotions so strong => During the preteen and teen years, changing hormones, growing, and lots of new pressures can make feelings stronger and change faster. Sleep, food, exercise and talking about it help. Tell an adult if moods feel overwhelming.
how do i know what i'm feeling | naming emotions | i don't know how i feel => Try pausing and noticing your body, like a tight chest or heavy feeling, then pick a word: sad, worried, frustrated, lonely, excited. Naming a feeling actually helps calm it.
what do i do if i feel overwhelmed | everything is too much | feeling overwhelmed => Stop, breathe slowly, and write down everything on your mind. Pick just one small thing to do next. Ask someone to help sort out the rest.
