# tag: music.instruments
what is a double bass | upright bass | double bass vs bass guitar => The double bass is the largest, lowest instrument in the string family, played standing or on a tall stool, with a bow or plucked. It's common in orchestras and jazz.
what is a harp | how many strings does a harp have | pedal harp => A concert pedal harp has 47 strings and seven pedals that change the pitch of strings. Red strings mark C and black or blue strings mark F so harpists can find notes.
what is an oboe | oboe sound | oboe tuning orchestra => The oboe is a double reed woodwind with a bright, piercing sound. Orchestras usually tune to the oboe's A because its pitch is steady and easy to hear.
what is a bassoon | bassoon vs oboe | low woodwind => The bassoon is a large double reed woodwind with a low, rich sound. Its tube is about 2.5 m long, folded in half.
what is a saxophone | is a saxophone brass or woodwind | types of saxophones => The saxophone is a woodwind, even though it's made of brass, because it uses a reed. Common types are soprano, alto, tenor and baritone. It was invented by Adolphe Sax in the 1840s.
what is a timpani | kettle drums | timpani tuning => Timpani are large kettle shaped drums that can be tuned to specific notes, often using a foot pedal. They're the main drums in an orchestra.
what is a banjo | banjo sound | banjo strings => A banjo has a round drum like body with a skin head that gives it a bright, twangy sound. It's important in bluegrass, folk and country. Most have 4 or 5 strings.
what is a mandolin | mandolin vs ukulele | mandolin strings => A mandolin is a small string instrument with 8 strings in 4 pairs, tuned like a violin and played with a pick. It's used in bluegrass and folk.
what is an accordion | how does an accordion work | accordion music => An accordion has bellows the player squeezes and pulls to push air over metal reeds, with keys or buttons to choose notes. It's used in polka, tango, folk and zydeco.
what is a didgeridoo | didgeridoo origin | circular breathing => The didgeridoo is a long wooden wind instrument from Aboriginal Australians, at least 1,000 years old. Players use circular breathing to keep a continuous drone.
what is a djembe | djembe drum | west african drum => The djembe is a goblet shaped hand drum from West Africa. Players hit the center for a deep bass tone and the edge for higher tones and slaps.
what are tabla drums | tabla india | indian drums => Tabla are a pair of hand drums from India, a smaller wooden one and a larger metal one, played with fingers and palms to create complex rhythms.
what is a pipe organ | how does a pipe organ work | largest instrument organ => A pipe organ pushes air through pipes of different sizes, each making one pitch. Large organs have thousands of pipes, several keyboards and pedals for the feet.
what is an electric drum kit | electronic drums | e drums vs acoustic => Electronic drums use pads with sensors that trigger sounds through a module, so you can play quietly with headphones. They're great for practicing at home.
what is a guitar amp | guitar amplifier | do electric guitars need an amp => Electric guitars need an amplifier to be heard because their pickups make a small electrical signal. Amps can add effects like distortion and reverb.
what are guitar pedals | effects pedals | distortion pedal => Guitar pedals change the sound, like distortion and overdrive for grit, delay for echoes, reverb for space, wah for vowel sounds, and chorus for a shimmery effect.
what is a pickup on a guitar | single coil vs humbucker | guitar pickups => Pickups are magnets wrapped in wire that turn string vibrations into an electrical signal. Single coil pickups sound bright, and humbuckers sound fuller and less noisy.
what is a 12 string guitar | 12 string guitar sound | twelve string => A 12 string guitar has six pairs of strings, giving a full, shimmering sound. The lower pairs include an octave string.
what is a left handed guitar | can lefties play guitar | left handed instruments => Left handed guitars are mirror images of regular ones. Some lefties play right handed or restring a regular guitar upside down. Many famous guitarists were lefties.
# tag: music.genres
what is bluegrass | bluegrass music | bluegrass instruments => Bluegrass is American roots music from Appalachia, played on acoustic instruments like banjo, fiddle, mandolin, guitar and upright bass, with fast picking and high harmonies.
what is soul music | soul music history | motown => Soul music grew from gospel and R&B in the 1950s and 1960s. Motown, a Detroit record label, produced hits by artists like Stevie Wonder, The Supremes and Marvin Gaye.
what is funk music | funk music history | funk bass => Funk, from the 1960s and 1970s, focuses on a strong groove, syncopated bass lines and tight rhythms. James Brown is called the Godfather of Soul and helped create funk.
what is disco | disco music | disco era => Disco was dance music popular in the 1970s with a steady four on the floor beat, strings and lush production, played in dance clubs.
what is grunge | grunge music | seattle sound => Grunge was a rock style from Seattle in the late 1980s and early 1990s, with distorted guitars and angsty lyrics. Nirvana and Pearl Jam were leading bands.
what is indie music | indie meaning | indie vs mainstream => Indie originally meant music released by independent labels instead of major ones. It now also describes a range of alternative styles.
what is house music | techno vs house | electronic dance genres => House music started in Chicago in the 1980s with a four on the floor beat around 120 to 130 BPM. Techno started in Detroit and tends to be more mechanical and repetitive.
what is dubstep | dubstep music | wobble bass => Dubstep is an electronic genre from London in the early 2000s, known for heavy bass, wobbly sounds and half time drums, usually around 140 BPM.
what is drum and bass | dnb music | jungle music => Drum and bass is an electronic genre from the UK with fast breakbeat drums around 160 to 180 BPM and deep bass lines.
what is synthwave | retrowave | 80s inspired music => Synthwave is electronic music inspired by 1980s movie soundtracks and video games, using vintage synth sounds.
what is afrobeats | afrobeats music | african pop => Afrobeats is a popular music style from West Africa, especially Nigeria and Ghana, mixing African rhythms with pop, hip hop and dancehall. Artists like Burna Boy and Wizkid are well known.
what is reggaeton | reggaeton beat | dembow rhythm => Reggaeton is a Latin music style from Puerto Rico and Panama with a dembow drum rhythm, mixing reggae, hip hop and Latin music. Bad Bunny and Daddy Yankee are famous artists.
what is ska | ska music | ska upbeat => Ska began in Jamaica in the late 1950s, with an upbeat rhythm stressing the offbeat, and led to rocksteady and reggae.
what is opera singing | opera voice | why opera singers are loud => Opera singers train to project over a full orchestra without microphones, using strong breath support and resonance. Operas are often sung in Italian, German or French.
