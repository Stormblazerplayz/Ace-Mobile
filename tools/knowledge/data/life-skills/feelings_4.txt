# tag: life.feelings.situations
what do i do if i feel nervous about a doctor shot | scared of needles | afraid of shots => Lots of people feel this way. Tell the nurse, take slow deep breaths, look away, and wiggle your toes or squeeze a hand. It's over in seconds. Numbing cream can help for some people.
what do i do if i am scared of storms | afraid of thunder | storm anxiety => Learn how storms work and how your home keeps you safe, stay inside, use headphones or music, and do something calming with family. Knowing your safety plan helps.
what do i do if i am scared of dogs | afraid of dogs | fear of animals => Start small, like watching calm dogs from a distance, then meeting a gentle dog with its owner. Learn dog body language. Fears often shrink with slow, safe practice.
what do i do if i am scared of heights | fear of heights | nervous on high places => Many people are. Take slow steps, focus on the next safe move instead of looking down, breathe slowly, and only go as high as you feel okay. Gradual practice helps.
how do i stop worrying about the news | scary news | worried about the world => It's okay to care. Limit how much news you watch, talk with a trusted adult about what you saw, and focus on things you can do, like helping in your community.
how do i deal with a friend moving away | best friend moved | friend moving to another city => It's sad to say goodbye. Make a plan to stay in touch with video calls, games or letters, and spend time with other friends. Real friendships can last across distance.
how do i deal with my parent being away | parent deployed | parent travels for work => Missing a parent is hard. Keep in touch with calls, messages or a shared journal, keep routines, and talk about your feelings with your other caregivers.
how do i deal with feeling guilty | cant stop feeling guilty | guilt wont go away => If you did something wrong, apologize and make it right if you can, then let yourself move on. If guilt sticks around for something that wasn't your fault, talk to a trusted adult.
how do i deal with being the youngest | youngest sibling problems | always left out by older siblings => Find your own interests and friends, ask for fair rules, and tell your parents if you feel left out. Being the youngest can also mean learning faster from watching others.
how do i deal with being the oldest | oldest sibling pressure | too much responsibility => It's okay to feel pressure. Talk to your parents about what's fair, ask for time for yourself, and remember you're still a kid too.
what do i do if my parents dont understand my hobby | parents dont get my interests | parents think gaming is a waste => Show them what you love about it and what you're learning, like building, problem solving or teamwork. Invite them to try it with you.
how do i deal with feeling burned out from sports | tired of my sport | sports burnout => Signs include dreading practice and feeling exhausted. Talk with your parents and coach, take a short break, or try a different sport or level. Sports should be mostly fun.
how do i calm down before bed when my brain wont stop | racing thoughts at night | cant sleep worrying => Write your worries in a notebook to deal with tomorrow, do slow breathing, listen to calm sounds, and keep screens off. If it happens a lot, tell a parent.
how do i deal with a nightmare | woke up from a nightmare | bad dream scared => Remind yourself it was a dream and you're safe. Turn on a light, get a drink of water, breathe slowly and think of something happy. Tell a parent if nightmares happen often.
how do i deal with embarrassment at school | embarrassed in front of class | did something embarrassing => It feels huge, but most people forget fast because they're thinking about themselves. Laugh it off if you can, and remember everyone has embarrassing moments.
how do i handle someone spreading rumors about me | rumors about me | someone lied about me => Stay calm, don't spread rumors back, tell your real friends the truth, and talk to a trusted adult, especially if it's online or keeps happening.
how do i handle being teased about my interests | made fun of for my hobby | teased for liking something => Your interests are yours to enjoy. Ignore or calmly respond with "I like it," find friends who share them, and tell an adult if teasing turns into bullying.
how do i deal with being excluded from a group chat | left out of group chat | removed from group chat => It hurts. Talk privately with a friend you trust about it, focus on people who include you, and talk to a trusted adult if the chat is being used to be mean.
how do i deal with losing a game online | angry at losing online | tilted in games => Take a break after a loss, breathe, and remember everyone loses. Playing while angry usually leads to more losses. Come back when you're calm.
how do i deal with feeling addicted to screens | cant stop playing games | too much screen time feelings => Notice how you feel after long sessions, set timers, keep devices out of your bedroom at night, and plan other fun things. Ask a parent to help with limits.
how do i deal with a big change in my body | body changing during puberty | puberty feelings => Puberty brings lots of changes and feelings, and everyone goes through it at their own pace. Ask a parent, doctor or trusted adult any questions, since it's all normal.
what do i do if my friend is sad all the time | friend seems depressed | worried about a friend => Tell your friend you care and you've noticed, listen, and encourage them to talk to a trusted adult. Tell an adult yourself if you're worried, even if they asked you not to.
what if a friend tells me a secret that worries me | friend told me a dangerous secret | should i tell an adult => If a secret involves someone being hurt, in danger or thinking about hurting themselves, tell a trusted adult. Keeping someone safe matters more than keeping a secret.
how do i deal with being angry at my parents | mad at my parents | parents are unfair => Take some time to cool down, then calmly explain how you feel and why, and listen to their side. You might not get everything, but being heard helps.
how do i ask for a hug or comfort | need comfort | how to tell parents i need support => It's okay to say "I'm having a hard day, can we talk?" or "Can I have a hug?" People who care about you usually want to know.
# tag: life.feelings.kindness
how can i be kinder | ways to be kind | acts of kindness ideas => Hold doors, give genuine compliments, include someone sitting alone, help without being asked, thank people, and write a nice note. Small acts make a big difference.
what is random acts of kindness | kindness ideas for school | kindness challenge => Random acts of kindness are small surprise good deeds, like leaving a nice note, helping a classmate, or picking up litter. They make others and you feel good.
how do i include someone who is left out | include the new kid | making someone feel welcome => Invite them to sit with you or join a game, ask about their interests, and introduce them to friends. Being welcoming can change someone's whole day.
how do i be a good sport when my friend wins | congratulate a friend | happy for others => Say "Nice job!" and mean it, ask how they did it, and celebrate them. Being happy for others makes you a better friend.
what is empathy example | how to show empathy | putting myself in their shoes => Empathy is understanding how someone else feels. If a friend's pet died, saying "That must be really hard, I'm sorry" shows empathy.
how do i be grateful | practicing gratitude | ways to show gratitude => Notice good things each day, say thank you often, write thank you notes, and tell people what you appreciate about them. Gratitude can boost happiness.
how do i be patient with a younger sibling | annoying little brother or sister | patience with siblings => Remember they're still learning, take a breath before reacting, give them small ways to help, and take a break when you need one.
how do i forgive myself | forgiving myself | let go of a mistake => Admit what happened, learn from it, make amends if you can, and remind yourself everyone makes mistakes. Treat yourself as kindly as you'd treat a friend.
how do i cheer myself up | ways to feel better | mood boosters => Go outside, ride your bike, listen to favorite music, talk to a friend, watch something funny, create something, or help someone else.
what is a kindness journal | three good things | daily gratitude => Each night, write three good things that happened and why. Research suggests this simple habit can improve mood over time.
