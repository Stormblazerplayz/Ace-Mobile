# General Pokemon facts, hand-written. Counts are as of 2025.

# tag: pokemon.facts
who created pokemon | who invented pokemon | who made pokemon => Pokemon was created by Satoshi Tajiri, who founded the studio Game Freak. He was inspired by his childhood hobby of collecting bugs.
what does pokemon stand for | what does pokemon mean | why is it called pokemon => Pokemon is short for Pocket Monsters, the series' original Japanese name.
how many pokemon are there | how many pokemon exist | total number of pokemon => As of 2025 there are 1,025 Pokemon species, counting up to Pecharunt from the Scarlet and Violet DLC.
how many pokemon are in each generation | pokemon per generation => The 9 generations added 151, 100, 135, 107, 156, 72, 88, 96 and 120 Pokemon, for 1,025 in total as of 2025.
how many generations of pokemon are there | what generation is pokemon on => As of 2025 there are 9 generations of Pokemon. Generation 9 began with Pokemon Scarlet and Violet in 2022.
what are the pokemon regions | list of pokemon regions | what region is each pokemon game in => The main regions are Kanto (Gen 1), Johto (Gen 2), Hoenn (Gen 3), Sinnoh (Gen 4), Unova (Gen 5), Kalos (Gen 6), Alola (Gen 7), Galar (Gen 8) and Paldea (Gen 9).
what region is kanto based on | what are pokemon regions based on => Many regions are based on real places: Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh on parts of Japan, Unova on New York, Kalos on France, Alola on Hawaii, Galar on Great Britain and Paldea on Spain and Portugal.
what is hisui | what region is legends arceus in => Hisui is the old name for the Sinnoh region, long ago. It is the setting of Pokemon Legends: Arceus, released in January 2022.
what are the gen 1 pokemon games | what games are generation 1 => Generation 1 is Pokemon Red, Blue (Green in Japan) and Yellow, released for the Game Boy.
what are the gen 2 pokemon games | what games are generation 2 => Generation 2 is Pokemon Gold, Silver and Crystal, set in Johto. They added day and night, held items, breeding and two new types, Dark and Steel.
what are the gen 3 pokemon games | what games are generation 3 => Generation 3 is Pokemon Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, set in Hoenn, plus the Kanto remakes FireRed and LeafGreen. Gen 3 added abilities and natures.
what are the gen 4 pokemon games | what games are generation 4 => Generation 4 is Pokemon Diamond, Pearl and Platinum, set in Sinnoh, plus HeartGold and SoulSilver. It split moves into physical and special based on each move.
what are the gen 5 pokemon games | what games are generation 5 => Generation 5 is Pokemon Black and White, and their sequels Black 2 and White 2, set in Unova. It added the most new Pokemon of any generation, 156.
what are the gen 6 pokemon games | what games are generation 6 => Generation 6 is Pokemon X and Y, set in Kalos, plus Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. It added Mega Evolution and the Fairy type.
what are the gen 7 pokemon games | what games are generation 7 => Generation 7 is Pokemon Sun and Moon and Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, set in Alola, plus Let's Go Pikachu and Eevee. It added Z-Moves and Alolan forms.
what are the gen 8 pokemon games | what games are generation 8 => Generation 8 is Pokemon Sword and Shield, set in Galar, plus Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl and Legends: Arceus. It added Dynamax and Gigantamax.
what are the gen 9 pokemon games | what games are generation 9 => Generation 9 is Pokemon Scarlet and Violet, set in Paldea. They were the first fully open-world main games and added Terastallization.
how many types are there in pokemon | what are all the pokemon types | list every pokemon type => There are 18 types: Normal, Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice, Fighting, Poison, Ground, Flying, Psychic, Bug, Rock, Ghost, Dragon, Dark, Steel and Fairy.
when was the fairy type added | what is the newest pokemon type => Fairy is the newest type, added in Generation 6 with Pokemon X and Y in 2013. It was added partly to balance out the very strong Dragon type.
when were dark and steel types added | was dark type in gen 1 => The Dark and Steel types were added in Generation 2 with Pokemon Gold and Silver. Gen 1 had only 15 types.
what is stab in pokemon | same type attack bonus => STAB means Same Type Attack Bonus. When a Pokemon uses a move that matches one of its own types, the move does 1.5 times damage.
what is super effective | how does type effectiveness work => A super effective move does 2 times damage, and against a Pokemon with two weak types it does 4 times. Not very effective moves do half damage, and some types are fully immune.
what are the shiny odds | what are the chances of a shiny | how rare is a shiny pokemon => A wild shiny Pokemon has a 1 in 4,096 chance from Generation 6 onward. In Generations 2 to 5 the odds were 1 in 8,192.
what is a shiny pokemon | what does shiny mean in pokemon | why is my pokemon a different color => A shiny Pokemon is a rare version with different colors than normal. It sparkles when it appears, but it has the same stats as a normal one.
what is the shiny charm | how do you get the shiny charm => The Shiny Charm is a key item that raises your shiny odds. In most games you get it by completing the regional Pokedex.
what is the masuda method | how to breed shinies => The Masuda Method means breeding two Pokemon from games set to different languages. It raises the chance of a shiny egg, to about 1 in 683 from Generation 6 onward.
what is mega evolution | how does mega evolution work => Mega Evolution is a temporary, stronger form some Pokemon can take during battle when holding a Mega Stone. It first appeared in Pokemon X and Y and returns in Legends: Z-A.
what are z moves | how do z moves work => Z-Moves are powerful once-per-battle moves from Pokemon Sun and Moon. A Pokemon holding a Z-Crystal can turn one of its moves into a Z-Move.
what is dynamax | what is gigantamax | how does dynamax work => Dynamax makes a Pokemon giant for three turns with stronger Max Moves. Gigantamax is a special version for certain Pokemon that also changes how they look. Both are from Pokemon Sword and Shield.
what is terastallization | what is tera type | how does tera work => Terastallization, from Pokemon Scarlet and Violet, makes a Pokemon shine like a gem and changes its type to its Tera Type for the rest of the battle.
what is a legendary pokemon | what is the difference between legendary and mythical => Legendary Pokemon are rare, powerful Pokemon tied to a game's story, like Mewtwo or Rayquaza. Mythical Pokemon, like Mew and Celebi, are even rarer and usually given out only at special events.
what was the first pokemon ever made | what pokemon was designed first => Rhydon is often said to be the first Pokemon ever designed. Bulbasaur is number 1 in the Pokedex.
who designed pikachu | why is pikachu the mascot | what does pikachu name mean => Pikachu was designed by Atsuko Nishida. Its name comes from Japanese sounds: pika for a sparkle of electricity and chu for a mouse squeak.
what is the pokedex | what does the pokedex do => The Pokedex is a device that records data on every Pokemon you see or catch. Completing it is one of the main goals of each game.
how many pokemon can you carry | how many pokemon in a party => You can carry up to 6 Pokemon in your party. Extra Pokemon you catch go to storage boxes in the PC.
what is the max level in pokemon | how high can pokemon level go => The highest level a Pokemon can reach is 100. A Rare Candy raises a Pokemon by one level.
how many gym badges are there | how many gyms in pokemon => Most main Pokemon games have 8 gym badges to collect before you face the Elite Four and the Champion.
what is the elite four | who is the champion in pokemon => The Elite Four are four very strong trainers you battle one after another at the end of most Pokemon games, followed by the Champion, the best trainer in the region.
what are the different poke balls | what is the best poke ball | what does a master ball do => The basic balls are the Poke Ball, Great Ball and Ultra Ball, each better than the last. The Master Ball catches any wild Pokemon without fail, but you usually only get one.
how do you catch pokemon easier | tips for catching pokemon => Lower the Pokemon's HP into the red without fainting it, and give it a status condition like sleep or paralysis. Then use a better ball such as an Ultra Ball.
what are ivs in pokemon | what are evs | what are individual values => IVs are hidden numbers from 0 to 31 for each stat that make every Pokemon a little different. EVs are points a Pokemon earns by battling that slowly raise its stats.
what are natures in pokemon | how many natures are there => There are 25 natures. Most raise one stat by 10 percent and lower another by 10 percent, while 5 natures change nothing.
what are abilities in pokemon | what is a hidden ability => Abilities are passive powers, like Levitate making a Pokemon immune to Ground moves. Abilities were added in Generation 3, and Hidden Abilities are rarer ones added in Generation 5.
how do pokemon evolve | ways to evolve pokemon => Pokemon evolve in different ways: by reaching a level, using an evolution stone, trading, high friendship, holding an item, or special conditions like time of day.
what does ditto do | why is ditto good for breeding => Ditto can transform into its opponent. In breeding it can make an egg with almost any Pokemon that can breed, which makes it very useful.
what is missingno | missingno glitch => MissingNo. is a famous glitch Pokemon from Pokemon Red and Blue that appeared because of a programming bug. Encountering it could duplicate items but could also mess up your save.
what is pokemon go | when did pokemon go come out | who makes pokemon go => Pokemon GO is a mobile game from July 2016 that uses your phone's GPS and camera so you find and catch Pokemon in the real world. It was made by Niantic, and in 2025 Niantic sold its games business, including Pokemon GO, to Scopely.
what is pokemon unite | how does pokemon unite work => Pokemon Unite is a free team battle game from 2021 where two teams of five Pokemon battle to score points in the other team's goals.
what is the pokemon trading card game | when did pokemon cards come out | what is pokemon tcg pocket => The Pokemon Trading Card Game began in Japan in 1996. Pokemon TCG Pocket is a mobile version from 2024 where you open digital packs each day and battle.
when did the pokemon anime start | who is ash ketchum | did ash become champion => The Pokemon anime started in Japan in 1997, following Ash Ketchum and Pikachu. Ash became World Champion in 2022, and the new series Pokemon Horizons, starring Liko and Roy, began in 2023.
who is professor oak | who gives you your first pokemon => Professor Oak is the Pokemon Professor of Kanto who gives you your first Pokemon in the Gen 1 games. Each region has its own professor, and most are named after a kind of tree, like Elm, Birch and Rowan.
who are team rocket | what is team rocket => Team Rocket is a criminal group that steals Pokemon for profit. In the anime, Jessie, James and Meowth are the Team Rocket trio who keep chasing Pikachu.
what is the strongest pokemon | what pokemon has the highest stats => By base stats, Eternamax Eternatus has the highest total at 1,125, but it can't be used normally. Of usable Pokemon, Arceus and Mega forms of Mewtwo and Rayquaza are among the strongest.
what is a starter pokemon | what are the starter types => A starter is the first Pokemon you choose at the start of a game. In main series games you almost always pick from a Grass, Fire or Water type.
what is a pokemon egg | how do you hatch pokemon eggs => Eggs come from breeding two compatible Pokemon, and you hatch them by walking or moving around with the egg in your party. Baby Pokemon come from eggs.
what are regional forms | what are alolan forms | what is a regional variant => Regional forms are versions of a Pokemon that look different and may have different types in a certain region, like Alolan Vulpix, which is an Ice type instead of Fire.
what is a paradox pokemon | what are paradox pokemon => Paradox Pokemon, from Scarlet and Violet, are ancient or futuristic versions of known Pokemon, like Great Tusk, an ancient form related to Donphan.
what is pokerus | is pokerus good => Pokerus is a rare, harmless virus in the Pokemon games that makes a Pokemon gain EVs twice as fast. It is good to have.
