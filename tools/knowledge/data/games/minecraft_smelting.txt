# tag: minecraft.smelting
how do you use a furnace | how does smelting work | how to cook food in minecraft => Put the item in the top slot and fuel like coal in the bottom slot. The result comes out on the right after about 10 seconds.
what is the best fuel in minecraft | best furnace fuel | what fuel lasts longest => A lava bucket lasts the longest, smelting 100 items. A block of coal smelts 80, and coal or charcoal smelts 8.
how many items does coal smelt | how long does coal burn | coal fuel => One coal or charcoal smelts 8 items.
can you use wood as fuel | how many items does a log smelt | planks as fuel => Yes. Logs and planks each smelt 1.5 items, and a stick smelts half an item.
can you use dried kelp as fuel | dried kelp block fuel | kelp fuel => Yes. A dried kelp block smelts 20 items, and you can grow kelp to make it forever.
can you use bamboo as fuel | bamboo fuel | scaffolding fuel => Yes, but it's weak. Bamboo and scaffolding each smelt only a quarter of an item.
how do you make charcoal | what is charcoal | charcoal vs coal => Smelt logs in a furnace. Charcoal works just like coal for fuel and torches, but you can't make a block of coal from it.
how do you make smooth stone | smooth stone recipe | how to get smooth stone => Smelt regular stone in a furnace.
how do you make stone from cobblestone | how to get stone | smelt cobblestone => Smelt cobblestone in a furnace to get stone. You can also mine stone with Silk Touch.
how do you make terracotta | how to get terracotta | smelt clay block => Smelt a clay block in a furnace. Badlands biomes also have lots of terracotta.
how do you make bricks in minecraft | how to get a brick | smelt clay ball => Smelt clay balls in a furnace to get bricks, then put 4 bricks in a 2 by 2 square for a bricks block.
how do you make nether bricks | how to get nether brick | smelt netherrack => Smelt netherrack in a furnace to get a nether brick item.
how do you make cracked stone bricks | cracked bricks recipe | how to get cracked stone bricks => Smelt stone bricks in a furnace.
how do you make smooth sandstone | smooth quartz | smooth sandstone recipe => Smelt sandstone for smooth sandstone, and smelt a block of quartz for smooth quartz.
how do you get iron ingots | how to smelt raw iron | what do you do with raw iron => Smelt raw iron in a furnace or blast furnace to get iron ingots. Mining iron ore gives raw iron unless you use Silk Touch.
how do you get gold ingots | how to smelt raw gold | gold ingot from ore => Smelt raw gold in a furnace or blast furnace. In the Nether, nether gold ore drops gold nuggets instead.
how do you get copper ingots | how to smelt raw copper | copper ingot => Smelt raw copper in a furnace or blast furnace.
how do you make popped chorus fruit | smelt chorus fruit | popped chorus => Smelt chorus fruit in a furnace.

how do you get an iron nugget from armor | smelt iron tools | recycle iron gear => Smelt iron or chainmail tools, weapons or armor to get 1 iron nugget each. Gold gear gives 1 gold nugget.
what does a blast furnace do | blast furnace vs furnace | why use a blast furnace => A blast furnace smelts ores, raw metals and metal gear twice as fast as a furnace, but it can't cook food or smelt other blocks.
what does a smoker do | smoker vs furnace | why use a smoker => A smoker cooks food twice as fast as a furnace, but it only works on food.
how do you cook with a campfire | campfire cooking | can you cook on a campfire => Place up to 4 raw food items on a campfire. They cook in 30 seconds with no fuel, but you don't get XP.
do you get xp from smelting | furnace xp | how to get xp from a furnace => Yes. Taking items out of a furnace gives XP, and smelting things like gold or diamonds from ore gives the most.
what is the fastest way to cook food | quickest way to cook meat | best way to cook => A smoker cooks food in 5 seconds, twice as fast as a furnace. A campfire cooks 4 items at once with no fuel.
can a hopper feed a furnace | automatic furnace | hopper furnace setup => Yes. A hopper on top puts items in, a hopper on the side adds fuel, and a hopper under it takes the results out.
