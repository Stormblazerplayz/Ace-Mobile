# General gaming words and slang, hand-written.

# tag: games.terms
what does fps mean in games | frames per second meaning => FPS means frames per second, how many pictures your game draws each second. 60 FPS looks smooth, and higher feels even smoother. FPS can also mean first-person shooter.
what is a first person shooter | examples of fps games => A first-person shooter is a game where you see through your character's eyes and shoot, like Call of Duty, Halo, Valorant or Arsenal on Roblox.
what is a third person game | third person vs first person | what does tps mean => In a third-person game the camera is behind your character so you can see them, like Fortnite. A third-person shooter is sometimes called a TPS.
what is ping in games | what does high ping mean => Ping is how long it takes your game to talk to the server and back, in milliseconds. Under 50 ms is great, and over 150 ms feels laggy.
what does packet loss do in games | how to fix packet loss => Packet loss is when data between you and the server gets lost, causing teleporting and missed shots. A wired connection usually helps.
what is rubber banding in games | why do i keep teleporting back | rubberbanding => Rubber banding is when you get snapped back to where you were a moment ago because the server and your game disagree, usually from lag.
what is input delay | why do my controls feel slow => Input lag is the delay between pressing a button and seeing it happen. Game mode on your TV and wired controllers can lower it.
what does rng mean | what is rng in games | rng meaning => RNG means random number generator. In games it means luck, like whether you get a rare drop.
what does npc mean | what is an npc | npc meaning in games => An NPC is a non-player character, any character the game controls instead of a person, like a shopkeeper or villager.
what does dps mean | what is dps in games | dps meaning => DPS means damage per second, how fast you deal damage. It's also the name for a character role focused on dealing damage.
what is a tank in games | tank role meaning | what does a tank do => A tank is a tough character who takes hits and protects the team so the damage dealers can attack.
what is a healer in games | what is a support role | support class meaning => A healer or support keeps teammates alive and helps them with healing, shields or buffs.
what does aggro mean | what is aggro in games | pulling aggro => Aggro is an enemy's attention. If you pull aggro, the enemy starts attacking you.
what is a speedrun | what does speedrunning mean | how do speedruns work => A speedrun is beating a game or part of it as fast as possible. Speedrunners use timers, routes and sometimes glitches.
any% vs 100% | speedrun categories => Any% means beating the game as fast as possible by any means. 100% means finishing everything in the game as fast as possible.
what is a tas speedrun | what does tas mean | tool assisted speedrun => TAS means tool-assisted speedrun. It uses slowed-down input tools to make frame-perfect runs no human could do live.
what does wr and pb mean | what is a pb in gaming | world record speedrun => WR means world record, the fastest time anyone has done. PB means personal best, your own fastest time.
what does meta mean in games | what is the meta | meta meaning gaming => The meta is whatever strategy, character or weapon is strongest and most popular in a game at that time. It changes when updates buff or nerf things.
what does nerf mean | what does buff mean | nerf vs buff => A nerf makes something weaker in an update. A buff makes something stronger.
what does op mean in games | what is overpowered | op meaning => OP means overpowered, something so strong it feels unfair.
what is a noob | noob vs newbie => A noob is a beginner. Newbie is the nicer way to say it, while noob is often used as a tease.
what is gg in games | gg wp meaning => GG means good game, said at the end of a match. GG WP means good game, well played.
what does afk mean | afk meaning | what is afk in games => AFK means away from keyboard, when a player isn't playing for a bit.
what does pvp mean | what does pve mean | pvp vs pve => PvP means player versus player, fighting other people. PvE means player versus environment, fighting the game's own enemies.
what does mmo mean | what is an mmorpg | mmo meaning => MMO means massively multiplayer online, a game with huge numbers of players in one world. An MMORPG is an MMO role-playing game, like World of Warcraft.
what is an rpg | what does rpg mean in games | role playing game => RPG means role-playing game. You play a character who levels up, gets better gear and goes on quests, like Pokémon or Zelda-style adventures.
what does xp mean | what is experience points | xp meaning games => XP means experience points. You earn them by doing things, and enough XP levels you up.
what does hp mean in games | what is hp | health points meaning => HP means health points or hit points. When your HP hits zero, you're defeated.
what is a respawn | what does respawn mean | respawn timer => Respawning is coming back to life after you're defeated, usually at a spawn point after a short timer.
what is spawn camping | why is spawn killing bad | spawn camping meaning => Spawn camping is waiting where players respawn to defeat them right away. Most players think it's unfair.
what is camping in games | what is a camper | camping meaning => Camping is staying hidden in one spot and waiting for enemies to come to you.
what is a crit | critical hit meaning | what does crit mean => A crit, or critical hit, is an extra-strong hit that does more damage than normal. Many games make crits random.
what is a debuff | what does debuff mean | buff and debuff => A debuff is a bad effect on a character, like slowness or poison. A buff is a good effect that makes them stronger.
what is a boss in games | what is a boss fight | boss meaning => A boss is a very strong enemy, often at the end of a level, that's harder than normal enemies.
what is loot in games | loot drop meaning => Loot is the stuff you collect, like weapons, items and money, from chests or defeated enemies.
what is a drop rate | what does drop rate mean | drop chance => The drop rate is the chance an item drops. A 1 percent drop rate means it drops about once every 100 tries on average.
what is grinding in games | what does grinding mean | grind meaning => Grinding means doing the same thing over and over to earn XP, money or items.
what is farming in games | what does farming mean in games => Farming means collecting something over and over on purpose, like farming coins, XP or a rare drop.
what does dlc mean | what is dlc | is dlc worth it => DLC means downloadable content, extra stuff added to a game after release, like new levels, characters or story. Some is free and some costs money.
what is a microtransaction | microtransactions meaning => Microtransactions are small real-money purchases inside a game, like skins, currency or boosts. Ask a parent before buying.
what is a loot box | are loot boxes gambling | loot box meaning => A loot box is a paid item that gives a random reward. Some countries regulate them because they work like gambling.
what is a battle pass | battle pass meaning => A battle pass is a seasonal reward track. You level it up by playing, and the paid version unlocks extra rewards like skins.
what is gacha | what is a gacha game | gacha meaning => Gacha is a system where you spend currency to pull random characters or items. Games like Genshin Impact use it.
what does f2p mean | what is free to play | f2p meaning => F2P means free to play, a game you can play without paying. Many make money from optional purchases.
what does p2w mean | what is pay to win | pay to win meaning => P2W means pay to win, when paying real money gives a big advantage over other players.
what is a skin in games | what are cosmetics | skin meaning gaming => A skin is a cosmetic look for a character or item. It changes how it looks, not how strong it is.
what is a patch in games | patch notes meaning => A patch is an update that fixes bugs or balances the game. A hotfix is a quick small patch for an urgent problem. Patch notes list the changes.
what is early access | is early access finished | early access meaning => Early access means a game is still being made but you can buy and play it now while the developers keep updating it.
what is a beta in games | beta test meaning | alpha vs beta game => A beta is a test version before the full release, used to find bugs. An alpha is an even earlier, rougher version.
what is an open world game | open world meaning | examples of open world games => An open world game lets you explore a big map freely in any order, like Breath of the Wild, Minecraft or GTA.
what is a sandbox game | sandbox meaning | sandbox vs open world => A sandbox game gives you tools to build and play however you want, with few set goals. Minecraft and Roblox are sandbox games.
what is procedural generation | what does procedurally generated mean | random world generation => Procedural generation means the game builds worlds with rules and random numbers instead of designers placing everything. Minecraft and No Man's Sky do this.
what is permadeath | what does permadeath mean | permadeath games => Permadeath means when you die, your character or save is gone for good, like Hardcore mode in Minecraft.
what is a roguelike | roguelike vs roguelite | what does roguelite mean => A roguelike has random levels and permadeath, so each run starts fresh. A roguelite also keeps some upgrades between runs, like Hades.
what is a metroidvania | metroidvania meaning | metroidvania games => A metroidvania is a 2D exploration game with a big connected map, where new abilities open new areas. Hollow Knight and Metroid Dread are examples.
what is a soulslike | soulslike meaning | souls games => A soulslike is a hard action game with tough bosses and careful combat, like Dark Souls or Elden Ring.
what is a glitch | what is a bug in a game | glitch vs bug => A bug is a mistake in a game's code. A glitch is the weird thing that happens because of it, like falling through the floor.
what is an aimbot | what is wallhack | cheats in shooters => An aimbot is a cheat that aims for you, and a wallhack lets you see players through walls. Both can get you banned.
what is a smurf in games | what is smurfing | smurf account meaning => A smurf is a good player using a new account to play against beginners. Most players think it's unfair.
what does carry mean in games | getting carried meaning | carry a team => To carry means one player does most of the work to win for the team. Getting carried means someone else won it for you.
what is a clutch | what does clutch mean in games | clutch play => A clutch is winning a tough moment when it all depends on you, like winning a 1 versus 3 at the end.
what is an ace | ace meaning valorant => An ace is when one player defeats the whole enemy team by themselves in a round.
what is a combo in games | combo meaning | fighting game combo => A combo is a chain of attacks that hit one after another, often doing extra damage.
what is kiting in games | what does kiting mean | kite enemies => Kiting means attacking an enemy while staying out of its reach, so it chases you but can't hit you.
what is a rush strategy | what does rush mean in games | zerg rush => Rushing means attacking fast and early, before the other team is ready.
what is a lobby in games | game lobby meaning | waiting lobby => A lobby is where players wait before a match starts.
what is matchmaking | how does matchmaking work | matchmaking meaning => Matchmaking is how a game puts players together in a match, often trying to match people of similar skill.
what is elo in games | what is mmr | elo vs mmr => Elo and MMR are hidden skill ratings. Win and your number goes up, lose and it goes down, and matchmaking uses it to find fair games.
what is ranked mode | ranked vs casual | what does ranked mean => Ranked mode is the competitive mode where you earn or lose rank based on wins. Casual matches don't affect your rank.
what is crossplay | crossplay meaning => Crossplay means players on different devices, like PC, console and phone, can play together.
what is cross save | cross progression meaning | cross progression => Cross save, or cross progression, means your progress carries over between devices.
what is split screen | split screen meaning | couch co-op => Split screen means two or more players share one screen, each getting part of it, on the same console.
what is co-op | what does co op mean | coop games => Co-op means cooperative, players working together on the same team against the game.
what is desync | what does desync mean | desync in games => Desync is when your game and the server disagree about what happened, so things jump around or hits don't count.
server tick rate | 64 tick vs 128 tick => Tick rate is how many times per second the server updates the game. Higher tick rates make online games feel more accurate.
what is fov in games | what does field of view mean | best fov => FOV means field of view, how much of the world you can see on screen at once. Higher FOV shows more but makes things look smaller.
what is the hud in a game | what does hud mean | heads up display => The HUD, or heads-up display, is the info on screen during play, like your health, map and ammo.
what is dpi on a mouse | mouse sensitivity | dpi meaning => DPI is how far your cursor moves when you move your mouse. Players combine it with in-game sensitivity to aim comfortably.
what are keybinds | what does keybind mean | change controls => Keybinds are which keys do which actions. Most PC games let you change them in the settings.
what does 144hz mean | hz vs fps => Refresh rate, in hertz, is how many times per second your screen updates. A 144 Hz monitor can show up to 144 FPS.
what is an easter egg in a game | game easter eggs | easter egg meaning => An easter egg is a hidden secret or joke the developers put in a game for players to find.
what is lore in games | game lore => Lore is a game's backstory and world history, like the stories of its characters and places.
what is a cutscene | cutscene meaning | can you skip cutscenes => A cutscene is a movie-like scene in a game where you watch the story instead of playing.
what does toxic mean in gaming | what is a toxic player | how to deal with toxic players => A toxic player is mean, insults people or ruins games on purpose. Mute, block and report them instead of arguing.
what does tryhard mean | what is a sweaty player | tryhard meaning => A tryhard is someone who plays super seriously even in casual games. Sweaty means the same thing.
what does rage quit mean | rage quitting | why do people rage quit => Rage quitting means leaving a game angrily after losing or getting frustrated. Taking a break is better.
what is tilt in gaming | being tilted => Being tilted means you're frustrated after losses and start playing worse. A short break helps you reset.
what is a mod in games | what are game mods | modding meaning => A mod is a change made by players that adds or changes things in a game, like new items, maps or graphics.
what is a modpack | modpack meaning | minecraft modpacks => A modpack is a bundle of many mods set up to work together, often installed with a launcher like CurseForge or Modrinth.
what does indie game mean | what is an indie game | indie games examples => An indie game is made by a small team or one person without a big publisher, like Stardew Valley, Undertale or Hollow Knight.
what is a aaa game | what does triple a mean | aaa games => A AAA game is a big-budget game from a major studio, like Call of Duty or Spider-Man.
what is a remaster | remake vs remaster | what is a remake => A remaster updates an old game's graphics and performance. A remake rebuilds the game from scratch, often with big changes.
what is a sequel | what is a prequel | spin off meaning => A sequel continues a game's story, a prequel happens before it, and a spin-off uses the same world or characters in a different kind of game.
what are achievements | what are trophies in games | achievement hunting => Achievements, or trophies on PlayStation, are rewards for completing challenges in a game.
what is a checkpoint | save point meaning | checkpoint in games => A checkpoint is a spot where the game saves your progress, so you restart there if you fail.
what is a side quest | main quest vs side quest | side quest meaning => The main quest is the main story. Side quests are optional extra missions for rewards or more story.
what is fast travel | fast travel meaning | how does fast travel work => Fast travel lets you jump instantly to places you've already found on the map.
what are i frames | invincibility frames | dodge i-frames => I-frames, or invincibility frames, are short moments, like during a dodge roll, when attacks can't hurt you.
what does frame perfect mean | frame perfect trick | frame meaning speedrun => Frame perfect means an input has to happen on one exact frame, often just 1/60th of a second, to work.
what is a hit marker | hitmarker meaning | what does a hitmarker show => A hit marker is a small mark that pops up on your crosshair to show your shot hit.
what is a crosshair | crosshair meaning | best crosshair => The crosshair is the aiming mark in the middle of the screen in shooting games.
what is a headshot | why do headshots do more damage | headshot meaning => A headshot is hitting an enemy in the head. Most shooters give headshots extra damage.
