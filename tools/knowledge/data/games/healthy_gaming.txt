# Healthy gaming habits and online safety for young players, hand-written.

# tag: games.healthy
how long should i play video games a day | how much gaming is too much | how many hours of games is ok => There is no single magic number, but a good rule is to keep gaming from crowding out sleep, homework, being active and time with family and friends. Agree on a daily limit with your parents and stick to it.
how often should i take a break from gaming | when should i take gaming breaks | do i need breaks while gaming => Take a short break about every 45 to 60 minutes. Stand up, stretch, get a drink of water and look at something far away for a minute.
why do my eyes hurt after gaming | how to stop eye strain from screens | tired eyes from ipad => Staring at a screen makes you blink less, which dries and tires your eyes. Try the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds, and keep the screen brightness close to the room's light.
how should i sit while gaming | best posture for gaming | why does my neck hurt from gaming => Sit with your back supported, feet flat, and the screen at about eye level so you are not looking down for a long time. If you play on an iPad, a stand helps keep your neck straight.
why do my hands hurt after gaming | wrist pain from gaming | sore thumbs from controller => Gripping a controller or tapping a screen for a long time can make hands and wrists sore. Take breaks, stretch your fingers and wrists, and stop if it keeps hurting. Tell a parent if the pain doesn't go away.
should i game before bed | does gaming affect sleep | why cant i sleep after playing games => Gaming right before bed can make it harder to fall asleep because exciting games keep your brain alert and screen light can delay sleepiness. Try stopping about an hour before bedtime.
how much sleep does a 12 year old need | how many hours should a kid sleep => Kids aged 6 to 12 need about 9 to 12 hours of sleep a night, and teens 13 to 18 need about 8 to 10 hours, according to the American Academy of Sleep Medicine.
is gaming bad for you | are video games good or bad | can games be good for your brain => Gaming in healthy amounts is fine for most people and can build problem solving, teamwork and creativity. It becomes a problem when it pushes out sleep, school, exercise or friends.
what is gaming addiction | can you get addicted to video games | signs of playing too much => Signs of playing too much include feeling upset when you can't play, losing sleep, skipping homework or meals, and losing interest in other things. If that sounds like you, talk to a parent or another trusted adult.
how do i stop playing when i should | how to stop gaming when time is up | hard to stop playing games => Set a timer before you start, finish at a natural stopping point like the end of a match, and give yourself a 5-minute warning. Saving often helps you stop without losing progress.
why do i get so angry at games | how to stop raging at games | how to calm down when losing => Getting frustrated is normal. When you feel rage building, pause the game, take a few slow breaths or walk away for a few minutes. Games are more fun when you come back calm.
should i eat and drink while gaming | snacks while gaming => Keep water nearby and eat proper meals away from the screen when you can. Playing through meals or forgetting to drink is a sign it's time for a break.
how to balance gaming and homework | homework or games first | how to manage time with games => A simple plan is homework first, then games. Using gaming as a reward after your work is done makes it easier to enjoy without feeling guilty.
is it bad to play games all day on weekends | gaming all weekend => Long gaming days are fine once in a while, but mix in time outside, moving your body and seeing friends. Taking breaks every hour still matters on weekends.
how do parents set screen time | ipad screen time limits => Screen Time is an iPad and iPhone feature that shows how long you use apps and lets parents set daily limits for apps or games. It's in the Settings app.
what are parental controls | why do parents use parental controls | what do parental controls do => Parental controls let parents set limits on games and devices, like time limits, age ratings, spending and who you can chat with. Nintendo Switch, Xbox, PlayStation, Roblox and iPads all have them.
what does esrb mean | what is an esrb rating | what does rated e10 mean => ESRB ratings tell you who a game is meant for in the United States and Canada. E is for Everyone, E10+ is for ages 10 and up, T is for Teen (13 and up) and M is for Mature (17 and up).
what does pegi mean | what is pegi 12 | pegi rating meaning => PEGI is the age rating system used in Europe. Its ratings are 3, 7, 12, 16 and 18, meaning the youngest age the game is suitable for.
should i share my real name online | is it safe to give my address in a game | what info should i keep private online => Never share your real full name, address, school, phone number, passwords or photos with people you meet in games. Use a username that does not reveal who you are.
what should i do if a stranger messages me in a game | someone online asked where i live | creepy message in a game => Don't reply or share anything about yourself. Block and report the player, and tell a parent or trusted adult right away. You won't be in trouble for telling.
what if someone asks me to keep a secret online | online friend wants to meet up | someone asked for pictures in a game => That's a big warning sign. Stop talking to them, don't send anything, and tell a parent or trusted adult right away. Safe people don't ask kids to keep secrets from their parents.
someone is being mean to me in a game | how to deal with bullies online => Cyberbullying is when someone uses messages, chat or games to hurt, threaten or embarrass someone over and over. Don't fight back. Save screenshots, block them, report them and tell a trusted adult.
what is a good password for games | how to keep my account safe => Use a long password with several random words, numbers and symbols, and use a different one for each account. Turn on two-step verification if the game offers it, and never share your password, even with friends.
what is two step verification | should i turn on 2fa => Two-step verification, or 2FA, means you need a second code, often from an app or email, as well as your password to sign in. It makes it much harder for someone to steal your account.
someone said they will give me free robux | free vbucks scam => Sites and people that promise free Robux, V-Bucks or skins are scams. They try to steal your account or password. The only real way to get them is through the official game or store.
how to tell if something is a scam in a game | what are common game scams | how do scammers trick gamers => Common scams include fake free currency sites, fake trades, people pretending to be game staff and links that ask you to log in. Real staff will never ask for your password.
someone wants to trade but go first | is trust trading safe => Be careful with any trade where you have to give your items first and trust the other person to send theirs back. Use the game's official trade window, which swaps both sides at the same time.
should i ask before buying things in games | can i buy robux without asking | buying in game stuff with parents card => Always ask a parent before spending real money in a game, even small amounts. Purchases add up fast, and parents can set up limits so you don't spend by accident.
what are microtransactions | what does in app purchases mean => Microtransactions are small purchases you make inside a game with real money, like skins, coins or passes. Many free games make their money this way.
how do i stay safe in voice chat | is voice chat safe for kids => Only use voice chat with people you know in real life, or if your parents say it's okay. Never share personal details out loud, and you can mute or leave anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
is it okay to take a break from a game i'm sick of | what if a game isnt fun anymore | gaming burnout => Yes. If a game stops being fun, it's fine to take a break or try something different. Burnout happens to pro players too.
how do i keep gaming fun with friends | playing nicely with friends online => Take turns choosing the game, be a good sport when you win or lose, and don't blame teammates. Playing together is about having fun, not just winning.
is it ok to play games that are rated higher than my age | should i play m rated games at 12 => Age ratings are there to help families decide. Check with your parents before playing games rated above your age, since they may contain violence, scary scenes or online chat with adults.
does gaming help with anything | what skills do video games teach | can games make you smarter => Games can help with problem solving, quick decisions, hand-eye coordination, teamwork, planning and creativity. Building games like Minecraft can even teach basic engineering and logic.
how do i get better without getting frustrated | how to improve at games faster => Practice one skill at a time, watch how good players do it, take breaks when you're tired and notice what you improved. Short focused practice beats hours of tired playing.
what should i do if i lose all my progress | lost my save file => It's frustrating, but check if the game has cloud saves or backups first. In the future, turn on cloud saving or back up your worlds so you can restore them.
