what is trailmakers | what kind of game is trailmakers | how does trailmakers work => Trailmakers is a building game where you make cars, planes, boats and machines out of blocks and then test them in a physics world. Parts can break off when you crash, so you have to build tough designs.
who made trailmakers | trailmakers developer | when did trailmakers come out => Trailmakers is made by Flashbulb Games, a studio in Denmark. It started in early access in 2018 and had its full release in 2019.
can you play trailmakers with friends | is trailmakers multiplayer => Yes, Trailmakers has online multiplayer, so you can build and drive with friends in the same world.
what parts can you build with in trailmakers | trailmakers blocks => Trailmakers creations are made from blocks such as wheels, engines, propellers, thrusters, wings, seats and structural blocks. Where you place weight and power changes how your vehicle handles.
what is beamng | what is beamng drive | what kind of game is beamng => BeamNG.drive is a driving simulator famous for realistic crashes. Every car is built from a soft-body physics model, so metal bends, crumples and breaks like a real car would.
who made beamng | beamng developer | when did beamng come out => BeamNG.drive is made by BeamNG GmbH, a company in Germany. It has been in early access on Steam since 2015 and keeps getting big updates.
what is soft body physics | why are beamng crashes so realistic | how does beamng physics work => Soft-body physics means objects can bend and squash instead of acting like solid blocks. In BeamNG, each vehicle is a mesh of points connected by beams, and the game calculates how every beam stretches or breaks in a crash.
what can you do in beamng | beamng game modes | is beamng just crashing => In BeamNG.drive you can drive freely around big maps, try scenarios and time trials, tune cars and build crashes in a sandbox. There is also a huge library of community mods with new cars and maps.

who made no man's sky | when did no mans sky come out | no mans sky developer => No Man's Sky was made by Hello Games, a small studio in England, and came out in August 2016. It launched with fewer features than people expected, but years of free updates turned it into a much-loved game.
how many planets are in no man's sky | how big is no mans sky => No Man's Sky has about 18 quintillion planets (18,446,744,073,709,551,616). They are created by procedural generation, so nobody could ever visit them all.
how are no man's sky planets made | is no mans sky made by hand => No Man's Sky builds its planets, creatures and plants with procedural generation, which means math rules create them from a seed number instead of designers making each one by hand. Minecraft worlds are made in a similar way.
is no man's sky multiplayer | can you play no mans sky with friends => Yes, No Man's Sky added multiplayer in its NEXT update in 2018, so you can explore and build with friends.
what are sentinels in no man's sky | why are robots attacking me in no mans sky => Sentinels are robot guards that patrol planets. They attack if you mine too much or shoot at things, and the more you anger them, the stronger their response gets.
what is a multi tool in no man's sky | what does the multitool do => The Multi-Tool is your main handheld tool in No Man's Sky. It mines resources, scans plants and animals, and can be upgraded with weapons and better mining lasers.
