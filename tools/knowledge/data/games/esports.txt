# Esports, hand-written. Only long-standing facts; no current rosters or prize records.

# tag: games.esports
what are esports | what does esports mean | is gaming a sport => Esports means competitive video gaming, where players or teams compete in organized matches and tournaments, often with prize money and live audiences.
how do esports players make money | can you get paid to play video games | do pro gamers get paid => Pro players earn money from team salaries, tournament prize money, sponsors and streaming. Only a small number of players earn a full living from it.
how do you become a pro gamer | how to go pro in gaming | can i become an esports player => Most pros start by getting very good at one game, climbing ranked ladders, joining amateur teams and entering online tournaments. Many teams also look for teamwork, good attitude and practice habits, not only skill.
how old do you have to be to play esports | minimum age for pro gaming | can a 12 year old go pro => It depends on the game and league. Many big pro leagues require players to be at least 16 or 17, but there are school and youth leagues for younger players.
what is a scrim | what does scrim mean | what are scrims in esports => A scrim is a practice match between two teams. It is short for scrimmage, and teams use scrims to test strategies without anything at stake.
what is a bootcamp in esports | esports bootcamp meaning => An esports bootcamp is a period when a team lives or trains together, often before a big tournament, to practice many hours a day as a group.
what is a caster | what is a shoutcaster | who talks during esports matches => A caster, or shoutcaster, is a commentator who explains what is happening in an esports match, like a sports announcer on TV.
what is an esports org | what is an esports organization | what does org mean in esports => An esports organization, or org, is a company that runs pro teams. It signs players, pays salaries, finds sponsors and often has teams in several different games.
what is a lan tournament | what does lan mean in esports | lan event meaning => A LAN tournament is one where players compete in the same building on a local network instead of online. Big finals are usually played on LAN in front of a live crowd.
what is a bracket in a tournament | what is a single elimination bracket | double elimination meaning => A bracket shows who plays who in a tournament. In single elimination one loss knocks you out, while in double elimination you are out only after losing twice.
what does bo3 mean | what is best of 3 | bo5 meaning => Bo3 means best of three: the first team to win two games wins the match. Bo5 means best of five, so you need three wins.
what is a seed in a tournament | what does seeding mean => Seeding ranks teams before a tournament so the strongest are spread out in the bracket and do not meet each other in the first round.
what is the league of legends world championship | what is worlds in league of legends | lol worlds => Worlds is the yearly world championship of League of Legends, run by Riot Games. Teams from leagues around the world compete, and it is one of the most watched esports events.
what is the international dota 2 | what is ti in dota | the international tournament => The International, often called TI, is the yearly world championship for Dota 2, run by Valve since 2011. It became famous for very large prize pools that were partly funded by players buying in-game items.
what is the fortnite world cup | when was the fortnite world cup => The Fortnite World Cup was held in New York in July 2019. The solo champion was Kyle Giersdorf, known as Bugha, who won 3 million dollars at age 16.
what is the fncs | fortnite champion series meaning => The FNCS, or Fortnite Champion Series, is Epic Games' main competitive Fortnite circuit, with online qualifiers and seasonal finals.
what is the rocket league championship series | what is rlcs => The RLCS, or Rocket League Championship Series, is the main pro league for Rocket League, where teams of three play car soccer.
what is valorant champions | what is vct | valorant esports league => VCT, the Valorant Champions Tour, is Riot Games' pro league for Valorant, and the season ends with a world championship event called Champions.
what is a cs major | counter strike major meaning | what are majors in cs => A Major is one of the biggest Counter-Strike tournaments of the year, supported by Valve. Winning a Major is the top achievement in Counter-Strike esports.
what is evo | evo fighting game tournament | what is evo championship => Evo, short for Evolution Championship Series, is the biggest fighting game tournament, held every year in Las Vegas, with games like Street Fighter and Tekken.
is there competitive minecraft | minecraft esports | does minecraft have tournaments => Minecraft has no big official pro league, but there are popular community events like Minecraft Championship (MCC), plus speedrunning and PvP servers with competitive play.
what is mcc minecraft | what is minecraft championship | how does mcc work => Minecraft Championship, or MCC, is a community event run by Noxcrew where teams of popular Minecraft creators compete in minigames, and the top two teams meet in a final duel called Dodgebolt.
is there competitive smash bros | smash bros esports | super smash bros tournaments => Yes, Super Smash Bros has a big community tournament scene, especially for Super Smash Bros Melee and Super Smash Bros Ultimate, with events like Evo and Genesis.
are there pokemon tournaments | pokemon world championships | pokemon vgc meaning => Yes, The Pokemon Company runs the Pokemon World Championships every year, with divisions for the video games (called VGC), the trading card game, Pokemon GO and Pokemon Unite. There is a junior division for kids.
what is games done quick | what is gdq | agdq and sgdq => Games Done Quick is a charity marathon where speedrunners play games live for a week. Awesome Games Done Quick happens in winter and Summer Games Done Quick in summer, and they have raised millions for charity.
what is a world record in speedrunning | where do speedrunners post times | what is speedrun.com => Speedrunners post their times on speedrun.com, which keeps leaderboards for each game and category. A world record is the fastest verified time in a category.
what is a pro player's apm | what does apm mean | actions per minute => APM means actions per minute, how many clicks and key presses a player makes each minute. It is a common stat in strategy games like StarCraft.
what is a meta in esports | what is the pro meta | why do pros all pick the same thing => The meta is the set of strategies, characters or items that most players see as the strongest in a game. Pros often pick the same things because they win most often, until a game update changes the balance.
what is a patch in esports | why do updates change the meta => A patch is a game update that changes things like character strength or weapon damage. Pro teams have to adapt their strategies after each patch.
what is a coach in esports | do esports teams have coaches => Yes, most pro esports teams have coaches who plan strategies, review recordings of matches and help players improve, just like in regular sports.
what is a vod review | what does vod mean | why watch your own games => A VOD is a recorded video of a match, and a VOD review means watching it back to spot mistakes and learn. Pro teams do this a lot.
what is a sub in esports | what is a substitute player => A sub, or substitute, is an extra player on a team's roster who can play when a starter is sick, tired or needed for a different strategy.
what is an igl | what does igl mean | in game leader meaning => IGL means in-game leader, the player who makes calls during a match, like where to go and when to attack. It is common in team shooters like Counter-Strike and Valorant.
what does clutch mean in esports | what is a 1v3 clutch => A clutch is when a player wins a round when the odds are against them, like the last player alive on their team beating three enemies in a 1v3.
what is an ace in esports | ace in counter strike => In shooters like Valorant and Counter-Strike, an ace is when one player eliminates all five players on the enemy team in a single round.
what is a collegiate esports | can you get a scholarship for gaming | college esports => Many colleges now have esports teams, and some offer scholarships for players, similar to traditional sports scholarships.
what is a school esports club | esports in middle school | youth esports leagues => Many schools have esports clubs where students play on teams together. Youth leagues usually pick age-appropriate games and teach teamwork and sportsmanship.
what is twitch | what is twitch used for | is twitch for gaming => Twitch is a live streaming website owned by Amazon where people stream themselves playing games and viewers chat live. Most big esports tournaments are streamed there and on YouTube. You must be 13 or older to make an account.
what makes a good esports teammate | how to be a good teammate in games => Good teammates communicate clearly, stay calm after mistakes, give useful callouts instead of blame and keep trying when the team is losing.
what is gg wp | why say gg at the end => GG means good game and WP means well played. Saying it at the end of a match, win or lose, is a common way to show good sportsmanship.
what is the biggest esports game | most watched esports games => League of Legends, Counter-Strike, Valorant, Dota 2 and Mobile Legends are among the most watched esports games, with world finals drawing millions of viewers.
