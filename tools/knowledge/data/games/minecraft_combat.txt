# Minecraft combat, armor and survival mechanics, hand-written. Java Edition 1.21 unless it says otherwise.

# tag: minecraft.combat
how much damage does a diamond sword do | diamond sword damage | how strong is a diamond sword => A diamond sword does 7 damage (3.5 hearts) per full hit in Java Edition. Sharpness adds more.
how much damage does a netherite sword do | netherite sword damage | how strong is a netherite sword => A netherite sword does 8 damage (4 hearts) per full hit in Java Edition, the most of any sword.
how much damage does an iron sword do | iron sword damage | how strong is an iron sword => An iron sword does 6 damage (3 hearts) per full hit in Java Edition.
how much damage does a stone sword do | stone sword damage | wooden sword damage => A stone sword does 5 damage, and wooden and golden swords do 4, in Java Edition.
how much damage does a diamond axe do | axe damage minecraft | is an axe better than a sword => In Java Edition a diamond or iron axe does 9 damage and a netherite axe does 10, more than swords, but axes swing slower. Swords are faster and get Sweeping Edge.
how much damage does a trident do | trident damage | is a trident good => A trident does 9 damage in melee and 8 when thrown. With Loyalty it flies back to you.
how much damage does a mace do | mace base damage | how strong is the mace => A mace does 6 damage on its own, plus extra for every block you fall before the hit, so a long fall can one-shot most mobs.
how much damage does a bow do | bow damage minecraft | how strong is a fully charged bow => A fully charged bow arrow does about 6 damage, and up to about 10 on a critical hit. Power raises it.
how much damage does your fist do | punch damage minecraft | how strong is your hand => An empty hand does 1 damage (half a heart).
what is attack cooldown | why do my hits do no damage | how to time attacks in java => In Java Edition since 1.9, weapons need to recharge between swings. Wait until the bar under your crosshair fills for full damage.
how do you do a critical hit | crit minecraft | critical hit damage => Jump and hit a mob while you're falling. A critical hit does 50 percent more damage and shows star particles.
what is a sweep attack | how does sweeping work | sword sweep minecraft => In Java Edition, a fully charged sword hit while standing still or walking also hits mobs right next to the target.
how do you use a shield | how to block in minecraft | what does a shield block => Hold a shield in your off hand and hold right click to block. It stops most attacks from the front, including arrows and creeper blasts.
how do you disable a shield | axe vs shield | why did my shield stop working => Hitting a blocking player with an axe disables their shield for 5 seconds.
what is the best armor in minecraft | best armor set | netherite vs diamond armor => Netherite armor is the best. It has the same armor points as diamond but more toughness and durability, knockback resistance, and it floats in lava.
how many armor points does diamond armor give | diamond armor protection | full diamond armor bars => A full diamond set gives 20 armor points (10 armor icons): helmet 3, chestplate 8, leggings 6 and boots 3, plus 2 toughness per piece.
how many armor points does netherite armor give | netherite armor protection | netherite toughness => A full netherite set gives 20 armor points like diamond, with 3 toughness per piece and 10 percent knockback resistance per piece.
how many armor points does iron armor give | iron armor protection | full iron armor => A full iron set gives 15 armor points: helmet 2, chestplate 6, leggings 5 and boots 2.
how many armor points does chainmail give | chainmail armor protection | is chainmail better than iron => Chainmail gives 12 armor points, a bit less than iron's 15. You can't craft it, but you can buy it from armorer villagers.
how many armor points does gold armor give | is gold armor good | golden armor protection => Gold armor gives 11 armor points and breaks fast, but it gets good enchantments and piglins won't attack you while you wear a piece.
how many armor points does leather armor give | is leather armor good | leather armor protection => Leather armor gives 7 armor points. You can dye it, and leather boots let you walk on powder snow.
what is armor toughness | what does toughness do | toughness minecraft => Toughness lowers damage from very strong hits. Diamond has 2 toughness per piece and netherite has 3.
what is the max armor in minecraft | how many armor bars can you have | max armor points => The armor bar maxes out at 20 points, or 10 icons, which a full diamond or netherite set reaches.
how do you get chainmail armor | where to find chainmail | can you craft chainmail => You can't craft chainmail. Buy it from armorer villagers, find it in some chests, or get it from mobs wearing it.
how much health does a player have | how many hearts do you have in minecraft | max health minecraft => A player has 20 health, which is 10 hearts. Health Boost and absorption can add more.
how does fall damage work | how many blocks before fall damage | how far can you fall => You take 1 damage for every block you fall past 3 blocks. Landing in water, on hay bales, slime blocks, beds or powder snow helps.
how long can you hold your breath in minecraft | how long until you drown | drowning minecraft => You have about 15 seconds of air underwater. After the bubbles run out, you take 2 damage every second.
how much damage does lava do | can you survive lava | lava damage minecraft => Lava does 4 damage every half second and sets you on fire. A fire resistance potion makes you immune.
how does hunger work in minecraft | when do you heal naturally | hunger bar => You heal on your own when your hunger bar is 9 drumsticks or more. Below 3 drumsticks you can't sprint, and at 0 you start starving.
what is food saturation | hidden hunger bar | how does the saturation bar work => Saturation is a hidden bar that runs out before your hunger bar drops. Foods like golden carrots and steak give lots of saturation.
can you starve to death in minecraft | starving on easy | does hunger kill you => On Easy, starving stops at 5 hearts, on Normal it stops at half a heart, and on Hard it can kill you.
what is the void in minecraft | how far down is the void | void damage => The void is below the bottom of the world. In the Overworld you start taking heavy damage 64 blocks below Y -64, and nothing survives it.
how do you stop taking damage from fire | how to put out fire on yourself | on fire minecraft => Jump in water, stand in rain or drink a fire resistance potion.
what difficulty should i play on | difference between easy normal and hard | difficulty levels => Peaceful has no hostile mobs, Easy has weaker mobs, Normal is the standard game and Hard has stronger mobs, zombies breaking doors and starving can kill you.
what does peaceful mode do | is there hunger on peaceful | peaceful difficulty => On Peaceful, hostile mobs don't spawn, you heal by yourself and your hunger bar never drops.
what is hardcore difficulty | can you change difficulty in hardcore | hardcore rules => Hardcore is locked on Hard difficulty and you only get one life. When you die you can only spectate the world.
how do you get xp fast in minecraft | best way to level up | xp farming => Mine ores like coal and diamonds, smelt things, breed animals, trade with villagers and build a mob farm. Mob farms and the Ender Dragon give the most.
what happens to your xp when you die | do you lose levels when you die | dropped xp => You drop some of your XP, up to 100 points (about 7 levels), and the rest is lost unless keep inventory is on.
how do you survive a creeper explosion | how to stop creepers | creeper tips => Hit it and back up, or block with a shield. A creeper's blast does the most damage up close and less through armor, and Blast Protection helps.
how do you kill an enderman | enderman fighting tips | how to beat endermen => Stand under a 2 block high ceiling or in water. Endermen can't reach you there, and they can't be hit by arrows because they teleport away.
how do you fight a skeleton | how to beat skeletons | skeleton tips => Use a shield to block arrows, then get close and fight. They burn in sunlight, so you can wait for day.
how do you fight piglins | how to not get attacked by piglins | piglin tips => Wear at least one piece of gold armor and don't open their chests or mine gold near them. Piglin brutes still attack.
how do you beat a raid easily | raid defense | how to protect against raids => Fight from high ground, bring a shield, totems and a good bow, and kill evokers first. Iron golems help.
what is pvp in minecraft | how to get better at pvp | minecraft pvp tips => PvP means player versus player. Practice timing your hits, critical hits, strafing and using a shield, and keep healing items ready.
how does the totem of undying work | when does a totem save you | totem effects => Hold it in your main or off hand. When you'd die, it saves you with 1 heart and gives Regeneration, Absorption and Fire Resistance. It doesn't work against the void in older versions.
