how tall is the minecraft world | what is the build height limit | max build height => Since Java 1.18 the Overworld goes from Y -64 at the bottom to Y 320 at the top, which is 384 blocks tall.
how big is a minecraft world | how far can you go in minecraft | where is the world border => The world border is about 30 million blocks from the center in each direction, at X and Z 29,999,984. You'd need weeks of walking to get there.
what is a chunk in minecraft | how big is a chunk | what are chunks => A chunk is a 16 by 16 block column that goes from the bottom of the world to the top. The game loads and saves the world one chunk at a time.
how far can you see in minecraft | render distance chunks => Render distance is how many chunks around you the game shows. Higher looks better but needs a faster computer.
how high is the nether | nether height | nether roof y level => The Nether goes from Y 0 to Y 128, with bedrock at the bottom and a bedrock roof near the top.
can you go on top of the nether roof | nether roof | how to get above the nether bedrock => In Java Edition players can get on top of the bedrock roof with ender pearl tricks. Building there is allowed, and it's a fast and safe place to travel.
how many dimensions are in minecraft | what are the minecraft dimensions | overworld nether and end => There are 3 dimensions: the Overworld, the Nether and the End.
what is the overworld | where do you start in minecraft | overworld minecraft => The Overworld is the main dimension where you start, with grass, oceans, caves, villages and a day and night cycle.
what is the nether | what is in the nether | how dangerous is the nether => The Nether is a hot, dangerous dimension full of lava, with ghasts, piglins, blazes and fortresses. Water evaporates there and beds explode.
can you place water in the nether | why does water disappear in the nether | water in nether => No. Water evaporates instantly when you pour it in the Nether. Ice also melts into nothing there.
what is the end | what is in the end dimension | end dimension minecraft => The End is a dark dimension of floating end stone islands in the void. The Ender Dragon lives on the main island, and End cities are on the outer islands.
how long is a minecraft night | how long is day and night | minecraft day length => A full day is 20 minutes: about 10 minutes of day, 7 minutes of night and the rest sunrise and sunset.
does minecraft have moon phases | full moon minecraft => Yes, there are 8 moon phases. The brighter the moon, the more slimes spawn in swamps and the more likely mobs wear armor.
what is the max light level | how does light work in minecraft | light levels explained => Light goes from 0 to 15. The sun and blocks like torches light things up, and light drops by 1 for each block away from the source.
what makes mobs spawn | when do monsters spawn | hostile mob spawning rules => Most hostile mobs spawn in the dark, at block light level 0, on solid blocks, at least 24 blocks from a player.
what are the different villages | village types | what biomes have villages => Villages spawn in plains, deserts, savannas, taigas and snowy plains, and each type has its own building style.
what is a pillager outpost | where are pillager outposts | pillager outpost loot => A pillager outpost is a dark oak watchtower full of pillagers, sometimes with caged iron golems or allays nearby. Its chest can have crossbows and armor trims.
what is a desert temple | desert pyramid trap | how to loot a desert temple => A desert temple is a sandstone pyramid. Under the middle is a room with 4 chests and a pressure plate wired to TNT, so don't step on the plate.
what is a jungle temple | jungle temple traps | how to get jungle temple loot => A jungle temple is a mossy cobblestone building with tripwire arrow traps and a lever puzzle that opens a hidden chest.
what is a witch hut | where do witch huts spawn | swamp hut => A witch hut is a small wooden hut on stilts in swamps. Witches spawn there, and it usually has a black cat.
what is an igloo | igloo basement | igloo secret room => An igloo is a snow house in snowy biomes. Some have a hidden trapdoor under the carpet leading to a basement with a zombie villager, a cleric and a brewing stand.
what is a shipwreck | shipwreck loot | where are shipwrecks => Shipwrecks are sunken or beached wooden ships. Their chests have maps to buried treasure, iron, emeralds and food.
what are ocean ruins | ocean ruin loot | underwater ruins => Ocean ruins are stone or sandstone remains on the ocean floor guarded by drowned. Chests can have treasure maps, and warm ruins have suspicious sand.
what is a ruined portal | ruined portal loot | how to fix a ruined portal => A ruined portal is a broken nether portal with a chest full of gold items. Add the missing obsidian and light it to make it work.
what is a dungeon in minecraft | monster spawner room | dungeon loot => A dungeon is a small cobblestone room with a mob spawner and up to 2 chests. Loot can include saddles, music discs, name tags and enchanted golden apples.
what is an abandoned mineshaft | mineshaft loot | how to find a mineshaft => Mineshafts are old wooden tunnels underground with rails, cobwebs, cave spider spawners and chest minecarts. Badlands mineshafts are partly above ground.
what is a stronghold | what is in a stronghold | stronghold rooms => A stronghold is an underground stone brick maze with libraries, prison cells, a fountain room and the End portal room.
what are trail ruins | trail ruins loot | where are trail ruins => Trail ruins are buried terracotta ruins from Java 1.20, found in jungles, taigas and birch forests. Brush their suspicious gravel for sherds, armor trims and the Relic disc.
nether fortress loot | what spawns in a nether fortress => A nether fortress is a big nether brick building with blazes, wither skeletons and nether wart. Chests can have diamonds, saddles and horse armor.
what is an end ship | where is the elytra in the end ship | end ship loot => An end ship floats next to some End cities. It has an elytra in an item frame, a dragon head and 2 chests.
bastion types | bastion treasure room => A bastion remnant is a blackstone castle in the Nether full of piglins. The treasure room has a magma cube spawner, gold blocks and the best chest.
what is a desert well | desert well suspicious sand | desert well minecraft => A desert well is a tiny sandstone well with water in deserts. It can have suspicious sand that hides sherds.
what are fossils in minecraft | where do fossils spawn | fossil bones => Fossils are buried bone block skeletons in deserts and swamps. In the Nether's soul sand valleys they show up above ground.
what is an amethyst geode | where to find amethyst | how to farm amethyst => Amethyst geodes are underground balls of smooth basalt, calcite and amethyst. Budding amethyst grows crystals on its sides that you can harvest, but it can't be moved, even with Silk Touch.
what is a lava lake | where do lava lakes spawn | lava pools => Lava lakes spawn underground and sometimes on the surface. Deep down near Y -55 lava is very common, so be careful when mining for diamonds.
what is the deep dark | deep dark biome | how deep is the deep dark => The Deep Dark is the scariest biome, deep underground below Y 0, usually under mountains. It's covered in sculk and is home to ancient cities and the Warden.
what is a spawner | how do monster spawners work | can you pick up a spawner => A spawner spawns one type of mob when a player is within 16 blocks. You can't pick one up in Survival, even with Silk Touch, but torches around it stop most spawns.
how do you disable a mob spawner | stop a spawner | light up a spawner => Place torches on and around it, or break it with a pickaxe. Breaking it drops XP but not the spawner.
how do you grow wheat | how to farm wheat in minecraft | wheat seeds => Plant wheat seeds on farmland near water. Harvest the wheat when it turns yellow-brown, and you get more seeds back.
how do you grow carrots | how to farm carrots | where to find carrots => Plant carrots on farmland. Find your first ones in village farms or from zombies, and each harvest gives more carrots.
how do you grow potatoes | how to farm potatoes | poisonous potato => Plant potatoes on farmland. Harvesting can sometimes give a poisonous potato, which you shouldn't eat.
how do you grow pumpkins | how to grow melons | pumpkin and melon farm => Plant the seeds on farmland with an empty dirt or grass block next to it. The stem grows the pumpkin or melon onto that empty block.
how do you get a carved pumpkin | how to carve a pumpkin | jack o lantern minecraft => Use shears on a pumpkin to carve it and get 4 pumpkin seeds. Wear a carved pumpkin on your head and endermen won't get angry when you look at them.

how do you grow bamboo | bamboo farm | where to find bamboo => Plant bamboo on dirt, sand, gravel, grass or moss. It grows fast and tall. Find it in jungles, bamboo jungles or by fishing in jungles.
how do you grow cocoa beans | where do cocoa beans grow | cocoa farm => Place cocoa beans on the side of a jungle log. They grow into orange pods you can harvest for brown dye and cookies.

how do you grow chorus fruit | where can chorus flowers grow | chorus farm => Plant a chorus flower on end stone. It grows into a tall chorus plant that drops chorus fruit when broken.
how do you grow beetroot | beetroot seeds | what is beetroot for => Plant beetroot seeds on farmland. Beetroots make red dye and beetroot soup.
