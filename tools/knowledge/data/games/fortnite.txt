# Fortnite basics, hand-written.

# tag: games.fortnite
what is fortnite | what kind of game is fortnite | how does fortnite work => Fortnite is a free online game by Epic Games. Its most famous mode is Battle Royale, where up to 100 players drop onto an island and fight to be the last one standing.
who made fortnite | what company makes fortnite | fortnite developer => Fortnite is made by Epic Games, the company that also makes the Unreal Engine.
when did fortnite come out | how old is fortnite | when was fortnite battle royale released => Fortnite came out in July 2017 with Save the World, and Battle Royale was added in September 2017.
how many players are in a fortnite match | fortnite lobby size | how many people per fortnite game => A normal Battle Royale match has up to 100 players.
what is a victory royale | how do you win fortnite | what happens when you win fortnite => A Victory Royale is what you get for being the last player or team alive in Battle Royale.
what is the storm in fortnite | how does the storm work | why does the circle shrink => The storm is the closing circle that hurts you if you're outside it. It shrinks over the match to push everyone together.
what is the battle bus | how do you drop in fortnite | battle bus fortnite => The Battle Bus is the flying bus that carries everyone over the island at the start. Jump out when you want and glide down to land.
what are v bucks | how do you get v bucks | what can you buy with v bucks => V-Bucks are Fortnite's money. Buy them with real money, earn some from the Battle Pass, and spend them on skins, emotes and passes. Ask a parent before buying.
what is the fortnite battle pass | how does the battle pass work | is the battle pass worth it => The Battle Pass is a paid pass for each season. You level it up by playing to unlock skins and rewards, and it can give back enough V-Bucks to buy the next one.
what is the fortnite item shop | when does the item shop reset | fortnite shop => The Item Shop sells skins, emotes, pickaxes and gliders for V-Bucks. It changes every day.
what is fortnite crew | how does fortnite crew work | fortnite subscription => Fortnite Crew is a monthly subscription that gives a skin pack, V-Bucks every month and that season's Battle Pass.
what is zero build in fortnite | how does zero build work | fortnite no building mode => Zero Build is Battle Royale without building, so fights are all about aim, cover and movement.
how do you build in fortnite | building materials fortnite | wood brick metal fortnite => Gather wood, brick and metal by hitting things with your pickaxe, then build walls, floors, ramps and roofs. Wood builds fastest, and metal is the strongest once finished.
what are the weapon rarities in fortnite | fortnite rarity colors | what color is legendary in fortnite => From weakest to strongest: common is gray, uncommon is green, rare is blue, epic is purple and legendary is gold. Mythic items are special and also gold.
how much shield can you have in fortnite | fortnite health and shield | max hp in fortnite => You have 100 health and up to 100 shield.
how do shield potions work in fortnite | small shield vs big shield | how much shield does a mini give => A small shield potion gives 25 shield but only up to 50. A big shield potion gives 50 shield, up to 100.
what is lego fortnite | how does lego fortnite work | fortnite lego mode => LEGO Fortnite is a survival crafting mode from December 2023 where you build villages, gather resources and explore with LEGO versions of skins.
what is fortnite festival | fortnite music game | how does fortnite festival work => Fortnite Festival is a music game mode where you play songs like a band, similar to Rock Band.
what is rocket racing | fortnite racing mode | fortnite rocket racing => Rocket Racing is a Fortnite racing mode made with the creators of Rocket League.
what is save the world fortnite | is save the world free | fortnite pve mode => Save the World is Fortnite's paid player versus environment mode where you build forts and fight husks with friends.
what is fortnite creative | what is uefn | how do you make a fortnite map => Creative lets you build your own islands and games. UEFN, Unreal Editor for Fortnite, is a more powerful tool for making Fortnite islands on PC.
what is a fortnite chapter | fortnite chapters and seasons | how often do fortnite seasons change => Fortnite is split into chapters, each with several seasons. A new season usually brings a new Battle Pass and map changes, and a new chapter brings a whole new map.
what is fortnite og | fortnite og mode | what is the og map => Fortnite OG brings back the original Chapter 1 map and older weapons.
what platforms is fortnite on | can you play fortnite on ipad | where can you play fortnite => Fortnite is on PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch and Android. As of 2025 it's back on iPhone and iPad in some countries, like the US.
does fortnite have crossplay | can pc and console play fortnite together | fortnite cross platform => Yes. Fortnite has crossplay between PC, consoles and phones, and your account and skins carry over.
what age rating is fortnite | is fortnite ok for kids | fortnite esrb rating => Fortnite is rated T for Teen by the ESRB. The cartoon violence has no blood, and parents can set controls for chat and spending.
what are fortnite parental controls | how to limit fortnite spending | fortnite settings for parents => Epic's parental controls can limit voice and text chat, require a PIN to spend money, filter mature language and set play time reports.
who won the fortnite world cup | fortnite world cup 2019 | how much did bugha win => Bugha, Kyle Giersdorf, won the 2019 Fortnite World Cup solo final at 16 and took home 3 million dollars.
what is an emote in fortnite | fortnite dances | how do you use emotes => Emotes are dances and moves your character can do. Pick them in the locker and use them from the emote wheel in a match.
what is a fortnite skin | do skins make you better in fortnite | fortnite outfits => Skins, or outfits, change how your character looks. They're only cosmetic and don't make you stronger.
what is a pickaxe in fortnite | harvesting tool fortnite | what do you use the pickaxe for => The pickaxe, or harvesting tool, breaks objects to gather materials. Every skin has one, and you can swap it in the locker.
what is a sweat in fortnite | what does sweaty mean | fortnite sweats => A sweat is a player who tries really hard and builds super fast. People call very competitive lobbies sweaty.
what is box fighting | fortnite box fight | how to box fight => Box fighting is close-range fighting where players build boxes around themselves and edit walls to peek and shoot.
what does editing mean in fortnite | how to edit builds | fortnite edits => Editing lets you change a build piece, like making a window or door in a wall, so you can shoot through it or escape.
what is a 90 in fortnite | how to do 90s | fortnite 90s => A 90 is a building trick where you quickly build walls, ramps and floors while turning 90 degrees to climb high fast.
what is high ground in fortnite | why is high ground important | fortnite high ground => High ground means being above your enemy. It's easier to shoot down and harder for them to hit you.
fortnite tips for beginners | how to win more in fortnite => Land somewhere quieter, loot well, stay inside the storm circle, use cover, practice building or Zero Build aim in Creative, and watch how you lost.
what is arena mode in fortnite | fortnite ranked | how does ranked work in fortnite => Ranked mode puts you against players at your skill level and gives you a rank from Bronze up to Unreal based on your results.
what is unreal rank in fortnite | highest rank fortnite | fortnite ranks list => The ranks go Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Elite, Champion and Unreal. Unreal is the highest.
