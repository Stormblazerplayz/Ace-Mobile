# What Minecraft items and blocks are for, hand-written. Java Edition 1.21.

# tag: minecraft.items
what are ender pearls used for | how do ender pearls work => Throw an ender pearl to teleport where it lands, taking a little damage. They also make eyes of ender for finding the End portal.
what is blaze powder used for | what does blaze powder do | uses for blaze powder => Blaze powder fuels brewing stands, brews strength potions and makes eyes of ender, magma cream and fire charges.
what is a blaze rod used for | uses for blaze rods => Blaze rods make brewing stands and blaze powder, and end rods with popped chorus fruit.
what is a ghast tear used for | what does a ghast tear do | uses for ghast tears => Ghast tears brew potions of regeneration and make end crystals. In Java 1.21.6 and newer they also make dried ghast blocks.
what is a phantom membrane used for | what do phantom membranes do | uses for phantom membrane => Phantom membranes repair elytra in an anvil and brew potions of slow falling.
what is a nether star used for | what does a nether star do | uses for the nether star => A nether star is needed to craft a beacon. It can't be destroyed by explosions.
what is an ink sac used for | what do you use ink sacs for | uses for ink sac => Ink sacs make black dye and book and quills, and they can make sign text dark again after glow ink.
what are slimeballs used for | what do you use slime balls for | uses for slimeballs => Slimeballs make sticky pistons, leads, magma cream and slime blocks.
what is flint used for | what do you use flint for | uses for flint => Flint makes arrows, flint and steel, and fletching tables. You get it from breaking gravel.
what are feathers used for | what do you use feathers for | uses for feathers => Feathers make arrows and book and quills.
what is gunpowder used for | what do you use gunpowder for | uses for gunpowder => Gunpowder makes TNT, fire charges, firework rockets and firework stars, and turns potions into splash potions.
what is bone meal used for | what does bone meal do | how to use bone meal => Bone meal makes crops and saplings grow instantly, grows flowers and grass, and makes white dye.
what is a spider eye used for | what do you use spider eyes for | uses for spider eye => Spider eyes brew potions of poison and make fermented spider eyes. You can eat one, but it poisons you.
what is a rabbit's foot used for | what does a rabbit foot do | uses for rabbit's foot => A rabbit's foot brews potions of leaping.
what is a nautilus shell used for | uses for nautilus shell => Eight nautilus shells and a heart of the sea craft a conduit.
what are prismarine shards used for | what do you use prismarine for | uses for prismarine shards => Prismarine shards make prismarine blocks, prismarine bricks, dark prismarine and sea lanterns.
what is a shulker shell used for | uses for shulker shells => Two shulker shells and a chest make a shulker box, a portable chest that keeps its items when broken.
what is an echo shard used for | uses for echo shards => Eight echo shards and a compass make a recovery compass.
what is amethyst used for | what are amethyst shards for | uses for amethyst => Amethyst shards make spyglasses, tinted glass, calibrated sculk sensors and amethyst blocks, and can be an armor trim material.
what can you make with copper | uses for copper ingots => Copper makes lightning rods, spyglasses, brushes, copper blocks and copper bulbs. In Java 1.21.9 and newer it also makes copper tools and armor.
what are emeralds used for | what do you do with emeralds | uses for emeralds => Emeralds are the money for trading with villagers and wandering traders. They also make emerald blocks for beacons.
what is lapis lazuli used for | what do you use lapis for | uses for lapis lazuli => Lapis lazuli pays for enchanting at the enchanting table and makes blue dye.
what is redstone dust used for | what do you use redstone for | uses for redstone dust => Redstone carries power for circuits and makes repeaters, comparators, pistons, observers, clocks, compasses and more. It also makes potions last longer.
what is glowstone dust used for | what do you use glowstone for | uses for glowstone dust => Glowstone dust makes glowstone blocks and makes potions stronger. Glowstone blocks charge respawn anchors.
what is nether quartz used for | what do you use quartz for | uses for quartz => Nether quartz makes comparators, observers, daylight detectors and quartz blocks.
what is honeycomb used for | what do you use honeycomb for | uses for honeycomb => Honeycomb makes beehives, candles and honeycomb blocks, and waxes copper so it stops turning green.
what is a honey block used for | what does a honey block do | honey block uses => Honey blocks slow you down, stop fall damage when you land on them and let you slide down walls slowly. They stick to blocks when pushed by pistons.
what is a slime block used for | what does a slime block do | slime block uses => Slime blocks bounce you up, stop fall damage and stick to blocks when moved by pistons, which makes flying machines possible.
what is a target block used for | what does a target block do | target block uses => A target block sends a redstone signal when you hit it with an arrow or other projectile. Hitting closer to the middle makes a stronger signal.
what is a lightning rod used for | what does a lightning rod do | lightning rod uses => A lightning rod pulls in lightning strikes within 128 blocks so they don't start fires, and sends a redstone signal when hit.
what is scaffolding used for | what does scaffolding do | how to use scaffolding => Scaffolding lets you build tall things easily. Climb up by jumping inside it, sneak to climb down, and break the bottom to drop the whole tower.
what is a composter used for | what does a composter do | how to use a composter => Put plants, seeds and food in a composter to fill it. When full, it gives you bone meal. It's also the farmer villager's job block.
what is a lectern used for | what does a lectern do | lectern uses => Put a book and quill or written book on a lectern so others can read it. It's the librarian's job block and works with comparators.
what is a barrel used for | barrel vs chest | what does a barrel do => A barrel stores 27 stacks like a chest, but it opens even with a block on top. It's also the fisherman's job block.
what is a cauldron used for | what does a cauldron do | cauldron uses => A cauldron holds water, lava or powder snow. It can wash dye off leather armor, banners and shulker boxes, and fill glass bottles.
what is a bell used for | what does a bell do in minecraft | bell uses => Ringing a bell sends villagers inside and makes raiders nearby glow.
what is a lantern used for | lantern vs torch | what does a lantern do => A lantern gives light level 15 like a torch and can hang from the bottom of blocks or chains.
what is a campfire used for | what does a campfire do | campfire uses => Campfires cook food, make smoke signals, give light and calm bees when placed under a beehive. Walking on one burns you.
what is a soul campfire for | soul fire vs normal fire | blue fire minecraft => Soul campfires and soul torches burn blue, give less light and scare piglins away.
what is a grindstone used for | grindstone uses => A grindstone removes enchantments (except curses) and gives back some XP. It also repairs two of the same item. It's the weaponsmith's job block.
what is a smithing table used for | what does a smithing table do => A smithing table upgrades diamond gear to netherite and adds armor trims. It's the toolsmith's job block.
what is a loom used for | what does a loom do => A loom puts patterns on banners using dye and banner patterns. It's the shepherd's job block.
what is a stonecutter used for | what does a stonecutter do => A stonecutter turns stone blocks into stairs, slabs, walls and other shapes with no waste. It's the mason's job block.
what is a cartography table used for | what does a cartography table do | cartography table uses => A cartography table copies, zooms out and locks maps. It's the cartographer's job block.
what is a fletching table used for | what does a fletching table do | fletching table uses => A fletching table doesn't do anything for players yet. It's the job block that turns a villager into a fletcher.
what is a jukebox used for | what does a jukebox do | jukebox uses => A jukebox plays music discs and sends a redstone signal while it plays.
what is a note block used for | what does a note block do | note block uses => A note block plays a note when hit or powered. The block under it changes the instrument, so you can make songs with redstone.
what is a respawn anchor used for | why do you need a respawn anchor => A respawn anchor lets you set your spawn point in the Nether. Charge it with glowstone.
what is an ender chest used for | how does an ender chest work | ender chest uses => Every ender chest you open shows the same inventory, just for you, so you can reach your items from anywhere, even other dimensions.
what is a shulker box used for | how does a shulker box work | shulker box uses => A shulker box is a chest you can carry. Break it and it keeps everything inside, so you can carry 27 more stacks in one slot.
what is a hopper used for | how does a hopper work | hopper uses => A hopper pulls items from above and pushes them into the container it faces. A redstone signal stops it.
what is a dispenser used for | dispenser vs dropper | what does a dispenser do => A dispenser shoots or uses items when powered, like firing arrows, placing water or equipping armor. A dropper just drops the item out.
what is an observer used for | what does an observer do | observer uses => An observer sends a redstone pulse when the block in front of it changes, like a crop growing. It's the key part of many farms.
what is a daylight detector used for | what does a daylight sensor do | daylight sensor uses => A daylight detector sends a signal based on sunlight. Click it to switch it to night mode, which turns on lamps at night.
what is a tripwire hook used for | how do you make a tripwire | tripwire uses => Place two tripwire hooks facing each other and connect them with string. Walking through the string sends a redstone signal.
what is an item frame used for | what does an item frame do | item frame uses => An item frame shows an item on a wall. You can rotate the item, and comparators can read which way it's turned.
what is an armor stand used for | what does an armor stand do | armor stand uses => An armor stand displays armor and, in Java Edition, you can give it items with commands. Players use them to show off gear.
what is a flower pot used for | what can you put in a flower pot | flower pot uses => A flower pot holds a flower, sapling, mushroom, cactus, bamboo or fern as decoration.
what is a sculk sensor used for | what does a sculk sensor do | sculk sensor uses => A sculk sensor detects vibrations like footsteps and blocks breaking within 8 blocks and sends a redstone signal. Sneaking doesn't set it off.
what is a sculk catalyst used for | what does a sculk catalyst do | sculk catalyst uses => When a mob dies near a sculk catalyst, sculk spreads over nearby blocks. Mine it with Silk Touch to keep it.
what is a sculk shrieker used for | what does a sculk shrieker do | sculk shrieker uses => A sculk shrieker screams when a sculk sensor near it is triggered. In ancient cities it gives you Darkness and can summon the Warden.
what is crying obsidian used for | what does crying obsidian do | crying obsidian uses => Crying obsidian makes respawn anchors. It can't be used to build a working nether portal.
what is a magma block used for | what does a magma block do | magma block uses => A magma block hurts you if you stand on it without sneaking. Under water it makes a bubble column that pulls things down.
what is soul sand used for | what does soul sand do | soul sand uses => Soul sand slows you down, grows nether wart, makes upward bubble columns under water and is used to build the Wither.
what is blue ice used for | why is blue ice fast | blue ice uses => Blue ice is the slipperiest block, so boats go super fast on it. Players build ice roads in the Nether with it.
what is powder snow | how do you get powder snow | what does powder snow do => Powder snow is a trap block in snowy mountains that you sink into and slowly freeze. Leather boots let you walk on it. Collect it with a bucket.
what is a cobweb used for | what does a cobweb do | cobweb uses => Cobwebs slow down anything that walks into them. Break them with a sword or shears to get string, or the cobweb itself with shears.
what is a sponge used for | what does a sponge do | sponge uses => A sponge soaks up water around it when placed. It turns into a wet sponge, which you can dry in a furnace.
what is an end crystal used for | what do end crystals do | end crystal uses => End crystals heal the Ender Dragon. You can place 4 on the exit portal to respawn the dragon, and they explode when hit.
what is the dragon egg used for | is the dragon egg useful => The dragon egg has no use. It's a trophy you get from beating the Ender Dragon for the first time.
what is a bundle used for | how does a bundle work => A bundle holds a mix of different items in one inventory slot, up to 64 items worth of space.
what is a spyglass used for | what does a spyglass do => A spyglass lets you zoom in to see faraway things.
what is a goat horn used for | goat horn uses => A goat horn plays a loud sound that can be heard far away. There are 8 different sounds.
what is a trial key used for | what does a trial key do => A trial key opens a vault in a trial chamber for loot. You get keys by beating trial spawners.
what is a breeze rod used for | what are breeze rods for | uses for breeze rods => Breeze rods make wind charges and maces, and brew potions of wind charging.
what is a heavy core used for | heavy core uses => The heavy core is combined with a breeze rod to craft a mace.
what is a copper bulb | how does a copper bulb work | copper bulb redstone => A copper bulb is a light that toggles on or off each time it gets a redstone pulse. Older, more oxidized bulbs give less light.
what is the crafter used for | crafter redstone | what can the crafter do => The crafter crafts the recipe in its grid when it gets a redstone pulse, so you can build automatic crafting machines.
what is a decorated pot | what are decorated pots for | decorated pot uses => A decorated pot is a decoration made from bricks or pottery sherds. You can store one stack of items in it in newer versions.
what is suspicious sand | what is suspicious gravel | how to brush suspicious blocks => Suspicious sand and gravel hide items. Brush them to get things like pottery sherds, sniffer eggs and armor trims. Mining them breaks the item.
how does a recovery compass work | where did i die minecraft => A recovery compass points to where you last died, so you can find your items.
what is a lodestone used for | what does a lodestone do => A lodestone makes a compass point to it when you use the compass on it, even in the Nether.
what is a carrot on a stick used for | how do you steer a pig | carrot on a stick uses => Hold a carrot on a stick while riding a saddled pig to steer it. Use it to give the pig a speed boost.
what is a clock used for | what does a clock do in minecraft | clock uses => A clock shows the position of the sun and moon so you know the time of day, even underground.
what is a compass used for | where does a compass point | compass uses => A compass points to the world spawn point. In the Nether and End it spins around.
what is a map used for | how do maps work | map uses => Maps draw the area around you as you explore. You can zoom them out, copy them and hang them in item frames.
what is a fishing rod used for | can you pull mobs with a fishing rod => Fishing rods catch fish, junk and treasure. You can also hook mobs and players to pull them toward you.
what are snowballs used for | do snowballs do damage | snowball uses => Snowballs do no damage to most mobs, but they hurt blazes and push things back. They make snow blocks too.
what are eggs used for | what can you do with eggs | egg uses => Eggs make cakes and pumpkin pies, and throwing them can hatch baby chickens.
what is a bottle o' enchanting | what does a bottle o enchanting do | xp bottle => A bottle o' enchanting breaks into XP orbs when thrown. Buy them from cleric villagers or find them in chests.
what is a painting used for | how do you choose a painting | how to place a painting => Paintings decorate walls. In Java 1.21 and newer you can pick which painting to place from a menu in Creative.
what is an end rod used for | what does an end rod do | end rod uses => An end rod is a light source with light level 14 that you can place in any direction.
what is a sea pickle | how much light does a sea pickle give | sea pickle uses => Sea pickles live on coral and give light underwater. Up to 4 can sit on one block, giving more light the more there are.
what is a froglight | how do you get froglights | froglight colors => A froglight is a bright light block you get when a frog eats a small magma cube. Its color depends on which frog ate it.
what is a shroomlight | where do you find shroomlights | shroomlight uses => Shroomlights are light blocks found in huge crimson and warped fungi in the Nether. They give light level 15.
what is glow ink sac used for | how to make glowing signs | glow ink uses => Use a glow ink sac on a sign to make the text glow, or craft it with an item frame for a glow item frame.
what is a happy ghast | how do you get a happy ghast | how to ride a ghast => In Java 1.21.6 and newer, place a dried ghast block in water until a ghastling hatches, then let it grow into a happy ghast. Put a harness on it and up to 4 players can fly on it.
what is a dried ghast | how do you hatch a dried ghast | dried ghast in water => A dried ghast is a block added in Java 1.21.6. Put it in water and it rehydrates over about 20 minutes into a baby ghast called a ghastling.
what is a harness in minecraft | how do you make a harness | harness for happy ghast => A harness goes on a happy ghast so you can ride it. Craft it from 2 leather, 2 glass and 1 wool of any color in Java 1.21.6 and newer.
what is the creaking | what is a creaking heart | how to kill a creaking => The creaking is a mob from the pale garden that only moves when you aren't looking at it. Break the creaking heart in a pale oak log to kill it.
what is resin used for | how do you get resin | resin clumps => Resin clumps come from hitting a creaking, which grows resin on pale oak logs near its heart. Resin makes resin bricks and is an armor trim material.
what is a firefly bush | does the firefly bush glow | firefly bush light => A firefly bush is a plant added in Java 1.21.5 that shows glowing fireflies at night.
what is the pale garden | where is the pale garden | pale garden biome => The pale garden is a gray, quiet biome added in Java 1.21.4, found near dark forests, with pale oak trees and creakings.
