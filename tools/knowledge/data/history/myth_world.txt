what is norse mythology | who are the norse gods => Norse mythology is the set of stories and beliefs of the Vikings and other early Scandinavians. Its gods include Odin, Thor, Frigg, Loki, Freyja and Tyr.
who is odin | what is odin the god of => Odin was the chief of the Norse gods, god of wisdom, war, poetry and death. He gave up an eye for wisdom, rode an eight-legged horse named Sleipnir and had two ravens.
who is thor | what is thor the god of | what is thors hammer called => Thor was the Norse god of thunder and lightning, the strongest of the gods and protector of humans. His hammer is called Mjölnir. Thursday is named after him.
who is loki | is loki a god => Loki was a Norse trickster god, a shape-shifter who caused trouble and sometimes helped the gods. In the end he was said to fight against them at Ragnarök.
who is frigg | who is odins wife => Frigg was Odin's wife and the Norse goddess of marriage and motherhood. Friday may be named after her or the similar goddess Freyja.
who is freyja | what is freyja famous for => Freyja was the Norse goddess of love, beauty, war and magic. She rode a chariot pulled by two cats.
who is tyr | why is tuesday called tuesday => Tyr was the Norse god of war and justice who lost his hand to the giant wolf Fenrir. Tuesday is named after him.
who is baldur | how did baldur die => Baldur was a Norse god of light loved by everyone. Loki tricked a blind god into killing him with a dart made of mistletoe, the one thing that could hurt him.
who is heimdall | what is heimdall famous for => Heimdall was the watchman of the Norse gods who guarded the rainbow bridge Bifröst. He could see and hear incredibly far and would blow his horn when Ragnarök began.
who is hel in norse mythology | what is hel in norse mythology famous for => Hel was a daughter of Loki who ruled Helheim, a realm where many of the dead went. She was described as half living and half dead.
what is valhalla | explain valhalla => Valhalla was Odin's great hall where warriors who died bravely in battle went. There they feasted and trained for the final battle of Ragnarök.
who are the valkyries | what did valkyries do => Valkyries were female spirits who served Odin. They chose which warriors would die in battle and carried the bravest to Valhalla.
what is asgard | explain asgard => Asgard was the home of the Aesir gods in Norse mythology, connected to Midgard, the world of humans, by the rainbow bridge Bifröst.
what is yggdrasil | norse tree connecting nine worlds => Yggdrasil was a giant ash tree in Norse mythology whose branches and roots connected the nine worlds, including Asgard, Midgard and Helheim.
what are the nine realms | what are the nine worlds in norse mythology => The nine worlds of Norse myth included Asgard (gods), Midgard (humans), Jotunheim (giants), Alfheim (elves), Vanaheim, Niflheim, Muspelheim, Svartalfheim and Helheim, all on the tree Yggdrasil.
what is ragnarok | explain ragnarok => Ragnarök was the Norse end of the world, a great battle where gods like Odin and Thor would die fighting giants and monsters. Afterward a new world would rise from the sea.
what is midgard | explain midgard => Midgard, meaning "middle enclosure," was the world of humans in Norse myth, surrounded by a sea where the giant serpent Jörmungandr lived.
who is fenrir | what is fenrir famous for => Fenrir was a giant wolf, a son of Loki. The gods chained him with a magic ribbon, and at Ragnarök he was said to break free and swallow Odin.
what is jormungandr | what is the midgard serpent => Jörmungandr was a sea serpent so huge it circled the whole human world and bit its own tail. It was a child of Loki and Thor's great enemy.
who are the jotnar | what are frost giants => The jötnar were giants in Norse mythology, often enemies of the gods, living in Jotunheim. Some were frost giants and some fire giants.
which days of the week are named after norse gods | weekdays named for viking gods | is thursday named after thor => Tuesday is named for Tyr, Wednesday for Woden (Odin), Thursday for Thor, and Friday for Frigg or Freyja.
what are the eddas | where do norse myths come from => Most Norse myths come from two books written in Iceland in the 1200s: the Poetic Edda, a collection of old poems, and the Prose Edda, written by Snorri Sturluson.
what are runes | explain runes => Runes are the letters of old Germanic alphabets, used by the Vikings and others to carve messages in stone, wood and metal. The Viking-age alphabet, the Younger Futhark, had 16 runes.
# tag: culture.myth.egypt
who were the egyptian gods | how many gods did egypt have => Ancient Egyptians worshipped hundreds of gods and goddesses, often shown with animal heads. Important ones include Ra, Osiris, Isis, Horus, Anubis, Thoth and Set.
who is ra | what is ra the god of => Ra was the Egyptian sun god, often shown with a falcon head and a sun disk. Egyptians believed he sailed across the sky in a boat each day and through the underworld each night.
who is osiris | what is osiris famous for => Osiris was the Egyptian god of the afterlife and the dead. He was killed by his brother Set, brought back by his wife Isis, and became ruler of the underworld.
who is isis the egyptian goddess | what is isis the egyptian goddess famous for => Isis was an Egyptian goddess of magic, healing and motherhood. She brought her husband Osiris back to life and protected her son Horus.
who is horus | what is the eye of horus => Horus was the falcon-headed Egyptian god of the sky and kingship. He lost an eye fighting Set, and the Eye of Horus became a symbol of protection and healing.
who is anubis | why does anubis have a jackal head => Anubis was the jackal-headed Egyptian god of mummification and the dead. He guided souls and weighed their hearts. Jackals were seen near cemeteries, so he was linked to protecting graves.
who is set the egyptian god | what is set the egyptian god famous for => Set was the Egyptian god of storms, deserts and chaos. He killed his brother Osiris and fought his nephew Horus for the throne.
who is thoth | what is thoth famous for => Thoth was the Egyptian god of writing, wisdom and the moon, usually shown with the head of an ibis bird. He was said to have invented hieroglyphs.
who is bastet | what is bastet famous for => Bastet was the Egyptian goddess of home, cats and protection, shown as a cat or a woman with a cat's head.
who is sekhmet | what is sekhmet famous for => Sekhmet was a fierce Egyptian goddess of war and healing with the head of a lioness.
who is hathor | what is hathor famous for => Hathor was an Egyptian goddess of love, music, joy and motherhood, often shown as a cow or a woman with cow horns and a sun disk.
who is maat | what is maat famous for => Maat was the Egyptian goddess of truth, balance and order, shown with an ostrich feather on her head. Her feather was used to weigh the hearts of the dead.
who is amun | who is amun ra => Amun was a powerful Egyptian god of the city of Thebes. He was later combined with the sun god Ra as Amun-Ra, king of the gods.
who is aten | what is aten famous for => Aten was the sun disk god that the pharaoh Akhenaten made the main god of Egypt around 1350 BC. After Akhenaten died, Egypt went back to its old gods.
who is sobek | what is sobek famous for => Sobek was the Egyptian crocodile god of the Nile, linked to power, protection and fertility.
who is ptah | what is ptah famous for => Ptah was an Egyptian creator god and patron of craftsmen and builders, worshipped in the city of Memphis.
what is a scarab | why did egyptians like scarab beetles => The scarab is a dung beetle that rolls balls of dung. Egyptians linked it to the sun god pushing the sun across the sky, so scarab amulets stood for rebirth and protection.
what is an ankh | explain an ankh => The ankh is an Egyptian symbol shaped like a cross with a loop on top. It stood for life, and gods are often shown holding it.
what did egyptians believe about the afterlife | ancient egyptian afterlife beliefs => Egyptians believed that after death the soul traveled through the underworld and was judged. If it passed, it lived forever in a paradise called the Field of Reeds, so preserving the body was important.
