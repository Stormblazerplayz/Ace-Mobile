# tag: history.daily-life
how did people tell time before clocks | how did ancient people know the time => People used the Sun and shadows, sundials, water clocks that dripped at a steady rate, marked candles and hourglasses. Mechanical clocks appeared in European towers in the late 1200s.
how did people keep food cold before fridges | what did people use before refrigerators => People stored food in cool cellars, springhouses and icehouses. In the 1800s, many homes had an icebox cooled by blocks of ice delivered by an iceman. Electric fridges spread in the 1920s and 1930s.
how did people keep food from going bad long ago | how did people preserve food in the past => People dried, salted, smoked and pickled food, and later canned it. Salt pork, dried fruit, jerky and pickles could last for months.
how did people get water before pipes | how did people get water in the old days => People carried water from rivers, springs and wells. Ancient Romans built aqueducts to bring water to cities, but most homes didn't have running water until the 1900s.
when did people start using toilets | how old are toilets => Ancient cities like Mohenjo-daro in the Indus Valley had toilets with drains over 4,000 years ago. Modern flush toilets became common in the late 1800s.
what did people use before toilet paper | how did people wipe before toilet paper => People used leaves, moss, corncobs, rags, sponges on sticks in ancient Rome, and old newspapers and catalogs. Toilet paper rolls became common in the late 1800s.
when did people start brushing their teeth | what did people use before toothbrushes => People chewed on twigs to clean their teeth thousands of years ago. China had bristle toothbrushes by around the 1400s, and toothpaste in tubes came in the late 1800s.
how did people take baths long ago | did people take baths in the middle ages => Ancient Romans loved public bathhouses. In medieval Europe many towns had bathhouses too, but most people washed with a basin of water. Bathtubs with running water became common in the 1900s.
how did people light their homes before electricity | what did people use before light bulbs => People used fires, candles and oil lamps. In the 1800s, whale oil, kerosene lamps and gas lights were common until electric lights spread in the early 1900s.
how did people stay warm before heaters | how did people heat houses long ago => People burned wood or coal in fireplaces and stoves, wore layers of wool, and used warming pans filled with hot coals to heat their beds.
how did people cook before stoves | cooking in the old days => People cooked over open fires and in fireplaces using pots hung from hooks, and baked in brick or clay ovens. Iron wood stoves became common in the 1800s.
how did people wash clothes before washing machines | laundry long ago => People scrubbed clothes by hand on washboards, boiled them in big pots and wrung them out by hand. It could take a whole day, often called wash day.
how did people communicate before phones | how did people send messages long ago => People sent letters carried by messengers, horse riders and ships. Signal fires, drums and flags sent quick signals. The telegraph in the 1840s sent messages over wires almost instantly.
how long did it take for a letter to cross the ocean | how long did mail take in the 1700s => In the 1700s, a letter between Europe and America took about 6 weeks or more by sailing ship. Steamships in the mid 1800s cut it to about 10 to 14 days.
how did people travel before cars | what did people use to get around long ago => People walked, rode horses, donkeys or camels, and traveled in wagons, carriages and boats. Steam trains in the 1800s made long trips much faster.
how fast could a stagecoach go | how long did travel take by stagecoach => A stagecoach usually traveled about 5 to 8 miles an hour, changing horses every 10 to 15 miles. A trip of a few hundred miles could take days.
how long did it take pioneers to travel the oregon trail | how far did wagons go each day => It took pioneers about four to six months to travel the roughly 2,000 mile Oregon Trail. Wagons usually covered about 15 to 20 miles a day, and most people walked beside them.
what did pioneers eat | what food did pioneers take on the trail => Pioneers on the trail ate bacon, beans, flour biscuits, dried fruit, coffee and hardtack, plus any game they could hunt or berries they found.
what did kids do for fun long ago | what toys did kids have in the 1800s => Kids played with marbles, tops, hoops, jacks, dolls, kites and wooden toys, and played games like tag, hopscotch and leapfrog.
what toys did roman children play with | what toys did kids have in ancient times => Children in ancient Greece and Rome played with dolls, small carts, spinning tops, balls, yo-yo-like toys and knucklebones, a game like jacks.
did kids go to school long ago | what was school like in the 1800s => In the 1800s, many American kids went to one-room schoolhouses where one teacher taught all ages together. They wrote on small slates, and many children left school early to work.
what was school like in colonial times | explain school like in colonial times => Colonial children learned to read from hornbooks and the New England Primer. Many learned at home or in small dame schools, and girls often got less schooling than boys.
did kids have to work long ago | did children work in factories => Before laws protected them, many children worked long hours on farms, in factories, mines and mills, some as young as 5 or 6. US federal child labor laws came in 1938.
what did people eat in the middle ages | what did peasants eat => Medieval peasants mostly ate bread, porridge and a thick vegetable stew called pottage, with some cheese, eggs and beans. Meat was more often for the rich.
what did people drink in the middle ages | did people drink water in the middle ages => Medieval people drank water when it was clean, but ale was very common, including weak "small beer" for everyday drinking.
what did vikings eat | viking food => Vikings ate meat, fish, porridge, bread, vegetables, berries and dairy foods like milk and cheese. They preserved food by drying, salting and smoking it.
what did people wear in the middle ages | medieval clothes => Most medieval people wore wool clothing, like long tunics for men and long dresses for women. Rich people wore silk, fur and bright dyes, and laws sometimes said who could wear what.
what did people wear in colonial times | colonial clothing => Colonial men wore breeches, stockings, shirts and coats, and women wore long dresses with aprons and caps. Many clothes were homemade from wool or linen.
why did people wear wigs in the 1700s | powdered wigs history => Wigs became fashionable after French King Louis XIII and Louis XIV wore them. They were a sign of wealth, and some people also wore them to hide hair loss from illness.
how long did people live long ago | what was the life expectancy in the past => Average life expectancy was often only 30 to 40 years in the past, mainly because so many babies and children died. People who survived childhood often lived into their 50s, 60s or older.
what was medicine like long ago | how did doctors treat people in the past => Before modern medicine, doctors used herbs, bloodletting and remedies that often didn't work. Germ theory in the 1860s and later vaccines and antibiotics greatly improved health.
what was bloodletting | explain bloodletting => Bloodletting was an old treatment where doctors removed blood, sometimes using leeches, believing it balanced the body. It was common for over 2,000 years but usually did more harm than good.
how did people get news long ago | how did people hear news before tv => Town criers called out news in the streets. Later, newspapers spread news, and from the 1920s families gathered around the radio. TV news became common in the 1950s.
what did people do before tv | what did families do for fun before television => Families read books aloud, played music and board games, told stories, went to dances and fairs, and from the 1920s listened to radio shows together.
when did people start using money | what did people use before money => Before money, people traded goods directly, which is called bartering. Some early societies used shells, salt or grain, and the first coins were made in Lydia, in what is now Turkey, around 600 BC.
how did people shop long ago | when did supermarkets start => People bought food and goods at markets, from peddlers and at general stores. Supermarkets where you pick items yourself began with Piggly Wiggly in 1916.
when did people start having last names | where do last names come from => Last names became common in Europe from about the 1100s to 1400s. Many came from jobs, like Smith or Baker, from places, from fathers' names, like Johnson, or from nicknames.
what were knights' lives like | what did knights do all day => Knights trained for years, starting as pages at about 7 and squires at about 14. They practiced fighting, rode horses, served their lord, and joined tournaments in peacetime.
what was a castle like inside | what was it like living in a castle => Castles were often cold, damp and smoky. The great hall was used for meals and gatherings, and the lord's family slept in private rooms while servants slept wherever they could.
what was life like for a peasant | explain life like for a peasant => Medieval peasants worked hard farming their lord's land and their own strips, from sunrise to sunset. They lived in small one-room homes, sometimes sharing them with animals in winter.
how did people make light at night in the middle ages | medieval candles => Medieval people used candles made of tallow, which is animal fat, or more expensive beeswax, plus rushlights made from dried rushes dipped in fat.
what was a chamber pot | explain a chamber pot => A chamber pot was a bowl kept under the bed to use as a toilet at night, emptied the next morning. They were used for centuries before indoor plumbing.
what was an outhouse | explain an outhouse => An outhouse was a small shed outside a home with a toilet seat over a hole in the ground. Many rural American homes used them until the mid 1900s.
how did people do homework before computers | school before laptops => Students wrote with pencils, ink pens and paper, used books from libraries and encyclopedias, and did math by hand or with a slide rule.
what was a slide rule | explain a slide rule => A slide rule was a ruler-like tool with sliding scales used to multiply, divide and do other math quickly. Engineers used them, even for the Apollo missions, until pocket calculators appeared in the 1970s.
what did people use before calculators | math tools before calculators => People did math by hand, with an abacus, with printed tables, with slide rules and with mechanical adding machines.
how did people listen to music before radios | music before radio => People played instruments and sang at home, heard live concerts, and from 1877 could play recordings on phonographs. Radio broadcasts began in the 1920s.
what was a phonograph | what was a record player => The phonograph, invented by Thomas Edison in 1877, recorded and played sound from cylinders. Later record players played flat vinyl discs, which are popular again today.
what were vhs tapes | what did people use before streaming => Before streaming, people watched movies on VHS videotapes from the late 1970s, then DVDs from the late 1990s. Many people rented them from video stores.
what were floppy disks | explain floppy disks => Floppy disks were thin magnetic disks used to store computer files from the 1970s to the 2000s. A common size held only 1.44 megabytes. The save icon in many apps still looks like one.
what was a pay phone | explain a pay phone => A pay phone was a public telephone you paid coins to use, found on streets and in stores. They were common before cell phones and are now rare.
what was a pager | what was a beeper => A pager, or beeper, was a small device popular in the 1980s and 1990s that beeped and showed a phone number so you knew to call someone back.
what was dial up internet | explain dial up internet => Dial-up internet connected computers through phone lines with a screeching modem sound. It was very slow, and you couldn't use the phone while online. It was common in the 1990s.
what was a walkman | explain a walkman => The Walkman was a portable cassette tape player made by Sony in 1979 that let people listen to music with headphones on the go.
