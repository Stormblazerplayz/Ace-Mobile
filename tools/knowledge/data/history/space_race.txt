# tag: history.space
who was the first american in space | who was alan shepard => Alan Shepard became the first American in space on May 5, 1961, with a 15 minute flight in Freedom 7. Ten years later he walked on the Moon and hit golf balls there.
who was the first american to orbit the earth | who was john glenn => John Glenn was the first American to orbit Earth, circling it three times in Friendship 7 on February 20, 1962. In 1998, at 77, he flew on the Space Shuttle.
who was the first man on the moon | first person to walk on the moon => Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the Moon, on July 20, 1969, during Apollo 11. Buzz Aldrin joined him about 20 minutes later.
what did neil armstrong say on the moon | one small step for man => When he stepped onto the Moon, Neil Armstrong said, "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."
who was on apollo 11 | who were the apollo 11 astronauts => Apollo 11's crew was Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins. Collins stayed in orbit in the command module Columbia while the other two landed in the lunar module Eagle.
why did we stop going to the moon | why did the apollo program end => The Apollo program ended in 1972 mainly because it was very expensive and the US had already won the race, so Congress cut NASA's budget and moved on to other projects.
who was the first american woman in space | who was sally ride => Sally Ride became the first American woman in space on the Space Shuttle Challenger in June 1983. She was also a physicist and later wrote science books for kids.
who was the first african american in space | who was guion bluford => Guion Bluford became the first African American in space in 1983. Mae Jemison became the first African American woman in space in 1992.
who was the first person to do a spacewalk | who was alexei leonov => Soviet cosmonaut Alexei Leonov made the first spacewalk on March 18, 1965, floating outside his spacecraft for about 12 minutes. His suit puffed up and he had trouble getting back in.
who was the first american to spacewalk | ed white spacewalk => Ed White made the first American spacewalk during Gemini 4 in June 1965.
who was katherine johnson | who were the hidden figures => Katherine Johnson was a NASA mathematician whose calculations helped send John Glenn into orbit and Apollo 11 to the Moon. She and other Black women computers were shown in the movie Hidden Figures.
who was wernher von braun | what is wernher von braun famous for => Wernher von Braun was a German rocket engineer who built rockets for Nazi Germany and after the war led design of the Saturn V rocket for NASA.
who was sergei korolev | what is sergei korolev famous for => Sergei Korolev was the secret chief designer of the Soviet space program, behind Sputnik and Yuri Gagarin's flight. His name was kept secret until he died in 1966.
when was the international space station built | how long has the iss had people on it => Building of the International Space Station began in 1998, and astronauts have lived aboard it continuously since November 2, 2000.
what was voyager | when did voyager launch | where is voyager now => Voyager 1 and 2 were launched in 1977 to explore the outer planets. Voyager 1 entered interstellar space in 2012 and is the farthest human-made object from Earth.
who was the first person to fly to space privately | first space tourist => Dennis Tito, an American businessman, became the first paying space tourist when he flew to the International Space Station on a Russian Soyuz in 2001.
when did spacex first send astronauts | first private company to send astronauts => SpaceX's Crew Dragon became the first private spacecraft to carry astronauts to orbit, flying Bob Behnken and Doug Hurley to the International Space Station in May 2020.
who was the first chinese astronaut | yang liwei => Yang Liwei became China's first astronaut in space in October 2003, making China the third country to launch people into space on its own.
who was the youngest person in space | who was gherman titov => Soviet cosmonaut Gherman Titov was 25 when he flew in August 1961, the youngest person to orbit Earth, and he was the first to spend a full day in space.
