who made the statue of david | who sculpted the statue of david | when was the statue of david made => The statue of David was made by Michelangelo in 1501 to 1504. It's a marble statue about 5.2 meters (17 feet) tall in the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence, Italy.
who made the thinker | who sculpted the thinker | when was the thinker made => The Thinker was made by Auguste Rodin in 1880 as part of a bigger work, then enlarged later. Many bronze casts exist. A famous one is at the Musée Rodin in Paris.
who made the pieta | who sculpted the pieta | when was the pieta made => The Pietà was made by Michelangelo in 1498 and 1499. It shows Mary holding Jesus after the crucifixion. It's in St. Peter's Basilica in Vatican City.
who made venus de milo | who sculpted venus de milo | when was venus de milo made => The Venus de Milo was made by an unknown Greek sculptor, possibly Alexandros of Antioch, in about 150 to 125 BC. It was found on the Greek island of Milos in 1820, missing its arms. It's in the Louvre.
who made winged victory of samothrace | who sculpted winged victory of samothrace | when was winged victory of samothrace made => The Winged Victory of Samothrace was made by an unknown Greek sculptor in about 200 to 190 BC. It shows the goddess Nike and stands at the top of a staircase in the Louvre.
who made the discus thrower | who sculpted the discus thrower | when was the discus thrower made => The Discus Thrower was made by the Greek sculptor Myron in about 450 BC. The original bronze is lost, but Roman marble copies survive.
who made the lincoln statue | who sculpted the lincoln statue | when was the lincoln statue made | who made the statue in the lincoln memorial => The Lincoln Memorial statue was made by Daniel Chester French, carved by the Piccirilli brothers, in 1920. The seated Lincoln is about 5.8 meters (19 feet) tall.
