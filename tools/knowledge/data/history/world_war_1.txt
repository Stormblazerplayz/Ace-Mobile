# tag: history.ww1
what was world war 1 | what was ww1 | what was the great war => World War 1 was a huge war from 1914 to 1918 between the Allies, led by Britain, France, Russia and later the US, and the Central Powers, led by Germany and Austria-Hungary. It was called the Great War.
what caused world war 1 | why did ww1 start | main causes of ww1 => The main causes were militarism, alliances, imperialism and nationalism. The spark was the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary in June 1914.
who was franz ferdinand | who killed franz ferdinand => Archduke Franz Ferdinand, heir to Austria-Hungary's throne, was shot with his wife Sophie in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914, by Gavrilo Princip, a young Bosnian Serb. This set off World War 1.
who were the allies in ww1 | which countries were on the allied side in ww1 => The main Allies were France, Britain, Russia, Italy (from 1915), Japan, Serbia, Belgium and the United States (from 1917).
who were the central powers in ww1 | tell me about the central powers in ww1 => The Central Powers were Germany, Austria-Hungary, the Ottoman Empire and Bulgaria.
who won ww1 | who won world war 1 => The Allies won World War 1. Germany agreed to an armistice on November 11, 1918, and the Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919.
how many people died in ww1 | ww1 death toll => About 9 to 10 million soldiers and millions of civilians died in World War 1, perhaps around 20 million deaths in all.
what is armistice day | why is veterans day november 11 | what is remembrance day => The fighting in World War 1 stopped at 11 a.m. on November 11, 1918, the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month. That date became Armistice Day, now Veterans Day in the US and Remembrance Day in many countries.
why do people wear poppies | what do poppies mean for remembrance => Red poppies grew in the battlefields of Belgium and France and were made famous by the 1915 poem "In Flanders Fields" by John McCrae. People wear them to remember those who died in war.
what was trench warfare | what were the trenches like in ww1 => On the Western Front, armies dug long lines of trenches facing each other. Soldiers lived in mud, rats and lice, and attacks across "no man's land" usually failed with huge losses.
what is no mans land | explain no mans land => No man's land was the area between enemy trenches in World War 1, full of barbed wire, craters and mud. Crossing it under machine gun fire was deadly.
what was the western front | what was the eastern front in ww1 => The Western Front was the line of trenches through Belgium and France where Germany fought France, Britain and later the US. The Eastern Front was where Germany and Austria-Hungary fought Russia.
what was the battle of the somme | how many died on the first day of the somme => The Battle of the Somme in France lasted from July to November 1916, with over a million casualties. On its first day, July 1, 1916, the British army had about 57,000 casualties, nearly 20,000 killed.
what was the battle of verdun | explain the battle of verdun => The Battle of Verdun in France lasted most of 1916, about 10 months, between France and Germany. Around 700,000 soldiers were killed, wounded or missing. France held on.
what was gallipoli | what happened at gallipoli => Gallipoli was a 1915 Allied attempt to capture the Dardanelles strait from the Ottoman Empire. It failed after eight months. Australia and New Zealand remember it on Anzac Day, April 25.
what was the christmas truce | explain the christmas truce => On Christmas 1914, soldiers along parts of the Western Front stopped fighting, met in no man's land, swapped gifts and sang carols. Some even played football. It didn't happen again on that scale.
what new weapons were used in ww1 | ww1 technology => World War 1 brought machine guns, poison gas, tanks, airplanes, submarines and huge artillery guns into war on a massive scale.
when were tanks first used | first tank in battle => Tanks were first used in battle by the British at the Somme on September 15, 1916. They were slow and broke down often but could cross trenches.
what was poison gas in ww1 | explain poison gas in ww1 => Germany first used chlorine gas on a large scale at Ypres in April 1915. Later mustard gas caused terrible burns. Soldiers began wearing gas masks.
who was the red baron | what is the red baron famous for => The Red Baron was Manfred von Richthofen, a German fighter pilot who flew a red plane and shot down 80 Allied planes, the most of any pilot in World War 1. He was killed in 1918.
what was the lusitania | why did the lusitania sink => The Lusitania was a British ocean liner sunk by a German U-boat on May 7, 1915. Nearly 1,200 people died, including 128 Americans, which turned many Americans against Germany.
what is a u boat | explain a u boat => A U-boat was a German submarine. The name comes from Unterseeboot, German for "undersea boat."
why did the us join ww1 | when did america enter world war 1 => The US entered World War 1 in April 1917 after Germany restarted unlimited submarine attacks on ships and secretly asked Mexico to join a war against the US in the Zimmermann Telegram.
what was the zimmermann telegram | explain the zimmermann telegram => The Zimmermann Telegram was a secret 1917 German message asking Mexico to attack the US, promising to help Mexico get back Texas, New Mexico and Arizona. British codebreakers decoded it.
what was the treaty of versailles | what did the treaty of versailles do => The Treaty of Versailles, signed June 28, 1919, ended the war with Germany. Germany lost land and colonies, had to limit its army, accept blame and pay huge reparations, which caused anger later.
what was the league of nations | why did the league of nations fail => The League of Nations was created in 1920 to keep world peace. It failed partly because the US never joined and it had no army to stop aggressive countries. The UN replaced it in 1945.
what were woodrow wilsons fourteen points | explain woodrow wilsons fourteen points => The Fourteen Points were Woodrow Wilson's 1918 plan for peace, including open diplomacy, free seas, self-rule for nations and a League of Nations.
what happened to the ottoman empire after ww1 | how did the ottoman empire end => The Ottoman Empire lost the war and was broken up. Britain and France took control of much of the Middle East, and in 1923 the Republic of Turkey was founded.
who was mustafa kemal ataturk | what is mustafa kemal ataturk famous for => Mustafa Kemal Atatürk was an Ottoman officer who led the Turkish War of Independence and founded the Republic of Turkey in 1923, becoming its first president.
what was shell shock | explain shell shock => Shell shock was the name in World War 1 for the mental trauma soldiers suffered from constant shelling and horror. Today a similar condition is called PTSD.
what was the home front in ww1 | what did women do in ww1 => At home, people faced food rationing and worked to make weapons. Millions of women took jobs in factories, farms and offices, which helped them win the vote in several countries.
what were the doughboys | explain the doughboys => Doughboys was a nickname for American soldiers in World War 1. About 2 million American soldiers went to France.
who was the kaiser | who was kaiser wilhelm => Kaiser Wilhelm II was emperor of Germany during World War 1. He gave up the throne in November 1918 and fled to the Netherlands.
what was the war to end all wars | explain the war to end all wars => People called World War 1 "the war to end all wars" because they hoped it was so terrible no one would fight like that again. World War 2 began just 21 years later.
what was the battle of the marne | explain the battle of the marne => At the First Battle of the Marne in September 1914, French and British forces stopped the German advance toward Paris. Some French soldiers were rushed to the front in Paris taxis.
what was the race to the sea in ww1 | explain the race to the sea in ww1 => After the Marne in 1914, both sides kept trying to get around each other to the north, until trenches stretched from the Swiss border to the North Sea.
what was the battle of jutland | explain the battle of jutland => The Battle of Jutland in 1916 was the biggest sea battle of World War 1, between the British and German navies. Britain lost more ships, but the German fleet stayed in port for most of the rest of the war.
what was passchendaele | explain passchendaele => The Battle of Passchendaele, near Ypres in Belgium, lasted from July to November 1917. Soldiers fought in deep, deadly mud for a few kilometers of ground.
what were the war poets | who was wilfred owen => The War Poets were soldiers who wrote about World War 1, like Wilfred Owen, Siegfried Sassoon and John McCrae. Owen's poems showed the horror of war, and he was killed a week before it ended.
did animals help in ww1 | animals in world war 1 => Yes. Millions of horses, mules and donkeys carried supplies, dogs carried messages and pigeons flew messages across battlefields. A pigeon named Cher Ami saved a trapped US battalion in 1918.
