who was the first person to fly solo across the atlantic | first solo transatlantic flight => Charles Lindbergh made the first solo nonstop flight across the Atlantic in May 1927, flying from New York to Paris in about 33 and a half hours.
who was the first woman to fly across the atlantic alone | first woman solo atlantic flight => Amelia Earhart became the first woman to fly solo across the Atlantic in May 1932, from Newfoundland to Northern Ireland.
who was the first person to sail around the world alone | first solo voyage around the world => Joshua Slocum, a Canadian-born American sailor, completed the first solo voyage around the world from 1895 to 1898 in his small boat, the Spray.
who first sailed all the way around the world | first circumnavigation of the earth => Ferdinand Magellan's Spanish expedition made the first trip around the world from 1519 to 1522. Magellan was killed on the way, and Juan Sebastián Elcano led the survivors home.
who was the first european to reach america | did vikings reach america before columbus => The Viking explorer Leif Erikson reached North America around the year 1000, about 500 years before Columbus. A Viking site has been found at L'Anse aux Meadows in Newfoundland, Canada.
who first reached the top of mount everest | first people to climb everest => Edmund Hillary of New Zealand and Tenzing Norgay, a Sherpa from Nepal, reached the summit of Mount Everest on May 29, 1953.
who was the first woman to climb mount everest | first woman on everest => Junko Tabei of Japan became the first woman to reach the top of Mount Everest on May 16, 1975.
who first reached the south pole | first person at the south pole => Norwegian explorer Roald Amundsen's team reached the South Pole first, on December 14, 1911.
who first reached the north pole | first person at the north pole => Robert Peary and Matthew Henson claimed to reach the North Pole in 1909, but it's disputed. The first confirmed visit on the ice was by a Soviet team that landed by plane in 1948.
who was the first person to go over niagara falls in a barrel | first person to survive niagara falls barrel => Annie Edson Taylor, a 63 year old teacher, became the first person to go over Niagara Falls in a barrel and survive, on October 24, 1901.
who was the first woman doctor in the us | first female doctor in america => Elizabeth Blackwell became the first woman to earn a medical degree in the United States, in 1849.

who was the first woman elected to congress | first woman in congress => Jeannette Rankin of Montana became the first woman elected to Congress in 1916, before women could vote nationwide.
who was the first woman senator | first female us senator => Rebecca Latimer Felton of Georgia was the first woman to serve in the Senate, for one day in 1922. Hattie Caraway of Arkansas was the first woman elected to the Senate, in 1932.
who was the first woman to run for president | first female presidential candidate => Victoria Woodhull ran for president in 1872, before women could even vote. In 2016, Hillary Clinton became the first woman nominated by a major party.
who was the first woman governor | first female governor in the us => Nellie Tayloe Ross of Wyoming became the first woman governor in the US in January 1925.
who was the first black person elected to congress | first african american congressman => Joseph Rainey of South Carolina became the first Black member of the House of Representatives in December 1870.
who was the first woman to win a nobel prize | first female nobel prize winner => Marie Curie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize, in Physics in 1903. She won a second one in Chemistry in 1911.
who was the first woman prime minister in the world | world's first female prime minister => Sirimavo Bandaranaike of Sri Lanka, then called Ceylon, became the world's first woman prime minister in 1960.
who was the first woman to lead a country | first female head of state => Sirimavo Bandaranaike of Sri Lanka became the first modern woman prime minister in 1960. Long before, women like Hatshepsut of Egypt ruled as pharaoh.
who was the first person to break the sound barrier | who flew faster than sound first => Chuck Yeager flew faster than the speed of sound on October 14, 1947, in the Bell X-1 rocket plane.
who was the first person to run a mile under four minutes | first sub four minute mile => Roger Bannister ran a mile in 3 minutes 59.4 seconds on May 6, 1954, in Oxford, England.
who made the first phone call | what were the first words said on a telephone => On March 10, 1876, Alexander Graham Bell said "Mr. Watson, come here, I want to see you" to his assistant Thomas Watson on the first working telephone.
who made the first movie with sound | first talking movie => The Jazz Singer, released in 1927, was the first feature film with synchronized talking and singing.

what was the first university in america | oldest college in the us => Harvard, founded in 1636 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, is the oldest college in the United States.

what was the first public library in america | first free public library => The Boston Public Library, opened in 1854, was the first large free public library in the US.
what was the first public school in america | oldest public school in the us => Boston Latin School, founded in 1635, is the oldest public school in the United States.
what was the first zoo in america | oldest zoo in the us => The Philadelphia Zoo, opened in 1874, was the first zoo in the US.
when was the first super bowl | who won the first super bowl => The first Super Bowl was played on January 15, 1967. The Green Bay Packers beat the Kansas City Chiefs 35 to 10.
when was the first world series | who won the first world series => The first World Series was played in 1903. The Boston Americans, later called the Red Sox, beat the Pittsburgh Pirates.
when was the first thanksgiving parade | first macys parade => The first Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York was held in 1924. Giant balloons were added in 1927.
when was the first ferris wheel built | who invented the ferris wheel => George Ferris built the first Ferris wheel for the 1893 World's Fair in Chicago. It was about 80 meters (264 feet) tall.
when was the first roller coaster built | first roller coaster in america => The first roller coaster in the US was the Switchback Railway at Coney Island, New York, opened in 1884. Earlier ice slides in Russia inspired roller coasters.
when was the first escalator | who built the first escalator => The first working escalator was shown as a ride at Coney Island in 1896, and they soon appeared in stores.
when was the first skyscraper built | first skyscraper in the world => The Home Insurance Building in Chicago, finished in 1885, is often called the first skyscraper because it had a steel frame. It was 10 stories tall.
when was the first subway | first underground train in the world => The world's first underground railway opened in London in January 1863. The first US subway opened in Boston in 1897, and New York's opened in 1904.
when was the first airplane passenger flight | first airline => The first scheduled airline service with airplanes began in Florida on January 1, 1914, flying passengers across Tampa Bay.
when was the first credit card | first credit card ever => The Diners Club card, introduced in 1950, is often called the first modern credit card. It started after a man forgot his wallet at a restaurant.
when was the first video call | first video phone => AT&T showed a Picturephone at the 1964 World's Fair in New York, but few people used it. Video calls became common with internet services in the 2000s.
when was the first website made | first website ever => Tim Berners-Lee published the first website in 1991 at CERN in Switzerland. It explained how the World Wide Web worked.
when was the first text message sent | first sms text => The first text message was sent on December 3, 1992. Engineer Neil Papworth sent "Merry Christmas" from a computer to a phone in the UK.
when was the first selfie taken | first self portrait photo => Robert Cornelius took what is thought to be the first photo self-portrait in 1839 in Philadelphia. He had to sit still for several minutes.
when was the first emoji created | what year was the first emoji created => Shigetaka Kurita made a famous early set of 176 emoji for a Japanese phone company in 1999.
when was the first youtube video | first video ever uploaded to youtube => The first YouTube video, "Me at the zoo," was uploaded on April 23, 2005, by co-founder Jawed Karim. It's 19 seconds long.
