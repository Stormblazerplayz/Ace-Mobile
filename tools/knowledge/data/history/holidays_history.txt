# tag: culture.holidays.history
why do we eat turkey on thanksgiving | how come we eat turkey on thanksgiving => Turkey became the main Thanksgiving food in the 1800s because it was big enough for a family and native to America. The 1621 feast probably had deer and wild fowl.
why does the president pardon a turkey | what is the presidential turkey pardon => The president "pardons" a turkey each year before Thanksgiving so it won't be eaten. The tradition became official under George H. W. Bush in 1989.
what is martin luther king jr day | when did mlk day become a holiday => Martin Luther King Jr. Day is the third Monday in January, near his January 15 birthday. Ronald Reagan signed it into law in 1983, and it was first observed in 1986.
what is flag day | explain flag day => Flag Day, June 14, celebrates the day in 1777 when the Continental Congress adopted the first official US flag.
why do we wear green on st patricks day | how come people wear green on st patricks day => Green is linked to Ireland, the "Emerald Isle," and to the shamrock, which legend says Saint Patrick used to teach about the Christian Trinity.
why do we drop a ball on new years eve | times square ball drop history => The Times Square ball drop in New York started in 1907. It came from "time balls" that were dropped at a set time so ships could set their clocks.
what is anzac day | explain anzac day => Anzac Day, April 25, honors Australian and New Zealand soldiers. It marks the landing at Gallipoli in 1915 during World War 1.
why are the months named what they are | where do month names come from => Month names come from the Roman calendar: January for Janus, March for Mars, June probably for Juno, July for Julius Caesar and August for Augustus. September to December come from Latin numbers 7 to 10.
why is september not the seventh month | september means seven so why is it ninth => September means "seventh month" in Latin because the early Roman calendar started in March. When January and February moved to the start, September became the ninth month.
what is the gregorian calendar | explain the gregorian calendar => The Gregorian calendar, which most of the world uses, was introduced by Pope Gregory XIII in 1582 to fix errors in the older Julian calendar. Britain and its colonies switched in 1752.
