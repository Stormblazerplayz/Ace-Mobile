# tag: history.concepts
why do years go backwards in bc | why does bc count down => BC years count backward because they count how many years before AD 1. So 500 BC came before 400 BC, and someone born in 50 BC was 49 or 50 in AD 1.
what century is it | what century are we in => We live in the 21st century, which runs from 2001 to 2100. Centuries are numbered one higher than their first digits, so the 1900s were the 20th century.
why is the 1800s called the 19th century | how do you count centuries => The 1st century was years 1 to 100, so the 2nd century was 101 to 200. Counting this way, the years 1801 to 1900 are the 19th century.
what is a decade | what is a millennium => A decade is 10 years, a century is 100 years, and a millennium is 1,000 years. The third millennium began in 2001.
what is history | why do we study history => History is the study of the past, especially what people did and why. Studying it helps us understand how the world got the way it is and learn from past mistakes and successes.
what is a historian | what does a historian do => A historian studies and writes about the past, using sources like letters, diaries, records, photographs and artifacts to figure out what happened and why.
what is an artifact | explain an artifact => An artifact is an object made or used by people in the past, like a tool, coin, pot or piece of jewelry.
what is a timeline | explain a timeline => A timeline shows events in the order they happened, often along a line with dates, so you can see what came before or after.
what is a civilization | what makes a civilization => A civilization is a complex society with cities, a government, jobs that specialize, a system of writing or record keeping, and shared culture. Early ones grew in Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Indus Valley and China.
what is an empire | what is the difference between a kingdom and an empire => An empire is a large state that rules over many different peoples and lands, usually under an emperor. A kingdom is usually smaller and ruled by a king or queen.
what is a colony | colony meaning in history => A colony is a land controlled and settled by a country far away. Colonialism is when powerful countries take control of other lands and peoples, often taking their resources.
what is imperialism | explain imperialism => Imperialism is when a country extends its power over other lands, by force, colonies or control of trade. European powers did this across Africa and Asia in the 1800s.
what is independence | what does it mean when a country becomes independent => Independence means a country governs itself instead of being ruled by another country. Many nations won independence from colonial empires in the 1900s.
what is a revolution in history | explain a revolution in history => In history, a revolution is a big, often fast change, like overthrowing a government, as in the American and French Revolutions, or changing how people live, as in the Industrial Revolution.
what does civil war mean | definition of a civil war | explain a civil war => A civil war is a war between groups within the same country, like the American Civil War from 1861 to 1865 or the English Civil War in the 1640s.
what is an alliance | what is an ally => An alliance is an agreement between countries to help each other, often in war. The countries in it are called allies.
what is an armistice | explain an armistice => An armistice is an agreement to stop fighting, like the one that ended World War 1 on November 11, 1918, or the one that paused the Korean War in 1953.
what is a propaganda poster | explain a propaganda poster => A propaganda poster is a poster made, often by a government, to persuade people, like World War 2 posters urging people to buy war bonds or save food.
what were hunter gatherers | explain hunter gatherers => Hunter-gatherers were people who got food by hunting animals and gathering wild plants instead of farming. All humans lived this way for most of history.
what is a nomad | what are nomads => Nomads are people who move from place to place instead of living in one spot, often following their herds or seasons, like the Mongols and many Bedouin.
what is feudalism in simple words | explain feudalism in simple words => Feudalism was the medieval system where a king gave land to nobles, nobles gave land to knights for military service, and peasants farmed the land in return for protection.
what is a serf | explain a serf => A serf was a medieval peasant tied to a lord's land. Serfs couldn't leave without permission and had to work the lord's fields, but they weren't owned like enslaved people.
what is the enlightenment | explain the enlightenment => The Enlightenment was a movement in the 1600s and 1700s when thinkers like John Locke, Voltaire and Montesquieu argued for reason, science and people's rights. It inspired the American and French Revolutions.
what is the industrial revolution in simple words | explain the industrial revolution in simple words => The Industrial Revolution was when people started making things in factories with machines instead of by hand. It began in Britain in the late 1700s and spread through the 1800s.
what is the modern era | explain the modern era => Historians often call the time after about 1500 the modern era, and the time after about 1800 the late modern era. The 1900s and today are sometimes called contemporary history.
what is ancient history | explain ancient history => Ancient history covers the time from the start of writing, around 3200 BC, to about AD 500, including Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece and Rome.
what is a monument | explain a monument => A monument is a statue, building or structure made to remember a person or event, like the Washington Monument or the Lincoln Memorial.
what is a museum | when was the first museum => A museum is a place that keeps and displays objects of history, art or science. The Ashmolean in Oxford, opened in 1683, is one of the oldest public museums.
what is a time capsule | explain a time capsule => A time capsule is a container of objects and messages sealed and buried or stored for people to open in the future, often 25, 50 or 100 years later.
what is genealogy | what is a family tree => Genealogy is the study of family history. A family tree is a chart showing your ancestors, like parents, grandparents and great-grandparents.
what is oral history | explain oral history => Oral history is history passed down by people speaking, like stories told by elders or interviews with people who lived through events.
