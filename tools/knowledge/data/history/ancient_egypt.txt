how did king tut die | cause of tutankhamun's death => No one knows for sure. Scans show Tutankhamun had a broken leg, a bad foot and malaria, and he may have died from an infection after an injury.
who was ramses ii | who was ramses the great => Ramses II, or Ramses the Great, ruled Egypt for 66 years, from about 1279 to 1213 BC. He built huge monuments like Abu Simbel and fought the Hittites at the Battle of Kadesh.

who was akhenaten | who was nefertiti => Akhenaten was a pharaoh who around 1350 BC tried to make Egypt worship mainly one god, the sun disk Aten. Nefertiti was his queen, famous for a beautiful painted bust found in 1912.

who were mark antony and cleopatra | tell me about mark antony and cleopatra => Mark Antony was a Roman general who became Cleopatra's partner. Together they fought Octavian, lost the Battle of Actium in 31 BC, and both took their own lives in 30 BC.
who was the last pharaoh of egypt | last ruler of ancient egypt => Cleopatra VII was the last active ruler of ancient Egypt. After her death in 30 BC, Egypt became part of the Roman Empire.

pyramid building methods | how did egyptians move the stones | what tools built the pyramids => Workers cut huge limestone blocks with copper tools, dragged them on sledges, sometimes wetting the sand to make it easier, and pulled them up ramps. Boats carried stone along the Nile and canals.
did slaves build the pyramids | who were the pyramid workers => Most historians now think the pyramids were built mainly by paid Egyptian workers and farmers, not slaves. Their villages and tombs have been found near Giza.

how many blocks are in the great pyramid | how heavy are the pyramid stones => The Great Pyramid has about 2.3 million stone blocks. Most weigh around 2.5 tons, and some granite blocks inside weigh 25 tons or more.

why did they take out the brain | what did egyptians do with organs => Embalmers pulled the brain out through the nose and threw it away, because they thought the heart did the thinking. Other organs were kept in special containers called canopic jars.
what is a sarcophagus | what is a coffin in egypt => A sarcophagus is a large stone coffin. Important Egyptians were placed in nested wooden coffins inside a stone sarcophagus.
did egyptians mummify animals | animal mummies => Yes. Egyptians mummified millions of animals, like cats, ibis birds, crocodiles and baboons, often as offerings to the gods linked to those animals.
what is the valley of the kings | explain the valley of the kings => The Valley of the Kings is a valley near Luxor where pharaohs of the New Kingdom, about 1539 to 1075 BC, were buried in tombs cut into the rock to hide them from robbers.
what is the book of the dead | explain the book of the dead => The Book of the Dead is a collection of spells written on papyrus and placed in tombs to help the dead person get through the underworld safely.

who decoded hieroglyphs | who figured out the rosetta stone => French scholar Jean-François Champollion announced in 1822 that he had cracked hieroglyphs, using the Rosetta Stone and other texts.
who found the rosetta stone | which museum has the rosetta stone => French soldiers found the Rosetta Stone in 1799 near the town of Rashid (Rosetta) in Egypt. The British took it in 1801, and it is in the British Museum in London.

what did ancient egyptians eat | egyptian food in ancient times => Ancient Egyptians mainly ate bread and drank beer made from barley, along with onions, lentils, beans, fish, fruit like dates and figs, and sometimes meat.
what did ancient egyptians wear | ancient egyptian clothes => Most Egyptians wore light linen clothes because of the heat. Men often wore kilts and women long dresses, and both wore makeup like black eyeliner called kohl.
