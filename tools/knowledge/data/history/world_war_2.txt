who were the axis powers | which countries were the axis => The main Axis powers were Germany, Italy and Japan. Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria and others also joined them.
who were the allies in ww2 | which countries were the allies in world war 2 => The main Allies were Britain, the Soviet Union, the United States and China, along with France, Canada, Australia, India, Poland and many more.

which country lost the most people in ww2 | soviet union deaths in ww2 => The Soviet Union lost the most people, around 27 million soldiers and civilians. China also lost many millions, perhaps 15 to 20 million.

how did hitler die | when did hitler die => Adolf Hitler took his own life in his bunker in Berlin on April 30, 1945, as Soviet troops closed in on the city.

what were concentration camps | what was auschwitz => Concentration camps were prisons where the Nazis held people in brutal conditions. Auschwitz, in occupied Poland, was the largest camp, where about 1.1 million people were murdered, most of them Jewish.
when was auschwitz liberated | what is holocaust remembrance day => Soviet troops freed Auschwitz on January 27, 1945. That date is now International Holocaust Remembrance Day.

who was oskar schindler | what is oskar schindler famous for => Oskar Schindler was a German businessman who saved about 1,200 Jewish people by employing them in his factories during the Holocaust. The movie Schindler's List tells his story.

who was joseph stalin | what is joseph stalin famous for => Joseph Stalin ruled the Soviet Union from the late 1920s until his death in 1953. He led it to victory over Germany in World War 2, but his brutal rule killed millions of his own people.
who was hirohito | who was the emperor of japan in ww2 => Hirohito was emperor of Japan from 1926 to 1989, including during World War 2. He announced Japan's surrender on the radio in August 1945.

what was the blitz | how long did the blitz last => The Blitz was Germany's bombing of British cities, especially London, from September 1940 to May 1941. About 43,000 civilians were killed.
what was the french resistance | explain the french resistance => The French Resistance was made up of people who secretly fought the German occupation of France by spying, sabotage and helping Allied soldiers escape.

what was the siege of leningrad | explain the siege of leningrad => Germany besieged the Soviet city of Leningrad, now St. Petersburg, for about 872 days from 1941 to 1944. Around a million civilians died, mostly from starvation.

what did fdr say about pearl harbor | a date which will live in infamy => The day after Pearl Harbor, Franklin D. Roosevelt called December 7, 1941, "a date which will live in infamy," and Congress declared war on Japan.
when did the us enter ww2 | why did america join world war 2 => The US entered World War 2 in December 1941, after Japan attacked Pearl Harbor. Germany and Italy then declared war on the US.
what was the uss arizona | explain the uss arizona => The USS Arizona was a battleship sunk at Pearl Harbor, where 1,177 crew members died. A memorial now stands over its wreck.
what was the battle of midway | why was midway important => At Midway in June 1942, the US Navy sank four Japanese aircraft carriers, partly thanks to codebreakers who learned Japan's plans. It was the turning point of the Pacific war.
what was island hopping in ww2 | explain island hopping in ww2 => Island hopping was the US plan in the Pacific to capture key islands one by one, skipping strongly defended ones, to get close enough to attack Japan.
what was the battle of iwo jima | what is the iwo jima flag photo => The US captured the island of Iwo Jima from Japan in a brutal battle in February and March 1945. A famous photo shows six Marines raising the American flag on Mount Suribachi.

what does the d in d day stand for | d day meaning of the d => The D in D-Day just stands for "day," meaning the day an operation begins. The Normandy landing is the most famous D-Day.
