who found the lascaux cave paintings | how were the lascaux caves discovered => Four teenagers in France found the Lascaux cave in 1940 after their dog, according to the story, fell into a hole. Its walls hold animal paintings about 17,000 years old.

who found the chauvet cave | what is the chauvet cave | when was chauvet cave discovered => Three cave explorers found the Chauvet Cave in southern France in 1994. Its paintings of lions, horses and rhinos are over 30,000 years old, among the oldest known.

who found the antikythera mechanism | ancient greek computer => Sponge divers found the Antikythera mechanism in a Roman-era shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera in 1901. It's an ancient Greek gear device for predicting the positions of the Sun, Moon and planets.
