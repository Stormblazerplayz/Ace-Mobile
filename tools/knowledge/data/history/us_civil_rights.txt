what was the civil rights movement | when was the civil rights movement | what did the civil rights movement do => The civil rights movement was a struggle, mainly in the 1950s and 1960s, by Black Americans and their allies to end segregation and racial discrimination and win equal rights, especially voting rights.
what is segregation | what does segregation mean => Segregation is keeping people apart by race. In the US, Jim Crow laws forced Black and white people to use separate schools, buses, restaurants and even water fountains.
what was plessy v ferguson | what does separate but equal mean => In Plessy v. Ferguson (1896), the Supreme Court said segregation was legal if facilities were "separate but equal." In reality Black facilities were almost always worse.
what was brown v board of education | when was brown v board | what did brown v board decide => In Brown v. Board of Education (1954), the Supreme Court ruled that segregated public schools are unconstitutional, overturning "separate but equal" for schools.
who was thurgood marshall | what is thurgood marshall famous for => Thurgood Marshall was the lawyer who won Brown v. Board of Education in 1954. In 1967 he became the first Black justice on the Supreme Court.
what was the montgomery bus boycott | how long was the montgomery bus boycott => After Rosa Parks was arrested in December 1955, Black residents of Montgomery, Alabama, refused to ride city buses for 381 days. It ended in December 1956 after the Supreme Court ruled bus segregation illegal.
what was the i have a dream speech | when did mlk give the i have a dream speech => Martin Luther King Jr. gave his "I Have a Dream" speech on August 28, 1963, at the March on Washington, in front of the Lincoln Memorial. He described a future where people are judged by their character, not skin color.
what was the march on washington | how many people were at the march on washington => The March on Washington for Jobs and Freedom on August 28, 1963, drew about 250,000 people to Washington, D.C. to demand civil rights and jobs.
how did martin luther king jr die | when was mlk killed | who killed martin luther king => Martin Luther King Jr. was assassinated on April 4, 1968, in Memphis, Tennessee, where he had gone to support striking sanitation workers. James Earl Ray was convicted of the killing.
when is martin luther king day | why do we have mlk day => Martin Luther King Jr. Day is the third Monday in January, near his January 15 birthday. It became a federal holiday in 1983 and was first observed in 1986.
did martin luther king win the nobel prize | what was mlk's nobel prize for => Yes. Martin Luther King Jr. won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1964, at 35, for leading the civil rights movement through nonviolence.
what is nonviolent protest | why did mlk use nonviolence => Nonviolent protest means fighting injustice with peaceful actions like marches, boycotts and sit-ins instead of violence. King was inspired by Gandhi and believed it could change hearts and laws.
who was malcolm x | what did malcolm x believe => Malcolm X was a powerful speaker for Black pride and self-defense, and a leader in the Nation of Islam before leaving it in 1964. He was assassinated in New York in February 1965.
who was emmett till | what is emmett till famous for => Emmett Till was a 14 year old Black boy from Chicago who was brutally murdered in Mississippi in 1955 after being accused of whistling at a white woman. His mother's open-casket funeral helped spark the civil rights movement.

who were the freedom riders | what were the freedom rides => The Freedom Riders were Black and white activists who rode buses through the South in 1961 to test laws banning segregation on interstate travel. Some buses were attacked and burned.
what was the birmingham campaign | what was the letter from birmingham jail => In 1963 activists protested segregation in Birmingham, Alabama, and police used dogs and fire hoses on children. King, jailed there, wrote his famous "Letter from Birmingham Jail" defending peaceful protest.

what was the civil rights act of 1964 | what did the civil rights act do => The Civil Rights Act of 1964, signed by Lyndon B. Johnson, banned discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex or national origin and ended legal segregation in public places.
what was the voting rights act | when was the voting rights act passed => The Voting Rights Act of 1965 banned unfair voting rules like literacy tests and had the federal government watch elections in places that blocked Black voters.

who was john lewis | what is john lewis famous for => John Lewis was a civil rights leader who was beaten at Selma on Bloody Sunday in 1965. He later served in Congress from Georgia for over 30 years, until his death in 2020.

who was langston hughes | what is langston hughes famous for => Langston Hughes was a poet and writer of the Harlem Renaissance who wrote about Black American life, with poems like "I, Too" and "Harlem," which asks "What happens to a dream deferred?"

who was madam cj walker | what is madam cj walker famous for => Madam C. J. Walker built a successful hair care business for Black women in the early 1900s and is often called the first female self-made millionaire in America.

who is dolores huerta | si se puede => Dolores Huerta co-founded the United Farm Workers with Cesar Chavez and came up with the slogan "Sí, se puede," meaning "Yes, we can."
