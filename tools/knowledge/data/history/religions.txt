what are the major world religions | biggest religions in the world | what religions are there => The largest world religions by followers are Christianity, Islam, Hinduism and Buddhism. Others include Sikhism, Judaism, Shinto, Taoism, Confucianism, Jainism and the Baháʼí Faith, and many people are not religious.
what is the largest religion | which religion has the most followers => Christianity has the most followers, about 2.3 to 2.4 billion people, followed by Islam with about 2 billion.
what is monotheism | what is polytheism => Monotheism is the belief in one God, as in Judaism, Christianity and Islam. Polytheism is belief in many gods, as in ancient Greek and Egyptian religion.
what are the abrahamic religions | why are judaism christianity and islam related => Judaism, Christianity and Islam are called Abrahamic religions because they all trace their history back to Abraham and believe in one God.
what is an atheist | what is an agnostic => An atheist is someone who does not believe in any god. An agnostic is someone who thinks we can't know for sure whether a god exists.
what is christianity | what do christians believe => Christianity is a religion based on the life and teachings of Jesus. Christians believe Jesus is the Son of God, that he died and rose again, and they follow teachings like loving God and your neighbor.
who was jesus | who is jesus christ => Jesus was a Jewish teacher from Nazareth who lived about 2,000 years ago in Roman-ruled Judea. Christians believe he is the Son of God. He was crucified around AD 30 to 33.
what is the bible | how many books are in the bible => The Bible is the holy book of Christianity, made of the Old Testament and the New Testament. Protestant Bibles have 66 books, and Catholic Bibles have 73.
what is the new testament | what is the old testament => The Old Testament contains scriptures shared largely with Judaism, from creation through the prophets. The New Testament tells of Jesus's life in the four Gospels and the early church.
what are the gospels | who wrote the gospels => The Gospels are the first four books of the New Testament, named Matthew, Mark, Luke and John. They tell the story of Jesus's life, teachings, death and resurrection.
what is christmas about | why do christians celebrate christmas => Christians celebrate Christmas on December 25 to remember the birth of Jesus. Many Orthodox Christians celebrate on January 7.
what is easter about | why do christians celebrate easter => Easter is the most important Christian holiday, celebrating the belief that Jesus rose from the dead three days after he was crucified. Its date changes each year in spring.
what are the branches of christianity | catholic vs protestant vs orthodox => The three main branches are Catholic, Eastern Orthodox and Protestant. The Catholic and Orthodox churches split in 1054, and Protestants broke from Catholicism starting in 1517.
who is the pope | what does the pope do => The pope is the leader of the Catholic Church and the bishop of Rome, living in Vatican City. Catholics believe the pope is the successor of Saint Peter.
what is the reformation | who was martin luther in religion => The Protestant Reformation began in 1517 when Martin Luther, a German monk, posted his 95 Theses criticizing the Catholic Church. It led to new Protestant churches like Lutherans.
what is a church | what is a cathedral => A church is a building where Christians worship. A cathedral is a large, important church that is the seat of a bishop.
what is lent | explain lent => Lent is the 40 day period before Easter when many Christians pray, fast or give something up. It starts on Ash Wednesday.
what is the ten commandments | what are the ten commandments => The Ten Commandments are rules that, according to the Bible, God gave to Moses on Mount Sinai. They include worshipping only God, honoring your parents, and not killing, stealing or lying.
who was moses | what is moses famous for => Moses is a prophet in Judaism, Christianity and Islam. According to the Bible he led the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt and received the Ten Commandments.
who was abraham in the bible | what is abraham in the bible famous for => Abraham is honored in Judaism, Christianity and Islam as the father of their faith, who made a covenant with God. Jewish and Arab people both trace ancestry to him in their traditions.
what is noahs ark | explain noahs ark => In the Bible, God told Noah to build a huge boat called the Ark to save his family and pairs of every animal from a great flood.
what is islam | what do muslims believe => Islam is a religion whose followers, Muslims, believe in one God, called Allah in Arabic, and that Muhammad was God's final prophet. Their holy book is the Quran.
who was muhammad | who is the prophet muhammad => Muhammad (about 570 to 632) was born in Mecca in Arabia. Muslims believe he was the last prophet of God and received the Quran. Out of respect, Muslims don't make pictures of him.
what is the quran | what language is the quran in => The Quran is the holy book of Islam, written in Arabic. Muslims believe it is the word of God revealed to Muhammad. It has 114 chapters called surahs.
what are the five pillars of islam | explain the five pillars of islam => The Five Pillars are the main duties of Muslims: the declaration of faith (shahada), prayer five times a day (salat), giving to the poor (zakat), fasting in Ramadan (sawm) and the pilgrimage to Mecca (hajj).
what is ramadan | why do muslims fast => Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar, when Muslims fast from dawn to sunset to grow closer to God and remember people in need. It ends with the festival Eid al-Fitr.
what is eid | what is eid al fitr => Eid al-Fitr is the Muslim celebration at the end of Ramadan, with prayers, feasts, gifts and charity. Eid al-Adha, later in the year, honors Abraham's faith.
what is the hajj | explain the hajj => The Hajj is the pilgrimage to Mecca that every Muslim who is able should make at least once in their life. Millions go each year.
what is a mosque | explain a mosque => A mosque is a building where Muslims pray. Many have a dome and a tall tower called a minaret, from which the call to prayer is given.
what is the difference between sunni and shia | explain the difference between sunni and shia => Sunni and Shia are the two main branches of Islam. They split after Muhammad's death over who should lead the community. Most Muslims, about 85 to 90 percent, are Sunni.
what is judaism | what do jewish people believe => Judaism is one of the oldest religions that believe in one God, going back over 3,000 years. Jewish people follow the Torah and traditions of the Hebrew Bible.
what is the torah | explain the torah => The Torah is the most important Jewish scripture: the first five books of the Hebrew Bible. In synagogues it is handwritten on a scroll.
what is a synagogue | what is a rabbi => A synagogue is a Jewish house of worship and study. A rabbi is a Jewish teacher and religious leader.
what is the sabbath | what is shabbat => Shabbat is the Jewish day of rest, from sunset Friday to nightfall Saturday, remembering that God rested on the seventh day. Many Christians observe Sunday as a day of worship.
what is hanukkah | explain hanukkah => Hanukkah is an eight-day Jewish festival of lights in winter that remembers the rededication of the Temple in Jerusalem around 164 BC. Families light a menorah, one more candle each night.
what is passover | explain passover => Passover is a Jewish holiday in spring that remembers the Israelites' freedom from slavery in Egypt. Families share a special meal called a Seder and eat matzah.
what is yom kippur | what is rosh hashanah => Rosh Hashanah is the Jewish New Year, in early fall. Ten days later comes Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement, the holiest day of the year, spent fasting and praying.
what is a bar mitzvah | what is a bat mitzvah => A bar mitzvah (for boys, at 13) or bat mitzvah (for girls, at 12 or 13) is a Jewish coming of age ceremony, after which they take on adult religious duties.
what is the star of david | explain the star of david => The Star of David is a six-pointed star that is a symbol of Judaism and the Jewish people. It's on the flag of Israel.
what is kosher | what is halal => Kosher food follows Jewish dietary laws and halal food follows Islamic ones. Both, for example, forbid eating pork.
what is hinduism | what do hindus believe => Hinduism is one of the oldest living religions, from India. Many Hindus believe in a supreme reality called Brahman, worship many gods as its forms, and believe in karma and reincarnation.
what is reincarnation | explain reincarnation => Reincarnation is the belief that after death a soul is reborn in a new body. It is part of Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism and Jainism.
what is karma | explain karma => Karma is the idea in Hinduism, Buddhism and other religions that your actions, good or bad, shape what happens to you in the future and in future lives.
what is diwali | explain diwali => Diwali is the Hindu festival of lights, in October or November, celebrating light over darkness. Families light lamps called diyas, set off fireworks and share sweets. Sikhs and Jains also celebrate it.
what is holi | explain holi => Holi is a Hindu spring festival of colors where people throw colored powder and water, celebrating the end of winter and good over evil.
why are cows sacred in india | why dont hindus eat beef => Many Hindus consider cows sacred as symbols of life and motherhood because they give milk, so many Hindus don't eat beef.
what is a hindu temple called | what is a mandir => A Hindu temple is called a mandir. It holds images of gods, and people go there to pray and make offerings in worship called puja.
what is buddhism | what do buddhists believe => Buddhism is a religion and philosophy founded by Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, in India around 2,500 years ago. Buddhists aim to end suffering by following the Eightfold Path toward enlightenment.
who was buddha | who was siddhartha gautama => Siddhartha Gautama was a prince in ancient India and Nepal who gave up his riches to find the cause of suffering. After meditating under a tree he reached enlightenment and became the Buddha, meaning "awakened one."
what are the four noble truths | explain the four noble truths => The Four Noble Truths are the Buddha's main teaching: life involves suffering, suffering comes from craving, suffering can end, and the way to end it is the Eightfold Path.
what is the eightfold path | explain the eightfold path => The Eightfold Path is the Buddhist guide to living well: right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration.
what is nirvana | explain nirvana => Nirvana is the goal of Buddhism: a state of peace and freedom from suffering and from the cycle of rebirth.
what is meditation in religion | explain meditation in religion => Meditation is calming and focusing the mind, often by paying attention to your breathing. It's important in Buddhism and Hinduism, and many people do it just to relax.
what is sikhism | what do sikhs believe => Sikhism is a religion founded by Guru Nanak in the Punjab region of South Asia around 1500. Sikhs believe in one God, equality of all people, honest work and helping others.
what is a gurdwara | explain a gurdwara => A gurdwara is a Sikh place of worship. Every gurdwara has a free community kitchen called a langar that serves meals to anyone.
why do sikhs wear turbans | what does a sikh turban mean => Many Sikhs don't cut their hair as a sign of respect for God's creation, and wear a turban to cover and care for it. It's an important symbol of Sikh faith and identity.
what is the guru granth sahib | explain the guru granth sahib => The Guru Granth Sahib is the holy scripture of Sikhism, treated with great respect as the eternal living Guru.
what is jainism | explain jainism => Jainism is an ancient religion from India that teaches ahimsa, nonviolence toward all living things. Many Jains are strict vegetarians.
what is shinto | explain shinto => Shinto is the traditional religion of Japan, centered on kami, spirits in nature and ancestors. Its shrines often have a red gate called a torii.
what is the bahai faith | explain the bahai faith => The Baháʼí Faith was founded in Iran in the 1800s by Baháʼu'lláh. It teaches the unity of God, religion and all people.
what is animism | explain animism => Animism is the belief that animals, plants, rivers and places have spirits. It's part of many traditional religions around the world.
what is a pilgrimage | explain a pilgrimage => A pilgrimage is a journey to a holy place for religious reasons, like Muslims going to Mecca, Hindus to the Ganges River, or Christians to Jerusalem or Rome.
what is jerusalem important for | why is jerusalem holy => Jerusalem is holy to three religions. It has the Western Wall for Jews, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre for Christians, and the Al-Aqsa Mosque and Dome of the Rock for Muslims.
what is freedom of religion | explain freedom of religion => Freedom of religion means people can choose to follow any religion or none without being punished. In the US it's protected by the 1st Amendment.
what is the dead sea scrolls | explain the dead sea scrolls => The Dead Sea Scrolls are ancient Jewish manuscripts found in caves near the Dead Sea starting in 1947. Some are over 2,000 years old and include the oldest known copies of books of the Hebrew Bible.
what is the vatican | explain the vatican => Vatican City is the world's smallest country, inside Rome, Italy. It's the home of the pope and the center of the Catholic Church.
what is a monk | what is a nun => Monks and nuns are people who live by religious vows, often in a community, devoting their lives to prayer and service. Monks are men and nuns are women.
what is a prophet | explain a prophet => A prophet is a person believed to speak messages from God. Moses, Jesus and Muhammad are examples in different faiths.
what is a saint | explain a saint => In Christianity, a saint is a person recognized as especially holy. The Catholic Church officially declares saints through a process called canonization.
how many people are not religious | what percent of people have no religion => About 1 in 6 people worldwide, roughly 1.2 billion, say they are not religious or don't belong to any religion.
