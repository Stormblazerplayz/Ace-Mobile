# Algebra methods with tiny worked examples. Written by hand.
# tag: school.math.algebra
how do you solve a one step equation | solving one step equations | how to solve x plus 5 equals 12 => Do the opposite operation to both sides to get x alone. For x + 5 = 12, subtract 5 from both sides: x = 7. For 4x = 20, divide both sides by 4: x = 5.
how do you solve a two step equation | solving two step equations | how to solve 3x plus 4 equals 19 => Undo the adding or subtracting first, then the multiplying or dividing. For 3x + 4 = 19: subtract 4 to get 3x = 15, then divide by 3 to get x = 5.
how do you solve multi step equations | solving equations with distributing | how to solve 2 x plus 3 equals 14 => Distribute, combine like terms, then get x alone. 2(x + 3) = 14 → 2x + 6 = 14 → 2x = 8 → x = 4.
how do you solve equations with variables on both sides | variables on both sides | how to solve 5x plus 2 equals 3x plus 10 => Move all the x terms to one side and the numbers to the other. 5x + 2 = 3x + 10 → subtract 3x: 2x + 2 = 10 → subtract 2: 2x = 8 → x = 4.
how do you solve equations with fractions | solving equations with fractions | how to get rid of fractions in an equation => Multiply every term by the common denominator to clear the fractions. For x/2 + x/3 = 5, multiply by 6: 3x + 2x = 30, so 5x = 30 and x = 6.
how do you check your answer to an equation | how to check if a solution is right | plug in to check => Put your answer back into the original equation. If both sides come out equal, it's right. For 3x + 4 = 19 with x = 5: 3(5) + 4 = 19. Correct.
what does it mean if an equation has no solution | no solution equation example | when does an equation have no solution => You end up with something false, like 3 = 7, after the variables cancel. x + 2 = x + 5 has no solution because no number makes 2 equal 5.
what does infinitely many solutions mean | infinite solutions equation | when does an equation have infinite solutions => The variables cancel and you get something always true, like 6 = 6. 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6 is true for every x.
what is the distributive property | how do you distribute in algebra | distributive property example => Multiply the outside number by each term inside the brackets. 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12, and -2(y - 5) = -2y + 10.
how do you combine like terms | combining like terms | how to simplify 3x plus 2 plus 5x => Add or subtract terms with the same variable and power, and add the plain numbers. 3x + 2 + 5x - 7 = 8x - 5.
how do you simplify an expression | simplifying algebraic expressions | how to simplify algebra => Distribute any brackets, then combine like terms. 2(3x - 1) + 4x = 6x - 2 + 4x = 10x - 2.
how do you evaluate an expression | evaluate an expression when x equals 3 | substitute values into an expression => Replace each variable with its value and follow the order of operations. For 2x² - 5 when x = 3: 2(9) - 5 = 13.
how do you write an expression from words | translating words into algebra | words to algebraic expressions => Turn each phrase into symbols. "5 more than a number" is n + 5, "twice a number" is 2n, "3 less than a number" is n - 3, and "a number divided by 4" is n/4.
how do you solve a word problem with algebra | setting up equations from word problems | algebra word problem steps => Let a variable stand for the unknown, write an equation from the facts, solve it, and check it makes sense. "Twice a number plus 6 is 20" → 2n + 6 = 20 → n = 7.
how do you solve a literal equation | rearranging formulas | how to solve for a different variable => Treat the other letters like numbers and get the one you want alone. To solve A = lw for w, divide both sides by l: w = A/l.
how do you solve an inequality | solving inequalities | how to solve x minus 4 is greater than 6 => Solve it like an equation, keeping the inequality sign. x - 4 > 6 → x > 10. Just remember to flip the sign if you multiply or divide by a negative.
why do you flip the inequality sign | when do you flip the inequality sign | dividing an inequality by a negative => Flip it whenever you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number. -2x < 8 → x > -4. That's because multiplying by a negative reverses the order of numbers.
how do you graph an inequality on a number line | open circle or closed circle | number line inequality graph => Use an open circle for < or > (the number isn't included) and a closed circle for ≤ or ≥ (it is). Then shade toward the numbers that work: x > 3 is an open circle at 3 shaded right.
what is a compound inequality | how do you solve a compound inequality | and vs or inequalities => A compound inequality joins two inequalities. An "and" one like -2 < x < 5 means x is between both; an "or" one like x < -1 or x > 4 means either can be true.
how do you solve an absolute value equation | absolute value equations | how to solve the absolute value of x minus 3 equals 5 => Split it into two equations, one positive and one negative. |x - 3| = 5 gives x - 3 = 5 or x - 3 = -5, so x = 8 or x = -2.
how do you find the slope of a line from two points | slope formula | how to find slope with two points => Slope = (y₂ - y₁)/(x₂ - x₁). For (1, 2) and (4, 11): (11 - 2)/(4 - 1) = 9/3 = 3.
how do you find slope from a graph | rise over run | how to count slope on a graph => Pick two points on the line, count the rise (up or down) and the run (right), and divide. Up 4 and right 2 gives a slope of 4/2 = 2.
what does a positive or negative slope look like | positive vs negative slope | what does a zero slope mean => A positive slope goes up from left to right and a negative slope goes down. A slope of 0 is a flat horizontal line, and a vertical line has an undefined slope.
why is the slope of a vertical line undefined | undefined slope | vertical line slope => A vertical line has no run, so the slope formula divides by 0, which can't be done. Its equation looks like x = 3.
how do you graph a line from y mx b | graphing slope intercept form | how to graph y equals 2x plus 1 => Plot the y-intercept b on the y-axis, then use the slope as rise over run to find more points. For y = 2x + 1, start at (0, 1), go up 2 and right 1 to (1, 3), and draw the line.
how do you write the equation of a line from two points | equation of a line through two points | find the equation given two points => Find the slope, then plug a point in to find b. For (1, 3) and (3, 7): slope = 4/2 = 2, then 3 = 2(1) + b gives b = 1, so y = 2x + 1.
how do you write an equation from a slope and a point | slope and a point to equation | line with slope 3 through 2 5 => Use point-slope form, y - y₁ = m(x - x₁). Slope 3 through (2, 5): y - 5 = 3(x - 2), which simplifies to y = 3x - 1.
how do you find the slope of a parallel line | parallel lines same slope | equation of a parallel line => Parallel lines have the same slope. A line parallel to y = 4x - 1 also has slope 4, like y = 4x + 7.
how do you find the slope of a perpendicular line | negative reciprocal slope | perpendicular slope rule => Flip the slope and change its sign (the negative reciprocal). A line perpendicular to one with slope 2/3 has slope -3/2.
how do you graph a line using intercepts | intercept method | graphing with x and y intercepts => Find both intercepts and connect them. For 2x + 3y = 12: when x = 0, y = 4; when y = 0, x = 6. Plot (0, 4) and (6, 0) and draw the line.
how do you graph a horizontal or vertical line | what does y equals 3 look like | graph of x equals 2 => The graph of y = 3 is a horizontal line through 3 on the y-axis. x = 2 is a vertical line through 2 on the x-axis.
how do you make a table of values | table of values for a graph | input output table => Pick some x values, plug each into the equation to get y, and write the pairs in a table. For y = x + 2: x = 0 → 2, x = 1 → 3, x = 2 → 4. Then plot the points.
how do you tell if a relation is a function | vertical line test | is this a function => Every input must have only one output. On a graph, use the vertical line test: if any vertical line hits the graph more than once, it's not a function.
how do you find f of 3 | evaluating functions | what is f of 2 if f of x is 3x minus 1 => Put the number in place of x. If f(x) = 3x - 1, then f(2) = 3(2) - 1 = 5.
what is a linear function | linear vs nonlinear function | how to tell if a function is linear => A linear function changes by the same amount every step, so its graph is a straight line, like f(x) = 2x + 3. In a table, the y values go up or down by a constant amount.
what is an exponential function | exponential growth | exponential vs linear growth => An exponential function multiplies by the same amount each step, like y = 2ˣ. Linear growth adds the same amount each time, while exponential growth gets faster and faster.
what is exponential decay | half life math | exponential decay example => Exponential decay shrinks by the same factor each step, like y = 100(0.5)ˣ, which halves every step: 100, 50, 25, 12.5.
what is a piecewise function | define piecewise function | how do piecewise functions work => A piecewise function uses different rules for different parts of its domain. For example, f(x) = x when x ≥ 0 and f(x) = -x when x < 0, which is the absolute value function.
what is an inverse function | how do you find an inverse function | inverse of f of x equals 2x plus 3 => An inverse function undoes the original. Swap x and y and solve for y. For y = 2x + 3: x = 2y + 3, so y = (x - 3)/2.
what is a composite function | f of g of x | how do you compose functions => A composite function puts one function inside another. If f(x) = x + 1 and g(x) = 2x, then f(g(x)) = 2x + 1 and g(f(x)) = 2x + 2.
how do you solve a system of equations by graphing | graphing systems of equations | solve two equations with a graph => Graph both lines. The point where they cross is the solution. If they're parallel, there's no solution; if they're the same line, there are infinitely many.
how do you solve a system by substitution | substitution method | solving systems with substitution => Solve one equation for a variable and plug it into the other. If y = x + 1 and 2x + y = 10, then 2x + (x + 1) = 10, so x = 3 and y = 4.
how do you solve a system by elimination | elimination method | solving systems with elimination => Add or subtract the equations so one variable cancels. x + y = 10 and x - y = 4 add to 2x = 14, so x = 7 and y = 3.
how do you multiply binomials | foil method | how to multiply x plus 2 times x plus 3 => Multiply every term in the first by every term in the second (FOIL: First, Outer, Inner, Last). (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6.
how do you square a binomial | x plus 3 squared | what is a plus b squared => (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b², not a² + b². So (x + 3)² = x² + 6x + 9.
what is the difference of squares | difference of two squares formula | factor x squared minus 9 => The difference of squares rule is a² - b² = (a + b)(a - b). So x² - 9 = (x + 3)(x - 3), and 4x² - 25 = (2x + 5)(2x - 5).
how do you factor out the gcf | factoring out a common factor | how to factor 6x plus 9 => Find the biggest factor every term shares and pull it out front. 6x + 9 = 3(2x + 3), and 4x² + 8x = 4x(x + 2).
how do you factor a trinomial | factoring x squared plus bx plus c | how to factor x squared plus 7x plus 12 => Find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b. For x² + 7x + 12, 3 and 4 multiply to 12 and add to 7, so it's (x + 3)(x + 4).
how do you factor when a is not 1 | factoring 2x squared plus 7x plus 3 | ac method factoring => Use the ac method. For 2x² + 7x + 3, ac = 6; find numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 7 (1 and 6). Split: 2x² + x + 6x + 3 = x(2x + 1) + 3(2x + 1) = (x + 3)(2x + 1).
how do you factor by grouping | factoring by grouping | factor four terms => Group the terms in pairs and factor each pair, then pull out the shared bracket. x³ + 2x² + 3x + 6 = x²(x + 2) + 3(x + 2) = (x² + 3)(x + 2).
how do you add and subtract polynomials | adding polynomials | subtracting polynomials => Combine like terms. For subtraction, change the sign of every term in the second polynomial first. (3x² + 2x) - (x² - 5x) = 2x² + 7x.
how do you multiply polynomials | multiplying polynomials | how to multiply a binomial by a trinomial => Multiply each term of the first by each term of the second, then combine like terms. (x + 1)(x² + 2x + 3) = x³ + 2x² + 3x + x² + 2x + 3 = x³ + 3x² + 5x + 3.
how do you solve a quadratic by factoring | solving quadratics by factoring | zero product property => Get 0 on one side, factor, then set each factor to 0. x² + 5x + 6 = 0 → (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0 → x = -2 or x = -3.
what is the quadratic formula | how do you use the quadratic formula | quadratic formula equation => For ax² + bx + c = 0, x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / (2a). For x² - 3x - 4 = 0: a = 1, b = -3, c = -4, so x = (3 ± √25)/2, giving x = 4 or x = -1.
what is the discriminant | how many solutions does a quadratic have | b squared minus 4ac => The discriminant is b² - 4ac. If it's positive, there are two real solutions; if it's 0, there's one; if it's negative, there are no real solutions.
what is completing the square | how do you complete the square | completing the square example => Make the left side a perfect square. For x² + 6x = 7, add (6/2)² = 9 to both sides: x² + 6x + 9 = 16, so (x + 3)² = 16, x + 3 = ±4, and x = 1 or x = -7.
how do you solve x squared equals a number | solving x squared equals 25 | square root both sides => Take the square root of both sides and remember plus or minus. x² = 25 gives x = 5 or x = -5.
how do you find the vertex of a parabola | vertex formula | how to find the vertex of y equals x squared minus 4x plus 3 => The x-coordinate is -b/(2a); plug it in to get y. For y = x² - 4x + 3: x = 4/2 = 2, and y = 4 - 8 + 3 = -1, so the vertex is (2, -1).
what is vertex form | vertex form of a quadratic | y equals a x minus h squared plus k => Vertex form is y = a(x - h)² + k, where (h, k) is the vertex. y = 2(x - 3)² + 1 has its vertex at (3, 1) and opens up because a is positive.
how do you know if a parabola opens up or down | which way does a parabola open | positive vs negative a in a quadratic => Look at a, the number in front of x². If a is positive, it opens up like a smile; if a is negative, it opens down like a frown.
what are the roots of a quadratic | roots zeros and x intercepts | what are zeros of a function => The roots (also called zeros or solutions) are the x values that make the quadratic equal 0. On a graph they're where the parabola crosses the x-axis.
how do you find the nth term of an arithmetic sequence | arithmetic sequence formula | explicit formula arithmetic sequence => Use aₙ = a₁ + (n - 1)d, where d is the common difference. For 5, 8, 11, ..., the 10th term is 5 + 9 × 3 = 32.
how do you find the nth term of a geometric sequence | geometric sequence formula | explicit formula geometric sequence => Use aₙ = a₁ × r⁽ⁿ⁻¹⁾, where r is the common ratio. For 3, 6, 12, ..., the 6th term is 3 × 2⁵ = 96.
how do you find the common difference | common difference in a sequence | how to find d in an arithmetic sequence => Subtract any term from the next one. In 7, 11, 15, 19, the common difference is 11 - 7 = 4.
how do you find the common ratio | common ratio in a sequence | how to find r in a geometric sequence => Divide any term by the one before it. In 2, 10, 50, 250, the common ratio is 10 ÷ 2 = 5.
how do you find the next number in a pattern | number pattern rules | how to figure out a number sequence => Check the differences between terms first. If they're the same, add that amount; if not, check for multiplying, squares (1, 4, 9, 16) or adding the two before (Fibonacci).
how do you find the sum of an arithmetic series | sum of 1 to 100 | gauss trick adding numbers => Add the first and last terms, multiply by how many terms there are, and halve it. 1 + 2 + ... + 100 = (1 + 100) × 100 ÷ 2 = 5,050. Gauss is said to have found this trick as a schoolboy.
what is a recursive formula | recursive vs explicit formula | define recursive sequence => A recursive formula gives each term from the one before, like aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + 3 with a₁ = 2. An explicit formula gives any term directly, like aₙ = 3n - 1.
how do you simplify a rational expression | simplifying algebraic fractions | cancel factors in a fraction => Factor the top and bottom, then cancel common factors (not terms). (x² - 4)/(x + 2) = (x + 2)(x - 2)/(x + 2) = x - 2, for x not equal to -2.
can you cancel terms in a fraction | why cant you cancel the x in x plus 2 over x | common algebra mistakes with fractions => Only cancel factors that multiply the whole top and bottom. In (x + 2)/x you can't cancel the x, because it's added on top, not multiplied. You can cancel in 2x/x = 2.
what are common algebra mistakes | algebra mistakes to avoid | why do i keep getting algebra wrong => Watch for: forgetting to distribute to every term, dropping negative signs, thinking (a + b)² is a² + b², not flipping the inequality when dividing by a negative, and doing different things to each side.
what is a solution to a system of inequalities | graphing systems of inequalities | shading inequalities on a graph => Graph each inequality, shading the side that works (dashed line for < or >, solid for ≤ or ≥). The answer is the region where all the shadings overlap.
how do you graph a linear inequality | graph y greater than 2x plus 1 | which side to shade => Graph the line (dashed for < or >, solid for ≤ or ≥), then test a point like (0, 0). If it makes the inequality true, shade its side; if not, shade the other side.
what is a function table | input output rule | how to find the rule of a function table => A function table lists inputs and outputs. To find the rule, see how each output relates to its input: if 1 → 5, 2 → 7, 3 → 9, the rule is "multiply by 2, then add 3", or y = 2x + 3.
what is rate of change | how do you find rate of change | average rate of change => Rate of change is how much the output changes per unit of input, the same as slope for a line. If a plant grows from 4 cm to 16 cm in 6 days, the rate is 12 ÷ 6 = 2 cm per day.
what is the difference between an equation and a function | equation vs function | is every equation a function => A function is a rule with exactly one output for each input. Many equations, like y = 3x + 2, are functions, but some aren't: x² + y² = 25 is a circle and gives two y values for most x.
how do you solve a proportion with a variable | solving proportions with x | x over 6 equals 10 over 15 => Cross-multiply and divide. x/6 = 10/15 → 15x = 60 → x = 4.
what is a radical equation | how do you solve a radical equation | solve square root of x plus 3 equals 5 => Get the root alone, then square both sides. √(x + 3) = 5 → x + 3 = 25 → x = 22. Always check, because squaring can create false answers.
how do you solve an exponential equation | solving exponential equations | 2 to the x equals 32 => Write both sides with the same base and set the exponents equal. 2ˣ = 32 = 2⁵, so x = 5. If you can't match bases, use logarithms.
define zero product property | why set each factor equal to zero => If two things multiply to 0, at least one of them must be 0. That's why (x - 4)(x + 1) = 0 means x = 4 or x = -1.
what is slope used for in real life | real life examples of slope | slope in the real world => Slope describes rates like speed (miles per hour), cost per item, the steepness of a ramp or roof, and the grade of a road.
what are the properties of equality | addition property of equality | do the same thing to both sides => Whatever you do to one side of an equation, you must do to the other: add, subtract, multiply or divide (but not by 0) by the same amount, and it stays balanced.
what is the commutative property | commutative property of addition | does order matter in multiplication => The commutative property says order doesn't matter for adding or multiplying: 3 + 5 = 5 + 3 and 4 × 7 = 7 × 4. It doesn't work for subtraction or division.
what is the associative property | associative property of multiplication | does grouping matter in addition => The associative property says grouping doesn't matter for adding or multiplying: (2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4) and (2 × 3) × 4 = 2 × (3 × 4).
what is the identity property | additive identity | multiplicative identity => Adding 0 or multiplying by 1 leaves a number unchanged: 9 + 0 = 9 and 9 × 1 = 9. So 0 is the additive identity and 1 is the multiplicative identity.
what is the zero property of multiplication | anything times zero | is anything times 0 zero => Any number multiplied by 0 equals 0. 527 × 0 = 0.
what is the inverse property | multiplicative inverse property | additive inverse and multiplicative inverse => A number plus its opposite is 0 (5 + -5 = 0), and a nonzero number times its reciprocal is 1 (5 × 1/5 = 1).
what is the transitive property | transitive property of equality | if a equals b and b equals c => The transitive property says if a = b and b = c, then a = c. If Sam's height equals Ali's, and Ali's equals Jo's, then Sam and Jo are the same height.
what is the reflexive property | symmetric property of equality | reflexive and symmetric properties => The reflexive property says a = a. The symmetric property says if a = b, then b = a. They're basic rules used in proofs.
what is the substitution property | substitution property of equality | what does substitute mean in math => If two things are equal, you can swap one for the other. If x = 4, then x + 3 can be written as 4 + 3.
