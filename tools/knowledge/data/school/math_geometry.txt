# Geometry formulas and methods with tiny worked examples. Written by hand.
# tag: school.math.area
how do you find the area of a square | area of a square formula | square area => Multiply the side length by itself: A = s². A square with 6 cm sides has an area of 36 cm².
rectangle area formula | how to work out the area of a rectangle => Multiply length by width: A = l × w. A 8 m by 3 m rectangle has an area of 24 m².
how do you find the area of a parallelogram | parallelogram area formula | area of a parallelogram => Multiply the base by the perpendicular height: A = b × h. Use the straight-up height, not the slanted side. Base 9 and height 4 give an area of 36.
how do you find the area of a trapezoid | trapezoid area formula | area of a trapezium => Add the two parallel sides, multiply by the height and halve it: A = ½(a + b)h. Parallel sides 5 and 9 with height 4 give ½ × 14 × 4 = 28.
how do you find the area of a rhombus | rhombus area formula | area of a rhombus using diagonals => Multiply the diagonals and halve it: A = (d₁ × d₂)/2. Diagonals of 6 and 8 give an area of 24. You can also use base × height.
how do you find the area of a kite | kite area formula | area of a kite => Multiply the two diagonals and halve it: A = (d₁ × d₂)/2. Diagonals of 10 and 7 give an area of 35.
how do you find the area of a semicircle | semicircle area formula | area of half a circle => Find the area of the whole circle and halve it: A = ½πr². A semicircle with radius 4 has an area of 8π, about 25.1.
how do you find the area of a sector | sector area formula | area of a slice of a circle => A = (angle/360) × πr². A 90° sector of a circle with radius 6 has area ¼ × 36π = 9π, about 28.3.
how do you find the area of a regular polygon | regular polygon area formula | apothem area formula => A = ½ × perimeter × apothem, where the apothem is the distance from the center to the middle of a side. A regular hexagon with perimeter 36 and apothem 5.2 has an area of about 93.6.
how do you find the area of a regular hexagon | hexagon area formula | area of a hexagon with side length => For a regular hexagon with side s, A = (3√3/2)s², about 2.598s². With 4 cm sides, the area is about 41.6 cm².
how do you find the area of an equilateral triangle | equilateral triangle area formula | area of a triangle with all sides equal => A = (√3/4)s², about 0.433 × s². An equilateral triangle with 10 cm sides has an area of about 43.3 cm².
how do you find the area of a triangle with three sides | herons formula | triangle area without height => Use Heron's formula. Find s = half the perimeter, then A = √(s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)). For sides 5, 6, 7: s = 9, so A = √(9 × 4 × 3 × 2) = √216, about 14.7.
how do you find the area of a compound shape | area of composite figures | area of an l shape => Split it into simple shapes like rectangles and triangles, find each area, then add them (or subtract any cut-out parts). An L shape can be two rectangles.
how do you find the area of a shaded region | shaded area problems | area of the shaded part => Find the area of the whole shape and subtract the area of the unshaded part. A 10 by 10 square with a circle of radius 3 cut out leaves 100 - 9π, about 71.7.
how do you find the area of an ellipse | ellipse area formula | area of an oval => A = πab, where a and b are half the longest and shortest widths. An ellipse 10 wide and 6 tall has a = 5 and b = 3, so A = 15π, about 47.1.
how do you find the area of a triangle on a grid | counting squares for area | estimate area by counting squares => Count the whole squares inside, then add up the part squares (two halves make one). For a triangle, you can also draw a rectangle around it and take half.
what is the difference between area and perimeter | area vs perimeter | when do you use area or perimeter => Perimeter is the distance around a shape, measured in units like cm. Area is the space inside, measured in square units like cm². Fencing a yard uses perimeter; carpeting a room uses area.
why is area measured in square units | what does cm squared mean | why is area squared => Area counts how many unit squares fit inside a shape. A 3 cm by 4 cm rectangle holds 12 squares that are each 1 cm by 1 cm, so its area is 12 cm².
# tag: school.math.perimeter
how do you find the perimeter of a rectangle | rectangle perimeter formula | perimeter of a rectangle => Add all four sides, or use P = 2(l + w). A 7 by 3 rectangle has a perimeter of 2 × 10 = 20.
how do you find the perimeter of a square | square perimeter formula | perimeter of a square => Multiply one side by 4: P = 4s. A square with 9 cm sides has a perimeter of 36 cm.
how do you find the perimeter of a triangle | triangle perimeter | perimeter of a triangle => Add the three side lengths. A triangle with sides 3, 4 and 5 has a perimeter of 12.
how do you find the circumference of a circle | circumference formula | how to work out circumference => C = πd or C = 2πr. A circle with diameter 10 cm has a circumference of 10π, about 31.4 cm.
how do you find the perimeter of a semicircle | semicircle perimeter | perimeter of half a circle => Add half the circumference to the diameter: P = πr + 2r. With radius 5, that's 5π + 10, about 25.7.
how do you find the length of an arc | arc length formula | how long is an arc => Arc length = (angle/360) × 2πr. A 60° arc on a circle with radius 9 is (1/6) × 18π = 3π, about 9.42.
how do you find the radius from the circumference | radius from circumference | find diameter from circumference => Divide the circumference by 2π to get the radius (or by π for the diameter). If C = 31.4, then r = 31.4 ÷ 6.28 = 5.
how do you find the radius from the area | radius from area of a circle | find radius when you know area => Divide the area by π, then take the square root. If A = 50.24, then r² = 50.24 ÷ 3.14 = 16, so r = 4.
how do you find the perimeter of a compound shape | perimeter of composite figures | perimeter of an l shape => Add up the lengths of all the outside edges only. Work out any missing sides from the ones you know, like a missing side = total width minus the known part.
how do you find a missing side from the perimeter | missing side length perimeter | rectangle perimeter find the width => Subtract the known sides from the perimeter. A rectangle with perimeter 30 and length 9: 2 × 9 = 18, 30 - 18 = 12, so each width is 6.
# tag: school.math.volume
how do you find the volume of a cube | cube volume formula | volume of a cube => Cube the side length: V = s³. A cube with 4 cm sides has a volume of 64 cm³.
how do you find the volume of a rectangular prism | volume of a box | rectangular prism volume formula => Multiply length × width × height: V = lwh. A box 5 by 3 by 2 has a volume of 30 cubic units.
how do you find the volume of a prism | prism volume formula | volume of a triangular prism => Multiply the area of the base by the length (or height): V = B × h. A triangular prism with a base triangle of area 6 cm² and length 10 cm has a volume of 60 cm³.
how do you find the volume of a cylinder | cylinder volume formula | volume of a can => V = πr²h. A cylinder with radius 3 cm and height 10 cm has a volume of 90π, about 283 cm³.
how do you find the volume of a cone | cone volume formula | volume of a cone => V = ⅓πr²h, a third of the matching cylinder. A cone with radius 3 and height 4 has a volume of ⅓ × 9π × 4 = 12π, about 37.7.
how do you find the volume of a sphere | sphere volume formula | volume of a ball => V = (4/3)πr³. A sphere with radius 3 cm has a volume of (4/3) × 27π = 36π, about 113 cm³.
how do you find the volume of a pyramid | pyramid volume formula | volume of a square pyramid => V = ⅓ × base area × height. A square pyramid with a 6 by 6 base and height 5 has a volume of ⅓ × 36 × 5 = 60.
how do you find the volume of a hemisphere | hemisphere volume formula | volume of half a sphere => V = (2/3)πr³, half of a sphere. With radius 3, it's (2/3) × 27π = 18π, about 56.5.
why is a cone a third of a cylinder | cone vs cylinder volume | why is there a third in the cone formula => A cone holds exactly one third of a cylinder with the same base and height. You can check it by filling a cone with water and pouring it into the matching cylinder: it takes three cones.
how do you find the volume of a compound shape | volume of composite solids | volume of combined shapes => Split the solid into simple shapes, find each volume and add them. A cylinder with a cone on top: πr²h for the cylinder plus ⅓πr²h for the cone.
how do you find the height from the volume | missing dimension volume | find the height of a box from volume => Divide the volume by the area of the base. A box with volume 120 cm³ and a 6 by 5 base has height 120 ÷ 30 = 4 cm.
how many cubic centimeters are in a liter | ml to cm3 | is a milliliter the same as a cubic centimeter => 1 milliliter equals 1 cubic centimeter, so 1 liter is 1,000 cm³. A cube 10 cm on each side holds exactly 1 liter.
what is the difference between volume and capacity | volume vs capacity | capacity meaning in math => Volume is how much space an object takes up, often in cubic units. Capacity is how much a container can hold, often in liters, milliliters or gallons.
# tag: school.math.surface-area
how do you find the surface area of a cube | cube surface area formula | surface area of a cube => Find the area of one face and multiply by 6: SA = 6s². A cube with 3 cm sides has a surface area of 6 × 9 = 54 cm².
how do you find the surface area of a rectangular prism | surface area of a box | rectangular prism surface area formula => SA = 2(lw + lh + wh). A box 4 by 3 by 2 has SA = 2(12 + 8 + 6) = 52 square units.
how do you find the surface area of a cylinder | cylinder surface area formula | surface area of a can => SA = 2πr² + 2πrh: two circles plus the curved side. With r = 2 and h = 5: 8π + 20π = 28π, about 88.
how do you find the surface area of a sphere | sphere surface area formula | surface area of a ball => SA = 4πr². A sphere with radius 5 has a surface area of 100π, about 314.
how do you find the surface area of a cone | cone surface area formula | surface area of a cone with slant height => SA = πr² + πrl, where l is the slant height. With r = 3 and l = 5: 9π + 15π = 24π, about 75.4.
how do you find the surface area of a pyramid | square pyramid surface area | surface area of a pyramid => Add the base area to the areas of the triangle faces. A square pyramid with base 6 and slant height 5 has 36 + 4 × (½ × 6 × 5) = 36 + 60 = 96.
how do you find the surface area of a triangular prism | triangular prism surface area | surface area of a prism => Add the areas of the two triangle ends and the three rectangle sides. Using the net helps you see every face.
how do you use a net to find surface area | surface area from a net | why use a net for surface area => Unfold the solid into a flat net, find the area of each flat shape, and add them all. It makes sure you don't miss or double-count any face.
what is the difference between surface area and volume | surface area vs volume | volume or surface area which one => Surface area is the total area of the outside (square units), like the paint needed to cover a box. Volume is the space inside (cubic units), like how much the box holds.
what is slant height | slant height vs height | how do you find slant height => Slant height is the distance up the sloped face of a cone or pyramid, while the height goes straight up through the middle. For a cone, l = √(r² + h²).
# tag: school.math.angles
how do you measure an angle with a protractor | how to use a protractor | reading a protractor => Put the protractor's center hole on the vertex and line the zero line up with one arm. Read where the other arm crosses the scale, using the scale that starts at 0 on your first arm.
how do you draw an angle with a protractor | drawing angles | how to draw a 65 degree angle => Draw a line and mark the vertex. Put the protractor's center on the vertex with the zero line on your line, mark a dot at 65°, then join the vertex to the dot.
how do you find a missing angle in a triangle | missing angle triangle | find the third angle of a triangle => Subtract the two angles you know from 180°. If two angles are 50° and 70°, the third is 180 - 120 = 60°.
how do you find a missing angle on a straight line | angles on a straight line | angles that make a line add up to => Angles on a straight line add up to 180°. If one is 130°, the other is 50°.
how many degrees are around a point | angles around a point add up to | angles at a point => Angles all the way around a point add up to 360°. If three angles around a point are 100°, 120° and 90°, the last one is 50°.
what is the exterior angle theorem | exterior angle of a triangle | triangle exterior angle rule => An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles. If the inside angles are 40° and 75°, the exterior angle at the third corner is 115°.
how do you find the sum of interior angles of a polygon | interior angle sum formula | angles in a polygon formula => Use (n - 2) × 180°, where n is the number of sides. A hexagon has (6 - 2) × 180 = 720°.
how do you find one interior angle of a regular polygon | interior angle of a regular polygon formula | each angle of a regular polygon => Divide the angle sum by the number of sides: (n - 2) × 180 ÷ n. A regular octagon has 1080 ÷ 8 = 135° in each corner.
what do the exterior angles of a polygon add up to | sum of exterior angles | exterior angles of any polygon => The exterior angles of any convex polygon add up to 360°, one at each vertex, no matter how many sides it has.
how do you find the number of sides from an angle | how many sides does a regular polygon have if each angle is | sides from exterior angle => Divide 360 by one exterior angle. If each interior angle is 140°, the exterior angle is 40°, so 360 ÷ 40 = 9 sides.
how do you name an angle | angle abc meaning | how to label an angle => Use three points with the vertex in the middle, like ∠ABC, where B is the vertex. You can also use just the vertex (∠B) or a number written inside the angle.
what is an angle bisector | define angle bisector | how do you bisect an angle => An angle bisector is a ray that splits an angle into two equal angles. With a compass, draw an arc across both arms, then arcs from those two points; join the vertex to where they cross.
what is a perpendicular bisector | define perpendicular bisector | how to construct a perpendicular bisector => A perpendicular bisector cuts a segment in half at a right angle. Every point on it is the same distance from both ends of the segment.
what is the angle between clock hands | how to find the angle on a clock | angle at 3 oclock => Each hour mark is 30° apart (360 ÷ 12). At 3:00 the hands are 3 marks apart, so the angle is 90°. The hour hand also moves 0.5° per minute.
# tag: school.math.triangles
what is the triangle inequality theorem | can these three sides make a triangle | triangle inequality rule => Any two sides of a triangle must add up to more than the third side. Sides 3, 4 and 8 can't make a triangle because 3 + 4 is less than 8.
what is the biggest angle in a triangle | which side is opposite the largest angle | largest side and largest angle => The largest angle is always opposite the longest side, and the smallest angle is opposite the shortest side.
can a triangle have two right angles | can a triangle have two obtuse angles | how many right angles can a triangle have => No. The angles add up to 180°, so two right angles would already use it all. A triangle can have at most one right angle or one obtuse angle.
what are the angles of an equilateral triangle | equilateral triangle angles | each angle in an equilateral triangle => Each angle in an equilateral triangle is 60°, since all three are equal and 180 ÷ 3 = 60.
how do you find the angles of an isosceles triangle | isosceles triangle angles | base angles of an isosceles triangle => The two base angles are equal. If the top angle is 40°, the base angles share 140°, so each is 70°.
what are congruent triangles | how do you prove triangles are congruent | sss sas asa aas => Congruent triangles are identical in size and shape. You can prove it with SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and the angle between), ASA or AAS (two angles and a side), or HL for right triangles.
what are similar triangles | how do you prove triangles are similar | aa similarity => Similar triangles have equal angles and sides in the same ratio. Showing two pairs of equal angles (AA) is enough, or use SSS or SAS similarity with proportional sides.
how do you use similar triangles to find a missing side | similar triangles missing side | proportions with similar triangles => Set up a proportion with matching sides. If a 3-4-5 triangle is similar to one with shortest side 6, the scale factor is 2, so the other sides are 8 and 10.
how do you find the height of a tree with shadows | shadow problem similar triangles | measure a tall object using its shadow => Use similar triangles. If a 2 m stick casts a 3 m shadow and the tree casts a 15 m shadow at the same time, the tree is 2 × (15 ÷ 3) = 10 m tall.
what is the midpoint of a triangle side | what is a median of a triangle | define median triangle => A median of a triangle joins a corner to the midpoint of the opposite side. The three medians meet at the centroid, the triangle's balance point.
what is an altitude of a triangle | define altitude geometry | triangle height altitude => An altitude is a line from a corner that meets the opposite side (or its extension) at a right angle. Its length is the triangle's height for that base.
what are the special right triangles | 30 60 90 triangle | 45 45 90 triangle => In a 45-45-90 triangle the sides are in the ratio 1 : 1 : √2. In a 30-60-90 triangle they're 1 : √3 : 2, with the shortest side opposite 30°.
# tag: school.math.pythagoras
how do you use the pythagorean theorem | pythagorean theorem example | how to find the hypotenuse => In a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse. For legs 6 and 8: 36 + 64 = 100, so c = √100 = 10.
how do you find a missing leg with pythagoras | find a short side using pythagorean theorem | missing side of a right triangle => Subtract instead of add: a² = c² - b². With hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5: 169 - 25 = 144, so the other leg is 12.
what is a pythagorean triple | pythagorean triples list | common right triangle side lengths => A Pythagorean triple is three whole numbers that fit a² + b² = c². Common ones are 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17 and 7-24-25, plus multiples like 6-8-10.
how do you know if a triangle is a right triangle | converse of the pythagorean theorem | check if a triangle is right angled => Square the sides. If the two smaller squares add up to the biggest square, it's a right triangle. 9, 12, 15: 81 + 144 = 225 = 15², so yes.
how do you find the distance between two points | distance formula | distance between two points on a graph => Use d = √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²), which is Pythagoras on a grid. From (1, 2) to (4, 6): √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.
how do you find the midpoint of a line segment | midpoint formula | midpoint between two points => Average the x values and average the y values: ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2). The midpoint of (2, 4) and (8, 10) is (5, 7).
how do you find the diagonal of a rectangle | diagonal of a rectangle formula | length of a rectangle diagonal => Use Pythagoras: d = √(l² + w²). A 9 by 12 rectangle has a diagonal of √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15.
how do you find the diagonal of a square | diagonal of a square formula | square diagonal length => The diagonal is the side times √2, about 1.414 × s. A square with 10 cm sides has a diagonal of about 14.1 cm.
how do you find the diagonal of a box | space diagonal of a rectangular prism | 3d pythagorean theorem => Use d = √(l² + w² + h²). A box 3 by 4 by 12 has a diagonal of √(9 + 16 + 144) = √169 = 13.
who discovered the pythagorean theorem | was pythagoras the first to know the theorem | history of the pythagorean theorem => It's named after the Greek thinker Pythagoras (around 570 to 495 BC), but the Babylonians knew and used the rule more than 1,000 years earlier, and it was also known in ancient India and China.
# tag: school.math.circles
what is pi exactly | why is pi 3.14 | what does pi mean => Pi (π) is the circumference of any circle divided by its diameter, about 3.14159. It's irrational, so its digits never end or repeat. 22/7 is a handy rough fraction for it.
how many digits of pi are there | does pi ever end | how many digits of pi do we know => Pi never ends. Computers have worked out trillions of digits, but you only need a few for most math: 3.14 or 3.1416 is plenty for school.
what are the first digits of pi | pi to 10 decimal places => Pi to 10 decimal places is 3.1415926535.
how do you find the diameter from the radius | radius to diameter | diameter from radius => Double the radius: d = 2r. A radius of 7 cm means a diameter of 14 cm.
what is an inscribed angle | inscribed angle theorem | angle at the circumference => An inscribed angle has its vertex on the circle. It's half the central angle that cuts off the same arc, so an arc with a 100° central angle has a 50° inscribed angle.
what is a central angle | define central angle | central angle of a circle => A central angle has its vertex at the center of a circle and its arms are radii. It has the same measure as the arc it cuts off.
what is the angle in a semicircle | angle in a semicircle theorem | thales theorem => Any angle drawn from the ends of a diameter to a point on the circle is a right angle (90°). This is called Thales' theorem.
what is the equation of a circle | circle equation on a graph | x squared plus y squared equals r squared => A circle with center (h, k) and radius r is (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r². A circle centered at the origin with radius 5 is x² + y² = 25.
what are concentric circles | define concentric circles | concentric meaning => Concentric circles share the same center but have different radii, like the rings of a target.
what is a cyclic quadrilateral | opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral | quadrilateral in a circle => A cyclic quadrilateral has all four corners on a circle. Its opposite angles add up to 180°.
# tag: school.math.transformations
how do you reflect a point over the x axis | reflection across the x axis rule | reflect over x axis => Keep x and change the sign of y: (x, y) → (x, -y). The point (3, 5) becomes (3, -5).
how do you reflect a point over the y axis | reflection across the y axis rule | reflect over y axis => Change the sign of x and keep y: (x, y) → (-x, y). The point (3, 5) becomes (-3, 5).
how do you reflect over the line y equals x | reflection over y equals x rule | swap x and y reflection => Swap the coordinates: (x, y) → (y, x). The point (2, 7) becomes (7, 2).
how do you rotate a point 90 degrees | 90 degree rotation rule | rotate 90 degrees counterclockwise about the origin => Rotating 90° counterclockwise about the origin sends (x, y) to (-y, x). The point (2, 5) goes to (-5, 2). Clockwise 90° sends (x, y) to (y, -x).
how do you rotate a point 180 degrees | 180 degree rotation rule | rotate 180 about the origin => Change the signs of both coordinates: (x, y) → (-x, -y). The point (4, -3) goes to (-4, 3).
how do you rotate a point 270 degrees | 270 degree rotation rule | rotate 270 counterclockwise => Rotating 270° counterclockwise (same as 90° clockwise) about the origin sends (x, y) to (y, -x). The point (2, 5) goes to (5, -2).
how do you translate a shape on a graph | translation rule coordinates | translate a point left and up => Add to x to move right (subtract to move left) and add to y to move up (subtract to move down). Moving (2, 3) 4 left and 1 up gives (-2, 4).
how do you dilate a shape | dilation on a coordinate plane | dilate by a scale factor from the origin => Multiply both coordinates by the scale factor (when the center is the origin). A dilation by 3 sends (2, -1) to (6, -3).
which transformations keep the same size | rigid transformations | which transformation changes size => Translations, reflections and rotations are rigid: the shape stays the same size and shape (congruent). Dilations change the size, making a similar shape.
what does prime mean in geometry | what does a prime after a letter mean | a prime b prime => A prime mark (') labels the new shape after a transformation. If triangle ABC is moved, the image is called A'B'C', read "A prime, B prime, C prime".
what is a glide reflection | define glide reflection | glide reflection example => A glide reflection is a translation followed by a reflection over a line parallel to the slide, like footprints in the sand.
# tag: school.math.coordinates
how do you plot a point on a graph | plotting coordinates | how to plot 3 comma 4 => Start at the origin, move along the x-axis by the first number (right for positive), then up or down by the second number. For (3, 4), go 3 right and 4 up.
how do you remember which coordinate comes first | x or y first | along the corridor and up the stairs => The x coordinate always comes first. A common trick: "along the corridor, then up the stairs," so you go across first, then up or down.
how do you find the area of a shape on a coordinate grid | area from coordinates | area of a polygon on a graph => Draw the shape, find side lengths by counting or by subtracting coordinates, then use the usual area formula. For awkward shapes, box them in a rectangle and subtract the corner triangles.
what is the shoelace formula | area from vertices formula | shoelace method => The shoelace formula finds a polygon's area from its corner coordinates: list the points in order, cross-multiply x of each with y of the next, subtract the other way, add up and halve the absolute value.
# tag: school.math.solids
how many faces edges and vertices does a cylinder have | cylinder faces and edges | does a cylinder have vertices => A cylinder has 2 flat circular faces and 1 curved surface, 2 curved edges and 0 vertices. Some teachers count only the 2 flat faces as faces.
how many faces edges and vertices does a cone have | cone faces and edges | does a cone have a vertex => A cone has 1 flat circular face and 1 curved surface, 1 curved edge and 1 apex (the point). Whether the apex counts as a vertex depends on the textbook, so check how your class counts it.
how many faces edges and vertices does a sphere have | does a sphere have faces | sphere edges => A sphere has 1 curved surface and no flat faces, no edges and no vertices.
what is eulers formula for shapes | euler's formula f plus v minus e | faces plus vertices minus edges => For any polyhedron without holes, Faces + Vertices - Edges = 2. A cube has 6 + 8 - 12 = 2.
what is the difference between a prism and a pyramid | prism vs pyramid | how to tell a prism from a pyramid => A prism has two matching bases joined by rectangles, so it's the same shape all the way through. A pyramid has one base and triangle sides that meet at a point.
what shapes make up a cube net | how many nets does a cube have | different cube nets => A cube's net is 6 squares joined edge to edge. There are 11 different nets that fold into a cube.
# tag: school.math.measurement
how many degrees in a right angle | how big is a right angle | right angle degrees => A right angle is exactly 90 degrees, a quarter of a full turn. It's marked with a small square in the corner.
what are square units | what does units squared mean | square units meaning => Square units measure area. 1 square meter (m²) is the area of a square 1 meter on each side.
what are cubic units | what does units cubed mean | cubic units meaning => Cubic units measure volume. 1 cubic centimeter (cm³) is the space inside a cube 1 cm on each side.
how many square feet in a square yard | square yard to square feet | why is a square yard 9 square feet => There are 9 square feet in a square yard, because a yard is 3 feet and 3 × 3 = 9. Area conversions square the length conversion.
how many square centimeters in a square meter | square meter to square cm | why is a square meter 10000 square centimeters => There are 10,000 cm² in 1 m², because 1 m = 100 cm and 100 × 100 = 10,000.
what is the formula sheet for area and volume | list of area and volume formulas | geometry formulas cheat sheet => Rectangle lw, triangle ½bh, circle πr², trapezoid ½(a + b)h. Box lwh, prism (base area) × h, cylinder πr²h, cone ⅓πr²h, pyramid ⅓(base area)h, sphere (4/3)πr³.
