# More math terms (grades 4 to 10) and short how-tos. Written by hand.
# tag: school.math.terms
what is a factor pair | factor pairs meaning | how do you find factor pairs => A factor pair is two numbers that multiply to make a given number. The factor pairs of 12 are 1 × 12, 2 × 6 and 3 × 4. Test each number from 1 up until the pairs repeat.
what is an apex in math | apex of a cone | apex of a pyramid => The apex is the top point of a cone or pyramid, where the sides meet, farthest from the base.
what is a face of a 3d shape | define face in geometry | faces of a solid => A face is a flat surface of a 3D shape. A cube has 6 square faces. Curved surfaces, like on a cylinder, are usually not called faces.
what is the base of a 3d shape | base of a prism | base of a cylinder => The base is the face a solid is named after or stands on. A prism has two matching bases; a pyramid or cone has one.
what is lateral area | lateral surface area | lateral faces => Lateral area is the area of the sides of a 3D shape, not counting the bases. For a cylinder it's 2πrh, the curved surface only.
what is an interior angle | exterior angle | interior vs exterior angle => An interior angle is inside a polygon at a corner. An exterior angle is between one side and the extension of the next side; at each corner, the two add to 180°.
what is a midpoint | define midpoint => The midpoint is the point exactly halfway along a line segment, splitting it into two equal parts.
what is a bisector | define bisect | what does bisect mean in math => To bisect means to cut into two equal parts. A bisector is a line, ray or segment that does that to a segment or an angle.
what are congruent angles | congruent sides | tick marks on a shape meaning => Congruent angles have the same measure, and congruent sides have the same length. In diagrams, matching tick marks show equal sides and matching arcs show equal angles.
what is a right angle symbol | small square in an angle | box in the corner of an angle => A small square drawn in a corner shows it's a right angle, exactly 90°.
define coordinates | what are coordinates => Coordinates are numbers that give a point's position. On a graph, (3, 5) means 3 along the x-axis and 5 up the y-axis.
interval scale | what is an interval in math => An interval is the equal step between numbers on a graph's scale, like counting by 2s or 10s. It can also mean all numbers between two values, like 3 to 7.
what does evaluate mean in math | define evaluate math | evaluate meaning => To evaluate means to find the value of an expression by substituting numbers for the variables and calculating. If x = 4, 2x + 1 evaluates to 9.
what does simplify mean in math | define simplify math | simplify meaning => To simplify means to write something in its simplest form: combine like terms, reduce fractions, or do the arithmetic you can. 2x + 3x simplifies to 5x.
what does solve mean in math | define solve | solving for x => To solve means to find the value of the variable that makes an equation true. Solving x + 3 = 10 gives x = 7.
what is a constant term | define constant term | the number without a variable => A constant term is a term with no variable, so its value never changes. In 4x² - x + 9, the constant term is 9.
what is a double number line | double number line diagram | using a double number line => A double number line has two number lines lined up to show equivalent ratios. One line might show cups of flour and the other cookies made.
what is a percent bar | percent bar model | percent tape diagram => A percent bar is a bar from 0% to 100% matched to amounts below it. It helps you see that, for example, 25% of 80 is 20.
what is variability in statistics | spread of data | measures of variability => Variability (spread) describes how much data values differ from each other. Range, interquartile range and standard deviation measure it.
what is a cluster in data | gaps and clusters | peak in a dot plot => A cluster is a group of data values close together. A gap is an empty space where there are no values, and a peak is the most common value or values.
what is a symmetric distribution | symmetric data | bell shaped data => A distribution is symmetric if its left and right halves are mirror images. The mean and median are then about the same.
what is bivariate data | two variable data | bivariate vs univariate => Univariate data has one variable, like test scores. Bivariate data pairs two variables for each item, like height and shoe size, and is shown on a scatter plot.
what is a trial in probability | define trial | trials and outcomes => A trial is one round of an experiment, like one flip of a coin or one roll of a die.
what is a theoretical probability example | equally likely outcomes | probability of a spinner => When outcomes are equally likely, probability = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes. A spinner with 4 equal parts, 1 red, gives P(red) = 1/4.
what is a unit in math | units of measure | what is a unit => A unit is a standard amount used to measure, like a meter, a pound or a second. In counting, a unit can also mean one whole.
what is a metric unit | customary unit | metric vs customary => Metric units (meter, gram, liter) are based on tens and used by most of the world. US customary units (inch, pound, gallon) are used mostly in the United States.
what is the formula for perimeter of any polygon | perimeter of a regular polygon | perimeter of a hexagon => Add the lengths of all the sides. For a regular polygon with n sides of length s, the perimeter is n × s, so a regular hexagon with 4 cm sides has a perimeter of 24 cm.
how do you find volume by counting cubes | volume with unit cubes | counting cubes volume => Count how many unit cubes fill the shape. For a box, count cubes in one layer (length × width) and multiply by the number of layers (height).
how do you classify triangles | ways to classify triangles | types of triangles by sides and angles => By sides: equilateral (all equal), isosceles (two equal) or scalene (none equal). By angles: acute (all under 90°), right (one 90°) or obtuse (one over 90°).
how do you classify quadrilaterals | types of quadrilaterals | how to name a quadrilateral => Check the sides and angles: four right angles and equal sides is a square; four right angles is a rectangle; four equal sides is a rhombus; two pairs of parallel sides is a parallelogram; one pair is a trapezoid.
what is a function rule | find the rule of a function => A function rule tells how to get each output from its input, like "multiply by 3, then subtract 1", written y = 3x - 1.
what is a linear pattern | growing pattern | how do you describe a pattern => A linear pattern grows by the same amount each step, like 4, 7, 10, 13 (add 3). Describe it with a starting value and the amount added.
what is a geometric pattern | multiplying pattern | doubling pattern => A geometric pattern multiplies by the same number each step, like 3, 6, 12, 24 (times 2).
what is a scale drawing | scale factor in a drawing | how do you read a scale => A scale drawing shows an object bigger or smaller than real life. A scale of 1 cm : 5 m means each centimeter on the drawing stands for 5 meters.
what is a unit circle used for | why learn the unit circle | unit circle angles => The unit circle helps find sine and cosine of any angle, since each point is (cos θ, sin θ). It also explains why trig values repeat and can be negative.
what is a number sentence | define number sentence | number sentence examples => A number sentence uses numbers and symbols to show a relationship, like 4 + 5 = 9 or 12 > 7.
balance method | what does balancing an equation mean => An equation is like a balance scale: whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other to keep it equal. That's how you move terms to solve.
does order matter in addition | order of numbers in multiplication => Order doesn't matter in addition or multiplication: 3 + 5 = 5 + 3 and 4 × 6 = 6 × 4. It does matter for subtraction and division.
what is a multiple of 10 | multiples of 10 | multiples of 100 => Multiples of 10 are 10, 20, 30 and so on; they all end in 0. Multiples of 100 end in 00.
what is a mixed decimal | mixed decimal meaning | whole number and decimal => A mixed decimal has a whole number part and a decimal part, like 3.75.
unit price meaning | price per unit => The unit price is the cost of one unit, like price per ounce. Divide the total price by the number of units: $4.80 for 12 ounces is $0.40 per ounce.
what is a tally mark | how to count tally marks | tally marks of 5 => Tally marks are lines used to count, drawn in groups of five: four lines with a fifth line across them. It makes counting by fives easy.
what is an equivalent expression | equivalent expressions examples | how to show expressions are equivalent => Equivalent expressions have the same value for every value of the variable, like 2(x + 3) and 2x + 6. Use properties like the distributive property to show it.
what is an inverse operation used for in equations | undo operations to solve | inverse operations equations => You solve equations by undoing operations: subtract to undo adding, divide to undo multiplying. For 3x + 4 = 19, subtract 4 then divide by 3 to get x = 5.
what is a counterexample used for | prove something false | disprove with an example => One counterexample is enough to show a general statement is false. "All primes are odd" is false because 2 is prime and even.
what is an indirect proof | proof by contradiction | define proof by contradiction => In a proof by contradiction, you assume the opposite of what you want to prove and show it leads to something impossible, so the original statement must be true.
what is a function's input and output | what is an independent variable | dependent variable in math => The input (independent variable, usually x) is what you choose. The output (dependent variable, usually y) depends on it. In "cost depends on the number of tickets", tickets are the input.
what is a nonlinear function | linear vs nonlinear => A linear function has a constant rate of change and a straight-line graph. A nonlinear function, like y = x², curves, so its rate of change varies.
what is an exponential vs quadratic | exponential vs polynomial growth | which grows faster x squared or 2 to the x => Exponential functions like 2ˣ eventually grow much faster than any polynomial like x². By x = 10, x² is 100 but 2ˣ is 1,024.
what is a rational function | define rational function | rational function examples => A rational function is a fraction with polynomials on top and bottom, like 1/x or (x + 1)/(x - 2). It's undefined where the bottom is zero.
what is an asymptote example | horizontal asymptote | vertical asymptote => An asymptote is a line a graph gets closer and closer to. y = 1/x has a vertical asymptote at x = 0 and a horizontal one at y = 0.
