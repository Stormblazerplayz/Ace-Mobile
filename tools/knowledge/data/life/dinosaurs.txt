what are the three periods of the dinosaurs | what is the mesozoic era => The Mesozoic Era, the age of dinosaurs, has three periods: the Triassic (about 252 to 201 million years ago), the Jurassic (201 to 145) and the Cretaceous (145 to 66).

how do scientists know how old fossils are | how are dinosaur bones dated => Scientists date the volcanic ash layers above and below fossils using radioactive elements that decay at a known rate. Fossils in between must be about that age.
what does dinosaur mean | who named the dinosaurs => The word dinosaur means "terrible lizard". British scientist Richard Owen made up the name in 1842.
what was the first dinosaur ever found | first dinosaur to be named => Megalosaurus was the first dinosaur to get a scientific name, in 1824, by William Buckland in England.

Sinosauropteryx was orange-brown with a striped tail, and Anchiornis was black and white with a reddish crest.
