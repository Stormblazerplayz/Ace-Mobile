# tag: prehistoric.reptiles
what is the biggest pterosaur | how big was quetzalcoatlus | biggest flying animal ever => Quetzalcoatlus was one of the biggest flying animals ever, with wings about 10 to 11 m across. Standing on the ground, it was about as tall as a giraffe.
what was pteranodon | did pteranodon have teeth | how big was pteranodon => Pteranodon was a pterosaur with wings about 6 to 7 m across and a long, toothless beak. It flew over seas about 86 to 84 million years ago and ate fish.
what was the first animal to fly | when did pterosaurs appear | first flying vertebrates => Insects flew first, over 300 million years ago. Pterosaurs, which appeared about 228 million years ago, were the first animals with backbones to fly by flapping.
what was a mosasaurus | how big was mosasaurus | was mosasaurus a dinosaur => Mosasaurus was a giant sea reptile, maybe about 12 to 17 m long, related to monitor lizards and snakes. It ruled the seas at the end of the Cretaceous and wasn't a dinosaur.
what is a plesiosaur | did plesiosaurs have long necks | is the loch ness monster a plesiosaur => Plesiosaurs were sea reptiles with four flippers, and many had very long necks. They died out 66 million years ago, so the Loch Ness monster can't be one.
what was an ichthyosaur | did ichthyosaurs look like dolphins | did ichthyosaurs lay eggs => Ichthyosaurs were sea reptiles shaped a lot like dolphins. Fossils of mothers giving birth show they had live babies instead of laying eggs.
what was sarcosuchus | what is supercroc | biggest crocodile ever => Sarcosuchus, nicknamed SuperCroc, lived about 112 million years ago in Africa and was about 9 to 9.5 m long, roughly twice the length of a big saltwater crocodile.
did crocodiles eat dinosaurs | what is deinosuchus | giant crocodile that ate dinosaurs => Deinosuchus was a giant alligator relative about 10 m long that lived in North America around 82 to 73 million years ago. Bite marks on dinosaur bones show it ate them.
what is titanoboa | what was the biggest snake ever | how big was titanoboa => Titanoboa lived about 60 million years ago in what's now Colombia, just after the dinosaurs died out. It was about 13 m long and over 1,000 kg, the biggest snake ever found.

# tag: prehistoric.sea
what was megalodon | how big was megalodon | how big were megalodon teeth => Megalodon was a giant shark that lived from about 23 to 3.6 million years ago. Estimates say it reached about 15 to 20 m long, and its teeth could be about 18 cm.
is megalodon still alive | could megalodon be in the deep ocean | when did megalodon go extinct => No. Megalodon went extinct about 3.6 million years ago. A shark that big would leave bitten whales and teeth everywhere, and none are found from recent times.
what was dunkleosteus | armored fish with a strong bite => Dunkleosteus was a giant armored fish that lived about 360 million years ago. Instead of teeth it had sharp bony plates that sliced like scissors.
what were trilobites | when did trilobites live | how long were trilobites around => Trilobites were sea creatures with hard, segmented shells, related to insects and crabs. They lived for about 270 million years, from about 521 to 252 million years ago, with over 20,000 species.
what were ammonites | what is an ammonite fossil => Ammonites were sea animals with coiled shells, related to octopuses and squid. They were super common and died out with the dinosaurs 66 million years ago.
what was anomalocaris | what was the first big predator => Anomalocaris was one of the first big predators, swimming in seas over 500 million years ago. It was shrimp-like with grabbing arms and grew to around half a metre or more.
what was the cambrian explosion | when did complex animals appear => The Cambrian explosion, starting about 538 million years ago, was when most major animal groups quickly appeared in the fossil record, like early arthropods and the first fish-like animals.
what is a living fossil | examples of living fossils => A living fossil is a living thing that looks a lot like its ancient relatives, like the coelacanth, horseshoe crab, ginkgo tree and nautilus.

# tag: prehistoric.ice-age
when did woolly mammoths go extinct | when did the last mammoth die => Most woolly mammoths died out around 10,000 years ago, but a small group survived on Wrangel Island in the Arctic until about 4,000 years ago, while the Egyptian pyramids already stood.
how big was a woolly mammoth | what did woolly mammoths eat => Woolly mammoths were about the size of African elephants, around 3 to 3.5 m tall. They ate grasses and other tundra plants and had thick fur and small ears to stay warm.
how do we know so much about mammoths | have frozen mammoths been found => Whole mammoths, some with fur, skin and even stomach contents, have been found frozen in Siberian permafrost, plus cave paintings drawn by early humans.
whats the difference between a mammoth and a mastodon | mammoth vs mastodon => Mammoths were grazers with ridged teeth for grass and big curved tusks. Mastodons were browsers with cone-shaped teeth for leaves and twigs, and were shorter and stockier.
what was a saber toothed tiger | how long were saber tooth teeth | was smilodon a tiger => Smilodon, the saber-toothed cat, had canine teeth up to about 18 cm long showing out of its mouth. It wasn't a tiger, just a different big cat, and died out about 10,000 years ago.
what are the la brea tar pits | what was found in the la brea tar pits => The La Brea Tar Pits in Los Angeles trapped animals in sticky asphalt for tens of thousands of years. Millions of fossils have been found, including saber-toothed cats, dire wolves and mammoths.
were dire wolves real | what is a dire wolf => Yes. Dire wolves were real wolf-like animals, bigger and heavier than gray wolves, that lived in the Americas until about 13,000 to 10,000 years ago.
what was a giant ground sloth | how big was megatherium => Megatherium was a giant ground sloth in South America about as big as an elephant, around 6 m long. It could stand on its back legs to reach leaves.
what was a glyptodon | giant armadillo the size of a car => Glyptodon was a relative of armadillos about the size of a small car, with a domed shell made of bony plates. It lived in South America until about 10,000 years ago.
what was the woolly rhino | did rhinos have fur => The woolly rhinoceros had long shaggy fur and lived in cold parts of Europe and Asia during the Ice Age. It died out roughly 14,000 to 10,000 years ago.
what was the biggest land mammal ever | what is paraceratherium => Paraceratherium, a hornless rhino relative, was one of the biggest land mammals ever, around 4.8 m tall at the shoulder and maybe 15 to 20 tonnes. It lived about 34 to 23 million years ago in Asia.
what was the last ice age | when did the ice age end => During the last ice age, huge ice sheets covered much of North America and Europe. The coldest point was about 20,000 years ago, and it ended about 11,700 years ago.
why did ice age animals go extinct | what killed the mammoths => Most scientists think a mix of a warming climate and hunting by humans wiped out many Ice Age giants like mammoths, giant sloths and saber-toothed cats.

# tag: prehistoric.extinct
what was haasts eagle | what is the biggest eagle ever => Haast's eagle of New Zealand was the biggest eagle ever, about 15 kg, and hunted moas. It died out when the moas disappeared, around the 1400s.
what were terror birds | did giant birds hunt mammals => Terror birds were tall, flightless predator birds in South America, some about 3 m tall, with huge hooked beaks for hunting mammals.
what was stellers sea cow | how fast did stellers sea cow go extinct => Steller's sea cow was a giant relative of manatees, up to about 8 to 9 m long. It was discovered in 1741 near Alaska and hunted to extinction by 1768, just 27 years later.
what was the quagga | was there a zebra with half stripes => The quagga was a kind of plains zebra from South Africa with stripes only on its front half. The last one died in an Amsterdam zoo in 1883.
what was the irish elk | how big were irish elk antlers => The Irish elk was a giant deer, not really an elk, with antlers up to about 3.6 m across, the biggest of any deer. It died out roughly 7,700 years ago.
what was arthropleura | was there a giant millipede => Arthropleura was a giant millipede relative about 2.5 m long that lived around 300 million years ago. It was the biggest bug-like animal to ever live on land.
how many animals have gone extinct | what does extinct mean => Extinct means a species has completely died out. Scientists estimate that over 99 percent of all species that ever lived on Earth are now extinct.
what is de extinction | can scientists bring back extinct animals => De-extinction means trying to bring back extinct species, or animals like them, by editing the DNA of living relatives. Some companies are trying with mammoths, but it's very hard and debated.

# tag: prehistoric.earth-history
how old is life on earth | when did life begin => The oldest signs of life are about 3.5 to 3.7 billion years old. Earth itself is about 4.5 billion years old.
what were the first living things | what was the first life on earth => The first living things were tiny single cells, like bacteria. Some built layered rock mounds called stromatolites, which still form in a few places today.
when did the first animals appear | what were the first animals => The first animals appeared over 600 million years ago in the oceans. They were soft-bodied, like sponges and strange flat creatures called Ediacarans.
when did plants first grow on land | first land plants => Simple plants like mosses started growing on land about 470 million years ago. Trees and forests came later, around 385 million years ago.
when did animals first walk on land | what is tiktaalik => Animals with backbones first crawled onto land about 375 million years ago. Tiktaalik was a fish with sturdy fins and a neck, a link between fish and four-legged animals.
when did the first mammals appear | did mammals live with dinosaurs => The first mammals appeared over 200 million years ago and lived alongside dinosaurs. Most were small, like shrews, and only got big after the dinosaurs died out.
when did flowers first appear | did dinosaurs have flowers => Flowering plants appeared about 130 million years ago, during the Cretaceous. Early dinosaurs lived in a world of ferns, cycads and conifers with no flowers.
when did grass appear | was there grass during the dinosaurs => Grasses evolved during the late age of dinosaurs, but big grasslands only spread millions of years later, after the dinosaurs were gone.
what are the five mass extinctions | how many mass extinctions have there been => There have been five big mass extinctions, about 445, 372, 252, 201 and 66 million years ago. The last one wiped out the non-bird dinosaurs.
what was the great dying | what was the worst mass extinction => The Great Dying, about 252 million years ago, was the worst mass extinction. Huge volcanic eruptions in Siberia heated the planet, and around 90 percent of ocean species died.
what is pangaea | were the continents ever joined => Pangaea was a supercontinent that held almost all the land on Earth, about 335 to 175 million years ago. It slowly broke apart into the continents we know, which still drift a few centimetres a year.
is oil made from dinosaurs | did oil come from dinosaurs | how did oil and coal form => Fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas formed from ancient plants and tiny sea creatures buried for millions of years. Oil mostly came from plankton and algae, not dinosaurs.
what is amber | have animals been found in amber => Amber is fossilized tree resin. Insects, spiders, feathers and even a dinosaur's feathered tail have been found trapped inside.

# tag: prehistoric.evolution
what is evolution | how does evolution work => Evolution is how living things change over many generations. Living things with traits that help them survive have more babies and pass those traits on, which is called natural selection.
who came up with evolution | who discovered natural selection | who figured out evolution => Charles Darwin explained evolution by natural selection in his 1859 book On the Origin of Species. Alfred Russel Wallace came up with the same idea at about the same time.
did humans evolve from monkeys | are humans related to apes => Humans didn't evolve from modern monkeys or apes. We share common ancestors with them, and our last shared ancestor with chimpanzees lived roughly 6 to 7 million years ago.
what is natural selection | example of natural selection => Natural selection means living things that are better suited to their environment survive and have more young. Over time, helpful traits like camouflage become more common.
what is adaptation | what is an animal adaptation => An adaptation is a trait that helps a living thing survive where it lives, like a polar bear's thick fur, a cactus's spines or a giraffe's long neck.
what is a species | what does species mean => A species is a group of living things that are alike and can usually have babies together that can also have babies. Lions and tigers are different species.
what is a common ancestor | do all living things share an ancestor => A common ancestor is an older species that two or more species descend from. All life on Earth is thought to share one very ancient common ancestor.
who was lucy the fossil | what is australopithecus => Lucy is a famous fossil of an early human relative, Australopithecus afarensis, found in Ethiopia in 1974. She lived about 3.2 million years ago and walked upright.
when did humans first appear | how long have humans existed => Our species, Homo sapiens, appeared in Africa about 300,000 years ago. Earlier human relatives in our group, Homo, go back about 2.8 million years.
what happened to neanderthals | do humans have neanderthal dna | when did neanderthals die out => Neanderthals were close human relatives who lived in Europe and Asia until about 40,000 years ago. Many people today carry a little Neanderthal DNA because our ancestors had babies with them.
what is a mutation | are mutations bad => A mutation is a change in DNA. Most do nothing, some are harmful, and a few are helpful. Helpful mutations give natural selection something to work with.
what is extinction | why do species go extinct => Extinction is when a whole species dies out. It can happen from habitat loss, new predators, disease, climate changes or hunting.
what is a vestigial organ | why do humans have a tailbone => Vestigial parts are leftovers from ancestors that no longer do their old job. Your tailbone is what's left of a tail, and goosebumps are left from when ancestors had thick fur.
