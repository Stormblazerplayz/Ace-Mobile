# tag: body.brain
how much does the brain weigh | how big is the human brain => An adult brain weighs about 1.3 to 1.4 kg, around 2 percent of body weight, and is about the size of two fists together.
what are the parts of the brain | what are the main parts of the brain => The main parts are the cerebrum, which handles thinking, senses and movement, the cerebellum, which handles balance and coordination, and the brainstem, which runs breathing and heartbeat.
what does the cerebellum do | what part of the brain controls balance => The cerebellum, at the back of your brain, controls balance, coordination and smooth movements, like riding a bike or catching a ball.
what does the brainstem do | what part of the brain controls breathing => The brainstem connects your brain to your spinal cord and controls automatic things like breathing, heart rate, swallowing and sleep.
what are the lobes of the brain | what does the frontal lobe do => Each side of the cerebrum has four lobes: frontal for thinking and planning, parietal for touch, temporal for hearing and memory, and occipital for vision.
what are neurons | what do brain cells do => Neurons are nerve cells that send electrical and chemical signals. Your brain has about 86 billion of them, connected by trillions of links.
how fast do nerve signals travel | how fast does the brain send messages => The fastest nerve signals travel about 120 m a second, around 430 km/h. Others are much slower.
do we only use 10 percent of our brain | is the 10 percent brain myth true => No, that's a myth. Brain scans show we use virtually all of our brain, just not every part at the exact same moment.
are people left brained or right brained | is the left brain right brain thing real => The idea that people are "left-brained" or "right-brained" is a myth. The two sides do have some specialties, but everyone uses both sides together.
does the brain feel pain | can your brain feel pain => The brain itself has no pain sensors, which is why some brain surgery can happen while patients are awake. Headaches come from nerves around the brain and head.
what is the spinal cord | what does the spinal cord do => The spinal cord is a thick bundle of nerves running down inside your spine. It carries messages between your brain and body and handles some reflexes on its own.
what are nerves | how do nerves work => Nerves are bundles of long nerve cell fibers that carry signals between your brain, spinal cord and the rest of your body.
how does memory work | how does the brain remember things => Your brain stores memories by strengthening connections between neurons. A part called the hippocampus helps turn new experiences into long-term memories, especially during sleep.
what is short term memory and long term memory | difference between short and long term memory => Short-term memory holds a few things for seconds to minutes, like a phone number. Long-term memory stores things for years, like how to ride a bike.
why do we forget things | why do i forget stuff so easily => We forget because the brain prunes memories it doesn't use, new memories can block old ones, and some things were never stored well in the first place.
why should you wear a helmet | do helmets really protect your brain => A helmet absorbs the shock of a crash so your skull and brain take less of it. It can greatly reduce the risk of serious head injury when biking, skating or skiing.
does the brain keep growing | when does your brain stop developing => Your brain reaches nearly adult size by about age 6, but it keeps rewiring and maturing into your mid-20s. The part for planning and self-control finishes last.
what is neuroplasticity | can your brain change => Neuroplasticity means your brain can rewire itself by making new connections when you learn and practice. That's why practice makes skills easier.
why do we get brain fog | why cant i concentrate => Trouble focusing can come from poor sleep, not eating, dehydration, stress or too much screen time. Breaks, water, a snack and a good night's sleep often help.
what is dopamine | what is the happy chemical => Dopamine is a brain chemical linked to motivation and reward. It's released when you expect something good, which is also why games and apps can be so hard to put down.
what is serotonin | what does serotonin do => Serotonin is a brain chemical that helps control mood, sleep and appetite.
what is the brain made of | is the brain mostly water => The brain is about 75 percent water and is one of the fattiest organs, since the coating around nerve fibers is made of fat.
how much energy does the brain use | does thinking burn calories => Your brain uses about 20 percent of your body's energy, even though it's only about 2 percent of your weight.
what is a reflex arc | why do you pull your hand off a hot stove so fast => Some reflexes go through your spinal cord without waiting for your brain, so you pull your hand off something hot before you even feel the pain.
are bigger brains smarter | does brain size matter for intelligence => Not really. Brain size compared to body size and how the brain is wired matter more. Elephants and whales have bigger brains than humans.
what is a seizure | what is epilepsy => A seizure is a sudden burst of electrical activity in the brain that can cause shaking or staring spells. Epilepsy means having repeated seizures. If you see one, keep the person safe and get an adult.

# tag: body.feelings
what happens in your brain when you are scared | what is fight or flight => When you're scared, a part of your brain called the amygdala triggers fight or flight: adrenaline makes your heart race, breathing speed up and muscles tense to deal with danger.
why do we get butterflies in our stomach | why does my stomach feel weird when im nervous => When you're nervous, adrenaline shifts blood away from your stomach to your muscles, and your gut has lots of nerves that react, causing that fluttery feeling.
why do we cry when we are sad | is crying good for you => Scientists aren't sure exactly why we cry from emotions, but it can signal to others that we need help, and many people feel calmer after a good cry.
why do we blush | what makes your face turn red => When you're embarrassed, adrenaline widens blood vessels in your face, so more blood flows there and your cheeks turn red.
why do we laugh | is laughing good for you => Laughing helps people bond and shows we're friendly. It may also lower stress hormones and relax your muscles.
what is stress | why does stress feel bad in my body => Stress is your body's reaction to pressure or worry. Stress hormones can cause headaches, a tight stomach and trouble sleeping. Breathing slowly, moving your body and talking to someone help.
