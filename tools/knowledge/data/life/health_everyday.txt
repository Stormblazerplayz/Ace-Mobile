# tag: health.everyday
what is eye strain | why do my eyes hurt after screens | what is digital eye strain => Eye strain is when your eyes feel tired, dry, sore or blurry after focusing up close for a long time, like on screens or books. Breaks, blinking and good lighting help.
how do i rest my eyes from screens | how often should i take a screen break => Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds. It lets your eye muscles relax.
why do my eyes get dry on screens | do we blink less on screens => People blink much less when staring at screens, so the eyes dry out. Remembering to blink fully and taking breaks helps.
is blue light bad for your eyes | does blue light damage eyes | what is blue light => Blue light from screens hasn't been shown to damage eyes, but bright screens late at night can make it harder to fall asleep by affecting your body clock.
how far should a screen be from my eyes | how close should i hold my ipad => Hold tablets and books about 30 to 40 cm away, and keep computer screens about an arm's length away, with the top of the screen at or a bit below eye level.
does sitting close to the tv hurt your eyes | will sitting close to the screen ruin my eyes => It won't permanently damage your eyes, but it can cause eye strain and tiredness. Kids who sit close may sometimes need glasses, so mention it at an eye check.
does reading in the dark hurt your eyes | is reading in dim light bad => Reading in dim light can make your eyes tired and sore, but it doesn't cause lasting damage. Good light just makes reading more comfortable.
why is spending time outside good for your eyes | does going outside prevent nearsightedness => Studies suggest kids who spend more time outdoors in daylight are less likely to become nearsighted. Looking far away and bright light both seem to help.
what is screen brightness best for eyes | should i use dark mode => Set screen brightness to match the room, not much brighter or darker. Dark mode can feel more comfortable at night, but breaks matter most.
how do i know if i need an eye test | how often should kids get eye exams => Signs include squinting, headaches, rubbing your eyes, losing your place reading or trouble seeing the board. Many kids get vision checked at school or checkups, and an eye doctor can test more closely.
what is tech neck | why does my neck hurt from my phone | does looking down at a phone hurt your neck => Tech neck is soreness from bending your head down at screens for long periods. Raising the screen closer to eye level and taking breaks helps.
how should i sit when gaming | how should i sit on the couch with my ipad => Sit with your back supported, feet on the floor or a cushion, and hold the device higher with a pillow or stand so you're not hunched over. Get up and move every so often.
is slouching bad for you | will slouching ruin my back => Slouching now and then won't ruin your back, but slumping for hours can cause aches. Changing positions often and moving around is the best fix.
is sitting too long bad for you | why should i not sit all day => Sitting for long stretches without moving can make muscles stiff and is linked to health problems. Stand up, stretch or walk around at least every hour.
is a standing desk good | should kids use standing desks => Standing desks can help you move more, but standing still all day isn't perfect either. Switching between sitting, standing and moving is best.
are headphones bad for your ears | how loud should my headphones be => Keep the volume at about 60% or lower and take breaks. If someone near you can hear your music, it's too loud. Loud sounds over time can permanently damage hearing.
why are earbuds worse than headphones | are earbuds bad for hearing | earbuds or headphones which is safer => Earbuds sit right in your ear canal, so sound is closer to your eardrum, and people often turn them up to block outside noise. Noise-cancelling headphones let you listen quieter.
why do i shiver | why do we shiver when cold | what is shivering => Shivering is your muscles quickly tightening and relaxing to make heat when your body gets cold.
why does my eye twitch | what causes eyelid twitching => Eyelid twitches are usually harmless muscle spasms caused by tiredness, stress, caffeine or eye strain. They usually stop on their own.
why do i get a stitch when running | how do i get rid of a side stitch => A side stitch is a sharp pain under your ribs during exercise. Slowing down, taking deep breaths and pressing on the spot often helps, and avoiding big meals before running can prevent it.
why do i get hiccups when i eat fast | how do you stop hiccups => Eating or drinking quickly, swallowing air or fizzy drinks can trigger hiccups. Sipping cold water slowly or holding your breath briefly may help, and they usually stop on their own.
why do my lips get chapped | how do i fix chapped lips => Lips have no oil glands, so cold, wind and licking them dries them out. Lip balm and drinking water help, and try not to lick or peel them.
why are my hands always cold | why do my feet get cold => When you're cold, your body sends less blood to your hands and feet to keep your core warm. Gloves, warm socks and moving around help.
why do we have belly buttons | what is a belly button | why do some people have outie belly buttons => Your belly button is the scar where your umbilical cord was attached before birth, giving you food and oxygen. Innies and outies just depend on how it healed.
why do we have toenails | what are nails for | why do we have fingernails => Nails protect your fingertips and toes and help you pick up small things, scratch and feel. They're made of keratin, like hair.
what is double jointed | can you be double jointed => "Double jointed" people don't have extra joints. They have looser ligaments, so their joints bend farther than usual. It's called hypermobility.
why are some people left handed | how many people are left handed => About 10% of people are left handed. It's linked to genes and how the brain develops before birth, and it's totally normal.
what does ambidextrous mean | can you train yourself to be ambidextrous => Ambidextrous means using both hands equally well. It's rare, but you can get better with your other hand through lots of practice.
why do we have two nostrils | do you breathe out of one nostril at a time => Your nose takes turns, with one nostril letting more air through for a few hours, then switching. It's called the nasal cycle and may help your sense of smell.
why do we have a uvula | what is the dangly thing in the back of your throat => That's the uvula. It helps close off your nose when you swallow and helps with some speech sounds and making saliva.
what are earlobes for | why do we have earlobes => Earlobes don't have a clear job. They're soft skin and fat with lots of blood, and whether yours are attached or free is partly genetic.
why do we drool when we sleep | why do i drool on my pillow => When you're asleep your muscles relax, including the ones that keep your mouth closed and swallow, so saliva can leak out.
why do we stretch when we wake up | what is pandiculation => Stretching after rest, called pandiculation, wakes up your muscles, increases blood flow and helps you feel alert.
why do we get the shivers when we pee | what is pee shivers => Scientists aren't totally sure. One idea is that peeing triggers a small shift in your nervous system and a drop in body warmth, causing a little shiver.
what happens to your body when you exercise | what does exercise do to your body => Your heart pumps faster, you breathe harder to bring in oxygen, your muscles burn fuel and you sweat to cool down. Over time, your heart, lungs, muscles and bones get stronger.
what are endorphins | why does exercise make you happy | what is a runners high => Endorphins are natural chemicals your brain releases during exercise, laughter and fun that ease pain and lift your mood. Exercise also boosts other feel-good brain chemicals.
what is cardio | what is aerobic exercise | what counts as cardio => Cardio or aerobic exercise gets your heart pumping and breathing harder for a while, like running, biking, swimming, dancing and soccer.
what is stamina | how do i build stamina | what is endurance => Stamina or endurance is how long you can keep going. You build it by exercising a little longer or harder bit by bit over weeks.
why does exercise make you stronger | how does exercise build muscle => Exercise makes tiny tears in muscle fibers, and as your body repairs them with protein and rest, the muscles grow back stronger.
does exercise help your brain | does exercise make you smarter | does exercise help with focus => Yes. Exercise sends more blood and oxygen to your brain and can improve focus, memory, mood and sleep.
what is a resting heart rate | what is a good resting heart rate for kids => Resting heart rate is how fast your heart beats when you're calm. For kids around 10 to 12 it's often about 70 to 100 beats per minute, and fit people tend to have lower ones.
what is heart rate zone | how fast should my heart beat when exercising => When you exercise hard, your heart rate can go well above 150 beats per minute, which is normal for kids. If you feel faint or chest pain, stop and tell an adult.
why do my legs feel like jelly | why are my legs wobbly after exercise => After hard exercise, your muscles are tired and low on fuel, and nerve signals get weaker, so your legs can feel shaky. Rest, water and a snack help.
is it ok to exercise when sick | should i play sports with a cold => With a mild cold above the neck, light activity may be OK. With a fever, body aches or stomach bug, rest instead. Ask a parent if unsure.
what is a pulled muscle | what is a muscle strain | what does a strained muscle feel like => A pulled muscle, or strain, is when muscle fibers stretch too far or tear a bit, causing pain, soreness and sometimes swelling. Rest and gentle care help. See a doctor if it's very painful.
what is a tendon | tendon vs ligament => Tendons connect muscles to bones, while ligaments connect bones to other bones at joints.
what should i do if a snake bites me | what do you do for a snake bite => Stay calm, keep still and call emergency services right away. Keep the bitten part still and around heart level. Don't cut, suck or put ice on it. Try to remember what the snake looked like.
does pee help a jellyfish sting | should you pee on a jellyfish sting => No, that's a myth and can make it worse. Rinsing with seawater, or vinegar for some types, is better. Ask a lifeguard.
what should i do if a wasp stings me | wasp sting vs bee sting => Wash it and use a cold pack. Wasps don't leave a stinger, but bees do, so scrape a bee stinger out. Get emergency help if there's swelling of the face, trouble breathing or dizziness.
what causes a black eye | how do you treat a black eye => A black eye is a bruise around the eye from a hit. A cold pack (wrapped in cloth) helps swelling. See a doctor if you have vision problems, eye pain or blood in the eye.
what do i do if i stub my toe | why does stubbing your toe hurt so much => Toes have lots of nerve endings and little padding, so stubs hurt a lot. Ice and rest help. If the toe looks crooked or the pain lasts, see a doctor.
what if something gets stuck in my ear | what if a bug goes in your ear => Don't poke at it with anything. Tell an adult. A doctor can safely remove objects. For a bug, an adult may tilt your head so it can crawl out.
what if something gets stuck in my nose | what do i do if i put something up my nose => Don't try to dig it out. Breathe through your mouth and tell an adult right away. A doctor can remove it safely.
what if a fish hook gets stuck in my skin | how do you remove a fishhook => Don't pull it out yourself. Tell an adult, and see a doctor if it's deep or near the eye. Barbed hooks are tricky to remove safely.
how do i stay cool in hot weather | how do i stay safe in a heat wave => Drink water often, rest in the shade, wear light loose clothes and a hat, and avoid hard exercise in the hottest part of the day.
what is secondhand smoke | is secondhand smoke dangerous => Secondhand smoke is the smoke you breathe from someone else's cigarette. It's harmful too, especially for kids' lungs.
what is nicotine | why is nicotine addictive => Nicotine is an addictive drug in tobacco and most vapes. It changes how the brain's reward system works, so the body craves more. It's especially harmful to developing teen brains.
why is alcohol bad for kids | why cant kids drink alcohol => Alcohol affects the brain, judgment and coordination, and kids' growing brains and bodies are more sensitive to harm. That's why there are age limits.
how do i balance screen time | how do i stop using screens so much => Set times for screens, keep devices out of your bedroom at night, plan outdoor or hands-on activities and use app timers. Making a plan with your family helps.
why is playing outside good for kids | what are the benefits of being outdoors => Outdoor play gets you moving, gives you sunlight for vitamin D, boosts your mood, helps your eyes and builds creativity.
what is self care for kids | how do i take care of my body => Taking care of your body means sleeping enough, eating well, drinking water, moving every day, keeping clean, and telling a trusted adult when something feels wrong.
how do i tell an adult im not feeling well | what do i say when im sick => Tell them what hurts, where, how long it's been happening and how bad it is. It's always OK to speak up about how you feel.
what is a trusted adult | who should i talk to if something is wrong => A trusted adult is someone who keeps you safe and listens, like a parent, family member, teacher, school nurse, counselor or coach.
what is a school nurse | what does a school nurse do => A school nurse helps students who feel sick or get hurt at school, gives approved medicine and helps kids with health conditions like asthma or allergies.
how much should a 12 year old weigh | how much should i weigh => It varies a lot because kids grow at different times and rates. Doctors use growth charts to check you're growing well, so don't compare with friends.
is it normal to grow at different times | why am i growing slower than my friends => Yes. Kids start puberty and growth spurts at different ages, sometimes years apart. Most people catch up by their late teens.
how do i build healthy habits | how do i start a healthy habit => Start small, like one extra glass of water a day or a short walk, and connect it to something you already do. Tracking it on a chart helps it stick.
what are the benefits of drinking water | does water help you think => Drinking enough water helps you focus, keeps your energy up, helps prevent headaches and keeps your body temperature steady.
what is good hygiene for kids | why is hygiene important => Good hygiene means washing hands, bathing, brushing teeth and wearing clean clothes. It stops germs spreading and helps you feel good.
why does my knee hurt | what is osgood schlatter => Knee pain in active kids is common. Osgood-Schlatter is a sore bump below the kneecap during growth spurts, often in sports with running and jumping. Rest helps, and a doctor can check it.
why do my legs ache at night | what helps aching legs => Leg aches at night are often growing pains from an active day. Stretching, a warm bath or gentle massage can help.
what should be in a first aid kit for hiking | what to bring for safety on a hike => Bring water, snacks, a whistle, sun protection, a map, bandages, blister care and a phone. Tell someone where you're going.
what is a vaccine schedule | why do kids get so many shots => A vaccine schedule lists which vaccines to get at which ages, timed to protect kids when they're most at risk. Your doctor keeps track of it.
what vaccines do 12 year olds get | what shots do you get at 11 or 12 => Many countries offer vaccines around age 11 or 12, such as HPV, meningitis and a tetanus booster, plus yearly flu shots. Your doctor will know the schedule where you live.
what is tetanus | what does a tetanus shot do => Tetanus is a serious illness from bacteria that can get into deep or dirty wounds and cause muscle stiffness. Vaccines protect you, with boosters every so often.
why shouldnt you touch wild animals | can you get sick from wild animals => Wild animals can bite, scratch or carry diseases like rabies or parasites. Watch them from a distance and tell an adult about sick or hurt animals.
