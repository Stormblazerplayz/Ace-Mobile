# tag: body.growth
how tall will i be | how do you predict how tall i will be => Your height mostly depends on your genes, so your parents' heights give the best clue. Sleep, good food and exercise help you reach your full height. Doctors can estimate using growth charts.
when do you stop growing taller | at what age do people stop growing => Most girls stop growing taller around 14 to 16, and most boys around 16 to 18, though some keep growing a little longer.
what is a growth spurt | when do growth spurts happen => A growth spurt is a time of fast growth. The biggest happens during puberty, when kids can grow around 8 to 10 cm in a single year.
what are growth plates | how do bones grow longer => Growth plates are areas of cartilage near the ends of long bones where new bone forms. When puberty ends, they harden and you stop growing taller.
does sleep help you grow | do you grow when you sleep => Yes. Your body releases most of its growth hormone during deep sleep, which is one reason kids and teens need lots of it.
does coffee stunt your growth | can kids drink coffee => There's no good evidence coffee stunts growth. But caffeine can mess with sleep, which matters for growing, so it's best for kids to skip it.
why are some people taller than others | what decides how tall you are => Height is mostly decided by genes, with nutrition and health also playing a big part. That's why people in many countries are taller now than 100 years ago.
what is the tallest person ever | how tall was robert wadlow => Robert Wadlow of the US was the tallest person ever recorded, at 2.72 m (8 ft 11 in). He had a condition that made his body make too much growth hormone.

# tag: body.puberty
what causes puberty | what hormones cause puberty => Puberty starts when your brain signals your body to make more sex hormones, mainly estrogen in girls and testosterone in boys. These cause the body changes.
why does your voice change during puberty | why do boys voices crack => During puberty, the voice box grows and the vocal cords get longer and thicker, especially in boys, making the voice deeper. While it adjusts, the voice can crack.
what changes happen to boys in puberty | how do boys bodies change during puberty => Boys usually grow taller, get broader shoulders and more muscle, grow body and facial hair, and their voices deepen. Their skin may get oilier too.
what changes happen to girls in puberty | how do girls bodies change during puberty => Girls usually grow taller, develop breasts and wider hips, grow body hair and start having periods. Their skin may get oilier too.
what is a period | what is menstruation => A period is when the lining of the uterus comes out as blood through the vagina, about once a month. It's a normal part of puberty for girls and usually lasts a few days.
why do teens get moody | why do i feel emotional during puberty => Changing hormones, a growing brain, and lots of new pressures can make feelings stronger and moods change fast. Talking to someone you trust helps.
why do teenagers sleep so late | why are teens tired in the morning => During puberty, the body clock shifts later, so teens naturally feel sleepy later at night. They still need about 8 to 10 hours of sleep.
is it normal to be shorter than my friends | why am i the shortest in my class => Yes. Everyone grows at different times. Some kids hit their growth spurt early and others catch up later. If you're worried, a doctor can check your growth.
who can i talk to about puberty | im embarrassed to ask about puberty => A parent, school nurse, doctor or another trusted adult can answer questions. Lots of kids feel awkward, and it's okay to ask.
