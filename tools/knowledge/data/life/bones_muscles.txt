# tag: body.bones
what are bones made of | what is inside a bone => Bones are made of a tough protein called collagen filled with calcium phosphate minerals. Inside many bones is spongy bone and soft marrow.
what is bone marrow | what does bone marrow do => Bone marrow is the soft tissue inside bones. Red marrow makes red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets, millions every second.
are bones alive | do bones grow => Yes, bones are living tissue with blood vessels and cells. They grow, repair themselves and are constantly rebuilt throughout your life.
why do babies have more bones than adults | how many bones does a baby have => Babies are born with around 270 to 300 bones, many partly made of cartilage. As they grow, some fuse together, leaving adults with 206.
where is the smallest bone in the body | what are the ear bones called => Your three smallest bones are in your middle ear: the malleus (hammer), incus (anvil) and stapes (stirrup). They pass sound vibrations to your inner ear.
what is the skull | how many bones are in your skull => Your skull protects your brain and holds your face together. It's made of 22 bones, mostly joined by wiggly seams, plus your lower jaw is the only one that moves.
what is the soft spot on a babys head | why do babies have a soft spot => Babies have soft spots called fontanelles where skull bones haven't joined yet. This lets the head squeeze through at birth and grow, and they close by about age 2.
what are vertebrae | how many vertebrae do adults have => Your spine has 33 vertebrae as a baby, and some fuse so adults usually have 26 bones. They stack up to protect your spinal cord.
how many ribs do you have | do boys and girls have the same number of ribs => Most people have 12 pairs of ribs, 24 total, whether they're male or female. A few people have an extra or missing rib.
what is the collarbone | what is the clavicle => The collarbone, or clavicle, connects your shoulder to your breastbone. It's one of the most commonly broken bones, often from falling on an outstretched arm.
how many bones are in your hand | how many bones are in your foot => Each hand has 27 bones, and each foot has 26. Together your hands and feet hold more than half the bones in your body.
what is the kneecap | what is the patella => Your kneecap, or patella, is a small bone at the front of your knee that protects the joint and helps your thigh muscles straighten your leg.
what is the funny bone | why does hitting your funny bone hurt => The funny bone isn't a bone. It's the ulnar nerve, which runs close to the skin at your elbow. Bumping it sends a zingy, tingly pain down to your little finger.
what is the strongest bone in the body | which bone is hardest to break => The femur, your thigh bone, is the strongest bone as well as the longest. It can handle huge forces when you run and jump.
what is a joint | what are the types of joints => A joint is where two bones meet. Hinge joints like knees and elbows bend one way, ball-and-socket joints like hips and shoulders spin around, and some joints, like in your skull, don't move.
what is cartilage | what is the bendy part of your ear => Cartilage is a tough, bendy tissue. It shapes your ears and nose and cushions the ends of bones in your joints.
what are ligaments | what are tendons => Ligaments are tough bands that connect bone to bone at joints. Tendons connect muscles to bones, so when a muscle pulls, the bone moves.
what is the achilles tendon | what is the strongest tendon => The Achilles tendon connects your calf muscles to your heel bone. It's the biggest and strongest tendon in your body.
why do my knuckles crack | is cracking your knuckles bad => The pop comes from gas bubbles in the fluid inside the joint. Studies haven't found that knuckle cracking causes arthritis.
how long does a broken bone take to heal | how do bones heal => Most broken bones in kids heal in about 4 to 8 weeks. The body builds new bone across the break, and a cast keeps it still while it heals.
what is a fracture | is a fracture the same as a break => Yes, a fracture is the same as a broken bone. It can be a small crack or a bone broken all the way through.
why do bones need calcium | how do i make my bones strong => Calcium and vitamin D build strong bones, and running, jumping and sports make bones denser. Kids and teens build most of their bone strength for life.
what is osteoporosis | what makes bones weak => Osteoporosis is when bones become thin and weak, so they break easily. It's more common in older people, and building strong bones while young helps prevent it.
what is the tailbone | do humans have a tail bone => Your tailbone, or coccyx, is a small set of fused bones at the bottom of your spine. It's left over from ancestors with tails, and some muscles attach to it.
what is the hyoid bone | which bone isnt connected to other bones => The hyoid is a U-shaped bone in your neck that supports your tongue. It's the only bone that isn't connected to any other bone.
are humans taller in the morning | why are you taller in the morning => Yes, you're about 1 cm taller in the morning. During the day, the cushions between your spine bones get squeezed, and they plump back up while you sleep.

# tag: body.muscles
how do muscles work | how do muscles move bones => Muscles can only pull, not push. They work in pairs, like your biceps bending your elbow and your triceps straightening it.
what are the three types of muscle | what kinds of muscles are there => There are three types: skeletal muscles that move your bones, smooth muscles in organs like your stomach, and cardiac muscle in your heart.
what are voluntary and involuntary muscles | which muscles move on their own => Voluntary muscles, like your arm and leg muscles, move when you decide. Involuntary muscles, like your heart and gut, work automatically.
what is the smallest muscle | what is the stapedius => The smallest muscle is the stapedius in your middle ear, about 1 mm long. It helps protect your ear from loud sounds.
what is the longest muscle in the body | what is the sartorius => The sartorius, which runs from your hip across your thigh to the inside of your knee, is the longest muscle.
what is the hardest working muscle | which muscle never rests => Your heart is the hardest working muscle. It beats about 100,000 times a day and never takes a break.
how do muscles get bigger | how do you build muscle => When you exercise, tiny tears form in muscle fibers. With protein, rest and sleep, your body repairs them a bit stronger and bigger.
what is a muscle cramp | why do i get leg cramps => A cramp is when a muscle suddenly tightens and won't relax. It can come from tired muscles, not enough water or staying in one position. Gently stretching helps.
how many muscles does it take to smile | is it easier to smile than frown => Smiling and frowning use several face muscles each, but the exact counts people share aren't reliable. Your face has over 40 muscles.
what are abs | what are abdominal muscles => Abs are the muscles across your belly. They support your spine, help you sit up and twist and protect your organs.
what are biceps and triceps | what muscle bends your arm => Your biceps is the muscle on the front of your upper arm that bends the elbow. Your triceps, on the back, straightens it.
what is the strongest muscle for its size | strongest muscle compared to its size => The masseter, your chewing muscle, can squeeze very hard for its size. Your heart, which never stops, and your gluteus maximus are also often called the strongest.
why do muscles shake when tired | why do my legs shake after exercise => When muscles are tired, nerve signals and muscle fibers don't fire as smoothly, so they shake. Rest, water and food help them recover.
does muscle turn into fat | can fat turn into muscle => No. Muscle and fat are different tissues and can't turn into each other. If you stop exercising, muscles shrink, and if you eat more than you use, fat can build up.
