# tag: body.sleep
what happens when you sleep | what does your body do while you sleep => While you sleep, your body repairs muscles, releases growth hormone and strengthens your immune system, and your brain sorts and stores memories from the day.
what are the stages of sleep | what is rem sleep => Sleep cycles through light sleep, deep sleep and REM sleep about every 90 minutes. REM, or rapid eye movement, is when most vivid dreaming happens.
why cant i remember my dreams | why do we forget dreams => Your brain doesn't store memories well during REM sleep, so dreams fade fast. You remember them better if you wake up during or right after one.
what is sleep paralysis | why cant i move when i wake up => Sleep paralysis is when you wake up but can't move for a few seconds or minutes, sometimes with scary dreamlike feelings. It's harmless and passes.
why do we snore | what causes snoring => Snoring happens when relaxed tissue in your throat vibrates as air passes through. Colds and allergies can make it worse. Loud nightly snoring in kids is worth mentioning to a doctor.
what happens if you dont sleep | what does lack of sleep do => Without enough sleep it's harder to focus, remember things, control your mood and fight off germs. You can also get clumsy and hungrier for junk food.
what is the longest anyone has stayed awake | world record for staying awake => In 1964, a high school student named Randy Gardner stayed awake about 11 days for a science fair project. Records like this aren't tracked anymore because it's dangerous.
what is melatonin | what makes you sleepy at night => Melatonin is a hormone your brain releases when it gets dark, telling your body it's time to sleep. Bright light, especially from screens, delays it.
what is a body clock | what is circadian rhythm => Your circadian rhythm is your body's built-in 24-hour clock that controls when you feel sleepy and alert. Sunlight in the morning helps keep it on track.
why do we yawn when others yawn | why are yawns contagious => Yawns are contagious for most people, maybe because of social bonding and empathy. Even reading about yawning can make you yawn.
why do we twitch when falling asleep | what is a hypnic jerk => A hypnic jerk is a sudden twitch as you're falling asleep, often with a feeling of falling. It's normal and harmless, and it's more common when you're tired or stressed.
why do we sleep with our eyes closed | why do we close our eyes to sleep => Closing your eyes blocks light that would keep your brain alert and stops your eyes from drying out.
do animals sleep | which animal sleeps the least => Almost all animals sleep in some way. Giraffes and elephants sleep only a few hours a day, while bats and koalas sleep around 20 hours.
