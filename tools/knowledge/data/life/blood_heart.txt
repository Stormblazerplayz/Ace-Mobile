# tag: body.heart
how big is your heart | how big is the human heart => Your heart is about the size of your fist. An adult heart weighs about 250 to 350 g.
how does the heart work | how does the heart pump blood => Your heart has four chambers. The right side pumps blood to your lungs to get oxygen, and the left side pumps that oxygen-rich blood to the rest of your body.
what are the chambers of the heart | what are atria and ventricles => The heart has two upper chambers called atria, which collect blood, and two lower chambers called ventricles, which pump it out.
what makes the heartbeat sound | why does the heart go lub dub => The "lub dub" is the sound of heart valves snapping shut. Lub is the valves between the upper and lower chambers, and dub is the valves leading out of the heart.
what are heart valves | what do valves in the heart do => Heart valves are flaps that open to let blood through and close to stop it flowing backward, keeping blood moving one way.
what is a normal heart rate for kids | how fast should a kids heart beat => A resting heart rate for kids about 6 to 12 years old is usually around 70 to 110 beats a minute, and it slows as you get older.
how do you check your pulse | how do i find my heart rate => Put two fingers on the inside of your wrist below your thumb, or on the side of your neck. Count the beats for 30 seconds and double it to get beats per minute.
why does my heart beat fast when im scared | why does your heart race => When you're scared or excited, your body releases adrenaline, which speeds up your heart to send more blood to your muscles in case you need to run.
why does my heart beat faster when i exercise | why does my heart pound when i run => Exercising muscles need more oxygen, so your heart pumps faster to deliver more blood. Regular exercise makes your heart stronger, so it beats slower at rest.
how many times does the heart beat in a lifetime | how many heartbeats in a whole life => Over an average lifetime, a human heart beats about 2.5 to 3 billion times.
what is a heart attack | what causes a heart attack => A heart attack is when blood flow to part of the heart muscle gets blocked, usually by a clot. Chest pain and trouble breathing are signs, and you should call emergency services right away.
what is blood pressure | what does blood pressure mean => Blood pressure is how hard your blood pushes on your artery walls. It's written as two numbers, like 110 over 70: the pressure when the heart beats and when it rests.
what is high blood pressure | why is high blood pressure bad => High blood pressure means blood pushes too hard on your arteries all the time. Over years, it can damage the heart, brain and kidneys, so doctors check it at checkups.
how do you keep your heart healthy | how to have a healthy heart => Exercise regularly, eat lots of fruits, veggies and whole grains, limit salty and sugary junk food, sleep well and never smoke.
is your heart on the left side | why do i feel my heartbeat on the left => Your heart is in the middle of your chest, tilted slightly to the left, which is why you feel your heartbeat more on the left.

# tag: body.blood
what are arteries and veins | whats the difference between arteries and veins => Arteries carry blood away from your heart, usually full of oxygen. Veins carry blood back to your heart. Tiny capillaries connect them.
what are capillaries | how small are capillaries => Capillaries are the tiniest blood vessels, so narrow that red blood cells pass through in single file. That's where oxygen and food pass into your cells.
how long are all your blood vessels | how many miles of blood vessels do you have => If you lined up all of an adult's blood vessels end to end, they'd stretch an estimated 100,000 km or so, enough to wrap around Earth more than twice.
what is blood plasma | what is the yellow part of blood | what is the liquid part of blood => Plasma is the pale yellow liquid part of blood, mostly water. It carries blood cells, nutrients, hormones and waste around your body.
what are platelets | what do platelets do => Platelets are tiny cell pieces in your blood that rush to a cut and stick together to plug it, starting a clot to stop the bleeding.
how do white blood cells fight germs | how does blood fight infections => White blood cells find and attack germs. Some swallow them whole, and others make antibodies that tag germs for destruction.
why is blood red | what makes blood red => Blood is red because red blood cells are packed with hemoglobin, an iron-containing protein that carries oxygen.
how long do red blood cells live | how often does your body make new blood => Red blood cells live about 120 days, then your spleen and liver break them down. Your body makes millions of new ones every second.
what are blood types | what are the different blood types => The main blood types are A, B, AB and O, each either positive or negative. They describe markers on your red blood cells.
what is the universal donor | what blood type can give to everyone => O negative is the universal donor for red blood cells, because it has none of the main markers. AB positive people can receive red blood cells from any type.
what is the most common blood type | rarest blood type => O positive is the most common blood type in many countries, including the United States. Blood type is inherited from your parents.
how can i learn what blood type i am | how do doctors test your blood type => You can find your blood type from a blood test at the doctor, when you donate blood, or by asking a parent if it's in your medical records.
how old do you have to be to donate blood | can kids give blood => In many places, including most of the US, you can donate blood at 16 with a parent's permission, or 17 on your own, if you're healthy and heavy enough.
how does blood clot | why do scabs form => When you get cut, platelets clump together and proteins form a sticky net called fibrin that traps blood cells. This dries into a scab that protects the wound while it heals.
what is anemia | why do people feel tired from low iron => Anemia means you don't have enough healthy red blood cells, so your body gets less oxygen. It often comes from low iron and can make you tired and pale. A doctor can test for it.
why do i get dizzy when i stand up fast | why do i see black spots when i stand up => When you stand quickly, blood briefly drops toward your legs before your body adjusts, so your brain gets a bit less. Standing up slowly helps. Tell a doctor if it happens a lot.
what is a bruise | what causes a bruise | why do i bruise so easily => A bruise is blood from tiny broken vessels leaking under your skin. It changes from red or purple to blue, green and yellow as your body breaks the blood down.
why do my fingers go white in the cold | why do my hands go numb when cold => In cold, your body narrows blood vessels in your fingers and toes to keep heat in your core, so they can look pale and feel numb. Warm them gently.
what is a nosebleed | why do nosebleeds happen => Nosebleeds happen when tiny blood vessels inside your nose break, often from dry air, picking or a bump. They're usually harmless.
what is hemophilia | why do some people bleed a lot => Hemophilia is a condition where blood doesn't clot properly, so people bleed longer from injuries. It's usually inherited, and treatments help.
