# tag: pets.hamsters
what do hamsters eat | what should i feed my hamster | hamster food => Feed your hamster a good hamster mix or pellets, plus small bits of fresh veggies like carrot, broccoli or cucumber a few times a week. A little boiled egg or plain cooked chicken gives protein.
what foods are bad for hamsters | what can hamsters not eat => Don't give hamsters chocolate, onion, garlic, citrus fruit, raw beans, almonds, sugary or salty snacks, or sticky foods that can get stuck in their cheek pouches.
can hamsters eat fruit | can hamsters eat apples => Small pieces of apple (no seeds), banana or berries are okay now and then. Fruit is sugary, so keep it to a tiny treat, especially for dwarf hamsters.
how big should a hamster cage be | what cage is best for a hamster => Bigger is better. Many hamster experts suggest a floor of at least about 100 by 50 cm, with deep bedding for burrowing, a wheel, a hide and chew toys.
what size wheel does a hamster need | why does my hamster need a big wheel => A hamster's back should stay straight when it runs. Syrian hamsters usually need a wheel about 28 cm or bigger, and wheels should have a solid surface, not bars.
can two hamsters live together | do hamsters need a friend => Syrian hamsters must live alone, because adults fight, sometimes badly. Some dwarf hamsters can live in pairs, but they may fight too, so many owners keep one per cage.
why is my hamster awake at night | are hamsters nocturnal => Hamsters are mostly active at night and dusk. Let them sleep during the day, and keep their cage away from your bed if the wheel keeps you up.
why did my hamster bite me | how do i stop my hamster biting => Hamsters bite when scared, woken up suddenly or if your hand smells like food. Wash your hands, move slowly and let it sniff you before picking it up.
how do i tame my hamster | how do i get my hamster to trust me => Give it a few days to settle in. Then sit by the cage, offer treats from your hand, and let it climb on your hand on its own before trying to lift it.
can hamsters have baths | how do you clean a hamster => Don't give hamsters water baths. They groom themselves, and dwarf hamsters like rolling in a dish of chinchilla sand to stay clean.
why do hamsters stuff their cheeks | how much can a hamster fit in its cheeks => Hamsters carry food in stretchy cheek pouches back to a hidden stash. The pouches stretch back to their shoulders.
do hamster teeth keep growing | why does my hamster chew the cage => Yes, hamster teeth grow their whole life, so they need wooden chews and hard food to wear them down. Chewing the bars often means boredom or a too-small cage.
what temperature does a hamster need | can hamsters get too cold => Hamsters do best at normal room temperature, about 20 to 24 °C. If it gets too cold, they can go into a sleepy state that looks like hibernation, so warm them gently and call a vet.
are hamster balls safe | should i put my hamster in a ball => Many experts say hamster balls are stressful and can hurt hamsters, since they can't see well or stop easily. A safe playpen is a better choice.

# tag: pets.guinea-pigs
how long do guinea pigs live | guinea pig lifespan => Guinea pigs usually live about 5 to 8 years.
what do guinea pigs eat | what should i feed my guinea pig => Guinea pigs need unlimited hay, plus fresh veggies every day and a small amount of guinea pig pellets. Hay is most of their diet.
why do guinea pigs need vitamin c | what foods have vitamin c for guinea pigs => Guinea pigs can't make their own vitamin C, like us. Bell peppers, kale and parsley are good sources, and without it they get sick.
do guinea pigs need a friend | should guinea pigs live alone => Guinea pigs are very social and should live with at least one other guinea pig. In Switzerland it's actually against the law to keep just one.
why do guinea pigs squeak | what is wheeking => Guinea pigs make a loud "wheek" when excited, often when they hear the fridge or food bag. Purring and chirping are other sounds they make.
what is popcorning | why does my guinea pig jump => Popcorning is when a guinea pig hops straight up in the air, often twisting. It means it's happy and excited.
where do guinea pigs come from | are guinea pigs from guinea => Guinea pigs come from the Andes in South America, not Guinea, and they aren't pigs. They're rodents.
can guinea pigs use a wheel or ball | do guinea pigs need a hamster wheel => No. Wheels and balls can hurt a guinea pig's back. They need lots of floor space to run around instead.
what foods are bad for guinea pigs | what can guinea pigs not eat => Avoid chocolate, onion, garlic, potatoes, avocado, iceberg lettuce, meat and dairy. Too much fruit isn't good either.

# tag: pets.rabbits
what do pet rabbits eat | what should i feed my bunny => Hay should be about 80 percent of a rabbit's diet, with fresh leafy greens every day and a small amount of pellets. Fresh water is a must.
do rabbits really eat carrots | are carrots good for rabbits => Carrots are sugary, so they're only an occasional small treat for rabbits. Wild rabbits mostly eat grass and leafy plants, not root vegetables.
what foods are bad for rabbits | what cant bunnies eat => Avoid chocolate, onions, garlic, avocado, iceberg lettuce, bread, crackers and sweets. Introduce new veggies slowly to avoid tummy trouble.
why do rabbits eat their poop | what are cecotropes => Rabbits make special soft poops called cecotropes, usually at night, and eat them to get extra nutrients. It's healthy and normal.
what is a binky | why does my bunny jump and twist => A binky is a happy jump and twist in the air. It means your rabbit is feeling great.
why do rabbits thump | what does it mean when a rabbit thumps => Rabbits thump their back feet to warn of danger or show they're scared or annoyed.
can rabbits be litter trained | how do i litter train my bunny => Yes. Rabbits often pick one corner as a toilet. Put a litter box with paper-based litter and hay there, and they'll usually start using it.
do rabbits need a friend | should rabbits live alone => Rabbits are social and usually happier with another neutered rabbit friend. They also need lots of space to run, not just a small hutch.
can you pick up a rabbit by the ears | how do you hold a rabbit => Never. It hurts and can injure them. Support a rabbit's chest and back legs with both hands, and keep it close and low to the ground.
are rabbits rodents | what kind of animal is a rabbit => Rabbits aren't rodents. They're lagomorphs, a group with hares and pikas, and they have a second small pair of top front teeth.

# tag: pets.small-mammals
how long do pet rats live | are rats good pets => Pet rats live about 2 to 3 years. They're smart, friendly and can learn their names, and they should live with at least one other rat.
how long do gerbils live | are gerbils good pets => Gerbils live about 2 to 4 years. They're active, curious and like living in pairs, and they need deep bedding to dig tunnels.
why shouldnt you pick up a gerbil by its tail | how do you hold a gerbil => A gerbil's tail skin can slip right off if grabbed, and it won't grow back. Scoop gerbils up from underneath.
how long do pet mice live | do mice make good pets => Pet mice usually live about 1 to 2 years. Female mice often like living in groups, while males may fight.
why do chinchillas take dust baths | can chinchillas get wet => Chinchillas roll in special dust to keep their super-thick fur clean. Water can soak deep into their fur and cause skin problems, so no water baths.
how long do chinchillas live | what temperature do chinchillas need => Chinchillas can live about 10 to 20 years. They come from the cold Andes and can overheat above about 25 °C.
how long do ferrets live | are ferrets good pets => Ferrets live about 6 to 10 years. They're playful and curious but need lots of attention, a meat-based diet and ferret-proofed rooms. They're illegal to own in some places.
what is a ferret war dance | why do ferrets hop sideways => The ferret "war dance" is a crazy hopping, sideways bouncing play. It means the ferret is excited and wants to play.
what do pet hedgehogs eat | are hedgehogs good pets => Pet African pygmy hedgehogs eat special hedgehog food or high-quality cat food plus some insects. They're night animals and need a warm room. They're illegal in some places.

# tag: pets.fish
goldfish lifespan | how many years can a goldfish live => With a big tank and good care, goldfish can live 10 to 15 years or more. One goldfish named Tish lived to 43.
can goldfish live in a bowl | is a fishbowl bad for goldfish => Goldfish shouldn't live in small bowls. They make lots of waste and can grow big, so they need a large tank with a filter.
how often should i feed my fish | how much should i feed my fish => Feed most fish once or twice a day, only what they can eat in about 2 minutes. Overfeeding is a common way to kill fish, because leftover food dirties the water.
how long can fish go without food | can i leave my fish for a week => Healthy adult fish can usually go a few days, and some up to a week or more, without food. For longer trips, ask someone to feed them.
how do i set up a fish tank | what do i need for a fish tank => You need a tank, filter, water conditioner, gravel or sand, and a heater for tropical fish. Set it up and let it "cycle" for a few weeks before adding fish.
what is cycling a fish tank | what is the nitrogen cycle => Cycling grows good bacteria in the filter that turn toxic fish waste (ammonia) into less harmful stuff. It takes about 4 to 6 weeks, and adding fish too soon can kill them.
why does my fish tank need water conditioner | can i use tap water for fish => Tap water has chlorine or chloramine that kills fish and the good bacteria. Water conditioner makes tap water safe.
how do i add new fish to my tank | how do you acclimate fish => Float the sealed bag in the tank for about 15 minutes so temperatures match, then slowly add small amounts of tank water to the bag before netting the fish in.
why is my fish tank cloudy | how do i fix cloudy aquarium water => Cloudy water in a new tank is usually a bacteria bloom that clears in days. In older tanks, it often means overfeeding or a dirty filter.
why is there algae in my fish tank | how do i stop algae => Algae grows with too much light and too many nutrients. Keep lights on about 8 hours a day, avoid overfeeding, do water changes and keep the tank out of direct sunlight.
why is my fish floating upside down | what is swim bladder disease => A fish floating sideways or upside down often has a swim bladder problem, sometimes from constipation or bad water. Check your water quality and ask a fish store or vet.
what are good beginner fish | easiest fish to take care of => Good beginner fish include guppies, platies, zebra danios, white cloud minnows and corydoras catfish. Check what size tank each one needs.
how long do betta fish live | betta fish lifespan => Betta fish usually live about 3 to 5 years with good care.
can betta fish live together | why do betta fish fight => Male bettas fight each other, so keep only one male per tank. They're also called Siamese fighting fish.
what does a betta fish need | how big of a tank does a betta need => A betta needs at least about a 19 liter (5 gallon) tank with a heater set around 25 to 27 °C, a gentle filter and places to hide.
how do betta fish breathe air | why does my betta go to the top => Bettas have a special organ called the labyrinth that lets them gulp air from the surface, so going up for air is normal.
what is a bubble nest | why is my betta making bubbles => Male bettas build bubble nests at the surface to hold eggs. It usually means he's healthy and comfortable.
do guppies have live babies | why does my guppy keep having babies => Yes. Guppies give birth to live, swimming babies called fry, often every month. Adults may eat the babies, so plants help them hide.
why do neon tetras need a group | how many neon tetras should i get => Neon tetras and other schooling fish feel safe in groups. Keep at least 6 together, or they get stressed.
do fish need a heater | which fish need warm water => Tropical fish like bettas, guppies and tetras need a heater to keep water around 24 to 27 °C. Goldfish are fine at cooler room temperature.

# tag: pets.reptiles
what do leopard geckos eat | what should i feed my leopard gecko => Leopard geckos eat live insects like crickets, mealworms and dubia roaches, dusted with calcium powder. They don't eat fruit or veggies.
how long do leopard geckos live | leopard gecko lifespan => Leopard geckos often live about 15 to 20 years with good care.
what does a leopard gecko need | leopard gecko tank setup => A leopard gecko needs a tank with a warm side around 30 to 32 °C and a cool side, hides on both sides, a humid hide for shedding and a shallow water dish.
what do bearded dragons eat | what should i feed my bearded dragon => Bearded dragons eat live insects and leafy greens. Babies need mostly insects, while adults eat mostly greens and veggies with some insects.
how long do bearded dragons live | bearded dragon lifespan => Bearded dragons usually live about 10 to 15 years.
why do bearded dragons need uvb | what light does a bearded dragon need => Bearded dragons need a UVB light to make vitamin D and use calcium, or they get a bone disease. They also need a hot basking spot.
why do bearded dragons wave | why does my bearded dragon bob its head => Arm waving usually shows submission or that they see you, and fast head bobbing is often a male showing dominance or interest.
are corn snakes good pets | what do corn snakes eat => Corn snakes are popular beginner snakes because they're calm and easy to care for. They eat thawed frozen mice, about every 1 to 2 weeks for adults.
how long do ball pythons live | why wont my ball python eat => Ball pythons can live 20 to 30 years or more. It's common for them to skip meals, especially in winter or before shedding, but a vet should check long fasts.
how long do pet turtles live | do red eared sliders need a big tank => Red-eared sliders can live 20 to 40 years and grow to about 30 cm, so they need a big tank, clean filtered water, a basking spot and UVB light.
why should you wash your hands after touching reptiles | can turtles make you sick => Reptiles and amphibians often carry salmonella germs, even when healthy. Always wash your hands with soap after touching them or their tank.
how do i set up an axolotl tank | what temperature do axolotls need => Axolotls need cool water, around 16 to 18 °C, so no heater. Use a gentle filter and bare bottom or fine sand, since they can swallow gravel.

# tag: pets.birds
how long do budgies live | budgie lifespan => Budgies, also called parakeets, usually live about 5 to 10 years, and some live longer.
what do budgies eat | what should i feed my parakeet => Budgies do best on pellets or a seed mix plus fresh veggies like leafy greens and carrot. Seeds alone aren't a balanced diet.
what foods are poisonous to birds | can birds eat avocado => Avocado is very toxic to pet birds. Also avoid chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, salty food, onion and fruit seeds and pits.
why are nonstick pans dangerous for birds | can teflon fumes kill a bird => Overheated nonstick pans can give off fumes that can kill pet birds in minutes, because bird lungs are very sensitive. Keep birds away from the kitchen.
can budgies talk | how do you teach a bird to talk => Many budgies can learn words, often males. Repeat short words or phrases clearly every day, and reward the bird when it copies you.
how long do cockatiels live | are cockatiels good pets => Cockatiels can live about 15 to 20 years. They're friendly, love whistling and need daily time out of the cage.
should birds live alone | do budgies need a friend => Budgies and many other small birds are flock animals and are often happier in pairs, especially if you're not home a lot.

# tag: pets.general
what is the best pet for a kid | what pet should i get => It depends on your time, space and family. Fish and small pets need less space, while dogs need lots of time and exercise. Research the care and lifespan before deciding.
how do i convince my parents to get a pet | how do i ask my parents for a dog => Show you're responsible: research the pet's care and costs, make a plan for feeding and cleaning, and offer to help with chores first. A written plan can really help.
should i adopt a pet from a shelter | is it better to adopt or buy a pet => Adopting from a shelter gives a homeless animal a family, and shelters often check the animal's health and personality first. Many great pets are waiting there.
why should you wash your hands after touching pets | can pets give you germs => Pets can carry germs like salmonella and parasites, even when they look healthy. Washing hands with soap after touching pets, their food or their cages keeps you healthy.
how much does it cost to have a dog | are dogs expensive => A dog costs a lot more than just buying it: food, vet visits, vaccines, toys, grooming and supplies can add up to hundreds or thousands each year. Talk about costs with your family.
