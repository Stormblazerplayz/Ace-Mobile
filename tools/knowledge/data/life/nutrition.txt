what nutrients does my body need | what are the main nutrients => Nutrients are the parts of food your body uses to grow, move and stay healthy. The main ones are carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fiber and water.
what are macronutrients | what does macronutrient mean | what are the three macros => Macronutrients are the nutrients you need in big amounts: carbohydrates, proteins and fats. They give your body energy and building materials.
what are micronutrients | what does micronutrient mean | are vitamins micronutrients => Micronutrients are vitamins and minerals. You only need tiny amounts, but your body can't work properly without them.
what are the food groups | what are the five food groups | name the food groups => The main food groups are fruits, vegetables, grains, protein foods and dairy (or calcium-rich alternatives). Eating from all of them helps you get every nutrient you need.
what is myplate | what does myplate show | how should my plate look => MyPlate is a US guide that shows a healthy plate: about half fruits and vegetables, about a quarter grains and about a quarter protein, with a serving of dairy on the side.
should kids eat carbs | do carbs make you fat => Carbs aren't bad. Growing kids need them for energy. Whole grains, fruit, beans and vegetables are the best kinds, while sugary snacks and drinks are better as treats.
what are simple and complex carbs | simple vs complex carbohydrates | what is a complex carb => Simple carbs are sugars that digest fast, like candy and juice. Complex carbs, like whole grains and beans, digest slower and give longer-lasting energy.
what does protein do for your body | why do i need protein | what is protein used for => Protein builds and repairs muscles, skin, hair, organs and blood. It also makes enzymes and antibodies that help your body work and fight germs.
what are good sources of protein | which foods are high in protein => Good protein foods include meat, fish, eggs, milk, yogurt, cheese, beans, lentils, tofu, nuts and seeds.
what are fats | does your body need fat | why do we need fat => Fats give energy, help build cells and your brain, keep you warm and help your body absorb vitamins A, D, E and K. You need some fat every day.
what are saturated fats | what foods have saturated fat | is saturated fat bad => Saturated fats are found in butter, cheese, fatty meat and coconut oil. Too much can be hard on your heart, so it's best to eat them in smaller amounts.
what are trans fats | why are trans fats bad | what foods have trans fats => Trans fats were common in some fried foods and packaged snacks. They're the least healthy fat because they raise bad cholesterol, and many countries now limit them.
what are omega 3s | what foods have omega 3 | why are omega 3 fats good => Omega-3s are healthy fats found in fish like salmon and sardines, plus walnuts, flaxseed and chia. They help your brain, eyes and heart.
what is cholesterol | is cholesterol bad | what does cholesterol do => Cholesterol is a waxy fat your body uses to build cells and make hormones. Your liver makes most of it. Too much of the "bad" kind in the blood can clog arteries over time.
what does calories mean | what are calories in food => A calorie is a unit of energy. Food calories tell you how much energy your body can get from that food.
how many calories does a kid need | how many calories should i eat a day | how much food does a 12 year old need => It depends on age, size, growth and how active you are, often somewhere around 1,800 to 2,600 calories for 12 year olds. Most kids don't need to count, just eat balanced meals and listen to hunger.
should kids count calories | is it good to count calories | should i be on a diet => Kids usually don't need to count calories or diet, because growing bodies need plenty of fuel. If you're worried about your weight or eating, talk with a parent or doctor.
what are vitamins for | why are vitamins important | what do vitamins do in the body => Vitamins help your body turn food into energy, heal, grow, fight germs and keep your eyes, skin and bones healthy. There are 13 essential vitamins.
what does vitamin a do | what is vitamin a good for | what foods have vitamin a => Vitamin A helps you see in dim light and keeps your skin and immune system healthy. It's in carrots, sweet potatoes, spinach, eggs and milk.
what is vitamin c good for | what foods have vitamin c => Vitamin C helps heal cuts, keeps gums healthy, helps your body absorb iron and supports your immune system. Oranges, strawberries, kiwis, peppers and broccoli are full of it.
what does vitamin d do | why do we need vitamin d | how do you get vitamin d => Vitamin D helps your body use calcium to build strong bones. Your skin makes it in sunlight, and it's also in fish, eggs and fortified milk.
what does vitamin e do | what is vitamin e good for | what foods have vitamin e => Vitamin E is an antioxidant that protects your cells and helps your immune system. It's found in nuts, seeds, vegetable oils and leafy greens.
what does vitamin k do | what is vitamin k for | what foods have vitamin k => Vitamin K helps your blood clot so cuts stop bleeding, and it helps build bones. Leafy greens like spinach, kale and broccoli are great sources.
what do b vitamins do | what are the b vitamins | what foods have b vitamins => The B vitamins help your body turn food into energy and make red blood cells. They're in whole grains, meat, eggs, dairy, beans and leafy greens.
what does vitamin b12 do | what foods have b12 | why do vegans need b12 => Vitamin B12 helps make red blood cells and keeps nerves healthy. It's found mainly in animal foods, so vegans usually need fortified foods or a supplement their doctor suggests.
what is folate | what does folic acid do | what foods have folate => Folate is a B vitamin that helps your body make new cells and DNA. It's in leafy greens, beans, oranges and fortified cereals.

what is rickets | what causes rickets => Rickets is a childhood bone disease from not enough vitamin D or calcium, which makes bones soft and bendy. Sunlight and good food prevent it.
what are minerals in food | why do we need minerals | what minerals does the body need => Minerals are elements like calcium, iron, zinc and potassium that come from food. They build bones and teeth, carry oxygen and help nerves and muscles work.
what does iron do | why do we need iron | what foods have iron => Iron helps red blood cells carry oxygen around your body. It's in red meat, beans, lentils, spinach, fortified cereals and dark chicken meat.
what happens if you dont get enough iron | why does low iron make you tired => Anemia means your blood doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells, often from low iron. It can make you tired, pale and short of breath. A doctor can check it with a blood test.
where can i get calcium | what has calcium besides milk => Milk, cheese and yogurt are rich in calcium, and so are fortified plant milks, tofu, broccoli, kale, almonds and canned fish with soft bones.
what does potassium do | what foods have potassium | why do we need potassium => Potassium helps your heart beat steadily and your muscles and nerves work. Bananas, potatoes, beans, oranges and yogurt are good sources.
what does zinc do | what foods have zinc | why do we need zinc => Zinc helps your immune system, wound healing, growth and sense of taste. It's found in meat, beans, nuts, seeds and whole grains.
what does magnesium do | what foods have magnesium => Magnesium helps muscles relax, nerves work and bones stay strong. It's in nuts, seeds, whole grains, beans and leafy greens.
what is sodium | why do we need salt | how much salt is too much => Sodium, mostly from salt, helps control fluids and nerves. Your body needs a little, but too much can raise blood pressure. Most extra salt comes from processed and fast foods.
what is iodine for | what does iodine do => Iodine helps your thyroid make hormones that control growth and energy use. Many countries add it to table salt to prevent iodine shortages.
why is fluoride in water | does fluoride help teeth => Fluoride is a mineral that makes tooth enamel stronger and helps prevent cavities. Many towns add a small amount to tap water, and it's in most toothpaste.
do kids need vitamin pills | should i take vitamins | are vitamin supplements necessary => Most kids who eat a varied diet get enough vitamins from food. Some may need a supplement, but only take one if a parent or doctor says so, since too much can be harmful.
can vitamins be harmful | what happens if you take too many vitamins => Yes. Some vitamins, like A and D, build up in the body and can make you sick in large amounts. Don't take extra vitamins or gummies unless a trusted adult gives them.
what is fiber good for | why do i need fiber | what foods have fiber => Fiber is a part of plants your body can't fully digest. It keeps you full, helps poop move smoothly and feeds good gut bacteria. Beans, whole grains, fruit, vegetables and nuts have lots.

what is added sugar | whats the difference between natural and added sugar | is sugar in fruit bad => Added sugar is put into foods like soda, candy and cereal. Sugar in whole fruit comes packed with fiber, water and vitamins, so fruit is a healthy choice.

is fruit juice healthy | is juice as good as fruit | how much juice should kids drink => 100% juice has vitamins but lots of sugar and little fiber. Whole fruit is better, and juice is best kept to a small glass a day.
is diet soda healthy | is diet soda better than regular | is sugar free soda bad => Diet soda has no sugar, but it has no nutrients either and can still harm teeth because it's acidic. Water and milk are better everyday drinks.
what does it mean to eat the rainbow | why should i eat different colored vegetables => Different colored fruits and veggies have different vitamins and plant chemicals. Eating many colors, like red tomatoes, orange carrots and green spinach, gives you more nutrients.
why are vegetables good for you | what do vegetables do for you => Vegetables are packed with vitamins, minerals and fiber with few calories. They help your immune system, gut, eyes and heart and give you lasting energy.

are bananas healthy | what is good about bananas | do bananas give you energy => Bananas are a healthy, easy snack with potassium, vitamin B6, fiber and natural sugar for quick energy.

is pasta healthy | is pasta bad for you | is whole wheat pasta better => Pasta is a fine source of energy, especially whole wheat pasta, which has more fiber. Pairing it with veggies and protein makes a balanced meal.
