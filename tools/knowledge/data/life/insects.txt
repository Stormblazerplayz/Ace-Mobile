what are dust mites | are there bugs in my bed => Dust mites are microscopic arachnids that eat tiny flakes of dead skin. They live in bedding and carpets and can trigger allergies, so washing sheets in hot water helps.

# tag: animals.insects.general

what are compound eyes | how do insects see => Compound eyes are made of many tiny units, each seeing a small piece of the view. Together they're great at spotting movement.
do insects feel pain | can bugs feel pain => Scientists are still studying this. Some experiments suggest insects like bees and fruit flies may feel something like pain, not just reflexes.
