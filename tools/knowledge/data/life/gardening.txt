# tag: plants.gardening
how do i know when to water a plant | should i water plants every day => Push your finger about 2 to 3 cm into the soil. If it feels dry, water deeply until it drains out the bottom. If it's still damp, wait.
what are signs of overwatering | how do i know if i watered my plant too much => Yellow, droopy leaves, soggy soil, mushy stems or a moldy smell can mean too much water. Roots need air, and constantly wet soil can make them rot.
what are signs of underwatering | why is my plant wilting => Dry, crispy leaf edges, dry soil and drooping that gets better after watering usually mean the plant is thirsty.
why are my plant leaves turning brown | why do plant tips go brown => Brown, crispy tips often come from dry air, underwatering, too much fertilizer or salty tap water.
how long do radishes take to grow | fastest vegetable to grow => Radishes are one of the fastest vegetables, often ready about 3 to 4 weeks after planting.
how do you grow tomatoes | how to grow tomatoes at home => Tomatoes need lots of sun, about 6 to 8 hours a day, warm weather, regular deep watering and a stake or cage to hold them up. Plant them after the last frost.
when should i plant my garden | when is the last frost => Plant most warm-weather crops after your area's last spring frost date, which you can look up for where you live. Cool-weather crops like peas and lettuce can go in earlier.
what does full sun mean for plants | how much sun do plants need => Full sun means at least 6 hours of direct sunlight a day. Part shade means about 3 to 6 hours, and shade plants need less.
what is soil made of | what is in dirt => Soil is a mix of tiny rock bits (sand, silt and clay), decayed plants and animals, water, air and billions of living things like bacteria, fungi and worms.
what does npk mean on fertilizer | what do the numbers on fertilizer mean => The three numbers show nitrogen (N) for leafy growth, phosphorus (P) for roots and flowers, and potassium (K) for overall health.
what is mulch | why do gardeners use mulch => Mulch is a layer of material like wood chips, straw or leaves spread over soil. It keeps moisture in, blocks weeds and keeps roots at a steady temperature.
why do pots need drainage holes | does a plant pot need a hole in the bottom => Drainage holes let extra water escape. Without them, water collects at the bottom and the roots can rot.
when should i repot a plant | how do you know a plant needs a bigger pot => Repot when roots grow out the bottom, circle tightly around the pot or the plant dries out very fast. Move up just one pot size, usually in spring.
what are the easiest houseplants | best plants for beginners indoors => Pothos, snake plants, spider plants, ZZ plants and peace lilies are hard to kill. Check if a plant is safe for pets before bringing it home.
can you regrow vegetables from scraps | what vegetables grow back from scraps => Yes. Green onions, celery bases, lettuce stumps and garlic cloves can regrow in water or soil. Potatoes and sweet potatoes sprout from pieces with eyes.
how do you grow an avocado from a pit | can i grow an avocado tree from a seed => Push three toothpicks into the pit and rest it, pointy end up, in a glass so the bottom sits in water. In about 2 to 8 weeks it should crack and sprout. Fruit takes many years, if ever.
how do you grow a bean in a jar | bean in a cup experiment => Line a jar with damp paper towel, slide a dry bean between the towel and the glass, and keep it damp. In a few days you'll see the root and shoot grow.
how long do sunflowers take to grow | how tall do sunflowers get => Sunflowers usually bloom about 70 to 100 days after planting. Giant kinds can grow over 3 m tall, and the record is over 9 m.
how do i get rid of aphids on my plants | what are the tiny green bugs on my plant => Spray aphids off with water, wipe them with soapy water, or let ladybugs eat them. Check the undersides of leaves where they hide.
why are worms good for a garden | should i put worms in my garden => Earthworms loosen soil, let air and water in, and leave rich castings that feed plants. A garden full of worms is a healthy one.
what is a pollinator garden | how do i attract bees and butterflies => A pollinator garden has flowers that bees, butterflies and hummingbirds love, like lavender, coneflowers, sunflowers and milkweed. Avoid bug sprays and include native plants.
what are grow lights | can plants grow under lamps => Grow lights are lamps, often LEDs, that give plants the light colors they need. They let you grow plants indoors or start seeds before spring.
what is germination time | how long do seeds take to sprout => Most garden seeds sprout in about 3 to 14 days, depending on the plant and temperature. Peppers and parsley can take a few weeks.
why didnt my seeds sprout | why wont my seeds grow => Common reasons are soil that's too cold, too dry or too wet, seeds planted too deep, or old seeds. Check the packet for the right depth and temperature.
what is a seedling | when do you move seedlings outside => A seedling is a young plant just sprouted. Before planting outside, set them out for a few hours a day for about a week to get used to sun and wind. This is called hardening off.
what soil ph do plants like | is my soil acidic => Soil pH measures how acidic or alkaline soil is. Most garden plants like slightly acidic to neutral soil, about pH 6 to 7. Blueberries like more acidic soil.
can you grow plants in sand | will plants grow in sandy soil => Plain sand drains too fast and holds few nutrients, so most plants struggle. Mixing in compost helps a lot. Cacti and some beach plants do fine in sandy soil.
why do farmers rotate crops | what is crop rotation => Crop rotation means planting different crops in a field each year. It keeps soil healthy, since different plants use different nutrients, and it breaks pest cycles.
what is a greenhouse | how does a greenhouse work => A greenhouse is a glass or plastic building that traps heat from sunlight, keeping plants warm so they can grow in cold weather.
what should i plant in winter | what grows in cold weather => Garlic is planted in fall and harvested in summer. Kale, spinach and some lettuces can handle cold, especially with a cover. Indoors, you can grow herbs and microgreens.
what are microgreens | how do you grow microgreens => Microgreens are tiny young plants like radish, pea or sunflower shoots, harvested about 1 to 3 weeks after sprouting. They grow well on a sunny windowsill.

# tag: plants.flowers
how many petals does a rose have | where do roses come from => Wild roses usually have 5 petals, while garden roses can have dozens. Roses grow wild across the Northern Hemisphere, and people have grown them for thousands of years.
do roses have thorns | are rose thorns really thorns => Rose "thorns" are technically prickles, sharp growths from the stem's outer skin. They help roses climb and keep animals from eating them.
why do sunflower seeds grow in spirals | what is the pattern in a sunflower => Sunflower seeds grow in two sets of spirals going opposite ways. The numbers of spirals are usually Fibonacci numbers like 34 and 55, which packs seeds in tightly.
is a sunflower one flower | is a daisy one flower => A sunflower or daisy head is actually hundreds of tiny flowers packed together. The petals around the edge and the center bits are all separate little flowers.
what was tulip mania | why were tulips so expensive => In the 1630s in the Netherlands, tulip bulbs became so popular that some sold for more than a house. The prices crashed in 1637, and it's often called the first economic bubble.
what is a lotus flower | why is the lotus special => The lotus grows from muddy ponds but its leaves stay clean, because tiny bumps make water roll off and carry dirt away. It's a symbol of purity in many Asian cultures.
what is the biggest flower family | how many kinds of orchids are there => Orchids are one of the biggest plant families, with about 28,000 species. Vanilla comes from an orchid.
are dandelions weeds | can you eat dandelions => Dandelions are often called weeds, but every part is edible, from leaves to flowers to roots. Their seeds float on fluffy parachutes and can travel far on the wind.
why do some flowers close at night | do flowers sleep at night => Some flowers close at night to protect their pollen from dew and cold and to save energy, then open again in the morning when pollinators are active.
which flowers bloom at night | what flowers open at night => Moonflowers, evening primrose, night-blooming jasmine and some cacti open at night. They're often white and strongly scented to attract moths and bats.
why is lavender popular | what is lavender used for => Lavender is loved for its calming scent and is used in soaps, oils and teas. Bees love its purple flowers, and it grows well in sunny, dry spots.
what flowers do hummingbirds like | how do i attract hummingbirds => Hummingbirds love bright red, orange and pink tube-shaped flowers, like salvia, bee balm, trumpet vine and fuchsia.
why do flowers die after you cut them | how do you make cut flowers last longer => Cut flowers lose their roots, so they slowly run out of water and food. Trim the stems at an angle, change the water every couple of days and remove leaves below the water.
why do people give flowers on valentines day | what do red roses mean => Red roses became a symbol of love in Europe hundreds of years ago, partly from old stories and poems, and people still give them on Valentine's Day.
what is a bulb | how do bulbs grow => A bulb, like an onion, tulip or daffodil, is an underground bud wrapped in fleshy leaves that store food. Many bulbs are planted in fall to bloom in spring.
what is a wildflower | why are wildflowers important => Wildflowers grow naturally without being planted by people. They feed native bees, butterflies and other insects.
why do bees like flowers | what do bees get from flowers => Flowers give bees sugary nectar for energy and pollen for protein. In return, bees carry pollen between flowers so plants can make seeds.
