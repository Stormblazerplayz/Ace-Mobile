# tag: science.earth.gems
what is a ruby made of | why are rubies red | ruby mineral => A ruby is the mineral corundum (aluminium oxide) colored red by a little chromium. It's 9 on the Mohs scale, second only to diamond among common gems.
what is a sapphire made of | why are sapphires blue | can sapphires be other colors => A sapphire is corundum, the same mineral as ruby. Traces of iron and titanium make it blue, and sapphires also come in yellow, pink, green and other colors. Only red corundum is called ruby.
what is an emerald made of | why are emeralds green | emerald mineral => An emerald is the mineral beryl colored green by small amounts of chromium or vanadium. It's about 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale and often has tiny inclusions inside.
what is aquamarine | aquamarine mineral | is aquamarine related to emerald => Aquamarine is the pale blue to blue-green form of beryl, the same mineral as emerald, colored by iron.
what is topaz | topaz colors | topaz hardness => Topaz is a silicate mineral containing aluminium and fluorine. It's 8 on the Mohs scale and comes in many colors. Most blue topaz sold has been treated to get its color.
what is amethyst made of | why is amethyst purple | amethyst mineral => Amethyst is purple quartz. Its color comes from traces of iron and natural radiation. Heating it can turn it yellow, into citrine.
what is citrine | citrine mineral | yellow quartz => Citrine is yellow to orange quartz. Natural citrine is rare, so most is made by heating amethyst.
what is rose quartz | why is rose quartz pink | pink quartz => Rose quartz is pale pink quartz. Its color comes from tiny fibers of another mineral inside it.
what is an opal made of | why do opals flash colors | opal play of color => Opal is made of tiny silica spheres with water between them. In precious opal, the spheres are neatly stacked and split light into flashing rainbow colors. Australia produces most of the world's opal.
what is a garnet | garnet colors | garnet mineral => Garnets are a group of silicate minerals, usually deep red, but also orange, green and other colors. They're about 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale.
what is peridot | peridot mineral | why is peridot green => Peridot is gem-quality olivine, an olive-green mineral common deep in Earth's mantle. Some has even been found in meteorites.
what is jade | nephrite vs jadeite | jade mineral => Jade is two different minerals: nephrite and jadeite. Both are very tough because their crystals are tightly interlocked, which made jade great for tools and carvings.
what is turquoise | turquoise mineral | why is turquoise blue => Turquoise is a blue to green mineral made of copper, aluminium and phosphate. It's fairly soft, about 5 to 6 on the Mohs scale.
lapis lazuli made of | ultramarine pigment => Lapis lazuli is a deep blue rock made mostly of the mineral lazurite, often with gold-colored pyrite flecks. It was ground up to make the expensive paint color ultramarine.
what is tanzanite | where is tanzanite found | tanzanite color => Tanzanite is a blue-violet variety of the mineral zoisite found in only one small area of Tanzania, near Mount Kilimanjaro. It was discovered in 1967.
what is tourmaline color | watermelon tourmaline | tourmaline gem => Tourmaline comes in more colors than almost any other gem. Watermelon tourmaline is pink in the middle and green on the outside, like a slice of watermelon.
what is moonstone | why does moonstone glow | moonstone mineral => Moonstone is a type of feldspar. Thin layers inside it scatter light, giving a soft, floating blue or white glow.
what is onyx | what is agate | agate vs onyx => Agate and onyx are kinds of chalcedony, a quartz made of tiny crystals. Agate has curved, colorful bands, and onyx has straight, parallel bands, often black and white.
how do pearls form | are pearls gems => A pearl is made by a mollusk, like an oyster, coating an irritant with layers of nacre (calcium carbonate). Pearls are gems but not minerals, because they're made by living things.
what is amber gem | is amber a stone | amber made of => Amber is fossilized tree resin, not a mineral. It's light enough to float in salty water and sometimes holds ancient insects.
what is the hope diamond | why is the hope diamond blue | famous blue diamond => The Hope Diamond is a famous 45.52 carat blue diamond in the Smithsonian museum in Washington, DC. Traces of boron make it blue, and it glows red-orange under ultraviolet light.
what is the biggest diamond ever found | cullinan diamond | largest diamond => The Cullinan Diamond, found in South Africa in 1905, was about 3,106 carats, the biggest gem diamond ever found. It was cut into stones including two in the British Crown Jewels.
what is a carat | how much is a carat | carat vs karat => A carat is a unit of mass for gems equal to 0.2 grams. Karat, with a k, measures how pure gold is, out of 24.
how are diamonds formed | where do diamonds come from | how old are diamonds => Most diamonds formed 150 to 250 km deep in the mantle, under huge heat and pressure, often 1 to 3 billion years ago. Volcanic eruptions brought them up in rocks called kimberlite.
how are lab grown diamonds made | are lab diamonds real | synthetic diamonds => Lab-grown diamonds are real diamond, made either by high pressure and heat or by depositing carbon from a gas. They're chemically the same as mined ones.
what is cubic zirconia | is cubic zirconia a diamond | fake diamonds => Cubic zirconia is a cheap human-made crystal of zirconium oxide that looks like diamond. It's softer, about 8 on the Mohs scale, and heavier.
what are the four cs of diamonds | how are diamonds graded | diamond quality => Diamonds are graded by the four Cs: cut (how well it's shaped), color, clarity (how few flaws it has) and carat (its weight).
how do you tell a real diamond | is my diamond real | diamond test => Jewelers use tools that measure how fast heat moves through the stone, since diamond conducts heat extremely well. Real diamond also scratches glass and doesn't fog up long when you breathe on it.
what is a gemologist | what does a gemologist do | gemology => A gemologist is an expert who identifies and grades gemstones, using tools like loupes, microscopes and refractometers.
what is mother of pearl | nacre | why is mother of pearl shiny => Mother of pearl, or nacre, is the shiny inner layer of some shells. It's made of thin layers of calcium carbonate that bend light into rainbow sheens.
