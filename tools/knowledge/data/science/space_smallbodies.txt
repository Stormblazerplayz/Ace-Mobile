# tag: science.space.small-bodies
what is vesta | dawn mission vesta => Vesta is one of the biggest asteroids, about 525 km across, and the brightest one seen from Earth. NASA's Dawn spacecraft orbited it in 2011 and 2012, and some meteorites on Earth came from it.
what is pallas asteroid | pallas asteroid facts | second asteroid discovered => Pallas is one of the largest asteroids, about 510 km across, discovered by Heinrich Olbers in 1802. Its orbit is steeply tilted.
what is bennu | osiris rex bennu => Bennu is a small, rubble-pile asteroid about 490 meters wide. NASA's OSIRIS-REx collected a sample from it that landed on Earth on 24 September 2023.
what is ryugu | hayabusa2 | japan asteroid sample => Ryugu is a dark, diamond-shaped asteroid about 900 meters wide. Japan's Hayabusa2 brought samples of it back to Earth in December 2020, and they contained amino acids.
what is itokawa | hayabusa itokawa | first asteroid sample return => Itokawa is a peanut-shaped asteroid about 330 meters long. Japan's first Hayabusa mission brought tiny grains from it to Earth in 2010, the first asteroid sample ever returned.
what is apophis | will apophis hit earth | apophis 2029 => Apophis is an asteroid about 340 meters wide. It will pass very close to Earth on 13 April 2029, closer than some satellites, but scientists have ruled out an impact for at least the next 100 years.
what is psyche asteroid | metal asteroid => Psyche is a large asteroid about 220 km across that may be rich in metal. NASA's Psyche spacecraft launched in October 2023 and is expected to arrive in 2029.
what is eros asteroid | near shoemaker | first landing on an asteroid => Eros is a peanut-shaped near-Earth asteroid about 34 km long. NASA's NEAR Shoemaker orbited it and then landed on it in February 2001, the first landing on an asteroid.
what is arrokoth | ultima thule | most distant object visited => Arrokoth is a small icy object about 36 km long in the Kuiper Belt, shaped like a flattened snowman. New Horizons flew past it on 1 January 2019, the most distant object ever visited.
what is sedna | sedna dwarf planet | sedna orbit => Sedna is a small, reddish icy world about 1,000 km across with an extremely long, stretched orbit that takes about 11,000 years. It was discovered in 2003.
what is oumuamua | first interstellar object | oumuamua shape => ʻOumuamua, found in October 2017, was the first object known to visit our solar system from another star. It was long and thin or flat, and it sped up slightly as it left, which scientists still debate.
what is 3i atlas | third interstellar object | interstellar comet 2025 => 3I/ATLAS is the third known object to come from outside our solar system, discovered on 1 July 2025. It's a comet passing through on a path that will take it back out to interstellar space.
what is comet borisov | 2i borisov | second interstellar object => 2I/Borisov was the second known interstellar object and the first interstellar comet, discovered in 2019 by amateur astronomer Gennadiy Borisov.
what was comet hale bopp | hale bopp 1997 | brightest comet => Comet Hale-Bopp was one of the most-watched comets ever, easy to see without a telescope for about 18 months around 1997. It won't return for over 2,000 years.
what was shoemaker levy 9 | comet hit jupiter | 1994 jupiter impact => Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 broke into pieces and crashed into Jupiter in July 1994, leaving dark scars bigger than Earth. It was the first collision of two solar system objects ever seen.
what was comet neowise | neowise 2020 | comet in 2020 => Comet NEOWISE was a bright comet seen without a telescope in July 2020. It won't be back for about 6,800 years.
what was comet tsuchinshan atlas | 2024 comet | bright comet 2024 => Comet Tsuchinshan-ATLAS was a bright comet that could be seen without a telescope in October 2024, with a long tail in the evening sky.
what is comet 67p | rosetta comet => Comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko is a rubber-duck-shaped comet about 4 km across. Europe's Rosetta orbited it from 2014 to 2016, and its Philae lander made the first landing on a comet in November 2014.
what is comet encke | shortest period comet | comet that returns every 3 years => Comet Encke has one of the shortest orbits of any comet, returning every 3.3 years. Its dust causes the Taurid meteor showers.
what is comet tempel 1 | deep impact mission | spacecraft hit a comet => NASA's Deep Impact fired a copper impactor into Comet Tempel 1 on 4 July 2005, blasting out material so scientists could study what comets are made of.
how big is a comet nucleus | how big are comets | comet size => A comet's solid nucleus is usually about 1 to 20 km across, but its glowing coma can be bigger than the Sun, and its tail can stretch for millions of kilometers.
why do comets have two tails | comet ion tail | comet dust tail => Many comets have two tails: a curved, whitish dust tail, and a straight, bluish tail of charged gas called the ion tail, pushed by the solar wind. Both point away from the Sun.
what is a trojan asteroid | jupiter trojans | lucy mission => Trojan asteroids share a planet's orbit, gathering 60 degrees ahead of and behind it. Jupiter has thousands. NASA's Lucy, launched in 2021, will visit several of them starting in 2027.
how many asteroids are there | number of known asteroids | how many asteroids have been found => Over 1.4 million asteroids have been found as of 2025. Most orbit in the main belt between Mars and Jupiter, and there are millions more too small to have been spotted.
what is a near earth asteroid | how many asteroids come near earth | planetary defense => Near-Earth asteroids have orbits that bring them close to Earth's. Over 37,000 are known as of 2025, and NASA's planetary defense program tracks them to spot any threat early.
what is the torino scale | asteroid danger scale => The Torino scale rates the danger of an asteroid hitting Earth from 0 (no hazard) to 10 (certain collision causing global catastrophe). Almost every known object is rated 0.
what is the main asteroid belt made of | is the asteroid belt crowded | asteroid belt spacing => The main belt is mostly empty space. Asteroids there are usually hundreds of thousands of kilometers apart, so spacecraft fly through without trouble. All of them together have less mass than our Moon.
what is a centaur in space | chiron centaur | objects between jupiter and neptune => Centaurs are icy small bodies orbiting between Jupiter and Neptune, partly asteroid and partly comet. Chiron, discovered in 1977, was the first known.
what is the big dipper | how to find the big dipper => The Big Dipper is an asterism, a star pattern, of seven bright stars in the constellation Ursa Major. It's shaped like a ladle and is easy to spot in the northern sky.
how to find polaris | using the big dipper to find north => Find the Big Dipper, then follow the two stars at the end of its bowl, Merak and Dubhe, about five times their distance. You'll reach Polaris, the North Star, at the end of the Little Dipper's handle.
what is the little dipper | little dipper stars | ursa minor dipper => The Little Dipper is an asterism in Ursa Minor. Its handle ends at Polaris, the North Star, but most of its stars are faint, so it's hard to see from cities.
what is the winter triangle | winter hexagon | betelgeuse sirius procyon => The Winter Triangle links Betelgeuse, Sirius and Procyon. The bigger Winter Hexagon connects Sirius, Procyon, Pollux, Capella, Aldebaran and Rigel. Both shine on northern winter evenings.
what is the great square of pegasus | pegasus square | autumn star pattern => The Great Square of Pegasus is a large box of four stars seen on autumn evenings. Three of its stars belong to Pegasus and one, Alpheratz, belongs to Andromeda.
what is the teapot in sagittarius | sagittarius teapot | where is the center of the galaxy in the sky => The Teapot is an asterism in Sagittarius shaped like a teapot. The Milky Way rises like steam from its spout, toward the center of our galaxy.
what is the northern cross | cygnus cross | northern cross stars => The Northern Cross is the main star pattern of Cygnus the swan, with Deneb at the top. It lies along the Milky Way.
what is the southern cross | how to find south with the southern cross | crux pointers => The Southern Cross is the constellation Crux, a small, bright cross of four main stars. Extending its long axis about four and a half times points close to the south celestial pole.
what is the false cross | false cross vs southern cross | stars that look like the southern cross => The False Cross is a star pattern in Carina and Vela that looks like the Southern Cross but is bigger and dimmer, and it can fool stargazers.
what is the sickle of leo | how to find leo | leo backwards question mark => The Sickle is a curve of stars in Leo that looks like a backwards question mark, marking the lion's head and mane, with Regulus at the bottom.
what is the w in cassiopeia | how to find cassiopeia | cassiopeia shape => Cassiopeia's five bright stars form a W or M shape on the opposite side of Polaris from the Big Dipper.
