# tag: science.earth.records
what is the hottest temperature ever recorded on earth | hottest air temperature record | hottest place ever => The official record is 56.7 °C (134 °F), measured at Death Valley, California, on 10 July 1913, though some scientists doubt that reading. Satellites have measured ground surfaces over 70 °C.
what is the coldest temperature ever recorded on earth | vostok station record => The coldest air temperature measured at a weather station is minus 89.2 °C, at Vostok Station in Antarctica on 21 July 1983. Satellites found surface spots near minus 98 °C on the Antarctic plateau.
what is the strongest wind ever recorded | fastest wind speed on earth | highest wind gust => The highest wind gust measured, not counting tornadoes, was 408 km/h on Barrow Island, Australia, during Cyclone Olivia in 1996. Radar has measured tornado winds around 480 km/h.
what is the biggest hailstone ever | largest hailstone record | biggest hail => The record hailstone fell in Vivian, South Dakota, on 23 July 2010. It was 20 cm across and weighed about 0.88 kg.
what is the rainiest place on earth | wettest place in the world | most rain in a year => Mawsynram and Cherrapunji in northeast India are among the wettest places, averaging around 11 to 12 meters of rain a year.
how deep is the deepest mine | deepest mine in the world | mponeng gold mine => The deepest mines are gold mines in South Africa, reaching around 4 km down. The rock there is about 60 °C, so the tunnels need huge cooling systems.
what is the deepest lake in the world | lake baikal depth | deepest freshwater lake => Lake Baikal in Russia is the deepest lake, about 1,642 meters deep. It holds about a fifth of all the fresh liquid water on Earth's surface.
angel falls height | tallest waterfall in the world => Angel Falls in Venezuela is the tallest waterfall on land, about 979 meters high, with a single uninterrupted drop of about 807 meters.
what is the longest cave | mammoth cave length | longest cave system => Mammoth Cave in Kentucky is the longest known cave system, with over 680 km of passages mapped as of 2025.
mauna kea vs everest | tallest mountain from the seafloor => Mauna Kea in Hawaii rises about 10,200 meters from its base on the ocean floor, more than Everest's height above sea level. Everest still has the highest peak above sea level, at 8,849 meters.
what is the biggest earthquake ever recorded | strongest earthquake => The strongest earthquake recorded was the magnitude 9.5 Valdivia earthquake in Chile on 22 May 1960. It caused a tsunami that crossed the Pacific to Hawaii and Japan.
what is the biggest iceberg ever | largest iceberg recorded | b15 iceberg => The largest iceberg measured was B-15, which broke off Antarctica's Ross Ice Shelf in March 2000. It was about 295 km long and 37 km wide, larger than Jamaica.
# tag: science.physics.numbers
how many atoms are in the human body | number of atoms in a person | how many atoms make a person => An adult human body contains about 7 billion billion billion atoms (7 × 10²⁷). Most of them are hydrogen.
number of cells in your body | how many cells do humans have => An adult has about 30 trillion human cells, and about the same number of bacteria living mostly in the gut. Most of your cells by number are red blood cells.
how big is a nucleus compared to an atom | how much of an atom is empty space => An atom's nucleus is about 100,000 times smaller across than the atom. If an atom were a stadium, the nucleus would be about the size of a pea in the middle.
how many grains of sand are on earth | sand grains vs stars | more stars than sand => Rough estimates suggest about 10 billion billion (10¹⁹) grains of sand on Earth's beaches. The universe likely has far more stars than that, around a billion trillion (10²¹ to 10²⁴).
how hot is the center of the sun | sun core temperature | how hot is the suns surface => The Sun's core is about 15 million °C, and its visible surface is about 5,500 °C. Its outer atmosphere, the corona, is over a million degrees.
how hot is a candle flame | candle flame temperature => A candle flame is about 1,000 °C in its bright yellow part and hotter, around 1,400 °C, near the faint blue base, where the burning is most complete.
how cold is liquid nitrogen | liquid nitrogen temperature | what does liquid nitrogen do => Liquid nitrogen boils at minus 196 °C. It's used to freeze food fast, store cells and remove warts, and it can cause frostbite in seconds.
absolute zero temperature | coldest thing in the universe => Absolute zero is minus 273.15 °C, or 0 kelvin, where particles have the least possible motion. Labs have cooled atoms to within a billionth of a degree of it.
how fast does the earth go around the sun | earths orbital speed => Earth orbits the Sun at about 107,000 km/h, or nearly 30 km every second.
how fast does light go around the earth | how many times can light circle the earth in a second | speed of light example => Light travels about 300,000 km per second, fast enough to go around Earth about 7.5 times in one second.
how fast is the fastest human made object | fastest spacecraft | parker solar probe speed => NASA's Parker Solar Probe is the fastest human-made object. In December 2024, it reached about 692,000 km/h as it swooped close to the Sun.
mass of the earth | how heavy is earth => Earth's mass is about 6 million billion billion kilograms (5.97 × 10²⁴ kg).
how deep is the crust | crust thickness => Earth's crust is about 5 to 10 km thick under the oceans and about 30 to 50 km thick under continents, up to around 70 km under big mountain ranges.
how hot is the earths core | inner core temperature => Earth's inner core is about 5,200 °C, close to the temperature of the Sun's surface.
how long does it take light from the moon to reach us | moonlight travel time | how far is the moon in light seconds => Moonlight takes about 1.3 seconds to reach Earth, since the Moon is about 384,400 km away.
how many stars can you see at night | how many stars are visible | stars visible without telescope => On a dark, clear night far from city lights, you can see about 2,000 to 3,000 stars at once. Across the whole sky, about 9,000 stars are bright enough to see without a telescope.
number of galaxies in the universe | how many galaxies can we see => Astronomers estimate there are hundreds of billions to around 2 trillion galaxies in the observable universe.
how big is the observable universe | size of the universe | how far can we see in space => The observable universe is about 93 billion light-years across. The whole universe may be much bigger or even infinite.
how old is the oldest rock on earth | oldest rocks | oldest mineral crystal => The oldest known bits of Earth are zircon crystals from Jack Hills, Australia, about 4.4 billion years old. The oldest known rocks, in Canada, are about 4 billion years old.
oldest living tree | methuselah tree => Some Great Basin bristlecone pines in California are over 4,800 years old. A tree called Methuselah is one of the oldest known individual trees.
