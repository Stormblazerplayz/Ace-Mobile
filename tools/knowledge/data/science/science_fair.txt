what are science fair projects with pendulums | pendulum experiment | what affects a pendulum swing => Hang a weight on a string and time 10 swings. Change only the string length, then only the weight. You'll find length changes the swing time but weight barely does.

what goes on a science fair board | science fair board layout | how to set up a tri fold board => Put the title at the top center, the question and hypothesis on the left, the method and materials, the results with graphs in the middle, and the conclusion on the right.
how do i make my science fair board look good | science fair board tips | display board design => Use a big readable title, short text in large fonts, clear graphs and photos of your experiment, and a simple color scheme. Make it readable from a few steps away.

how long does a science fair project take | science fair timeline => Give yourself at least 3 to 4 weeks: one week to plan and research, one or two for experiments, and one to analyze and make your board. Plant projects need more time.
what is the difference between a demonstration and an experiment | is a volcano a good science fair project | demo vs experiment => A demonstration just shows something, like a baking soda volcano. An experiment tests a question by changing one thing and measuring results. Most fairs want experiments.
how do i turn a demo into an experiment | make baking soda volcano an experiment | science fair improve an idea => Change one variable and measure. For a volcano, test how the amount of baking soda changes how high the foam rises, with three trials each.
what is a science fair abstract | how to write an abstract | abstract for science project => An abstract is a short summary, usually under 250 words, giving your question, method, main results and conclusion.
what is background research for a science project | how to research a science project | research paper science fair => Background research means reading about the science behind your question, so you can make a good hypothesis and explain your results. Keep a list of your sources.
how many trials should a science fair project have | how many times should i test | science fair trials => Do at least three trials for each condition, and more if you can. More trials make your results more reliable.
