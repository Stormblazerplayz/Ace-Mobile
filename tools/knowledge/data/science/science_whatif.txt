# tag: science.what-if
what if the moon disappeared | what would happen without the moon | what if there was no moon => Tides would shrink to about a third, made only by the Sun. Nights would be much darker, many animals that time things by moonlight would be confused, and over a very long time Earth's tilt could wobble more, changing seasons.
what if the sun disappeared | what would happen if the sun vanished | what if the sun went out => For about 8 minutes we wouldn't notice, because sunlight takes that long to arrive. Then it would go dark, Earth would fly off in a straight line, and the surface would freeze within weeks to months. Deep-sea vent life might survive.
what if earth stopped spinning | what would happen if the earth stopped rotating | what if the earth stopped turning => If it stopped suddenly, everything not attached to the bedrock would keep moving east at up to about 1,600 km/h, causing unimaginable destruction. If it stopped slowly, a day would last a year, with six months of light and six of dark.
what if earth spun faster | what if a day was shorter | what if the earth rotated twice as fast => Days would be shorter, winds and storms would be stronger, and Earth would bulge more at the equator. At about 17 times faster, things at the equator would become weightless.
what if earth had no tilt | what if the earth wasnt tilted | no seasons => With no tilt, there'd be no seasons. Each place would have about the same weather all year, and days and nights would be about 12 hours everywhere.
what if earth was closer to the sun | what if earth moved closer to the sun | too close to the sun => Even 5 to 10% closer could trigger a runaway greenhouse effect, boiling away the oceans like Venus. Earth sits in the habitable zone, where liquid water can exist.
what if you dug a hole through the earth | what if you jumped into a hole through the earth | falling through the earth => In an imaginary airless tunnel straight through the center, you'd fall for about 42 minutes, zoom through the middle, and just reach the other side before falling back. In reality, the heat and pressure make it impossible.
what if you fell into a black hole | what happens if you fall in a black hole | spaghettification => Near a small black hole, gravity would pull your feet much harder than your head and stretch you like spaghetti. With a giant black hole, you could cross the edge without feeling it, but you could never get back out.
what if you went to space without a spacesuit | what happens to a body in space without a suit | can you survive in space without a suit => You'd pass out in about 10 to 15 seconds from lack of oxygen, and could survive only about a minute or two if rescued fast. You wouldn't explode, but your skin would swell and the water on your tongue would boil.
what if you went to jupiter | could you stand on jupiter | what if you landed on jupiter => You couldn't land, because Jupiter has no solid surface. You'd sink through thicker and thicker gas until the crushing pressure and heat destroyed any spacecraft.
what if you went to venus | what would happen on venus => The surface of Venus is about 464 °C with pressure about 92 times Earth's, like being 900 meters underwater. Soviet Venera landers survived only about an hour or two.
what if a big asteroid hit earth | what would happen if an asteroid hit earth | asteroid impact effects => A rock 10 km across, like the one 66 million years ago, would cause huge fires, tsunamis and years of dark, cold skies. NASA tracks nearly all asteroids that big and none threaten Earth for at least the next century.
what if all the ice on earth melted | ice melt map => Sea levels would rise about 70 meters, flooding coasts, low countries and many big cities. It would take thousands of years to fully happen.
what if there was no gravity | what if gravity turned off | what would happen without gravity => The air and oceans would drift off into space, Earth would break apart, and the Sun would stop holding together. Gravity is what holds planets and stars in one piece.
what if gravity was twice as strong | what if earth had more gravity | stronger gravity effects => You'd feel twice as heavy, walking would be exhausting, falls would be more dangerous, and trees and animals would evolve to be shorter and sturdier.
could a flat earth exist | why cant planets be flat => A flat planet couldn't stay flat: gravity pulls everything toward the center, squashing any big object into a ball. That's why all large planets and moons are round.
what if we lived on mars | what would life on mars be like => You'd need a pressurized home and spacesuit, since the air is thin carbon dioxide and it averages about minus 65 °C. Gravity is 38% of Earth's, a day lasts 24 hours 37 minutes, and radiation is high.
what if the oceans dried up | what if all the water disappeared | earth without oceans => Most life would die, the weather would collapse with no water cycle, and the land would become a hot and cold desert. The ocean also absorbs heat and carbon dioxide and makes much of our oxygen.
what if plants stopped photosynthesis | what if all plants died | what would happen without plants => Nearly all food chains would collapse, since plants feed almost everything. Oxygen in the air would fall very slowly over thousands of years, but starvation would come far sooner.
what if all bacteria disappeared | what if there were no bacteria | world without germs => It would be a disaster. Dead things would stop rotting, soil would lose nutrients, many animals couldn't digest food, and nitrogen cycles would break down.
what if bees went extinct | world without pollinators => Many fruits, nuts and vegetables would become scarce and expensive, because bees help pollinate about a third of the crops we eat. Wind-pollinated crops like wheat, rice and corn would be fine.
what if mosquitoes went extinct | what if we got rid of mosquitoes | mosquito extinction effects => Fewer people would get malaria and other diseases. Some fish, birds and bats eat them, but scientists think most ecosystems could adapt, though they debate it.
what if dinosaurs never went extinct | what if the asteroid missed | dinosaurs and humans => Mammals might have stayed small, and humans might never have evolved. The asteroid opened space for mammals to grow and spread.
what if you could travel at the speed of light | what if you rode on a beam of light | light speed travel => Nothing with mass can reach light speed, because it would take infinite energy. As you get closer, time slows for you, so near light speed you could cross the galaxy in your lifetime while thousands of years pass on Earth.
what if you traveled back in time | is time travel possible | can we go back in time => Traveling forward in time is real: fast travel and strong gravity slow your clock. Traveling back isn't known to be possible, and it would cause paradoxes, like stopping your own birth.
what if humans could photosynthesize | could people make food from sunlight | photosynthesis in humans => It wouldn't be enough. Your skin covers about 2 square meters, which would make only a tiny fraction of the energy you need. That's why plants that rely on it don't run around.
what if you were shrunk to the size of an ant | what if you were tiny | small creatures physics => You could fall from any height without getting hurt, water drops would be sticky blobs, and you'd lose body heat fast. Gravity matters less and surface tension matters more when you're small.
what if animals were as big as buildings | why cant insects be giant | square cube law => Giant insects couldn't breathe, because their air tubes don't scale up, and huge animals would need impossibly thick legs. As size doubles, weight grows about eight times but bone strength only four times.
what if the earths core cooled | what if earths core stopped | what if earth lost its magnetic field => Earth would lose its magnetic field, letting the solar wind slowly strip away the atmosphere, as probably happened on Mars. The core is cooling very slowly and will stay hot for billions of years.
what if earth had two suns | planets with two suns | real tatooine => You'd see two shadows and two sunsets. Real planets that orbit two stars, like Kepler-16b, have been found since 2011.
what if a star exploded near earth | what if a nearby supernova happened | how close is a dangerous supernova => A supernova within about 30 to 50 light years could damage the ozone layer. No star that close is ready to explode, and Betelgeuse at roughly 550 to 700 light years is far enough to be safe.
what if the sun became a black hole | will the sun become a black hole | sun black hole mass => The Sun is far too small to become a black hole. But if it did, with the same mass, Earth would keep orbiting just the same. It would just be dark and cold.
what if the speed of light was slower | what if light was slow | slow light thought experiment => If light were much slower, you'd see things where they were moments ago, and strange relativity effects would show up in everyday life. In labs, light has even been slowed to walking speed inside special materials.
what if you sneezed in space | can you burp in space | bodily functions in space => Sneezing is fine, but tiny droplets float around, so astronauts cover their mouths. Burping is messy because gas and food don't separate without gravity, so it often comes with a little vomit.
what if you cried in space | do tears fall in space | crying in space => Tears don't fall in space. They form a watery ball that sticks to your eye and can sting until wiped away, as astronaut Chris Hadfield showed.
what if you lit a candle in space | does fire burn in space | flames in zero gravity => In microgravity, a flame becomes a dim blue sphere, because hot gas doesn't rise without gravity. Fires are very dangerous on spacecraft, so they're tested carefully.
what if the earth had no atmosphere | what if air disappeared | earth without air => There would be no air to breathe, no weather and no sound. The sky would look black, temperatures would swing hugely between day and night, and meteors would hit the ground.
what if volcanoes stopped erupting | why are volcanoes important for earth | volcanoes and life => Over millions of years, volcanoes help recycle carbon and minerals, and helped build the atmosphere and oceans. Without them, carbon would slowly get locked away and Earth would cool.
what if lightning didnt exist | why is lightning important | lightning and nitrogen => Lightning helps fix nitrogen from the air into forms plants can use, though soil bacteria do most of that work. Without it, some wildfires that renew forests wouldn't start.
what if everyone jumped at once | can people make an earthquake by jumping | everyone jump at the same time => It would barely do anything. Earth is about 80 trillion times heavier than all humans, so a jump would make a tiny wobble far too small to feel.
what if earth had no moon tides | what if there were no tides | life without tides => Tides mix coastal waters and nutrients, and tide pools may have helped life move onto land. Without the Moon, tides would be much smaller, made only by the Sun.
