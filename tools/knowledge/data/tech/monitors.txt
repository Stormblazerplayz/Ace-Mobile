# tag: tech.monitors
what is a good refresh rate for gaming | is 200hz good | 144hz vs 240hz => Higher refresh rates make motion smoother and lower input lag. 144 Hz is a big jump from 60 Hz, and 200 to 240 Hz feels even smoother if your PC can reach those FPS.
do i need 200 fps for a 200hz monitor | fps higher than refresh rate => You get the smoothest result when FPS is close to your refresh rate, but anything above 60 still feels better on a high refresh screen. Extra FPS above the refresh rate lowers input lag a little.
why does my 200hz monitor feel like 60hz | high refresh not working => Windows may be set to 60 Hz. Change it in Settings, System, Display, Advanced display, use a DisplayPort cable, and check the monitor's own menu has the full rate turned on.
should i use displayport or hdmi for my monitor | dp vs hdmi gaming => DisplayPort is usually the best choice for PC monitors and supports high refresh rates. HDMI 2.1 is also great. Older HDMI versions may cap you below your monitor's top refresh rate.
what is response time on a monitor | 1ms response time meaning => Response time is how fast pixels change color, in milliseconds. Faster means less blur and ghosting in motion. OLEDs are nearly instant.
what is overdrive on a monitor | monitor overdrive setting => Overdrive pushes pixels to change faster. The middle setting is often best; the highest can cause bright or dark trails called overshoot.
what is g sync | what is freesync => G-SYNC (NVIDIA) and FreeSync (AMD) make the monitor's refresh rate match the game's FPS, so there's no tearing and less stutter. Many FreeSync monitors also work as G-SYNC Compatible.
how do i turn on g sync | enable gsync nvidia => Open the NVIDIA Control Panel, Display, Set up G-SYNC, and tick Enable G-SYNC, G-SYNC Compatible. Turn on Adaptive-Sync or FreeSync in the monitor's menu too.
how do i turn on freesync on amd | enable freesync => Turn on FreeSync in the monitor's menu, then in AMD Software open Gaming, Display, and turn on AMD FreeSync.
what fps cap should i use with gsync | cap fps for vrr => Cap your FPS a few frames below your refresh rate, like about 195 on a 200 Hz monitor, so it stays in the VRR range. NVIDIA Reflex can set a cap for you.
what does ips mean on a monitor | ips vs va vs tn => IPS has great colors and viewing angles. VA has deeper blacks but can smear in dark scenes. TN is fast and cheap but has poor colors. OLED has perfect blacks and fast response but costs more.
what is an oled monitor | is oled good for gaming => OLED pixels make their own light, giving perfect blacks, huge contrast and nearly instant response. They're excellent for gaming but cost more and can get burn-in from static images.
what is 1080p vs 1440p | which resolution should i get => 1080p (1920x1080) is easy to run at high FPS. 1440p (2560x1440) is sharper and a great middle ground for midrange GPUs. 4K (3840x2160) needs a very strong GPU for high FPS.
what monitor size is best for 1080p | 24 or 27 inch monitor => 24 inches suits 1080p well. 27 inches is the sweet spot for 1440p. At 27 inches 1080p looks less sharp because the pixels are bigger.
what is ppi | pixel density => PPI (pixels per inch) is how tightly packed the pixels are. Higher PPI looks sharper. A 27 inch 1440p monitor has about 109 PPI.
what is hdr on a monitor | is hdr400 worth it => HDR shows brighter highlights and more color range. Monitors labeled HDR400 often can't get bright enough to show real HDR, so it can look washed out. OLED or HDR1000 monitors do it much better.
what is a curved monitor | is a curved monitor good => Curved monitors wrap around your view, which helps on wide screens. On a normal 24 inch screen, curved vs flat is mostly personal taste.
what is an ultrawide monitor | 21 9 monitor => An ultrawide has a 21:9 shape, wider than normal 16:9. It's great for racing games and editing timelines, but some games show black bars on the sides.
what is a monitor's aspect ratio | 16 9 meaning => Aspect ratio is width compared to height. 16:9 is standard, 21:9 is ultrawide, and 16:10 is a little taller, common on laptops.
what is motion blur reduction | elmb or dyac => Backlight strobing (names like ELMB, DyAc or ULMB) flashes the backlight to make motion look sharper. It dims the screen and usually can't run with VRR at the same time.
what is a monitor stand vesa | vesa mount => VESA is a standard screw pattern on the back of monitors, like 100x100 mm. It lets you put the monitor on any matching arm or wall mount.
how do i clean my monitor screen | wipe a monitor safely => Turn it off and wipe gently with a dry microfiber cloth. For marks, dampen the cloth slightly with water. Never spray liquid on the screen or use glass cleaner.
how far should my monitor be | monitor distance from eyes => About an arm's length away, with the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level. Take breaks using the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
what is a monitor osd | monitor menu buttons => The OSD (on-screen display) is the monitor's own menu, opened with buttons on the back or bottom. It controls brightness, input, overdrive, FreeSync and color modes.
what is black equalizer | see enemies in dark corners => Black equalizer or shadow boost brightens dark areas so you can see into shadows. It's in many gaming monitors' menus and can help in shooters.
what is a 10 bit monitor | 8 bit vs 10 bit color => A 10 bit panel can show far more shades than 8 bit, giving smoother gradients. It matters most for HDR and color work.
what does srgb mode mean | srgb vs wide gamut => sRGB is the standard color space most content uses. Wide gamut monitors show extra-saturated colors; an sRGB mode makes normal content look accurate.
how do i use my tv as a monitor | tv as pc monitor => Connect with HDMI, set the TV to Game Mode to cut lag, and turn on PC mode or 4:4:4 if it has it, so text looks sharp.
is 60hz bad for gaming | can i game on 60hz => 60 Hz is fine for most games, but high refresh monitors make fast games feel much smoother and more responsive.
how do i check if my monitor is running at full hz | test refresh rate => Check Settings, System, Display, Advanced display, or use the monitor's OSD, which often shows the live refresh rate. Websites like TestUFO also show it.
what is the testufo website | ghosting test => TestUFO (testufo.com) shows moving test images to check refresh rate, ghosting and motion blur on your monitor.
