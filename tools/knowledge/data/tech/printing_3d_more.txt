# tag: tech.printing.more
why does my a1 say the nozzle is clogged | a1 extrusion error => Unload the filament, heat the nozzle, and try loading again. If nothing comes out, do a cold pull or swap the hotend; the A1's quick-swap hotend makes this easy.
why is my ams lite not feeding | ams lite filament stuck => Check the spool turns freely, the filament end is cut clean at an angle, the PTFE tubes are seated, and there's no tangle. Cardboard spools and heavy spools can slip.
why does my a1 keep failing the first layer in one corner | bed not level a1 corner => Clean that area of the plate, check nothing is stuck under the plate, and run bed leveling again. If it persists, the bed may need the tramming check from Bambu's wiki.
how do i print tpu on the a1 | tpu with ams lite => Print TPU from the external spool holder, not through the AMS lite, use the TPU preset, print slowly, and dry it first.
what nozzle sizes does the a1 support | a1 0.2 0.6 0.8 nozzle => Bambu sells A1 hotends in 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm. Swap them with the quick-swap clip and change the nozzle setting in the slicer to match.
how loud is the bambu a1 | can i run my a1 overnight => The A1 is fairly quiet in Silent mode but not silent. Many people run prints overnight, but keep it away from where people sleep and make sure smoke alarms work.
how do i send a print from bambu studio | print plate bambu studio => Slice the plate, click Print plate, choose your printer, check the filament mapping and options like timelapse and bed leveling, then click Send.
what is filament mapping in bambu studio | ams slot mapping => When printing, Bambu Studio matches each color in the model to an AMS slot. Check the mapping before sending so the right spool prints each part.
how do i print several things at once | multiple plates bambu studio => Add models to one plate and press A to auto-arrange them, or add more plates with the plus button and print them one by one.
how do i scale a model in bambu studio | resize a model slicer => Select the model, press S or use the Scale tool, and type a percentage or exact size in millimeters. Keep the lock on to scale evenly.
how do i rotate a model to lie flat | lay on face bambu studio => Select the model, press F or click Lay on face, then click the face that should touch the bed.
what is auto orient in the slicer | auto orient in the slicer explained => Auto orient turns the model to the position the slicer thinks needs the least support and gives the best surface. Check it, since it doesn't know which side you want nice.
how do i see the print preview layer by layer | slicer preview slider => After slicing, open the Preview tab and drag the slider on the right to see each layer, and the bottom slider to watch the moves in a layer.
how long will this print take | bambu print time estimate => After slicing, Bambu Studio shows the estimated time and filament used in the bottom right. Real times can vary a bit.
how do i make a print faster | speed up 3d print => Use a bigger layer height, fewer walls if strength isn't needed, lower infill, a bigger nozzle, and print several parts per plate. Avoid supports when you can.
what is sparse infill vs solid infill | sparse infill vs solid infill explained => Sparse infill is the low-density pattern inside. Solid infill fills completely, used for top and bottom layers and small areas.
what is the best infill for speed | best infill for speed explained => Lightning infill is fastest for decorative prints since it only supports the top. Grid or rectilinear infill is quick for general parts; gyroid is great for strength in all directions.
what is lightning infill | lightning infill explained => Lightning infill grows thin branches only where top surfaces need support, saving lots of time and filament. It's for looks, not strength.
how do i print a model bigger than my bed | split large model => Cut it into pieces with the slicer's Cut tool, add connectors, print the parts, and glue them together.
how do i make threads that work in 3d prints | printing screw threads => Use a thread tool in your CAD app, add a little clearance, and print with a small layer height and the threads standing up. M8 and larger threads print best.
how do i print text that's readable | small text 3d print => Make text at least about 1 mm stroke width, emboss it 0.6 mm or more, use a fine layer height, and print it facing up. A 0.2 mm nozzle helps with tiny text.
what is a support blocker | stop supports in one place => A support blocker is a shape you add in the slicer where you don't want supports. Paint-on supports work similarly.
why does my print have a weird line on the side | z banding => Repeating lines up the side can come from a dirty Z screw or rod, wobbly frame, or temperature swings. Clean and check the Z axis and print on a sturdy surface.
what is wet filament popping | wet filament popping explained => Popping or crackling at the nozzle is water in the filament turning to steam. Stop and dry the spool, or prints get bubbly and weak.
why are my prints shiny in some spots and matte in others | uneven gloss on print => Speed and temperature changes affect how shiny some filaments look. Constant speed walls and steady cooling make the finish more even.
how do i make prints watertight | waterproof 3d print => Use more walls (4 or more), slightly higher temperature and flow, and PETG for water. Sealing with epoxy or a coating makes it fully watertight.
are 3d printed cups food safe | can i drink from a 3d print => Not reliably. Layer lines trap bacteria, and many filaments and nozzles aren't certified food safe. Use them for decoration, not food or drinks.
how strong is pla compared to petg | which is tougher pla or petg => PLA is stiffer and easier to print but brittle and weak in heat. PETG is tougher, bends before breaking and handles heat and sunlight better.
best selling 3d prints ideas | what prints do people buy => Popular sellers include custom name signs, desk organizers, articulated toys, phone stands, cosplay props and replacement parts. Check model licenses before selling others' designs.
how do i photograph my 3d prints to sell | product photos => Use soft daylight or a light box, a plain background, a phone on a stand and a few angles. Show the size with a hand or ruler.
what is a 3d print farm | 3d print farm explained => A print farm is many printers running at once, often to sell prints. Good cooling, power, filament storage and organized files matter a lot.
what is an articulated print | flexi dragon => Articulated prints have joints printed in place so they wiggle, like flexi dragons and fidget toys. They need good bed adhesion and the right clearances.
how do i print a cosplay helmet | large cosplay prints => Split the helmet into parts, print in PLA or PETG with 3 walls and low infill, glue the parts, then sand, fill and paint.
how do i add a magnet to a print | embed magnets pause print => Design a hole slightly bigger than the magnet, add a pause at the layer just above it in the slicer, drop magnets in when it pauses, and resume.
what is a filament runout sensor | filament runout sensor explained => It detects when the filament runs out and pauses the print so you can load more instead of printing air.
