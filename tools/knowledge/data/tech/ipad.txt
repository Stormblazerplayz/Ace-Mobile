# tag: tech.ipad.basics
how do i screenshot on an ipad with no home button | ipad air screenshot buttons => Press the top button and either volume button at the same time, then let go quickly. On iPads with a Home button, press the top button and the Home button together.
how do i take a full page screenshot on ipad | screenshot a whole web page ipad => Take a screenshot in Safari, tap the preview, then tap Full Page at the top. Tap Done and save it as a PDF to Files.
how do i screen record on my ipad | record my ipad screen => Open Control Center and tap the Screen Recording button (a circle in a ring). Recording starts after 3 seconds. Tap the red indicator to stop, and the video goes to Photos.
how do i add screen recording to control center | screen record button missing ipad => Open Control Center, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control, and pick Screen Recording.
how do i record my ipad screen with sound | screen recording with mic => In Control Center, touch and hold the Screen Recording button and tap Microphone to turn it on, then start recording.
how do i force restart my ipad | ipad frozen won't turn off => On iPads without a Home button: press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then hold the top button until the Apple logo appears.
how do i turn off my ipad | shut down ipad => Press and hold the top button and either volume button until the slider shows, then drag it. You can also go to Settings, General, Shut Down.
how do i open control center on ipad | swipe for control center => Swipe down from the top right corner of the screen.
how do i see my notifications on ipad | notification center ipad => Swipe down from the top middle or top left of the screen. Swipe up to see older ones.
how do i close apps on ipad | force close an app ipad => Swipe up from the bottom and pause in the middle to open the App Switcher, then swipe the app's card up off the screen. You don't need to do this normally, only if an app is stuck.
how do i search my ipad | spotlight ipad => Swipe down from the middle of the Home Screen to open Spotlight, then type what you're looking for.
how do i rearrange apps on my ipad | move app icons => Touch and hold the Home Screen until icons jiggle, then drag apps where you want. Drag one app onto another to make a folder. Tap Done when finished.
how do i add a widget on ipad | ipad home screen widgets => Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Pick one, choose a size and tap Add Widget.
what is the app library on ipad | find all my apps ipad => The App Library is in the Dock on the right side (or swipe left past your last Home Screen page). It groups every app into categories and has a search bar.
how do i hide an app on ipad | lock an app with face id => Touch and hold the app, then tap Require Face ID (or Touch ID). You can lock it, or hide it so it only shows in a hidden folder in the App Library.
how do i delete an app on ipad | uninstall app ipad => Touch and hold the app, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Remove from Home Screen keeps it in the App Library instead.
how do i update my ipad | ipados update => Open Settings, General, Software Update, and tap Update Now. Plug it in and use Wi-Fi first.
what ipados version do i have | check ipad version => Open Settings, General, About. It shows iPadOS Version and your model name.
what is ipados 26 | why did ipados jump to 26 => In 2025 Apple renamed its versions by year, so iPadOS 18 was followed by iPadOS 26. It added a new windowing system with free-moving windows and a menu bar, and the Liquid Glass design.
how do i update apps on ipad | app store updates => Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, and pull down to refresh. Tap Update All. Turn on App Updates in Settings, App Store to do it automatically.
how do i turn on dark mode on ipad | ipad dark theme => Open Settings, Display and Brightness, and tap Dark. Or touch and hold the brightness slider in Control Center and tap the Dark Mode button.
what is night shift on ipad | warmer screen ipad night => Night Shift makes colors warmer at night. Turn it on in Settings, Display and Brightness, Night Shift, and set a schedule.
what is true tone | turn off true tone => True Tone adjusts the screen's white balance to the room's light. Turn it off in Settings, Display and Brightness if you need exact colors for art.
how do i make text bigger on ipad | ipad font size => Open Settings, Display and Brightness, Text Size, and drag the slider. Accessibility, Display and Text Size has even bigger sizes.
how do i stop my ipad screen turning off | auto lock ipad => Open Settings, Display and Brightness, Auto-Lock, and pick a longer time or Never.
what is low power mode on ipad | save ipad battery => Low Power Mode cuts background activity and effects to make the battery last longer. Turn it on in Settings, Battery, or from Control Center.
how do i see what uses my ipad battery | battery usage by app => Open Settings, Battery. It shows which apps used the most battery in the last 24 hours or 10 days.
how do i set up face id or touch id on ipad | add fingerprint ipad => Open Settings, then Touch ID and Passcode (or Face ID and Passcode), enter your passcode, and follow the steps to add a fingerprint or face.
how do i change my ipad passcode | new passcode ipad => Open Settings, Touch ID (or Face ID) and Passcode, enter your passcode, and tap Change Passcode.
how do i use focus mode on ipad | do not disturb ipad => Open Control Center and tap Focus, then pick Do Not Disturb or another focus. Set up custom focuses in Settings, Focus.
how do i stop notifications from an app on ipad | turn off app notifications => Open Settings, Notifications, tap the app, and turn off Allow Notifications.
how do i use picture in picture on ipad | pip video ipad => While a video plays full screen, swipe up to go Home, and it shrinks into a small floating window. Drag it to any corner, or pinch to resize it.
how do i mirror my ipad to a tv | airplay ipad to tv => Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your Apple TV or AirPlay smart TV. A USB-C to HDMI adapter also works.
how do i use an external monitor with ipad | ipad second screen => Connect a USB-C monitor or a USB-C to HDMI adapter. iPads with M-series chips can extend the screen with Stage Manager; others mirror it.
how do i connect a game controller to ipad | ps5 controller ipad | xbox controller ipad => Put the controller in pairing mode (PS + Create on PlayStation, or hold the pair button on Xbox), then open Settings, Bluetooth and tap it.
how do i connect airpods to ipad | pair headphones ipad => Open the AirPods case near the unlocked iPad and tap Connect. For other headphones, put them in pairing mode and tap them in Settings, Bluetooth.
how do i use guided access | lock ipad to one app => Turn on Guided Access in Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access. Then triple-click the top button in an app to lock the iPad to it. Triple-click again and enter the code to exit.
how do i copy text from a photo on ipad | live text => Open the photo, touch and hold the text, adjust the selection and tap Copy. You can also tap the Live Text button in the corner of a photo.
how do i cut out the subject of a photo on ipad | remove background ipad => Open the photo in Photos, touch and hold the main subject until it glows, then tap Copy or Share to use it as a sticker or image with no background.
how do i change my ipad name | rename ipad airdrop name => Open Settings, General, About, Name, and type a new one. This is what shows in AirDrop and Find My.
how do i find my lost ipad | find my ipad => Use the Find My app on another Apple device or sign in at icloud.com/find. You can play a sound, see it on a map or mark it as lost. Find My must be on in Settings first.
what is the apple account | apple id vs apple account => As of 2024, Apple renamed the Apple ID to Apple Account. It's the same login you use for the App Store, iCloud and Find My.
how do i turn on ask to buy | parent approval app store => A parent turns on Ask to Buy in Family Sharing for a child under 18. The child's purchase and free download requests go to the parent to approve.
# tag: tech.ipad.screentime
what is screen time on ipad | how do i see my screen time => Screen Time shows how long you use your iPad and each app, and lets parents set limits. Open Settings, Screen Time to see your daily average and app usage.
how do i set an app limit on ipad | limit time on an app => Open Settings, Screen Time, App Limits, Add Limit, choose apps or categories, and set a daily time.
what is downtime in screen time | schedule time away from ipad => Downtime blocks most apps during a schedule, like bedtime, except calls and apps you choose to always allow. Set it in Settings, Screen Time, Downtime.
what is screen distance on ipad | ipad says too close => Screen Distance uses the Face ID camera to warn you when you hold the iPad closer than about 12 inches for a long time, to help your eyes. Turn it on in Settings, Screen Time, Screen Distance.
what is content and privacy restrictions | block apps on ipad => In Settings, Screen Time, Content and Privacy Restrictions, parents can block explicit content, stop app installs and lock privacy settings.
what are communication limits | who can contact me ipad => Communication Limits in Screen Time control who you can call, FaceTime or message, during allowed screen time and during downtime.
# tag: tech.ipad.storage
how do i see what's using my ipad storage | ipad storage full | check ipad space => Open Settings, General, iPad Storage. It shows a bar of what uses space and lists apps by size with suggestions.
what does offload app mean | offload unused apps => Offloading deletes the app but keeps its documents and data. Tap the app icon later to download it again with your stuff still there. Turn it on in iPad Storage.
how do i delete photos to free space on ipad | recently deleted album => After deleting photos, open the Recently Deleted album in Photos and tap Delete All, since deleted photos stay there for 30 days.
how do i store photos in icloud to save space | optimize ipad storage => Turn on iCloud Photos and Optimize iPad Storage in Settings, Photos. Full quality originals stay in iCloud and smaller versions stay on the iPad.
how much icloud storage do i get for free | icloud 5gb => As of 2025, every Apple Account gets 5 GB of free iCloud storage. iCloud+ plans give more for a monthly fee.
how do i back up my ipad to icloud | ipad backup => Open Settings, tap your name, iCloud, iCloud Backup, and turn on Back Up This iPad. Tap Back Up Now while on Wi-Fi.
how do i back up my ipad to a computer | ipad backup on pc => On a Mac, plug it in and select it in Finder, then click Back Up Now. On Windows, use the Apple Devices app (or iTunes on older setups).
how do i reset my ipad | erase ipad => Open Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPad, then Erase All Content and Settings. Back up first, and turn off Find My before giving it away.
how do i reset network settings on ipad | ipad wifi not working fix => Open Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPad, Reset, Reset Network Settings. It forgets Wi-Fi passwords, so you'll need to reconnect.
how do i clear safari history on ipad | delete browsing history ipad => Open Settings, Apps, Safari, and tap Clear History and Website Data. On older iPadOS, Safari is directly in Settings.
how do i use a usb drive with my ipad | external drive ipad => Plug the USB-C drive in, then open the Files app. The drive shows under Locations, and you can drag files on and off it.
# tag: tech.ipad.files
how do i use the files app | where are my files on ipad => Open Files and tap Browse. iCloud Drive holds files synced across devices, On My iPad holds local files, and other cloud apps and drives show under Locations.
how do i unzip a file on ipad | open zip on ipad => Tap the ZIP file in the Files app and it extracts into a folder next to it.
how do i make a zip file on ipad | compress files ipad => In the Files app, select the files, tap More (three dots) and choose Compress.
how do i find my downloads on ipad | safari downloads ipad => Open the Files app and look in the Downloads folder in iCloud Drive or On My iPad. In Safari, tap the downloads arrow in the address bar.
how do i scan a document with my ipad | scan paper to pdf => In the Files app tap the three dots, then Scan Documents, and hold the iPad over the paper. Notes can scan documents too.
how do i save a web page as pdf on ipad | print to pdf ipad => In Safari tap Share, then Print, then Share again (or pinch out on the preview) and choose Save to Files.
how do i sign a pdf on my ipad | fill in a form ipad => Open the PDF in Files or Mail, tap the Markup pen, then the plus button and choose Signature, or use the text tool to fill in boxes.
# tag: tech.ipad.airdrop
how do i airdrop something | how does airdrop work | send a photo with airdrop => Tap the Share button on a photo or file, tap AirDrop, and pick the nearby person's device. Both devices need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on and must be close.
how do i turn on airdrop receiving | airdrop everyone for 10 minutes => Open Settings, General, AirDrop, and pick Contacts Only or Everyone for 10 Minutes. After 10 minutes it goes back to Contacts Only.
why can't i see someone on airdrop | airdrop not working => Make sure both devices are unlocked, near each other, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and that the other person's AirDrop allows you. Turning off personal hotspot can help.
is airdrop safe | someone airdropped me a picture => Keep AirDrop on Contacts Only so strangers can't send you things. If you get something upsetting from a stranger, decline it and tell a trusted adult.
how do i airdrop to my windows pc | send files ipad to pc => AirDrop doesn't work with Windows. Use iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive, email, or a USB-C cable with the Files app.
# tag: tech.ipad.multitasking
what is stage manager on ipad | how do i use stage manager => Stage Manager lets you use overlapping, resizable app windows on supported iPads, with recent apps lined up on the left. Turn it on in Control Center or in the multitasking settings.
how do i turn on windowed apps on ipados 26 | ipad windows like a mac => As of 2025 with iPadOS 26, open Settings, Multitasking and Gestures, and pick Windowed Apps, Stage Manager or Full Screen Apps. Windowed apps can be moved and resized freely.
how do i use split view on ipad | two apps side by side ipad => On iPadOS 18 and earlier, tap the three dots at the top of an app, tap the Split View button, then pick the second app. On iPadOS 26, use Windowed Apps and drag windows side by side.
how do i use slide over on ipad | small floating app ipad => On iPadOS 18 and earlier, tap the three dots at the top of an app and choose Slide Over to float it on the side. Swipe it off the edge to hide it.
how do i use universal control | use my mac mouse on my ipad => With a Mac and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account and close together, move the Mac's pointer past the screen edge toward the iPad. Turn it on in the Mac's Displays settings.
what is sidecar | use ipad as a mac second screen => Sidecar turns your iPad into a second display for a Mac. On the Mac, open Control Center, Screen Mirroring, and pick the iPad.
can i use my ipad as a second monitor for windows | ipad extra screen pc => Not built in. Third-party apps like Duet Display or spacedesk can do it over a cable or Wi-Fi.
# tag: tech.ipad.pencil
which apple pencil works with my ipad | apple pencil compatibility => It depends on the model. Newer iPad Air and iPad Pro models with M chips work with Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C). Older models may use the 1st or 2nd generation. Check Apple's compatibility list for your exact model.
how do i pair my apple pencil | apple pencil not connecting => Attach a magnetic Pencil (2nd generation or Pro) to the side of the iPad and tap Connect. Plug a USB-C Pencil into the iPad with a cable. A 1st generation Pencil plugs into the Lightning port or an adapter.
how do i charge my apple pencil | apple pencil battery => Pencil Pro and 2nd generation charge while attached to the iPad's magnetic side. Apple Pencil (USB-C) charges with a USB-C cable. See the battery level in the Batteries widget.
how do i change what double tap does on apple pencil | pencil double tap setting => Open Settings, Apple Pencil, and pick what double-tap does, like switching to the eraser or showing the color palette.
what is apple pencil squeeze | pencil pro features => Apple Pencil Pro adds squeeze to bring up a tool palette, barrel roll to turn shaped brushes, haptic feedback and Find My support.
what is scribble on ipad | write in text boxes with pencil => Scribble turns handwriting with the Apple Pencil into typed text in any text box. Scratch out a word to delete it. Turn it on in Settings, Apple Pencil.
how do i make a quick note on ipad | swipe for quick note => Swipe up from the bottom right corner with your Apple Pencil or finger to open a Quick Note over any app.
what is math notes on ipad | ipad solve handwritten math => In the Notes app or Calculator on iPadOS 18 and later, write an equation with an equals sign and the iPad solves it in handwriting style. It can also graph equations.
what is smart script | ipad fixes my handwriting => Smart Script on iPadOS 18 and later can make your handwriting neater as you write in Notes and lets you paste typed text in your own handwriting style.
how do i screenshot with apple pencil | pencil swipe screenshot => Swipe up from either bottom corner of the screen with the Apple Pencil to take a screenshot and open Markup.
what drawing apps work with apple pencil | best ipad art app => Procreate is a popular paid drawing app. Free options include Sketchbook, Infinite Painter's free version and Apple's own Freeform and Notes.
how do i draw a perfect shape on ipad | shape recognition notes => In Notes or Markup, draw a shape like a circle or arrow and pause at the end without lifting the Pencil. It snaps into a clean shape.
what is a paper like screen protector | matte screen protector for drawing => It's a matte screen protector that adds a little grip so the Pencil feels more like pencil on paper. It makes the screen a bit less sharp and wears down Pencil tips faster.
how do i replace an apple pencil tip | apple pencil tip worn => Twist the old tip counterclockwise to unscrew it and screw on a new one. Replace it when you can feel metal or it scratches.
# tag: tech.ipad.apps
what is the shortcuts app | what can shortcuts do on ipad => Shortcuts lets you chain actions into one tap, like resizing photos, starting a playlist, or turning on Focus. Automations can run them at a time or when something happens.
how do i make a shortcut on ipad | create a shortcut => Open the Shortcuts app, tap the plus button, search for actions and add them in order, then name it. Run it from the app, a widget or Siri.
how do i make an automation on ipad | run a shortcut automatically => In Shortcuts open the Automation tab, tap New Automation, pick a trigger like Time of Day or opening an app, and add the actions.
how do i change app icons on ipad | custom app icons => On iPadOS 18 and later, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, Customize, and choose Dark or Tinted icons. Shortcuts can also make custom icons that open apps.
what is freeform on ipad | freeform app => Freeform is Apple's free whiteboard app for sketching, notes, pictures and links on a huge canvas. You can share boards with others.
how do i use the measure app on ipad | measure things with ipad => Open Measure, point the camera at the object, and tap the plus button at each end. iPads with LiDAR measure more accurately.
how do i use voice memos on ipad | record audio ipad => Open Voice Memos and tap the red record button. Tap it again to stop. Recordings sync to your other Apple devices with iCloud.
how do i use the magnifier on ipad | use ipad as a magnifying glass => Open the Magnifier app (or add it to Control Center) to zoom in on small text with the camera.
how do i use dictation on ipad without internet | offline dictation => On supported iPads, dictation works offline for many languages. Tap the microphone on the keyboard and speak.
how do i translate on ipad | ipad translate app => Open the Translate app, pick two languages, and type or speak. It can also translate text in photos with the camera.
how do i video call on ipad | facetime on ipad => Open FaceTime, tap New FaceTime, type a contact's name, number or email, and tap the video button. Only call people you know.
how do i turn on center stage | facetime camera follows me => Center Stage keeps you in frame during video calls on supported iPads. Turn it on in Control Center while in a call, under Video Effects.
how do i print from my ipad | airprint => Tap Share in the app, choose Print, and pick an AirPrint printer on the same Wi-Fi.
how do i use split keyboard or floating keyboard | small keyboard ipad => Pinch the on-screen keyboard with two fingers to shrink it into a floating keyboard. Spread it out to go back to full size.
how do i turn off keyboard clicks on ipad | keyboard sound ipad => Open Settings, Sounds, and turn off Keyboard Clicks.
how do i see wifi password on ipad | share wifi password ipad => Open the Passwords app (iPadOS 18 and later), tap Wi-Fi and pick the network to see its password. Or bring your device near a friend's Apple device to share it.
what is the passwords app | saved passwords ipad => The Passwords app, added in iPadOS 18, stores your saved passwords, passkeys, Wi-Fi passwords and verification codes, synced with iCloud Keychain.
how do i stop apps tracking me on ipad | ask app not to track => Open Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track.
how do i check app permissions on ipad | which apps use my camera => Open Settings, Privacy and Security, and tap Camera, Microphone, Location Services or Photos to see which apps have access and turn them off.
what is the orange or green dot on my ipad | green light at top of screen => Green means an app is using the camera, and orange means it's using the microphone. Open Control Center to see which app.
how do i stop my ipad from rotating | rotation lock ipad => Open Control Center and tap the lock with a circular arrow to lock the screen orientation.
why won't my ipad charge | ipad not charging => Try another cable and power adapter, check the port for lint, and force restart. Using a weak phone charger can make a big iPad charge very slowly.
why is my ipad so slow | ipad lagging => Restart it, update iPadOS, free up storage if it's nearly full, close stuck apps, and turn off Background App Refresh for apps you don't need.
why is my ipad getting hot | ipad overheating => Games, charging, video calls and bright screens heat it up. Take off thick cases while charging, lower brightness and let it rest. It may slow down to cool itself.
how do i turn off background app refresh | stop apps running in background ipad => Open Settings, General, Background App Refresh, and turn it off for apps that don't need it, or for everything.
