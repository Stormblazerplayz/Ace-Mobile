# tag: tech.apps.convert
how do i turn a video into an mp3 | extract audio from video => Open the video in VLC and use Media, Convert/Save with an audio profile, or import it into Audacity and export as MP3. Resolve can also render audio only.
how do i compress a pdf | make pdf smaller => Use a trusted tool like Adobe's free online compressor, or on a Mac open it in Preview and choose File, Export with a Reduce File Size filter.
how do i convert a pdf to word | edit a pdf in word => Open the PDF in Microsoft Word, which converts it into an editable document, or upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.
how do i resize lots of images at once | bulk image resize => On Windows, PowerToys Image Resizer adds a right-click option to resize many images. On Mac, select them in Finder and use Quick Actions, Convert Image.
how do i turn screenshots into one pdf | combine pictures into pdf windows => Select them and choose Print, then Microsoft Print to PDF on Windows, or on Mac right-click, Quick Actions, Create PDF.
what is vlc media player | vlc player => VLC is a free media player that plays almost any video or audio file, including MKV and odd formats Windows can't open.
how do i make a video play faster in vlc | vlc speed keys => Press ] to speed up and [ to slow down in VLC, or use Playback, Speed in the menu.
how do i convert video formats | mov to mp4 converter => Use HandBrake or VLC to convert videos. For a simple MKV to MP4 change without quality loss, remux it in OBS or a similar tool.
how do i record audio on my pc | voice recorder windows => Use the Sound Recorder app in Windows 11, or Audacity for more editing tools.
how do i trim a video on windows without an editor | cut a clip windows photos => Open it in Clipchamp or the Photos app's video editor, drag the ends of the clip to trim, and export.
# tag: tech.apps.access
what is voiceover on iphone | screen reader iphone => VoiceOver reads everything on screen aloud for people who can't see it well. Turn it on in Settings, Accessibility, VoiceOver. It changes how taps work, so learn the gestures first.
what is assistivetouch | on screen home button => AssistiveTouch adds a floating button that does things like home, screenshots and volume without pressing physical buttons. Turn it on in Settings, Accessibility, Touch.
what is eye tracking on ipad | control ipad with eyes => In iPadOS 18 and later, Eye Tracking lets you control the iPad by looking at the screen, using the front camera. It's in Settings, Accessibility.
what is sound recognition on iphone | alert for sounds => Sound Recognition notifies you when the iPhone or iPad hears sounds like a doorbell, alarm or crying baby. It's in Settings, Accessibility.
what is live speech | type to speak => Live Speech lets you type and have the device speak your words aloud in calls and in person. It's in Settings, Accessibility.
what is personal voice | make a voice like mine => Personal Voice lets an iPhone or iPad create a voice that sounds like you, used with Live Speech. It's meant for people at risk of losing their speech.
what is mono audio | one ear audio => Mono Audio plays both left and right sound in each ear, which helps if you use one earbud or hear better in one ear. Find it in accessibility settings.
what is reduce motion | stop screen animations => Reduce Motion cuts zooming and sliding animations, which helps if motion makes you feel sick. It's in accessibility settings on iPad, iPhone, Mac and Windows.
what is high contrast mode on windows | contrast themes => Contrast themes in Settings, Accessibility make text and buttons stand out with strong colors, which helps low vision.
what is switch control | switch control explained => Switch Control lets someone use a device with one or a few buttons or switches instead of touching the screen, scanning through items to pick one.
# tag: tech.apps.smarthome
what is thread smart home | thread network => Thread is a low-power mesh network for smart home gadgets. Devices pass messages to each other, so range improves as you add more.
what is zigbee | zigbee explained => Zigbee is a low-power wireless system many smart bulbs and sensors use. It usually needs a hub to connect to your phone and Wi-Fi.
what is home assistant | home assistant explained => Home Assistant is free software, often on a Raspberry Pi, that controls smart home devices from many brands in one place, with automations.
what is a smart home automation | smart home routine => An automation makes devices act on their own, like lights turning on at sunset or when you open a door.
what is a smart bulb | smart bulb explained => A smart bulb connects to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Zigbee so you can change brightness and color from an app or by voice.
what is apple home | homekit => Apple Home (HomeKit) controls smart devices from iPhone, iPad and Mac. An Apple TV or HomePod is needed as a home hub for automations.
what is google home app | google home app explained => The Google Home app sets up and controls Google Nest speakers, Chromecast and other smart devices, plus routines.
# tag: tech.apps.calls
what is shareplay | shareplay explained => SharePlay lets you watch videos, listen to music or share your screen together during a FaceTime call. Apps have to support it.
how do i make a group facetime | facetime group call => In FaceTime, tap New FaceTime and add several contacts, or start one from a group chat in Messages.
how do i share my screen on facetime | facetime screen share => During a call, tap the share content button and choose Share My Screen.
what is a meeting link | join a video call => A meeting link opens a video call in Zoom, Teams or Meet. Click it, allow camera and mic, and choose whether to join with them on or off.
how do i blur my background in a video call | background blur zoom => Zoom, Teams and Meet all have background blur in their video or effects settings. Windows Studio Effects can also blur on supported PCs.
why does my voice echo in video calls | echo in zoom => Echo usually comes from someone's speakers being picked up by their mic. Use headphones, or lower the speaker volume.
what is game streaming from my pc to my ipad | moonlight sunshine => Apps like Moonlight with Sunshine, or Steam Link, stream games from your PC to an iPad over your home network so you can play anywhere in the house.
what is parsec | parsec explained => Parsec is an app for streaming your computer to another device with low lag, popular for playing games together remotely.
