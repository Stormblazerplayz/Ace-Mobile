# tag: tech.vehicles
how do you charge an electric car | ev charging levels => EVs charge from a regular outlet slowly, from a Level 2 home or public charger in several hours, or from a DC fast charger in about 20 to 40 minutes for most of the battery.
what is nacs | tesla charging plug => NACS is Tesla's charging plug, now standardized as SAE J3400. Most carmakers in North America started switching to it around 2025.
what is range anxiety | will an ev run out of battery => Range anxiety is worry that an electric car will run out of charge before reaching a charger. Most modern EVs go 400 km or more per charge, and route planners show chargers on the way.
what is waymo | driverless taxi => Waymo is a self-driving taxi service from Google's parent company, Alphabet. Riders use an app, and no one sits in the driver's seat.
what is adaptive cruise control | car keeps distance automatically => Adaptive cruise control holds a set speed but slows down and speeds up to keep a safe gap behind the car ahead. The driver still has to pay attention.
what is lane keeping assist | car stays in lane => Lane keeping assist uses a camera to see lane lines and gently steers the car back if it drifts. It's a helper, not self-driving.
what is an obd2 port | car diagnostic scanner => Cars since the late 1990s have an OBD-II port under the dashboard. A cheap scanner plugged in reads the codes that explain a check engine light.
how do e-bikes work | electric bike assist => An e-bike has a battery and motor that add power when you pedal, or with a throttle on some types. Age and speed rules for riding them vary by place.
are hoverboard and e-scooter batteries dangerous | lithium battery fire scooter => Their lithium batteries can catch fire if damaged, cheap or charged with the wrong charger. Use certified models, the original charger, and don't charge them overnight or near exits.
what is a tire pressure sensor | tpms light => TPMS sensors in the wheels warn you when a tire is low on air with a dashboard light shaped like a horseshoe with an exclamation mark.
