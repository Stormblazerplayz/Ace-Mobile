# tag: tech.apps.cloud
what is google drive | how do i use google drive => Google Drive stores files online so you can reach them from any device. Free accounts get 15 GB shared with Gmail and Photos as of 2025. Drag files into drive.google.com to upload.
how do i share a google drive folder | share files google drive => Right-click the folder, choose Share, add people's emails and pick Viewer, Commenter or Editor. "Anyone with the link" lets anyone who gets the link open it, so use it carefully.
how do i download a whole google drive folder | save drive folder to pc => Right-click the folder and choose Download. Drive zips it up first, which can take a while for big folders.
what is icloud drive | where are my icloud files => iCloud Drive keeps files in sync across your Apple devices. Open the Files app on iPad and tap iCloud Drive, or use iCloud for Windows on a PC.
how do i get icloud on my windows pc | icloud for windows => Install iCloud for Windows from the Microsoft Store and sign in with your Apple Account. iCloud Drive and Photos then appear in File Explorer.
how do i get my ipad photos onto my pc | transfer photos ipad to windows => Turn on iCloud Photos and use iCloud for Windows, or plug the iPad into the PC, tap Trust, and import in the Windows Photos app.
what is files on demand onedrive | cloud icon onedrive => Files On-Demand shows OneDrive files in File Explorer without downloading them. A cloud icon means online only, a green tick means saved on the PC. Right-click and choose Always keep on this device to keep a copy.
why is my cloud storage full | google storage full => Big videos, photo backups and email attachments fill it. Check what uses space in the service's storage page, delete large files, and empty its trash.
what is dropbox | dropbox vs google drive => Dropbox is a cloud storage service that syncs a folder across your devices and makes sharing files easy. Free accounts get a small amount of space.
what is the difference between sync and backup | is cloud sync a backup => Sync makes every device match, so deleting a file on one deletes it everywhere. A backup is a separate copy you can restore from, so keep both if files matter.
how do i make a gmail filter | gmail organize email => In Gmail, click the search options button in the search bar, set rules like a sender's address, click Create filter, and pick actions like label or skip the inbox.
how do i undo send in gmail | unsend email => Click Undo right after sending. Set how long you have, up to 30 seconds, in Gmail Settings, General, Undo Send.
how do i block an email address in gmail | stop emails from one sender => Open an email from them, click the three dots and choose Block. Their future emails go to Spam.
what is cc and bcc in email | cc vs bcc => CC sends a copy that everyone can see. BCC sends a hidden copy; other people won't see who was BCC'd.
how do i write a good email to a teacher | email etiquette => Use a clear subject, start with a greeting like "Hi Ms. Lee", say what you need in a few sentences, say thank you, and sign with your name.
what is an email attachment size limit | file too big to email => Gmail allows attachments up to 25 MB. For bigger files, it uploads them to Google Drive and sends a link instead.
how do i unsubscribe from emails | stop marketing emails => Look for the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email or at the top in Gmail. Only use it for real companies; for spam, mark it as spam instead.
what is two step verification for google | google account security => Turn on 2-Step Verification at myaccount.google.com, under Security. Use Google prompts or an authenticator app, and save backup codes.
how do i check my google account security | google security checkup => Go to myaccount.google.com and open Security Checkup. It shows signed in devices, recent activity and weak spots to fix.
how do i see which devices are logged into my account | sign out other devices => Most services list them in security settings: Google in Your devices, Microsoft at account.microsoft.com, Discord in Devices. Sign out any you don't recognize and change your password.
how do i recover a hacked google account | google account recovery => Go to accounts.google.com/signin/recovery and follow the steps, using your recovery phone or email. Have a parent help.
how do i use google photos | back up photos to google => Install Google Photos, sign in and turn on Backup. Photos upload when you're on Wi-Fi and use your Google storage.
what is the epic games store | free games epic => The Epic Games Store is a PC game store and launcher. As of 2025, it gives away free games regularly; claim them on the store page while they're free.
what is the xbox app on pc | pc game pass app => The Xbox app on Windows is where you download PC Game Pass games, chat with Xbox friends and manage game installs.
what is the microsoft store | is microsoft store safe => The Microsoft Store is Windows' built-in app store. Apps there are checked by Microsoft, so it's usually safer than random download sites.
what is notion | notion app => Notion is an app for notes, to-do lists and project pages, with databases and templates. It works on PC, Mac, iPad and the web.
what is obsidian notes app | markdown notes => Obsidian is a note app that saves notes as plain Markdown files on your device, with links between notes and lots of plugins.
what is a pdf reader | how do i edit a pdf => Browsers like Edge and Chrome open PDFs for free. Edge can also draw, highlight and fill forms. Full editing needs apps like Adobe Acrobat or free alternatives.
what is libreoffice | free microsoft office => LibreOffice is a free, open source office suite with Writer, Calc and Impress that opens Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.
can i use microsoft office for free | free word online => The web versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint are free with a Microsoft account at office.com. Many schools also give students free Microsoft 365.
what is google classroom | how do i turn in work google classroom => Google Classroom is where many teachers post assignments. Open the assignment, click Add or create to attach your work, then click Turn in.
what is a vpn app on my phone | do i need a vpn => Most people don't need a VPN at home. Free VPN apps can collect and sell your data. Ask a parent before installing one.
what is a qr code login | sign in by scanning code => Some services let you sign in on a TV or PC by scanning a code with your phone where you're already signed in. Only scan codes on your own screen, never ones someone sends you.
what is an app store rating | age ratings apps => App stores show age ratings like 4+, 9+, 12+ or 17+ for content. Games also use ESRB (like E, T, M) or PEGI (like 7, 12, 16) ratings.
what does esrb rating mean | e10 t m rating => ESRB ratings in North America are E (Everyone), E10+ (10 and up), T (Teen, 13 and up), M (Mature, 17 and up) and AO (Adults Only).
