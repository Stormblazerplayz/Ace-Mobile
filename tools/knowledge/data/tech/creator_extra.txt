# tag: tech.creator.extra
why can't i upload videos longer than 15 minutes | youtube verify phone number => YouTube asks you to verify your account, usually with a phone code, before uploading videos over 15 minutes, adding custom thumbnails or live streaming. A parent can help with this.
what is content id on youtube | automatic copyright match => Content ID scans uploads against music and video that owners registered. A match can block the video, mute it or send its ad money to the owner, which is a claim, not a strike.
what is a youtube premiere | premiere a video => A Premiere plays a new upload at a set time like a live event, with a countdown and live chat, so viewers can watch it together.
what are twitch channel points | channel points rewards => Viewers earn channel points just by watching and can spend them on rewards the streamer sets up, like highlighting a message or picking a song.
what is a twitch moderator | make someone a mod => Mods help run chat by deleting messages and timing out rule breakers. Type /mod followed by their username in your chat to make them a mod.
