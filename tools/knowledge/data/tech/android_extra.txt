# tag: tech.android.extra
what is bixby | samsung voice assistant => Bixby is Samsung's voice assistant on Galaxy phones, TVs and appliances. You can set the side button to open Bixby, Gemini or the power menu in Advanced features.
what is samsung notes | notes app on galaxy => Samsung Notes is Samsung's note app with handwriting for the S Pen, PDF markup, voice recordings and syncing to a PC.
what is live translate on samsung | translate phone calls galaxy => Live Translate is a Galaxy AI feature that translates phone calls in real time, showing and speaking the other person's words in your language.
what is samsung kids | kid mode on galaxy tablet => Samsung Kids is a locked-down mode with approved apps, games and time limits, so a younger kid can use a Galaxy phone or tablet safely.
what are modes and routines on samsung | automate galaxy settings => Modes and Routines changes settings automatically, like turning on Do Not Disturb at bedtime or a gaming mode when a game opens.
what is auto blocker on samsung | block unknown apps galaxy => Auto Blocker blocks apps from unauthorized sources and suspicious commands over USB. It's in Security and privacy settings and is a good thing to leave on.
what is device care on samsung | samsung optimize now => Device care, in Battery and device care settings, checks battery, storage and memory and has an Optimize now button that closes background apps and scans for problems.
what is battery protection on samsung | stop charging at 80 percent => Battery protection stops charging around 80 to 85 percent to reduce wear, which helps the battery last more years.
what is magic editor in google photos | move things in a photo ai => Magic Editor uses AI to move or resize people and objects, change the sky or fill in backgrounds in a photo.
what is best take on pixel | fix closed eyes group photo => Best Take combines several group shots so you can swap in each person's best face, like one where they're smiling with eyes open.
what is hold for me on pixel | phone waits on hold for me => Hold for Me lets a Pixel wait on hold during a call and alerts you when a real person picks up.
what is material you on android | colors match my wallpaper => Material You picks colors from your wallpaper and uses them across menus, widgets and, if you turn on themed icons, your app icons.
what is private space on android | hide apps android 15 => Private space, added in Android 15, is a separate locked area for apps you want hidden. Its apps disappear from recent apps and notifications when locked.
what is theft detection lock on android | phone locks if snatched => Theft Detection Lock uses motion sensors and AI to lock the screen if someone grabs the phone and runs or rides off with it.
should i root my android phone | rooting android => Rooting gives full control of the system, but it can break banking and game apps, void the warranty and weaken security. Most people are better off not rooting.
what is f-droid | open source android app store => F-Droid is an app store with only free and open-source Android apps, installed from its website. Install only from its official site.
how do i pair galaxy buds | connect galaxy buds => Open the case near a Galaxy phone and tap Connect in the pop-up. On other Android phones, use the Galaxy Wearable app.
