# tag: tech.photo.extra
what is blue hour in photography | blue hour photos => Blue hour is the short time just before sunrise and after sunset when the sky turns deep blue. City lights and sky balance nicely then.
how do i photograph stars | night sky photo with phone => Get away from city lights, put the phone or camera on a tripod and use night mode or a long exposure of 10 to 30 seconds. A moonless night shows the most stars.
how do i lock focus and exposure on iphone | ae af lock => Touch and hold on the subject in the Camera app until AE/AF Lock appears. Then slide up or down to adjust brightness.
what is a polarizing filter | cpl filter for camera => A circular polarizer cuts reflections on water and glass and makes skies bluer. You twist it to change how strong the effect is.
how do i scan old printed photos | digitize old photos => Use a flatbed scanner at 600 DPI, or a phone app like Google's PhotoScan that removes glare by taking several shots.
how do i print my photos | order prints from phone => Many stores and online services print from your phone or computer. Use the full-resolution original, not a screenshot, for sharp prints.
how do i take a group photo where everyone looks good | group photo tips => Use a tripod and timer or burst mode, have the light behind you, put people at different heights and take several shots.
what is a bluetooth remote shutter | take photos from far away with phone => A small Bluetooth remote pairs with your phone and snaps a photo when you press it. An Apple Watch or the volume buttons on wired earbuds work too.
what is action mode on iphone | super stable phone video => Action Mode gives very steady video while running or biking by cropping in and stabilizing heavily. It works best in good light.
what is apple log video | log recording iphone => Apple Log, on iPhone 15 Pro and later, records flat, low-contrast video that keeps more highlight and shadow detail for color grading in apps like Resolve.
what is spatial video | 3d video iphone => Spatial video records in 3D on newer iPhones and Apple Vision Pro, so it looks deep when watched on Vision Pro or other supported headsets.
what are clip on phone lenses | add lens to phone camera => Clip-on lenses add macro, fisheye or wide views to a phone. Cheap ones often blur the edges, and many phones now have good built-in ultra-wide and macro.
