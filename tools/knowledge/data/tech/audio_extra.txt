# tag: tech.audio.extra
what is a cardioid mic | mic polar pattern => Cardioid mics pick up sound mostly from the front and ignore the back, which helps block keyboard and room noise. Speak into the side the maker marks as the front.
what is a shock mount for a mic | stop desk bumps in mic => A shock mount holds the mic in elastic bands so bumps and vibrations from the desk or boom arm don't thump into the recording.
what is a noise gate | mute mic when not talking => A noise gate mutes your mic until your voice gets louder than a set level, cutting out background hum and keyboard noise between words.
why does my bluetooth headset sound bad when i use the mic | bluetooth hands free low quality => Classic Bluetooth switches to a low-quality hands-free mode when the mic is on. Use a separate mic, a USB dongle headset, or the newer LE Audio if both devices support it.
what is loudness equalization | even out pc volume => Loudness Equalization in Windows sound enhancements evens out loud and quiet sounds. It helps hear footsteps but can make sounds less natural.
what is equalizer apo | system wide eq windows => Equalizer APO is a free Windows tool that adds an equalizer to all sound on the PC. Peace is a popular easy interface for it.
what is chatmix on a headset | balance game and voice chat => ChatMix is a dial on some headsets that balances game sound and voice chat by showing up as two separate audio devices on the PC.
why doesn't my front headphone jack work | front panel audio not working => Make sure the case's HD Audio cable is plugged into the motherboard's front audio header, then check the jack-detection setting in the audio software.
what is the 60 60 rule for headphones | safe headphone volume => Listen at no more than about 60 percent volume for 60 minutes at a time, then give your ears a break. Phones can warn you about loud listening too.
