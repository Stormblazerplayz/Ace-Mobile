# tag: tech.streaming.obs
what is studio mode in obs | obs preview and program => Studio Mode shows a preview on the left and what viewers see on the right. You set up the next scene in private, then click Transition to switch.
how do i hear my audio sources in obs | obs audio monitoring => Open Advanced Audio Properties, find the source and set Audio Monitoring to Monitor and Output. Use headphones so it doesn't echo.
how do i change where obs saves recordings | obs recording folder => Go to Settings > Output and change the Recording Path. Pick a drive with plenty of space.
what are profiles and scene collections in obs | obs profiles => Profiles store settings like bitrate and resolution, and scene collections store your scenes and sources. You can keep separate ones for streaming and recording.
what is the obs virtual camera | use obs as webcam => Click Start Virtual Camera and OBS shows up as a webcam in apps like Discord or Zoom, so you can use your scenes in video calls.
how do i color correct a source in obs | obs color filter => Right-click the source, choose Filters, and add a Color Correction filter under Effect Filters to change brightness, contrast or saturation.
how do i crop a source in obs | alt drag crop obs => Hold Alt and drag a source's edge to crop it on Windows, or add a Crop/Pad filter for exact numbers.
how do i make a source fill the screen in obs | fit to screen obs => Select the source and press Ctrl+F to fit it to the screen, or right-click it and use Transform options.
what is the obs auto configuration wizard | obs auto setup => Find it in the Tools menu. It tests your PC and internet and suggests settings for streaming or recording.
what is canvas vs output resolution in obs | base vs scaled resolution => Canvas, or base, resolution is the size of your scene. Output, or scaled, resolution is what gets streamed or recorded, and can be smaller to save performance.
what does encoding overloaded mean in obs | obs encoder overloaded => Your PC can't encode fast enough. Switch to a hardware encoder like NVENC, lower the output resolution or frame rate, or pick a faster preset.
window capture vs game capture vs display capture | which capture in obs => Game Capture is best for full-screen games, Window Capture grabs one app window, and Display Capture records your whole screen.
how do i fix audio out of sync in obs | obs sync offset => In Advanced Audio Properties, add a Sync Offset in milliseconds to the source that's early, like your mic, until it lines up.
how do i see obs stats | obs performance stats => Open the Stats dock from the Docks menu to see dropped frames, CPU use and encoding lag while you stream or record.
what is the twitch vod track | keep music out of vods => The Twitch VOD Track setting sends a separate audio track to your saved VODs, so you can leave copyrighted music out of them.
how do i add text in obs | obs text source => Add a Text source from the plus button. On Windows it's called Text (GDI+), and you can pick the font, color and outline.
how do i put one scene inside another in obs | nested scenes obs => Add a Scene as a source in another scene. Changing the nested scene updates it everywhere, which is great for webcam frames or overlays.
how do i lock a source in obs | stop moving sources by accident => Click the padlock icon next to the source in the Sources list. Locked sources can't be moved or resized by accident.
how do i put a source in front in obs | layer order obs => Sources at the top of the list appear in front. Drag them up or down to change the order.
how do i pause a recording in obs | obs pause button => Click the pause button next to Stop Recording. It may only appear for some recording formats and encoders.
can obs split recordings into parts | obs automatic file splitting => Yes. In Settings > Output under Recording, turn on automatic file splitting by time or size.
what is obs websocket | control obs from other apps => OBS WebSocket is built into OBS and lets apps like Stream Deck or phone remotes control your scenes and recording.
