# tag: tech.procreate.extra
how do i blur in procreate | gaussian blur procreate => Tap the magic wand Adjustments icon, choose Gaussian Blur, pick Layer or Pencil, then slide your finger across the screen to set the amount.
what is quickmenu in procreate | procreate quick menu => QuickMenu is a ring of six actions you can pop up with a gesture you choose in Gesture controls, like a touch and hold or square button tap.
how do i watch my procreate timelapse | timelapse replay procreate => Open the wrench Actions menu, tap Video, then Time-lapse Replay. Export it from the same menu to share how you drew it.
what is procreate pocket | procreate on iphone => Procreate Pocket is the iPhone version of Procreate, sold separately, with most of the same brushes and tools on a smaller screen.
can i paint 3d models in procreate | procreate 3d painting => Yes, Procreate can open OBJ, USDZ and USDA models with UVs and paint right on them, and you can view them in AR.
what is palm rejection | hand touching ipad while drawing => Palm rejection ignores your hand resting on the screen while you draw with Apple Pencil. If touches still interfere, turn off finger painting in your art app.
what is barrel roll on apple pencil pro | rotate pencil to turn brush => Apple Pencil Pro has a gyroscope, so rolling it in your fingers rotates shaped brushes and pens in apps that support it.
what is lumafusion | video editor for ipad => LumaFusion is a paid video editing app for iPad and iPhone with multiple tracks, keyframes and effects, popular before Resolve came to iPad.
