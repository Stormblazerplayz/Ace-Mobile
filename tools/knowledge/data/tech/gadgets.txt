# tag: tech.gadgets
what is a smart speaker | how does alexa work => A smart speaker like an Amazon Echo or Google Nest listens for a wake word, sends your request to the cloud, and answers or controls smart home devices.
what is a smart home | smart home devices => A smart home connects devices like lights, plugs, thermostats and cameras to an app or voice assistant so you can control or automate them.
what is matter smart home | matter standard => Matter is a smart home standard backed by Apple, Google, Amazon and others, so devices work across ecosystems without a separate hub for each brand.
what is a smart plug | what can a smart plug do => A smart plug turns anything plugged into it on or off from an app, a schedule or voice. Don't use it for space heaters or high-power devices unless it's rated for them.
what is a smartwatch | apple watch vs fitness tracker => A smartwatch shows notifications, runs apps and tracks health and fitness. Fitness trackers are simpler, with longer battery life.
what is nfc | what does nfc do => NFC (near field communication) lets devices exchange data when very close, used for tap to pay, transit cards and quick pairing.
what is uwb | ultra wideband airtag => UWB (ultra-wideband) measures distance and direction very precisely, which lets iPhones point you to a nearby AirTag with Precision Finding.
what is an airtag | how do airtags work => An AirTag is a small Apple tracker. Nearby Apple devices anonymously report its location to Find My. Use it on your own things only.
what is a chromecast | google tv streamer => Chromecast and Google TV devices plug into a TV's HDMI port and play shows and videos sent from phones or their own apps.
what is an apple tv box | apple tv 4k => Apple TV 4K is a streaming box for TVs that runs apps, games and AirPlay from iPhones and iPads.
what is a roku | streaming stick => A Roku is a streaming device or TV system for apps like Netflix and YouTube, controlled with a simple remote.
what is an e reader | kindle vs ipad for reading => E-readers like Kindle use E Ink screens that look like paper, are easy on the eyes in sunlight and last weeks per charge. iPads show color and video but tire eyes more.
what is a vr headset | how does vr work => A VR headset shows a 3D world on screens in front of your eyes and tracks your head and hands. Meta Quest headsets work standalone or connected to a PC.
how old do you have to be for meta quest | vr age limit => As of 2025, Meta allows Quest accounts for ages 10 and up with parent-managed accounts for 10 to 12. Take breaks and play in a clear space.
how do i stop vr motion sickness | feel sick in vr => Start with short sessions, use teleport movement and comfort settings, stop at the first sign of feeling sick, and keep a fan blowing on you.
what is pc vr | quest link => PC VR runs games on a gaming PC and streams them to the headset, by cable with Quest Link or wirelessly with Air Link or Virtual Desktop. It needs a strong GPU and good Wi-Fi.
what is the vr guardian | vr play area => The guardian (boundary) is a virtual wall you draw around your play space. If you get close to it, the headset warns you so you don't hit furniture.
what is mixed reality | passthrough vr => Mixed reality shows the real room through the headset's cameras (passthrough) with virtual objects added on top.
what is apple vision pro | vision pro headset => Apple Vision Pro is Apple's mixed reality headset that blends apps with your real surroundings, controlled by eyes, hands and voice. It's very expensive.
what is a 3d pen | 3d printing pen => A 3D pen melts plastic filament so you can draw in the air. It's a fun, cheap way to try shapes, but much less precise than a printer.
what is a laser cutter | laser engraver safety => A laser cutter or engraver cuts or marks wood, acrylic and more with a strong laser. It needs eye protection, ventilation, fire safety and an adult.
what is a cricut | vinyl cutter => A Cricut is a cutting machine for vinyl, paper and fabric, used for stickers, shirt designs and crafts.
what is a raspberry pi used for at home | home server projects => People use Raspberry Pis for retro game consoles, home servers, ad blockers like Pi-hole, weather stations and robot brains.
what is a nas | network storage => A NAS is a small always-on computer with drives that stores and shares files for everyone on your home network.
what is a webcam privacy cover | cover laptop camera => It's a small sliding cover for a laptop camera. It blocks the camera when you're not using it.
what is a stream deck alternative | macro pad => A macro pad is a small keyboard of programmable keys for shortcuts, like switching scenes or launching apps. Many are cheaper than a Stream Deck.
what is a gaming chair worth it | ergonomic chair => Gaming chairs look cool, but a good office chair with adjustable height, lumbar support and armrests is often more comfortable for long sessions.
