# tag: tech.printing.bambu
how big can the bambu a1 print | bambu a1 build volume => The Bambu Lab A1 prints up to 256 x 256 x 256 mm. The smaller A1 mini prints up to 180 x 180 x 180 mm.
what does the a1 calibrate by itself | bambu a1 auto calibration => The A1 automatically levels the bed, sets the Z offset, tunes vibration compensation, cancels motor noise and calibrates flow dynamics before prints, so you rarely tune by hand.
should i run calibration before every print a1 | skip calibration bambu => You can skip some steps for back-to-back prints with the same filament and plate. Run full calibration after moving the printer, changing the nozzle or when first layers look wrong.
what filaments can the a1 print | can bambu a1 print abs => The A1 prints PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA well. ABS and ASA warp and give off fumes on an open-frame printer, so they're not recommended without an enclosure.
what is the bambu ams lite | how does the ams lite work with a1 => The AMS lite holds 4 spools and feeds them automatically for multicolor prints or backup spools. It cuts the filament and purges the old color between swaps.
why does multicolor printing waste so much filament | purge waste ams => Each color change pushes out the old color, which becomes the "poop" out the back. Reduce it by printing more copies per plate, using flush into infill or objects, and lowering flush volumes carefully.
how do i swap the nozzle on a bambu a1 | a1 hotend change => The A1 has a quick-swap hotend. Let it cool, open the clip on the front of the toolhead, pull the hotend out, push the new one in and close the clip. Then set the nozzle size in Bambu Studio.
what is the bambu a1 heatbed max temp | a1 nozzle max temp => The A1's nozzle goes up to 300 C and its heated bed up to 100 C.
what is bambu studio | bambu slicer => Bambu Studio is Bambu Lab's free slicer. It turns 3D models into print instructions, has presets for Bambu printers and filaments, and sends jobs over Wi-Fi.
what is orca slicer | orca vs bambu studio => OrcaSlicer is a free open source slicer based on Bambu Studio, with extra calibration tools and support for many printer brands.
what is makerworld | bambu model site => MakerWorld is Bambu Lab's site for downloading 3D models, often with ready-to-print Bambu profiles. You can also upload your own designs and earn points.
what is bambu handy | bambu phone app => Bambu Handy is the phone app for Bambu printers. It lets you start prints from MakerWorld, watch the camera, and get notifications when prints finish or fail.
what is lan only mode bambu | print without cloud => LAN Only mode lets you send prints from your computer over your home network without Bambu's cloud. Turn it on in the printer's settings screen.
what plate comes with the a1 | textured pei plate => The A1 comes with a textured PEI plate. PLA and PETG stick well when it's clean, and prints pop off once it cools. The bottom of prints gets a textured look.
how do i clean a textured pei plate | textured plate not sticking => Wash it with warm water and dish soap, rinse well and dry it without touching the surface. Wiping with isopropyl alcohol between prints helps too.
what is the cool plate | smooth pei vs textured => Bambu's Cool Plate and smooth PEI plates give shiny, flat bottoms. Smooth PEI holds PETG so tightly it can tear the plate, so use a glue stick as a release layer for PETG.
why is my a1 so loud | a1 noise => The A1 has motor noise cancellation that runs during calibration. Fans and fast moves still make noise; Silent mode in the print speed menu slows it down and quiets it.
how do i update bambu firmware | a1 firmware update => Open the Device tab in Bambu Studio or the Bambu Handy app and check firmware. The printer screen also shows updates under settings. Don't turn it off while it updates.
how do i print from my phone with bambu | start print from bambu handy => Open a model on MakerWorld in Bambu Handy, tap Print, pick your printer, plate and filament, and send it. Make sure the plate on the printer is clear first.
what is the a1 camera for | bambu timelapse => The A1's camera lets you watch prints remotely in Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy and can record timelapses. Turn timelapse on in the print settings before sending.
how do i load filament on the a1 | change filament a1 without ams => On the A1 screen choose Filament, then Load, pick the type, and push the filament into the extruder until it grabs. Unload first if another spool is in.
how do i do a cold pull | clean nozzle cold pull => Heat the nozzle to printing temp, push filament through, cool to about 90 C for PLA, then pull it out firmly. The tip should come out shaped like the inside of the nozzle with gunk attached.
how do i add a custom filament profile in bambu studio | third party filament bambu => Pick the generic preset (like Generic PLA), print a flow and pressure advance test, then save it as a new preset with the brand's name.
# tag: tech.printing.filament
what temperature should i print petg at | petg settings => PETG usually prints at 230 to 250 C on the nozzle with the bed at 70 to 80 C. Use less cooling than PLA for good layer bonding.
what temperature is abs | abs print settings => ABS prints at about 240 to 260 C with a 90 to 110 C bed. It needs an enclosure to stop warping and good ventilation for fumes.
what is asa filament | asa vs abs => ASA is like ABS but handles sunlight and weather much better, so it's great for outdoor parts. It needs similar high temps and an enclosure.
what temperature for tpu | how do i print tpu => TPU prints at around 210 to 230 C with a 35 to 50 C bed. Print slowly, turn retraction down, and dry the spool first. Use 95A TPU for easier printing.
what is nylon filament | how to print nylon => Nylon is very tough and slippery, great for gears and hinges. It needs high temps (about 250 to 280 C), and must be bone dry since it soaks up moisture fast.
what is carbon fiber filament | pla cf => CF filaments are PLA, PETG or nylon mixed with tiny carbon fibers for stiffness and a matte look. They're abrasive, so use a hardened steel nozzle.
do i need a hardened steel nozzle | abrasive filament nozzle => Use a hardened steel nozzle for abrasive filaments like carbon fiber, glass fiber, glow-in-the-dark and some wood fills. They wear out brass and stainless nozzles quickly.
what is pla plus | pla vs pla+ => PLA+ (or PLA Pro) is PLA with additives that make it a bit tougher and less brittle. Settings are close to normal PLA, maybe 5 to 10 C hotter.
what is matte pla | matte vs regular pla => Matte PLA has a flat, non-shiny finish that hides layer lines well. It's often a little weaker than normal PLA.
what is pla basic vs pla matte bambu | bambu filament types => Bambu sells PLA Basic (shiny, easy), PLA Matte (flat finish), PLA Silk (glossy), PLA-CF (stiff), PETG HF (fast PETG) and more, with matching presets in Bambu Studio.
what is pva filament | water soluble support => PVA dissolves in water, so it's used as support material for complex prints with a multi-material system. Keep it very dry.
what is wood filament | wood pla => Wood PLA has fine wood fibers mixed in, giving a wood look and smell. Use a 0.6 mm nozzle or bigger to avoid clogs, and a hardened nozzle helps.
what is glow in the dark filament | glow pla => Glow PLA charges in light and glows in the dark. It's very abrasive, so print it with a hardened steel nozzle.
will pla melt in a hot car | pla heat resistance => PLA starts to soften around 55 to 60 C, which a car in summer can reach. Use PETG, ASA or ABS for parts that get hot.
how do i know if my filament is wet | wet filament signs => Popping or hissing sounds, steam, extra stringing, bubbles, rough surfaces and weak brittle prints all point to wet filament. Dry it before printing.
what temperature to dry pla | filament drying temp | how long to dry petg => Use a filament dryer or a food dehydrator. Common guides: PLA about 50 C for 4 to 8 hours, PETG about 65 C, TPU about 50 to 55 C, nylon 70 to 80 C. Check the maker's numbers.
can i dry filament in my oven | dry filament oven => Ovens aren't accurate at low temps and can melt the spool, so a filament dryer is safer. If you use an oven, an adult should check the real temperature with a thermometer.
why is my filament brittle and snapping | pla snaps in the tube => Old PLA gets brittle, often from moisture or being bent for a long time. Drying can help, but very brittle PLA may need to be replaced.
what diameter is filament | 1.75 vs 2.85 => Most printers, including Bambu Lab printers, use 1.75 mm filament. Some older or larger printers use 2.85 mm. They aren't interchangeable.
what is a spool weight | how much is 1kg of filament => Most spools hold 1 kg of filament. 1 kg of 1.75 mm PLA is about 330 meters long. The slicer shows how many grams each print uses.
how do i stop filament tangling | knot in the spool => Never let the loose end go; clip it into the spool's side holes. A real tangle usually came from the factory or a loose end crossing under other loops.
what is cardboard spool | do cardboard spools work in ams => Cardboard spools are eco friendly but can shed dust and slip in some AMS units. Many people print or buy reusable spool adapters or rings.
what is the glass transition temperature | why does my print soften => It's the temperature where plastic starts to go soft. PLA is about 60 C, PETG about 80 C, ABS about 100 C.
# tag: tech.printing.problems
why is my print stringing | stringing fix | hairs between parts => Dry the filament, lower the nozzle temperature by 5 to 10 C, check retraction is on, and turn on wipe and z-hop. Wet PETG strings the most.
why are my first layers bumpy or rough | first layer not smooth => The nozzle may be too close (rough, scraping) or too far (gaps, round lines). Clean the plate, rerun bed leveling, and check the Z offset or flow calibration.
why does my nozzle drag through the print | nozzle hitting print => Curling overhangs can catch the nozzle. Add more cooling, turn on z-hop, slow down overhangs, and dry the filament.
why do my supports fuse to the print | supports hard to remove => Raise the top Z distance in support settings, use support interface layers, or use a different material for the interface, like PETG supports under PLA.
what are tree supports | tree vs normal supports => Tree supports grow like branches from the bed and only touch where needed, using less filament and leaving fewer marks. Normal (grid) supports are sturdier for big flat overhangs.
how do i paint on supports in bambu studio | support painting => Select the model, click the support painting tool, paint the areas that need supports (or block them), and set supports to Enable with "For painted only" or use enforcers.
why does my print have holes in the top | top surface gaps => Add more top layers (5 or more), increase infill, and check flow. Pillowing on thin tops happens when there isn't enough support under the top skin.
what is pillowing | lumpy top layer 3d print => Pillowing is bumpy, lumpy top surfaces where top layers sag between infill lines. More top layers, more infill and better cooling fix it.
why does my print have a zit or blob line | seam line on print => That's the seam, where each layer starts and ends. In the slicer, set Seam position to Aligned or Back to hide it, or Random to scatter it.
what is ringing or ghosting | ripples on print => Ringing is a faint echo of edges caused by vibrations at high speed. Tighten belts, put the printer on a sturdy surface, and run vibration calibration.
why are there gaps between walls and infill | walls not touching infill => It's usually under-extrusion or low infill overlap. Check for a partial clog, dry the filament and recalibrate flow. Raise infill-wall overlap slightly in the slicer.
why does the print come off halfway through | print detached from bed => Clean the plate with soap and water, add a brim, lower print speed for the first layers, and keep drafts off the printer. Tall thin parts need more bed contact.
why is my pla print soft or wobbly | print feels weak => Add more walls (3 or 4), raise infill, print a bit hotter for better layer bonding, and check the filament isn't wet or old.
why did my print warp even with pla | pla warping => PLA can warp on big flat prints if the bed is dirty, there's a draft, or the bed is too cold. Clean the plate, add a brim and keep the room warmer.
why are the layers splitting | layer separation => Layers splitting means poor bonding, from printing too cold, too much cooling or drafts. Raise nozzle temp and lower fan speed, especially with PETG and ABS.
why does my printer say heatbed temperature malfunction | a1 heatbed error => It can mean a loose or damaged heatbed cable or sensor. Turn off the printer, check the cable, update firmware, and contact Bambu support.
what is heat creep | filament softening too early => Heat creep is when heat climbs up the hotend and softens filament too early, causing jams. Make sure the hotend fan runs, and don't print PLA in a very hot enclosure.
why is my filament grinding | extruder clicking => Clicking or grinding means the extruder can't push filament through. It's often a partial clog, printing too cold or too fast, or a tangled spool.
what does the ams do when filament runs out | ams backup spool => If you load a matching filament in another slot and turn on auto refill, the AMS switches to it when a spool runs out. Otherwise the print pauses so you can load more.
how do i fix layer lines on curved tops | stair stepping => Use variable layer height in the slicer to print thinner layers on curves, or lower the layer height for the whole model.
why is the bottom of my print shiny or rough | bottom surface finish => The bottom copies the plate's texture. Textured PEI gives a sparkly rough bottom; a smooth PEI or Cool Plate gives a glossy one.
why are circles not round | holes too small 3d print => Holes often print a bit small. Add clearance in your design (about 0.2 mm), or use the slicer's hole size compensation (X-Y hole compensation).
# tag: tech.printing.slicer
what is wall loops | how many wall loops => Wall loops are the outlines printed around each layer. 2 is the default; 3 or 4 make parts much stronger, often more than adding infill.
how many top and bottom layers should i use | top shell layers setting => 4 to 5 top layers and 3 to 4 bottom layers work well at 0.2 mm layer height, giving solid surfaces without wasting much plastic.
what is z hop | does z hop help => Z-hop lifts the nozzle a little when traveling between parts so it doesn't drag across the print. It helps with tipping and scratches but can add a bit of stringing.
what is pressure advance | flow dynamics calibration => Pressure advance predicts how much pressure builds in the nozzle and adjusts extrusion so corners stay sharp and lines stay even. Bambu calls the auto version flow dynamics calibration.
what is flow ratio | flow rate calibration => Flow ratio scales how much plastic comes out. If prints look over-stuffed or have gaps, a flow rate calibration test finds the right value for that filament.
what is max volumetric speed | why is my print slower than set speed => Max volumetric speed is how many cubic millimeters per second the hotend can melt. The slicer slows down moves that would go over it, so high speeds don't always apply.
what is a prime tower | wipe tower => A prime tower is a small tower printed beside the model in multicolor jobs to clean the nozzle after each color change, so colors stay sharp.
what is vase mode | spiral vase => Vase mode prints one continuous spiral wall with no top and no seam. It's fast and great for vases and lampshades but makes a single thin wall.
what is fuzzy skin | rough texture setting slicer => Fuzzy skin makes the nozzle jitter on outer walls, giving a rough textured surface that hides layer lines and looks grippy.
what is adaptive layer height | variable layer height bambu => It changes layer height across the model: thinner on curves for detail, thicker on straight walls for speed. Find it in Bambu Studio's variable layer height tool.
how do i split a model in bambu studio | cut tool slicer => Select the model and use the Cut tool (C key) to slice it with a plane. You can add connectors like dowels or plugs so the parts line up when glued.
how do i add text to a model in bambu studio | emboss text => Right-click the model, choose Add part, then Text, type your words, and set the depth. It can emboss or cut into the surface.
what is a modifier in the slicer | change settings for part of a model => A modifier is a shape you add to a model to change settings only inside it, like more infill around a screw hole.
what is a brim ear | mouse ears => Brim ears are small round brims only at corners, where warping starts, so they're easier to remove than a full brim.
what is print by object | sequential printing => Print by object finishes one object before starting the next, so a failure only ruins one. Objects must be spaced so the toolhead doesn't hit finished parts.
what layer height should i use | 0.2mm layer height => 0.2 mm is the standard for a 0.4 mm nozzle. Use 0.12 to 0.16 mm for fine detail and 0.24 to 0.28 mm for faster, rougher prints.
how do i make prints stronger | strongest print settings => Add more wall loops, use 15 to 25 percent gyroid infill, print hotter, and orient the part so layer lines don't run across the direction it gets pulled or bent.
which way should i orient my print | print orientation => Put the biggest flat side on the bed, reduce overhangs, and point layer lines so the main forces press along them, not across them, since layers split apart most easily.
what is the 0.2mm vs 0.6mm nozzle | bigger nozzle faster => A 0.6 mm nozzle prints thicker lines faster and makes stronger parts, but with less fine detail. 0.2 mm nozzles give tiny details but are very slow.
how do i print multicolor without ams | color change at layer => In the slicer, add a color change (pause) at a layer in the preview. The printer pauses so you can swap filament by hand. It works for stripes or text that changes color by height.
how do i paint colors on a model in bambu studio | color painting => Select the model, press N or click the color painting tool, pick a filament color and brush over the surface. Fill and smart fill tools color whole areas.
what is an ams flush volume | flushing volumes bambu => It's how much plastic gets pushed out to clear the old color. Going from dark to light needs more flushing than light to dark.
# tag: tech.printing.design
what software can i use to design 3d prints | best cad for 3d printing => Tinkercad is easy and free in a browser. Fusion has a free personal use license and is great for precise parts. Onshape, FreeCAD and Blender are other free choices; Blender is best for artistic shapes.
what is tinkercad | is tinkercad good for beginners => Tinkercad is a free, browser-based 3D design tool where you combine and cut basic shapes. It's great for beginners and exports STL files for printing.
what is fusion 360 | fusion for 3d printing => Autodesk Fusion is a professional CAD app for precise, measurable parts using sketches and features. Hobbyists can get a free personal use license with some limits.
what is parametric cad | cad vs blender => Parametric CAD (like Fusion or Onshape) builds parts from sketches with exact dimensions you can change later. Blender sculpts meshes freely, better for characters and art than for precise mechanical parts.
what is a step file | step vs stl => A STEP file stores exact CAD shapes and can be edited in CAD apps. An STL is a triangle mesh that slicers read but is hard to edit precisely.
what tolerance should i use for parts that fit together | clearance for 3d printed fit => Start with about 0.2 mm of gap per side for a snug fit and 0.3 to 0.4 mm for a loose sliding fit. Print a small test first since every printer differs.
how thick should walls be in my design | minimum wall thickness 3d print => Make walls at least 0.8 to 1.2 mm, about two to three nozzle widths for a 0.4 mm nozzle, so the slicer can print them solidly.
what is a print in place model | print in place hinge => Print-in-place models have moving parts printed already assembled, like hinges or chains, with small gaps so they don't fuse.
how do i make screw holes in prints | heat set inserts => For screws that get removed often, melt brass heat-set inserts into printed holes with a soldering iron. For one-time use, screws straight into plastic holes work.
should i use a chamfer or fillet on the bottom of a print | rounded bottom edge prints badly => Use a 45 degree chamfer on bottom edges instead of a rounded fillet. A fillet makes steep overhangs right at the bed, while a chamfer prints cleanly.
how do i fix a broken stl file | repair mesh 3d print => Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer can repair many errors when importing. For bad meshes, Windows 3D Builder or Meshmixer can fix holes and flipped faces.
how do i make a model from a photo | photo to 3d model => Photogrammetry apps take many photos around an object and build a 3D mesh. Phones with LiDAR can scan too. Expect to clean the mesh before printing.
how do i print a lithophane | lithophane => A lithophane is a thin print that shows a photo when backlit, with thicker spots darker. Use a lithophane generator website or MakerWorld tool, then print it upright with 100 percent infill.
can i sell things i 3d print | selling prints license => You can sell your own designs. For other people's models, check the license; many say non-commercial only, and some designers sell commercial licenses.
how do i price a 3d print to sell | 3d print pricing => Add filament cost (grams used times price per gram), electricity, printer wear, failed print risk, your design and packing time, and a profit margin.
how much does a 3d print cost in filament | cost per print => Check the grams the slicer shows and multiply by the price per gram. At $20 for a 1 kg spool, that's 2 cents per gram, so a 50 gram print costs about $1.
# tag: tech.printing.general
what is fdm printing | how does fdm work => FDM (fused deposition modeling) melts plastic filament and lays it down layer by layer to build an object. It's the most common type of home 3D printer.
what is a bowden vs direct drive extruder | direct drive extruder => A direct drive extruder sits on the toolhead, pushing filament right into the hotend, which helps with flexible TPU. A Bowden extruder sits on the frame and pushes through a long tube.
what is a corexy printer | bed slinger vs corexy => A bed slinger, like the A1, moves the bed back and forth for one axis. CoreXY printers keep the bed moving only down, which allows faster, steadier printing, often in an enclosure.
what is an enclosure for a 3d printer | do i need an enclosure => An enclosure keeps warm air in and drafts out, which stops ABS and ASA from warping. PLA prints better without much heat, so keep the door open or skip the enclosure for PLA.
are 3d printer fumes dangerous | is 3d printing safe indoors => Printers give off tiny particles and fumes, especially with ABS and ASA. Print in a ventilated room, not where you sleep, and use PLA indoors when possible.
can a 3d printer catch fire | 3d printer fire safety => It's rare, but hot parts can fail. Keep firmware updated, don't leave it running while nobody's home for long, keep flammable stuff away, and have a smoke alarm nearby.
how do i remove supports cleanly | post processing supports => Let the print cool, then use pliers and a flush cutter to pull supports away. Clean leftover marks with a deburring tool or fine sandpaper.
how do i smooth a pla print | sand 3d print => Sand from about 200 grit up to 400 or higher, wet sanding at higher grits. Filler primer fills layer lines before painting.
how do i paint a 3d print | best paint for pla => Sand lightly, spray a filler primer, then use acrylic paints. Finish with a clear coat to protect it.
how do i glue 3d printed parts | best glue for pla => Super glue (CA glue) works well for PLA and PETG. For big parts, add pins or use the slicer's cut connectors so they line up.
what is acetone smoothing | smooth abs prints => Acetone vapor melts the surface of ABS so layer lines disappear. It doesn't work on PLA and must be done with care, good airflow and an adult.
how long do 3d printers last | 3d printer maintenance => With care, many years. Clean the plate, keep rods and rails clean, check belts, replace worn nozzles, and keep firmware updated.
how often should i lubricate my a1 | a1 maintenance => Bambu's wiki gives maintenance schedules for the A1, like cleaning and greasing the rails and checking belts every few hundred hours. Check the wiki for your model's exact steps.
what is a resin printer | resin printer safety => Resin printers cure liquid resin with UV light for very fine detail, great for miniatures. Uncured resin is toxic to touch and breathe, so it needs gloves, ventilation and an adult.
what is the difference between stl and obj | stl vs obj for printing => STL stores only shape. OBJ can also store colors and textures. 3MF is best for printing since it keeps colors, settings and multiple plates.
how long can i leave filament out | does pla go bad => PLA can sit out for weeks in a dry room, but humidity slowly gets in. Store spools in sealed bags or boxes with desiccant for best results.
