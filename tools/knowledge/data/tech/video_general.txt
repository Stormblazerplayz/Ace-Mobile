# tag: tech.video.concepts
what frame rate should i edit in | 24 vs 30 vs 60 fps video => 24 fps looks cinematic, 30 fps is common for online video, and 60 fps is smooth for gaming footage. Edit in the same frame rate you recorded in.
what resolution should i upload to youtube | upload 1440p for better quality => Upload at least 1080p. Uploading at 1440p or 4K makes YouTube use a better codec, so even 1080p-heavy gaming footage often looks sharper when exported at 1440p.
what is bitrate in video | higher bitrate better quality => Bitrate is how much data per second a video uses, like 20 Mbps. Higher bitrate keeps more detail, especially in fast motion, but makes bigger files.
what is a codec | codec vs container => A codec compresses video or audio, like H.264, H.265 or AV1. A container like MP4, MOV or MKV is the file wrapper that holds the video, audio and other tracks.
what is av1 | av1 codec => AV1 is a newer, very efficient video codec. It gives great quality at low bitrates and is used by YouTube and newer GPUs for streaming and recording.
mp4 vs mov vs mkv | which video format should i use => MP4 works almost everywhere and is best for sharing. MOV is Apple's format, common from iPhones and editors. MKV is great for recording since it survives crashes, but remux it to MP4 for editing.
what is aspect ratio for videos | 16 9 vs 9 16 => 16:9 is the normal wide shape for YouTube and TVs. 9:16 is tall, for Shorts, TikTok and Reels. 1:1 is square.
jump cut editing style | why do youtubers cut so much => A jump cut skips forward within the same shot, making the subject jump slightly. YouTubers use them to cut out pauses and keep pace fast.
what is a j cut and l cut | j cut l cut editing => In a J-cut, the next clip's audio starts before its video. In an L-cut, the video changes but the last clip's audio keeps going. Both make edits feel smooth and natural.
what is color grading vs color correction | grading vs correcting color => Color correction fixes problems so footage looks natural and matches. Color grading adds a creative look, like warm and golden or cold and blue.
what is log footage | why does my footage look grey => Log footage records a flat, grey-looking image that keeps more detail in highlights and shadows. It needs color grading or a LUT to look normal.
what is hdr video | sdr vs hdr video => HDR video has brighter highlights and more color range than normal SDR. It looks great on HDR screens but needs careful editing; SDR is simpler for most YouTube videos.
what is a render | why does rendering take long => Rendering creates the final video file by processing every frame with all edits and effects. Long videos, high resolution and heavy effects take longer.
what is hardware encoding | nvenc export => Hardware encoding uses special parts of the GPU (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, Intel Quick Sync) to export or record video fast, with little CPU use.
how do i make my video file smaller | compress a video => Export with H.265, lower the bitrate, or lower the resolution. Free HandBrake can recompress videos with presets.
what is handbrake | free video converter => HandBrake is a free, open source tool for converting and compressing videos into formats like MP4 with H.264, H.265 or AV1.
what is remuxing | mkv to mp4 without quality loss => Remuxing copies the video and audio into a new container without re-encoding, so there's no quality loss. OBS has a Remux Recordings option in its File menu.
what is a keyframe in animation and editing | keyframes explained => A keyframe marks a value at a point in time, like position or size. The software fills in the motion between keyframes.
what is a transition | do too many transitions look bad => A transition blends one clip into the next, like a fade or wipe. Most edits use simple straight cuts; save fancy transitions for special moments.
how do i make a video intro | youtube intro tips => Keep it under 5 to 10 seconds or skip it. Many creators start with a hook that shows what the video is about in the first few seconds instead.
what is a hook in a video | how to start a video so people keep watching => The hook is the first few seconds that grab viewers, like showing the best moment or asking a question the video answers.
what is pacing in editing | make my video less boring => Pacing is how fast things move. Cut out pauses, mistakes and dead time, change shots every few seconds, and add B-roll or zooms to keep energy up.
what is sound design in video | sound effects editing => Sound design is adding effects and ambience, like whooshes on transitions or impact sounds. Good audio makes videos feel much more professional.
what is fair use | can i use clips from other videos => Fair use is a US legal idea that allows limited use of copyrighted material for things like commentary or criticism. It's decided case by case, so it's risky to rely on.
what video editors are good for beginners | free video editing software => DaVinci Resolve is free and powerful. CapCut is easy for short videos. Clipchamp comes with Windows, and iMovie is free on Apple devices.
what is capcut | is capcut good for shorts => CapCut is a free, easy editor popular for TikTok and Shorts, with auto captions and effects. Check its terms and use it with a parent's okay.
what is imovie | edit video on ipad => iMovie is Apple's free video editor for iPad, iPhone and Mac. It's simple, with trimming, titles, music and green screen.
can i use davinci resolve on ipad | resolve for ipad => Yes. DaVinci Resolve for iPad is available and supports the Cut and Color pages on iPads with M1 or newer chips, with a free version.
what is clipchamp | windows video editor => Clipchamp is Microsoft's simple video editor included with Windows 11. It's browser-based and good for quick edits.
what is a lower third | name tag graphic video => A lower third is a text graphic in the bottom part of the screen, often showing a name or topic.
what is a timeline in editing | editing timeline tracks => The timeline is where you arrange clips in order over time, with video tracks stacked above audio tracks.
what is a nle | non linear editor => A non-linear editor (NLE) lets you jump to and change any part of the video at any time, like Resolve, Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro.
what is premiere pro | premiere vs resolve => Adobe Premiere Pro is a popular paid video editor by subscription. Resolve is free and has stronger built-in color tools. Both are used by pros.
what is final cut pro | final cut vs resolve => Final Cut Pro is Apple's paid video editor for Mac and iPad, known for being fast on Apple silicon.
what is after effects | adobe after effects used for => Adobe After Effects is for motion graphics and visual effects, like animated text and compositing. Resolve's Fusion page does similar work for free.
how do i record gameplay on pc | best way to record games => Use OBS Studio for full control, the NVIDIA App or AMD Software for easy high-quality clips, or the Xbox Game Bar with Windows key + Alt + R.
how do i record my voice for videos | good audio for youtube => Use a USB or XLR mic close to your mouth, record in a quiet room with soft things around to cut echo, and keep levels peaking around -12 to -6 dB.
what is audio clipping | my audio sounds distorted => Clipping happens when sound is too loud for the recording, flattening the peaks into harsh distortion. Lower the mic gain; you can't fully fix clipped audio later.
what is xlr vs usb mic | usb microphone vs xlr for streaming => USB mics plug straight into the computer and are easy. XLR mics need an audio interface but are more flexible and upgradeable.
what sample rate should i record audio at | 48khz vs 44.1 => Use 48 kHz for video, since it's the video standard. 44.1 kHz is the music CD standard. Match it across your gear to avoid sync problems.
