# tag: tech.shortcuts.apps3
how do i turn on gmail keyboard shortcuts | enable gmail shortcuts => In Gmail on a computer, open Settings > See all settings, turn Keyboard shortcuts on and save. Then press Shift+? to see them all.
how do i write a new email with a key in gmail | c key gmail => With Gmail shortcuts on, press C to start a new email.
how do i reply with a key in gmail | r key gmail reply => With shortcuts on, open an email and press R to reply, A to reply all or F to forward.
how do i archive an email with a key in gmail | e key gmail => With shortcuts on, press E to archive the open or selected email, or # to delete it.
how do i jump to the gmail search box | slash key gmail => With shortcuts on, press / to jump straight to the search box.
how do i move between emails in gmail with keys | j and k gmail => With shortcuts on, press J for the older email and K for the newer one.
how do i mark an email as read with keys in gmail | shift i gmail => With shortcuts on, press Shift+I to mark as read and Shift+U to mark as unread.
how do i star an email with a key in gmail | s key gmail star => With shortcuts on, press S to star or unstar an email.
how do i go to my inbox with keys in gmail | g then i gmail => With shortcuts on, press G then I to go to your inbox, or G then S for starred mail.
how do i write a new email in outlook with keys | ctrl n outlook => In the Outlook app on Windows, press Ctrl+N in your mail folder to start a new email.
how do i reply all with keys in outlook | ctrl shift r outlook => In Outlook on Windows, press Ctrl+R to reply, Ctrl+Shift+R to reply all and Ctrl+F to forward.
how do i send an email with keys in outlook | ctrl enter outlook send => In Outlook, press Ctrl+Enter to send the email you're writing. It may ask you to confirm the first time.
how do i switch to the outlook calendar with keys | ctrl 2 outlook => In Outlook on Windows, press Ctrl+1 for mail and Ctrl+2 for the calendar.
how do i mark an email as read in outlook with keys | ctrl q outlook => In Outlook on Windows, press Ctrl+Q to mark as read and Ctrl+U to mark as unread.
how do i draw a line in fusion 360 with a key | l key fusion => In a Fusion sketch, press L for the Line tool. C draws a center circle and R a rectangle.
how do i extrude in fusion 360 with a key | e key fusion => Press E to open Extrude, then pick the sketch profile and drag or type a distance.
how do i add a dimension in fusion 360 | d key fusion sketch => Press D in a sketch, click a line or two points, and type the size. Dimensions lock your sketch to exact measurements.
how do i open the fusion 360 shortcut box | s key fusion => Press S to open a search box for any command. You can also pin your favorite tools there.
how do i fillet an edge in fusion 360 with a key | f key fusion => Press F for Fillet, select edges and drag or type a radius to round them.
how do i move a body in fusion 360 | m key fusion => Press M for Move/Copy, select the body or component and drag the arrows or type a distance.
how do i make a construction line in fusion 360 | x key fusion construction => Select sketch lines and press X to turn them into construction lines, which guide your sketch but don't make shapes.
how do i trim lines in fusion 360 | t key fusion trim => Press T in a sketch and click line segments to delete them up to where they cross other lines.
how do i offset a sketch in fusion 360 | o key fusion => Press O for Offset, select sketch lines and drag to make a copy a set distance away, handy for walls and borders.
how do i use press pull in fusion 360 | q key fusion => Press Q for Press Pull, then click a face to push or pull it, or an edge to fillet it.
how do i add a joint in fusion 360 with a key | j key fusion => Press J to add a joint that connects two components so they move together or around a point.
how do i pause a video in vlc | space bar vlc => Press the space bar to play or pause, F for full screen and M to mute.
how do i skip forward in vlc | vlc jump keys => In VLC on Windows, Alt+Right jumps forward about 10 seconds and Ctrl+Right about a minute. Use Left instead to go back.
how do i go frame by frame in vlc | e key vlc => Press E to step forward one frame at a time while paused.
how do i change subtitles in vlc | v key vlc subtitles => Press V to cycle through subtitle tracks, or use the Subtitle menu to load a subtitle file.
how do i change the audio track in vlc | b key vlc => Press B to cycle through audio tracks, like different languages.
how do i change the volume in vlc with keys | ctrl up vlc => Press Ctrl+Up or Ctrl+Down to change the volume, or scroll the mouse wheel over the video.
