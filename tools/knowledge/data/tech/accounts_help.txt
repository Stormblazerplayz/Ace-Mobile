# tag: tech.accounts
what do i do if my game account got hacked | someone stole my account => Use the site's official Forgot password or account recovery page, change your email password too, turn on 2FA and contact the game's support. Tell a parent, and never pay anyone who offers to recover it.
i forgot my password what do i do | reset a forgotten password => Click Forgot password on the real sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email or phone. Type the site's address yourself instead of using links from messages.
why am i locked out of my account | too many wrong password tries => Sites lock accounts for a while after several wrong tries to stop guessing attacks. Wait the time it says or use the official recovery option.
how do i appeal a ban in a game | my account got banned unfairly => Use the game's official support or appeal page, explain calmly what happened and give details like dates. Never use sites that promise unbans for money.
what is a shadowban | why is no one seeing my posts => A shadowban is when a platform quietly hides your posts or limits who sees them without telling you, often for spammy behavior. Following the rules and waiting usually fixes it.
what if the birthday on my account is wrong | change age on account => Many services don't let you change your birthday yourself, so contact support with a parent. Always give your real age, since age settings turn on safety protections.
how do i delete an account i don't use | close old accounts => Look in the account's settings for Delete account, or search the site's help pages. Deleting old accounts means less of your info could leak.
how do i change my username | rename my account => Most apps let you change it in profile or account settings. Some limit how often, like once every few weeks, and some old names become free for others to take.
what are terms of service | do i have to read terms and conditions => Terms of service are the rules you agree to when using an app, like age limits and what gets you banned. Skim the important parts, or ask a parent to check them with you.
