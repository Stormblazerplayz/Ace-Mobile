# tag: tech.gaming.gear2
what is a racing wheel for gaming | sim racing wheel => A racing wheel with pedals clamps to a desk or stand and gives realistic steering for games like Gran Turismo, Forza and F1, much more precise than a controller.
what is force feedback on a wheel | direct drive vs belt wheel => Force feedback pushes back through the wheel so you feel grip and bumps. Direct drive wheels attach the wheel straight to the motor for the strongest, most detailed feel, while gear and belt wheels cost less.
what is a hotas | flight stick for games => A HOTAS, short for hands on throttle and stick, is a joystick plus a separate throttle for flight and space games like Microsoft Flight Simulator and Elite Dangerous.
what is a leverless controller | hitbox controller => A leverless controller replaces the joystick with buttons for directions, popular in fighting games because buttons can be pressed faster and more precisely.
what is a tkl keyboard | tenkeyless meaning => TKL, or tenkeyless, is a full keyboard without the number pad. It leaves more room for your mouse, which helps low-sensitivity gamers.
what is keyboard foam | why add foam to a keyboard => Foam inside a keyboard case fills empty space so typing sounds less hollow and pingy. Many custom keyboards come with it already.
what is a desk mat | big mousepad for whole desk => A desk mat is an extra-large mousepad that covers most of the desk, so the keyboard and mouse sit on one smooth surface.
