# tag: tech.chromebook
how do i right click on a chromebook | chromebook right click touchpad => Tap the touchpad with two fingers, or hold Alt and click.
how do i turn on caps lock on a chromebook | chromebook has no caps lock key => Press the Search or Launcher key and Alt together to toggle Caps Lock.
how do i forward delete on a chromebook | chromebook delete key => Press Alt + Backspace to delete the character in front of the cursor, like a Delete key.
how do i use f keys on a chromebook | chromebook function keys => Hold the Search or Launcher key and press a top-row key to use it as F1 to F12.
how do i see all chromebook shortcuts | chromebook shortcut list => Press Ctrl + Alt + / to open the keyboard shortcuts list, where you can search for any shortcut.
how do i open task manager on a chromebook | chromebook frozen tab => Press Search + Esc to open Task Manager, then pick the app or tab and click End process.
how do i split the screen on a chromebook | two windows side by side chromebook => Drag a window to the left or right edge, or press Alt + [ or Alt + ] to snap it to a half.
how do i see clipboard history on a chromebook | chromebook paste old copies => Press Search + V to open clipboard history and pick something you copied earlier.
how do i type emoji on a chromebook | chromebook emoji picker => Press Search + Shift + Space to open the emoji picker. It also has symbols and GIFs.
how do i screen record on a chromebook | chromebook video capture => Press Ctrl + Shift + Show windows to open Screen capture, switch to the video option and pick what to record.
how do i lock my chromebook | chromebook lock screen shortcut => Press Search + L to lock it right away.
how do i update a chromebook | chromeos update => Open Settings, About ChromeOS, and click Check for updates. Restart when it asks.
what is powerwash on a chromebook | factory reset chromebook => Powerwash erases everything on the Chromebook and resets it. Go to Settings, Reset settings, or press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + R at the sign-in screen.
what is a chromebook auto update expiration date | how long do chromebooks get updates => Each Chromebook gets updates until a set date. Models from 2019 and later get about 10 years from their release, and you can see the date in Settings, About ChromeOS.
what is chromeos flex | turn an old pc into a chromebook => ChromeOS Flex is a free version of ChromeOS you can install on many older Windows PCs and Macs to make them fast and useful again.
can i run linux apps on a chromebook | chromebook linux setup => Many Chromebooks can turn on a Linux development environment in Settings, About ChromeOS, Developers. School-managed ones often block it.
can i get android apps on a chromebook | play store on chromebook => Most Chromebooks can install Android apps from the Google Play Store, though some apps aren't made for big screens.
why can't i change things on my school chromebook | managed chromebook => School Chromebooks are managed by the school, which controls apps, sites and settings. Ask your school's tech help, and don't try to get around the rules.
what is phone hub on a chromebook | connect android phone to chromebook => Phone Hub links an Android phone to your Chromebook to see notifications, check battery, use its hotspot and see recent photos.
what is guest mode on a chromebook | use chromebook without signing in => Guest mode lets someone browse without an account. Everything they do is deleted when they sign out.
how do i zip files on a chromebook | open a zip file chromebook => In the Files app, select the files, right-click and choose Zip selection. Double-click a zip to open it like a folder.
how do i make a new desk on a chromebook | chromebook virtual desks => Press Search + Shift + = to make a new desk, and Search + [ or ] to switch between desks.
what is the show windows key on a chromebook | chromebook overview key => The Show windows key, which looks like a rectangle with lines, shows all your open windows and desks so you can switch.
how do i turn on the screen reader on a chromebook | chromevox => Press Ctrl + Alt + Z to turn ChromeVox, the built-in screen reader, on or off.
how do i zoom the whole screen on a chromebook | chromebook magnifier => Press Ctrl + Search + M to turn on the full-screen magnifier, then use Ctrl + Alt with brightness keys or pinch to change the zoom.
