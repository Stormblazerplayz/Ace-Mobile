# tag: tech.iphone
how do i take a screenshot on an iphone | iphone screenshot buttons => On iPhones with Face ID, press the side button and volume up together. On iPhones with a Home button, press the side (or top) button and the Home button together.
how do i force restart my iphone | iphone frozen => Press and release volume up, press and release volume down, then press and hold the side button until the Apple logo shows.
how do i turn on silent mode on iphone | mute my iphone => Flip the Ring/Silent switch on the side toward the back, or on iPhone 15 Pro and later, press and hold the Action button. You can also use Control Center.
what is the action button on iphone | change action button => The Action button on iPhone 15 Pro and later replaces the mute switch. In Settings, Action Button, you can make it run the flashlight, camera, a shortcut or more.
what is the dynamic island | iphone pill at top => The Dynamic Island is the black pill at the top of newer iPhones that shows live info like timers, music and calls. Tap it to open the app, or touch and hold to expand it.
how do i use back tap on iphone | tap back of iphone shortcut => Open Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap, and pick an action for Double Tap or Triple Tap, like a screenshot.
how do i turn on the flashlight on iphone | iphone torch => Open Control Center and tap the flashlight, or touch and hold the flashlight on the Lock Screen. Touch and hold it in Control Center to change brightness.
how do i check my iphone battery health | iphone battery maximum capacity => Open Settings, Battery, Battery Health. Maximum Capacity shows how much charge the battery holds compared to new.
what is optimized battery charging | iphone stops at 80 percent => It learns your routine and waits at 80 percent until just before you usually unplug, to slow battery aging. iPhone 15 and later can also limit charging to 80 percent or other levels.
how do i see my iphone storage | iphone storage full => Open Settings, General, iPhone Storage to see what uses space, with tips to free some up.
how do i back up my iphone | iphone icloud backup => Open Settings, tap your name, iCloud, iCloud Backup, and turn on Back Up This iPhone. Tap Back Up Now on Wi-Fi.
how do i transfer to a new iphone | quick start iphone => Turn on the new iPhone and hold it near the old one. Quick Start appears and copies your data and settings wirelessly.
how do i share my location with my family | find my iphone share location => Open Find My, tap People, then Share My Location, and pick a family member. Only share your location with family and people you trust in real life.
how do i block a number on iphone | stop calls from someone => Open Phone, Recents, tap the info button next to the number, and tap Block Caller. Tell a trusted adult if someone keeps bothering you.
how do i silence unknown callers | stop spam calls iphone => Open Settings, Apps, Phone (or Settings, Phone on older iOS), and turn on Silence Unknown Callers. Calls from numbers not in your contacts go straight to voicemail.
how do i turn off read receipts in imessage | read receipts iphone => Open Settings, Apps, Messages, and turn off Send Read Receipts. You can also turn it off for one person in their conversation's info.
how do i unsend a message on iphone | undo send imessage => Touch and hold an iMessage you just sent and tap Undo Send within 2 minutes. Both people need iOS 16 or later.
how do i edit a sent message on iphone | edit imessage => Touch and hold the iMessage and tap Edit within 15 minutes. The other person sees that it was edited.
what is the difference between blue and green messages | imessage vs sms => Blue bubbles are iMessages sent over the internet between Apple devices. Green are regular SMS or RCS texts to non-Apple phones.
how do i scan a qr code with my iphone | iphone qr code => Open the Camera app and point it at the QR code, then tap the link that appears. Check the link before opening it, since QR codes can lead to scam sites.
how do i use the iphone as a hotspot | personal hotspot => Open Settings, Personal Hotspot, and turn on Allow Others to Join. Ask a parent first, since it uses mobile data.
how do i set up focus on iphone | sleep focus => Open Settings, Focus, and pick or add a Focus like Sleep or Do Not Disturb. Choose who can reach you and which apps can notify you.
how do i change my iphone wallpaper | iphone lock screen customize => Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize or the plus button, and pick a photo, colors or a preset. You can add widgets and change the clock style.
how do i add widgets to the iphone lock screen | lock screen widgets => Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, and tap the widget area to add ones like weather or battery.
what is standby mode on iphone | iphone clock while charging => StandBy shows a big clock, photos or widgets when an iPhone on iOS 17 or later is charging, on its side and locked.
how do i turn on live captions on iphone | captions for any audio => Open Settings, Accessibility, Live Captions, and turn it on. It shows captions for videos, calls and audio on supported iPhones.
how do i measure my heart rate with iphone | iphone health app => The iPhone itself doesn't measure heart rate. An Apple Watch or a compatible device sends that data to the Health app.
how do i use iphone camera night mode | low light photos iphone => On supported iPhones, Night mode turns on automatically in the dark; a yellow moon icon appears. Hold still while it captures.
how do i shoot in 4k 60 on iphone | change video quality iphone => Open Settings, Camera, Record Video, and pick 4K at 60 fps. You can also tap the resolution and frame rate in the corner of the Camera app.
what is apple proraw | proraw vs heif => ProRAW keeps much more image data for editing, on Pro iPhones. Files are much bigger than normal HEIF photos, so use it when you plan to edit.
what is cinematic mode | blurry background video iphone => Cinematic mode records video with a blurred background and lets you change the focus later. Find it in the Camera app's modes.
how do i make my iphone photos jpg | iphone heic to jpg => Open Settings, Camera, Formats, and choose Most Compatible to save photos as JPEG and videos as H.264 instead of HEIF and HEVC.
how do i turn off live photos | live photo off => Tap the Live Photos button (circles) at the top of the Camera app to turn it off. To keep it off, open Settings, Camera, Preserve Settings, and turn on Live Photo.
how do i use iphone as a webcam for my mac | continuity camera => With iOS 16 or later and macOS Ventura or later, place the iPhone near the Mac on a stand. Pick it as the camera in FaceTime, Zoom or other apps.
what is namedrop | share contact iphone => NameDrop lets you share contact info by holding two iPhones close together. You choose what to share and can tap Receive Only. Only use it with people you know.
how do i stop my iphone screen dimming | iphone auto brightness => Open Settings, Accessibility, Display and Text Size, and turn off Auto-Brightness. Low Power Mode and Attention Aware Features can also dim it.
what is sim vs esim | what is an esim => A SIM card is a tiny chip that links your phone to a mobile network. An eSIM does the same job built into the phone, with no physical card. US iPhone 14 and later use eSIM only.
how do i turn on airplane mode | what does airplane mode do => Open Control Center and tap the airplane. It turns off mobile signal, and usually Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, though you can turn those back on.
how do i update my iphone | ios update => Open Settings, General, Software Update, and tap Update Now while on Wi-Fi and charging.
what is ios 26 | why is it called ios 26 => In 2025 Apple switched to year-based names, so iOS 18 was followed by iOS 26. It brought the new Liquid Glass design and features like call screening and live translation.
how do i turn on dark mode on iphone | iphone dark theme => Open Settings, Display and Brightness, and choose Dark, or set it to Automatic so it switches at sunset.
how do i free up iphone storage fast | delete messages to free space => Delete apps you don't use, clear the Recently Deleted photo album, turn on Offload Unused Apps, and set Messages to keep messages for 1 year in Settings, Apps, Messages.
how do i find my iphone if it's lost | track my missing iphone => Sign in at icloud.com/find or use Find My on another Apple device. Play a sound, see it on a map or mark it lost. Find My must be turned on first.
what is stolen device protection | iphone thief protection => Stolen Device Protection asks for Face ID or Touch ID, with no passcode fallback, for sensitive changes when the iPhone is away from familiar places. Turn it on in Settings, Face ID and Passcode.
how do i record a phone call on iphone | iphone call recording => On iOS 18.1 and later in supported regions, tap the record button during a call. Everyone on the call hears that it's being recorded. Always ask permission before recording someone.
