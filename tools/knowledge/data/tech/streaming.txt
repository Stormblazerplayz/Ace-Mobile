# tag: tech.streaming.obs
what is obs | what is obs studio => OBS Studio is free, open source software for recording and live streaming. You build scenes from sources like your game, webcam, mic and images.
how do i set up obs for the first time | obs setup beginner => Run the Auto-Configuration Wizard from the Tools menu, choose streaming or recording, then add a scene with a Game Capture or Display Capture source and your mic.
what are scenes and sources in obs | obs scenes explained => A scene is a layout, like "Gameplay" or "Starting Soon". Sources are the pieces inside it, like a game capture, webcam, text or images. Switch scenes during a stream.
why is my obs game capture black | obs black screen capture => Run OBS as administrator, try Window Capture or Display Capture instead, and on laptops make OBS run on the same GPU as the game in Windows Graphics settings.
what is the best obs recording format | record in mkv obs => Record to MKV or Hybrid MP4 so a crash doesn't ruin the file. Remux MKV to MP4 afterward with File, Remux Recordings if your editor needs MP4.
what encoder should i use in obs | nvenc vs x264 => Use your GPU's hardware encoder (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF or Intel QSV) so recording barely affects game FPS. x264 uses the CPU and can hurt performance.
what obs recording settings are best | obs quality settings recording => For recording, use a hardware encoder with a quality-based mode (CQP or CQ around 18 to 23), your monitor's resolution, and 60 fps for gameplay. Lower numbers mean higher quality and bigger files.
what bitrate should i stream at | stream bitrate twitch => As of 2025, Twitch recommends up to about 6000 Kbps for 1080p 60. Make sure your upload speed is comfortably higher than your bitrate, or lower it to 4500 for 720p 60.
what resolution should i stream at | 720p or 1080p stream => If your upload or bitrate is limited, 720p at 60 fps often looks cleaner than a blurry 1080p. With enough bitrate or on YouTube, 1080p 60 is great.
why is my stream dropping frames | dropped frames obs => Dropped frames mean your connection can't keep up. Use Ethernet, lower the bitrate, and pick a closer server. "Skipped frames" instead means the encoder or GPU is overloaded.
what is a stream key | is my stream key secret => The stream key connects OBS to your channel. Keep it secret like a password; anyone who has it can stream to your channel. Reset it if it leaks.
how do i add my webcam to obs | webcam in obs => In your scene, click the plus under Sources, choose Video Capture Device, and pick your camera. Drag the corners to resize it.
how do i remove my background in obs without green screen | obs background blur => Some cameras and apps (like NVIDIA Broadcast) blur or remove backgrounds. In OBS, filters like Chroma Key need a green screen for clean results.
how do i use a green screen in obs | chroma key obs => Right-click the webcam source, choose Filters, add Chroma Key, and set it to green. Light the green screen evenly to avoid patches.
how do i make my mic sound better in obs | obs mic filters => Right-click your mic in the Audio Mixer, choose Filters, and add Noise Suppression, then Noise Gate, then Compressor, and finally a Limiter at about -1 dB.
how do i stop my game being too loud in obs | balance audio obs => Use the Audio Mixer sliders so your voice peaks in the yellow zone and game audio sits lower. Keep the meter out of the red to avoid distortion.
how do i record discord and game audio separately | obs separate audio tracks => In OBS Settings, Output, Recording, tick multiple audio tracks. Then in Advanced Audio Properties, assign mic, game and Discord to different tracks, using Application Audio Capture sources.
what is application audio capture | capture one app's sound obs => Application Audio Capture is an OBS source that records sound from one app only, like the game or Spotify, so you can control each separately.
what is the obs replay buffer | save last 30 seconds obs => The replay buffer keeps the last few seconds or minutes in memory. Turn it on in Settings, Output, set a hotkey, and press it to save a clip after something cool happens.
how do i add alerts to my stream | follower alerts => Services like StreamElements or Streamlabs give you a browser source link. Add it in OBS as a Browser source and alerts appear when people follow or subscribe.
what is a browser source in obs | add a webpage to obs => A browser source shows a web page inside OBS, like alerts, chat boxes or overlays from streaming tools.
how do i stream to youtube from obs | obs youtube live => In OBS Settings, Stream, choose YouTube and connect your account, or paste the stream key from YouTube Studio's Go Live page. YouTube may need your channel verified first.
how do i stream to twitch | twitch obs setup => In OBS Settings, Stream, choose Twitch and connect your account, pick your bitrate in Output, then click Start Streaming. Twitch requires users to be 13 or older.
how old do you have to be to stream | can kids stream on twitch => Twitch and YouTube both require you to be at least 13 in most countries, and kids under 18 need a parent's permission and help. Never share personal info on stream.
how do i stay safe while live streaming | streaming safety for kids => Hide your real name, school, location and house details, don't show windows or the view outside, turn on chat moderation, and stream with a parent's knowledge.
what is stream delay | add a delay to my stream => A stream delay holds your broadcast for a set time, which stops people using your stream to find you in matches (stream sniping). Set it in OBS Settings, Advanced.
what is a vertical stream | stream for tiktok and shorts => Vertical streams are 9:16 for mobile viewers on TikTok, Shorts and Instagram. OBS can run a vertical canvas with plugins like Aitum Vertical.
how do i stream to multiple platforms | multistream => Services like Restream send your stream to several platforms at once, or OBS plugins can send multiple outputs if your upload speed can handle it.
what is a capture card | do i need a capture card => A capture card brings video from a console or another PC into your computer for recording or streaming. You don't need one to stream PC games from the same PC.
how do i stream my switch | record nintendo switch gameplay => Connect the Switch dock's HDMI to a capture card, then the capture card's HDMI out to your TV, and add it in OBS as a Video Capture Device.
what is nvidia broadcast | ai mic noise removal => NVIDIA Broadcast is a free app for RTX cards that removes background noise from your mic and can blur or replace your webcam background using AI.
what is a stream deck | elgato stream deck => A Stream Deck is a pad of customizable buttons with tiny screens to switch scenes, mute your mic, play sounds or run shortcuts while streaming.
why does my stream look blocky | pixelated stream => Blockiness comes from too little bitrate for the motion. Raise the bitrate if your upload allows, lower the resolution to 720p, or use a better encoder preset.
how do i record my screen with obs without games | obs display capture => Add a Display Capture source to record your whole screen, or Window Capture for one app. Click Start Recording.
what is a scene transition in obs | stinger transition => It's the effect when you switch scenes, like Fade or Cut. Stingers let you play a short animated video as the transition.
# tag: tech.streaming.youtube
what is made for kids on youtube | made for kids setting => YouTube requires creators to mark videos made mainly for children. Those videos have comments and some features turned off. It's a legal requirement linked to COPPA.
why is my youtube upload only in sd | youtube hd processing => YouTube processes low resolution first, and HD or 4K versions appear later, sometimes after an hour or more. Wait before deciding the quality is bad.
how do i add chapters to a youtube video | youtube timestamps => Add timestamps in the description, starting with 0:00, with at least three in order, each at least 10 seconds long. YouTube turns them into chapters.
how do i schedule a youtube video | upload later youtube => Upload it, and on the Visibility step choose Schedule, then pick a date and time for it to go public.
what are end screens on youtube | add end screen => End screens are clickable elements in the last 5 to 20 seconds of a video, like other videos or a subscribe button. Add them in YouTube Studio's upload or Editor screen.
what is unlisted on youtube | private vs unlisted => Private videos only you (and people you invite) can see. Unlisted videos can be watched by anyone with the link but don't show in search or on your channel.
how do i upload captions to youtube | add subtitles youtube => In YouTube Studio, open the video, go to Subtitles, and upload a subtitle file (like .srt) or check and edit the auto captions.
what is a youtube thumbnail size | thumbnail resolution => Use 1280 x 720 pixels (16:9) for thumbnails, under 2 MB, as JPG or PNG. You may need to verify your account to upload custom thumbnails.
how do i trim a video after uploading to youtube | youtube studio editor => In YouTube Studio open the video, click Editor, then Trim and cut to remove parts. It can take a while to process.
