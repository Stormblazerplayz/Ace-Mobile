# tag: tech.acronyms
what does usb stand for | usb full name => USB stands for Universal Serial Bus. It's the standard plug for connecting and charging devices.
what does hdmi stand for | hdmi full name => HDMI stands for High-Definition Multimedia Interface. It carries video and sound in one cable.
what does cpu stand for | cpu full name => CPU stands for Central Processing Unit, the main chip that runs your programs.
what does gpu stand for | gpu full name => GPU stands for Graphics Processing Unit, the chip that draws images, games and video.
what does ram stand for | ram full name => RAM stands for Random Access Memory, the fast short-term memory your computer uses while it's on.
what does ssd stand for | ssd full name => SSD stands for Solid State Drive, a storage drive with no moving parts.
what does hdd stand for | hdd full name => HDD stands for Hard Disk Drive, an older storage drive with spinning disks inside.
what does nvme stand for | nvme full name => NVMe stands for Non-Volatile Memory Express, the fast way modern M.2 SSDs talk to the computer.
what does pcie stand for | pcie full name => PCIe stands for Peripheral Component Interconnect Express, the slots and lanes that connect graphics cards and SSDs.
what does psu stand for | psu full name => PSU stands for Power Supply Unit, the box that turns wall power into power your PC parts can use.
what does bios stand for | bios full name => BIOS stands for Basic Input/Output System. Modern PCs use UEFI, which stands for Unified Extensible Firmware Interface.
what does oled stand for | oled full name => OLED stands for Organic Light-Emitting Diode. Each pixel is made of organic material that glows by itself.
what does lcd stand for | lcd full name => LCD stands for Liquid Crystal Display, a screen that uses liquid crystals to block or let through a backlight.
what does ips stand for on a monitor | ips va tn meaning => IPS means In-Plane Switching, VA means Vertical Alignment and TN means Twisted Nematic. They're the three main types of LCD panel.
what does hdr stand for | hdr full name => HDR stands for High Dynamic Range, meaning brighter highlights and deeper shadows with more detail in both.
what does dpi stand for | dpi full name => DPI stands for dots per inch. For mice it means how far the cursor moves per inch, and for printing it means how fine the dots are.
what does rgb stand for | rgb full name => RGB stands for red, green and blue, the three colors of light screens mix to make every other color.
what does pdf stand for | pdf full name => PDF stands for Portable Document Format, made so a document looks the same on any device.
what does jpeg stand for | jpg full name => JPEG stands for Joint Photographic Experts Group, the group that created the format.
what does png stand for | png full name => PNG stands for Portable Network Graphics, an image format that keeps quality and supports see-through backgrounds.
what does gif stand for | is it gif or jif => GIF stands for Graphics Interchange Format. Its creator said it like jif, but most people say it with a hard g, and both are understood.
what does mp4 stand for | mp3 and mp4 meaning => MP3 is short for MPEG-1 Audio Layer III and MP4 is MPEG-4 Part 14. MPEG is the Moving Picture Experts Group that made them.
what does wifi stand for | is wifi short for something => Wi-Fi isn't really short for anything. It's a brand name created to sound catchy, though some people say it means wireless fidelity.
where does the name bluetooth come from | why is it called bluetooth => Bluetooth is named after Harald Bluetooth, an old Danish king who united tribes, just like the tech unites devices. Its logo mixes his initials in runes.
what does qr stand for in qr code | qr code full name => QR stands for Quick Response, because the codes were designed to be read very fast.
what does captcha stand for | captcha full name => CAPTCHA stands for Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart.
what does sim stand for | sim card full name => SIM stands for Subscriber Identity Module, the chip that links your phone to your phone number and plan.
what do vr ar and xr stand for | xr meaning => VR is virtual reality, AR is augmented reality and XR is extended reality, a term that covers both and everything in between.
what does tpm stand for | tpm full name => TPM stands for Trusted Platform Module, a security chip that stores encryption keys. Windows 11 needs TPM 2.0.
what does ddr stand for in ram | ddr5 meaning => DDR stands for Double Data Rate, because the memory sends data twice per clock tick. The number is the generation.
what does oem mean | oem windows key => OEM stands for Original Equipment Manufacturer, the company that makes a product or part. OEM software comes tied to a specific PC.
what does rma mean | rma a graphics card => RMA stands for Return Merchandise Authorization, the warranty process for sending a broken part back to be fixed or replaced.
what does msrp mean | msrp price => MSRP stands for Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price, the price the maker suggests stores sell it for.
what does eol mean in tech | end of life product => EOL means end of life, when a company stops selling or updating a product, so it gets no more fixes.
what does sff mean in pc building | small form factor pc => SFF stands for small form factor, meaning very compact PC builds that need small parts and careful cooling.
what does atx stand for | atx and itx meaning => ATX stands for Advanced Technology eXtended, the standard size for motherboards and cases. ITX and Micro-ATX are smaller versions.
what does ups stand for in computers | ups battery meaning => UPS stands for Uninterruptible Power Supply, a battery box that keeps your PC running for a few minutes in a power cut.
what does nas stand for | nas full name => NAS stands for Network Attached Storage, a box of drives that every device on your home network can use.
what does raid stand for | raid full name => RAID stands for Redundant Array of Independent Disks, a way of combining drives for speed or backup.
what does dlss stand for | fsr full name => DLSS stands for Deep Learning Super Sampling from Nvidia. AMD's FSR stands for FidelityFX Super Resolution.
what does xmp stand for | expo stands for => XMP stands for Extreme Memory Profile from Intel, and AMD's EXPO stands for Extended Profiles for Overclocking.
what does lut stand for | lut full name => LUT stands for lookup table, a file that maps one set of colors to another to change a video's look.
what does lufs stand for | lufs full name => LUFS stands for Loudness Units relative to Full Scale, the way streaming sites measure how loud audio sounds.
what does stl stand for | stl file full name => STL usually stands for stereolithography, an early kind of 3D printing. The file stores a model as triangles.
what does pla stand for | pla petg abs full names => PLA is polylactic acid, PETG is polyethylene terephthalate glycol, ABS is acrylonitrile butadiene styrene and TPU is thermoplastic polyurethane.
what does lipo stand for | lipo full name => LiPo stands for lithium polymer, the light, powerful batteries used in drones and RC models.
what does rth mean on a drone | rth full name => RTH stands for return to home, where a GPS drone flies itself back to where it took off.
what does ai stand for | ai full name => AI stands for artificial intelligence, computer systems that do tasks that normally need human thinking.
what does wpa stand for | wpa full name => WPA stands for Wi-Fi Protected Access, the security that encrypts your Wi-Fi. WPA3 is the newest version.
what does ip stand for | ip full name => IP stands for Internet Protocol, the rules that give each device an address so data can find it.
