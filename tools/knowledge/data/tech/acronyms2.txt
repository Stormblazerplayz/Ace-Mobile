# tag: tech.acronyms2
what does gui stand for | gui vs cli => GUI stands for graphical user interface, with windows and buttons you click. CLI means command-line interface, where you type commands.
what does ui and ux mean | ui vs ux design => UI means user interface, how an app looks and its buttons. UX means user experience, how easy and pleasant it feels to use.
what does iot stand for | internet of things => IoT stands for Internet of Things, everyday devices like plugs, cameras and fridges that connect to the internet.
what does qos stand for | qos full name => QoS stands for Quality of Service, a router feature that gives some traffic, like games or calls, priority.
what does upnp stand for | upnp full name => UPnP stands for Universal Plug and Play, which lets devices like consoles open the network ports they need automatically.
what do hd fhd qhd and uhd mean | screen resolution names => HD is 1280 by 720, Full HD is 1920 by 1080, QHD is 2560 by 1440 and UHD, or 4K, is 3840 by 2160.
what does eula stand for | eula and tos meaning => EULA means end user license agreement and TOS means terms of service. They're the rules you agree to when using software or a website.
what does mfa stand for | mfa vs 2fa => MFA stands for multi-factor authentication, which means using more than one proof to log in. 2FA is MFA with exactly two.
what does otp stand for | one time password => OTP stands for one-time password, a code that works only once, like the ones texted or shown in authenticator apps.
what do sms and mms stand for | sms vs mms => SMS means Short Message Service, for plain texts. MMS means Multimedia Messaging Service, for pictures and videos over the cell network.
what does rcs stand for | rcs full name => RCS stands for Rich Communication Services, a newer texting standard with typing indicators, read receipts and better media.
what does esim stand for | esim full name => eSIM means embedded SIM, a SIM chip built into the phone that you set up digitally instead of inserting a card.
what is an imei number | find my imei => IMEI stands for International Mobile Equipment Identity, a unique number for each phone. Dial *#06# to see it, and keep it private.
what does amoled stand for | amoled full name => AMOLED stands for Active-Matrix Organic Light-Emitting Diode, a kind of OLED screen used in many phones.
what is ltpo | ltpo display => LTPO is a screen technology that lets phones and watches lower their refresh rate down to 1 Hz to save battery, like on always-on displays.
what does anc stand for | anc full name => ANC stands for active noise cancellation, where headphones play opposite sound waves to cancel out background noise.
what does flac stand for | flac full name => FLAC stands for Free Lossless Audio Codec, which shrinks audio without losing any quality, unlike MP3.
what does wav stand for | wav full name => WAV is short for Waveform Audio File Format, an uncompressed audio file that's high quality but large.
what does mkv stand for | mkv file meaning => MKV files use the Matroska container, named after Russian nesting dolls, because it can hold many video, audio and subtitle tracks.
what does heic stand for | hevc full name => HEIC stands for High Efficiency Image Container, and HEVC for High Efficiency Video Coding. Both save space compared with JPEG and H.264.
what does nvenc stand for | nvenc full name => NVENC is short for Nvidia Encoder, a chip area on GeForce cards that records and streams video without using much CPU.
what does obs stand for | obs full name => OBS stands for Open Broadcaster Software, a free app for recording and streaming.
what does vod stand for | vod meaning streaming => VOD stands for video on demand, like a saved stream or any video you can watch whenever you want.
what does rtmp stand for | rtmp server url => RTMP stands for Real-Time Messaging Protocol, the system many streaming sites use to receive your live stream from OBS.
what does gnss mean | gps vs gnss => GNSS means global navigation satellite system, the general name. GPS is the American system, and others include Galileo, GLONASS and BeiDou.
what does imu stand for | imu sensor => IMU stands for inertial measurement unit, a chip with motion sensors like a gyroscope and accelerometer that tells a drone or phone how it's moving.
what does rtf bnf and pnp mean on drones | bind and fly meaning => RTF means ready to fly, with a controller included. BNF means bind and fly, needing your own radio. PNP means plug and play, needing your own radio and receiver.
what does rc stand for | rc car meaning => RC stands for radio control, like RC cars, planes and drones controlled with a handheld transmitter.
what does cnc stand for | cnc machine => CNC stands for computer numerical control, machines that cut wood, metal or plastic by following computer instructions.
what does ams stand for bambu | ams full name => AMS stands for Automatic Material System, Bambu Lab's unit that feeds several filaments for multicolor printing.
what does pei stand for | pei plate meaning => PEI stands for polyetherimide, a plastic coating on print plates that helps prints stick when hot and release when cool.
what does dm stand for | slide into dms => DM stands for direct message, a private message to one person instead of a public post.
what does pfp mean | pfp meaning => PFP means profile picture, the image people see next to your name online.
what does faq stand for | faq meaning => FAQ stands for frequently asked questions, a page answering common questions so you can find help fast.
what does nfc tag mean | nfc sticker => An NFC tag is a tiny sticker with a chip that phones can read by tapping, to open a link or run a shortcut.
what does mac stand for in mac address | media access control => In MAC address, MAC stands for Media Access Control. It has nothing to do with Apple's Mac computers.
