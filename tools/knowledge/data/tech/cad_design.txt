# tag: tech.cad.basics
what is cad | computer aided design => CAD means computer-aided design: software for drawing exact parts with real measurements, used for 3D printing, machines and buildings.
what is onshape | onshape free plan => Onshape is a parametric CAD program that runs in a web browser and on iPad. Its free plan makes your designs public, so don't use it for secret projects.
what is shapr3d | cad on ipad => Shapr3D is a CAD app built for iPad and Apple Pencil, also on Mac and Windows. Its free tier limits how many designs you can keep and export at good quality.
what is nomad sculpt | sculpting on ipad => Nomad Sculpt is a paid iPad app for digital sculpting, like modeling clay. It's great for characters and creatures and exports STL or OBJ for printing.
what is freecad | free cad software => FreeCAD is a free, open-source parametric CAD program for Windows, Mac and Linux. It's powerful but harder to learn than Fusion or Onshape.
what is a sketch in cad | cad sketch => A sketch is a flat 2D drawing of lines, circles and arcs with exact sizes. You then turn it into 3D with tools like extrude or revolve.
what are constraints in cad | fully constrained sketch => Constraints lock sketch rules like parallel, equal, tangent or a set length. A fully constrained sketch can't change by accident and usually turns black or a different color.
what is extrude in cad | extrude a sketch => Extrude pulls a 2D sketch straight out into a 3D shape, like pushing play dough through a shaped hole. It can also cut into a body.
what is revolve in cad | spin a sketch => Revolve spins a sketch around an axis to make round parts like cups, wheels or vases.
what is a fillet in cad | fillet vs chamfer => A fillet rounds an edge with a curve, and a chamfer cuts it at an angle. For 3D printing, chamfers on bottom edges print better than fillets.
what is shell in cad | hollow out a part => Shell hollows a solid part, leaving walls of a set thickness, like making a box from a block.
what is a pattern in cad | circular pattern => A pattern copies a feature many times in a line or a circle, like holes around a wheel, all staying linked to the original.
what is the timeline in fusion 360 | fusion history => The timeline at the bottom of Fusion records every step. You can go back and change an early step, and the later steps update.
what are parameters in fusion 360 | user parameters => Parameters are named values like wall_thickness that you use in dimensions. Change the number once and every feature using it updates.
what is a component in fusion 360 | bodies vs components => A body is a single solid shape, and a component is a part that can hold bodies and move with joints. Make each separate part its own component.
what are joints in fusion 360 | animate parts fusion => Joints connect components so they move like real hinges, sliders or wheels. You can drag them to test if the design works.
is fusion 360 free | fusion personal use => As of 2025, Autodesk offers a free Personal Use license of Fusion for hobby, non-commercial projects, with some limited features.
how do i export from fusion for printing | fusion to stl => Right-click the body or component in the browser and choose Save As Mesh, then pick 3MF or STL. Or use File, 3D Print to send it to a slicer.
what is a step file used for | step file used for explained => STEP files keep exact CAD geometry so other CAD programs can edit them accurately. STL only stores triangles, so it's harder to edit later.
how do i use holes in tinkercad | make a hole tinkercad => Drag in a shape, change it to Hole in the shape panel, place it overlapping a solid, then select both and click Group to cut it out.
how do i align things in tinkercad | tinkercad align tool => Select the shapes and press L or click Align, then click the dots to line them up by their edges or centers.
how do i use the workplane in tinkercad | build on a slanted face => Press W and click a face to put the workplane on it, so new shapes sit flat on that face. Press W and click the empty grid to reset it.
how do i measure exactly in tinkercad | tinkercad ruler => Drag the Ruler onto the workplane, then select a shape to see and type its exact dimensions and distances.
what is the duplicate and repeat trick in tinkercad | ctrl d tinkercad pattern => Press Ctrl + D to duplicate a shape, move or rotate the copy, then press Ctrl + D again. Tinkercad repeats the same move each time, making patterns.
can i use tinkercad on ipad | tinkercad app => Yes, Tinkercad has an iPad app and also works in a browser, and it's free with an account.
how do i measure a real object for a design | use calipers => Use digital calipers to measure to a tenth of a millimeter. Measure twice, and print a small test piece before the full design.
what are digital calipers | caliper tool => Digital calipers measure outside sizes, inside holes and depths very precisely. They're one of the most useful tools for 3D printing design.
what is the 45 degree rule in 3d printing | overhang angle => Most printers can print overhangs up to about 45 degrees from vertical without supports. Design steeper parts with chamfers or plan for supports.
what is a teardrop hole | horizontal holes 3d print => A teardrop-shaped top on a sideways hole lets it print without supports, because the point keeps the overhang under 45 degrees.
what is elephant's foot | bottom layer bulge => Elephant's foot is when the first layers squish out wider than the rest. A small chamfer on the bottom edge or the slicer's compensation setting fixes it.
how do i design snap fits | snap fit 3d print => A snap fit uses a thin bendy hook that clicks into a slot. Print it so the hook bends along the layers, not across them, or it will snap.
how do i design a box with a lid | 3d print box lid => Make the lid's lip about 0.2 to 0.3 mm smaller on each side than the box opening, then print a test to check the fit.
what is a living hinge | flexible hinge print => A living hinge is a very thin flexible strip joining two parts. It works best in PETG, TPU or polypropylene, since PLA cracks after a few bends.
how do i design for printing without supports | supportless design => Use chamfers instead of overhangs, put flat faces on the bed, split tricky parts into pieces, and use teardrop holes.
what is mesh modeling vs parametric | blender or fusion for printing => Mesh modeling (Blender, Nomad) is great for organic shapes like characters. Parametric CAD (Fusion, Onshape) is better for exact mechanical parts with measurements.
what is a remix on makerworld | remix a model => A remix is a changed version of someone else's model. Check its license first, and credit the original designer when you share it.
what do creative commons licenses mean for models | cc by nc => CC licenses say what you can do with a model. BY means credit the creator, NC means no selling, and ND means no changes.
what is a 3d scanner | scan objects to print => A 3D scanner captures a real object as a mesh. Phone scanning apps work for rough shapes but usually need cleanup before printing.
how do i make a keychain to 3d print | design a keychain => Make a flat shape about 3 to 4 mm thick, add raised text, and cut a hole of at least 4 mm near one edge for the ring.
how do i make a phone stand to print | design phone stand => Measure your phone's thickness, add 1 to 2 mm of room, and angle the back about 60 to 70 degrees. Add width at the base so it doesn't tip.
how do i make a name plate with two colors | two color sign print => Make a base plate and raised letters as separate parts, then print with a color change at the layer where the letters start, or assign colors with the AMS.
how do i make a drone part in cad | design fpv parts => Measure mounting holes carefully (FPV stacks often use 20 x 20 or 30.5 x 30.5 mm patterns), print in PETG or TPU for impact, and test-fit before flying.
what is a cad assembly | assembly in cad => An assembly is several parts put together in CAD, so you can check they fit and move correctly before printing.
