# tag: tech.safety.scams2
what is a qr code scam | quishing => Scammers stick fake QR codes on posters, parking meters or emails that lead to fake login or payment pages. Check the link your camera shows before opening it.
what is a fake family emergency call | voice scam pretending to be family => Scammers can fake a relative's voice with AI and claim they're in trouble and need money fast. Hang up and call the person or a parent on a number you know.
are cheap game key sites safe | grey market game keys => Some key reseller sites sell stolen or region-locked keys that can get revoked. Buying from official stores like Steam or the console store is safest.
is it safe to buy or sell game accounts | buying a gaming account => Buying or selling accounts usually breaks the game's rules and can get the account banned. Scammers also take the money or steal the account back.
what is an unknown tracker alert | airtag found moving with you => Phones warn you if an unknown AirTag or similar tracker seems to be traveling with you. Tell a trusted adult, and use the alert to make it beep and find it.
is it safe to charge my phone at public usb ports | juice jacking => Real cases are rare, but a hacked USB port could try to mess with your phone. Using your own charger in a wall outlet or a power bank is safest.
i got added to a group chat with strangers | random group chat spam => It's often spam or a scam. Don't reply or click links. Leave the chat, block or report it, and tell an adult if anything feels wrong.
email says verify your account or it will be deleted | urgent account warning email => Urgency is a classic phishing trick. Don't click the link. Open the app or type the website yourself to check if anything is really wrong.
why does a website keep sending me notifications | stop browser notification spam => The site got permission to show notifications, often through a tricky Allow button. Remove it in your browser's site settings under Notifications.
a pop up says my computer is infected | fake virus warning website => Real antivirus warnings don't come from websites. Close the tab, or press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to end the browser if it won't close, and don't call any number shown.
what is a lookalike website | typo website address => Scammers buy addresses that look almost right, like rob1ox or amaz0n, to trick people. Check the spelling and use bookmarks for important sites.
how do i spot fake download buttons | which download button is real => Download sites often show big ads that look like download buttons. The real link is usually smaller text, and official sites are safest.
are torrents safe | is torrenting dangerous => Torrent files from unknown people often hide malware, and downloading paid games, movies or music this way is illegal in many places.
i found a usb stick on the ground | should i plug in a random usb => Don't plug it in. Unknown USB sticks can carry malware that runs when connected. Hand it to a lost and found or an adult.
someone wants to pay me to use my account | rent my account scam => Never let others use your accounts. It can get you banned and they may steal it or use it for scams in your name.
what is a fake giveaway on youtube | comment says i won => Comments or messages saying you won something and need to contact someone are scams. Real giveaways don't ask for payment, passwords or codes.
