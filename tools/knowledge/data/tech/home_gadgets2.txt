what are led light strips | govee led strip => LED strips are bendable tapes of lights you stick behind a desk, TV or bed. App-controlled ones can sync colors to music or your screen.
what is a smart lock | phone unlocks door => A smart lock lets you unlock a door with your phone, a code or a fingerprint, and can show who came and went.
what is a smart thermostat | nest thermostat => A smart thermostat learns your schedule and lets you change the heating or cooling from your phone, which can save energy.
what is a security camera for home | indoor camera privacy => Home security cameras stream video to your phone and alert you to motion. Keep indoor cameras out of private rooms and use strong passwords with 2FA.
what is a dash cam | car camera => A dash cam records the road from inside a car, which helps show what happened if there's an accident.
what are walkie talkies | how far do walkie talkies work => Walkie-talkies let people talk over radio without phones or internet. Most kid models reach a few hundred meters to a couple of kilometers depending on trees and buildings.
what is ham radio | amateur radio license => Ham radio is a hobby where licensed people talk by radio across towns or even the world. Many countries have beginner license exams, and kids can take them too.
what is a smart ring | oura ring => A smart ring is a ring with sensors that track sleep, heart rate and activity, like a tiny fitness tracker.
what is an always on display | aod phone => An always-on display shows the time and notifications on a dim lock screen while the phone is locked, using very little battery on OLED screens.
what is a soundbar subwoofer | do i need a subwoofer => A subwoofer is a speaker just for deep bass, like explosions and music beats. Many soundbars come with one, which makes movies and games feel bigger.
how do i set up surround sound | 5.1 speaker setup => For 5.1, put the center speaker under the TV, front left and right beside it, two surround speakers to the sides or slightly behind you and the subwoofer anywhere nearby.
how do i watch free tv with an antenna | digital antenna channels => Plug an indoor antenna into the TV and run a channel scan in its settings. It picks up free broadcast channels in many areas.
what is a tv wall mount vesa size | which tv mount fits => TVs list a VESA size like 300 by 300, the distance between the screw holes on the back. Pick a mount that supports that size and the TV's weight, and have an adult install it into studs.
what is a projector screen for | do i need a projector screen => A projector screen reflects light evenly for a brighter, sharper picture than a wall. A smooth white wall still works fine to start.
are blue light glasses worth it | do blue light glasses help => Research hasn't shown that blue light glasses prevent eye strain much. Breaks, good lighting and not sitting too close help more.
