# tag: tech.video.resolve2
how do i use the cut page in resolve | cut page basics => On the Cut page, drag clips from the Media Pool to the timeline, trim with the edges, and use the upper timeline to see the whole edit and the lower one to fine-tune. Smart Insert and Append buttons add clips fast.
what is source tape in resolve | cut page source tape => Source Tape on the Cut page plays every clip in the bin back to back like one long tape, so you can scrub through all your footage quickly and mark the parts to use.
how do i add a zoom transition in resolve | smooth zoom transition => Use the Zoom transition under Video Transitions, or keyframe Zoom on both clips around the cut in the Inspector for a custom zoom.
how do i make text appear letter by letter in resolve | typewriter effect resolve => Use a Text+ title and set the Write On parameters in the Inspector's Text tab, keyframing the end value from 0 to 1, or use a Fusion title template with a typewriter style.
how do i animate text sliding in resolve | text animation resolve => Keyframe the title's Position in the Inspector, or drag a Fusion Title template like Slide In from Effects, Titles onto the timeline.
how do i add a picture in picture in resolve | facecam overlay resolve => Put the facecam clip on a track above the gameplay, then in the Inspector lower Zoom and change Position to place it in a corner. Add Cropping to trim edges.
how do i crop a video in resolve | cropping inspector => Select the clip and open the Inspector, then use the Cropping sliders for left, right, top and bottom.
how do i flip a video in resolve | mirror clip resolve => Select the clip, open the Inspector, and click the Flip horizontal or Flip vertical buttons under Transform.
how do i reverse a clip in resolve | play video backwards => Right-click the clip, choose Change Clip Speed, and tick Reverse Speed.
how do i add a blur background for vertical video | blurred background shorts => Put a copy of the clip on a track below, zoom it to fill the frame and add a Gaussian Blur effect. The sharp copy above stays smaller in the middle.
how do i add sound effects in resolve | sfx resolve => Import the sound file into the Media Pool and drag it onto an audio track where it should play. Adjust its volume so it doesn't drown out your voice.
how do i duck music under my voice in resolve | auto ducking => On the Fairlight page, add a Ducker effect to the music track and set it to listen to your voice track, or simply lower the music volume where you speak with keyframes.
how do i record a voiceover in resolve | voice over davinci => On the Fairlight page, open Workspace, Show Panel, ADR, or make a track and set its input to your mic, arm it with R, and press record. Or record in another app and import the file.
how do i change audio volume keyframes in resolve | volume keyframes => Alt + click (Option + click on Mac) on the volume line of an audio clip to add points, then drag them up or down to change volume over time.
how do i sync audio and video in resolve | auto sync audio => Select the video and separate audio clips in the Media Pool, right-click and choose Auto Sync Audio, Based on Waveform. Resolve lines them up by the sound.
how do i use markers to edit to music | cut on the beat resolve => Play the music and press M on each beat to add markers, then line up your cuts to the markers with snapping on.
what is a multicam clip in resolve | multicam clip in resolve explained => A multicam clip groups footage from several cameras so you can switch angles while playing back, like a live director. Make one by selecting synced clips and choosing Create New Multicam Clip.
how do i add a vignette in resolve | darken edges davinci => On the Color page, add a node with a circular window, invert it, soften the edge and lower Gain or Offset. Or use the Vignette effect from the Effects library.
how do i make my footage look cinematic in resolve | cinematic look => Shoot at 24 fps with the 180 degree shutter rule, grade with a soft contrast curve, warm highlights and teal-ish shadows lightly, and consider a 2.39:1 crop with Output Blanking.
how do i add black bars in resolve | output blanking => Open Timeline, Output Blanking, and pick a ratio like 2.39 to add cinematic bars.
how do i match colors between two clips in resolve | shot match => On the Color page, select the clip to change, right-click the reference clip's thumbnail and choose Shot Match to This Clip. Fine-tune by hand after.
what are power windows in resolve | color a part of the image => Power windows are shapes on the Color page (circle, square, curve) that limit a correction to one area, like brightening a face.
what is the qualifier in resolve | change one color resolve => The qualifier picks pixels by hue, saturation and luminance, like selecting only blue sky, so you can change just that color.
how do i make a black and white video in resolve | grayscale footage davinci => On the Color page, lower Saturation to 0 in the primaries, or use the Color Wheels' Sat setting. Adjust contrast to keep it punchy.
what are curves in resolve | custom curves color => Curves change brightness and color by dragging a line. An S-shaped curve adds contrast. Hue vs Sat curves change saturation of specific colors.
how do i export a still image in resolve | grab still => On the Color page, right-click the viewer and choose Grab Still, then right-click it in the Gallery and Export. Or render a single frame from the Deliver page.
how do i render a transparent video from resolve | export with alpha channel => On the Deliver page, choose a format that supports alpha, like QuickTime with an alpha-capable codec, and tick Export Alpha. Only certain formats can carry transparency.
what is a fusion composition in resolve | fusion composition clip => A Fusion composition is an empty effects clip you can add from Effects. Open it on the Fusion page to build titles or effects from nodes.
how do i add a glow effect in resolve | glowing text davinci => Add the Glow effect from the OpenFX or Effects library to a clip or title, and tune its threshold and spread in the Inspector or the effects panel.
how do i add film grain in resolve | resolve film look => Resolve Studio has a Film Grain effect. In the free version, you can overlay a grain video clip on top with a blend mode like Overlay.
how do i fix a crooked video in resolve | rotate a clip => Select the clip and change Rotation Angle in the Inspector, then raise Zoom slightly so no edges show.
how do i speed up a long clip without editing | timelapse in resolve => Right-click the clip, choose Change Clip Speed and set a high percentage like 800 percent, or use Retime Controls for a custom speed.
how do i export for instagram reels from resolve | reels export => Use a 1080 x 1920 vertical timeline, export H.264 MP4 at 30 or 60 fps, and keep important text away from the bottom and right where buttons cover it.
how do i export a gif from resolve | resolve gif export => Resolve doesn't export GIFs in the Deliver presets. Export a short MP4, then convert it with a free tool like ShareX, ScreenToGif or an online converter.
why does my resolve export look washed out | gamma shift export => It's often a gamma tag mismatch between Resolve and the player. Many people set Output Color Space gamma tag to Rec.709-A on Mac exports, or simply check it on YouTube, which usually looks right.
why is my resolve render so slow | speed up render => Turn on hardware encoding in the Deliver settings, render from optimized media or the render cache, and avoid heavy noise reduction. A good GPU helps most.
how do i make a youtube thumbnail in resolve | thumbnail from a video frame => Find a frame, grab a still on the Color page and export it as PNG, then add text in a design tool. Resolve isn't a great thumbnail editor on its own.
how do i keep my resolve projects organized | bins in resolve => Make bins (folders) in the Media Pool for footage, audio, music, graphics and timelines. Smart bins can sort clips automatically by rules.
what is a timeline backup in resolve | recover lost resolve project => Turn on Project Backups in Preferences, User, Project Save and Load. Resolve then keeps backups you can restore from the Project Manager.
how do i delete unused clips in resolve | clean up media pool => In the Media Pool, right-click and choose Remove Unused Clips. It removes them from the project, not from your drive.
