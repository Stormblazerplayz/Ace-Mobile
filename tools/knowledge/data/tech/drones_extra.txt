# tag: tech.drones.extra
what is propwash | drone wobbles when dropping => Propwash is the shaky wobble when an FPV drone drops into its own dirty air from the props. Smoother flying lines, good PID tuning and RPM filtering reduce it.
what is horizon mode | fpv horizon vs angle mode => Horizon mode self-levels like angle mode near the center of the sticks but lets the drone flip when you push the sticks far.
what is camera uptilt on fpv | why is my fpv camera tilted up => The camera is tilted up so you still look forward when the drone leans to fly fast. Beginners use about 10 to 20 degrees, and faster pilots use more.
what is the difference between freestyle and racing drones | racing quad vs freestyle quad => Racing drones are as light and fast as possible for gates and lap times. Freestyle drones are tougher and often carry an action camera for tricks.
what does 5 inch drone mean | fpv frame size => The size is the propeller length the frame fits. A 5 inch drone uses 5 inch props and is the most common size for freestyle and racing.
what are walksnail and hdzero | digital fpv systems => Walksnail and HDZero are digital FPV video systems, like DJI's. HDZero has very low, steady delay, and Walksnail has a sharper picture.
what are dji geo zones | drone says no fly zone => GEO zones are areas DJI's app marks as restricted, like near airports. The drone may refuse to take off or fly there unless the area is allowed and unlocked.
why does my drone video have jello | wobbly lines in drone footage => Jello is wobble caused by vibrations reaching the camera. Check for bent or damaged props and loose parts, and use soft camera mounts.
what is battery sag on a drone | voltage drops when punching throttle => Sag is when the battery voltage dips under heavy throttle and recovers after. Big sag means an old or weak battery.
what is a 249 gram drone | sub 250g drone => Drones under 250 grams, like the DJI Mini series, face lighter rules in many countries. Check your local rules, since they still apply.
why does my fpv drone flip over on takeoff | quad flips when armed => Usually the props are on the wrong motors, a motor spins the wrong way or the flight controller orientation is wrong. Check motor direction in Betaflight.
