# tag: tech.cables
what is usb c vs usb a | different usb plugs => USB-A is the older flat rectangle plug. USB-C is the small oval plug that goes in either way up. The shape doesn't tell you the speed; that depends on the standard inside.
what is usb 3 vs usb 2 | blue usb port meaning => USB 2.0 tops out at 480 Mbps. USB 3 ports are much faster, starting at 5 Gbps, and are often blue or marked SS. Plug fast drives into USB 3 ports.
what is thunderbolt | thunderbolt vs usb c => Thunderbolt uses the USB-C plug but supports up to 40 Gbps or more, multiple displays and docks. Look for the lightning bolt symbol by the port.
what is usb4 | usb 4 speed => USB4 is a newer USB standard over USB-C, with speeds of 40 Gbps or more and support for displays and docks, based on Thunderbolt technology.
why is my usb c cable charging slowly | not all usb c cables are the same => Some USB-C cables only support slow charging or USB 2.0 data. For fast charging, use a cable rated for the wattage, like 60 W or 240 W, and a charger that supports it.
what is usb power delivery | usb pd charging => USB Power Delivery (PD) lets USB-C chargers and devices agree on higher power, from phones at about 20 W to laptops at 100 W or more.
what is a gan charger | small powerful charger => GaN (gallium nitride) chargers are smaller and run cooler than older chargers of the same wattage, so you can carry one charger for phone, iPad and laptop.
what wattage charger does my ipad need | best ipad charger => iPads charge fastest with a USB-C PD charger of about 20 W or more. A higher wattage charger is safe; the iPad only takes what it needs.
is it bad to use a higher watt charger | can a big charger damage my phone => No. With USB-C PD, the device asks for only the power it can handle. Cheap uncertified chargers are the real risk, so buy from known brands.
what is qi wireless charging | qi2 => Qi is the common wireless charging standard. Qi2 adds magnets to line up the phone, like Apple's MagSafe, for faster and more reliable charging.
what is magsafe | magnetic iphone charger => MagSafe is Apple's magnetic system for iPhones (12 and later) that snaps chargers, wallets and stands into place. Newer MacBooks have a separate MagSafe power plug.
can i take a power bank on a plane | power bank flight rules => Power banks must go in carry-on bags, not checked luggage. Many airlines allow up to 100 Wh without approval, but rules vary and some airlines limit use on board, so check your airline.
what is wh on a battery | mah vs wh => Wh (watt-hours) measures energy and lets you compare batteries with different voltages. Multiply mAh by volts and divide by 1000 to get Wh.
what hdmi cable do i need | hdmi 2.1 cable => For 4K 120 Hz or high refresh monitors over HDMI, use a certified Ultra High Speed HDMI cable. Older High Speed cables work for 1080p and 4K 60.
what displayport cable do i need | dp 1.4 vs 2.1 => DisplayPort 1.4 handles 1440p at very high refresh rates and 4K at 120 Hz or more with compression. Use a VESA certified cable; DP 2.1 cables support even higher bandwidth.
can i use hdmi to displayport adapter | hdmi to dp => Adapters work in one direction and can limit refresh rate. Connecting directly with a matching DisplayPort or HDMI cable is best for high refresh gaming.
what is an hdmi arc port | hdmi earc => ARC and eARC let a TV send sound back down the HDMI cable to a soundbar or receiver, so one cable carries video in and audio out.
what is a 3.5mm jack | headphone jack types => The 3.5 mm jack is the standard headphone plug. TRS plugs carry stereo sound; TRRS plugs (with an extra ring) add a microphone, used by headsets.
what is a headphone splitter | separate mic and headphone plugs => Some headsets have one 4-pole plug, but PCs may have separate pink (mic) and green (headphones) jacks. A splitter adapter connects them.
what color are the audio jacks on a pc | pink green blue jacks => Green is headphones or speakers, pink is microphone, and blue is line in. Gaming boards may have extra colors for surround speakers.
why is my extension cord or power strip important | surge protector => A surge protector shields your PC from voltage spikes. Plain power strips don't. Never chain power strips together or overload them with heaters.
what is a ups | battery backup for pc => A UPS is a battery backup that keeps a PC running for a few minutes in a power cut so you can save and shut down safely.
how long should a charger cable be | does cable length matter => For charging, shorter cables lose a bit less power, but 1 to 2 meter cables are fine. For high-speed data and video, long passive cables may not reach full speed.
what is a kvm switch | one keyboard two computers => A KVM switch lets one keyboard, mouse and monitor control two or more computers, switching with a button.
what is a usb hub | powered usb hub => A USB hub adds more USB ports. A powered hub has its own power adapter, which helps with drives, webcams and many devices at once.
what is a dock for a laptop | usb c dock => A dock connects to your laptop with one cable and gives you more ports, like monitors, Ethernet, USB and charging.
why does my laptop charge with some usb c chargers but not others | laptop won't charge from phone charger => The laptop may need more watts than the charger gives, or the charger or cable may not support USB Power Delivery. Check the laptop's required wattage.
