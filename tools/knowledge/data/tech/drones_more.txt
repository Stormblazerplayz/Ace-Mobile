# tag: tech.drones.camera
what are drone flight modes | cine normal sport mode => Most camera drones have Cine for slow smooth shots, Normal for everyday flying, and Sport for top speed. Obstacle sensing is often reduced or off in Sport mode.
what is obstacle avoidance on a drone | drone sensors avoid crash => Obstacle sensors let a drone see things around it and stop or steer around them. They don't catch thin branches or wires well, so don't rely on them completely.
what is activetrack | drone follow me mode => ActiveTrack and other follow modes make a camera drone follow a person or object automatically. Keep it in sight and in open areas, because it can still hit things.
what are quickshots | automatic drone shots => QuickShots are automatic flight moves like Dronie, Rocket, Circle and Helix that film cinematic clips with one tap.
what is the dji fly app | dji app => DJI Fly is the phone app for flying many DJI drones like the Mini and Air series. It shows the camera view, settings and flight data.
should i update my drone firmware | drone firmware update => Yes, updates fix bugs and safety problems. Update the drone, controller and batteries at home before a flight, never at the flying site with low battery.
how do i set return to home altitude | rth height => In the drone app's safety settings, set the return-to-home height above the tallest trees and buildings around you, and update the home point before each flight.
what happens if my drone loses signal | drone signal lost => Most GPS drones start return to home automatically. Keep calm, point the controller's antennas toward the drone, and don't fly behind buildings or trees that block signal.
what is drone interference | drone signal weak => Wi-Fi, power lines, metal structures and crowds of phones can weaken the drone's signal. Fly in open areas and keep a clear line of sight.
what is magnetic interference on a drone | drone compass interference => Metal like rebar, cars and steel bridges confuses the compass, which can cause wobbly or drifting flight. Take off away from metal.
what is visual line of sight | vlos drone rule => Visual line of sight means you can see the drone with your own eyes the whole flight. Most countries require it for hobby flying, so check your country's rules.
can i fly a drone at night | drone night flying => Rules for night flying vary by country, and many require lights you can see from far away. Check your country's rules first, and fly only somewhere you know well.
can i fly my drone over the ocean or a lake | drone over water => You can, but it's risky. Many drones' downward sensors get confused by water, and a crash means losing it. Keep the battery high and stay close.
do drones work in the cold | drone battery cold weather => Cold weather makes batteries drop faster. Keep batteries warm before flying, hover a minute to warm them up, and land earlier than usual.
can birds attack drones | drone and birds => Yes, birds like hawks and gulls sometimes attack drones near nests. Fly away from birds, and never chase wildlife.
is it okay to fly over my neighbor's house | drone privacy => Respect people's privacy. Don't film people or homes without permission, and check your local rules about flying over private property.
what is drone insurance | do i need drone insurance => Drone insurance covers damage to the drone or to other people's stuff. Some countries require it for certain flying, so check your country's rules.
what is a dji care refresh | drone damage plan => DJI Care Refresh is a paid plan that replaces a crashed or water-damaged DJI drone for a fee. It must be bought soon after activation.
should i use propeller guards | prop guards drone => Propeller guards protect props and people when learning indoors or near objects. They add weight and catch wind, so remove them for windy outdoor flights.
how do i install drone propellers correctly | props in props out => Each prop is marked to match a motor's spin direction, so check the markings or colors. Wrong props mean the drone flips on takeoff.
how do i calibrate the drone imu | imu calibration => Do it on a flat, level surface indoors when the app asks. Keep the drone still, follow each step, and don't calibrate unless the app or a problem needs it.
how do i calibrate my drone gimbal | gimbal tilted horizon => In the camera or gimbal settings, run gimbal auto calibration with the drone on a level surface. There's often a manual horizon adjustment too.
what camera settings should i use on a drone | drone photo settings => Keep ISO at 100, use an ND filter to keep shutter speed near double the frame rate for video, and shoot RAW photos if the drone supports it.
how do i take a drone panorama | drone pano => Use the drone's Pano mode, like Sphere, 180 or Wide, and it takes and stitches the photos for you. Hold still while it shoots.
how do i edit drone footage in resolve | drone video editing => Import the clips, cut the best parts, stabilize if needed, then on the Color page convert D-Log with a LUT or color space transform and add contrast.
# tag: tech.drones.fpv2
what is an xt60 connector | drone battery plug => XT60 is the common yellow battery plug on 5 inch FPV drones. Smaller drones often use XT30 or BT2.0 connectors.
how do i solder fpv parts | soldering fpv => Use a good temperature-controlled iron, flux and tinned wires and pads. Always work with an adult, ventilation and eye protection, and unplug batteries first.
what is a smoke stopper | first power up fpv => A smoke stopper limits current the first time you plug in a newly built drone, so a short circuit doesn't fry parts.
what is binding a receiver | bind elrs receiver => Binding pairs your radio with the drone's receiver so they only listen to each other. ExpressLRS usually binds with a matching bind phrase.
what is failsafe on an fpv drone | failsafe betaflight => Failsafe is what the drone does when it loses the radio signal, usually dropping or disarming so it doesn't fly away. Test it with props off.
what is an arm switch | arming a drone => The arm switch lets the motors spin. Keep it on a switch you won't bump, and always disarm right after landing or crashing.
what is a drone beeper | find crashed drone => A beeper makes a loud sound so you can find the drone after a crash in long grass. You can turn it on with a radio switch.
what is gps rescue | betaflight gps rescue => GPS Rescue is a Betaflight feature that flies an FPV drone back toward home if the signal is lost, using a GPS module. It needs careful setup and testing.
what is osd in fpv | on screen display => The OSD puts flight info like battery voltage, timer and signal strength on your goggles' screen.
what is dvr on fpv goggles | record fpv goggles => DVR records the video you see in the goggles, which helps find a crashed drone and review flights. Digital systems record high-quality footage too.
what are vtx channels and bands | fpv channels => VTX bands and channels pick the frequency your video uses. Each pilot flying together needs a different channel so they don't interfere.
what are pids on a drone | betaflight pid tuning => PIDs control how hard the flight controller corrects the drone's movement. Default Betaflight PIDs fly well on most builds, so change them slowly if at all.
what is the rpm filter | bidirectional dshot => The RPM filter uses motor speed data from the ESC to remove motor noise from the gyro, making the drone fly smoother. It needs bidirectional DShot turned on.
how do i fix an fpv drone after a crash | fpv crash repair => Check props, arms, motors and wires. Replace bent props first, spin motors by hand to feel for grit, and test hover gently before flying hard.
what spare parts should i carry for fpv | fpv field kit => Carry extra props, a prop tool or wrench, spare screws, zip ties, tape, a battery checker and a small hex driver set.
what is a battery checker | lipo voltage checker => A small battery checker shows each cell's voltage, so you can spot damaged cells and know when a pack is at storage voltage.
how many batteries should i have for fpv | fpv battery count => Most pilots bring four to six packs, since each flight lasts only a few minutes. More packs mean more practice per trip.
