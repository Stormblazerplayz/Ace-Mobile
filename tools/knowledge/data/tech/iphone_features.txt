# tag: tech.iphone.features
how do i customize control center on iphone | add buttons to control center => Swipe down from the top right, tap the plus in the top left, then add, remove, resize or move controls. You can also make extra pages of controls.
what is the camera control button | iphone 16 camera button => Camera Control is the button on the side of iPhone 16 and later models. Click it to open the camera, then light-press and slide to change zoom or exposure.
what is visual look up | identify a plant from a photo => Open a photo and tap the info button with a sparkle. If it finds something, it can tell you the plant, animal, landmark or artwork.
how do i identify a song on iphone | shazam iphone => Add the Music Recognition button to Control Center and tap it while the song plays. You can also ask Siri what song is playing.
what is reachability on iphone | reach the top of the screen with one hand => Reachability pulls the top of the screen down. Swipe down on the bottom edge of the screen after turning it on in Accessibility > Touch.
how do i undo typing on iphone | shake to undo => Swipe left with three fingers to undo, or right to redo. You can also shake the phone if Shake to Undo is turned on.
what is the accessibility shortcut | triple click side button => Triple-clicking the side button can turn on an accessibility feature of your choice, like Magnifier or Guided Access. Set it in Accessibility > Accessibility Shortcut.
what are background sounds on iphone | rain noise iphone => Background Sounds plays calming noise like rain or ocean to help you focus or relax. Find it in Accessibility > Audio & Visual, or add it to Control Center.
what is vehicle motion cues | iphone car sickness dots => Vehicle Motion Cues shows moving dots on the screen edges that match a car's motion, which can reduce motion sickness when using your phone as a passenger.
how do i use math notes on iphone | calculator math notes => In Calculator, tap the calculator icon and choose Math Notes, or open Notes. Type an equation ending in an equals sign and it solves it for you.
how do i convert units in the iphone calculator | calculator unit conversion => Tap the calculator icon in the bottom left, turn on Convert, then pick the units. It handles things like length, weight, temperature and currency.
how do i delete one digit in the iphone calculator | calculator backspace => Swipe left or right across the number at the top to delete the last digit. Newer versions also show a delete button.
how do i find my iphone's lost mode | mark iphone as lost => In the Find My app or on icloud.com/find, choose your device and turn on Mark As Lost. It locks the phone and can show a message with a contact number.
how do i put apps anywhere on the iphone home screen | leave gaps between apps => Newer iOS versions let you drag apps into any spot on the Home Screen, leaving empty gaps, instead of always filling from the top.
how do i clear all notifications on iphone | delete every notification => Swipe up on the Lock Screen to see Notification Center, then tap the X at the top and tap Clear.
what is notification summary | group notifications at a set time => Scheduled Summary collects less urgent notifications and delivers them at times you pick. Set it in Settings > Notifications.
how do i type to siri | talk to siri without voice => Turn on Type to Siri in Settings > Accessibility > Siri. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence you can double-tap the bottom of the screen to type.
what is a focus filter | hide work stuff in focus => Focus filters change what apps show during a Focus, like showing only some calendars or mail accounts. Add them at the bottom of each Focus setting.
how do i merge duplicate photos | duplicates album iphone => Photos finds duplicate pictures and puts them in a Duplicates album. Tap Merge to keep the best version.
how do i make a memoji | create memoji => In Messages, tap the plus, choose Stickers, then tap the Memoji button to make a cartoon version of yourself.
how do i make text bold in imessage | text effects imessage => Select the text, tap the text formatting button, then choose bold, italic, underline or animated effects like shake or explode.
what is rcs on iphone | iphone texting android better => RCS is a newer texting standard. iPhones with iOS 18 or later use it with Android phones, adding typing indicators, read receipts and better photo quality.
can android users join facetime | facetime link for android => Yes. Create a FaceTime link and send it, and Android or Windows users can join from a web browser.
what is clean up in photos | remove objects iphone photo => Clean Up removes distracting objects from photos. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.
can my iphone charge my airpods | reverse charging iphone usb c => iPhones with USB-C can charge AirPods or an Apple Watch through a USB-C cable. They don't have wireless reverse charging.
how fast is iphone usb c transfer | iphone 15 usb speed => Standard iPhone 15 and 16 models transfer at USB 2 speeds. Pro models reach USB 3 speeds with a USB 3 cable.
when did iphone switch to usb c | does my iphone use lightning => iPhone 15 and later use USB-C. iPhone 14 and earlier use Lightning.
how do i set an iphone charging limit | charge to 80 percent iphone => On iPhone 15 and later, go to Settings > Battery > Charging and pick a limit like 80 percent. Older iPhones use Optimized Battery Charging instead.
is my iphone waterproof | can i swim with my iphone => iPhones are water resistant, not waterproof, and the resistance wears off over time. Keep them out of pools and the sea, and water damage isn't covered by warranty.
what does liquid detected in the port mean | iphone liquid detected => Your iPhone found water in the charging port. Unplug it, tap the phone gently with the port down and let it dry before charging again.
why does my iphone say it needs to cool down | iphone too hot warning => The iPhone got too hot and is limiting features to protect itself. Move it out of the sun, stop charging and let it cool.
what is dual sim | two numbers one phone => Dual SIM lets one phone use two phone numbers or plans at once, often one physical SIM and one eSIM, or two eSIMs.
what is a carrier locked phone | unlocked phone meaning => A locked phone only works with one carrier's SIM. An unlocked phone works with any compatible carrier.
how do i turn on private browsing lock in safari | lock private tabs => Safari locks Private Browsing tabs when you leave them. Turn on Require Face ID to Unlock Private Browsing in Safari settings.
