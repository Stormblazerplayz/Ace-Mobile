# tag: tech.apps.services
what is reddit | how does reddit work => Reddit is a site made of communities called subreddits, each about one topic. People post, comment and upvote, and you need to be at least 13 to have an account.
what is pinterest | pinterest app => Pinterest is a visual app where people save pictures and ideas to boards, like art references, recipes or room ideas.
what is whatsapp | whatsapp app => WhatsApp is a free messaging and calling app that uses the internet, with end-to-end encryption. You need to be at least 13 in most countries.
what is the signal app | signal messenger => Signal is a free messaging app focused on privacy. Messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted and it collects very little data.
what is telegram | telegram app => Telegram is a messaging app with chats, big group channels and bots. Regular chats aren't end-to-end encrypted unless you use Secret Chats.
what is bluesky | bluesky social => Bluesky is a social network similar to X, where people post short messages and follow others, built on an open system.
what is x formerly twitter | is twitter called x => Twitter was renamed X in 2023. It's a social network for short posts, news and replies.
what is tiktok | how does the tiktok app work => TikTok is an app for short videos, with a For You feed picked by an algorithm. You need to be 13, and teen accounts get extra limits like a default daily screen time reminder.
what are instagram teen accounts | instagram for under 16 => Instagram teen accounts are private by default and limit who can message you. Under-16s need a parent's okay to loosen those settings.
what is ghost mode on snapchat | hide location snap map => Ghost Mode hides your location on the Snap Map so friends can't see where you are. It's a good idea to keep it on.
what is bereal | bereal app => BeReal is a photo app that asks everyone to post a picture at the same random time each day, using the front and back cameras at once.
what is duolingo | learn languages app => Duolingo is a free app with short lessons for learning languages, plus math and music. Daily streaks keep you practicing.
what is khan academy | free lessons online => Khan Academy is a free nonprofit site with videos and practice for math, science, coding and more, for all grade levels.
what is spotify free vs premium | spotify premium worth it => Spotify Free plays music with ads and limits skipping on phones. Premium removes ads, allows downloads for offline listening and lets you play any song.
what is apple music | apple music vs spotify => Apple Music is Apple's paid music streaming service with a big song library, lyrics and lossless audio. It works on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android.
what is soundcloud | upload my music online => SoundCloud is a site where anyone can upload songs and beats, popular with new artists sharing their music for free.
what is bandcamp | buy music from artists => Bandcamp is a store where you can buy music and merch straight from artists, who keep most of the money.
what is wattpad | read stories online => Wattpad is an app for reading and writing stories, where writers post chapters and readers comment.
what is webtoon | read comics on phone => Webtoon is an app for reading comics made to scroll down on a phone. Many series are free with new episodes each week.
what is crunchyroll | where to watch anime => Crunchyroll is a streaming service focused on anime, with some free shows and more with a subscription.
what is plex | plex media server => Plex lets you turn a computer into a home media server, so you can stream your own movies and music to your phone or TV.
what is jellyfin | free media server => Jellyfin is a free, open-source media server similar to Plex, for streaming your own videos at home.
what is midjourney | midjourney ai => Midjourney is a paid AI image generator that makes pictures from text prompts.
what is stable diffusion | stable diffusion ai => Stable Diffusion is an AI image model you can run on your own PC with a good graphics card, using free apps.
what is perplexity ai | ai search engine => Perplexity is an AI search tool that answers questions and shows links to its sources, so you can check what it says.
what is patreon | support creators monthly => Patreon lets fans pay creators a monthly amount for extra content like early videos or behind-the-scenes posts.
what is ko fi | buy me a coffee => Ko-fi lets people send small one-time tips to creators, often shown as buying them a coffee.
what is linktree | link in bio => Linktree makes one page that holds all your links, so you can put a single link in your profile bio.
what is ebay | how does ebay work => eBay is a site where people and shops sell new and used things, by auction or a fixed price. Check seller ratings before buying.
what is google earth | explore the world in 3d => Google Earth shows the whole planet in 3D from satellite photos. You can fly around cities, mountains and even look at old images.
what is google keep | google notes app => Google Keep is a free notes app for quick notes, checklists and reminders that sync to your Google account.
what is trello | trello boards => Trello organizes projects with boards, lists and cards you drag from To Do to Done, which is handy for planning a game or video.
what is todoist | to do list app => Todoist is a to-do list app where you add tasks with due dates and projects, and it reminds you on all your devices.
what is life360 | family location app => Life360 is a family app that shares members' locations with each other. Talk with your family about when location sharing is used.
what is waze | waze vs google maps => Waze is a driving navigation app where drivers report traffic, crashes and speed cameras to help others find faster routes.
what is a fitbit | fitness tracker band => A Fitbit is a wristband or watch from Google that tracks steps, heart rate and sleep and shows them in its app.
what is a garmin watch | garmin gps watch => Garmin makes sports watches with built-in GPS for running, cycling and hiking, known for long battery life.
what is sonos | sonos speakers => Sonos makes Wi-Fi speakers and soundbars that play music in several rooms at once, controlled from an app.
what is a ring doorbell | video doorbell => A video doorbell has a camera so you can see and talk to whoever is at the door from your phone.
what is a tile tracker | samsung smarttag => Tile and Samsung SmartTag are small trackers, like AirTags, that you attach to keys or bags to find them with a phone app.
what is kick streaming | kick vs twitch => Kick is a live streaming site similar to Twitch, where creators stream games and chat with viewers.
