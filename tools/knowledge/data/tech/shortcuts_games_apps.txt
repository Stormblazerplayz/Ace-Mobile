# tag: tech.shortcuts.games
what does f3 do in minecraft | minecraft debug screen => F3 opens the debug screen in Minecraft Java Edition, showing coordinates, FPS, the biome, the direction you face and more.
how do i see chunk borders in minecraft | f3 g => Press F3 + G in Minecraft Java Edition to show chunk borders as lines around you.
how do i see hitboxes in minecraft | f3 b => Press F3 + B in Minecraft Java Edition to show hitboxes around mobs, items and players.
how do i reload resource packs in minecraft | f3 t => Press F3 + T in Minecraft Java Edition to reload textures, sounds and resource packs without restarting.
how do i reload chunks in minecraft | f3 a => Press F3 + A in Minecraft Java Edition to reload all chunks, which can fix invisible blocks.
how do i see advanced tooltips in minecraft | f3 h => Press F3 + H in Minecraft Java Edition to show item IDs and durability in tooltips.
how do i hide the hud in minecraft | f1 minecraft => Press F1 in Minecraft Java Edition to hide the hotbar, hand and crosshair for clean screenshots.
f2 minecraft | where do minecraft screenshots save => Press F2 in Minecraft Java Edition. Screenshots save in the .minecraft folder, inside screenshots.
how do i change camera view in minecraft | f5 minecraft => Press F5 in Minecraft Java Edition to switch between first person, third person behind, and third person front.
how do i see all f3 shortcuts in minecraft | f3 q => Press F3 + Q in Minecraft Java Edition to list all the F3 key combos in chat.
how do i clear chat in minecraft | f3 d => Press F3 + D in Minecraft Java Edition to clear the chat history on your screen.
how do i switch to spectator quickly in minecraft | f3 n => Press F3 + N in Minecraft Java Edition to switch between your last game mode and Spectator, if cheats are allowed.
how do i open the gamemode switcher in minecraft | f3 f4 => Press F3 + F4 in Minecraft Java Edition to open the game mode switcher, then keep tapping F4 to pick a mode. Cheats must be on.
how do i stop minecraft pausing when i alt tab | f3 p => Press F3 + P in Minecraft Java Edition to toggle pausing when the window loses focus.
how do i copy my coordinates in minecraft | f3 c => Press F3 + C in Minecraft Java Edition to copy a teleport command with your location and facing direction to the clipboard. Holding F3 + C for 10 seconds forces a debug crash, so tap it.
how do i open the nvidia overlay | alt z nvidia => Press Alt + Z to open the NVIDIA App overlay for recording, screenshots, filters and performance stats.
how do i save an nvidia instant replay | alt f10 => Press Alt + F10 to save the last few minutes when NVIDIA Instant Replay is on. Alt + F9 starts or stops a manual recording.
how do i take an nvidia screenshot | alt f1 nvidia => Press Alt + F1 to take a screenshot with the NVIDIA App overlay. They save to your Videos folder by default.
how do i open the amd overlay | alt r amd => Press Alt + R to open the AMD Software overlay in game. Ctrl + Shift + O shows or hides the performance metrics.
how do i open the steam big picture mode | big picture steam => Click View, then Big Picture Mode in Steam, or press the Guide button on a connected controller.
# tag: tech.shortcuts.apps
how do i undo in procreate | two finger tap procreate => Tap the canvas with two fingers to undo in Procreate, and three fingers to redo. Hold two fingers down to undo quickly many steps.
how do i make a perfect shape in procreate | quickshape => Draw a line or shape and keep your Pencil held down at the end. QuickShape snaps it into a clean line, circle or shape. Tap Edit Shape at the top to adjust it.
how do i fill a shape with color in procreate | color drop procreate => Drag the color circle from the top right and drop it inside a closed shape to fill it. Keep holding and slide left or right to change the threshold.
how do i pick a color from my drawing in procreate | eyedropper procreate => Touch and hold the canvas with one finger to open the eyedropper, or tap the square button on the sidebar, then let go on the color you want.
how do i copy and paste in procreate | three finger swipe procreate => Swipe down with three fingers on the canvas to open the Copy and Paste menu.
how do i clear a layer in procreate | scrub to clear layer => Scrub the canvas back and forth with three fingers to clear the active layer.
how do i hide the procreate interface | four finger tap procreate => Tap the canvas with four fingers to hide the interface for full screen drawing. Tap again to bring it back.
how do i fit the canvas to the screen in procreate | quick pinch procreate => Pinch the canvas quickly with two fingers to snap it back to fit the screen.
what is alpha lock in procreate | color inside the lines procreate => Swipe right with two fingers on a layer to turn on Alpha Lock, so you only paint where the layer already has pixels.
what is a clipping mask in procreate | shade without going outside procreate => Tap a layer above another and choose Clipping Mask. It only shows where the layer below has content, great for shading without changing the base.
how do i open the brush size quickly in procreate | procreate sidebar sliders => Use the left sidebar sliders: the top changes size and the bottom changes opacity. Hold the slider and drag sideways for finer control.
how do i use streamline in procreate | smooth lines procreate => Tap the brush, open Stroke Properties and raise StreamLine. It smooths shaky lines, great for lettering and clean line art.
how do i record a timelapse in procreate | procreate timelapse export => Procreate records your drawing automatically. Open Actions (wrench), Video, Time-lapse Replay or Export Time-lapse Video.
how do i undo in krita | krita shortcuts => Krita uses Ctrl + Z to undo, B for the brush, E for the eraser toggle, Ctrl + T to transform, and Space to pan. Hold Shift and drag to change brush size.
how do i use the spot healing in photoshop | photoshop basics shortcuts => Photoshop uses B for brush, E for eraser, V for move, Ctrl + T for Free Transform, and J for the Spot Healing Brush. Ctrl + Z undoes, with more history on repeat.
how do i cut audio in audacity | audacity basics => Drag across the waveform to select a part, then press Delete or Ctrl + X to cut it. Space plays and stops, and Ctrl + D duplicates the selection.
how do i remove noise in audacity | audacity noise reduction => Select a few seconds of only background noise, choose Effect, Noise Removal and Repair, Noise Reduction, and click Get Noise Profile. Then select the whole clip and apply it with moderate settings.
how do i mute in zoom | zoom shortcuts => Press Alt + A to mute and unmute in Zoom on Windows, and Alt + V to start or stop video. Hold Space for push to talk while muted.
how do i mute in teams | teams shortcuts => Press Ctrl + Shift + M to mute or unmute in Microsoft Teams, and Ctrl + Shift + O to turn the camera on or off.
how do i mute in google meet | google meet shortcuts => Press Ctrl + D to mute or unmute and Ctrl + E to turn the camera on or off in Google Meet. On Mac use Command.
how do i skip songs with the keyboard in spotify | spotify shortcuts => In the Spotify desktop app, Space plays and pauses, Ctrl + Right Arrow skips forward and Ctrl + Left Arrow goes back. Many keyboards' media keys also work.
how do i use media keys on my keyboard | play pause key => Play/Pause, Next and Previous media keys control Spotify, YouTube in browsers and most players, even when they're not in front. Some keyboards need Fn held.
