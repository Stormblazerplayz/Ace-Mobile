how do cell towers work | how does my phone connect to the network => Your phone talks by radio to the nearest cell tower, which connects to the carrier's network by cable. As you move, your phone hands off to the next tower.
how does texting work | how are sms messages sent => A normal text, or SMS, travels over the cell network through your carrier to the other person's carrier. iMessage and RCS send messages over the internet instead.
what is phone roaming | roaming charges abroad => Roaming means using another carrier's network, usually in another country. It can cost extra, so check your plan or use Wi-Fi before traveling.
what is volte | voice over lte => VoLTE carries phone calls over the 4G network, giving clearer calls and letting you use data during a call.
what is mmwave 5g | 5g mmwave vs sub 6 => mmWave 5G uses very high frequencies for huge speeds over short distances, often only outdoors in busy areas. Most 5G uses lower frequencies that reach farther.
how does a microphone work | how do mics pick up sound => Sound waves move a thin part inside the mic called a diaphragm. That movement is turned into an electrical signal that matches the sound.
how does a smart speaker hear its wake word | is alexa always listening => The speaker listens on the device for its wake word, like Alexa. It only starts sending audio to the cloud after it hears that word.
what is imap vs pop | email account settings imap => IMAP keeps your email on the server, so it syncs across all your devices. POP downloads mail to one device. Use IMAP for almost everything.
what is smtp | outgoing mail server => SMTP is the system used to send email from your app to mail servers and on to the other person's mailbox.
how do spam filters work | why did my email go to spam => Spam filters look at the sender's reputation, links and wording and whether the email is properly signed, then send suspicious mail to the spam folder.
how do authenticator app codes work | why do 2fa codes change every 30 seconds => Your phone and the website share a secret when you set it up. Both use that secret and the clock to make the same six-digit code, which changes every 30 seconds.
how do search engines find websites | what is a web crawler => Search engines use programs called crawlers that follow links across the web, read pages and store them in a giant index to search later.
how does search autocomplete work | why does google finish my search => Autocomplete suggests searches based on what many people search for, what's trending and sometimes your own past searches.
how does video streaming change quality | what is adaptive bitrate => Streaming apps keep several quality versions of a video and switch between them as your internet speeds up or slows down, so it keeps playing.
how do video calls work | how does facetime send video => Your device compresses your camera and mic into small packets and sends them over the internet, and the other device decodes them almost instantly.
how do online games stay in sync | client and server in games => In most online games, a server keeps the true game state. Each player's game sends inputs and predicts what happens, then the server corrects any differences.
how does a projector make a picture | how do projectors work => A projector shines a bright light through or off a tiny image chip and uses a lens to make the picture big on a wall or screen.
how does a touchscreen know where i tap | how do phone screens feel fingers => Phone screens have a grid of tiny wires that hold an electric field. Your finger changes the field at that spot, and the phone measures where.
how does noise cancelling block sound | how does anc work => Microphones on the headphones hear outside noise, and the headphones play the opposite sound wave, which cancels much of the noise out.
how does a usb cable carry power and data | wires inside a usb cable => A USB cable has separate wires for power and for data. USB-C cables can carry more power and faster data depending on the cable's rating.
how does a phone camera blur the background | how does portrait mode work => Portrait mode uses two cameras or depth sensing to figure out how far things are, then blurs the background in software.
