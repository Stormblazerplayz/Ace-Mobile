# tag: tech.mac.more
how do i make a new desktop space on mac | add space mission control => Open Mission Control with Control + Up Arrow, then click the plus button at the top right to add a desktop.
what is stage manager on mac | mac stage manager => Stage Manager keeps your current app in the middle and lines up other recent apps on the left. Turn it on from Control Center.
how do i use hot corners on mac | mac hot corners => In System Settings, Desktop and Dock, click Hot Corners and pick an action for each corner, like Mission Control or Lock Screen.
how do i change the default browser on mac | set chrome default mac => Open System Settings, Desktop and Dock, and choose a browser from Default web browser.
how do i make a mac app open at login | login items mac => Open System Settings, General, Login Items and click the plus to add an app, or remove apps you don't want starting.
how do i check mac storage | what's using space on my mac => Open System Settings, General, Storage to see what's using space and get tips to free it up.
how do i use preview to edit images on mac | preview app tricks => Preview can crop, resize, rotate, mark up and convert images. Open the Markup toolbar and use Tools, Adjust Size to resize.
how do i convert an image to jpg on mac | png to jpeg mac => Open it in Preview, choose File, Export, and pick JPEG as the format.
how do i merge pdfs on mac | combine pdf preview => Open one PDF in Preview, show the thumbnails sidebar, and drag the other PDF's pages into it. Then save.
how do i make a pdf from images on mac | photos into one pdf mac => Select the images in Finder, right-click and choose Quick Actions, Create PDF.
how do i use quick actions in finder | quick actions mac => Right-click files in Finder and choose Quick Actions to rotate images, create a PDF, convert images or trim videos without opening an app.
how do i zip a file on mac | compress on mac => Right-click the file or folder in Finder and choose Compress. Double-click a zip to unzip it.
how do i format a usb drive on mac | disk utility erase => Open Disk Utility, select the drive, click Erase and choose ExFAT to work with Windows and Mac.
what is disk utility on mac | disk utility on mac explained => Disk Utility manages drives: erasing, formatting, checking for errors with First Aid, and making partitions.
how do i see my mac's battery health | mac battery condition => Open System Settings, Battery and click the info button next to Battery Health to see the condition and maximum capacity.
what is optimized charging on macbook | mac charge limit => Optimized Battery Charging learns your routine and waits at 80 percent until you need it, to slow battery aging.
how do i use airplay to a tv from mac | mirror mac to tv => Click Screen Mirroring in Control Center and pick your Apple TV or AirPlay TV.
how do i use handoff | continue on mac from iphone => With Handoff, an app you're using on iPhone or iPad shows up in the Mac's Dock so you can keep going there. Both need the same Apple Account with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on.
what is universal clipboard | copy on iphone paste on mac => Universal Clipboard lets you copy on one Apple device and paste on another nearby, using the same Apple Account with Handoff on.
what is iphone mirroring on mac | control iphone from mac => iPhone Mirroring in macOS Sequoia and later shows your iPhone's screen on the Mac so you can use its apps with the keyboard and trackpad.
how do i change the trackpad speed on mac | mac pointer speed => Open System Settings, Trackpad and drag the Tracking speed slider.
how do i see the date and time with seconds on mac | mac clock seconds => Open System Settings, Control Center, Clock Options and turn on Display the time with seconds.
how do i reset nvram on a mac | pram reset => Apple silicon Macs reset it automatically if needed, so there's nothing to do. On older Intel Macs, hold Option + Command + P + R while starting up.
what is recovery mode on mac | mac recovery => Recovery lets you reinstall macOS, use Disk Utility or restore from Time Machine. On Apple silicon, hold the power button at startup until options appear.
what is safe mode on a mac | safe mode on a mac explained => Safe mode starts the Mac with only essential software to help find problems. On Apple silicon, hold the power button, pick the disk, then hold Shift and click Continue in Safe Mode.
how do i let apps record my screen on mac | screen recording permission => Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Screen and System Audio Recording, and turn it on for the app, like OBS or Discord.
what does the green dot or orange dot mean on mac | camera and mic indicator => A green light by the camera means it's on, and an orange dot in the menu bar means the mic is being used.
how do i find my files on mac quickly | spotlight search tips => Press Command + Space and type part of the file name, or search for kinds like png or pdf.
how do i use dictation on mac | talk to type mac => Press the microphone key or Fn twice, then speak. Turn on Dictation in System Settings, Keyboard first.
can i use davinci resolve on mac | resolve on apple silicon => Yes, DaVinci Resolve runs natively on Apple silicon Macs and works very well on them.
how do i install blender on mac | blender apple silicon download => Download it from blender.org, open the dmg, and drag Blender into Applications. Pick the Apple silicon version for M-series Macs.
# tag: tech.iphone.more
how do i change my iphone ringtone | iphone ringtone settings => Open Settings, Sounds and Haptics, Ringtone, and pick one. Custom ringtones can be made in GarageBand.
how do i share wifi password from iphone | share wifi to friend => When a nearby friend's Apple device tries to join your Wi-Fi, your iPhone shows a Share Password prompt. You need each other in Contacts.
how do i see my saved wifi passwords on iphone | passwords app wifi => Open the Passwords app and tap Wi-Fi to see networks and their passwords after unlocking with Face ID.
how do i set up a verification code in passwords app | iphone 2fa codes => In the Passwords app, open an account, tap Edit, then Set Up Verification Code to use the iPhone as an authenticator.
how do i hide my email when signing up | hide my email => With iCloud+, Sign in with Apple or Hide My Email creates a random address that forwards to your real email.
how do i send a message later on iphone | schedule imessage => In iOS 18 and later, tap the plus in Messages, choose Send Later, and pick a time.
how do i react to a message on iphone | tapback => Touch and hold a message and choose a Tapback like a heart or thumbs up. In iOS 18 and later you can also react with any emoji.
how do i pin a conversation in messages | pin chat iphone => Swipe right on the conversation, or touch and hold it and choose Pin.
how do i turn off message previews on the lock screen | hide notification text lock screen => Open Settings, Notifications, Show Previews and choose When Unlocked or Never.
what is check in on iphone | messages check in => Check In in Messages tells a friend or family member when you arrive safely, and shares info if you don't.
how do i use emergency sos via satellite | satellite sos iphone => On supported iPhones without cell or Wi-Fi coverage, call your emergency number and the iPhone offers to text emergency services through satellite. It shows you how to point the phone.
how do i turn on crash detection | iphone crash detection => Crash Detection is on by default on iPhone 14 and later. It's in Settings, Emergency SOS, Call After Severe Crash.
how do i make my iphone vibrate only | vibrate no ring iphone => Flip the Ring/Silent switch or use the Action button to turn on Silent mode, and make sure Haptics is on in Settings, Sounds and Haptics.
how do i turn off haptic feedback on iphone | stop iphone vibrating on taps => Open Settings, Sounds and Haptics, and set Haptics to Never Play or turn off System Haptics.
how do i clean my iphone charging port | lightning port dirty => Turn the phone off and gently remove lint with a wooden toothpick. Never use metal or liquids.
how do i check if my iphone is charging fast | iphone fast charge => Use a 20 W or higher USB-C Power Delivery charger with a USB-C cable. Fast charging gets most iPhones to about 50 percent in around 30 minutes.
how do i reduce iphone data usage | low data mode => Turn on Low Data Mode in Settings, Cellular, Cellular Data Options, and turn off cellular data for apps that don't need it.
how do i see how much data i've used on iphone | iphone data usage per app => Open Settings, Cellular, and scroll down to see how much data each app used since the last reset. Reset the statistics at the start of each billing month.
what is wi fi assist | wifi assist iphone => Wi-Fi Assist uses cellular data when Wi-Fi is weak. Turn it off at the bottom of Settings, Cellular if you're worried about data.
what is the iphone app privacy report | app privacy report => It shows which apps used your location, camera, mic and contacts, and which websites they contacted. Turn it on in Settings, Privacy and Security.
how do i edit a video on iphone | trim video iphone => Open the video in Photos, tap Edit, then drag the ends of the timeline to trim. Tap Done and choose to save as a new clip.
how do i mute a video's sound on iphone | remove audio from video iphone => In Photos, tap Edit on the video, then tap the speaker icon at the top so it's crossed out.
how do i make a contact poster | contact poster iphone => Open Contacts, tap My Card, then Contact Photo and Poster to design how you appear on others' iPhones when you call.
# tag: tech.android.more
how do i split screen on android | multi window android => Open recent apps, tap an app's icon at the top of its card and choose Split screen or Open in split screen view, then pick a second app.
how do i turn on dark mode on android | android dark theme => Open Settings, Display and turn on Dark theme, or use the Quick Settings tile.
how do i customize quick settings on android | edit quick tiles => Swipe down twice from the top, tap the pencil or edit button, and drag tiles in or out.
how do i set screen time limits on android | app timers android => In Settings, Digital Wellbeing and parental controls, tap Dashboard and set an app timer for each app.
how do i turn on battery saver on android | android power saving mode => Open Settings, Battery and turn on Battery Saver, or use its Quick Settings tile.
how do i share wifi from android with a qr code | android wifi qr => Open Settings, Network, Internet, tap the gear by your Wi-Fi, then Share. A QR code appears for the other phone to scan.
how do i cast my android screen to a tv | android cast screen => Swipe down for Quick Settings and tap Screen Cast or Smart View on Samsung, then choose a Chromecast or smart TV.
how do i change the keyboard on android | gboard settings => Open Settings, System, Keyboard, and choose a keyboard. Gboard's settings let you change themes, sounds and autocorrect.
how do i block a number on android | stop calls android => In the Phone app, open Recents, tap the number, and choose Block or Report spam.
what is circle to search | circle to search android => On many newer Android phones, long-press the home button or navigation bar, then circle anything on the screen to search Google for it.
how do i turn on usb debugging | adb android => Turn on Developer options first, then open it and switch on USB debugging. Only turn it on when you need it for development.
what is adb | android debug bridge => ADB is a command-line tool for talking to an Android device from a computer, used to install apps, read logs and test apps you make.
what is a launcher on android | change home screen android => A launcher is the home screen app. You can install a different one from Google Play to change icons, gestures and layout.
