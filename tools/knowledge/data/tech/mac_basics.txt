# tag: tech.mac
how do i install apps on a mac | install from dmg => Download from the Mac App Store or the developer's site. For a .dmg file, open it and drag the app into the Applications folder, then eject the disk image.
how do i uninstall an app on mac | delete mac app => Drag the app from Applications to the Trash and empty it. Apps from the App Store can be deleted from Launchpad by holding Option and clicking the X.
how do i open system settings on mac | mac settings => Click the Apple menu at the top left and choose System Settings.
how do i check my mac specs | about this mac => Click the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. It shows the chip, memory, macOS version and serial number. Click More Info for details.
what is the finder | mac file explorer => Finder is the Mac's file manager, like File Explorer on Windows. Click the smiling face icon in the Dock to open it.
what is the dock on mac | add apps to dock => The Dock is the row of apps at the bottom of the screen. Drag an app into it to keep it there, or drag one out to remove it.
what is the menu bar on mac | top bar mac => The menu bar runs along the top of the screen. The left side shows the app's menus, and the right side shows Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center and the clock.
how do i update my mac | macos update => Open System Settings, General, Software Update, and click Update Now.
what is macos tahoe | latest macos name => As of 2025, the newest macOS is macOS Tahoe 26, released in fall 2025. Apple switched to year-based version numbers that year.
what is apple silicon | m1 m2 m3 m4 chips => Apple silicon is Apple's own chip family for Macs, starting with M1 in 2020 and followed by M2, M3, M4 and newer. They're fast and power efficient.
can macs play games | gaming on mac => Macs with Apple silicon play many games, including Minecraft, Roblox and a growing list of big titles, but far fewer than Windows PCs. Many popular shooters with anti-cheat don't run on Mac.
how do i right click on a macbook trackpad | two finger click => Click with two fingers on the trackpad, or hold Control and click. Change it in System Settings, Trackpad, Secondary click.
what are mac trackpad gestures | swipe between apps mac => Swipe up with three or four fingers for Mission Control, swipe left or right with three or four fingers to switch full-screen apps and spaces, and pinch to zoom.
how do i turn on tap to click on mac | trackpad tap to click => Open System Settings, Trackpad, and turn on Tap to click.
how do i change the mouse scroll direction on mac | natural scrolling => Open System Settings, Trackpad or Mouse, and turn off Natural scrolling to scroll like Windows.
how do i back up my mac | time machine => Plug in an external drive and open System Settings, General, Time Machine, then click Add Backup Disk. It backs up automatically every hour.
how do i force a mac to restart | mac frozen => Hold the power button (or Touch ID button) for about 10 seconds until it turns off, then press it again to turn it on.
what is terminal on mac | open terminal mac => Terminal is the Mac's command line app. Press Command + Space, type Terminal and press Return.
what is homebrew | install brew mac => Homebrew is a free package manager for Mac that installs developer tools from Terminal, like brew install python. Get it from its official site brew.sh.
how do i see hidden files on mac finder | show library folder mac => Press Command + Shift + . (period) in Finder. To open the Library folder, hold Option and click the Go menu.
how do i connect my mac to a tv | mac hdmi => Use an HDMI port or a USB-C to HDMI adapter, or AirPlay to a supported TV from Control Center, Screen Mirroring.
how do i use airdrop on mac | airdrop from mac => Open a Finder window and click AirDrop in the sidebar, then drag files onto the person's icon. Or right-click a file, Share, AirDrop.
how do i run windows apps on a mac | windows on mac => Apple silicon Macs can't install Windows directly. Apps like Parallels Desktop run Windows for ARM in a virtual machine, and some games work through CrossOver or Apple's Game Porting Toolkit.
do i need a mac for ios apps | can i make iphone apps on windows => Xcode is Apple's free app for building iPhone, iPad and Mac apps with Swift. It only runs on a Mac.
how do i free up space on mac | mac storage full => Open System Settings, General, Storage. It shows what uses space with recommendations like storing files in iCloud and emptying the Trash automatically.
how do i turn on filevault | mac encryption => Open System Settings, Privacy and Security, FileVault, and turn it on. It encrypts your drive so others can't read it without your password.
what is gatekeeper | app can't be opened because it's from an unidentified developer => Gatekeeper blocks apps that aren't from the App Store or known developers. If you trust the app, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway.
how do i split screen on mac | tile windows mac => Hover over the green button at the top left of a window and pick a tile option. On macOS Sequoia and later, you can also drag a window to a screen edge to tile it.
how do i take a screenshot with a timer on mac | delayed screenshot mac => Press Command + Shift + 5, click Options, and choose a 5 or 10 second timer.
