# tag: tech.privacy
what is google location history | google maps timeline => Timeline saves places you've been if Location History is on. You can view, pause or delete it in Google Maps under Your Timeline.
how do i turn off personalized ads | stop ads based on what i do => On Google, open My Ad Center and turn off personalized ads. On iPhone, open Settings, Privacy and Security, Apple Advertising.
should i cover my webcam | webcam cover slider => A small slide cover is a cheap way to be sure nobody sees you, even though the light normally turns on when the camera is in use.
what is a privacy screen filter | screen protector that hides screen from sides => A privacy filter darkens the screen when viewed from an angle, so people beside you can't read it. It makes the screen a little dimmer straight on.
what is a data broker | companies that sell your info => Data brokers collect and sell info about people, like addresses and shopping habits. Some sites let you ask them to delete your info.
does a photo show where it was taken | remove location from photo => Phone photos often save GPS location inside the file. On iPhone, tap Options when sharing and turn off Location before sending.
how do i download all my data from google | google takeout => Google Takeout at takeout.google.com lets you download copies of your Gmail, Photos, Drive and more as zip files.
what does a cookie banner mean | accept all cookies or reject => The banner asks if the site may use cookies for tracking and ads. Choosing reject or only necessary cookies still lets the site work in most cases.
should my gaming profile be private | xbox playstation privacy settings => It's safer to make your profile visible to friends only and limit who can message you. Ask a parent to check the console's family settings with you.
what is a vpn good for and not good for | does a vpn make me anonymous => A VPN hides your traffic from the local network and your internet provider, which helps on public Wi-Fi. It doesn't stop sites from knowing you when you sign in.
how do i stop people finding me by my phone number | hide my number on apps => In apps like Discord, WhatsApp and Instagram, turn off finding you by phone number or contacts in privacy settings.
what is two step verification on a game account | protect my epic account => Turn on two-factor in your Epic, Steam, PlayStation or Xbox account settings. It stops people who steal your password from logging in.
