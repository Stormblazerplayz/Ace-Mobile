# tag: tech.ipad.apps
how do i make a checklist in apple notes | notes checklist ipad => In a note, tap the checklist button (a circle with a tick) in the toolbar. Tap a circle to tick an item, and finished items can move to the bottom automatically.
how do i draw in apple notes | sketch in notes app => Open a note and tap the pen tip button in the toolbar to show the drawing tools, or just start writing with Apple Pencil on the page.
how do i lock a note on ipad | password protect a note => Open the note, tap the more button (three dots) and choose Lock. You can unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, depending on your settings.
how do i scan a document into notes | scan paper with ipad => In a note, tap the paperclip or camera button and choose Scan Documents. Hold the iPad over the page and it captures and straightens it.
how do i make a folder in apple notes | organize notes in folders => On the folders list, tap the new folder button at the bottom, give it a name, and drag notes into it.
how do i pin a note | keep a note at the top => Swipe right on the note in the list and tap the pin, or touch and hold it and choose Pin Note.
how do i make a table in apple notes | notes table => In a note, tap the table button in the toolbar. Tap the bars at the top or side of the table to add rows or columns.
how do i share a note with a friend | collaborate in notes => Open the note, tap the share button and choose Collaborate, then pick how to send the invite. Everyone invited can edit it.
how do i record audio in apple notes | voice recording notes => In iPadOS 18 or later, tap the paperclip in a note and choose Record Audio. Newer devices with Apple Intelligence can also make a transcript.
how do i make a presentation on ipad | keynote basics => Open Keynote, tap the plus to create a presentation, pick a theme, then tap the plus in the toolbar to add text, shapes, photos or charts. Tap the play button to present.
how do i add animations in keynote | magic move => Select an object and tap Animate to add Build In or Build Out effects. For smooth movement between slides, duplicate the slide, move things and use the Magic Move transition.
how do i export keynote as powerpoint | keynote to pptx => Open the presentation, tap the more button, choose Export and pick PowerPoint. You can also export as PDF or a movie.
how do i make a document in pages | pages app basics => Open Pages, tap Choose a Template, pick Blank or a design, and start typing. Use the paintbrush button to change fonts and styles.
how do i export a pages file as word | pages to docx => Tap the more button, choose Export and pick Word. You can also export to PDF or EPUB.
how do i make a spreadsheet on ipad | numbers app basics => Open Numbers, create a spreadsheet from a template, tap a cell and type. Type an equals sign to start a formula, like =SUM(A1:A5).
how do i record music on ipad for free | garageband basics => Open GarageBand, create a song, and pick an instrument like Keyboard, Drums or Audio Recorder. Tap the record button and play, then switch to the tracks view to edit.
what are live loops in garageband | garageband loop grid => Live Loops is a grid of loop cells you tap to start and stop in time, like a DJ. You can record a performance by tapping cells while recording.
how do i export a song from garageband ipad | share garageband track => In the My Songs browser, touch and hold the song, tap Share and pick Song. Choose quality, then save it to Files or send it.
how do i make a ringtone in garageband | custom ringtone iphone => In GarageBand on iPhone, open a song, tap Share in My Songs and choose Ringtone. It must be 30 seconds or shorter, and you can assign it in Settings, Sounds.
how do i use imovie on ipad | edit video on ipad free => Open iMovie, tap Start New Project, choose Movie, select clips and tap Create Movie. Tap a clip on the timeline to trim, split or change speed.
how do i add music in imovie | soundtrack imovie => Tap the plus button, choose Audio and Music, then pick a soundtrack or a song from your files and tap the plus to add it.
how do i export from imovie ipad | save imovie video => Tap Done, then the share button, and choose Save Video. Pick a resolution like 1080p or 4K.
how do i format a usb drive on ipad | erase a flash drive ipad => In iPadOS 18 and later, touch and hold the drive in the Files sidebar and choose Erase. Pick a format like ExFAT to work with Windows and Mac.
how do i connect to a pc shared folder from ipad | connect to server ipad => In Files, tap the more button and choose Connect to Server, then type smb:// followed by the PC's name or IP and sign in.
how do i get files from my pc to my ipad | transfer files pc to ipad => Easy ways are iCloud Drive or OneDrive on both, a USB drive, a shared folder over SMB, or emailing yourself small files.
how do i change the default browser on ipad | set chrome default => Go to Settings, Apps, pick the browser, then Default Browser App and choose it. On older iPadOS it's under Settings and the app's name.
how do i convert photos to jpg on ipad | heic to jpeg ipad => Share the photos to a Shortcut that uses Convert Image to JPEG, or set Camera Formats to Most Compatible so new photos are already JPG.
how do i merge pdfs on ipad | combine pdfs => In Files, select the PDFs, tap the more button and choose Create PDF. It joins them into one file.
how do i rotate a pdf page on ipad | turn sideways pdf page => Open it in Files, show the thumbnail sidebar, touch and hold a page and choose Rotate Left or Rotate Right.
how do i make a timer on ipad | ipad clock timer => Open the Clock app, tap Timer, set the time and tap Start. You can also ask Siri to set a timer.
how do i check the weather on ipad | ipad weather app => Open the Weather app, which iPad has had since iPadOS 16. Tap the list button to add other cities.
how do i see my ipad calendar on my pc | sync calendar => Use iCloud.com in a browser to see your Apple calendar, or add your Google or Outlook account to the iPad's Calendar so both stay in sync.
how do i add a google account to ipad mail | gmail on ipad => Go to Settings, Apps, Mail, Mail Accounts, Add Account and choose Google. On older iPadOS it's in Settings, Mail, Accounts. Or just install the Gmail app.
how do i make a contact on ipad | add a contact => Open Contacts, tap the plus, type the name and details and tap Done.
how do i connect my ipad to a tv with a cable | ipad hdmi => Use a USB-C to HDMI adapter or cable. Most USB-C iPads mirror the screen, and apps like video players can show full screen on the TV.
how do i use my ipad as a drawing tablet for pc | ipad as pen tablet windows => Apple doesn't support this on Windows, but third-party apps like Astropad Studio or Duet Display can do it. Expect some lag compared with a real drawing tablet.
how do i reset just the settings on ipad | reset all settings => Go to Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPad, Reset, Reset All Settings. Your apps and files stay, but settings like Wi-Fi passwords and layout reset.
what is activation lock | ipad locked to owner => Activation Lock ties a device to the owner's Apple Account when Find My is on. After an erase, it still needs that account's password to set up, which stops thieves using it.
how do i check my ipad model | which ipad do i have => Go to Settings, General, About and look at Model Name. The model number there can be searched on Apple's support site too.
how do i limit charging on ipad | 80 percent limit ipad => Newer iPads such as the M2 iPad Air and later Pros have an 80 Percent Limit option in Settings, Battery, Battery Health to slow battery aging.
how do i turn off auto brightness on ipad | ipad screen keeps dimming => Go to Settings, Accessibility, Display and Text Size and turn off Auto-Brightness at the bottom.
how do i zoom the whole ipad screen | accessibility zoom => Turn on Zoom in Settings, Accessibility, Zoom. Then double-tap with three fingers to zoom in and drag three fingers to move around.
how do i make the ipad read text out loud | speak screen => Turn on Speak Selection or Speak Screen in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content. With Speak Screen, swipe down with two fingers from the top to hear the page.
how do i turn off autocorrect on ipad | stop ipad changing my words => Go to Settings, General, Keyboard and turn off Auto-Correction. You can also turn off Auto-Capitalization there.
how do i change the ipad lock screen wallpaper | ipad wallpaper => Go to Settings, Wallpaper and tap Add New Wallpaper, or touch and hold the Lock Screen to customize it.
how do i take a photo with the ipad timer | camera timer ipad => In the Camera app, tap the arrow or the timer button at the top and pick 3 or 10 seconds.
how do i hide photos on ipad | hidden album => Select the photo, tap the more button and choose Hide. It moves to the Hidden album, which needs Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open.
how do i recover deleted photos on ipad | get back deleted photos => Open Photos and find Recently Deleted. Photos stay there for about 30 days before being erased, and you can tap Recover.
how do i edit a photo on ipad | crop photo ipad => Open the photo and tap Edit. Use Adjust for light and color, Filters for looks, and Crop to cut or straighten.
how do i remove something from a photo on ipad | clean up tool => On iPads with Apple Intelligence, tap Edit then Clean Up and circle what to remove. Otherwise use a free app like Snapseed with its Healing tool.
how do i make a photo slideshow on ipad | memories movie => In Photos, open an album, tap the more button and choose Slideshow, or use Memories to make a short movie with music. iMovie gives more control.
how do i make a gif on ipad | gif from live photo => Open a Live Photo, tap the Live button and choose Loop or Bounce, which play like a GIF. A shortcut or a GIF app can save it as a real GIF file.
how do i get a refund from the app store | refund an app => Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase and request a refund with a reason. Apple decides each case.
how do i cancel an app subscription | stop a subscription ipad => Go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose it and tap Cancel Subscription. It runs until the paid period ends.
what is family sharing | share apps with family => Family Sharing lets up to six people share purchases, iCloud storage and subscriptions, and lets parents approve kids' purchases with Ask to Buy.
