# tag: tech.consoles.switch
how do i fix joy con drift | my joycon is drifting | switch stick moves by itself => First update the controller and calibrate it: System Settings, Controllers and Sensors, Calibrate Control Sticks. If it still drifts, contact Nintendo support, which repairs drifting Joy-Con, often for free in many regions.
how do i update my nintendo switch | switch system update => Open System Settings, System, System Update. The Switch must be online. Games update when you open them or from the game's Options, Software Update.
how do i update my joy cons | update switch controllers => Attach them to the console, then open System Settings, Controllers and Sensors, Update Controllers.
how do i pair a pro controller to switch | connect controller to switch => Open Controllers on the Home menu, then Change Grip/Order, and press the small sync button on top of the controller. Or plug it into the dock with a USB-C cable.
how do i add storage to my switch | microsd card switch => The original Switch, OLED and Lite take microSDXC cards, so a 128 or 256 GB card adds lots of space. Turn the console off before inserting it, in the slot under the kickstand.
what microsd card does the switch 2 need | switch 2 storage card => The Nintendo Switch 2 only works with microSD Express cards for games. Regular microSD cards can only copy screenshots and videos.
how do i move games to my sd card | switch data management => Open System Settings, Data Management, Move Data Between System / microSD Card, and pick the games to move.
how do i delete games on switch | archive software switch => Open System Settings, Data Management, Manage Software, pick the game, and choose Archive Software. It frees space but keeps your save data and icon.
how do i take a screenshot on switch | switch capture button => Press the square Capture button on the left Joy-Con or Pro Controller. Hold it to save the last 30 seconds of gameplay as a video in supported games.
how do i send switch screenshots to my phone | switch album to phone => Open Album, pick up to 10 screenshots or one video, choose Send to Smartphone, and scan the QR codes with your phone.
how do i send switch videos to my pc | copy screenshots usb switch => Plug the Switch into a PC with a USB-C data cable, then open System Settings, Data Management, Manage Screenshots and Videos, Copy to a Computer via USB Connection.
how do i connect my switch to a tv | switch dock setup => Plug the dock's AC adapter and HDMI cable in, connect the HDMI to the TV, and slide the Switch into the dock. Switch the TV to that HDMI input. Switch Lite can't connect to a TV.
why won't my switch show on the tv | switch dock not working => Plug things in this order: power to the dock, HDMI to the TV, then insert the Switch. Use the official adapter and try another HDMI port. Some third-party docks have damaged consoles.
how do i turn off my switch completely | switch power off => Hold the Power button for about 3 seconds and choose Power Options, Turn Off. If it's frozen, hold Power for 12 seconds to force it off.
what is switch maintenance mode | switch won't start => Turn it off, then hold volume up and volume down while pressing Power. Maintenance Mode lets you update or restore settings without deleting saves. Ask an adult before using the restore options.
how do i set up parental controls on switch | switch parental controls app => Parents can install the Nintendo Switch Parental Controls app on a phone and link it to the console. It sets play time limits, game age ratings and restrictions on messaging.
how do i back up switch save data | switch cloud saves => With Nintendo Switch Online, save data cloud backs up most game saves automatically. Some games don't support it, so check under Data Management, Save Data Cloud.
how do i transfer my switch data to switch 2 | system transfer => Use System Transfer during Switch 2 setup or later in System Settings to move users, saves and game data from the old Switch. Both consoles need to be updated and charged.
what is the nintendo switch 2 | switch 2 vs switch => The Switch 2 launched June 5, 2025. It has a bigger 7.9 inch 1080p 120 Hz screen, 4K output when docked, 256 GB storage, magnetic Joy-Con 2 that work like mice, and GameChat. It plays most original Switch games.
what is a game key card | switch 2 game key card => A game-key card is a Switch 2 cartridge that holds a key, not the full game. You insert it to download the game, then need the card inserted to play.
what is gamechat on switch 2 | c button switch 2 => GameChat lets you voice chat, share your screen and use a camera with friends. Press the C button on the Joy-Con 2 to open it. Kids' use is controlled by parental controls.
how do i calibrate the switch motion controls | gyro calibration switch => Open System Settings, Controllers and Sensors, Calibrate Motion Controls, and follow the steps with the controller on a flat surface.
how do i fix switch joy con not connecting | joycon wont pair => Slide the Joy-Con onto the console to pair it again, update controllers, and restart the Switch. Remove other Bluetooth devices nearby if it keeps disconnecting.
how do i get more battery life on switch | switch battery tips => Lower brightness, turn on airplane mode in single-player games, close games you aren't playing, and turn off controller vibration if you don't need it.
what is nintendo switch online | do i need switch online => Nintendo Switch Online is a paid membership for online multiplayer in most games, save data cloud and a library of classic NES, SNES and other games. Free games like Fortnite don't need it for online play.
how do i redeem a code on switch | eshop code => Open the Nintendo eShop, pick your account, scroll down on the left and choose Redeem Code, then type the 16 character code. Never trust free code websites.
how do i stop accidental purchases on switch | eshop password => Open eShop settings on the account (or on the Nintendo Account website) and turn on Password Entry Settings so a password is needed for every purchase.
how do i clean switch joy con rails | joycon loose => Clean the rails gently with a dry cloth. If a Joy-Con slides off too easily, the small lock part may be worn, which Nintendo support can repair.
why is my switch screen not rotating or touch not working | switch touchscreen not working => Restart the console, remove any thick or bubbly screen protector, and check System Settings for updates. Touch only works in handheld mode.
# tag: tech.consoles.playstation
how do i put my ps5 in safe mode | ps5 safe mode => Turn the PS5 off fully, then hold the Power button until you hear a second beep about 7 seconds later. Connect the controller with a USB cable and press the PS button.
how do i rebuild the ps5 database | ps5 slow menus fix => Enter Safe Mode and choose Clear Cache and Rebuild Database, then Rebuild Database. It can fix slow menus and missing games, without deleting saves.
how do i add storage to my ps5 | ps5 m.2 ssd => Fit a PCIe Gen 4 NVMe M.2 SSD with a heatsink into the PS5's expansion slot, then let the console format it. Sony recommends drives with at least 5,500 MB/s read speed.
can i play ps5 games from a usb drive | ps5 external hard drive => You can store PS5 games on a USB drive but must move them to internal or M.2 storage to play. PS4 games can play straight from a USB drive.
how do i turn on 120hz on ps5 | ps5 120 fps => Open Settings, Screen and Video, Video Output, and set 120 Hz Output to Automatic. You need an HDMI 2.1 TV or monitor, and the game must support 120 Hz.
what is rest mode on ps5 | ps5 rest mode safe => Rest Mode is a low power sleep that keeps downloads going and charges controllers. If it causes problems, turn on Disable USB power in rest mode or use full shutdown.
how do i pair a ps5 controller to my ps5 | dualsense pairing ps5 => Plug the controller into the PS5 with a USB cable and press the PS button. To pair another controller wirelessly, go to Settings, Accessories, General, Bluetooth Accessories.
how do i update my ps5 controller | dualsense firmware => The PS5 asks you to update when needed. Plug it in with USB and follow the prompt, or check Settings, Accessories, Controllers, Wireless Controller Device Software.
what is ps5 pro | ps5 pro vs ps5 => The PS5 Pro, released in November 2024, has a stronger GPU, better ray tracing and PSSR AI upscaling for sharper images at higher frame rates. It plays the same games as the PS5.
what is ps remote play | play ps5 on ipad => PS Remote Play lets you stream your PS5 to a phone, tablet or PC over the internet. Install the PS Remote Play app, sign in, and turn on Remote Play in the PS5 settings.
how do i set up ps5 parental controls | ps5 family settings => Parents can manage a child's PS5 account through PlayStation Family Management on the PS5 or the PlayStation app, setting play time, age ratings, spending limits and chat options.
# tag: tech.consoles.xbox
what is quick resume on xbox | xbox quick resume => Quick Resume on Xbox Series X and S keeps several games paused in the background, so you jump back to where you left off in seconds.
what is xbox game pass | is game pass worth it => Game Pass is a paid subscription with a big library of games to download or stream, including new Xbox games on launch day in some tiers. As of 2025, there are several tiers with different prices and features.
how do i pair an xbox controller to an xbox | xbox controller sync => Press the pair button on the console, then hold the pair button on top of the controller until the Xbox logo flashes fast and then stays on.
how do i update my xbox controller | xbox accessories app => On Xbox, open Settings, Devices and connections, Accessories, pick the controller, and choose Firmware version to update. On PC, use the Xbox Accessories app.
how do i hard reset my xbox | xbox power cycle => Hold the Xbox button on the console for about 10 seconds until it turns off, unplug it for a minute, then turn it back on. This clears temporary problems.
how do i clear the xbox cache | clear persistent storage xbox => Open Settings, Devices and connections, Blu-ray, Persistent storage, and select Clear persistent storage. A full power cycle also clears temporary cache.
what is the difference between xbox series x and series s | series x vs series s => Series X is stronger, aims for 4K and has a disc drive on the standard model. Series S is smaller, cheaper and aims for 1440p or lower, with no disc drive.
how do i turn on 120hz on xbox | xbox 120 fps => Open Settings, General, TV and display options, and set Refresh rate to 120 Hz. Your TV or monitor needs to support it.
what is xbox cloud gaming | play xbox on ipad => Xbox Cloud Gaming streams games from Microsoft's servers to phones, tablets, PCs and TVs, with a Game Pass Ultimate membership and a good internet connection.
# tag: tech.consoles.general
what is a steam deck | steam deck vs switch => The Steam Deck is Valve's handheld gaming PC. It runs SteamOS and plays many PC games from your Steam library, while the Switch plays Nintendo games.
how do i use desktop mode on steam deck | steam deck desktop => Press the Steam button, go to Power, and choose Switch to Desktop. Use the touchpads as a mouse, and Steam + X opens the keyboard.
what does hdmi 2.1 do for consoles | do i need hdmi 2.1 => HDMI 2.1 supports 4K at 120 Hz and VRR, which PS5 and Xbox Series X use. You need an HDMI 2.1 port on the TV and the cable that came with the console.
what is game mode on a tv | tv input lag => Game Mode on a TV turns off extra picture processing to cut input lag a lot. Many TVs turn it on automatically with ALLM when a console connects.
what is vrr on a tv | vrr for consoles => VRR (variable refresh rate) matches the TV to the console's frame rate to stop tearing and reduce stutter. Turn it on in both the TV and console settings if both support it.
how do i stop stick drift on any controller | controller drift fix => Clean around the stick with a bit of isopropyl alcohol on a cotton swab while moving it, set a small deadzone in game, and recalibrate. Bad drift usually needs a repair or replacement.
are hall effect sticks better | hall effect joystick drift => Hall effect sticks use magnets instead of rubbing contacts, so they don't wear out and drift the same way. Many third-party controllers use them.
can i use a ps5 controller on switch | controller cross compatibility => Not officially. Switch works with Joy-Con, Pro Controllers and licensed controllers. PS and Xbox controllers work on PCs, phones and tablets.
how do i clean my console | dusty ps5 or xbox => Turn it off and unplug it, then wipe it with a dry microfiber cloth and use a vacuum or gentle air on the vents. PS5 owners can vacuum the dust catchers under the side covers.
should my console stand up or lie flat | ps5 vertical or horizontal => Both are fine. Just give it a few inches of space around the vents and keep it out of closed cabinets so it doesn't overheat.
