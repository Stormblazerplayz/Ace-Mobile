# tag: tech.pc.hardware.extra
what is userbenchmark | is userbenchmark accurate => UserBenchmark's rankings are widely criticized as unreliable and biased. Reviews from sites that test many games, plus tools like 3DMark and Cinebench, give a fairer picture.
what is 3dmark time spy | gpu score test => Time Spy is a DirectX 12 gaming benchmark in 3DMark. Compare your graphics score to others with the same GPU to spot cooling or settings problems.
what is memtest86 | memtest usb ram check => MemTest86 boots from a USB stick and tests RAM for errors outside Windows. Any error at all means unstable or faulty memory, often fixed by lowering EXPO or swapping a stick.
what is prime95 | cpu torture test => Prime95 is a very heavy CPU stress test. It's good for checking stability and cooling, but it runs hotter than games, so don't panic at high temps during a short test.
what is geekbench | compare phone and pc speed => Geekbench is a benchmark that runs on phones, tablets and computers, giving single-core and multi-core scores you can compare across devices.
what is aio gurgling | water sound in liquid cooler => Gurgling usually means air bubbles in the pump. Mount the radiator so the pump isn't the highest point, and the noise often settles after a few days.
what is push pull on a radiator | fans on both sides radiator => Push-pull puts fans on both sides of a radiator. It helps a little with thick radiators but usually only a few degrees, and costs more fans.
what is a custom water loop | custom liquid cooling => A custom loop has a separate pump, reservoir, tubes and blocks for the CPU and GPU. It looks amazing and runs cool but costs a lot and needs maintenance and leak testing.
what is positive pressure in a pc case | positive vs negative airflow => Positive pressure means more air comes in than goes out, so air leaks out of gaps and dust stays out, as long as intakes have filters.
what are sleeved cable extensions | custom pc cables => Sleeved extensions plug onto your power supply cables to make them look neat and colorful. Make sure 12VHPWR or 12V-2x6 ones are high quality and fully seated.
what is thermal putty | putty vs thermal pads => Thermal putty is a soft paste-like material used instead of pads on GPU memory and VRMs. It fills gaps of any thickness, which helps when pad sizes are unknown.
