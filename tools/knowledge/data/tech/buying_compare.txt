# tag: tech.buying
ipad or laptop for school | ipad vs laptop => An iPad with a keyboard is great for notes, reading and drawing. A laptop is better for typing long work, coding and running full desktop programs like Blender.
macbook air vs macbook pro | which macbook should i get => The MacBook Air is light, silent and fanless, great for most people. The Pro has fans and a stronger chip for long video editing, 3D or coding sessions.
ps5 or xbox series x | ps5 vs xbox => Both are similar in power. Pick the one with the exclusive games and the console your friends play on. Xbox has Game Pass, and PlayStation has exclusives like Spider-Man.
switch 2 or steam deck | switch 2 vs steam deck => Switch 2 is the only way to play Nintendo games like Mario and Zelda. Steam Deck plays your PC library on Steam. Choose by which games you want.
gaming pc or console | pc vs console for gaming => A console is cheaper to start and simple. A PC costs more but does more, like editing, modding, higher FPS and cheaper games on sales.
intel or amd cpu for gaming | amd vs intel 2025 => As of 2025, AMD's Ryzen X3D chips like the 9800X3D are the top gaming CPUs, while both brands make good all-round chips. Compare reviews for your budget.
wired or wireless mouse for gaming | wireless gaming mouse lag => Modern gaming wireless mice using a 2.4 GHz dongle feel as fast as wired. Avoid Bluetooth mode for gaming, since it adds lag.
1440p or 4k monitor for gaming | which resolution monitor to buy => 1440p at a high refresh rate is the sweet spot for most gaming PCs. 4K looks sharper but needs a much stronger GPU for high FPS.
airpods or airpods pro | which airpods to get => AirPods Pro have active noise cancellation, ear tips for a better seal, and transparency mode. Regular AirPods are open-fit, lighter and cheaper.
headset or headphones and mic | gaming headset vs headphones => A headset is simple, all in one. Headphones plus a separate USB mic usually sound better for the same money, especially for streaming.
how much should i spend on a gaming pc | gaming pc budget => Prices change, but as of 2025 a solid 1080p gaming build often starts around 700 to 900 US dollars, and a strong 1440p build is often 1,200 to 1,600 dollars.
is it worth buying a refurbished device | refurbished vs new => Official refurbished devices from Apple or the maker are checked and come with a warranty, so they're often a great deal. Third-party refurbished varies more.
what is an open box deal | open box electronics => Open box items were returned or used as display units and sold cheaper. Check the warranty and return policy, and that all parts are included.
how do i spot fake reviews | fake product reviews => Be wary of lots of short five-star reviews posted close together, reviews that don't mention the product, and brand names you've never heard of. Check trusted review sites too.
what should i check before buying a used gpu | used graphics card check => Meet in person or use a platform with buyer protection, see it running a game or benchmark, check temperatures and fan noise, and look for damage. Bring an adult.
what is a cpu and motherboard combo | bundle deal pc parts => Shops often sell a CPU, motherboard and RAM together for less. Make sure the BIOS supports the CPU out of the box.
how long does a gaming pc last | when to upgrade pc => A good gaming PC usually plays new games well for four to six years, longer if you lower settings or upgrade the GPU.
bambu a1 vs a1 mini | a1 or a1 mini => The A1 has a 256 mm cube build volume and the A1 mini 180 mm. Both auto-calibrate and work with the AMS lite. The A1 fits bigger prints like helmets.
what printer comes after the a1 | bambu p1s vs a1 => The Bambu P1S is an enclosed CoreXY printer that can handle ABS and ASA better. The X1 and H2 series add more features at higher prices.
which drone should a beginner buy | first drone => A small sub-250 gram camera drone with GPS and return to home is easiest. For FPV, start with a simulator and a tiny whoop. Check your country's rules either way.
which vr headset for beginners | best vr headset for kids => Meta Quest headsets are standalone and easy to start with, but Meta sets a minimum age. Check the age rules and set up parental supervision.
what tablet is best for drawing | drawing tablet vs ipad => An iPad with Apple Pencil is great for drawing anywhere. A pen tablet or pen display for a PC is cheaper or bigger and works with desktop art apps.
should i buy the newest phone | last year's phone => Last year's model is often almost as good and much cheaper. New phones usually bring small yearly improvements.
how much storage should my next phone have | phone storage size => If you take lots of photos, videos and games, 256 GB is a safer choice than 128 GB, because you can't add storage to an iPhone later.
