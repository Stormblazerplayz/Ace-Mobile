# tag: tech.audio.production
what is fl studio | fl studio for beats => FL Studio is a popular music app for making beats, known for its pattern-based sequencer and piano roll. Paid versions include free lifetime updates.
what is ableton live | ableton for music => Ableton Live is a music production app famous for live performance and electronic music, with a clip view for trying ideas quickly.
what is logic pro | logic pro mac => Logic Pro is Apple's professional music app for Mac and iPad, a big step up from GarageBand with many instruments and effects.
what is bandlab | free music app online => BandLab is a free music-making app that works in a browser or on phones, letting you record, make beats and collaborate online.
what is musescore | free music notation => MuseScore is a free app for writing sheet music. It plays back what you write so you can hear your composition.
what is a vst plugin | music plugins => A VST is a plugin that adds an instrument or effect to a music app, like a synth, piano or reverb. Get them from trusted sites only.
what is an asio driver | asio4all => ASIO is a Windows audio driver system that gives music apps low delay. Audio interfaces usually come with their own ASIO driver, which works best.
what is buffer size in audio | audio latency buffer => Buffer size is how much audio the computer handles at once. Small buffers give less delay for recording, and bigger buffers stop crackles when mixing big projects.
what is audio bit depth | 16 bit vs 24 bit audio => Bit depth sets how much detail each audio sample stores. 24-bit gives more room for quiet and loud sounds when recording, and 16-bit is fine for listening.
what is phantom power | 48v mic power => Phantom power sends 48 volts down an XLR cable to power condenser mics. Dynamic mics like the SM58 don't need it.
what is gain staging | set mic gain => Gain staging means setting input levels so audio is loud enough to be clear but never clips. Aim for peaks around -12 to -6 dB when recording.
what is a piano roll | piano roll in music apps => A piano roll is a grid where you draw notes, with pitch going up and time going across. It's how most people write melodies in a DAW.
what is quantize in music | fix timing of notes => Quantize snaps notes to the nearest beat on the grid, fixing sloppy timing. Too much can make music sound robotic.
what is a sampler instrument | sampling in music production => A sampler plays recorded sounds, like a drum hit or voice, across the keyboard, so you can turn any sound into an instrument.
what is a click track | play to a click => A click track is a metronome you hear in headphones while recording, so everyone stays on the same tempo.
what is splice for music | sample library => Splice is a subscription service with a huge library of royalty-free sounds and loops for music producers.
what is an audio export format for music | wav or mp3 export => Export WAV for the best quality or for uploading to distributors, and MP3 for small files to share with friends.
what is mastering software | ai mastering => Mastering tools make a finished song louder and balanced for streaming. Some apps offer automatic AI mastering, but learning your own ears matters most.
what is monitor speakers vs regular speakers | studio monitors => Studio monitors are speakers made to sound flat and honest, so you hear your mix as it really is instead of with boosted bass.
