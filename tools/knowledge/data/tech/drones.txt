# tag: tech.drones.basics
how do quadcopters work | why do drone props spin opposite ways => A quadcopter has four propellers pushing air down. Two spin clockwise and two counterclockwise so it doesn't spin. Speeding up some motors and slowing others makes it tilt, turn and move.
how does a drone turn left and right | what is yaw on a drone => To yaw (spin in place), the drone speeds up the props spinning one way and slows the others, so the twisting forces don't cancel and the body turns.
what are pitch roll yaw and throttle | drone controls explained => Throttle moves up and down, pitch tilts forward and back, roll tilts left and right, and yaw spins the nose. On most radios the left stick is throttle and yaw, the right is pitch and roll (Mode 2).
what are the parts of a drone | drone parts list => A frame, four brushless motors, ESCs that control motor speed, a flight controller (the brain), a LiPo battery, propellers, a radio receiver, and for FPV a camera and video transmitter.
what does a flight controller do | drone brain => The flight controller reads its gyro and accelerometer thousands of times a second and adjusts each motor's speed to keep the drone stable and follow your stick inputs.
what is an esc | electronic speed controller => An ESC (electronic speed controller) turns battery power into the right signals to spin a brushless motor at the speed the flight controller asks for.
what is a brushless motor | brushed vs brushless => Brushless motors use electronics to switch the magnets instead of rubbing brushes, so they're more powerful, efficient and last longer. Most drones bigger than tiny toys use them.
what does kv mean on a motor | motor kv rating => KV is how many RPM a motor spins per volt with no load. High KV motors suit smaller props or lower voltage; low KV motors suit bigger props or higher voltage.
what does 4s mean on a battery | lipo cell count => S is the number of cells in series. Each LiPo cell is 3.7 V nominal (4.2 V full), so a 4S pack is about 14.8 V and 6S is about 22.2 V. Higher S means more power.
what does the c rating mean on a lipo | lipo 100c meaning => The C rating is roughly how fast a battery can safely give power compared to its capacity. Real-world C ratings are often exaggerated, so trust reviews.
what do propeller numbers mean | prop size 5040 => In a prop like 5x4.0x3 (or 5040 three blade), 5 is the diameter in inches, 4.0 the pitch, and 3 the number of blades. Bigger pitch pushes more air but uses more power.
what is a gps drone | return to home => GPS drones know where they are, so they can hold position and automatically fly back to the takeoff point (Return to Home) if the signal or battery runs low.
what is a gimbal | camera stabilizer drone => A gimbal is a motorized mount that keeps the camera level and steady while the drone tilts, giving smooth video.
how long can a drone fly | drone battery life => Small camera drones often fly 25 to 45 minutes per battery. FPV racing and freestyle drones usually fly only 3 to 6 minutes because they use so much power.
how far can a drone fly | drone range => It depends on the drone and radio, from a hundred meters for toys to several kilometers for camera drones. Laws usually require keeping it within your line of sight anyway.
can drones fly in the wind | drone wind limit => Light drones get pushed around easily. Check the maker's wind rating, avoid gusty days, and fly into the wind first so the trip back is easier.
can drones fly in the rain | is my drone waterproof => Most drones aren't waterproof. Water can short the electronics, so don't fly in rain or snow, and dry it out fully if it gets wet.
what is a tiny whoop | best beginner fpv drone => A tiny whoop is a small, light FPV drone with ducted props, safe enough to fly indoors. It's a great way to learn FPV without breaking much.
what is a toothpick drone | drone sizes fpv => Toothpicks are light 2 to 3.5 inch FPV drones with thin frames for flying outdoors. 5 inch drones are the common size for freestyle and racing.
what is a cinewhoop | ducted camera drone => A cinewhoop is a ducted FPV drone that carries a camera for smooth, close-up shots, like flying through a building. The ducts protect the props.
# tag: tech.drones.fpv
what is fpv | what does fpv mean => FPV means first person view. You wear goggles that show a live video feed from the drone's camera, so it feels like you're sitting in the drone.
analog vs digital fpv | dji o4 vs analog => Analog FPV has very low delay and cheap gear but a fuzzy picture. Digital systems like DJI, Walksnail and HDZero give much clearer HD video, with more cost.
what is acro mode | angle mode vs acro => Angle (self-level) mode keeps the drone level when you let go of the sticks. Acro mode doesn't level itself, which allows flips and freestyle but takes practice. Horizon mixes both.
what is betaflight | betaflight configurator => Betaflight is the most popular free firmware for FPV flight controllers. You set it up with the Betaflight app on a computer to change modes, rates and settings.
what is expresslrs | elrs radio => ExpressLRS (ELRS) is a popular open source radio link for FPV with long range and low latency. Your radio and the drone's receiver both need ELRS and the same bind phrase.
what radio controller should i get for fpv | fpv transmitter => Radios running EdgeTX, like ones from RadioMaster, are popular. Pick one with the radio protocol your drone uses, like ExpressLRS.
should i practice in a drone simulator | fpv sim => Yes. Simulators like Liftoff, VelociDrone, Uncrashed and the DRL Simulator let you learn acro flying with your real radio, without breaking anything. Practicing there saves money.
what are drone rates | betaflight rates => Rates set how fast the drone rotates when you move the sticks. Lower rates feel calmer for beginners; freestyle pilots often use higher rates for fast flips.
what is a vtx | video transmitter => The VTX sends the camera's video to your goggles. Its power and channel settings must follow your country's radio rules.
do i need a license for fpv | ham license fpv => It depends on your country. In some countries, like the US, many analog FPV video transmitters need an amateur radio license. Check your country's rules.
what are fpv goggles | box goggles vs slim => FPV goggles show the drone's camera feed on screens near your eyes. Box goggles are cheap and roomy; slim goggles are smaller and often clearer.
what is a spotter in fpv | do i need a spotter => A spotter watches the drone with their own eyes while you fly in goggles. Many countries' rules require a visual observer for FPV flights.
what is a flip in fpv | how to do a flip => In acro mode, push full roll or pitch quickly while adding a bit of throttle, then return the stick to center. Practice in a simulator first.
what is turtle mode | flip drone over after crash => Turtle mode spins the motors backward to flip a crashed drone back upright. Set it on a switch in Betaflight.
what is a blackbox log | betaflight blackbox => Blackbox records flight data like gyro readings and motor outputs. Pilots use it to tune their drones and find vibration problems.
# tag: tech.drones.safety
how do i keep lipo batteries safe | lipo safety tips => Charge them with a proper balance charger in a fireproof LiPo bag, never leave them charging alone, never use puffed or damaged packs, and store them at storage voltage. Ask an adult to help.
what is storage voltage for lipo | how do i store lipo batteries => Store LiPos at about 3.8 V per cell, using your charger's storage mode, in a cool fireproof bag or box. Leaving them fully charged for days wears them out faster.
what does a puffy lipo mean | swollen drone battery => A puffed LiPo is damaged and can catch fire. Stop using it and have an adult dispose of it safely, following local battery recycling rules.
how do i dispose of a lipo battery | throw away lipo => Have an adult take it to a battery recycling drop-off, after discharging it with the charger's discharge mode if the recycler asks. Never put LiPos in normal trash.
what is a balance charger | balance charging lipo => A balance charger charges each cell to the same voltage using the battery's small balance plug. It keeps the pack safe and healthy.
what should i do if my drone battery catches fire | lipo fire => Get away and tell an adult right away. LiPo fires are very hot. Keep people back, and call emergency services if it spreads.
how do i fly a drone safely | where is it safe to fly my drone => Fly in open areas away from people, roads and airports, keep it in sight, check the weather, check props before each flight, and know your country's rules. Ask a parent before flying.
how high can i fly my drone | drone height limit => Many countries cap recreational drones at about 400 feet (120 meters) above the ground, but rules vary. Check your country's rules.
do i need to register my drone | drone weight 250 grams => Many countries require registration or a test for drones over about 250 grams, and some for any drone with a camera. Check your country's aviation authority rules before flying.
what is remote id | drone remote id => Remote ID broadcasts a drone's ID and location so authorities can identify it. The US requires it for most registered drones. Check your country's rules.
what is the faa trust test | us drone test for kids => In the US, recreational flyers must pass the free TRUST test from the FAA, an online safety test. Kids can take it. Other countries have their own tests.
can i fly a drone near an airport | no fly zones => Airspace near airports is controlled and usually needs permission. Apps like the official ones from your country's aviation authority show where you can fly.
can i fly a drone over people | drone over crowds => It's usually not allowed or needs special rules, since a falling drone can hurt someone. Keep away from people, stadiums and events.
can i fly a drone in a national park | drones in parks => Many parks, including US National Parks, ban drones without a permit. Check the park's rules before going.
what should i check before a drone flight | preflight checklist => Check the batteries are charged, props aren't cracked and are on tight, the drone has GPS lock if it uses it, the weather is calm and the area is clear and allowed.
why did my drone drift away | drone flyaway => Drift often comes from a bad compass or GPS signal, wind, or an uncalibrated IMU. Calibrate the compass away from metal and wait for a strong GPS lock before takeoff.
how do i calibrate a drone compass | compass error drone => Follow your drone app's steps, usually spinning the drone level and then nose down, away from metal, cars and buildings.
what does an accelerometer do in a drone | drone imu => The IMU (gyro plus accelerometer) senses tilt and rotation so the flight controller can keep the drone stable. Calibrate it on a flat surface if the drone drifts.
# tag: tech.drones.camera
what is the best drone for a beginner | drone under 250 grams => As of 2025, small GPS camera drones under 250 grams, like DJI's Mini series, are popular for beginners because they're stable, easy to fly and face fewer rules in many countries.
what are nd filters for drones | nd filter => ND filters are like sunglasses for the camera. They let you use slower shutter speeds in bright light so video has natural motion blur.
what is the 180 degree shutter rule | shutter speed for video => Set shutter speed to about double your frame rate, like 1/60 for 30 fps or 1/50 for 24 or 25 fps. Motion then looks natural, not choppy.
what is d log | flat color profile drone => D-Log and similar flat profiles keep more detail in bright and dark areas, but look grey until you color grade them in an editor like DaVinci Resolve.
how do i make smooth drone videos | cinematic drone shots => Move the sticks slowly and steadily, use Cine or Tripod mode for slower speeds, fly in one direction at a time, and shoot at the golden hour near sunrise or sunset.
what is hyperlapse drone | drone timelapse => A hyperlapse is a timelapse where the camera moves. Many DJI drones have a Hyperlapse mode that flies a path and takes photos automatically.
how do i stabilize fpv footage | gyroflow => Gyroflow is free software that uses the camera's gyro data to stabilize shaky FPV footage smoothly. Action cameras like GoPro also have built-in stabilization.
