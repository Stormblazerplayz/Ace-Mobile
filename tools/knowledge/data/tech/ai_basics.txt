# tag: tech.ai
how does chatgpt or claude come up with answers | how do ai chatbots answer questions => A language model reads your message and predicts the most likely next piece of text, one token at a time, based on patterns learned from huge amounts of writing. It's very good pattern matching, not looking things up in a list.
does ai actually understand what it says | can ai think => AI models find deep patterns in language and can reason through problems surprisingly well, but they don't have feelings or experiences like people. Scientists still debate what "understanding" means for them.
does ai have feelings | is ai alive => No. AI chatbots can talk about feelings because they learned from human writing, but they aren't alive and don't feel happy or sad the way you do.
what is training data | what data are ai models trained on => Training data is the huge set of examples an AI learns from, like text from books and websites, or labeled pictures. The AI's skills and mistakes come from patterns in that data.
what are parameters in an ai model | what does 7b model mean => Parameters are the numbers inside a model that get tuned during training. A "7B" model has about 7 billion of them. More parameters can mean more skill but need more memory and power.
what is a context window | how much can ai remember in a chat => The context window is how much text a model can look at at once, measured in tokens. Things outside it, like very old parts of a long chat, can be forgotten.
what is temperature in ai | ai creativity setting => Temperature controls how random a model's word choices are. Low temperature gives focused, predictable answers; high temperature gives more creative and varied ones.
why does ai make stuff up | why does ai give wrong answers => Models predict text that sounds right, so they can confidently produce wrong facts, called hallucinations. Always check important facts with a reliable source.
how do ai image generators work | how does ai make pictures => Most image generators use diffusion: they start with random noise and remove it step by step, guided by your text prompt, until a picture forms. They learned from millions of captioned images.
what is a diffusion model | diffusion ai meaning => A diffusion model learns to turn random noise into images by reversing a process that adds noise. It powers many AI image and video generators.
how do self driving cars work | how does a tesla drive itself => They use cameras and sometimes radar or LiDAR to sense the road, AI to recognize cars, people and lanes, and planning software to decide how to steer and brake.
what is lidar | how does lidar work => LiDAR shoots laser pulses and times how long they take to bounce back, building a 3D map of the surroundings. Robots, self-driving cars and some iPads use it.
what is reinforcement learning | how do ai learn games => In reinforcement learning, an AI tries actions and gets rewards or penalties, slowly learning what works best. It's how AI learned to beat people at Go and many video games.
what is overfitting | ai memorizing training data => Overfitting is when a model learns its training examples too exactly, including noise, so it does badly on new data. It's like memorizing answers instead of learning the method.
what is ai bias | can ai be unfair => If training data has unfair patterns, the AI can repeat them, like treating groups of people differently. Builders test and adjust models to reduce this.
who made claude | what company made claude => Claude is made by Anthropic, an AI safety and research company founded in 2021.
who made chatgpt | what is openai => ChatGPT is made by OpenAI. It launched in November 2022 and made AI chatbots famous worldwide.
what is gemini ai | google ai chatbot => Gemini is Google's family of AI models and its chatbot app. It's also built into Google search, Android and Workspace apps.
what is a local ai model | can i run ai on my own pc => Open-weight models like Llama, Qwen or Mistral can run on your own computer with apps like Ollama or LM Studio. A GPU with lots of VRAM helps run bigger ones.
what is ollama | how do i use ollama => Ollama is a free app that downloads and runs open AI models locally. After installing it, a command like ollama run followed by a model name starts a chat in the terminal.
what are open weight models | open source ai => Open-weight models publish the trained numbers so anyone can download and run them. Examples include Meta's Llama, Mistral's models and Alibaba's Qwen.
how much vram do i need for local ai | run llm on gpu => As a rough guide, small 7 to 8 billion parameter models run in about 6 to 8 GB of VRAM when quantized. Bigger models need much more or run slowly on the CPU.
what is quantization in ai | what does q4 mean => Quantization stores a model's numbers with fewer bits, like 4 bit instead of 16 bit. It makes the model much smaller and faster with a small drop in quality.
what is fine tuning | how do you customize an ai => Fine-tuning trains an existing model a bit more on special examples, so it gets better at one task or style, like answering in a certain format.
what is rag in ai | retrieval augmented generation => RAG gives a model relevant documents to read before answering, so it can use up-to-date or private info instead of only what it learned in training.
what is an ai agent | ai that does tasks => An AI agent is a model that can take actions with tools, like searching, running code or clicking through apps, to finish a multi-step task.
what is multimodal ai | ai that sees images => Multimodal models handle more than text, like reading images, listening to audio or making pictures. You can show them a photo and ask about it.
is it cheating to use ai for homework | using ai for school => It depends on your teacher's rules. Using AI to explain ideas or check your understanding is often fine; turning in AI-written work as your own usually isn't. Ask your teacher.
can teachers tell if you used ai | ai detectors accurate => AI detectors are often wrong, both missing AI text and flagging human writing. Teachers often notice when writing doesn't match a student's style, so honesty is the best plan.
what is the turing test | can a computer fool a human judge => Alan Turing proposed the test in 1950: if a judge can't tell a machine from a person by chatting, the machine passes. Turing was a British mathematician and early computer scientist.
when was ai invented | history of ai => The term artificial intelligence was coined for the 1956 Dartmouth workshop. Big moments include IBM's Deep Blue beating chess champion Kasparov in 1997 and AlphaGo beating Go champion Lee Sedol in 2016.
how is ai used in video games | game ai enemies => Game enemies usually use scripted rules, state machines and pathfinding like A*, not chatbot-style AI. Some newer games experiment with AI voices and characters.
what is pathfinding | a star algorithm => Pathfinding finds a route around obstacles. A* is a popular algorithm that checks the most promising paths first to find a short route quickly, used in many games and robots.
what is ai voice cloning | can ai copy voices => AI can copy someone's voice from short recordings. Scammers sometimes use fake voices on calls, so families can agree on a secret word to check it's really them.
how can i tell if a picture is ai generated | spot ai images => Look for odd hands, warped text, strange backgrounds, too-perfect skin and things that don't make sense. Some images carry content credentials. It's getting harder, so check the source.
what is a gpu doing when training ai | why does ai need gpus => Training means doing trillions of multiplications to adjust parameters. GPUs do thousands of these at once, so huge clusters of them train big models.
what is ai alignment | what is ai safety => AI safety and alignment research tries to make AI systems helpful, honest and harmless, and to make sure they do what people actually intend.
will ai take over the world | is ai dangerous => AI as of 2025 is a tool that can be misused or make mistakes, so researchers work on safety and governments make rules. Movie-style robot takeovers aren't how real AI works.
what is a recommendation ai | how does tiktok know what i like => It tracks what you watch, like, skip and replay, compares you with similar users, and predicts what will keep you watching.
what is machine translation | how does google translate work => Modern translation uses neural networks trained on huge amounts of text in two languages, learning how sentences map from one to the other.
what is an embedding in ai | vector embeddings => An embedding turns words, pictures or sentences into lists of numbers where similar meanings end up close together. Search and recommendation systems use them.
what is a transformer in ai | what does gpt stand for => A transformer is the neural network design behind most modern language models. It uses attention to weigh how words relate. GPT stands for Generative Pre-trained Transformer.
what is attention in ai models | self attention meaning => Attention lets a model focus on the most relevant earlier words when predicting the next one, like linking "it" to the right noun in a sentence.
what is ai art copyright | can i sell ai art => Rules are still being decided. As of 2025, the US Copyright Office has said purely AI-generated images can't be copyrighted without real human authorship. Using AI to copy a living artist's style is also debated.
