# tag: tech.safety.passwords
good password tips | is my password good enough | how long should a password be => Long beats complicated. Use at least 12 to 16 characters, like a few random words joined together, and never reuse it on another site. Avoid names, birthdays and pet names.
what is a passphrase | password made of words => A passphrase is a password made of several random words, like "cactus-orbit-pencil-river". It's long, hard to guess and easier to remember than random letters.
why shouldn't i reuse passwords | same password everywhere bad => If one site gets hacked, attackers try that email and password on other sites. A different password for each account stops one leak from unlocking everything.
should i write my passwords down | where should i keep my passwords => A password manager is best. If you write them down, keep the paper somewhere safe at home, never in your backpack or on a sticky note on your screen.
what is a passkey | passkeys vs passwords => A passkey lets you sign in with your face, fingerprint or device PIN instead of a password. It can't be phished or guessed, so use passkeys when a site offers them.
should i share my password with my best friend | can i tell my friend my password => No. Even good friends can fall out, slip up or have their device hacked. Your parents or guardians are the only people who should know your passwords.
how do i know if my password was leaked | was i in a data breach => Check your email at haveibeenpwned.com, a well known breach checker. Many password managers and Google's and Apple's password tools also warn you about leaked passwords.
my password showed up in a breach what now | password found in breach => Change that password right away, and change it anywhere else you used it. Turn on two-factor authentication and tell a parent.
how often should i change my passwords | do i need to change passwords often => You don't need to change strong, unique passwords on a schedule. Change one right away if it leaks, if someone else learned it, or if you see strange account activity.
what is an authenticator app | google authenticator vs sms codes => An authenticator app makes a new 6 digit code every 30 seconds for signing in. It's safer than text message codes, which can be stolen with SIM swaps.
what are backup codes | 2fa recovery codes => Backup codes are one-time codes a site gives you when you turn on 2FA, for when you lose your phone. Print or save them somewhere safe with a parent.
someone is asking for my 2fa code | should i give my verification code => Never. A real company will never ask you to read out or send a code it texted you. Anyone asking is trying to break into your account.
what is a security key | yubikey => A security key is a small USB or NFC device you tap to sign in. It's one of the strongest kinds of 2FA and works with many big sites.
# tag: tech.safety.scams
what are the signs of phishing | how do i spot a fake email => Watch for urgent threats ("your account will be deleted"), prizes you didn't enter, misspelled sender addresses, odd links, and requests for passwords or codes. When unsure, go to the site yourself instead of clicking.
how do i check where a link really goes | hover over link => On a computer, hover over the link to see the real address at the bottom of the browser. On a phone, touch and hold it to preview. Look closely at the domain name before the first single slash.
what is a fake login page | site looks like roblox but url is weird => Scammers copy real login pages on look-alike addresses like rob1ox or roblox-free. Only sign in after typing the real address yourself or using the official app.
someone said i accidentally reported them on steam | i accidentally reported you scam => That's a well known scam. They'll send you to a fake "Steam admin" who asks for your login or payment. Steam never works that way; block and report them.
someone wants me to scan a qr code to verify on discord | discord qr code login scam => Don't scan it. Scanning a Discord login QR code from someone else signs them into your account. Real servers never need this.
someone asked me to buy gift cards | read the numbers on a gift card scam => Anyone asking you to pay with gift cards or to read the numbers on the back is almost always a scammer. Real businesses and family don't ask for payment that way. Tell an adult.
is a free nitro link real | free discord nitro scam => Random free Nitro links from strangers or hacked friends are usually scams that steal accounts. Real gifts show up as a gift box in Discord, not a website asking you to log in.
my friend sent me a weird link | friend's account hacked => If a friend suddenly sends a strange link or asks you to try their game, their account may be hacked. Don't click it. Check with them another way, like in person or by text.
someone asked me to test their game | try my game scam discord => A stranger asking you to download and test their game is a common way to spread account-stealing malware. Don't download it.
are cheats and hacks for games safe | downloading game cheats => No. Cheat programs often contain malware that steals accounts and passwords, and they can get you banned. They also ruin the game for others.
is it safe to download free versions of paid games | cracked games virus => Cracked or pirated games are a common way to spread malware, and pirating breaks the law. Use free games, sales, or ask for a gift instead.
roblox trade scam | someone wants me to give my items first => Trade scams trick you into giving items first, swap items at the last second, or send you to fake trade sites. Use the game's official trade window only and never trust "give first" deals.
what is a giveaway scam | you won a giveaway message => If you didn't enter a giveaway, you didn't win it. Messages asking you to pay shipping, log in or share info to claim a prize are scams.
microsoft support scam call | pop up says my computer has a virus call this number => Pop-ups or calls saying your computer is infected and to call a number are scams. Close the browser (Ctrl + Shift + Esc to end it if stuck) and never let strangers control your computer.
what is smishing | scam text messages => Smishing is phishing by text message, like fake delivery or bank alerts with links. Don't tap the link; check with the company in their real app or site.
what is social engineering | tricks hackers use => Social engineering is tricking people instead of hacking computers, like pretending to be staff, a friend or a moderator to get your password or codes. Slow down and check before you trust.
what should i do if i clicked a phishing link | i clicked a scam link => Don't type anything on that page. If you already entered a password, change it right away from the real site, turn on 2FA, run a virus scan, and tell a parent.
i got tricked online | a scammer got my stuff => Tell a parent or trusted adult right away. Change any passwords you shared, report the scammer on the platform, and don't send anything more, even if they threaten you.
what is a fake app | how do i spot a fake app => Fake apps copy real names and icons. Check the developer name, reviews and download count, and only install from the official app store.
what is a browser extension scam | dangerous chrome extensions => Some extensions steal data or inject ads. Only install extensions you really need, from well known developers, and remove ones you don't use.
# tag: tech.safety.privacy
what is personal information online | what counts as private info => Your full name, home address, school, phone number, birthday, passwords, photos showing where you live, and your location are all private. Share them only with a parent's okay.
does stuff online stay forever | can deleted posts come back => Your digital footprint is everything you post, like, comment and share. Screenshots and copies can last even after you delete things, so post as if anyone might see it later.
what is a good gamertag | should i use my real name as a username => Pick a username that doesn't include your real name, age, school, town or birth year. Something fun like a made-up word is safest.
can photos show where i live | photo location data => Phone photos can store GPS location in hidden data. Turn off location for the camera, and watch for house numbers, street signs or school logos in the background.
how do i turn off location in photos on iphone | stop iphone photos saving location => Open Settings, Privacy and Security, Location Services, Camera, and choose Never. When sharing, tap Options in the share sheet and turn off Location.
should i use my real birthday online | lying about age online => Many sites have age limits for good reasons, like COPPA rules for kids under 13. Use your real age so you get the right safety settings, and ask a parent before signing up.
what is coppa | why do sites need you to be 13 => COPPA is a US law that limits how websites collect data from kids under 13. That's why many apps require you to be 13 or need parent permission.
should my accounts be private or public | make my profile private => Keep accounts private so only approved friends see your posts. Check privacy settings on every app and turn off things like sharing location or showing your contacts.
what is location sharing | should i share my location in apps => Location sharing shows where you are on a map. Only share it with your family, and turn it off in apps like Snapchat's Snap Map (Ghost Mode) or game apps.
what are app permissions | why does this app want my contacts => Apps ask permission to use your camera, mic, location or contacts. Only allow what the app really needs to work. A flashlight app doesn't need your contacts.
school or cafe wifi safety | logging in on free wifi => Public Wi-Fi can be watched by others on it. Avoid signing into important accounts on open networks, and stick to sites with HTTPS.
do websites track me | what is tracking online => Many sites use cookies and trackers to see what you visit and show ads. Use private settings, block third-party cookies and say no to app tracking requests.
what is a privacy policy | should i read the privacy policy => A privacy policy explains what data an app collects and what it does with it. It's long, but a parent can help check the important parts before you sign up.
should i talk to ai chatbots about private stuff | is it safe to tell ai my secrets => Don't share personal details like your full name, address, school or passwords with AI chatbots. What you type may be stored. For worries, talk to a trusted adult.
# tag: tech.safety.people
someone online asked for a picture of me | stranger wants my photo => Don't send it. Block them and tell a parent or trusted adult. Adults asking kids for photos is a serious red flag, and you won't be in trouble for telling.
someone online is threatening to share my pictures | sextortion help => This is not your fault. Stop replying, don't pay or send anything more, keep the messages, block them, and tell a trusted adult right away. In the US, NCMEC's Take It Down service helps remove images of minors.
what are warning signs of a stranger online | red flags when talking to someone online => Watch for someone who gives lots of compliments or gifts, asks you to keep secrets, wants to move to private apps, asks for photos, or asks where you live. Tell a trusted adult.
someone online says they are 12 too | how do i know someone's real age online => You can't know for sure. People can fake their age, photos and voice. Never meet up or share private details, and talk to a parent if they push.
someone keeps messaging me and i want them to stop | how do i get someone to stop messaging me => You don't have to reply. Block and report them on the app, and tell a trusted adult, especially if they make you uncomfortable.
what should i do if something online makes me uncomfortable | something feels wrong online => Stop, close the app or game, and tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult. You can always leave a chat, block someone and report them. It's never too late to tell.
someone online is being extra nice and wants secrets | stranger gives compliments and gifts online => Grooming is when someone, often an adult, builds trust with a kid online using compliments, gifts or secrets so they can take advantage later. If someone makes you feel special but asks for secrets, tell a trusted adult.
should i accept friend requests from strangers | random friend request game => It's safest to only add people you know in real life. Strangers may seem nice but you can't know who they really are.
someone sent me robux or gifts and wants something | gifts from online stranger => Be careful. Strangers who send gifts may expect something back later, like photos or secrets. Don't accept, and tell a trusted adult.
how do i report someone in a game | report button => Most games and apps have a Report option on the player's profile or chat message. Pick the reason, add details, and also tell a trusted adult if it's serious.
# tag: tech.safety.bullying
what should i do if someone is cyberbullying me | people keep being mean to me in chats => Don't reply or get back at them. Take screenshots, block and report them, and tell a trusted adult. It's not your fault, and you don't have to handle it alone.
how do i help a friend being bullied online | my friend is getting bullied in a game => Be kind to your friend, don't join in or share the mean stuff, help them save screenshots and report it, and encourage them to tell a trusted adult.
should i screenshot mean messages | saving evidence of bullying => Yes. Screenshots of messages, usernames and dates help adults, schools and platforms take action, even if the bully deletes them later.
what if i said something mean online | i was mean to someone in a game => It's okay to make mistakes. Apologize, delete the mean post if you can, and try to do better. Everyone has a real person behind the screen.
what is trolling | what does troll mean online => Trolling is posting things to upset people on purpose. The best move is usually not to react, then block or report them.
what is doxxing | someone posted my address => Doxxing is posting someone's private info like their address or real name online to harass them. If it happens, tell a trusted adult right away and report it.
# tag: tech.safety.habits
why should i update my apps and devices | are updates important => Updates fix security holes that attackers use to break in. Turn on automatic updates for your phone, iPad, PC, browser and apps.
should i click accept all cookies | what happens if i accept cookies => Accepting cookies lets sites and advertisers track you more. Choosing Reject all or Necessary only is fine for most sites.
is it okay to buy things in games without asking | in game purchases => Always ask a parent before spending money. In-game currency can make it easy to spend more than you meant, and some purchases can't be refunded.
how much screen time is healthy | balancing screens and other stuff => There's no single right number, but take breaks, keep screens out of bed, and balance gaming with sleep, school, outside time and friends. Follow your family's rules.
how do i take breaks from screens | eye strain from screens => Use the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Stand up and move every hour.
what is the dark web | is the dark web dangerous => The dark web is a hidden part of the internet reached with special software. It has lots of illegal and dangerous stuff, so it's not a place for kids to explore.
what is ransomware | what does ransomware do => Ransomware locks or encrypts your files and demands payment to unlock them. Backups and avoiding sketchy downloads are the best protection.
what is spyware | what is a keylogger => Spyware secretly watches what you do. A keylogger records what you type, including passwords. Both often come from cheats, cracked software or fake downloads.
what is a trojan virus | trojan horse malware => A trojan is malware pretending to be something useful, like a free game or cheat, that opens your computer to attackers once you run it.
what is a computer worm | how do worms spread => A worm is malware that spreads itself from computer to computer over networks without you clicking anything. Keeping systems updated blocks most of them.
how do i make a safe account for a new game | setting up a gaming account safely => Use a unique password, turn on 2FA, pick a username without personal info, set privacy and chat settings to friends only, and have a parent set up spending limits.
