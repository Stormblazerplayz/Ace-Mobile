# tag: tech.mobile.trouble
why won't my iphone charge | iphone not charging => Try another cable and charger, gently clean lint from the port with a wooden toothpick, and force restart. If it shows Charging On Hold, the phone is too hot and will continue when cooler.
why is my iphone stuck on the apple logo | iphone boot loop => Force restart it first. If that fails, connect it to a computer and use recovery mode to update iOS, which tries to keep your data.
why does my iphone say sos only | no service iphone => It means there's no signal from your carrier, but emergency calls may work. Toggle airplane mode, restart, and check for a carrier update in Settings, General, About.
why isn't my iphone ringing | iphone silent calls => Check the Ring/Silent switch or Action button, Focus modes, and that the ringtone volume is up in Settings, Sounds and Haptics.
why does an app keep crashing on my iphone | iphone app crash => Update the app and iOS, restart the phone, and if it keeps happening, delete and reinstall the app.
why can't i download apps on my iphone | app store not downloading => Check storage, Wi-Fi, and that you're signed in to the App Store. Screen Time restrictions or Ask to Buy can also block downloads.
why is my ipad screen not responding to touch | ipad touch not working => Clean the screen, remove the case and screen protector to test, and force restart. If only some spots fail, it may need repair.
my ipad says ipad unavailable | forgot ipad passcode => After too many wrong passcodes, the iPad locks. You'll need to erase it, either from the lock screen's Forgot Passcode option or with a computer, then restore from a backup.
why is my icloud storage full | icloud full fix => Photos and device backups use the most. Delete old backups, turn off backup for big apps, delete large files, or ask a parent about more storage.
why aren't my photos syncing to icloud | icloud photos not uploading => Make sure iCloud Photos is on, the device is on Wi-Fi and charging, Low Power Mode is off, and there's free iCloud storage.
why doesn't my ipad show up on my pc | ipad not detected windows => Unlock the iPad, tap Trust when asked, try another cable, and install the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store.
why is my iphone or ipad not connecting to wifi | ios wifi problems => Forget the network and rejoin, restart the router and device, and if needed reset network settings.
why won't my bluetooth headphones connect to my ipad | earbuds won't pair ipad => Turn Bluetooth off and on, forget the device and pair it again, and make sure it's not still connected to another device.
why is my ipad sound not working | no sound on ipad => Check the volume, the silent mode in Control Center, and Bluetooth, since sound may be going to headphones. Restart the iPad if it persists.
why does my android say storage full but i deleted stuff | android storage still full => Empty the trash in Photos and Files apps, clear app caches, and check that downloaded media in apps like WhatsApp isn't taking space.
why is my phone not receiving texts | not getting messages => Restart the phone, check airplane mode and blocked numbers, and make sure messages settings and storage are fine. A carrier issue may need a call to them.
